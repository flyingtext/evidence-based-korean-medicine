## 續方

### Source Text

柏葶東久亭寺僧悟大師傅經驗方治飲水百杯，尚猶未足，小便如油，或如杏色。服此藥三五日，小便大出，毒歸於下，十日永除根本。此方令子和辨過，云是重劑，可用。

### Literal Translation

The empirically proven formula transmitted by the monk Master Wu (悟大師) of Jiuting Temple in Baidingdong (柏葶東) treats [a condition where] one drinks a hundred cups of water and still is not satisfied, with urine like oil, or of an apricot color. After taking this medicine for three to five days, the urine passes in great quantity, the toxin settles below, and in ten days the root is permanently removed. This formula was examined by Zihe (子和), who said it is a heavy preparation and can be used.

### Translator's Notes

- 柏葶東 kept as a place-name transliteration.
- 子和 refers to Zhang Zihe (張子和).

## 續方

### Source Text

悟公師親驗過矣。

水銀（四錢）　錫（二錢，用水銀研成沙子）　牡蠣（一兩）　密陀僧（一兩）　知母（一兩）　紫花苦參（一兩）　貝母（一兩）　黃丹（半兩）　栝蔞根（半兩）

上為細末，男子用不生兒豬肚一個，內藥，婦人用獖豬肚一個，麻線縫之，新瓦一合，繩系一兩遭，米一升，更用栝蔞根末半斤，卻於新汲水煮熟，取出放冷，用砂盆，內研爛，就和為丸，如豬肚丸法用之。

### Literal Translation

Master Wu himself personally tested it.

Mercury (水銀) (four qian), tin (錫) (two qian, ground with the mercury into sand), oyster shell (牡蠣) (one liang), litharge (密陀僧) (one liang), Anemarrhena (知母) (one liang), purple-flowered Sophora flavescens (紫花苦參) (one liang), Fritillaria (貝母) (one liang), yellow lead (黃丹) (half liang), Trichosanthes root (栝蔞根) (half liang).

Grind the above into a fine powder. For men, use one pig stomach from a sow that has not borne young, and place the medicine inside; for women, use one pig stomach from a castrated boar (獖豬). Sew it shut with hemp thread, [place it in] a new tile vessel (瓦) closed together, tie it with rope once or twice, [add] one sheng of rice, and further use half a jin of Trichosanthes root powder; cook it in freshly drawn water until done, take it out and let it cool, then grind it thoroughly in a sand basin, and mix it into pills, using them according to the method of Zhudu Wan (豬肚丸).

### Translator's Notes

- 獖豬 rendered as 'castrated boar'.
- 密陀僧 rendered as 'litharge'.
- 新瓦一合 rendered as 'a new tile vessel closed together'.
