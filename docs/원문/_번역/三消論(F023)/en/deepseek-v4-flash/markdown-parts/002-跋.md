## 跋

### Source Text

《三消》之論，劉河間之所作也。因麻徵君寓汴梁，暇日訪先生後裔，或舉教醫學者，即其人矣。徵君規詣其家，求先生平昔所著遺害。

### Literal Translation

The treatise on the Three Wastings (三消) was written by Liu Hejian (劉河間). Because the Admonisher Ma (麻徵君) was residing in Bianliang (汴梁), on a day of leisure he visited the descendants of the Master; one who was engaged in teaching medicine was that very person. The Admonisher went to his house and sought the works that the Master had written in his lifetime.

### Translator's Notes

- 徵君 rendered as 'Admonisher' (honorific title).
- 遺害 likely a typo for 遺稿 (surviving manuscripts); rendered as 'works written in his lifetime'.

## 跋

### Source Text

乃出《三消論》、《氣宜》、《病機》三書，未傳於世者，文多不全。止取《三消論》，於卷首增寫六位、藏象二圖，其餘未遑潤色，即付友人穆子昭。

子昭，乃河間門人穆大黃之後也。

### Literal Translation

Thereupon he brought out the three books Sanxiao Lun (三消論), Qi Yi (氣宜), and Bingji (病機), which had not been transmitted to the world, and whose texts were largely incomplete. He took only the Sanxiao Lun, and at the head of the scroll added two diagrams, of the six positions (六位) and of the visceral manifestations (藏象); the rest he had no time to polish, and he immediately gave it to his friend Mu Zizhao (穆子昭).

Zizhao was a descendant of Mu Dahuang (穆大黃), a disciple of Hejian.

### Translator's Notes

- 六位/藏象 rendered as 'six positions' and 'visceral manifestations'.
- 穆大黃 kept as a personal name.

## 跋

### Source Text

偶於鄉人霍司承君祥處，復見其文，然傳寫甚誤，但依仿而錄之，以付後之學者，詳為刊正云。

時甲辰年冬至日錦溪野老書

### Literal Translation

By chance, at the place of my fellow villager Huo Sicheng, courtesy name Junxiang (霍司承君祥), I saw the text again; however, the transmitted copies were very erroneous, so I merely followed the model and transcribed it, to hand it to later scholars to carefully correct and publish.

Written by the Old Rustic of Jinxi (錦溪野老) on the winter solstice day of the year jiachen (甲辰).

### Translator's Notes

- 霍司承君祥 parsed as surname Huo, given name Sicheng, courtesy name Junxiang.
- 刊正 rendered as 'correct and publish'.
