雲霞煙鎖大敦峰　忘卻行間轉太衝　坐望中封無路入　曲泉行水聽淙淙

Mist and rosy clouds lock the peak of Dadun; forgetting Xingjian, I turn to Taichong. Sitting and gazing at Zhongfeng, there is no road in; at Ququan the flowing water, I listen to its gurgling.
