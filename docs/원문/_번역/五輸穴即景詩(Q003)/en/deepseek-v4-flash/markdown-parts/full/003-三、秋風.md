## 三、秋風

### Source Text

秋風厲兌內庭西　陷谷衝陽過解谿　三里未知何日到　幾番翹首欲思齊

### Literal Translation

The autumn wind blows west of Lidui and Neiting; through Xiangu and Chongyang one passes Jiexi. When will one reach Sanli, none can know; how many times one lifts the head, longing to draw level.
