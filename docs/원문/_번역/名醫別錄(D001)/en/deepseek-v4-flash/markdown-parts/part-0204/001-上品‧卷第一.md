## 柴胡

### Source Text

（得茯苓、桔梗、大黃、石膏、麻子仁、甘草、桂，以水一斗煮，取四升，入硝石三方寸匕，治傷寒寒熱、頭痛、心下煩滿。半夏為之使，惡皂莢，畏女菀、藜蘆。）

《本經》原文：柴胡，味苦，平。

### Literal Translation

(With Poria [茯苓], Platycodon [桔梗], rhubarb [大黃], gypsum [石膏], hemp seed [麻子仁], licorice [甘草] and cinnamon [桂], boil with one dou of water, take four sheng, add three square-cun-spoonfuls of nitre [硝石], to treat cold damage with cold and heat, headache, and vexation and fullness below the heart. Pinellia [半夏] is its envoy; it is averse to Gleditsia [皂莢] and fears Aster [女菀] and Veratrum [藜蘆].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Bupleurum [柴胡], flavor bitter, neutral.
