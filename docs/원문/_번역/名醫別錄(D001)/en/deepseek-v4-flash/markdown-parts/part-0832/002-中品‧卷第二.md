## 嬰桃

### Source Text

味辛，平，無毒。主止泄腸　，除熱，調中，益脾氣，令人好色美志。一名牛桃，一英豆。

### Literal Translation

Acrid in flavor, neutral, non-toxic. It treats stopping diarrhea and intestinal [conditions], removing heat, regulating the center, boosting spleen qi (脾氣), and causing people to have good complexion and fine will. One name is Niutao (牛桃), one is Yingdou (英豆).
