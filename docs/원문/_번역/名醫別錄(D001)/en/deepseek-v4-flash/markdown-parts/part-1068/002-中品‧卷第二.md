## 赤小豆

### Source Text

味甘，酸，平，溫，無毒。主治寒熱、熱中、消渴，止泄，利小便，吐逆，卒　，滿。又，葉名藿，主治小便數，去煩熱。

Sweet-sour in flavor, neutral to warm, non-toxic. It treats chills and fever, heat-in-the-center (熱中), wasting-thirst (消渴), stopping diarrhea (止泄), benefiting urination (利小便), vomiting-with-rebellion (吐逆), sudden [blank], and fullness (滿). Also, the leaf is named huo (藿), and treats frequent urination (小便數) and removes vexing heat (去煩熱).
