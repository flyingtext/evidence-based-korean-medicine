## 防己

### Source Text

《本經》原文：防己，味辛平。主風寒，溫瘧熱氣諸癇，除邪，利大小便，一名解離。生川穀。

### Literal Translation

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 防己 (fángjǐ), flavor acrid, neutral. It governs wind-cold (風寒), warm malaria, heat-qi, and the various epilepsies (溫瘧熱氣諸癇), removes evil [qi], and benefits the large and small [intestines] (利大小便). One name is 解離 (jiělí). It grows in river valleys.
