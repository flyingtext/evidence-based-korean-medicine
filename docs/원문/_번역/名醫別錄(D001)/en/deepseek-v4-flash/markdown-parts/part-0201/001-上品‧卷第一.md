## 升麻

### Source Text

主解百毒，殺百精老物殃鬼，辟溫疾瘴邪毒蠱。久服不夭。一名周升麻。

It mainly resolves the hundred poisons, kills the hundred essence-things, old things and baleful ghosts, and wards off warm diseases, miasmic evil poison and gu [蠱]. Taken over a long time, one does not die young. One name is Zhou Shengma [周升麻].
