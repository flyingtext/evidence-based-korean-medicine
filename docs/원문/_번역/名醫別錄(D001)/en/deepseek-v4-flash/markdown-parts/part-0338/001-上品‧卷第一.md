## 辛夷

### Source Text

可作膏藥，用之去中心及外毛，毛射人肺，令人咳。生漢中。九采實，曝乾。

### Literal Translation

It may be made into a plaster medicine; when used, remove the center and the outer hairs, for the hairs shooting into the lung cause coughing. It grows in Hanzhong. The fruit is harvested in the ninth month and dried in the sun.
