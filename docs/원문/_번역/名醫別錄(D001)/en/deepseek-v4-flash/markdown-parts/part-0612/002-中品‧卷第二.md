## 本

### Source Text

（惡　茹。）

《本經》原文：　本，味辛，溫。主婦人疝瘕，陰中寒腫痛，腹中急，除風頭痛，長肌膚，悅顏色。

### Literal Translation

(Averse to 茹.) Original text of the Benjing: Gao-ben (藁本), flavor pungent, warm. It chiefly treats women's swelling-masses (疝瘕), cold swelling and pain within the genitals, abdominal tension, dispels wind and headache, lengthens the skin and flesh, and refines the complexion.
