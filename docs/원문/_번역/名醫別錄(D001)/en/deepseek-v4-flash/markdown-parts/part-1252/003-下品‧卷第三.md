## 蜀漆

### Source Text

五月采葉，陰（栝蔞為之使，惡貫眾。）

《本經》原文：蜀漆，味辛，平。主瘧及咳逆寒熱，腹中症堅痞結，積聚邪氣，蠱毒鬼注。

### Literal Translation

Gathered as leaves in the fifth month, dried in the shade. (栝蔞 (guālóu) is its envoy; it fears 貫眾 (guànzhòng).)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 蜀漆 (shǔqī), flavor acrid, neutral. It governs malaria and cough and retching with cold and heat (瘧及咳逆寒熱), hard masses and bound hardness in the abdomen (腹中症堅痞結), accumulations and evil-qi (積聚邪氣), and gu-poison and ghost-attack (蠱毒鬼注).
