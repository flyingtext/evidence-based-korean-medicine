## 曼諸石

### Source Text

味甘。主益五臟氣，輕身長年。一名陰精，六月、七月出石上，青黃色，夜有光。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor. It chiefly boosts the qi of the five viscera, lightens the body, and prolongs years. One name is Yinjing; in the sixth and seventh months it emerges on rocks, blue-yellow in color, and shines at night.
