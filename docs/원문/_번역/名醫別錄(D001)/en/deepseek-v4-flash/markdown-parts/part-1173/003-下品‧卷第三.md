## 旋覆花

### Source Text

根，主風濕，生平澤。五花，晒乾，廿日成。《本經》原文：旋覆花，味鹹，溫。

### Literal Translation

The root governs wind-damp (風濕). It grows in level marshes. The five flowers, dried in the sun, become [ready] in twenty days. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 旋覆花 (xuánfùhuā), flavor salty, warm.
