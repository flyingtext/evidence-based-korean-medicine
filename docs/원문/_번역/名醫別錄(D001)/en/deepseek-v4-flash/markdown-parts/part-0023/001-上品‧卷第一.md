## 扁青

### Source Text

《本經》原文：扁青，味甘，平。主目痛明目，折跌癰腫，金創不瘳，破積聚，解毒瓦斯，利精神。久服輕身

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Flat azurite [扁青], flavor sweet, neutral. It mainly treats eye pain and brightens the eyes, treats fractures, sprains, abscesses and swellings, and metal wounds that do not heal; it breaks accumulations, resolves poisonous qi, and benefits essence and spirit. Taken over a long time, it lightens the body
