## 石楠草

### Source Text

有毒。主治腳弱，五臟邪氣，除熱。女子不可久服，令思男。

### Literal Translation

Toxic. It governs weakness of the feet (腳弱), evil-qi of the five viscera (五臟邪氣), and removes heat (除熱). Women must not take it for a long time, for it makes them long for men (令思男).
