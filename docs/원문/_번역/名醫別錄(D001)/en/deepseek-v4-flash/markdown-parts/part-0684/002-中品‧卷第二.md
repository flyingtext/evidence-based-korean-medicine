## 狗脊

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：狗脊，味苦，平。主腰背強，關機緩急，周痹寒濕膝痛，頗利老人。一名百枝。

### Literal Translation

) Original text of the Benjing: Cibotium (狗脊), flavor bitter, level. It chiefly treats stiffness of the lumbar back, taut-relaxation of the joints and gates (關機), generalized impediment with cold-damp knee pain, and rather benefits the aged. One name is 百枝.
