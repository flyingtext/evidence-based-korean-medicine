## 棘刺花

### Source Text

實，主明目，心腹痿除熱，利小便。生道旁。四月采。

### Literal Translation

The fruit chiefly brightens the eyes, treats wilting of heart and abdomen with dispelling heat, and benefits urination. It grows beside the road. Gather in the fourth month.
