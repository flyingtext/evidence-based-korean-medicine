## 石膽

### Source Text

二月庚子、辛丑日采。（水英為之使，畏牡桂、菌桂、芫花、辛夷薇。）

《本經》原文：石膽，味酸，寒。

### Literal Translation

Gather it on the gengzi and xinchou days of the second month. (Water smartweed [水英] is its envoy; it fears Mu Gui [牡桂], Jun Gui [菌桂], Daphne genkwa flower [芫花], and Xinyi [辛夷].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Chalcanthite [石膽], flavor sour, cold.
