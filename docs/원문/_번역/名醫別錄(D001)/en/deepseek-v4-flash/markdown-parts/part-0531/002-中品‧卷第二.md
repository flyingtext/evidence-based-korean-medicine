## 凝水石

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：凝水石，味辛，寒。主身熱，腹中積聚邪氣，皮中如火燒，煩滿，水飲之。

### Literal Translation

) Original text of the Benjing: Glauberite/water stone (凝水石), flavor pungent, cold. It chiefly treats body heat, accumulations of pathogenic qi within the abdomen, the skin being as if burned by fire, vexation and fullness; drink it with water.
