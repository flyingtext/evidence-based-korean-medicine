## 龜甲

### Source Text

味甘，有毒。主治頭瘡難燥，女子陰瘡及驚恚氣，心腹痛不可久立，骨中寒熱，傷寒復，或肌體寒熱欲死，以作湯良。久服益氣資智，亦使人能食。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, poisonous. It treats head-sores that are hard to dry (頭瘡難燥), women's yin-sores (女子陰瘡) and fright and anger qi (驚恚氣), heart-abdominal pain with inability to stand long (心腹痛不可久立), cold and heat in the bones (骨中寒熱), recurrence after cold damage (傷寒復), or bodily chills and heat with desire to die (肌體寒熱欲死); it is good used to make a decoction. Taken for a long time it boosts qi and enriches wisdom (益氣資智) and also causes people to be able to eat.
