## 衛矛

### Source Text

無毒。主治中惡，腹痛，去白蟲，消皮膚風毒腫，令陰中解。生霍山。

### Literal Translation

Not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat attack by noxious qi, abdominal pain, dispel white worms, dissipate wind-toxin swelling of the skin, and cause the genitals to relax (令陰中解). It grows at Huoshan.
