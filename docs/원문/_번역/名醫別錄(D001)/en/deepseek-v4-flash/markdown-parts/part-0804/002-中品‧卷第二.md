## 垣衣

### Source Text

味酸，無毒。主治黃膽，心煩，咳逆，血氣，暴熱在腸胃，金瘡內塞。久服補中益氣長肌，好顏色。

### Literal Translation

Sour in flavor, non-toxic. It treats jaundice (黃膽), vexation of the heart, cough with rebellion (咳逆), blood qi (血氣), sudden heat in the stomach and intestines, and internal blockage of metal (sword) wounds. Taken for a long time it supplements the center, boosts qi, and grows the flesh, and gives a good complexion.
