## 卷柏

### Source Text

《本經》原文：卷柏，味辛，溫。主五臟邪氣，女子陰中寒熱痛，症瘕血閉絕子。久服輕身

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Selaginella [卷柏], flavor pungent, warm. It mainly treats evil qi of the five viscera, cold and heat pain in the female genitals, zheng [症] and jia [瘕], blood closure and failure to bear children. Taken over a long time, it lightens the body
