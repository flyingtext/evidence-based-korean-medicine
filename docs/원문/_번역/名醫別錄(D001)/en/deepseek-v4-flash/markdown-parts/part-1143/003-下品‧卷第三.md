## 蔓椒

### Source Text

采莖、根煮釀酒。《本經》原文：蔓椒，味苦，溫。主風寒濕痹，歷節疼，除四肢厥氣，膝痛。

### Literal Translation

Gather the stems and roots, boil and brew into wine. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 蔓椒 (mànjiāo), flavor bitter, warm. It governs wind-cold-damp bi (風寒濕痹), pain of the joints (歷節疼), removes cold-qi of the four limbs (四肢厥氣), and knee pain.
