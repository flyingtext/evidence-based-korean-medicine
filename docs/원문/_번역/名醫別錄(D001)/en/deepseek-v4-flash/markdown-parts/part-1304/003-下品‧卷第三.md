## 雷丸

### Source Text

味鹹，微寒，有小寒。逐邪氣，惡風，汗出，除皮中熱結，積聚蠱毒，白蟲、寸白自出不止。久服令人陰痿。

### Literal Translation

Flavor salty, slightly cold, slightly cold. It expels evil-qi (邪氣), evil wind (惡風), sweating (汗出), removes heat bindings in the skin (皮中熱結), accumulations and gu-poison (積聚蠱毒), and white worms and tapeworms coming out unceasingly by themselves (白蟲、寸白自出不止). Taken for a long time it makes one impotent (陰痿).
