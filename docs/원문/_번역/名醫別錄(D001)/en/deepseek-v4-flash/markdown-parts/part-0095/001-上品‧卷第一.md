## 松脂

### Source Text

味甘，無毒，主治胃中伏熱，咽乾，消渴，及風痹、死肌。煉之令白。其赤者治惡風生太山，六月采。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, non-toxic. It mainly treats latent heat in the stomach, dry throat, wasting-thirst [消渴], and wind bi [風痹] and dead flesh. Refine it to make it white. The red one treats evil wind. It grows on Mount Tai; gather it in the sixth month.
