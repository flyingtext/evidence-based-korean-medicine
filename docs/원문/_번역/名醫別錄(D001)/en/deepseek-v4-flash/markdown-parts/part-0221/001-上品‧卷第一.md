## 槐實

### Source Text

（景天為之使。）

又，八月斷槐大枝，使生嫩孽，煮汁釀酒，治大風痿痹甚效。《本經》原文：槐實，味苦，寒。

### Literal Translation

(Sedum [景天] is its envoy.)

Also, in the eighth month cut off a great branch of the locust tree so that tender shoots grow, boil the juice to make wine, and it treats great wind [大風] and wilting bi [痿痹] very effectively. Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Locust fruit [槐實], flavor bitter, cold.
