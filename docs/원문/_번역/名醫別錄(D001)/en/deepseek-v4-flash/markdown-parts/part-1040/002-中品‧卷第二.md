## 柿

### Source Text

味甘，寒，無毒。主通鼻耳氣，腸　不足。又，火柿，主殺毒，療金瘡，火瘡，生肉，止痛。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, cold, non-toxic. It treats freeing the qi of the nose and ears (通鼻耳氣) and insufficiency of the intestines (腸[blank]不足). Also, fire-persimmon (火柿) treats killing poison (殺毒), healing metal wounds (金瘡) and fire-wounds (火瘡), producing flesh (生肉), and stopping pain.
