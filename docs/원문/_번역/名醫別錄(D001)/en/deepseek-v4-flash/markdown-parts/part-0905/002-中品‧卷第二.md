## 羊角

### Source Text

味苦，微寒，無毒。主治百節中結氣，風頭痛及蠱毒、吐血，婦人產後余痛。燒之魅，辟虎野狼。

### Literal Translation

Bitter in flavor, slightly cold, non-toxic. It treats binding qi in the hundred joints (百節中結氣), wind headache (風頭痛), gu-poison and vomiting of blood (蠱毒、吐血), and women's residual postpartum pain (產後余痛). Burned, it wards off evil spirits and repels tigers and wolves.
