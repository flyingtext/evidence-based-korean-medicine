## 地芩

### Source Text

味苦，無毒。主治小兒癇，除邪，養胎，風痹，洗浴寒熱，月中青翳，女子帶下木積草處，如朝生，天雨生蓋，黃白色。四月采。

### Literal Translation

Flavor bitter, non-toxic. It governs children's epilepsy, removes evil, nourishes the fetus, wind bi, washing to treat chills and fever, and the green opacity of the eye in the month, and women's vaginal discharge. It grows among decaying wood and grass, like the morning-born; when it rains it is born with a cap, yellow-white in color. Gather in the fourth month.
