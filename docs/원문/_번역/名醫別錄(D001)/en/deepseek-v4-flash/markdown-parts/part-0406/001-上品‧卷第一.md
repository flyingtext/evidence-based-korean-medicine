## 蜂子

### Source Text

大黃蜂子，主治乾嘔，土蜂子，治嗌痛。生武都。（畏黃芩、芍藥、牡蠣。

### Literal Translation

The larvae of the great yellow wasp chiefly treat dry retching; the larvae of the earth wasp treat throat pain. It grows in Wudu. (It fears Huangqin (Scutellaria), Shaoyao (peony), and Muli (oyster shell).
