## 石龍芻

### Source Text

《本經》原文：石龍芻，味苦，微寒。主心腹邪氣，小便不利，淋閉風濕，鬼注惡毒。久服，補虛羸，輕身

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Shilongchu [石龍芻], flavor bitter, slightly cold. It mainly treats evil qi of the heart and abdomen, difficult urination, strangury and urinary obstruction with wind-damp, and ghost zhu [鬼注] and evil poison. Taken over a long time, it supplements deficiency and emaciation and lightens the body
