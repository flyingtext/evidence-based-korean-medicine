## 芥

### Source Text

久服溫中。又，子，主射工及疰氣發無恆處，丸服之；或搗為末，醋和塗之，隨手驗也。

Taken over a long time it warms the center. Also, the seed chiefly treats the archer worm (shegong) and zhu qi that erupts without fixed place; take it as pills; or pound it into powder and mix with vinegar to apply, and it is effective at once.
