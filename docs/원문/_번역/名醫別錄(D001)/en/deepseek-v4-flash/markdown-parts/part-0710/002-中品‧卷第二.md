## 白芷

### Source Text

二月、八月采根，曝乾。（當歸為之使，惡旋覆花。）

《本經》原文：白芷，味辛，溫。

### Literal Translation

Gather the root in the second and eighth months, expose to sun to dry. (Dang-gui 當歸 is its envoy; it is averse to inula 旋覆花.) Original text of the Benjing: Angelica-root 白芷, flavor pungent, warm.
