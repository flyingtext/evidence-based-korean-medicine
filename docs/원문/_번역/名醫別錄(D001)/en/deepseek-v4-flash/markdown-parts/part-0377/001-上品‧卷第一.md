## 龍齒

### Source Text

主治小兒五驚，十二癇，身熱不可近人，大人骨間寒熱，又殺蠱毒。角，主治驚癇螈　，身熱如火，腹中堅及熱泄。生晉地及太山岩水岸土穴石中死龍處，采無時。

### Literal Translation

It chiefly treats the five frights and twelve epilepsies in children, body heat such that one cannot be approached, cold-heat between the bones in adults, and also kills gu (parasitic) poison. The horn chiefly treats fright epilepsy with ... spasms, body heat like fire, hardness in the abdomen, and heat diarrhea. It grows in the land of Jin and in the rock caves and earth holes by the water banks of Mount Tai where dead dragons are found; harvested at no fixed time.
