## 陟厘

### Source Text

味甘，大溫，無毒。主治心腹大寒，溫中消穀，強胃氣，止泄痢。生江南池澤。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, greatly warm, non-toxic. It treats great cold in the heart and abdomen, warms the center and digests grain (消穀), strengthens stomach qi, and stops diarrhea and dysentery. It grows in the ponds and marshes of Jiangnan.
