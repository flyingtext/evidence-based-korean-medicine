## 水銀

### Source Text

（畏磁石。）

《本經》原文：水銀，味辛，寒。主疥　痂瘍白禿，殺皮膚中虱，墮胎除熱，殺金銀銅錫毒。

### Literal Translation

(It fears magnetite [磁石].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Mercury [水銀], flavor pungent, cold. It mainly treats scabies, scabs, sores and white baldness, kills lice in the skin, aborts the fetus and removes heat, and kills the poison of gold, silver, copper and tin.
