## 巴豆

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：巴豆，味辛，溫。主傷寒溫瘧寒熱，破症瘕結聚堅積，留飲痰癖，大腹水脹，蕩練五臟六生川穀。

### Literal Translation

)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 巴豆 (bādòu), flavor acrid, warm. It governs cold-damage, warm malaria, cold and heat, breaks masses, bound accumulations, and hard accumulations (症瘕結聚堅積), retained drink and phlegm masses (留飲痰癖), great abdominal water distension (大腹水脹), and sweeps and cleanses the five viscera and six bowels. It grows in river valleys.
