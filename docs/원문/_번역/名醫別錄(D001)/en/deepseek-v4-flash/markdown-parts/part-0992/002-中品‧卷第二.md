## 桑螵蛸

### Source Text

主傷中，疝瘕，陰痿，益精生子，女子血閉腰痛，通五淋，利小便水道

### Literal Translation

It treats injury of the center (傷中), hernias and abdominal masses (疝瘕), yin-wei (陰痿), benefiting essence and giving birth to children (益精生子), women's blood-closure with lumbar pain (血閉腰痛), freeing the five stranguries (通五淋), and benefiting urination and the water-course (利小便水道).
