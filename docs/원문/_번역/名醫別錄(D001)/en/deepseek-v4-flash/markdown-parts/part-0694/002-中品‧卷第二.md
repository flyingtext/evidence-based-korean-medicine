## 通草

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主治脾疸，常欲眠，心煩，噦出音聲，治耳聾，散癰腫、諸結不消，及瘡，惡瘡，鼠　，　折，　鼻，息肉，墮胎，去三蟲。一名丁翁，生石城及山陽。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat spleen jaundice, constant desire to sleep, heart vexation, vomiting with sound (噦出音聲), treat deafness, disperse abscesses and swellings and various bindings that do not resolve, and (treat) ulcers, foul ulcers, rat fistulas, [injuries, broken nose, polyps], abort the fetus, and dispel the three worms ...
