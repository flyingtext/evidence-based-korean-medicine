## 玉泉

### Source Text

生藍田，采時。（畏款冬花。）

《本經》原文：玉泉，味甘，平，主五臟百病，柔筋強骨，安魂魄，長肌肉，益氣。

### Literal Translation

It grows in Lantian; gather it at the proper time. (It fears Tussilago flower [款冬花].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Jade Spring [玉泉], flavor sweet, neutral, mainly treats the hundred diseases of the five viscera, softens sinews and strengthens bones, settles the hun and po [魂魄], increases flesh, and boosts qi.
