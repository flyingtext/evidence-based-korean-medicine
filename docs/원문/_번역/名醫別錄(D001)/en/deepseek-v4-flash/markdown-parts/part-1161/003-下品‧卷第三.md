## 大戟

### Source Text

十二月采根陰乾。（反甘草。）

《本經》原文：大戟，味苦，寒。

### Literal Translation

Gathered as root in the twelfth month, dried in the shade. (It is incompatible with 甘草 (gāncǎo).)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 大戟 (dàjǐ), flavor bitter, cold.
