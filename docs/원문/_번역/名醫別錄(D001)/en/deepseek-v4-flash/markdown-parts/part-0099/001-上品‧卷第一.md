## 松

### Source Text

根白皮，主辟穀，不飢。《本經》原文：松脂，味苦，溫。主疽惡創，頭瘍白禿，疥瘙風氣，安五臟，除熱。

### Literal Translation

The white bark of the root mainly treats abstention from grain [辟穀] and does not hunger. Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Pine resin [松脂], flavor bitter, warm. It mainly treats deep-rooted and malignant sores, head sores and white baldness, scabies and itching and wind qi, settles the five viscera, and removes heat.
