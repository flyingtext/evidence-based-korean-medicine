## 王不留行

### Source Text

二月采。《本經》原文：王不留行，味苦，平。主金創，止血，逐痛出刺，除風痹內寒，久服，輕身

### Literal Translation

Harvested in the second month. Original text of the Benjing: Wangbuliuxing (Vaccaria), bitter in flavor, neutral. It chiefly treats metal wounds, stops bleeding, expels pain and removes thorns, and removes wind bi (impediment) with internal cold; taken over a long time, it lightens the body.
