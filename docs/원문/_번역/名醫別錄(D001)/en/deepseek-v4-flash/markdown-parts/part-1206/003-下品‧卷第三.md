## 女青

### Source Text

又，葉嫩時，似蘿摩，圓端，大莖，實黑，莖葉汁黃白。雀瓢白汁，主蟲蛇毒。《本經》原文：女青，味辛，平。

### Literal Translation

Also, when the leaves are tender, it resembles 蘿摩 (luómó), with round tips, large stems, black fruit, and the juice of the stems and leaves is yellow-white. The white juice of 雀瓢 (quèpiáo) governs insect and snake poison. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 女青 (nǚqīng), flavor acrid, neutral.
