## 梅實

### Source Text

又，梅根，療風痹，出土者殺人。梅實，利筋脈，去痹。《本經》原文：梅實，味酸，平。

### Literal Translation

Also, the root of the plum (梅根) treats wind bi (風痹); those that emerge from the ground kill people (出土者殺人). Meishi (梅實, plum fruit) benefits the sinews and vessels (利筋脈) and removes bi (去痹). The original text of the Benjing: Meishi (梅實), sour in flavor, neutral.
