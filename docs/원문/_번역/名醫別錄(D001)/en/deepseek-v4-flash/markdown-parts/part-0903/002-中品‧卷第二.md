## 零羊角

### Source Text

味苦，微寒，無毒。主治傷寒，時氣寒熱，熱在肌膚，溫風注毒伏在骨間，除郁，狂越，僻謬，及食噎不通。久服強筋骨，輕身，起陰，益氣，利丈夫。

### Literal Translation

Bitter in flavor, slightly cold, non-toxic. It treats cold damage (傷寒), seasonal qi with chills and fever (時氣寒熱), heat in the skin and flesh (肌膚), warm-wind-zhu (溫風注) poison lurking in the bones (骨間), dispelling depression, mania and agitation (狂越), depravity and perversion (僻謬), and food-stuck blockage of the throat (食噎不通). Taken for a long time it strengthens the sinews and bones, lightens the body, raises the genitals (起陰), boosts qi (益氣), and benefits men.
