## 木香

### Source Text

溫，無毒。治氣劣，肌中偏寒，主氣不足，消毒，殺鬼、精物、溫瘧、蠱毒，行藥之久服輕身致神仙。一名蜜香。

Warm, non-toxic. It treats inferior qi, unilateral cold in the flesh, and insufficiency of qi; it resolves poison, kills ghosts, essence-things, warm malaria [溫瘧] and gu poison [蠱毒], and conducts the medicines. Taken over a long time, it lightens the body and reaches the spirit immortals. One name is Mixiang [蜜香].
