## 空青

### Source Text

味酸，大寒，無毒。主益肝氣，治目赤痛，去膚翳，止淚出，利水道，下乳汁，通關破堅積。久服令人不忘，志高神仙。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sour, greatly cold, non-toxic. It mainly boosts liver qi, treats red and painful eyes, removes superficial nebula [膚翳], stops tearing, frees the water passage, promotes the descent of milk, and opens the orifices and breaks hard accumulations. Taken over a long time, it makes one not forget, and raises the will to become a spirit immortal.
