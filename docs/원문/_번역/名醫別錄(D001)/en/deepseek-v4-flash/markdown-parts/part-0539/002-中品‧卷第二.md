## 玄石

### Source Text

味鹹，溫，無毒。主治大人小兒驚癇，女子絕孕，少腹寒痛，少精、身重。服之令人子。

### Literal Translation

Flavor salty, warm, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat fright-epilepsy (驚癇) in adults and children, women's barrenness (絕孕), cold pain of the lower abdomen, scant essence, and heaviness of body. Taking it enables one to bear children.
