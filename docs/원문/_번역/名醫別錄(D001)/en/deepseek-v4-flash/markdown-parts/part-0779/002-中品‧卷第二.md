## 井中苔及萍

### Source Text

大寒。主治漆瘡，熱瘡，水腫。井中藍、殺野葛、巴豆諸毒。

### Literal Translation

Greatly cold. Its chief actions are to treat lacquer-sores (漆瘡), heat sores, and edema. The moss within the well, 井中藍, kills the poisons of wild yam-arrowroot (野葛) and croton (巴豆).
