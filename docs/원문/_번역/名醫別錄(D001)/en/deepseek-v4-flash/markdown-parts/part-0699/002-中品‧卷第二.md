## 瞿麥

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：瞿麥，味苦，寒。主關格諸癃結，小便不通，出刺，決癰腫，明目去翳，破胎墮子，下閉血。一名巨句麥。

### Literal Translation

) Original text of the Benjing: Dianthus (瞿麥), flavor bitter, cold. It chiefly treats the blocked strangurias of bar and griddle (關格諸癃結), difficult urination, removes thorns, bursts abscesses and swellings, brightens the eyes and removes film, breaks the fetus and aborts the child, and down-drives blocked blood. One name is 巨句麥.
