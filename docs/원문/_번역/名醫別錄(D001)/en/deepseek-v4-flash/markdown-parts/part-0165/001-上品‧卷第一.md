## 絡石

### Source Text

《本經》原文：絡石，味苦，溫。主風熱死肌癰傷，口乾舌焦，癰腫不消，喉舌腫，水漿不下。久服，輕身

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Trachelospermum [絡石], flavor bitter, warm. It mainly treats wind-heat dead flesh and abscess wounds, dry mouth and parched tongue, abscesses and swellings that do not resolve, swelling of the throat and tongue, and failure of water and gruel to go down. Taken over a long time, it lightens the body
