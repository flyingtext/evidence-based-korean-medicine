## 王瓜

### Source Text

《本經》原文：王瓜，味苦，寒。主消渴內痹，瘀血月閉，寒熱酸疼，益氣愈聾。一名土瓜。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Cucumber (王瓜), flavor bitter, cold. It chiefly treats wasting-thirst and internal impediment, static blood with amenorrhea, cold-heat with soreness, boosts qi and cures deafness. One name is 土瓜.
