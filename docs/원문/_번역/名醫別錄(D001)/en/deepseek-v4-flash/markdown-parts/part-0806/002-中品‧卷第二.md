## 艾葉

### Source Text

味苦，微溫，無毒。主炙百病，可作煎，止下痢，吐血，下部　瘡，婦人漏血，利陰生肌肉，辟風寒，使人有子。一名冰台，一名醫草。

### Literal Translation

Bitter in flavor, slightly warm, non-toxic. It treats moxibustion for all diseases, can be made into a decoction, stops dysentery with discharge, vomiting of blood, lower-body sores, women's leaking blood (漏血), benefits the genitals (陰), produces flesh, wards off wind and cold, and causes people to have children. One name is Bingtai (冰台), one name is Yicao (醫草).
