## 蟹

### Source Text

有毒。解結散血，愈漆瘡，養筋益氣。爪，主破胞，墮胎。

Poisonous. It resolves knotting and disperses blood (解結散血), heals lacquer-sores (漆瘡), and nourishes the sinews and boosts qi (養筋益氣). The claw (爪) treats breaking the afterbirth (破胞) and aborts the fetus (墮胎).
