## 女菀

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：女菀，味辛，溫。主風寒洗洗，霍亂泄利，腸鳴上下無常處，驚癇寒熱百疾。生川穀。

### Literal Translation

)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 女菀 (nǚwǎn), flavor acrid, warm. It governs wind-cold with washing [sweating] (風寒洗洗), cholera with diarrhea (霍亂泄利), intestinal rumbling with no fixed place above and below (腸鳴上下無常處), and the hundred diseases of fright-epilepsy with cold and heat (驚癇寒熱百疾). It grows in river valleys.
