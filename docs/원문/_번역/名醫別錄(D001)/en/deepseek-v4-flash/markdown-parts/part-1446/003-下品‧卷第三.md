## 木甘草

### Source Text

主治癰腫盛熱，煮洗之。生木間，三月生，大葉如蛇床，四四相值，但折枝種之便五月華白，實核赤。三月三日采。

### Literal Translation

It governs abscesses and swellings with abundant heat; boil and wash with it. It grows among trees, born in the third month, with large leaves like Shechuang, four facing four; merely break off a branch and plant it, and in the fifth month the flowers are white and the fruit core is red. Gather on the third day of the third month.
