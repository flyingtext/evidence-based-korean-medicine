## 狸骨

### Source Text

味甘，溫，無毒。主治風疰、尸疰、鬼疰，毒瓦斯在皮中淫躍如針刺者，心腹痛走無常及鼠　惡瘡。頭骨尤良。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, warm, non-toxic. It treats wind-zhu (風疰), corpse-zhu (尸疰), ghost-zhu (鬼疰), poison vapor within the skin that leaps and darts like needle-pricks (毒瓦斯在皮中淫躍如針刺者), heart-abdominal pain that moves irregularly with no constancy (心腹痛走無常), and rat [fistula] (鼠瘻) and malignant sores (惡瘡). The skull (頭骨) is especially good.
