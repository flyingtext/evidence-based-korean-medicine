## 淮木

### Source Text

《本經》原文：淮木，味苦，平。主久咳上氣，腸中虛羸，女子陰蝕漏下，赤白沃。一名百歲城中木。

### Literal Translation

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 淮木 (huáimù), flavor bitter, neutral. It governs long-standing cough with upward counterflow (久咳上氣), deficiency and emaciation in the intestines (腸中虛羸), and erosion and leaking below in women (女子陰蝕漏下) with red and white discharge (赤白沃). One name is 百歲城中木 (bǎisuìchéngzhōngmù).
