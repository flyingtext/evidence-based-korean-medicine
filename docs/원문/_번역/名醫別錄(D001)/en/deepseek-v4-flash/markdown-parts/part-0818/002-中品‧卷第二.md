## 海藻

### Source Text

生東海，七月七日采曝乾。（反甘草。）

《本經》原文：海藻，味苦，寒。

### Literal Translation

It grows in the East Sea; it is gathered on the seventh day of the seventh month and sun-dried. (It is incompatible with gancao (甘草).)

The original text of the Benjing: Haizao (海藻), bitter in flavor, cold in nature.
