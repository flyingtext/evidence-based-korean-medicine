## 芫花

### Source Text

味苦，微溫，有小毒。消胸中痰水，喜唾，水腫，五水在五臟皮膚，及腰痛，下寒毒毒。久服令人虛。

### Literal Translation

Flavor bitter, slightly warm, slightly toxic. It disperses phlegm-water in the chest (胸中痰水), excessive salivation (喜唾), edema (水腫), the five waters in the five viscera and skin (五水在五臟皮膚), and low back pain, and descends cold-toxin and poison (下寒毒毒). Taken for a long time it makes one deficient.
