## 桃核

### Source Text

桃華，味苦，平，無毒。主除水氣，破石淋，利大小便，下三蟲，悅澤人面。三月三日采，陰乾。

### Literal Translation

Peach blossom, flavor bitter, neutral, non-toxic. It removes water qi, breaks stone strangury, benefits the large and small intestines, sends down the three worms, and makes the face of people lustrous. Gather on the third day of the third month and dry in the shade.
