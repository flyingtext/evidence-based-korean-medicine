## 柳華

### Source Text

一名柳絮。葉，主馬疥痂瘡；實，主潰癰，逐膿血，子汁，療渴。生川澤。

### Literal Translation

One name is 柳絮 (liǔxù). The leaves govern horse scabies and scab sores; the fruit governs ulcerating carbuncles (潰癰) and expels pus and blood (逐膿血); the juice of the seeds treats thirst (療渴). It grows in rivers and marshes.
