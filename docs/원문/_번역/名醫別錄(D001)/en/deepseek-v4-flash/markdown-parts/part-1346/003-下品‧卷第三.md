## 飛廉

### Source Text

主骨節熱，脛重酸疼。久服令人身輕。一名飛輕。

### Literal Translation

It governs heat of the bones and joints (骨節熱) and heaviness and soreness of the shins (脛重酸疼). Taken for a long time it makes the body light (令人身輕). One name is 飛輕 (fēiqīng).
