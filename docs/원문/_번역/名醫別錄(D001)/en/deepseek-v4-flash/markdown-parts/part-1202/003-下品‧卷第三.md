## 商陸

### Source Text

味酸，有毒。主治胸中邪氣，水腫，痿痹，腹滿洪直，疏五臟，散水氣。如人形者，神。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sour, toxic. It governs evil-qi in the chest (胸中邪氣), edema (水腫), atrophy-bi (痿痹), abdominal fullness and great straightness (腹滿洪直), dredges the five viscera, and disperses water-qi (散水氣). Those shaped like a person are numinous.
