## 白僵蠶

### Source Text

四月取自勿令中濕，濕有毒，不可用。又，末之，封丁腫，根當自出，極效。《本經》原文：白僵蠶，味鹹。

### Literal Translation

It is taken in the fourth month; do not let it become damp; when damp it is poisonous and must not be used. Also, powdered (末之), it seals ding-swelling (丁腫), and the root will emerge of itself; it is extremely effective. The original text of the Benjing: Baijiangcan (白僵蠶), salty in flavor.
