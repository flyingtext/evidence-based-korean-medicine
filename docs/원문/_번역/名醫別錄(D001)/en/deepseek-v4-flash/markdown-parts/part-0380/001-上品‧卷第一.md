## 牛黃

### Source Text

有小毒。主治小兒百病，諸癇，熱口不開，大人狂癲。又墮胎，久服輕身，增年，令人不忘。

Slightly toxic. It chiefly treats the hundred diseases of children, all epilepsies, heat with the mouth not opening, and mania and epilepsy in adults. It also aborts the fetus; taken over a long time it lightens the body, increases years, and makes one not forget.
