## 白石脂

### Source Text

味甘、酸，平，無毒。主養肺氣，濃腸，補骨髓，治五臟驚悸不足，心下煩，止腹下水，小腸　熱溏，便膿血，女子崩中漏下，赤白沃，排癰疽瘡痔。久服安心，不飢，輕長年。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet and sour, neutral, non-toxic. It mainly nourishes lung qi, thickens the intestines, supplements the bone marrow, treats palpitations and insufficiency of the five viscera, vexation below the heart, stops water below the abdomen, heat and loose stool of the small intestine, discharge of pus and blood, uterine bleeding [崩中] with leaking discharge in women, red and white discharge, and expels abscesses, deep-rooted sores, sores and hemorrhoids. Taken over a long time, it settles the heart, does not hunger, and lightens the body and prolongs life.
