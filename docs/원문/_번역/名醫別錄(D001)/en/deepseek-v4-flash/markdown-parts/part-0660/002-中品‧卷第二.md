## 續斷

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：續斷，味苦，微溫。主傷寒，補不足，金創癰傷折跌，續筋骨，婦人乳難。久服益氣力。

### Literal Translation

) Original text of the Benjing: Dipsacus (續斷), flavor bitter, slightly warm. It chiefly treats cold-damage, supplements insufficiency, metal-wound and ulcer-injury with fracture and sprain, rejoins sinew and bone, and women's difficult lactation. Taken over a long time it boosts qi and strength.
