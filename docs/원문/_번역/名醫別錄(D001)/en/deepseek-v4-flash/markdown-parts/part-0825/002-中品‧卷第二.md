## 乾薑

### Source Text

主治傷寒頭痛、鼻塞，咳逆上氣，止嘔吐。生犍為及荊州、揚州。九月又，生薑，微溫，辛，歸五臟。

### Literal Translation

It treats cold damage (傷寒) headache and nasal congestion, cough with rebellious qi (咳逆上氣), and stops vomiting. It grows in Jianwei and in Jingzhou and Yangzhou. In the ninth month [gathered]. Also, fresh ginger (生薑), slightly warm, acrid, enters the five viscera (五臟).
