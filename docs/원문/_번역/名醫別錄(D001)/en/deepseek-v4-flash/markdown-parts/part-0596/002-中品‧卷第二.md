### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：五味子，味酸，溫。主益氣，咳逆上氣，勞傷羸瘦，補不足，強陰，益男子精。生山谷。

) Original text of the Benjing: Schisandra (五味子), flavor sour, warm. It chiefly boosts qi, treats cough with counter-flow ascent of qi, taxation-damage with emaciation and weakness, supplements insufficiency, strengthens yin, and boosts men's essence. It grows in mountain valleys.
