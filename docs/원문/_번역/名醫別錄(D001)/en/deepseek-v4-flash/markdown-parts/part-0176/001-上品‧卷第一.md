## 卷柏

### Source Text

味甘，平，微寒，無毒。止咳逆，治脫肛，散淋結，頭中風眩，痿蹶，強陰，益精。久服令人好容體。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, neutral, slightly cold, non-toxic. It stops cough and counterflow, treats prolapse of the anus, disperses strangury binding, wind dizziness of the head, wilting and paralysis [痿蹶], strengthens yin, and boosts essence. Taken over a long time, it makes one have a good appearance and body.
