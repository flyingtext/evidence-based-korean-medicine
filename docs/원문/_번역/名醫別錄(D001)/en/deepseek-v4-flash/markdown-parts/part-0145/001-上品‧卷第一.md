## 甘草

### Source Text

二月、八月除日采根，曝乾。十日成。（朮、乾漆、苦參為之使，遠志，反大戟、芫花、甘遂、海藻。

### Literal Translation

Gather the root on the chu day of the second and eighth months and dry in the sun. It is complete in ten days. (Atractylodes [朮], dried lacquer [乾漆] and Sophora flavescens [苦參] are its envoys; [it is averse to] Polygala [遠志], and is incompatible with Euphorbia pekinensis [大戟], Daphne genkwa flower [芫花], Euphorbia kansui [甘遂] and Sargassum [海藻].
