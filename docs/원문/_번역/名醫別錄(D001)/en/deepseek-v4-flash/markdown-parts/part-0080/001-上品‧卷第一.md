## 六芝

### Source Text

（薯蕷為之使，得發良，得麻子仁、白瓜子、牡桂共人，惡恆山，畏扁青、茵陳蒿。）

《本經》原文：赤芝，味苦，平。主胸中結，益心氣，補中，增智慧，不忘。

### Literal Translation

(Dioscorea [薯蕷] is its envoy; with fa [發] it is good, and with hemp seed [麻子仁], white melon seed [白瓜子] and Mu Gui [牡桂] it enters people; it is averse to Dichroa [恆山] and fears flat azurite [扁青] and Artemisia capillaris [茵陳蒿].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Red lingzhi [赤芝], flavor bitter, neutral. It mainly treats binding in the chest, boosts heart qi, supplements the center, increases wisdom, and prevents forgetfulness.
