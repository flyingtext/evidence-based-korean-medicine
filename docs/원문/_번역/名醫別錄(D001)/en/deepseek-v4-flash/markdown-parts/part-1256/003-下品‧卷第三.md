## 半夏

### Source Text

五月、八月采根，曝乾。（射干為之使，惡皂莢，畏雄黃、生薑、秦皮、龜甲，反烏頭。）

《本經》原文：半夏，味辛，平。

### Literal Translation

Gathered as root in the fifth and eighth months, dried in the sun. (射干 (yègān) is its envoy; it fears 皂莢 (zàojiá); it fears 雄黃 (xiónghuáng), 生薑 (shēngjiāng), 秦皮 (qínpí), and 龜甲 (guījiǎ); it is incompatible with 烏頭 (wūtóu).)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 半夏 (bànxià), flavor acrid, neutral.
