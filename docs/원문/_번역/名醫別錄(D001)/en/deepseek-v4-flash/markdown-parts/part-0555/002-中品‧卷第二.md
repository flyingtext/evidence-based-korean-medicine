## 鉛丹

### Source Text

止小便利，除毒熱臍攣，金瘡溢血。生蜀郡。一名鉛華，生於鉛。

It stops excess urination (小便利), dispels toxic heat, navel spasm, and blood effusing from metal wounds (金瘡溢血). It grows in Shujun. One name is 鉛華; it is born from lead.
