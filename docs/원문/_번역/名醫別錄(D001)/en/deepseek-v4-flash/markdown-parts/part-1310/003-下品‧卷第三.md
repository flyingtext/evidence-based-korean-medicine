## 貫眾

### Source Text

（　菌為之使。）

《本經》原文：貫眾，味苦，微寒。主腹中邪熱氣，諸毒，殺三蟲。

### Literal Translation

(菌 (xùn) is its envoy.)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 貫眾 (guànzhòng), flavor bitter, slightly cold. It governs evil heat-qi in the abdomen (腹中邪熱氣), the various poisons (諸毒), and kills the three worms (殺三蟲).
