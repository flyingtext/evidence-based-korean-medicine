## 桂

### Source Text

味甘、辛，大熱，有毒。主溫中，利肝肺氣，心腹寒熱，冷疾，霍亂，轉筋，頭痛，腰痛，出汗，止煩，止唾、咳嗽、鼻　，能墮胎，堅骨節，通血脈，理疏不足，宣導百藥，無所畏。久服神仙，不老。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet and pungent, greatly hot, toxic. It mainly warms the center, benefits the qi of the liver and lungs, [treats] cold and heat of the heart and abdomen, cold diseases, cholera [霍亂], cramping of the sinews, headache, lower back pain, promotes sweating, stops vexation, stops salivation, cough and nasal [discharge], can abort the fetus, firms the bones and joints, frees the blood vessels, regulates insufficiency, and conducts and guides the hundred medicines without fear. Taken over a long time, it makes one a spirit immortal and does not age.
