## 薰草

### Source Text

味甘，平，無毒。主治明目，止淚，治泄精，去臭惡氣，傷寒頭痛，上氣，腰痛。蕙草。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, neutral, non-toxic. It treats brightening the eyes, stopping tears, treating seminal discharge (泄精), dispelling foul and malignant qi (臭惡氣), cold damage (傷寒) headache, rebellious qi (上氣), and lumbar pain. [It is] the Huicao (蕙草).
