## 雲母

### Source Text

一名云沙，色青黃。一名磷石，色正白。生太山、齊廬山，及琅琊北定山石間，二月采。

One name is Cloud Sand [云沙], blue-yellow in color. One name is Phosphor Stone [磷石], pure white in color. It grows on Mount Tai, Mount Qi-Lushan, and among the rocks of the northern Ding Mountains of Langya; gather it in the second month.
