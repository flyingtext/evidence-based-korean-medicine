## 蜣螂

### Source Text

《本經》原文：蜣螂，味鹹，寒。主小兒驚癇螈　，腹脹寒熱，大人癲疾狂易。一名蜣。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Qianglang, flavor salty, cold. It governs children's fright epilepsy with convulsions, abdominal distension with chills and fever, and adults' epilepsy and mania. One name is Qiang.
