## 吳唐草

### Source Text

味甘，平，無毒。主輕身，益氣長年。生故稻草中，夜日有光，草中有膏。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, neutral, non-toxic. It chiefly lightens the body, boosts qi, and prolongs years. It grows among old rice straw; at night and by day it has light, and there is oil within the grass.
