## 白並

### Source Text

葉如小竹根黃白皮。生山陵。三月、四月采根，曝乾。

### Literal Translation

The leaves resemble small bamboo; the root is yellow-white in bark. It grows on mountain ridges. The root is gathered in the third and fourth months and sun-dried.
