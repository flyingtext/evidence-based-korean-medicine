## 蠐螬

### Source Text

取無時，反行者良。（蜚虻為之使，惡附子。）

《本經》原文：蠐螬，味鹹，微溫。

### Literal Translation

It is taken at any season; those that travel backward (反行者) are good. (Feimang (蜚虻) is its envoy (使); it is averse to fuzi (附子).)

The original text of the Benjing: Qicao (蠐螬), salty in flavor, slightly warm.
