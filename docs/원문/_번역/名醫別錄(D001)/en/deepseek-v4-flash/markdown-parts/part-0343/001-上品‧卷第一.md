## 榆皮

### Source Text

二月采皮，取白曝乾。八月采實，並勿令中濕，濕則傷人。《本經》原文：榆皮，味甘，平。

### Literal Translation

The bark is harvested in the second month, taking the white and drying it in the sun. The fruit is harvested in the eighth month; in both cases do not let it become damp, for if damp it injures people. Original text of the Benjing: Yupi (Elm bark), sweet in flavor, neutral.
