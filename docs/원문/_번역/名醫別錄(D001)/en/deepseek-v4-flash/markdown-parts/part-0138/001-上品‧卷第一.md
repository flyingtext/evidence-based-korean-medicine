## 薯蕷

### Source Text

平，無毒。主治頭面游風、風頭、眼眩，下氣，止腰痛，補虛勞、羸瘦，充五臟，熱，強陰。秦楚名玉延，鄭越名土　。

### Literal Translation

Neutral, non-toxic. It mainly treats wandering wind [游風] of the head and face, wind headache [風頭], dizziness of the eyes, sends qi downward, stops lower back pain, supplements deficiency exhaustion and emaciation, fills the five viscera, [removes] heat, and strengthens yin. In Qin and Chu it is called Yuyan [玉延], in Zheng and Yue it is called Tu [土].
