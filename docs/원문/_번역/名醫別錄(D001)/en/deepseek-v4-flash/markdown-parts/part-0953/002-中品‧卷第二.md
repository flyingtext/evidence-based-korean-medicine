## 虎骨

### Source Text

主除邪惡氣，殺鬼疰毒，止驚悸，治惡瘡，鼠　，頭骨尤良。膏，治狗嚙瘡。爪，辟魅。

### Literal Translation

It treats dispelling evil qi (除邪惡氣), killing ghost-zhu poison (殺鬼疰毒), stopping fright-palpitation (驚悸), curing malignant sores (惡瘡), rat [fistula] (鼠瘻); the skull (頭骨) is especially good. The ointment (膏) treats dog-bite sores (狗嚙瘡). The claw (爪) wards off ghosts (辟魅).
