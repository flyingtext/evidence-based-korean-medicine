## 薤

### Source Text

味苦，無毒。歸骨，菜芝也。除寒熱，去水氣，溫中，散結，利病患，諸瘡中風寒水腫以塗之。

### Literal Translation

Bitter in flavor, non-toxic. It returns to the bones (歸骨) and is the xie of vegetables (菜芝). It removes chills and fever, removes water qi (去水氣), warms the center (溫中), disperses bindings (散結), benefits sick people (利病患), and is smeared on all sores with wind-cold-water-swelling (諸瘡中風寒水腫).
