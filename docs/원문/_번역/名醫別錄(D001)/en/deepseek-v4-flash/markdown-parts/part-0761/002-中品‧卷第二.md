## 紫草

### Source Text

三月采根，陰乾

《本經》原文：紫草，味苦，寒。主心腹邪氣，五疸，補中益氣，利九竅，通水道。一名紫丹，一名紫

### Literal Translation

Gather the root in the third month, dry in the shade. Original text of the Benjing: Purple gromwell (紫草), flavor bitter, cold. It chiefly treats heart and abdominal pathogenic qi, the five jaundices, supplements the center and boosts qi, benefits the nine orifices, and frees the waterway. One name is 紫丹, one name 紫[草].
