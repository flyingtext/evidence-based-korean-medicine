## 知母

### Source Text

無毒。主治傷寒久瘧煩熱，脅下邪氣，膈中惡，及風汗內疸。多服令人泄。

Not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat cold-damage and chronic malaria with vexation and heat, pathogenic qi below the hypochondrium, evil in the diaphragm, and wind-sweat with internal jaundice. Taken in excess it causes diarrhea.
