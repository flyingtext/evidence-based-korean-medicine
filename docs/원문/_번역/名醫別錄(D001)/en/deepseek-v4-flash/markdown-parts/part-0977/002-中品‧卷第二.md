## 石龍子

### Source Text

主五癃邪結氣，破石淋，下血，利小便水道。一名蜥蜴。生川穀。

### Literal Translation

It treats the five stranguries (五癃), binding evil qi (邪結氣), breaking stone strangury (石淋), sending down blood, and benefiting urination and the water-course (利小便水道). One name is Xiyi (蜥蜴). It grows in streams and valleys.
