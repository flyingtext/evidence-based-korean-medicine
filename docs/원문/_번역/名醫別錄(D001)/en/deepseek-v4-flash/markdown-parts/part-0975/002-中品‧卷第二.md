## 石龍子

### Source Text

有小毒。一名山龍子，一名守宮，一名石蜴。生平陽及荊山山石間。

### Literal Translation

Slightly poisonous. One name is Shanlongzi (山龍子), one name is Shougong (守宮), one name is Shiyi (石蜴). It grows among the rocks and mountains of Pingyang and Mount Jing (荊山).
