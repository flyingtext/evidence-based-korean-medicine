## 皂莢

### Source Text

有小毒。主治腹脹滿，消穀，破咳嗽囊結，婦人胞下落，明目，益精。可為沐藥，不湯。

### Literal Translation

Slightly toxic. It governs abdominal distension and fullness (腹脹滿), digests grain (消穀), breaks cough and sac bindings (破咳嗽囊結), [treats] the afterbirth falling in women (婦人胞下落), brightens the eyes, and benefits essence (益精). It may be made into a hair-washing medicine; it must not [enter] a decoction.
