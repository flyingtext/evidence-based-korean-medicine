## 白膠

### Source Text

溫，無毒。主治吐血，下血，崩中不止，四肢酸疼，多汗，淋露，折跌傷損。生云中，煮鹿角作之。

### Literal Translation

Warm, non-toxic. It chiefly treats vomiting of blood, downward bleeding, uterine collapse (bengzhong) that does not stop, soreness and pain of the four limbs, much sweating, dripping and leaking, and injuries from falls and fractures. It grows in Yunzhong; made by boiling deer antler.
