## 柏木

### Source Text

根，名檀桓，治腹百病，安魂魄，不飢渴。久服輕身，延年通神。生漢中及永昌。

### Literal Translation

The root, named 檀桓, treats the hundred diseases of the abdomen, calms the hun-po, and produces no hunger or thirst. Taken over a long time it lightens the body and prolongs years, communicating with the spirit-shen. It grows in Hanzhong and Yongchang.
