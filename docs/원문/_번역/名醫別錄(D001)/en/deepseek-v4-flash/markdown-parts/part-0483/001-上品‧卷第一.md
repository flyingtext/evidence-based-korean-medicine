## 莧實

### Source Text

生淮陽及田中，葉如藍，月采。《本經》原文：莧實，味甘，寒。主青盲，明目，除邪，利大小便，去寒熱。

### Literal Translation

It grows in Huaiyang and in the fields; the leaves are like indigo, harvested in the ... month. Original text of the Benjing: Xianshi (amaranth seed), sweet in flavor, cold. It chiefly treats green blindness (qingmang), brightens the eyes, removes evil, frees urination and defecation, and removes cold-heat.
