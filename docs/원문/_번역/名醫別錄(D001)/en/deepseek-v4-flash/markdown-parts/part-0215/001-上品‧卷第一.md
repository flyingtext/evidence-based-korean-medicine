## 酸棗

### Source Text

無毒。主治煩心不得眠，臍上下痛，血轉，久泄，虛汗，煩渴，補中，益肝氣，堅筋骨，助陰氣，令人肥健。生河東。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It mainly treats vexation of the heart with inability to sleep, pain above and below the navel, turning of blood, prolonged diarrhea, deficiency sweating, vexation and thirst, supplements the center, boosts liver qi, firms the sinews and bones, assists yin qi, and makes one fat and strong. It grows in Hedong.
