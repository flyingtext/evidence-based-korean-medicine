## 雀卵

### Source Text

又，雀矢和男首子乳，如薄泥，點目中　肉赤脈貫瞳子上者，即消。

### Literal Translation

Also, mix sparrow droppings (雀矢) with the milk of a woman's first, eldest child; when of the consistency of thin mud, apply it to fleshy growths (胬肉) and red vessels in the eye that pierce through the pupil (赤脈貫瞳子), and they are immediately eliminated.
