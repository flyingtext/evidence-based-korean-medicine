## 虎掌

### Source Text

二月、八月采，陰乾。（蜀漆為之惡莽草。）

《本經》原文：虎掌，味苦，溫。

### Literal Translation

Gathered in the second and eighth months, dried in the shade. (蜀漆 (shǔqī) is its envoy; it fears 莽草 (mǎngcǎo).)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 虎掌 (hǔzhǎng), flavor bitter, warm.
