## 蜂子

### Source Text

微寒，無毒。主治心腹痛，大人小兒腹中五蟲口吐出者，面目黃。久服輕身益氣。

### Literal Translation

Slightly cold, non-toxic. It chiefly treats pain of the heart and abdomen, the five worms in the abdomen of adults and children that are vomited out through the mouth, and yellowing of the face and eyes. Taken over a long time it lightens the body and boosts qi.
