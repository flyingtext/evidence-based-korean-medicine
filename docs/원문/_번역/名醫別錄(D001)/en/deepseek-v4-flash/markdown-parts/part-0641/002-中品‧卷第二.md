## 竹葉

### Source Text

芹竹葉大寒，無毒。主除煩熱，風　，喉　，嘔逆。根，消毒。

### Literal Translation

Qin-bamboo leaf (芹竹葉) is greatly cold, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to dispel vexation and heat, wind [disease], throat [impediment], and vomiting with counter-flow. The root resolves poisons.
