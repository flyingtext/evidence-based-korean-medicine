## 長石

### Source Text

生長子及太山及臨淄，采無時。《本經》原文：長石，味辛，寒。主身熱四肢寒厥，利小便，通血脈，明目去翳⿰牜⿱宀隹，下三蟲

### Literal Translation

It grows at 長子 and Mount Tai and Linzi; gather without a fixed time. Original text of the Benjing: Long-stone (長石), flavor pungent, cold. It chiefly treats body heat and cold cyanotic extremities (寒厥) of the four limbs, benefits urination, courses the blood vessels, brightens the eyes and removes film [over the eyes], and down-drives the three worms.
