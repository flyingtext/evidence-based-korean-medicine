## 犀角

### Source Text

味鹹，酸，微寒，無毒。主治傷寒，溫疫，頭痛，寒熱，諸毒瓦斯。久服駿健。

### Literal Translation

Salty and sour in flavor, slightly cold, non-toxic. It treats cold damage (傷寒), warm epidemic (溫疫), headache, chills and fever, and various poison vapors (諸毒瓦斯). Taken for a long time it makes one vigorous and strong (駿健).
