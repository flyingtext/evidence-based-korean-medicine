## 異草

### Source Text

味甘，無毒，主治痿痹，寒熱，去黑子。生蘺木上，葉如葵，莖傍有角，汁白。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, non-toxic. It treats wei and bi (痿痹), chills and fever, and removes black moles (黑子). It grows on the li (蘺) tree; the leaves resemble the mallow (葵), alongside the stem there are horns, and the juice is white.
