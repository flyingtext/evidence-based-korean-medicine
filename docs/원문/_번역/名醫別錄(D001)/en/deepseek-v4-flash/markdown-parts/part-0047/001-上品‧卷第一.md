## 紫石英

### Source Text

味辛，無毒。主治上氣心腹痛，寒熱、邪氣、結氣，補心氣不足，下焦，止消渴，除胃中久寒，散癰腫，令人悅澤。生太山，采無時。

### Literal Translation

Flavor pungent, non-toxic. It mainly treats ascent of qi, heart and abdominal pain, cold and heat, evil qi and bound qi, supplements insufficiency of heart qi, the lower burner, stops wasting-thirst [消渴], removes prolonged cold in the stomach, disperses abscesses and swellings, and makes one lustrous. It grows on Mount Tai; gather it at any time.
