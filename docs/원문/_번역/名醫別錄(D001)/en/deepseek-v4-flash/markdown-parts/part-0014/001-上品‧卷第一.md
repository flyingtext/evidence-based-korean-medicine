## 空青

### Source Text

生益州及越　山有銅處。銅精熏則生空青，其腹中三月中旬采，亦無時。《本經》原文：空青，味甘，寒。

### Literal Translation

It grows in Yizhou and in the mountains containing copper in Yue. When the essence of copper is smoked it produces hollow azurite [空青]; gather it in the middle ten days of the third month from within its belly, or at any time. Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Hollow azurite [空青], flavor sweet, cold.
