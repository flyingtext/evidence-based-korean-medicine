## 鉛丹

### Source Text

《本經》原文：鉛丹，味辛，微寒。主吐逆胃反，驚癇癲疾，除熱下氣。煉化還成九光。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Red lead (鉛丹), flavor pungent, slightly cold. It chiefly treats vomiting with stomach counter-flow, fright-epilepsy and manic epilepsy (癲疾), dispels heat and down-drives qi. Refined, it (again) turns back into the ninefold light.
