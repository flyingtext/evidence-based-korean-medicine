## 零羊角

### Source Text

生石城山及華采無時。《本經》原文：零羊角，味鹹寒。主明目，益氣，起陰，去惡血注下，辟蠱毒惡鬼不祥，安心氣，常不魘

### Literal Translation

It grows at Stone-City Mountain (石城山) and Huacai is gathered at any season. The original text of the Benjing: Lingyangjiao (羚羊角), salty-cold in flavor. It treats brightening the eyes, boosting qi (益氣), raising the genitals (起陰), removing malignant blood pouring down (惡血注下), warding off gu-poison and evil ghosts and inauspicious things (蠱毒惡鬼不祥), settling heart qi (安心氣), and keeping one always without nightmares (不魘).
