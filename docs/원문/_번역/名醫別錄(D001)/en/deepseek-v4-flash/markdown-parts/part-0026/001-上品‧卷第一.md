## 石膽

### Source Text

主明目目痛，金創諸癇痙，女子陰蝕痛，石淋寒熱，崩中下血，諸邪毒一名畢石。生山谷。

### Literal Translation

It mainly brightens the eyes and treats eye pain, metal wounds and all kinds of epilepsy and convulsions, painful erosion of the female genitals, stone strangury [石淋] with cold and heat, uterine bleeding [崩中] with downflow of blood, and all kinds of evil poison. One name is Bi stone [畢石]. It grows in mountain valleys.
