## 楝實

### Source Text

生荊山。《本經》原文：楝實，味苦，寒。主溫疾傷寒，大熱煩狂，殺三蟲，疥瘍，利小便水道。

### Literal Translation

It grows on Jingshan. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 楝實 (liànshí), flavor bitter, cold. It governs warm disease and cold-damage (溫疾傷寒), great heat, mania and vexation (大熱煩狂), kills the three worms (殺三蟲), scabies and ulcers (疥瘍), and benefits urination and the water passage (利小便水道).
