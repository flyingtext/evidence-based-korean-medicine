## 蜚蠊

### Source Text

又，蜚蠊，形似蠶蛾，腹下赤。二月、八月采。《本經》原文：蜚蠊，味鹹，寒。

### Literal Translation

Also, the cockroach, its form resembles the silkworm moth, with red beneath the abdomen. Gather in the second and eighth months. Original text of the Benjing: Feilian, flavor salty, cold.
