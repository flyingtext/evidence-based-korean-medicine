## 丹參

### Source Text

無毒。主養血，去心腹痼疾、結氣，腰脊強，腳痹，除風邪留熱。久服利人。

### Literal Translation

Not poisonous. Its chief actions are to nourish the blood, dispel chronic disease of heart and abdomen and bound qi, stiffness of the lumbar spine, impediment of the feet, and dispel wind-pathogen and lingering heat. Taken over a long time it benefits a person.
