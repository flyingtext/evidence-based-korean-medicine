## 茵芋

### Source Text

微溫，有毒陰乾。《本經》原文：茵芋，味苦，溫。主五臟邪氣，心腹寒熱，羸瘦，如瘧狀，發作有時，諸關節風濕痹痛。

### Literal Translation

Slightly warm, toxic, dried in the shade. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 茵芋 (yīnyù), flavor bitter, warm. It governs evil-qi of the five viscera (五臟邪氣), cold and heat of the heart and abdomen (心腹寒熱), emaciation (羸瘦), like the appearance of malaria with attacks at fixed times (如瘧狀，發作有時), and wind-damp bi pain of the various joints (諸關節風濕痹痛).
