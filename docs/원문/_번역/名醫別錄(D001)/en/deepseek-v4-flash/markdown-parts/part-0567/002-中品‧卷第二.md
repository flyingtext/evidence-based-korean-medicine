## 白肌石

### Source Text

味辛，無毒。主強筋骨，止渴，不飢，陰熱不足。一名肌石，一名洞石。

### Literal Translation

Flavor pungent, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to strengthen the sinews and bones, stop thirst, produce no hunger, and (treat) insufficiency of genital-yin heat (陰熱不足). One name is 肌石, one name is 洞石.
