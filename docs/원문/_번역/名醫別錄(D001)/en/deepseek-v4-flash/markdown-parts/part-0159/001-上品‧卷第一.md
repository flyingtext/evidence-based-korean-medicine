## 石龍芻

### Source Text

微溫，無毒。補內虛不足，治痞滿，身無潤澤，出汗，除莖中熱痛，殺鬼疰惡名龍珠，一名龍華，一名懸莞，一名草毒。九節多味者，良。

### Literal Translation

Slightly warm, non-toxic. It supplements internal deficiency and insufficiency, treats fullness [痞滿], lack of moisture and luster of the body, sweating, removes heat and pain in the penis, and kills ghost zhu [鬼疰] and evil. One name is Longzhu [龍珠], one name is Longhua [龍華], one name is Xuanwan [懸莞], one name is Caodu [草毒]. That with many nodes and much flavor is good.
