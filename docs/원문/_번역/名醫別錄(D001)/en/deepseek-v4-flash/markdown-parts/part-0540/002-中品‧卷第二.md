## 玄石

### Source Text

一名玄水石，一名處石。生太山之陽，山陰有銅，銅者雌，玄者雄。（惡松脂、柏實菌桂。

### Literal Translation

One name is 玄水石, one name is 處石. It grows on the southern (yang) side of Mount Tai; on the shaded side of the mountain there is copper; copper is the female (雌), this (玄石) is the male (雄). (Averse to turpentine 松脂, arborvitae seed 柏實 and 菌桂.
