## 膚青

### Source Text

生益州。《本經》原文：膚青，味辛，平。主蠱毒及蛇菜肉諸毒，惡瘡。

It grows in Yizhou. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 膚青 (fūqīng), flavor acrid, neutral. It governs gu-poison (蠱毒) and the various poisons of snake, vegetable, and meat, and malignant sores.
