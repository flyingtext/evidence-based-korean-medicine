## 陽起石

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：陽起石，味鹹，微溫。主崩中漏下，破子臧中血，症瘕結氣，寒熱腹痛，無子，陰痿不起，補不足。一名白石。

### Literal Translation

) Original text of the Benjing: Actinolite (陽起石), flavor salty, slightly warm. It chiefly treats flooding (崩) with dripping discharge (漏下), breaks blood within the uterus (子臧中血), abdominal masses with bound qi, chills and fever with abdominal pain, and childlessness, genital atrophy that does not rise, supplementing what is insufficient. One name is 白石.
