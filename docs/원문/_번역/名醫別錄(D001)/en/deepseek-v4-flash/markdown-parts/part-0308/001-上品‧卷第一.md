## 徐長卿

### Source Text

《本經》原文：徐長卿，味辛，溫。主鬼物百精蠱毒，疫疾邪惡氣，溫瘧。久服強悍輕身。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Xuchangqing (Cynanchum), pungent in flavor, warm. It chiefly treats ghost things, the hundred essences, gu (parasitic) poison, epidemic disease, evil qi, and warm malaria. Taken over a long time it makes one strong and lightens the body.
