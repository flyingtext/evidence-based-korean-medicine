## 芫花

### Source Text

三三日采花，陰乾。（決明為之使，反甘草。）

《本經》原文：芫花，味辛，溫。

### Literal Translation

Gathered as flowers on the third day of the third month, dried in the shade. (決明 (juémíng) is its envoy; it is incompatible with 甘草 (gāncǎo).)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 芫花 (yuánhuā), flavor acrid, warm.
