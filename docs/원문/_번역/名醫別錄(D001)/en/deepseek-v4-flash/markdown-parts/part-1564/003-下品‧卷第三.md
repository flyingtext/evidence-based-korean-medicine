## 螢火

### Source Text

七月七日取，陰乾。《本經》原文：螢火，味辛，微溫。主明目，小兒火瘡傷熱氣，蠱毒鬼注，通神精。

Take it on the seventh day of the seventh month and dry in the shade. Original text of the Benjing: Yinghuo, flavor acrid, slightly warm. It governs brightening the eyes, children's fire sores injured by heat qi, gu poison and ghost zhu, and unblocks the spirit and essence.
