## 肪

### Source Text

主殺石藥毒，解結縛蓄熱。肉，補除熱，和臟腑，利水道。又，鴨肪，主水腫。

### Literal Translation

It chiefly kills the poison of mineral medicines and resolves binding and accumulated heat. The flesh supplements and removes heat, harmonizes the viscera, and frees the water passage. Also, duck fat chiefly treats edema.
