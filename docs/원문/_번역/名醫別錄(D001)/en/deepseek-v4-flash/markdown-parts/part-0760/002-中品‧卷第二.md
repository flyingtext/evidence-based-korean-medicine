## 紫草

### Source Text

無毒。主治腹腫脹滿痛，以合膏，治小兒瘡及面渣。生碭山及楚地。

### Literal Translation

Not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat swelling, distension, fullness, and pain of the abdomen; combined into a plaster, it treats children's sores and facial acne. It grows at Dangshan and in the Chu region.
