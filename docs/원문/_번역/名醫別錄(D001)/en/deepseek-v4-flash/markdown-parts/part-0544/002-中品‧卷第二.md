## 理石

### Source Text

（滑石為之使，畏麻黃。）

《本經》原文：理石，味辛，寒。主身熱，利胃，解煩，益精明目，破積聚，去三蟲。

### Literal Translation

(Talc 滑石 is its envoy; it is in dread of ephedra 麻黃.) Original text of the Benjing: Lithic/理石 stone, flavor pungent, cold. It chiefly treats body heat, benefits the stomach, resolves vexation, boosts essence and brightens the eyes, breaks accumulations, and dispels the three worms.
