## 新雉木

### Source Text

味苦，香，溫，無毒。主治風頭，眩痛，可作沐藥。七月采，陰乾，實如桃。

### Literal Translation

Bitter in flavor, fragrant, warm, non-toxic. It treats wind headache (風頭) and dizziness-pain, and can be made into a hair-washing drug. It is gathered in the seventh month and dried in the shade; the fruit resembles a peach.
