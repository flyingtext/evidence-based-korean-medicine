## 蛞蝓

### Source Text

八月取。《本經》原文：蛞蝓，味鹹，寒。主賊風　僻，軼筋及脫肛，驚癇攣縮。

### Literal Translation

It is gathered in the eighth month. The original text of the Benjing: Kuoyu (蛞蝓), salty in flavor, cold in nature. It treats thief-wind (賊風) deviation (僻), sinew injury (軼筋) and anal prolapse (脫肛), and fright-epilepsy with cramping and contraction (驚癇攣縮).
