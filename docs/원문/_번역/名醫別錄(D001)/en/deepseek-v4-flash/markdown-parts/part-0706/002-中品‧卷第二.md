## 秦皮

### Source Text

（大戟為之惡吳茱萸。）

《本經》原文：秦皮，味苦，微寒。主風寒濕痹，洗洗寒氣，除熱，目中青翳白膜。

### Literal Translation

(Euphorbia 大戟 ... averse to evodia 吳茱萸.) Original text of the Benjing: Fraxinus (秦皮), flavor bitter, slightly cold. It chiefly treats wind-cold-damp impediment, the dripping cold qi, dispels heat, and treats blue films and white membranes of the eyes.
