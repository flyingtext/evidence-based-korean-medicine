## 秦椒

### Source Text

（惡栝蔞、防葵，畏雌黃。）

《本經》原文：秦椒，味辛，溫。主風邪氣，溫中，除寒痹，堅齒發，明目。

### Literal Translation

(It is averse to Gualou (Trichosanthes) and Fangku; it fears Cihuang (orpiment).)

Original text of the Benjing: Qinjiao (Zanthoxylum), pungent in flavor, warm. It chiefly treats wind evil qi, warms the center, removes cold bi (impediment), strengthens the teeth and hair, and brightens the eyes.
