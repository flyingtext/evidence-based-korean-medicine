## 石龍子

### Source Text

五月取，著石干。（惡硫黃、斑蝥、蕪荑。）

《本經》原文：石龍子，味鹹，寒。

### Literal Translation

It is taken in the fifth month and dried on stone (著石干). (Averse to liuhuang (硫黃), banmao (斑蝥), and wuyi (蕪荑).)

The original text of the Benjing: Shilongzi (石龍子), salty in flavor, cold in nature.
