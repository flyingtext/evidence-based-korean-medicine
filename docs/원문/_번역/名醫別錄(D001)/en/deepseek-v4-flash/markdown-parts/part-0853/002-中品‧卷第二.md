## 雀翹

### Source Text

生藍中，葉細黃，莖赤有刺。四月實，黃中黑；五月采，陰乾。

### Literal Translation

It grows among indigo (藍); the leaves are slender and yellow, the stem is red and thorned. In the fourth month it bears fruit, yellow with black within; it is gathered in the fifth month and dried in the shade.
