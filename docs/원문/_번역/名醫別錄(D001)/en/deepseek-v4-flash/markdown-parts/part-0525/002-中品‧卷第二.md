## 石硫黃

### Source Text

《本經》原文：石硫黃，味酸，溫。主婦人陰蝕疽痔惡血，堅筋骨，除頭禿。能化金銀銅鐵奇物。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Sulfur (石硫黃), flavor sour, warm. It chiefly treats women's erosion of the genitals, ulcers and hemorrhoids with foul blood, strengthens sinew and bone, and removes head baldness. It can transform gold, silver, copper and iron into remarkable objects.
