## 石葦

### Source Text

主勞熱邪氣，五癃閉不通利小便水道。一名石　。生山谷石上。

### Literal Translation

It chiefly treats taxation-heat and pathogenic qi, the five strangurias-with-blockage (五癃閉) where urination and waterway are not freed. One name is 石. It grows on rocks of mountain valleys.
