## 白冬瓜

### Source Text

味甘，微寒，主除小腹水脹，利小便，止渴。又，甘瓜子，主腹內結聚，破潰膿血，最為腸胃脾內壅要藥。《本經》原文：白瓜子，味甘，平。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, slightly cold. It chiefly removes water distension of the lower abdomen, promotes urination, and stops thirst. Also, the sweet melon seed chiefly treats binding and accumulation within the abdomen, breaks and drains pus and blood, and is the most important medicine for obstruction within the intestines, stomach, and spleen. Original text of the Benjing: Baiguazi (white melon seed), sweet in flavor, neutral.
