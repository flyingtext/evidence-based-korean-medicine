## 人參

### Source Text

（茯苓為之使，惡溲疏，藜蘆。）

《本經》原文：人參，味甘，微寒。主補五臟，安精神，定魂魄，止驚悸，除邪氣，明目，開心益智。

### Literal Translation

(Poria [茯苓] is its envoy; it is averse to [溲疏] and Veratrum [藜蘆].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Ginseng [人參], flavor sweet, slightly cold. It mainly supplements the five viscera, settles the essence and spirit, fixes the hun and po [魂魄], stops palpitations, removes evil qi, brightens the eyes, and opens the heart and boosts wisdom.
