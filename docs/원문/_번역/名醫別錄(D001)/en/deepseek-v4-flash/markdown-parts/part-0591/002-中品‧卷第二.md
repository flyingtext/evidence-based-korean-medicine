## 黃連

### Source Text

微寒，無毒。主治五臟冷熱，久下泄　、膿血，止消渴、大驚，除水，利骨，調胃，腸，益膽，治口瘡。生巫陽及蜀郡、太山。

### Literal Translation

Slightly cold, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat cold and heat of the five viscera, chronic down-flowing diarrhea with pus and blood, stop wasting-thirst and great fright, dispel water, benefit the bones, harmonize the stomach, [benefit] the intestines, boost the gallbladder, and treat mouth sores. It grows at Wuyang and Shujun and Mount Tai.
