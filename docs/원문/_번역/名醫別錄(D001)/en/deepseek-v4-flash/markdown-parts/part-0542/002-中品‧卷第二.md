## 理石

### Source Text

味甘，大寒，無毒。主除營衛中去來大熱、結熱，解煩毒，止消渴、及中風痿痹。一名肌石。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, greatly cold, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to dispel within the nutrient and defensive (營衛) the coming-and-going great heat and bound heat, resolve vexation and poison, stop wasting-thirst, and (treat) wind-stroke with wilting and impediment (痿痹). One name is 肌石.
