## 朴硝

### Source Text

入地千歲不變，色青白者佳，黃者傷人，赤者殺人。一名硝石朴。生益州有咸水之陽，采無時。

### Literal Translation

Buried in the earth for a thousand years it does not change; that which is blue-white in color is good, the yellow harms people, and the red kills people. One name is Nitre-Pu [硝石朴]. It grows in Yizhou on the sunny side of salt water; gather it at any time.
