## 羊躑躅

### Source Text

生太行山及淮南山。三月采花，陰乾

《本經》原文：羊躑躅，味辛，溫。主賊風在皮膚中淫淫痛，溫瘧惡毒諸痹。

### Literal Translation

It grows on Taihang Mountain and on Huainan Mountain. Gathered as flowers in the third month, dried in the shade.

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 羊躑躅 (yángzhízhú), flavor acrid, warm. It governs thief-wind in the skin with spreading pain (賊風在皮膚中淫淫痛), warm malaria, evil poison, and the various bi (溫瘧惡毒諸痹).
