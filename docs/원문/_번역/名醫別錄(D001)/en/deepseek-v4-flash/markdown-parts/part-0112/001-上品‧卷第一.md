## 麥門冬

### Source Text

（地黃、車前為之使，惡款冬瓠，畏苦參、青　。）

《本經》原文：麥門冬，味甘，平。主心腹結氣，傷中傷飽，胃絡脈絕，羸瘦短氣。

### Literal Translation

(Rehmannia [地黃] and plantain [車前] are its envoys; it is averse to Tussilago gourd [款冬瓠] and fears Sophora flavescens [苦參] and [青].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Ophiopogon [麥門冬], flavor sweet, neutral. It mainly treats bound qi of the heart and abdomen, damage to the center and overeating, severance of the stomach collateral vessels, and emaciation with short breath.
