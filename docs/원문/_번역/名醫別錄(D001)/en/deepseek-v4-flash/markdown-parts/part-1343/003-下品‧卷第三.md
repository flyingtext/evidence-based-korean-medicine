## 飛廉

### Source Text

無毒。主治頭眩頂重，皮間邪風如蜂螫針刺，魚子細起，熱瘡，癰疽，痔，濕痹，止邪，咳嗽，下乳汁。久服益氣，明目，不老。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It governs dizziness of the head and heaviness of the crown (頭眩頂重), evil wind between the skin like bee stings and needle pricks (皮間邪風如蜂螫針刺), fine eruptions like fish roe (魚子細起), heat sores (熱瘡), carbuncle and hemorrhoids (癰疽痔), damp-bi (濕痹), stops evil [qi] (止邪), cough (咳嗽), and descends milk (下乳汁). Taken for a long time it boosts qi (益氣), brightens the eyes (明目), and does not age (不老).
