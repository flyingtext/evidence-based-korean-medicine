## 桑根白皮

### Source Text

無毒。主去肺中水氣，止唾血，熱渴，水腫，腹滿，臚脹，利水道，去寸白，創。采無時，出土上者殺人。

### Literal Translation

Not poisonous. Its chief actions are to dispel water qi within the lungs, stop spitting of blood, heat-thirst, edema, abdominal fullness, dropsy distension (臚脹), benefit the waterway, dispel tapeworms (寸白), and [treat] wounds. Gather without a fixed time; that which emerges above the soil kills a person.

### Translator's Notes

- 出土上者殺人: the root part that comes out of the ground is toxic.
