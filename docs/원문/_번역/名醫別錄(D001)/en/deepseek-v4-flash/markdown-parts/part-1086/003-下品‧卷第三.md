## 青琅

### Source Text

無毒。主治白禿，侵淫在皮膚中。煮煉服之，起陰氣，可化為丹。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It governs white baldness (白禿) and spreading erosion within the skin. When boiled and refined and taken, it raises the yin qi (陰氣) and can be transformed into cinnabar (丹).
