## 昆布

### Source Text

味鹹，寒，無毒。主治十二種水腫，癭瘤聚結氣，　瘡。生東海。

### Literal Translation

Salty in flavor, cold, non-toxic. It treats the twelve kinds of edema (水腫), goiter-lump and accumulated knotting qi (癭瘤聚結氣), and sores. It grows in the East Sea.
