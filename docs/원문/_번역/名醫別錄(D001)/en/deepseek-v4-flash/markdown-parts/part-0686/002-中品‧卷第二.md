## 萆

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主治傷中恚怒，陰痿失溺，關節老血，老人五緩。一名赤節。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat injury of the center with anger-vexation, genital atrophy with loss of urine, old blood in the joints, and the five slownesses (五緩) of the aged. One name is 赤節.
