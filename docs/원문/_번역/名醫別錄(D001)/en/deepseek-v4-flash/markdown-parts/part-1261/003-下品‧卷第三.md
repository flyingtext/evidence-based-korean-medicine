## 牡丹

味苦，微寒，無毒。主除時氣，頭痛，客熱，五勞，勞氣，頭腰痛，風噤，癲疾。生郡及漢中。

Flavor bitter, slightly cold, non-toxic. It governs removing seasonal-qi (時氣), headache (頭痛), guest-heat (客熱), the five exhaustions (五勞), labor-qi (勞氣), head and low back pain (頭腰痛), wind-lockjaw (風噤), and mania disease (癲疾). It grows in 郡 (jùn) and in Hanzhong.
