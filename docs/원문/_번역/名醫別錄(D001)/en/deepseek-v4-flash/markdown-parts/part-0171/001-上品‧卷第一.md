## 龍膽

### Source Text

大寒，無毒。主除胃中伏熱，時氣溫熱，熱泄下痢，去腸中小蠱，益肝膽氣，止驚惕生齊朐及宛朐。二月、八月、十一月、十二月采根，陰乾。

### Literal Translation

Greatly cold, non-toxic. It mainly removes latent heat in the stomach, seasonal qi and warm heat, heat diarrhea and dysentery, removes small worms in the intestines, boosts the qi of the liver and gallbladder, and stops fright and agitation. It grows in Qiju and Wanqu. Gather the root in the second, eighth, eleventh and twelfth months and dry in the shade.
