## 枳實

### Source Text

味酸，微寒，無毒。主除胸脅淡癖，逐停水，破結實，消脹滿、心下急、痞痛、逆氣脅風痛，安胃氣、止溏泄，明目。生河內。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sour, slightly cold, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to dispel phlegm-stasis (淡癖) of the chest and hypochondrium, drive out stagnant water, break firm accumulations, disperse distension and fullness, urgent fullness below the heart, stuffiness pain, counter-flow qi and wind-pain of the hypochondrium, calm stomach qi, stop watery diarrhea (溏泄), and brighten the eyes. It grows in Henei.
