## 麻黃

### Source Text

微溫，無毒。主治五臟邪氣緩急，風脅痛，字乳余疾，止好唾，通腠理，疏傷寒頭痛解肌，泄邪惡氣，消赤黑斑毒。不可多服，令人虛。

### Literal Translation

Slightly warm, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat chronic-relaxation (緩急) of pathogenic qi of the five viscera, wind-pain of the hypochondrium, leftover disease after childbirth (字乳余疾), stop excessive spitting of saliva (好唾), free the interstices (腠理), relax the surface for cold-damage headache, drain pathogenic vile qi, and dissipate red and black pox-toxin (斑毒). It must not be taken in excess, for it causes vacuity.
