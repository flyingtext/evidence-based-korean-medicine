## 柴胡

### Source Text

微寒，無毒。主除傷寒，心下煩熱，諸痰熱結實，胸中邪逆，五臟間游氣，大腸停積脹，及濕痹拘攣，亦可作浴湯。一名山菜，一名茹草。

### Literal Translation

Slightly cold, non-toxic. It mainly removes cold damage [傷寒], vexation and heat below the heart, all phlegm-heat binding and excess, evil counterflow in the chest, wandering qi among the five viscera, accumulation and distension of the large intestine, and damp bi [濕痹] with cramping; it can also be made into a bath decoction. One name is Shancai [山菜], one name is Rucao [茹草].
