## 大麥

### Source Text

味鹹，溫、微寒，無毒。主治消渴，除熱，益氣，調中。又云令人多熱，為五穀長。

### Literal Translation

Salty in flavor, warm and slightly cold, non-toxic. It treats wasting-thirst (消渴), removing heat (除熱), boosting qi (益氣), and regulating the center (調中). It is also said to make people very hot (多熱) and to be the head of the five grains (五穀長).
