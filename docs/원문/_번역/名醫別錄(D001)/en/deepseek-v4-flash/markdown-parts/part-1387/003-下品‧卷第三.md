## 藎草

### Source Text

主久咳上氣喘逆，久寒驚悸，痂疥白禿瘍氣，殺皮膚小蟲。生川穀。

### Literal Translation

It governs long-standing cough with upward counterflow and wheezing (久咳上氣喘逆), long-standing cold with fright and palpitations (久寒驚悸), scabs, scabies, baldness, and ulcer-qi (痂疥白禿瘍氣), and kills small insects of the skin (殺皮膚小蟲). It grows in river valleys.
