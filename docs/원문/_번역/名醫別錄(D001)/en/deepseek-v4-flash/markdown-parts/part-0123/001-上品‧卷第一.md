## 生地黃

### Source Text

大寒。主治婦人崩中血不止，及產後血上薄心、悶絕，傷身、胎動、下血，胎不落，墮墜，踠折，瘀血，留血，衄鼻，吐血，皆搗飲之。一名芐，一名⿰虫忽，一名地脈。

### Literal Translation

Greatly cold. It mainly treats uterine bleeding [崩中] in women with unceasing blood, and postpartum blood rushing upward to oppress the heart with fainting and suffocation, injury to the body, fetal movement and downflow of blood, failure of the fetus to fall, falls and injuries, sprains and fractures, stagnant blood, retained blood, nosebleed, and vomiting of blood; all are pounded and drunk. One name is Hu [芐], one name is [⿰虫忽], one name is Dimai [地脈].
