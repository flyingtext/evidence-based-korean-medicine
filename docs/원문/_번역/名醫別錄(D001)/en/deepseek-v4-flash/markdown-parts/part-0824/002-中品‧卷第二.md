## 乾薑

### Source Text

大熱，無毒。主治寒冷腹痛，中惡，霍亂，脹滿，風邪諸毒，皮膚間結氣，止唾血。生薑，味辛，微溫。

### Literal Translation

Greatly hot, non-toxic. It treats cold abdominal pain, being attacked by malign qi (中惡), cholera (霍亂), distention and fullness, various poisons of wind evil (風邪諸毒), node-knotted qi between the skin, and stops spitting of blood (唾血). Shengjiang (生薑, fresh ginger), acrid in flavor, slightly warm.
