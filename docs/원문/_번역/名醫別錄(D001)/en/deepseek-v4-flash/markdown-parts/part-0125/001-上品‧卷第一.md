## 生地黃

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：乾地黃，味甘，寒。主折跌絕筋，傷中，逐血痹，填骨髓，長肌肉。作湯除寒熱積聚，除痹

### Literal Translation

)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Dried Rehmannia [乾地黃], flavor sweet, cold. It mainly treats fractures, sprains and severed sinews, damage to the center, expels blood bi [血痹], fills the bone marrow, and grows flesh. Made into a decoction it removes cold and heat accumulations and removes bi [痹]
