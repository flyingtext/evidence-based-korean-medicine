## 鍛石

### Source Text

又，療金瘡，止血大效。《本經》原文：鍛石，味辛，溫。主疽瘍疥瘙，熱氣，惡瘡癩疾，死肌墮眉，殺痔蟲，去黑子息肉。

### Literal Translation

Also, it treats metal wounds (金瘡) and stops bleeding with great efficacy. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 鍛石 (duànshí), flavor acrid, warm. It governs carbuncle, ulcer, scabies and itching, heat-qi, malignant sores and leprosy (癩疾), dead flesh and falling eyebrows, kills hemorrhoid worms, and removes black moles and polyps.
