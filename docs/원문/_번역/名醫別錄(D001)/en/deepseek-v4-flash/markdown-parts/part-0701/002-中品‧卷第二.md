## 敗醬

### Source Text

味鹹，微寒，無毒。主除癰腫，浮腫，結熱，風痹，不足，產後疾痛。一名鹿首，名馬草，一名澤敗。

Flavor salty, slightly cold, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to dispel abscesses and swellings, edema, bound heat, wind-impediment, insufficiency, and post-partum disease pain. One name is 鹿首, one name 馬草, one name 澤敗.
