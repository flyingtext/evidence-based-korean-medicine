## 梔子

### Source Text

九月采實，曝乾。《本經》原文：梔子，味苦，寒。主五內邪氣，胃中熱氣，面赤酒　渣鼻，白癩赤癩瘡瘍。

### Literal Translation

Gather the fruit in the ninth month, expose to sun to dry. Original text of the Benjing: Gardenia (梔子), flavor bitter, cold. It chiefly treats pathogenic qi of the five viscera, heat qi within the stomach, red acne-flush nose and face, and white and red leprosy sores and ulcerations.
