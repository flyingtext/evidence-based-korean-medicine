## 白英

### Source Text

春采葉，夏采莖，秋采花，冬采根。《本經》原文：白英，味甘，寒。主寒熱八疸消渴，補中益氣。

### Literal Translation

The leaves are harvested in spring, the stems in summer, the flowers in autumn, and the roots in winter. Original text of the Benjing: Baiying (Solanum lyratum), sweet in flavor, cold. It chiefly treats the eight types of jaundice and wasting-thirst, and supplements the center and boosts qi.
