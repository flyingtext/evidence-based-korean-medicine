## 龍骨

### Source Text

微寒，無毒。主治心腹煩滿，四肢痿枯，汗出，夜臥自驚，恚怒，伏氣在心下，不得息，腸癰內疽陰蝕，止汗，小便利，溺血，養精神，定魂魄，安五臟。

### Literal Translation

Slightly cold, non-toxic. It chiefly treats vexation and fullness of the heart and abdomen, wilting and withering of the four limbs, sweating, spontaneous fright when lying down at night, anger, hidden qi below the heart, inability to breathe, intestinal abscess, internal carbuncle, and yin erosion; it stops sweating, promotes urination, stops blood in the urine, nourishes the essence and spirit, settles the hun and po, and settles the five viscera.
