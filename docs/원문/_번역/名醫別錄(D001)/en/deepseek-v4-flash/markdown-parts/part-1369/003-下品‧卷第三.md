## 石下長卿

### Source Text

主鬼疰精物邪惡氣，殺百精蠱毒，老魅注易，亡走啼哭，悲傷恍惚。一名徐長卿。

### Literal Translation

It governs ghost-attack, essence-things, and evil foul qi (鬼疰精物邪惡氣), kills the hundred essences and gu-poison (殺百精蠱毒), old demons attacking and changing (老魅注易), running away and weeping and crying (亡走啼哭), and grief and confusion (悲傷恍惚). One name is 徐長卿 (xúchángqīng).
