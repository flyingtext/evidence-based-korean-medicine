## 別羈

### Source Text

二月、八月采。《本經》原文：別羈，味苦，微溫。主風寒濕痹身重，四肢疼酸，寒邪歷節痛。

### Literal Translation

Gathered in the second and eighth months. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 別羈 (biéjī), flavor bitter, slightly warm. It governs wind-cold-damp bi with heaviness of the body (風寒濕痹身重), soreness of the four limbs (四肢疼酸), and cold-evil pain of the joints (寒邪歷節痛).
