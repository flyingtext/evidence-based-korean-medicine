## 鯉魚膽

### Source Text

《本經》原文：鯉魚膽，味苦，寒。主目熱赤痛，青盲，明目。久服，強悍益志氣。

### Literal Translation

The original text of the Benjing: Liyu dan (鯉魚膽), bitter in flavor, cold in nature. It treats heat, redness, and pain of the eyes (目熱赤痛) and green blindness (青盲), and brightens the eyes. Taken for a long time, it strengthens and invigorates, and benefits the will and qi (強悍益志氣).
