## 路石

### Source Text

味甘，酸，無毒。主治心腹，止汗生肌，酒痂，益氣，耐寒，實骨髓。一名陵石。

### Literal Translation

Sweet and sour in flavor, non-toxic. It treats the heart and abdomen, stops sweating and produces flesh (生肌), [treats] wine scabs (酒痂), boosts qi (益氣), endures cold, and fills the bone marrow (實骨髓). One name is Lingshi (陵石).
