## 羊角

### Source Text

）羊髓，味甘，溫毒。主治男女傷中，陰氣不足，利血脈，益經氣，以酒服之。

### Literal Translation

Yang sui (羊髓, sheep marrow), sweet in flavor, warm-poisonous. It treats injury of the center in men and women (男女傷中), insufficiency of yin qi (陰氣不足), benefits the blood and vessels, and boosts the qi of the channels; it is taken with wine.
