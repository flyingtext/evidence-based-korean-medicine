## 磁石

### Source Text

一名處石。生太山及慈山山陰，有鐵者則生其陽，采時。（柴胡為之使，惡牡丹、莽草，畏黃石脂，殺鐵毒。

### Literal Translation

One name is 處石. It grows on the shaded side of Mount Tai and Mount Ci; where there is iron, it is born on the yang side; gather at the proper season. (Bupleurum 柴胡 is its envoy; it is averse to peony 牡丹 and 莽草, is in dread of yellow earth (黃石脂), and kills iron poisoning.)
