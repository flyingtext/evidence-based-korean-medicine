## 木蘭

### Source Text

無毒。主治中風、傷寒，及癰疽、水腫，去臭氣。一名杜蘭，皮似桂而香。

### Literal Translation

Not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat wind-stroke and cold-damage, as well as carbuncles and ulcers and edema, and to dispel foul qi. One name is 杜蘭; the bark resembles cinnamon and is fragrant.
