## 飛廉

### Source Text

正月采根；七月、八月采花，陰乾。（得烏頭惡麻黃。）

《本經》原文：飛廉，味苦，平。

### Literal Translation

Gathered as root in the first month; gathered as flowers in the seventh and eighth months, dried in the shade. (With 烏頭 (wūtóu) it is good; it fears 麻黃 (máhuáng).)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 飛廉 (fēilián), flavor bitter, neutral.
