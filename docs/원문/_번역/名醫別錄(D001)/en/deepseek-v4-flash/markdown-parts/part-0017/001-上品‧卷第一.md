## 曾青

### Source Text

無毒。主養肝膽，除寒熱，殺白蟲，治頭風、腦中寒，止煩渴，補不足，盛陰氣。生中及越　，采無時。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It mainly nourishes the liver and gallbladder, removes cold and heat, kills white worms, treats head wind [頭風] and cold in the brain, stops vexation and thirst, supplements deficiency, and enriches yin qi. It grows in Zhong and Yue; gather it at any time.
