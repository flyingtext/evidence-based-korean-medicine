## 心

### Source Text

《本經》原文：白馬莖，味鹹，平。主傷中脈絕，陰不起，強志，益氣，長肌肉肥健，生子。眼，主驚癇腹生平澤。

### Literal Translation

The original text of the Benjing: Baima jing (白馬莖), salty in flavor, neutral. It treats injury of the center with severing of the vessels (傷中脈絕), the genitals not rising (陰不起), strengthening the will, boosting qi (益氣), growing the muscles and making one plump and robust, and giving birth to children. The eye treats fright-epilepsy and abdominal [conditions]; it grows on level marshland.
