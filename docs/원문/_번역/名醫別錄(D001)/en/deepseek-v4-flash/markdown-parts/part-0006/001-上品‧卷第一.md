## 丹砂

### Source Text

無毒。主通血脈，止煩滿、消渴，益精神，悅澤人面，除中惡、腹痛、毒瓦斯、疥　、諸瘡。久服輕身神仙。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It mainly frees the blood vessels, stops vexation and fullness, and wasting-thirst [消渴]; it boosts essence and spirit, makes the face lustrous, and removes evil qi [中惡], abdominal pain, poisonous qi, scabies, and all kinds of sores. Taken over a long time, it lightens the body and makes one a spirit immortal.
