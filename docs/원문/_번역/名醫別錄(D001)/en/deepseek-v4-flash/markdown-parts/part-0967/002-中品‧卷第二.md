## 雄鵲肉

### Source Text

味甘，寒，無毒。主治石淋，消結熱。可燒作灰，以石投中散解者，是雄也。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, cold, non-toxic. It treats stone strangury (石淋) and dispels binding heat (結熱). It can be burned into ash; when a stone is cast in and it dissolves and scatters, it is the male.
