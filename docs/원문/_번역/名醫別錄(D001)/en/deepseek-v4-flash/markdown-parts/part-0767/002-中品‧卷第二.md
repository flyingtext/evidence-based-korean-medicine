## 白蘚

### Source Text

四月、五月采根，陰乾。（惡桑螵蛸、桔梗、茯苓、萆　。）

《本經》原文：白蘚，味苦，寒。

### Literal Translation

Gather the root in the fourth and fifth months, dry in the shade. (Averse to mantis ootheca 桑螵蛸, platycodon 桔梗, poria 茯苓, and dioscorea 萆.) Original text of the Benjing: Dictamnus (白蘚), flavor bitter, cold.
