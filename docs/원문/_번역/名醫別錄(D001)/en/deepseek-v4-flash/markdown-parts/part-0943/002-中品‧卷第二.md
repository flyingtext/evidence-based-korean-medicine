## 牡狗陰莖

### Source Text

主傷中，陰痿不起，令強熱大，生子，除女子帶下十二疾。一名狗精

### Literal Translation

It treats injury of the center (傷中), yin-wei with the genitals not rising (陰痿不起), causes [the genitals] to become strong, hot, and large, gives birth to children, and removes women's twelve diseases of dai xia (帶下). One name is Goujing (狗精).
