## 茜根

### Source Text

（畏鼠姑

《本經》原文：茜根，味苦，寒。主寒濕風痹，黃膽，補中。生川穀。

(It fears Shugu (mouse mushroom).

Original text of the Benjing: Qian'gen (Madder root), bitter in flavor, cold. It chiefly treats cold-damp wind bi (impediment) and jaundice, and supplements the center. It grows in the valleys of rivers.
