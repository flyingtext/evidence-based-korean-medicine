## 銅鏡鼻

### Source Text

主治伏尸，邪氣。生桂陽。《本經》原文：錫銅鏡鼻，主女子血閉症瘕，伏腸絕孕。

### Literal Translation

It governs corpse-attack (伏尸) and evil qi (邪氣). It grows in Guiyang. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 錫銅鏡鼻 (xītóngjìngbí), it governs blood-closure masses (血閉症瘕) in women, and severing the intestines and preventing pregnancy.
