## 蜀漆

### Source Text

微溫，有毒。主治胸中邪結氣吐出之。生江林山及蜀漢中，恆山苗也。

### Literal Translation

Slightly warm, toxic. It governs evil bound qi in the chest (胸中邪結氣) and vomits it out. It grows on Jianglin Mountain and in Shu and Hanzhong; it is the sprout of 恆山 (héngshān).
