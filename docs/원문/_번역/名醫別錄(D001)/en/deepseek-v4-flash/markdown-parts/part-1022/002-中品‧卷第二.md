## 烏賊魚骨

### Source Text

無毒。主治驚氣入腹，腹痛環臍，陰中寒腫，令人有子，又止瘡多膿汁不燥。肉平，主益氣強志。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It treats fright qi (驚氣) entering the abdomen, abdominal pain encircling the navel (腹痛環臍), cold swelling of the genitals (陰中寒腫), causing people to have children, and also stops sores that ooze much pus but do not dry (瘡多膿汁不燥). The flesh, neutral, treats boosting qi (益氣) and strengthening the will (強志).
