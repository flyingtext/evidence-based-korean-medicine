## 牡狗陰莖

### Source Text

治癲疾發作。肉，味鹹、酸，安五臟，補絕傷，輕身益氣。屎中骨，治寒熱，小兒驚癇。

### Literal Translation

It treats mania-epilepsy seizures (癲疾發作). The flesh, salty-sour in flavor, settles the five viscera (安五臟), supplements severed injuries (補絕傷), lightens the body, and boosts qi (益氣). The bone within the dung (屎中骨) treats chills and fever and children's fright-epilepsy (小兒驚癇).
