## 柳華

### Source Text

又治心腹內血，止痛琅。《本經》原文：柳華，味苦，寒。主風水黃膽，面熱黑。

### Literal Translation

Also it treats blood within the heart and abdomen (心腹內血) and stops pain. 琅 (láng). The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 柳華 (liǔhuá), flavor bitter, cold. It governs wind-water jaundice (風水黃膽) and heat-blackening of the face (面熱黑).
