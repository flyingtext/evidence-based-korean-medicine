## 節華

### Source Text

味苦，無毒。主治傷中，痿痹，溢腫。皮，主治脾中客熱氣。

### Literal Translation

Bitter in flavor, non-toxic. It treats injury of the center (傷中), wei and bi (痿痹), and overflowing swelling (溢腫). The bark treats guest heat qi within the spleen (脾中客熱氣).
