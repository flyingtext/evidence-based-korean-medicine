## 桑螵蛸

### Source Text

螳螂二月、三月采，當火炙，不爾令人泄。（得龍骨治泄精，畏旋覆花。）

《本經》原文：桑螵蛸，味鹹，平。

### Literal Translation

The mantis (螳螂) is gathered in the second and third months, and should be roasted over fire; otherwise it causes people to have diarrhea (泄). (With longgu (龍骨) it treats seminal discharge; it is averse to xuanfuhua (旋覆花).)

The original text of the Benjing: Sangpiaoxiao (桑螵蛸), salty in flavor, neutral.
