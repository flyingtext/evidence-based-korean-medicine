## 附子

### Source Text

味甘，大熱，有大毒。主治腳疼冷弱，腰脊風寒心腹冷痛，霍亂轉筋，下痢赤白，堅肌骨，強陰。又墮胎，為百藥長。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, greatly hot, greatly toxic. It governs pain, cold, and weakness of the feet (腳疼冷弱), wind-cold of the low back and spine (腰脊風寒), cold pain of the heart and abdomen (心腹冷痛), cholera with cramping of the sinews (霍亂轉筋), dysentery with red and white [discharge] (下痢赤白), strengthens the muscles and bones (堅肌骨), and strengthens yin (強陰). Also it causes abortion (墮胎), and is the chief of the hundred medicines (百藥長).
