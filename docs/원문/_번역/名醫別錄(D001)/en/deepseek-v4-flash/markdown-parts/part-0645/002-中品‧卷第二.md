## 苦竹葉及瀝

### Source Text

治口瘡，目痛明目，通利九竅；竹筍，味甘，無毒；主消渴，利水道，益氣，可久食；干筍，燒服，治五痔血。《本經》原文：竹葉，味苦，平。主咳逆上氣，溢筋急，惡瘍，殺小蟲。

### Literal Translation

It treats mouth sores, eye pain, brightens the eyes, and frees the nine orifices; bamboo shoot (竹筍), flavor sweet, not poisonous; chiefly treats wasting-thirst, benefits the waterway, boosts qi, and can be eaten long-term. Dried shoot, burned and taken, treats the five hemorrhoids with blood. Original text of the Benjing: Bamboo leaf (竹葉), flavor [pungent/generic]
