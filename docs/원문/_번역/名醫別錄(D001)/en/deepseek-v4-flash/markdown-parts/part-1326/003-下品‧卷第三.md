## 連翹

### Source Text

八月采，陰乾。《本經》原文：連翹，味苦，平。主寒熱鼠　瘰　，癰腫惡瘡，癭瘤結熱蠱毒。

### Literal Translation

Gathered in the eighth month, dried in the shade. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 連翹 (liánqiào), flavor bitter, neutral. It governs cold-heat, rat [fistula], and scrofula (寒熱鼠瘻瘰), carbuncle swelling and malignant sores (癰腫惡瘡), goiter masses and bound heat and gu-poison (癭瘤結熱蠱毒).
