## 桑上寄生

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主治金創，去痹，女子崩中，內傷不足，產後余疾農桑樹上。三月三日采莖、葉，陰乾。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, non-toxic. It chiefly treats metal wounds, removes bi (impediment), women's uterine collapse (bengzhong), internal injury with insufficiency, and residual illness after childbirth ... on mulberry and agricultural trees. The stems and leaves are harvested on the third day of the third month and dried in the shade.
