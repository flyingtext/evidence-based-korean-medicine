## 牛扁

### Source Text

無毒，生桂陽。《本經》原文：牛扁，味苦，微寒。主身皮瘡熱氣，可作浴湯，殺牛虱小蟲，又療牛病。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It grows in Guiyang. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 牛扁 (niúbiǎn), flavor bitter, slightly cold. It governs heat-qi of the sores of the body's skin (身皮瘡熱氣), may be made into a bathing decoction (浴湯), kills cattle lice and small insects (牛虱小蟲), and also treats cattle disease (療牛病).
