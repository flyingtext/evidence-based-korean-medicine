## 決明子

### Source Text

十月十日采，陰乾（蓍實為之使，惡大麻子。）

《本經》原文：決明子，味鹹，平。主青盲，目淫膚，赤白膜，眼赤痛淚出。

### Literal Translation

Gather on the tenth day of the tenth month, dry in the shade. (Yarrow-seed 蓍實 is its envoy; it is averse to hemp seed 大麻子.) Original text of the Benjing: Cassia seed (決明子), flavor salty, level. It chiefly treats green blindness (青盲), film over the eyes (淫膚), red and white membranes, and red painful tearing eyes.
