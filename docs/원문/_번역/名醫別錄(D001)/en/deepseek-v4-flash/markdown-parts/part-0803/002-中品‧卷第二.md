## 大小薊根

### Source Text

味甘，溫。主養精，保血。大薊，主治女子赤白沃，安胎，止吐血、衄鼻，令人五月采。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, warm. It treats nourishing essence (養精) and protecting blood (保血). Da Ji (大薊) treats women's red and white turbid discharge (赤白沃), settles the fetus, stops vomiting of blood (吐血) and nose-bleeding (衄鼻), and causes people to gather it in the fifth month.
