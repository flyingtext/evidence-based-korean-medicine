## 牡狗陰莖

### Source Text

無毒。六月上伏取，陰乾百日。膽，主痂瘍，惡瘡。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It is taken during the first fu of the sixth month and dried in the shade for a hundred days. The gall (膽) treats scab-sores (痂瘍) and malignant sores (惡瘡).
