## 牛黃

### Source Text

生晉地平澤，生於牛，得之即陰乾百日，使時燥，無令見日月光。（人參為之使，得牡丹、菖蒲利耳目，惡龍骨、地黃、龍膽、蜚蠊，畏牛漆。）

《本經》原文：牛黃，味苦，平。

### Literal Translation

It grows in the level marshes of the land of Jin, born in cattle; when obtained it is dried in the shade for a hundred days, making it thoroughly dry, and one must not let it see the light of sun or moon. (Renshen (ginseng) serves as its envoy; with Mudan (peony root bark) and Changpu (Acorus) it benefits the ears and eyes; it is averse to Longgu (dragon bone), Dihuang (Rehmannia), Longdan (Gentian), and Feilian; it fears Niuxi (Achyranthes).)

Original text of the Benjing: Niuhuang (bezoar), bitter in flavor, neutral.
