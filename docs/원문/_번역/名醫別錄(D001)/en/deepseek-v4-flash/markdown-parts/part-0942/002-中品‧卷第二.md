## 牡狗陰莖

### Source Text

又，狗骨灰，主下痢，生肌，敷馬瘡。烏狗血，主產難橫生，血上蕩心者。《本經》原文：牡狗陰莖，味鹹，平。

### Literal Translation

Also, dog-bone ash (狗骨灰) treats dysentery (下痢), produces flesh (生肌), and is applied to horse-sores (馬瘡). Black-dog blood (烏狗血) treats difficult labor with transverse birth (產難橫生) and blood surging upward to stir the heart (血上蕩心). The original text of the Benjing: Mugou yinjing (牡狗陰莖), salty in flavor, neutral.
