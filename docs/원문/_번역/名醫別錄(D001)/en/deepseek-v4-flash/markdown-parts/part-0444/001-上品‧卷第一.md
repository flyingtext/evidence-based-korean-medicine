## 秦龜

### Source Text

味苦，無毒。除濕痹氣，身重，四肢關節不可動搖。生山之陰土中。

### Literal Translation

Bitter in flavor, non-toxic. It removes damp bi (impediment) qi, heaviness of the body, and inability of the four limbs and joints to move. It grows in the earth of the shaded side of mountains.
