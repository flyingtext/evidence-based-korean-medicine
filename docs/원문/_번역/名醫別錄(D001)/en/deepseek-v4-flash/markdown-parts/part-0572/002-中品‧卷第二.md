Its chief actions are to warm the center, stop pain, dispel stagnant blood lodged within (客血內塞), wind-stroke with [sweat] not emerging, damp-impediment (濕痹), attack by noxious qi, exhaustion and cold in the guest, supplement the five viscera, and generate flesh. It grows in Longxi.
