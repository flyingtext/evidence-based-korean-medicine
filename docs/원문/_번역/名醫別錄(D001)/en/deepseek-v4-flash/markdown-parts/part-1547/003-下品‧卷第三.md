## 蛇蛻

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主治弄舌搖頭，大人五邪，言語僻越，惡瘡，嘔咳，明目。一名龍子皮生荊州及田野。

Flavor sweet, non-toxic. It governs tongue-playing and head-shaking, the five evils of adults, deviant and perverse speech, malignant sores, vomiting and cough, and brightens the eyes. One name is Longzipi. It grows in Jingzhou and in the fields.
