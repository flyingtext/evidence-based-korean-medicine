## 石龍芮

### Source Text

（大戟為之畏蛇蛻皮、吳茱萸。）

又，水堇，主治毒腫癰瘡、蛔蟲、齒齲。（《唐本注》謂石龍芮俗名水堇）

《本經》原文：石龍芮，味苦，平。

### Literal Translation

(Euphorbia pekinensis [大戟] is its [envoy]; it fears snake slough [蛇蛻皮] and Evodia [吳茱萸].)

Also, water smartweed [水堇] mainly treats toxic swelling, abscesses and sores, roundworms, and dental caries. (The Tang Bencao commentary [唐本注] says that Ranunculus sceleratus [石龍芮] is commonly called water smartweed [水堇].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Ranunculus sceleratus [石龍芮], flavor bitter, neutral.
