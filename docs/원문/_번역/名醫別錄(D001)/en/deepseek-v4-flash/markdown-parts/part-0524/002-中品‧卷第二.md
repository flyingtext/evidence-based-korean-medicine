## 石硫黃

### Source Text

大熱，有毒。主治心腹積聚，邪氣冷癖在脅，咳逆上氣，腳冷疼弱下部　瘡，止血，殺疥蟲。生東海牧羊中，及大山及河西山，礬石液也。

### Literal Translation

Greatly hot, poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat accumulations in heart and abdomen, pathogenic cold hypochondriac (癖) qi in the flanks, cough and counter-flow ascent of qi, cold painful weak feet, lower-body ulcers, stop bleeding, and kill scabies worms. It grows at Donghai Muchang (東海牧羊), and also on Mount Tai and the western (河西) mountains; it is the liquid of alum stone (礬石).

### Translator's Notes

- 石硫黃 = sulfur; 礬石液 = sulfur formed as the sap of alum-stone ores.
