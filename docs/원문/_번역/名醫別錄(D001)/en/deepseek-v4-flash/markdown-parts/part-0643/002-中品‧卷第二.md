## 淡竹葉

### Source Text

味辛，平、大寒。主治胸中淡熱，咳逆上氣。其瀝，大寒，治暴中風，風痹，胸中大熱，止煩悶。

### Literal Translation

Flavor pungent, level and greatly cold. Its chief actions are to treat heat-phlegm (淡熱) within the chest and cough with counter-flow ascent of qi. Its sap (瀝) is greatly cold; it treats sudden wind-stroke, wind-impediment, great heat within the chest, and stops vexation and oppression.
