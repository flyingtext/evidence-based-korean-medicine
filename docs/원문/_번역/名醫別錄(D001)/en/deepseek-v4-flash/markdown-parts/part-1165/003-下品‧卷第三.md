## 澤漆

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：澤漆，味苦，微寒。主皮膚熱，大腹水氣，四肢面目浮腫，丈夫陰氣不足。生川澤。

### Literal Translation

)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 澤漆 (zéqī), flavor bitter, slightly cold. It governs heat of the skin, great abdominal water-qi (大腹水氣), swelling of the four limbs, face, and eyes, and insufficiency of the male yin-qi (丈夫陰氣不足). It grows in rivers and marshes.
