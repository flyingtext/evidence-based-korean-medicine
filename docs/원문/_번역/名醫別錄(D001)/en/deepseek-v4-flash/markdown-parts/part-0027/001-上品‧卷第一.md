## 雲母

### Source Text

無毒。下氣堅肌，續絕補中，治五勞七傷，虛損少氣，止痢；久服悅澤不老，耐寒暑志高神仙。一名云珠，色多赤。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It sends qi downward, firms the flesh, continues the severed and supplements the center, treats the five kinds of exhaustion and seven kinds of damage [五勞七傷], deficiency and damage with scant qi, and stops dysentery. Taken over a long time, it makes one lustrous and does not age, endures cold and heat, and raises the will to become a spirit immortal. One name is Cloud Pearl [云珠], mostly red in color.
