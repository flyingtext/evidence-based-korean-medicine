## 蕤核

### Source Text

七月采實。《本經》原文：蕤核，味甘，溫。主心腹邪結氣，明目，目赤痛傷淚出。

### Literal Translation

The fruit is harvested in the seventh month. Original text of the Benjing: Ruiren (Prinsepia), sweet in flavor, warm. It chiefly treats evil binding qi of the heart and abdomen, brightens the eyes, and treats red, painful, injured eyes with tearing.
