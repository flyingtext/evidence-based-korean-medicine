## 蔥實

### Source Text

無毒。蔥白，平。主治寒傷，骨肉痛，喉痹不通，安胎，歸目，除肝邪氣，安中，利臟，益目精，殺百藥毒。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. Congbai (蔥白, scallion white), neutral. It treats injury from cold with pain of the bones and flesh (寒傷、骨肉痛), throat bi with blockage (喉痹不通), settling the fetus (安胎), returning to the eyes (歸目), removing evil qi of the liver (除肝邪氣), settling the center (安中), benefiting the viscera (利臟), benefiting the essence of the eyes (益目精), and killing the poison of the hundred drugs (殺百藥毒).
