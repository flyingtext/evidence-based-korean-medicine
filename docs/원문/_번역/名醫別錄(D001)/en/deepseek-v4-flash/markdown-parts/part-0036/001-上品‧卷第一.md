## 硝石

### Source Text

味辛，大寒，無毒。主治五臟十二經脈中百二十疾，暴傷寒、腹中大熱，止煩滿消渴利小便及　蝕瘡。天地至神之物，能化成十二種石。

### Literal Translation

Flavor pungent, greatly cold, non-toxic. It mainly treats the hundred and twenty diseases in the twelve channels of the five viscera, sudden cold damage [暴傷寒] and great heat in the abdomen; it stops vexation and fullness, wasting-thirst [消渴], and promotes urination, and treats erosive sores. It is the most spirit-like thing of heaven and earth, and can transform into the twelve kinds of stone.
