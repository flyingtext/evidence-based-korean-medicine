## 蕪荑

### Source Text

三月采實，陰乾。《本經》原文：蕪荑，味辛。主五內邪氣，散皮膚骨節中淫淫溫行毒，去三蟲，化食。

### Literal Translation

Gather the fruit in the third month, dry in the shade. Original text of the Benjing: Ulmus-芜荑 (蕪荑), flavor pungent. It chiefly treats pathogenic qi of the five viscera, dissipates the seeping warm toxin moving within the skin, bones, and joints (皮膚骨節中淫淫溫行毒), dispels the three worms, and transforms food.
