## 薇銜

### Source Text

微寒，無毒。主暴症，逐水，治痿蹶。久服輕身，明目。

### Literal Translation

Slightly cold, not poisonous. It chiefly treats sudden masses (暴症), drives down water, and treats wilting-tetany. Taken over a long time it lightens the body and brightens the eyes.
