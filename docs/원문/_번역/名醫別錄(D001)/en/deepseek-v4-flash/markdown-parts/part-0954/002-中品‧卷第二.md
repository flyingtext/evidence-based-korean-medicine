## 虎骨

### Source Text

肉，治惡心欲嘔，益氣力。又，屎，治惡瘡。其眼睛，治癲。

The flesh treats nausea with desire to vomit (惡心欲嘔) and boosts qi and strength (益氣力). Also, the dung treats malignant sores (惡瘡). Its eyes treat mania (癲).
