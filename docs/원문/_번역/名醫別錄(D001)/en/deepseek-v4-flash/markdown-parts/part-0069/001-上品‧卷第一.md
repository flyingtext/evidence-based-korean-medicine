## 禹餘糧

### Source Text

生東海及山島中，或池

《本經》原文：禹餘糧，味甘，寒。主咳逆寒熱煩滿，下赤白，血閉症瘕，大熱。煉餌服之，不飢輕身

### Literal Translation

It grows in the Eastern Sea and on islands in the mountains, or in ponds.

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Yuliang [禹餘糧], flavor sweet, cold. It mainly treats cough and counterflow, cold and heat, vexation and fullness, downflow of red and white, blood closure and zheng [症] and jia [瘕], and great heat. Refined and taken as an elixir, it does not hunger and lightens the body
