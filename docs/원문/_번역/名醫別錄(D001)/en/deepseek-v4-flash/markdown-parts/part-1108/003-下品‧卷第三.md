## 大鹽

### Source Text

（漏蘆為之使

《本經》原文：鹵鹹，味苦，寒。主大熱、消渴、狂煩，除邪及下蠱毒，柔肌膚。戎鹽，主明目目痛，益氣，堅肌骨，去毒蠱。

### Literal Translation

(漏蘆 (lòulú) is its envoy.

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 鹵鹹 (lǔxián), flavor bitter, cold. It governs great heat, wasting-thirst (消渴), mania and vexation, removes evil [qi] and descends gu-poison (蠱毒), and softens the skin and flesh. 戎鹽 (róngyán) governs brightening the eyes and eye pain, boosts qi, and strengthens the muscles and bones.
