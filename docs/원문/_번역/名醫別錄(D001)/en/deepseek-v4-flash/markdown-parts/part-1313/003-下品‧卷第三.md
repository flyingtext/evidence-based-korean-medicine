## 青葙子

### Source Text

三月采莖葉，陰乾；五月月采子。《本經》原文：青葙子，味苦，微寒。主邪氣，皮膚中熱，風瘙身癢，殺三蟲。

### Literal Translation

Gathered as stems and leaves in the third month, dried in the shade; gathered as seeds in the fifth month. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 青葙子 (qīngxiāngzǐ), flavor bitter, slightly cold. It governs evil-qi (邪氣), heat in the skin (皮膚中熱), wind-itching of the body (風瘙身癢), and kills the three worms (殺三蟲).
