## 髓

### Source Text

《本經》原文：鹿茸，味甘，溫。主漏下惡血，寒熱驚癇，益氣強志，生齒不老。角，主惡瘡癰腫，逐邪惡

### Literal Translation

The original text of the Benjing: Lurong (鹿茸), sweet in flavor, warm. It treats leaking of malignant blood (漏下惡血), chills and fever with fright-epilepsy (寒熱驚癇), boosting qi and strengthening the will (益氣強志), growing teeth, and not aging. The horn (角) treats malignant sores and carbuncle-swelling (惡瘡癰腫) and expels evil [qi] (逐邪惡).
