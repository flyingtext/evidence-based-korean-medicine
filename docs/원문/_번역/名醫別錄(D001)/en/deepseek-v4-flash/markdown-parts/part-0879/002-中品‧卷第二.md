## 蕙實

### Source Text

味辛。主明目，補中。根莖中湯，治傷寒，寒熱，出汗，中風，面腫，消渴，熱中，水，生魯山平澤。

### Literal Translation

Acrid in flavor. It treats brightening of the eyes and supplementing the center (補中). The root and stem [made into] a decoction treats cold damage (傷寒), chills and fever, sweating, wind stroke (中風), facial swelling, wasting-thirst (消渴), heat in the center (熱中), and water [accumulation]. It grows on the level marshland of Lushan.
