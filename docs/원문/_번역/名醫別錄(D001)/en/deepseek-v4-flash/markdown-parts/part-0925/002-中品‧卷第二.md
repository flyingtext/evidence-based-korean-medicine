## 牛角鰓

### Source Text

又，牛鼻中木卷，治小兒癇。草卷燒灰，主治小兒鼻下瘡。《本經》原文：牛角　，下閉血瘀血疼痛，女人帶下血。

### Literal Translation

Also, the wooden ring in the bull's nose (牛鼻中木卷) treats children's epilepsy (小兒癇). Grass-ring (草卷) burned to ash treats children's sores below the nose (小兒鼻下瘡). The original text of the Benjing: Niujiao [blank] (牛角) sends down closed blood and stagnant blood and pain (閉血瘀血疼痛), and women's discharge of blood with dai (帶下血).
