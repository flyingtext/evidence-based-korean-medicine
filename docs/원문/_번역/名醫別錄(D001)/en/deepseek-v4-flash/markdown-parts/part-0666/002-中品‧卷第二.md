## 山茱萸

### Source Text

（蓼實為之使，惡桔梗、防風、防己。）

《本經》原文：山茱萸，味酸，平。主心下邪氣寒熱，溫中，逐寒濕痹，去三蟲。

### Literal Translation

(Polygonum-seed 蓼實 is its envoy; it is averse to platycodon 桔梗, fangfeng 防風, and sinomenium 防己.) Original text of the Benjing: Cornus (山茱萸), flavor sour, level. It chiefly treats cold-heat of pathogenic qi below the heart, warms the center, drives out cold-damp impediment, and dispels the three worms.
