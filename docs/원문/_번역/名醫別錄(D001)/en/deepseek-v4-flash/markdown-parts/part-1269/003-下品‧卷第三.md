## 黃環

### Source Text

（鳶尾為之使，惡茯苓。）

《本經》原文：黃環，味苦，平。主蠱毒鬼注鬼魅，邪氣在臟中，除咳逆寒熱。

### Literal Translation

(鳶尾 (yuānwěi) is its envoy; it fears 茯苓 (fúlíng).)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 黃環 (huánghuán), flavor bitter, neutral. It governs gu-poison, ghost-attack, and ghost-spirits (蠱毒鬼注鬼魅), evil-qi in the viscera (邪氣在臟中), and removes cough and retching with cold and heat (咳逆寒熱).
