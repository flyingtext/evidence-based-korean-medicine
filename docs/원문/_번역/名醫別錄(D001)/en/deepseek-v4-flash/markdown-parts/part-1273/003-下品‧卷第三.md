## 巴戟天

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：巴戟天，味辛，微溫。主大風邪氣，陰痿不起，強筋骨，安五臟，補中增志益氣。生山谷。

### Literal Translation

)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 巴戟天 (bājǐtiān), flavor acrid, slightly warm. It governs great wind and evil-qi (大風邪氣), impotence with failure of erection (陰痿不起), strengthens the sinews and bones (強筋骨), settles the five viscera (安五臟), supplements the center, increases the will, and boosts qi (補中增志益氣). It grows in mountain valleys.
