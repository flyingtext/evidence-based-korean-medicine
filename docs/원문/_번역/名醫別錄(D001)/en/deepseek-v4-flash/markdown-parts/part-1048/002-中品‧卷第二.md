## 蓼實

### Source Text

《本經》原文：蓼實，味辛，溫。主明目，溫中，耐風寒，下水氣，面目浮腫，癰瘍。馬蓼，去腸中蛭蟲，

### Literal Translation

The original text of the Benjing: Liaoshi (蓼實), acrid in flavor, warm. It treats brightening the eyes, warming the center (溫中), enduring wind and cold (耐風寒), sending down water qi (下水氣), swelling of the face and eyes (面目浮腫), and carbuncle-sores (癰瘍). Ma liao (馬蓼) removes leeches (蛭蟲) from the intestines.
