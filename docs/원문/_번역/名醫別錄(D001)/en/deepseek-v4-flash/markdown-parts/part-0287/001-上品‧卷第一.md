## 景天

### Source Text

《本經》原文：景天，味苦，平。主大熱火瘡，身熱煩邪惡氣。華，主女人漏下赤白，輕身

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Jingtian (Sedum), bitter in flavor, neutral. It chiefly treats great heat, fire sores, body heat, vexation, and evil qi. Its flowers chiefly treat women's leaking below with red and white discharge, and lighten the body.
