## 白兔藿

### Source Text

者，煮飲之即解。生交州。《本經》原文：白兔藿，味苦，平。

### Literal Translation

For [those poisoned], when boiled and drunk it resolves [the poison] immediately. It grows in Jiaozhou. Original text of the Benjing: White-hare-vine (白兔藿), flavor bitter, level.

### Translator's Notes

- Subject of 煮飲之即解 is the boiled drink resolving the poison.
