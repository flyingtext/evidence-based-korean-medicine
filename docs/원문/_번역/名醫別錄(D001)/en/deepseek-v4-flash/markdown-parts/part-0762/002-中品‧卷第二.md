## 紫菀

### Source Text

味辛，無毒。主治咳唾膿血，止喘悸，五勞體虛，補不足，小兒驚癇。一名紫茜，一青苑。

### Literal Translation

Flavor pungent, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat coughing and spitting of pus and blood, stop wheezing and palpitation, the five taxations with vacuity of the body, supplement insufficiency, and children's fright-epilepsy. One name is 紫茜, one name 青苑.
