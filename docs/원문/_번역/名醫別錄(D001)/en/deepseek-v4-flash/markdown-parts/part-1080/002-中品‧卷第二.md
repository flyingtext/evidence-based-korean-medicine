## 粟米

### Source Text

味鹹，微寒，無毒。主養腎氣，去胃脾中熱，益氣。陳者，味苦，主治胃熱、消渴，小便。

### Literal Translation

Salty in flavor, slightly cold, non-toxic. It treats nourishing kidney qi (養腎氣), removing heat in the stomach and spleen (胃脾中熱), and boosting qi (益氣). The aged (陳者), bitter in flavor, treats heat in the stomach (胃熱), wasting-thirst (消渴), and urination.
