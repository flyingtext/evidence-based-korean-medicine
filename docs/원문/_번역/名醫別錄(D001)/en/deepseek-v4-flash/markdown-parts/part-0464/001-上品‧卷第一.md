## 藕實莖

### Source Text

又，藕，主熱渴，散血，生肌。久服令人心歡。《本經》原文：藕實莖，味甘，平。

Also, the lotus root chiefly treats heat thirst, disperses blood, and grows flesh. Taken over a long time it makes the heart joyful. Original text of the Benjing: Oushi jing (lotus seed and stem), sweet in flavor, neutral.
