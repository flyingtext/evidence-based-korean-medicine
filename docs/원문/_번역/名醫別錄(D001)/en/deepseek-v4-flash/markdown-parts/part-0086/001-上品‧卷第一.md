## 龍眼

### Source Text

《本經》原文：龍眼，味甘，平。主五臟邪氣，安志厭食。久服強魂聰明，輕身不老，通明神。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Longan [龍眼], flavor sweet, neutral. It mainly treats evil qi of the five viscera, settles the will and relieves aversion to food. Taken over a long time, it strengthens the hun [魂], makes one intelligent, lightens the body, does not age, and penetrates the spirit.
