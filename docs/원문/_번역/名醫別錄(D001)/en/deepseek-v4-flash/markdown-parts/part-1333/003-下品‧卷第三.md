## 茹

### Source Text

（甘草為之使，惡麥門冬。）

《本經》原文：　茹，味辛，寒。主蝕惡肉敗瘡死肌，殺疥蟲，排膿惡血，除大風熱氣，善忘不樂。

### Literal Translation

(甘草 (gāncǎo) is its envoy; it fears 麥門冬 (màiméndōng).)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 茹 (lǘ) 茹, flavor acrid, cold. It governs eroding evil flesh, decaying sores, and dead flesh (蝕惡肉敗瘡死肌), kills scabies worms (殺疥蟲), expels pus and evil blood (排膿惡血), removes great wind and heat-qi (除大風熱氣), and [treats] forgetfulness and unhappiness (善忘不樂).
