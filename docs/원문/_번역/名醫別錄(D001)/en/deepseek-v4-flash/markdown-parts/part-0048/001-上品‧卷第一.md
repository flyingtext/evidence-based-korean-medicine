## 紫石英

### Source Text

（人參、芍藥共治心中結氣；得天雄、菖蒲共治霍亂。畏扁青、附子，不薑。）

《本經》原文：紫石英，味甘，溫。

### Literal Translation

(With ginseng [人參] and peony [芍藥] it jointly treats bound qi in the heart; with Aconiti lateralis radix [天雄] and Acorus [菖蒲] it jointly treats cholera [霍亂]. It fears flat azurite [扁青] and aconite [附子], and does not [fear] ginger [薑].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Purple quartz [紫石英], flavor sweet, warm.
