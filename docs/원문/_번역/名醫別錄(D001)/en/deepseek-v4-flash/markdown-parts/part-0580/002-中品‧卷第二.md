## 黃

### Source Text

無毒。主治婦人子藏風邪氣，逐五臟間惡血，補丈夫虛損，五勞羸瘦，止渴，腹痛利，益氣，利陰氣。生白水者冷，補。

### Literal Translation

Not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat wind-pathogen of the female uterus (子臧), drive out foul blood among the five viscera, supplement men's vacuity damage, five taxations (五勞) with emaciation and weakness, stop thirst, [treat] abdominal pain with diarrhea, boost qi, and benefit yin qi. That which grows at Baishui is cold and supplements.
