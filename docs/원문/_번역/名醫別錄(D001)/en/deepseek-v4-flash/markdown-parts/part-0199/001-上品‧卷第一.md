味苦，微寒，無毒。主解毒入口皆吐出，中惡腹痛，時氣毒癘，頭痛寒熱，風腫諸毒喉痛口瘡。久服輕身長年。

Flavor bitter, slightly cold, non-toxic. It mainly resolves poison; whatever enters the mouth is all vomited out, [treats] evil qi [中惡] and abdominal pain, seasonal qi and poison epidemics, headache with cold and heat, wind swelling and all poisons, throat pain and mouth sores. Taken over a long time, it lightens the body and prolongs life.
