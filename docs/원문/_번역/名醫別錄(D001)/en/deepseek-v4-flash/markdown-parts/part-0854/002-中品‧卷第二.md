## 雞涅

### Source Text

味甘，平，無毒。主明目，目中寒風，諸不足，水腹，邪氣，補中，止泄利，女子白一名陰洛。生雞山，采無時。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, neutral, non-toxic. It treats brightening the eyes, wind-cold in the eyes (目中寒風), all sorts of insufficiencies (諸不足), water and abdominal [distension] (水腹), evil qi, supplementing the center (補中), stopping diarrhea (止泄利), and women's white [discharge] (白). One is named Yinluo (陰洛). It grows on Mount Ji (雞山) and is gathered at any season.
