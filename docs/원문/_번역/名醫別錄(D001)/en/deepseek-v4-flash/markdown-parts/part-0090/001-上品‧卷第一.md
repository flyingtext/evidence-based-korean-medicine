## 茯苓

### Source Text

無毒。止消渴，好唾，大腹淋瀝，膈中痰水，水腫淋結，開胸腑，調臟氣，伐腎邪，陰，益氣力，保神守中。其有根者，名茯神。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It stops wasting-thirst [消渴], excessive salivation, great abdominal dribbling urination, phlegm and water in the diaphragm, edema and strangury binding, opens the chest and bowels, harmonizes visceral qi, attacks kidney evil, [strengthens] yin, boosts qi and strength, and protects the spirit and guards the center. That which has roots is called Fushen [茯神].
