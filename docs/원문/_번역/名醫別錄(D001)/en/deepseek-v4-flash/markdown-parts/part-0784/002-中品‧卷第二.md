## 爵床

### Source Text

主腰脊痛不得著床，俯仰艱難，除熱；可作浴湯。生川穀。

### Literal Translation

It chiefly treats lumbar-spinal pain so one cannot reach the bed (不得著床) and bending and stretching with difficulty; it dispels heat and may be made into a bathing decoction. It grows in river valleys.
