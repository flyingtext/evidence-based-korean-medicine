## 桃核

### Source Text

主除尸蟲，出瘡中蟲。膠，煉之，主保中不飢，忍風寒；其實，味酸，多食令人有熱。生太山。

### Literal Translation

They remove corpse worms and bring out worms in sores. The gum, refined, governs protecting the center and not being hungry, and enduring wind and cold; its fruit, flavor sour, eaten in excess causes one to have heat. It grows in Taishan.
