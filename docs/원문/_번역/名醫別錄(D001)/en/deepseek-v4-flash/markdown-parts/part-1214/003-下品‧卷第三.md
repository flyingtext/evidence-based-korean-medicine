味辛，微溫，有大毒。主治風濕，丈夫腎濕，陰囊癢，寒熱歷節，掣引腰痛，不能行癰腫膿結。又墮胎。

### Literal Translation

Flavor acrid, slightly warm, greatly toxic. It governs wind-damp (風濕), dampness of the male kidneys (丈夫腎濕), itching of the scrotum (陰囊癢), cold-heat of the joints (寒熱歷節), pulling pain of the low back (掣引腰痛), inability to walk, and carbuncle swelling with pus bindings (癰腫膿結). Also it causes abortion (墮胎).
