## 柏木

### Source Text

（惡乾漆。）

《本經》原文：柏木，味苦，寒。主五臟腸胃中結熱，黃膽，腸痔，止泄利，女子漏下赤白，陰傷蝕瘡。

### Literal Translation

(Averse to 乾漆.) Original text of the Benjing: Phellodendron (柏木), flavor bitter, cold. It chiefly treats bound heat within the five viscera, intestines, and stomach, jaundice, intestinal hemorrhoids, stops diarrhea, and women's down-flow red and white discharge and erosive sores of the genitals.
