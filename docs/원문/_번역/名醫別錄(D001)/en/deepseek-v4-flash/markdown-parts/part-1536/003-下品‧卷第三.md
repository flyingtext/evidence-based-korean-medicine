## 麋脂

### Source Text

）角，味甘，無毒。治痹，止血，益氣力生南山及淮海邊澤中，十月取。《本經》原文：麋脂，味辛，溫。

### Literal Translation

The antler, flavor sweet, non-toxic. It treats bi, stops bleeding, and boosts qi and strength. It grows in the southern mountains and in the marshes of the Huai and the sea; take it in the tenth month. Original text of the Benjing: Milu fat, flavor acrid, warm.
