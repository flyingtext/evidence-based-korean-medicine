## 棗葉

### Source Text

散服使人瘦，久即嘔吐；揩熱痱瘡至良。

《本經》原文：大棗，味甘，平。主心腹邪氣，安中養脾，助十二經，平胃氣，通九竅，補生平澤。

### Literal Translation

Taken as a powder it makes one thin, and over a long time causes vomiting; rubbed on heat prickly-heat sores it is extremely good.

Original text of the Benjing: Dazao (jujube), sweet in flavor, neutral. It chiefly treats evil qi of the heart and abdomen, settles the center and nourishes the spleen, assists the twelve channels, harmonizes the stomach qi, frees the nine orifices, and supplements ... it grows in level marshes.
