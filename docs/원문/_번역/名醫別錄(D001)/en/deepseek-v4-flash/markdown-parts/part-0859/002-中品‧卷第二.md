## 天雄草

### Source Text

味甘，溫，無毒。主益氣，陰痿。生山澤中，狀如蘭，實如大豆，赤色。

Sweet in flavor, warm, non-toxic. It treats boosting qi (益氣) and yin-wei (陰痿, impotence). It grows in mountain marshland, in form resembles the orchid, and bears fruit like soybeans, red in color.
