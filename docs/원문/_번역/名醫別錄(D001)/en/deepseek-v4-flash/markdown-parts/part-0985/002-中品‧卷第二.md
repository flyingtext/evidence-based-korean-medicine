## 蚱蟬

### Source Text

又，殼名枯蟬，一名伏　，主小兒癇，女人生子不出，灰服之，主久痢。《本經》原文：蚱蟬，味鹹，寒。主小兒驚癇夜啼，癲病寒熱。

### Literal Translation

Also, the shell (殼) is named Ku chan (枯蟬), one name is Fu[blank] (伏[blank]); it treats children's epilepsy (小兒癇), women's failure to deliver children (女人生子不出); taken as ash, it treats chronic dysentery (久痢). The original text of the Benjing: Zhachan (蚱蟬), salty in flavor, cold in nature. It treats children's fright-epilepsy and night-crying (小兒驚癇夜啼), and mania disease with chills and fever (癲病寒熱).
