## 菖蒲

### Source Text

生上洛及蜀郡嚴道。一寸九節者良露根不可用。五月、十二月采根，陰乾。

### Literal Translation

It grows in Shangluo and in Yandao of Shu Commandery. That with nine nodes per cun is good; the exposed root must not be used. Gather the root in the fifth and twelfth months and dry in the shade.
