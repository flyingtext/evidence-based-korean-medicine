## 木瓜實

### Source Text

味酸，溫，無毒。主治濕痹邪氣，霍亂，大吐下，轉筋不止。其枝亦可煮用。

### Literal Translation

Sour in flavor, warm, non-toxic. It treats damp bi (濕痹) with evil qi, cholera (霍亂), great vomiting and diarrhea (大吐下), and turning (cramping) sinews that do not stop (轉筋不止). Its branches can also be boiled for use.
