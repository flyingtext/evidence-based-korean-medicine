## 白膠

### Source Text

（得火良，畏大黃。）

《本經》原文：白膠，味甘，平。主傷中勞絕，腰痛羸瘦，補中益氣，婦人血閉無子，止痛

### Literal Translation

(With fire it is good; it fears Dahuang (rhubarb).)

Original text of the Benjing: Baijiao (deer glue), sweet in flavor, neutral. It chiefly treats injury of the center with exhaustion, low back pain with emaciation, supplements the center and boosts qi, treats women's blood closure without children, and stops pain.
