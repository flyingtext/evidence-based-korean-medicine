## 龜甲

### Source Text

生南海及湖水中，采無時，勿令中濕，中濕即有毒。（惡沙參、蜚蠊。）

《本經》原文：龜甲，味鹹，平。

### Literal Translation

It grows in the South Sea and in lake waters; it is gathered at any season; do not let it become damp, for when damp it becomes poisonous. (Averse to shashen (沙參) and feilian (蜚蠊).)

The original text of the Benjing: Guijia (龜甲), salty in flavor, neutral.
