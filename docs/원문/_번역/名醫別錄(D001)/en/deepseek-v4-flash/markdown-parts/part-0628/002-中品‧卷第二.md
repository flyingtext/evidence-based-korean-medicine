It grows in Jin. Gather the root in the tenth month, expose to sun to dry. (Magnolia bark 厚朴 and 白薇 are its envoys; it is averse to peach flower 桃花 and is in dread of 秦椒, 石, and 莽草 ...) Original text of the Benjing: Fritillaria (貝母), flavor pungent, level.
