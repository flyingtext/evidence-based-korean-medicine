## 槐實

### Source Text

味酸、咸，無毒。以七月七日取之，搗取汁，銅器盛之，日煎，令可作丸，大如鼠矢內竅中，三易乃愈。又墮胎。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sour and salty, non-toxic. Take it on the seventh day of the seventh month, pound it to extract the juice, hold it in a copper vessel, and boil it daily until it can be made into pills as large as rat droppings; insert into the orifice, and it heals after three changes. It also aborts the fetus.
