## 水蘇

### Source Text

主下氣殺穀，除飲食，辟口臭，去毒，辟惡氣。久服通神明，輕身耐老

### Literal Translation

It treats sending qi downward and killing grain (下氣殺穀), removing food and drink (除飲食), warding off halitosis (辟口臭), removing poison (去毒), and warding off malignant qi (辟惡氣). Taken for a long time it communicates with the spirits (通神明), lightens the body, and endures aging (輕身耐老).
