## 赤箭

### Source Text

《本經》原文：赤箭，味辛，溫。主殺鬼精物，蠱毒惡氣。久服益氣力，長陰肥健，輕身增年。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Gastrodia [赤箭], flavor pungent, warm. It mainly kills ghosts, essence-things, gu poison [蠱毒] and evil qi. Taken over a long time, it boosts qi and strength, lengthens the yin and makes one fat and strong, lightens the body and increases years.
