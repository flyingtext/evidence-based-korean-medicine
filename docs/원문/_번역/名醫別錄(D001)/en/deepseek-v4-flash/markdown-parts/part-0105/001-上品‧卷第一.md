## 柏白皮

### Source Text

主治火灼，爛瘡，長毛髮。（牡蠣、桂、瓜子為之使，惡菊花、羊蹄、諸石及面曲

《本經》原文：柏實，味甘，平。主驚悸，安五臟，益氣，除濕痹。

### Literal Translation

It mainly treats burns by fire, rotten sores, and grows hair. (Oyster shell [牡蠣], cinnamon [桂] and melon seed [瓜子] are its envoys; it is averse to chrysanthemum [菊花], Rumex [羊蹄], all stones and flour yeast [面曲].

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Arborvitae seed [柏實], flavor sweet, neutral. It mainly treats palpitations, settles the five viscera, boosts qi, and removes damp bi [濕痹].
