## 蕈草

### Source Text

味鹹，平，無毒。主養心氣，除心溫溫心辛痛，浸淫身熱。可作鹽花。

Flavor salty, neutral, non-toxic. It governs nourishing the heart-qi (養心氣), removing warmth-warmth of the heart and acrid pain of the heart (心溫溫心辛痛), and spreading heat of the body (浸淫身熱). It may be made into salt flowers (鹽花).
