## 蘭草

### Source Text

四月、五月采。《本經》原文：蘭草，味辛，平。主利水道，殺蠱毒，辟不祥。

### Literal Translation

Harvested in the fourth and fifth months. Original text of the Benjing: Lancao (Eupatorium), pungent in flavor, neutral. It chiefly promotes the water passage, kills gu (parasitic) poison, and wards off inauspiciousness.
