味甘、酸，無毒。主咽燥渴。損人肺，不可多食。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet and sour, non-toxic. It governs dryness of the throat with thirst. It injures the lung of people; it must not be eaten in excess.

### Translator's Notes

- None.
