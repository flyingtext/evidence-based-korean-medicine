## 車前子

### Source Text

主氣癃，止痛，利水道小便，除濕痹。久服輕身耐老。一名當道。

### Literal Translation

It mainly treats qi strangury [氣癃], stops pain, frees the water passage and urination, and removes damp bi [濕痹]. Taken over a long time, it lightens the body and endures old age. One name is Dangdao [當道].
