## 黑石脂

### Source Text

《本經》原文：青石、赤石、黃石、白石、黑石脂等，味甘，平。主黃膽，泄利腸癖膿血，陰蝕下血赤白，五石脂各隨五色，

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: The blue, red, yellow, white and black stone fats [石脂], flavor sweet, neutral. They mainly treat jaundice [黃膽], diarrhea and dysentery with intestinal pi [腸癖] and pus and blood, erosive downflow of red and white blood, and the five stone fats each follow their five colors,
