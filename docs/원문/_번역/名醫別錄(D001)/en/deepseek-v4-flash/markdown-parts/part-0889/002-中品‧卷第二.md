## 委蛇

### Source Text

味甘，平，無毒。主治消渴，少氣，令人耐寒。生人家園中，大枝長須，多葉兩兩相子如芥子。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, neutral, non-toxic. It treats wasting-thirst (消渴) and shortness of qi (少氣), and causes people to endure cold. It grows in people's home gardens; the large branches have long tendrils, and many leaves paired two by two bear seeds like mustard-seeds (芥子).
