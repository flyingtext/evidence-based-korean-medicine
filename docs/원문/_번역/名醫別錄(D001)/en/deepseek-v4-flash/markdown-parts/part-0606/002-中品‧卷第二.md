## 芎

### Source Text

無毒。主除腦中冷動，面上游風去來，目淚出，多涕唾，忽忽如醉，諸寒冷氣，心腹痛，中惡，卒急腫痛，脅風痛，溫中內寒。一名胡窮，一名香果。

### Literal Translation

Not poisonous. Its chief actions are to dispel cold stirring within the brain, wind of the face moving to and fro, tearing of the eyes, copious nasal discharge and spittle, dizziness as if drunk, various cold qi, heart and abdominal pain, attack by noxious qi, sudden urgent swelling pain, wind-pain of the hypochondrium, and warming the center with endogenous cold. One name is 胡窮 [芎]
