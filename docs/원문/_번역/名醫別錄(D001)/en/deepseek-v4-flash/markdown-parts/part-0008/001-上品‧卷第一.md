## 丹砂

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：丹砂，味甘，微寒。主身體五臟百病，養精神，安魂魄，益氣明目，殺精魅邪惡鬼。久服通神明不老。

### Literal Translation

)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Cinnabar [丹砂], flavor sweet, slightly cold. It mainly treats the hundred diseases of the body and five viscera, nourishes essence and spirit, settles the hun and po [魂魄], boosts qi and brightens the eyes, and kills essence-ghosts, evil and baleful ghosts. Taken over a long time, it penetrates the spirit and does not age.
