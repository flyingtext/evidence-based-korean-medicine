## 豹肉

### Source Text

味酸，平，無毒。主安五臟，補絕傷，輕身益氣，久服，利人。

Sour in flavor, neutral, non-toxic. It treats settling the five viscera (安五臟), supplementing severed injuries (補絕傷), lightening the body and boosting qi (輕身益氣); taken for a long time it benefits people.
