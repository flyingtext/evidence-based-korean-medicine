## 丹參

### Source Text

（畏咸水，反藜蘆。）

《本經》原文：丹參，味苦，微寒。主心腹邪氣，腸鳴幽幽如走水，寒熱積聚，破症除瘕，止煩滿，益氣。

### Literal Translation

(In dread of salty water and incompatible with veratrum 藜蘆.) Original text of the Benjing: Salvia (丹參), flavor bitter, slightly cold. It chiefly treats heart and abdominal pathogenic qi, faint intestinal sounds as if water running (腸鳴幽幽如走水), cold-heat accumulations, breaks masses and removes swellings (症瘕), stops vexation and fullness, and boosts qi.
