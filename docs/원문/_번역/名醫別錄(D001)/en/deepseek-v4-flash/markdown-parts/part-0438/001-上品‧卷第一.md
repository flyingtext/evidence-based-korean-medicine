## 牡蠣

### Source Text

生東海，采時。（貝母為之使，得甘草、牛膝、遠志、蛇床良。惡麻黃、吳茱萸、辛夷。

It grows in the East Sea; harvested at the proper time. (Beimu (Fritillaria) serves as its envoy; with Gancao (licorice), Niuxi (Achyranthes), Yuanzhi (Polygala), and Shechuang it is good. It is averse to Mahuang (ephedra), Wuzhuyu (Evodia), and Xinyi (Magnolia flower).
