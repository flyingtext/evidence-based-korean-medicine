## 術

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主治大風在身面，風眩頭痛，目淚出，消痰水，逐皮間風水結腫，除心下急滿，及霍亂，吐下不止，利腰臍間血，益津液，暖胃，消穀，嗜食。一名山薑，一名山生鄭山、漢中、南鄭。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, non-toxic. It mainly treats great wind [大風] on the body and face, wind dizziness [風眩] and headache, tearing of the eyes, disperses phlegm and water, expels wind and water binding and swelling between the skin, removes urgency and fullness below the heart, and cholera [霍亂] with vomiting and diarrhea that do not stop; it frees blood between the lower back and navel, boosts fluids [津液], warms the stomach, digests grain, and makes one fond of eating. One name is Mountain Ginger [山薑], one name is Shansheng Zhengshan, Hanzhong, Nanzheng.
