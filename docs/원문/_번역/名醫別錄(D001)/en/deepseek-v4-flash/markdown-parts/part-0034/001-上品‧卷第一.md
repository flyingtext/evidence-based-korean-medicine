## 朴硝

### Source Text

（畏麥句薑。）

《本經》原文：朴硝，味苦，寒。主百病，除寒熱邪氣，逐六腑積聚，結固留。

### Literal Translation

(It fears Mai Ju Jiang [麥句薑].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Crude mirabilite [朴硝], flavor bitter, cold. It mainly treats the hundred diseases, removes cold and heat evil qi, and expels accumulations and gatherings of the six bowels, and hard bound retention.
