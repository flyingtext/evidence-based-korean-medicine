## 淫羊藿

### Source Text

無毒。主堅筋骨，消瘰　，赤癰，下部有瘡，洗出蟲。丈夫久服，令人無子。

### Literal Translation

Not poisonous. It chiefly strengthens sinew and bone, dissipates scroll-form swellings (瘰), red carbuncles, and ulcers of the lower body, (from which) washing brings out worms. Taken by men over a long time, it causes childlessness.

### Translator's Notes

- 丈夫久服，令人無子: noted as a cautionary effect of long-term use.
