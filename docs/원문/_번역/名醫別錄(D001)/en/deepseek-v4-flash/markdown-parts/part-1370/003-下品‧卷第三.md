## 羊桃

### Source Text

有毒。主去五臟五水，大腹，利小便，益氣，可作浴湯。一名萇楚，一名御弋，一名銚弋。

### Literal Translation

Toxic. It governs removing the five waters of the five viscera (五臟五水), great abdomen (大腹), benefits urination (利小便), boosts qi (益氣), and may be made into a bathing decoction (浴湯). One name is 萇楚 (chángchǔ), one name is 御弋 (yùyì), one name is 銚弋 (yáoyì).
