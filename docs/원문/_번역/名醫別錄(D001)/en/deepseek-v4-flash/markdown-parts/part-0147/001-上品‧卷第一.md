## 人參

### Source Text

微溫，無毒。主治腸胃中冷，心腹鼓痛，胸脅逆滿，霍亂吐逆，調中，止消渴通血脈破堅積，令人不忘。一名神草，一名人微，一名土精，一名血參。

### Literal Translation

Slightly warm, non-toxic. It mainly treats cold in the intestines and stomach, drumming pain of the heart and abdomen, counterflow fullness of the chest and flanks, cholera [霍亂] with vomiting and counterflow, harmonizes the center, stops wasting-thirst [消渴], frees the blood vessels, breaks hard accumulations, and makes one not forget. One name is Shencao [神草], one name is Renwei [人微], one name is Tujing [土精], one name is Xuecan [血參].
