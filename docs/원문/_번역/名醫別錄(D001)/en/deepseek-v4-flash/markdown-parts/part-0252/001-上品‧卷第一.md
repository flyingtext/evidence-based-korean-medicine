## 地膚子

### Source Text

無毒。主去皮膚中熱氣，散惡瘡疝瘕，強陰。久服使人潤澤。

Non-toxic. It mainly removes heat qi in the skin, disperses malignant sores and hernia and jia [疝瘕], and strengthens yin. Taken over a long time, it makes one moist and lustrous.
