## 牛角鰓

### Source Text

屎，寒腫，惡氣，用塗門戶著壁者。燔之，治鼠　，惡瘡。黃犍牛、烏牯牛溺，治水腫，腹脹滿，利小便。

### Literal Translation

Dung (屎) [treats] cold swelling (寒腫) and malignant qi (惡氣); it is used smeared on doors and walls. Roasted (燔之), it treats rat [fistula] (鼠瘻) and malignant sores (惡瘡). The urine of the yellow-sturdy bull (黃犍牛) and black-cow (烏牯牛) treats edema (水腫), abdominal distention and fullness (腹脹滿), and benefits urination.
