## 麥門冬

### Source Text

微寒，無毒。主治身重目黃，心下支滿，虛勞、客熱，口乾、燥渴，止嘔吐，愈痿強陰，益精，消穀調中，保神，定肺氣，安五臟，令人肥健，美顏色，有子。秦名、羊齊名愛韭，楚名烏韭，越名羊蓍，一名禹葭，一名禹餘糧。

### Literal Translation

Slightly cold, non-toxic. It mainly treats heaviness of the body and yellow eyes, propping fullness below the heart, deficiency exhaustion and guest heat [客熱], dry mouth and parched thirst, stops vomiting, cures wilting [痿] and strengthens yin, boosts essence, digests grain and harmonizes the center, protects the spirit, settles lung qi, settles the five viscera, makes one fat and strong, beautifies the complexion, and [enables one to] have children. In Qin it is called Yangqi [羊齊], in Ai it is called Jiujiu [愛韭], in Chu it is called Wujiu [烏韭], in Yue it is called Yangshi [羊蓍]; one name is Yujia [禹葭], one name is Yuyuliang [禹餘糧].
