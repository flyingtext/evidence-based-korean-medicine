## 豉

### Source Text

味苦，寒，無毒。主治傷寒、頭痛、寒熱、瘴氣、惡毒、煩躁、滿悶、虛勞、喘吸、兩腳疼冷，又殺六畜胎子諸毒。

### Literal Translation

Bitter in flavor, cold, non-toxic. It treats cold damage (傷寒), headache, chills and fever, miasma qi (瘴氣), malignant poison (惡毒), vexation and agitation (煩躁), fullness and stuffiness (滿悶), vacuity-labor (虛勞), wheezing and gasping (喘吸), and cold-pain of both feet (兩腳疼冷); also kills the various poisons of the fetus of the six domestic animals (六畜胎子諸毒).
