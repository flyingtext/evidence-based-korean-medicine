## 柳華

### Source Text

無毒。主治痂疥，惡瘡，金創。葉取煎煮，以洗馬疥，立愈。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It governs scabs and scabies (痂疥), malignant sores (惡瘡), and metal wounds (金創). The leaves, taken and boiled, are used to wash horse scabies, and [the horse] is cured immediately.
