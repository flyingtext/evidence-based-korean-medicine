## 牡蒿

### Source Text

味苦，溫，無毒。主充肌膚，益氣，令人暴肥，血脈滿盛，不可久服。生田野，五月八月采。

### Literal Translation

Bitter in flavor, warm, non-toxic. It treats filling up the skin and flesh (充肌膚), boosting qi (益氣), causing people to suddenly become plump, and making blood and vessels full and replete; one must not take it for a long time. It grows in fields and wilderness and is gathered in the fifth and eighth months.
