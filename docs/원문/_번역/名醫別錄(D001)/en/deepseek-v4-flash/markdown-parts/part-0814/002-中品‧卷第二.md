## 水萍

### Source Text

一名水白，一名水蘇。生雷澤。三月采，曝

《本經》原文：水萍，味辛，寒。

### Literal Translation

One name is Shuibai (水白), one name is Shuisu (水蘇). It grows in Lei Marsh (雷澤). It is gathered in the third month and sun-dried.

The original text of the Benjing: Shuiping (水萍), acrid in flavor, cold in nature.
