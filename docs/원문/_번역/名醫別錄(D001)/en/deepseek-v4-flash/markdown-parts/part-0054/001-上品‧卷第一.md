## 青石脂

### Source Text

味酸，平，無毒。主養肝膽氣，明目，治黃膽，泄痢，腸　，女子帶下百病，及疽惡瘡。久服補髓，益氣，不飢，延年。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sour, neutral, non-toxic. It mainly nourishes the qi of the liver and gallbladder, brightens the eyes, treats jaundice [黃膽], diarrhea and dysentery, intestinal [disease], the hundred diseases of women's vaginal discharge, and deep-rooted and malignant sores. Taken over a long time, it supplements the marrow, boosts qi, does not hunger, and prolongs life.
