## 紫參

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：紫參，味苦、辛，寒。主心腹積聚，寒熱邪氣，通九竅，利大小便。一名牡蒙。

### Literal Translation

)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 紫參 (zǐshēn), flavor bitter and acrid, cold. It governs accumulations of the heart and abdomen (心腹積聚), cold-heat and evil-qi (寒熱邪氣), frees the nine orifices (通九竅), and benefits the large and small [intestines] (利大小便). One name is 牡蒙 (mǔméng).
