## 知母

### Source Text

《本經》原文：知母，味苦，寒。主消渴熱中，除邪氣，肢體浮腫，下水，補不足，益氣。一名　母，一名母。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Anemarrhena (知母), flavor bitter, cold. It chiefly treats wasting-thirst with heat within, dispels pathogenic qi, swelling of the limbs and body, down-drives water, supplements insufficiency, and boosts qi. One name is 母, one name is 母.
