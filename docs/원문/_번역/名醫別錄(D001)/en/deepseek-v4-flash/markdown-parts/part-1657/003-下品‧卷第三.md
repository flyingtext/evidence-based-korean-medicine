## 杏核

### Source Text

（得火良，惡黃　、黃芩、根，解錫毒，畏　草。）

《本經》原文：杏核仁，味甘，溫。主咳逆上氣雷鳴，喉痹，下氣，產乳金創，寒心賁豚。

### Literal Translation

(With fire it is good; it is averse to Huang, Huangqin, and the root, resolves tin poison, and fears grass.)

Original text of the Benjing: Xingheren, flavor sweet, warm. It governs cough counterflow with qi rising and thunder, throat bi, sends down qi, childbirth and lactation with metal wounds, and cold heart with running piglet.
