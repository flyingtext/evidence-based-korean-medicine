## 牡狗陰莖

### Source Text

頭骨，主金血。四腳蹄，煮飲之，下乳汁。白狗血，味鹹，無毒。

### Literal Translation

The skull (頭骨) treats metal-blood (金血). The four feet and hooves (四腳蹄), boiled and drunk, bring down breast milk (下乳汁). The blood of the white dog (白狗血), salty in flavor, non-toxic.
