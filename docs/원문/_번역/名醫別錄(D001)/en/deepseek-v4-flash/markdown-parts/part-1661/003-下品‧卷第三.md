## 桃核

### Source Text

桃梟，味苦。主中惡腹痛，殺精魅五毒不祥。一名桃奴，一名梟景，是實著樹不落，實中者，正月采之。

### Literal Translation

Peach withered fruit, flavor bitter. It governs evil in the center with abdominal pain, and kills apparitions and the five poisons and the inauspicious. One name is Taonu, one name is Xiaojing; it is the fruit that remains on the tree and does not fall; of those that remain, gather them in the first month.
