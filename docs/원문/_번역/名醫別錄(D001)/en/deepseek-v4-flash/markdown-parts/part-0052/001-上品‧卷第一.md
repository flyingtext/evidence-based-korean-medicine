## 白石英

### Source Text

二月采，亦無時。《本經》原文：白石英，味甘，微溫。主消渴，陰痿不足，咳逆胸膈間久寒，益氣，除風濕痹。

### Literal Translation

Gather it in the second month, or at any time. Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: White quartz [白石英], flavor sweet, slightly warm. It mainly treats wasting-thirst [消渴], insufficiency of yin wilting [陰痿], cough and counterflow with prolonged cold between the chest and diaphragm, boosts qi, and removes wind-damp bi [風濕痹].
