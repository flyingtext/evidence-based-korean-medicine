## 大棗

### Source Text

一名干棗，一名美棗，一名良棗。八月采，曝乾。三歲陳核中仁，燔之，味苦，主治腹痛，邪氣。

### Literal Translation

One name is Ganzao (dried jujube), one name is Meizao, one name is Liangzao. Harvested in the eighth month, dried in the sun. The kernel within the pit of a three-year-old jujube, when roasted, is bitter in flavor and chiefly treats abdominal pain and evil qi.
