## 通草

### Source Text

正月采陰乾。《本經》原文：通草，味辛，平。主去惡蟲，除脾胃寒熱，通利九竅血脈關節，令人不忘。

### Literal Translation

Gather in the first month, dry in the shade. Original text of the Benjing: Pith (通草), flavor pungent, level. It chiefly dispels foul worms, dispels cold-heat of the spleen and stomach, and frees the nine orifices, blood vessels, and joints, causing a person not to forget.
