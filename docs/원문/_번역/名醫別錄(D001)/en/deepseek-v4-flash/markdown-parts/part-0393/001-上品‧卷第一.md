## 溺白

### Source Text

治鼻衄，湯火灼瘡。東向圊廁溺坑中青泥，治喉痹，消癰腫，若已有膿即潰。

### Literal Translation

It treats nosebleed and scalds and burns from hot water and fire. The green mud in the east-facing privy and latrine pit treats throat bi (impediment), disperses abscesses and swelling, and if pus is already present it causes them to rupture.
