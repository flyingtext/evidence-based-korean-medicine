## 鬼臼

### Source Text

微溫，有毒。主治咳嗽喉結，風邪，煩惑，失魄，妄見，去目中膚翳，殺大毒，不入一名天臼，一名解毒。生九真及宛朐。

### Literal Translation

Slightly warm, toxic. It governs cough and throat bindings (咳嗽喉結), wind-evil (風邪), vexation and confusion (煩惑), loss of the soul (失魄), seeing things that are not there (妄見), removes film over the eyes (目中膚翳), kills great poison, and does not enter... One name is 天臼 (tiānjiù), one name is 解毒 (jiědú). It grows in Jiuzhen and in Wanqu.
