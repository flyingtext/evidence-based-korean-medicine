## 雄黃

### Source Text

味甘，大溫，有毒。主治疥蟲，　瘡，目痛，鼻中息肉，及絕筋，破骨，百節中大積聚，癖氣，中惡，腹痛，鬼疰，殺諸蛇虺毒，解藜蘆毒，悅澤人面。餌服之，皆飛入中，勝鬼神，延年益壽，保中不飢。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, greatly warming, poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat scabies worms and ulcers, eye pain, nasal polyps, as well as severed sinews, broken bones, great accumulations within the hundred joints, hypochondriac (癖) qi, attack by noxious qi (中惡), abdominal pain, and possession by ghosts (鬼疰); it kills the poison of all snakes and vipers, resolves the poison of Veratrum (藜蘆), and makes the face radiant and fair. When taken as an ingested drug, all (of these) fly into the center, overcoming ghosts and spirits, prolonging years and extending life, preserving the center and producing no hunger.

### Translator's Notes

- 藜蘆 = Veratrum nigrum (Lülu).
