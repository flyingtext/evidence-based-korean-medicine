## 藍實

### Source Text

無毒。其葉汁，殺百藥毒，解野狼毒、射罔毒。其莖葉，可以染青。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. The juice of its leaves kills the poison of all medicines and resolves the poison of wolf poison (langdu) and shewang (Aconitum). Its stems and leaves can be used to dye blue.
