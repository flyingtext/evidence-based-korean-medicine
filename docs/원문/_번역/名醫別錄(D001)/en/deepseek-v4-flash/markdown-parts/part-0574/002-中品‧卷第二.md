## 防風

### Source Text

味辛，無毒。主治脅痛、脅風頭面去來，四肢攣急，字乳金瘡內痙。葉，主治中風熱出。

### Literal Translation

Flavor pungent, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat hypochondriac pain, wind of the hypochondrium issuing to and fro on the head and face, taut spasms of the four limbs, and post-partum (字乳) metal wound with internal tetany (內痙). The leaf chiefly treats wind-stroke with heat issuing [as sweat].
