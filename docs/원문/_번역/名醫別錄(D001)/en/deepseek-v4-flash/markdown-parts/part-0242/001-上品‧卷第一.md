## 菟絲子

### Source Text

生朝鮮田野，蔓延之上。色黃而細為赤網，色淺而大為菟累。九月采實，曝乾。

### Literal Translation

It grows in the fields of Korea, spreading and climbing. That which is yellow and fine is Chiwang [赤網]; that which is pale and large is Tulei [菟累]. Gather the fruits in the ninth month and dry in the sun.
