## 水萍

### Source Text

主暴熱身癢，下水氣，勝酒，長須發，止消渴。久服輕身。一名水華。

### Literal Translation

It treats sudden heat with generalized itching, sends down water qi (水氣), overcomes wine, grows the beard and hair, and stops wasting-thirst (消渴). Taken for a long time it lightens the body. One name is Shuihua (水華).
