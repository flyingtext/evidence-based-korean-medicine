## 豬苓

### Source Text

味苦，無毒。生衡山及濟陰、宛朐，二月、八月采，陰乾。《本經》原文：豬苓，味甘，平。

### Literal Translation

Flavor bitter, non-toxic. It grows on Mount Heng and in Jiyin and Wanqu; gather it in the second and eighth months and dry in the shade. Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Polyporus [豬苓], flavor sweet, neutral.
