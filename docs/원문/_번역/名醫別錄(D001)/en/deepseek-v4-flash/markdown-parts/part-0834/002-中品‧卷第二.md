## 五色符

### Source Text

味苦，微溫。主治咳逆，五臟邪氣，調中，益氣，明目，殺虱。青符、白符、赤符符、黃符，各隨色補其臟。

### Literal Translation

Bitter in flavor, slightly warm. It treats cough with rebellion (咳逆), evil qi of the five viscera (五臟邪氣), regulates the center, boosts qi (益氣), brightens the eyes, and kills lice. The green talisman (青符), white talisman (白符), red talisman (赤符), dark talisman (玄符), and yellow talisman (黃符) each supplement its corresponding viscus according to its color.
