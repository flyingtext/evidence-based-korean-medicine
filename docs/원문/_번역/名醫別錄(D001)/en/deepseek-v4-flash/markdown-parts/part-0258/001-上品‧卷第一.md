## 蒺藜子

### Source Text

味辛，微寒，無毒。主治身體風癢，頭痛，咳逆，傷肺，肺痿，止煩，下氣，小癰腫，陰　，可作摩粉。其葉，主風癢，可煮以浴。

### Literal Translation

Pungent in flavor, slightly cold, non-toxic. It chiefly treats wind-itching of the body, headache, cough with counterflow, injury to the lung, lung wilting (feiwei), stops vexation, sends qi downward, small abscesses and swelling, and yin (genital) ...; it may be made into a rubbing powder. Its leaves chiefly treat wind-itching and may be boiled for bathing.
