## 楮實

### Source Text

葉，味甘，無毒。主治兒身熱，食不生肌，可作浴湯；又治惡瘡，生肉。樹皮，主逐水，利小便。

### Literal Translation

The leaves, flavor sweet, non-toxic. They mainly treat heat of the body in children, eating without growing flesh, and can be made into a bath decoction; they also treat malignant sores and grow flesh. The bark of the tree mainly expels water and frees urination.
