## 雀卵

### Source Text

頭血治雀盲。雄雀矢，治目痛，決癰癤，女子帶下，溺不利，除疝瘕。五月取之良。

### Literal Translation

The blood of the head (頭血) treats night-blindness (雀盲). The droppings of the male sparrow (雄雀矢) treat eye-pain, opening carbuncles and furuncles (決癰癤), women's dai xia (帶下), difficulty of urination (溺不利), and removing abdominal masses and hernias (疝瘕). It is good to take them in the fifth month.
