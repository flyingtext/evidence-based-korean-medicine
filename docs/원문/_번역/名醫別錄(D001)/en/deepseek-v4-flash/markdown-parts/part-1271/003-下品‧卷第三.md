## 巴戟天

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主治頭面游風，小腹及陰中相引痛，下氣，補五勞，益精，利男子。生巴郡及下邳。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, non-toxic. It governs wandering wind of the head and face (頭面游風), pain drawing together in the lower abdomen and genitals (小腹及陰中相引痛), descends qi (下氣), supplements the five exhaustions (補五勞), benefits essence (益精), and benefits men (利男子). It grows in Bajun and in Xiapi.
