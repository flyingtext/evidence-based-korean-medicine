## 蓼實

### Source Text

無毒。葉歸舌，除大小腸邪氣，利中，益志。生雷澤。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. The leaf returns to the tongue (葉歸舌), removes evil qi of the large and small intestines (大小腸邪氣), benefits the center (利中), and boosts the will (益志). It grows in Lei Marsh (雷澤).
