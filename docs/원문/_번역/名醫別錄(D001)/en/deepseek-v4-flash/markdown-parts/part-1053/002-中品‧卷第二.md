## 薤

### Source Text

生魯山。《本經》原文：薤，味辛，溫。主金瘡瘡敗，輕身不飢耐老。

### Literal Translation

It grows in Lushan. The original text of the Benjing: Xie (薤), acrid in flavor, warm. It treats metal (sword) wounds with ulceration (金瘡瘡敗), lightens the body, and makes one not hungry and able to endure old age (輕身不飢耐老).
