## 貝子

### Source Text

《本經》原文：貝子，味鹹，平。主目翳，鬼注蠱毒，腹痛下血，五癃，利水道。燒用之良。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Beizi, flavor salty, neutral. It governs eye opacities, ghost zhu and gu poison, abdominal pain with blood discharge, the five urinary retentions, and benefits the water passage. Burning it is good.
