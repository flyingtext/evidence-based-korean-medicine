## 白頭翁

### Source Text

生嵩山及田野，四月采。《本經》原文：白頭翁，味苦，溫。主溫瘧狂易寒熱，症瘕積聚癭氣，逐血止痛，療金瘡。

### Literal Translation

It grows on Songshan and in the fields; gathered in the fourth month. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 白頭翁 (báitóuwēng), flavor bitter, warm. It governs warm malaria, mania, and cold-heat (溫瘧狂易寒熱), masses, accumulations, and goiter-qi (症瘕積聚癭氣), expels blood and stops pain (逐血止痛), and treats metal wounds (療金瘡).
