## 鹵鹹

### Source Text

味鹹，無毒。去五臟腸胃留熱，結氣，心下堅，食已嘔逆，喘滿，明目，目痛，生河鹽池。

### Literal Translation

Flavor salty, non-toxic. It removes retained heat in the five viscera and intestines and stomach, bound qi (結氣), hardness below the heart, vomiting and retching after eating, wheezing and fullness, brightens the eyes, and treats eye pain. It grows in the Heyan salt pond.
