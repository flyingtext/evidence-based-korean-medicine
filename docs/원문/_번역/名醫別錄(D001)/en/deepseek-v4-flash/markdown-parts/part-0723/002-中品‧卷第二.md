## 白薇

### Source Text

味鹹，大寒，無毒。主治傷中淋露，下水氣，利陰氣，益精。一名白幕，一名薇草，一名春草，一名骨美。

### Literal Translation

Flavor salty, greatly cold, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat injury of the center with dripping discharge (淋露), down-drive water qi, benefit yin qi, and boost essence. One name is 白幕, one name is 薇草, one name is 春草, one name is 骨美.
