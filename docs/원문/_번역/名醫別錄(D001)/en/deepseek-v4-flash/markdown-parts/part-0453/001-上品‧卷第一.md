## 葡萄

### Source Text

《本經》原文：葡萄，味甘，平。主筋骨涇痹，益氣倍力，強志，令人肥健，耐飢，忍風寒

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Putao (grape), sweet in flavor, neutral. It chiefly treats bi (impediment) of the sinews and bones, boosts qi, doubles strength, strengthens the will, makes one plump and strong, endures hunger, and withstands wind and cold.
