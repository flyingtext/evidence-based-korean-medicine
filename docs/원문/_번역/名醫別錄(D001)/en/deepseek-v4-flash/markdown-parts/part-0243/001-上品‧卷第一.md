## 菟絲子

### Source Text

（宜丸不宜煮，得酒良，松脂為之使，惡　菌。）

《本經》原文：菟絲子，味辛，平。主續絕傷，補不足，益氣力，肥健。

### Literal Translation

(It is suitable for pills and not for decoction; with wine it is good. Pine resin [松脂] is its envoy; it is averse to [菌].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Dodder seed [菟絲子], flavor pungent, neutral. It mainly continues severed damage, supplements insufficiency, boosts qi and strength, and makes one fat and strong.
