## 大戟

### Source Text

主蠱毒，十二水，腫滿急痛積聚，中風皮膚疼痛，吐逆。一名邛鉅。

It governs gu-poison (蠱毒), the twelve waters (十二水), swelling, fullness, urgent pain, and accumulations, wind-stroke with pain of the skin (中風皮膚疼痛), and vomiting and retching. One name is 邛鉅 (qióngjù).
