## 龍腦香及膏香

### Source Text

味辛、苦，微寒，一云溫，平，無毒。主治心腹邪氣，風濕積聚，耳膚翳。出婆律國，形似白松脂，作杉木氣，明淨者善；久經風日，或如雀米炭、相思子貯之，則不耗。

### Literal Translation

Acrid and bitter in flavor, slightly cold; one account says warm and neutral, non-toxic. It treats evil qi in the heart and abdomen (心腹邪氣), wind-damp accumulations, and nebulae (翳) of the ears and skin. It comes from the kingdom of Bolü (婆律國); in form it resembles white pine-resin (白松脂), emits a cedar-wood odor; the clear and bright ones are good. If stored for a long time exposed to wind and sun, or placed with queimeitan (雀米炭) and xiangsizi (相思子) seeds, it does not diminish.
