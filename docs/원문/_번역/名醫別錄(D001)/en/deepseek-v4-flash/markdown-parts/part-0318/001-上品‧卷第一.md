## 牡荊實

### Source Text

味苦，溫，無毒。主除骨間寒熱，通利胃氣，止咳逆，下氣。生河間南陽宛朐山谷平壽、都鄉高堤岸上，牡荊生田野。

### Literal Translation

Bitter in flavor, warm, non-toxic. It chiefly removes cold-heat between the bones, frees and benefits the stomach qi, stops cough with counterflow, and sends qi downward. It grows in the valleys of Hejian, Nanyang, and Wanqu, and on the banks of high dikes at Pingshou and Duxiang; Mujing grows in the fields.
