## 細辛

### Source Text

二月、八月采根，陰乾。（曾青、桑白皮為之使，反藜蘆、惡野狼毒、山茱萸、黃　，畏滑石、硝石。）

《本經》原文：細辛，味辛，溫。

### Literal Translation

Gather the root in the second and eighth months and dry in the shade. (Layered azurite [曾青] and mulberry white bark [桑白皮] are its envoys; it is incompatible with Veratrum [藜蘆], and is averse to wolf poison [野狼毒], Cornus [山茱萸] and [黃], and fears talc [滑石] and nitre [硝石].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Asarum [細辛], flavor pungent, warm.
