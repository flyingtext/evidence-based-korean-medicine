### Source Text

無毒。主治肢節不利，大飽絕力身重。生太山，九月采。

Non-toxic. It mainly treats discomfort of the limbs and joints, and heaviness of the body from overeating and exhaustion of strength. It grows on Mount Tai; gather it in the ninth month.
