## 硝石

### Source Text

生益州，及武都、隴西、西羌，采無時。（螢火為之使，惡苦參、苦菜，畏女菀。）

《本經》原文：硝石，味苦，寒。

### Literal Translation

It grows in Yizhou, and in Wudu, Longxi and Western Qiang; gather it at any time. (Firefly [螢火] is its envoy; it is averse to Sophora flavescens [苦參] and bitter greens [苦菜], and fears Aster [女菀].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Nitre [硝石], flavor bitter, cold.
