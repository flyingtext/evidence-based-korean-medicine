## 酸棗

### Source Text

八月采實，陰乾，卅日成。（惡防己。）

《本經》原文：酸棗，味酸，平。

### Literal Translation

Gather the fruits in the eighth month, dry them in the shade, and they are complete in thirty days. (It is averse to Fangji [防己].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Sour jujube [酸棗], flavor sour, neutral.
