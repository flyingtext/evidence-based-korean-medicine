## 雁肪

### Source Text

取無時。又，雁喉下白毛，療小兒癇。《本經》原文：雁肪，味甘，平。

### Literal Translation

Taken at no fixed time. Also, the white feathers under the throat of the wild goose treat infantile epilepsy. Original text of the Benjing: Yanfang (wild goose fat), sweet in flavor, neutral.
