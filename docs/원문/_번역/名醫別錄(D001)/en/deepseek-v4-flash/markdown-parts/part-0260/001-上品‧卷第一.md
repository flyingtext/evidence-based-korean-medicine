## 蒺藜子

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：蒺藜子，味苦，溫。主惡血，破症結積聚，喉痹乳難。久服，長肌肉，明目輕身。

### Literal Translation

)

Original text of the Benjing: Jilizi (Tribulus fruit), bitter in flavor, warm. It chiefly treats evil blood, breaks zheng accumulations and ji gatherings, throat bi (impediment), and difficult lactation. Taken over a long time, it grows the flesh, brightens the eyes, and lightens the body.
