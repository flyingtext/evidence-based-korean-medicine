## 枸杞

### Source Text

一名羊乳，一名卻暑，一名仙人杖，一名西王母杖。常山及諸丘陵阪岸上。冬采根，春夏采葉，秋采莖實。

### Literal Translation

One name is Yangru [羊乳], one name is Queshu [卻暑], one name is Xianren Zhang [仙人杖], one name is Xi Wangmu Zhang [西王母杖]. [It grows on] Mount Chang and on the slopes and banks of all hills. Gather the root in winter, the leaves in spring and summer, and the stems and fruits in autumn.
