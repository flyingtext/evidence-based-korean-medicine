## 黃石脂

### Source Text

味苦，平，無毒。主養脾氣，安五臟，調中，大人小兒泄痢腸　，下膿血，去白蟲黃膽，癰疽蟲。久服輕身延年。

### Literal Translation

Flavor bitter, neutral, non-toxic. It mainly nourishes spleen qi, settles the five viscera, harmonizes the center, treats diarrhea and dysentery and intestinal [disease] in adults and children, downflow of pus and blood, removes white worms and jaundice [黃膽], and abscesses and worms. Taken over a long time, it lightens the body and prolongs life.
