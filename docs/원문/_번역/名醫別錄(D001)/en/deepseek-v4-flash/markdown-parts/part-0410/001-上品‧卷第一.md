## 阿膠

### Source Text

微溫，無毒。主丈夫少腹痛，虛勞羸瘦，陰氣不足，腳酸不能久立，養肝氣。生東平郡，煮牛皮作之。

### Literal Translation

Slightly warm, non-toxic. It chiefly treats pain of the lower abdomen in men, deficiency and emaciation from exhaustion, insufficiency of yin qi, soreness of the feet such that one cannot stand long, and nourishes the liver qi. It grows in Dongping commandery; made by boiling cowhide.
