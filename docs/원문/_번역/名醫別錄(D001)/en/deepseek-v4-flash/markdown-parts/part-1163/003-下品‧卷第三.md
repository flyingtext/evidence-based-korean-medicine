## 澤漆

### Source Text

味辛，無毒。利大小腸，明目，輕身。一名漆莖，大戟苗也。

### Literal Translation

Flavor acrid, non-toxic. It benefits the large and small intestines, brightens the eyes, and lightens the body. One name is 漆莖 (qījīng); it is the sprout of 大戟 (dàjǐ).
