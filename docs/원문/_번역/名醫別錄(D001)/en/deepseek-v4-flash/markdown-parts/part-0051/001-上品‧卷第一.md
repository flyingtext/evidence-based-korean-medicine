## 白石英

### Source Text

生華陰山。大如指，長二、三寸，六面如削，白澈有光。其黃端白棱名黃石英，赤端名赤石端名青石英，黑端名黑石英。

### Literal Translation

It grows on Mount Huayin. It is as large as a finger, two or three cun long, with six faces as if cut, white and clear with luster. Its yellow end with white edges is called Yellow Quartz [黃石英]; the red end is called Red Quartz [赤石英]; the blue end is called Blue Quartz [青石英]; the black end is called Black Quartz [黑石英].
