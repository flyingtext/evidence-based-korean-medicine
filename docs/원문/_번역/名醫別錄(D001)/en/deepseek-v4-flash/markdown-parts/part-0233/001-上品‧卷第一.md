## 薏苡仁

### Source Text

八月采實，采根無時。《本經》原文：薏苡仁，味甘，微寒。主筋急拘攣，不可屈伸，風濕痹，下氣。

### Literal Translation

Gather the fruits in the eighth month; gather the root at any time. Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Coix seed [薏苡仁], flavor sweet, slightly cold. It mainly treats urgency, cramping and contraction of the sinews that cannot bend or extend, wind-damp bi [風濕痹], and sends qi downward.
