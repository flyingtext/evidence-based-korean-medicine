## 卵白

### Source Text

微寒。治目熱赤痛，除心下伏熱，止煩滿，咳逆，小兒下泄，婦人產難，胞衣不出。

醯漬之一宿，治黃膽，破大煩熱。

### Literal Translation

Slightly cold. It treats red, painful, hot eyes, removes hidden heat below the heart, stops vexation and fullness, cough with counterflow, diarrhea in children, difficult childbirth in women, and the placenta not coming out.

Soaked in vinegar for one night, it treats jaundice and breaks great vexation and heat.
