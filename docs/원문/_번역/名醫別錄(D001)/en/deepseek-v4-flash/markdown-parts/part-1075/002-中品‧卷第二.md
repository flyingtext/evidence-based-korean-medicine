## 小麥

### Source Text

味甘，微寒，無毒。主除熱，止燥渴、咽乾，利小便，養肝氣，止漏血唾血。以作曲溫。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, slightly cold, non-toxic. It treats removing heat (除熱), stopping dry thirst (燥渴) and dry throat (咽乾), benefiting urination (利小便), nourishing liver qi (養肝氣), and stopping leaking blood and spitting blood (漏血唾血). Made into qu (曲), warm.
