## 白堊

### Source Text

生邯鄲，采無時。《本經》原文：白堊，味苦，溫。主女子寒熱症瘕，月閉積聚，陰腫痛，漏下無子。

### Literal Translation

It grows in Handan; gathered at no fixed time. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 白堊 (bái'è), flavor bitter, warm. It governs cold-heat masses (症瘕) in women, amenorrhea with accumulations (月閉積聚), swelling and pain of the genitals, and leaking below with no child (漏下無子).
