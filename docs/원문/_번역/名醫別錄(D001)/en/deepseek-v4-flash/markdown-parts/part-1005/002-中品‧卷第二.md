## 文蛤

### Source Text

味鹹，平，無毒。主治咳逆胸痹，腰痛脅急，鼠　，大孔出血，崩中漏下。生東海，有文，取無時。

### Literal Translation

Salty in flavor, neutral, non-toxic. It treats cough with rebellion and chest bi (咳逆胸痹), lumbar pain and hypochondriac urgency (脅急), rat [fistula] (鼠[blank]), bleeding from the great orifice (大孔出血), and flooding and leaking from the center (崩中漏下). It grows in the East Sea, has markings (文), and is gathered at any season.
