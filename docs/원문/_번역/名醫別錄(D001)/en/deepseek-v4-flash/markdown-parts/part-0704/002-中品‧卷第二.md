## 秦皮

### Source Text

大寒，無毒。主治男子少精，婦人帶下，小兒癇，身熱，可作洗目湯。久服皮膚光澤肥大，有子。

### Literal Translation

Greatly cold, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat men's scant essence, women's vaginal discharge (帶下), children's epilepsy, and body heat; it may be made into an eye-washing decoction. Taken over a long time the skin becomes glossy and plump, and one bears children.
