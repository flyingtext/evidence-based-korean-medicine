## 蘩蔞

### Source Text

味酸，平，無毒。主治積年惡瘡不愈。五月五日日中采，干，用之當燔。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sour, neutral, non-toxic. It governs long-standing malignant sores that do not heal. Gather at noon on the fifth day of the fifth month, dry it, and when using it one should roast it.
