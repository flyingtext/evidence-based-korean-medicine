## 銀屑

### Source Text

味辛，平，有毒。主安五臟，定心神，止驚悸，除邪氣，久服輕身長年。生永昌，無時。

### Literal Translation

Flavor pungent, level-natured, poisonous. Its chief action is to calm the five viscera, settle the mind-heart, stop fright-palpitations, dispel pathogenic qi; taken over a long time it lightens the body and prolongs years. It grows in Yongchang; (gathered) without a fixed season.
