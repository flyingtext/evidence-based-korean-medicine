## 金屑

### Source Text

味辛，平，有毒。主鎮精神，堅骨髓，通利五臟，除邪毒瓦斯，服之神仙。生益州，采時。

### Literal Translation

Flavor pungent, level-natured, poisonous. Its chief action is to settle the spirit-shen, strengthen the bone marrow, dredge and free the five viscera, dispel pathogenic toxic qi; taking it makes one an immortals' adept. It grows in Yizhou; gather according to the proper season.

### Translator's Notes

- 金屑 realgar gold filings; 邪毒瓦斯 glossed as poisonous qi (毒氣).
