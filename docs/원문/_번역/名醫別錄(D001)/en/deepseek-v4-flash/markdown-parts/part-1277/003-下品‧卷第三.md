## 女菀

### Source Text

無毒。主治肺傷、咳逆，出汗，久寒在膀胱支滿，飲酒夜食發病。一名白菀，一名織菀，一名苑。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It governs injury of the lung with cough and retching (肺傷、咳逆), sweating (出汗), long-standing cold in the bladder with fullness (久寒在膀胱支滿), and disease from drinking wine and eating at night (飲酒夜食發病). One name is 白菀 (báiwǎn), one name is 織菀 (zhīwǎn), one name is 苑 (yuàn).
