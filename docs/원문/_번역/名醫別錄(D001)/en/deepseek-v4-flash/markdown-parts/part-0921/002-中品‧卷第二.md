## 牛角鰓

### Source Text

主安五臟，焦，溫骨髓，補中，續絕傷，益氣，止泄利，消渴，以酒服之。膽，味苦，大寒。除心腹熱渴，利，口焦燥，益目精。

### Literal Translation

It treats settling the five viscera (安五臟), [the] scorched [part] warms the bone marrow (焦、溫骨髓), supplements the center (補中), restores severed injuries (續絕傷), boosts qi (益氣), stops diarrhea (泄利), [and treats] wasting-thirst (消渴); it is taken with wine. The gall (膽), bitter in flavor, greatly cold. It removes heat-thirst of the heart and abdomen (心腹熱渴), benefits [urination], mouth dryness and scorching, and benefits the eyes' essence (益目精).
