## 屈草

### Source Text

《本經》原文：屈草，味苦。主胸脅下痛邪氣，腸間寒熱陰痹。久服，輕身益氣耐老。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Qucao, bitter in flavor. It chiefly treats pain below the chest and flanks with evil qi, and cold-heat yin bi (impediment) in the intestines. Taken over a long time, it lightens the body, boosts qi, and endures aging.
