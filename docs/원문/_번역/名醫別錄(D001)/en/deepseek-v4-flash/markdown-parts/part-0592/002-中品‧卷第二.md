## 黃連

### Source Text

二月、八月采。（黃芩、龍骨、理石為之使，菊花、芫花、玄參、白蘚，畏款冬，勝烏頭，解巴豆毒。）

《本經》原文：黃連，味苦，寒。

### Literal Translation

Gather in the second and eighth months. (Scutellaria 黃芩, dragon bone 龍骨, and 理石 are its envoys; [it is averse to] chrysanthemum 菊花, 芫花, figwort 玄參, and 白蘚, is in dread of coltsfoot 款冬, overcomes aconite 烏頭, and resolves the poison of croton 巴豆.) Original text of the Benjing: Coptis (黃連), flavor bitter
