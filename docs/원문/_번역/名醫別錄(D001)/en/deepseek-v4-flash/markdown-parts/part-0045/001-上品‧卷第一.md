## 滑石

### Source Text

惡曾青。）

《本經》原文：滑石，味甘，寒。主身熱泄　，女子乳難癃閉，利小便，蕩胃中積聚寒熱，益精氣。

### Literal Translation

It is averse to layered azurite [曾青].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Talc [滑石], flavor sweet, cold. It mainly treats heat of the body with diarrhea, difficult lactation and strangury and urinary obstruction in women, frees urination, washes away accumulations of cold and heat in the stomach, and boosts essence qi.
