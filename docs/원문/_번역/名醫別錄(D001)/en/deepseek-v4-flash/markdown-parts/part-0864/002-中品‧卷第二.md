## 英草華

### Source Text

味辛，平，無毒。主治痹氣，強陰，治面勞疽，解煩，堅筋骨，治風頭。可作沐藥蔓木上。

### Literal Translation

Acrid in flavor, neutral, non-toxic. It treats bi qi (痹氣), strengthens the genitals (強陰), treats facial laoju (面勞疽), resolves vexation (煩), strengthens the sinews and bones, and treats wind headache. It can be made into a hair-washing drug and climbs on vines and trees (蔓木上).
