## 水蛭

### Source Text

五月、六月采，曝乾。《本經》原文：水蛭，味鹹，平。主逐惡血瘀血月閉，破血瘕積聚，無子，利水道。

### Literal Translation

Gather in the fifth and sixth months and dry in the sun. Original text of the Benjing: Shuizhi, flavor salty, neutral. It expels malignant blood and stagnant blood with amenorrhea, breaks blood masses and accumulations, childlessness, and benefits the water passage.
