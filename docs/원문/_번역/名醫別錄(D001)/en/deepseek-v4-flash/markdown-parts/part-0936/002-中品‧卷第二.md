## 心

### Source Text

治消渴，破症堅積聚，男子伏梁積疝，婦人瘕疾。銅器承飲之。又，馬毛，主小兒驚癇。

### Literal Translation

It treats wasting-thirst (消渴), breaks firm masses and accumulations (症堅積聚), men's fuliang (伏梁) accumulated hernia (積疝), and women's ji disease (瘕疾). Gather it in a copper vessel and drink it. Also, horse's mane (馬毛) treats children's fright-epilepsy (小兒驚癇).
