## 梔子

### Source Text

大寒，無毒。主治目熱赤痛，胸心大小腸大熱，心中煩悶，胃中熱氣。一名越桃，生陽。

### Literal Translation

Greatly cold, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat heat-red pain of the eyes, great heat of the chest, heart, large and small intestines, vexation and oppression within the heart, and heat qi within the stomach. One name is 越桃. It grows in Yang [zhou].
