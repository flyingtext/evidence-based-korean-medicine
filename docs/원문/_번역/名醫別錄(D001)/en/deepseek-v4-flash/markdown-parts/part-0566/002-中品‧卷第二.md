## 遂石

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主治消渴，傷中，益氣。生太山陰，采無時。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat wasting-thirst, injury of the center (傷中), and boost qi. It grows on the shaded side of Mount Tai; gather without a fixed time.
