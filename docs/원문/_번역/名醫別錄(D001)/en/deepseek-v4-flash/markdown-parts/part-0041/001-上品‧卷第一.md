## 芒硝

### Source Text

味辛、苦，大寒。主治五臟積聚，久熱、胃閉，除邪氣，破留血、腹中淡實結搏，通脈，利大小便及月水，破五淋，推陳致新。生於朴硝。

### Literal Translation

Flavor pungent and bitter, greatly cold. It mainly treats accumulations and gatherings of the five viscera, prolonged heat and closure of the stomach, removes evil qi, breaks retained blood and hard bound phlegm and excess in the abdomen, frees the vessels, frees the large and small intestines and menstruation, breaks the five stranguries [五淋], and expels the old and brings forth the new. It is produced from crude mirabilite [朴硝].
