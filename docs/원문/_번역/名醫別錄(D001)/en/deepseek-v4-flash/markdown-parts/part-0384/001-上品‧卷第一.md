## 麝香

### Source Text

生中台，及益州，雍州山中。春分取之，生者益良。《本經》原文：麝香，味辛，溫。

### Literal Translation

It grows in Zhongtai and in the mountains of Yizhou and Yongzhou. Taken at the spring equinox; the fresh one is better. Original text of the Benjing: Shexiang (musk), pungent in flavor, warm.
