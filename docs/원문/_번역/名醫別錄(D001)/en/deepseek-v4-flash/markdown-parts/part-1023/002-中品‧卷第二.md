## 烏賊魚骨

### Source Text

生東海，取無時。（惡白蘞、白芨、附子。）

《本經》原文：烏賊魚骨，味鹹，微溫。

### Literal Translation

It grows in the East Sea and is gathered at any season. (Averse to bailian (白蘞), baiji (白芨), and fuzi (附子).)

The original text of the Benjing: Wuzeiyu gu (烏賊魚骨), salty in flavor, slightly warm.
