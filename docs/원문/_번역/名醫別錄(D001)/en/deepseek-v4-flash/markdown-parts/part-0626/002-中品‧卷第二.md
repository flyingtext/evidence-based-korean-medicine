## 大青

### Source Text

味苦，大寒，無毒。主治時氣頭痛，大熱，口瘡。三月、四月采莖，陰乾。

### Literal Translation

Flavor bitter, greatly cold, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat seasonal headache, great heat, and mouth sores. Gather the stem in the third and fourth months, dry in the shade.
