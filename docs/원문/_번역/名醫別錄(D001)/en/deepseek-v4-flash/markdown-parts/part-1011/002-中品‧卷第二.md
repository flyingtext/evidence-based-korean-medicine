## 蠡魚

### Source Text

無毒。主治五痔，有瘡者不可食，令人瘢白。生九江，取無時。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It treats the five hemorrhoids (五痔); those who have sores must not eat it, for it makes people's scars white (瘢白). It grows in Jiujiang and is gathered at any season.
