## 芍藥

### Source Text

味酸，微寒，有小毒。主通順血脈，緩中，散惡血，逐賊血，去水氣，利膀胱、大小腸，消癰腫，時行寒熱，中惡，腹痛，腰痛。一名白木，一名余容，一名犁食，一名解倉，一名　。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sour, slightly cold, slightly poisonous. Its chief actions are to course and free the blood vessels, relax the center, disperse foul blood, drive out robber blood (賊血), dispel water qi, benefit the bladder and large and small intestines, dissipate abscesses and swellings, seasonal chills and fever, attack by noxious qi, abdominal pain, and lumbar pain. One name is 白 [芍].
