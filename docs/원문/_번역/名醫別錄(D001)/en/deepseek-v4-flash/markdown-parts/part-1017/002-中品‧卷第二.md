## 鱉甲

### Source Text

無毒。主治溫瘧，血瘕，腰痛，小兒脅下堅。肉，味甘，治傷中，益氣，補不足。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It treats warm malaria (溫瘧), blood ji (血瘕), lumbar pain, and hardness below the flanks in children (小兒脅下堅). The flesh (肉), sweet in flavor, treats injury of the center (傷中), boosts qi (益氣), and supplements insufficiency (補不足).
