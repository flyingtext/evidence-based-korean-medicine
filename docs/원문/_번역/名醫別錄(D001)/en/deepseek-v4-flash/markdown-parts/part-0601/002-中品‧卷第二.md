## 芍藥

### Source Text

生中岳及丘陵。二月、八月采根，曝乾。（須丸為之使，惡石斛、芒硝，畏硝石、鱉甲、小薊，反藜蘆。

### Literal Translation

It grows in the central sacred mount and in hillocks. Gather the root in the second and eighth months, expose to sun to dry. (須丸 is its envoy; it is averse to dendrobium 石斛 and mirabilite 芒硝, is in dread of saltpeter 硝石, turtle shell 鱉甲, and 小薊, and is incompatible with veratrum 藜蘆.
