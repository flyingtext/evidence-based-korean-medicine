## 巴豆

### Source Text

生溫熟寒，有大毒。主治女子月閉，爛胎，金創膿血，不利丈夫陰，殺斑蝥毒。可練之，益血脈，令人色好，變化與鬼神通。

### Literal Translation

Raw it is warm, cooked it is cold, greatly toxic. It governs amenorrhea in women (女子月閉), rotting the fetus (爛胎), metal-wound pus and blood (金創膿血), is not beneficial to the male genitals (不利丈夫陰), and kills the poison of blister beetles (斑蝥毒). It may be refined; it benefits the blood vessels, makes one's complexion good, and its transformations communicate with ghosts and spirits.
