## 赤小豆

### Source Text

《本經》原文：赤小豆，主下水，排癰腫膿血。生平澤。

### Literal Translation

The original text of the Benjing: Chixiaodou (赤小豆) treats sending down water (下水) and expelling pus and blood from carbuncle-swelling (排癰腫膿血). It grows on level marshland.
