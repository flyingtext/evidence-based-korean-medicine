## 牡蠣

### Source Text

微寒，無毒。主除留熱在關節榮衛，虛熱去來不定，煩滿，止汗，心痛氣結，止渴，老血，澀大小腸，止大小便，治泄精，喉痹、咳嗽、心脅下痞熱。一名牡蛤。

### Literal Translation

Slightly cold, non-toxic. It chiefly removes retained heat in the joints and in the ying and wei, deficiency heat that comes and goes without fixed pattern, vexation and fullness, stops sweating, heart pain with qi binding, stops thirst, old blood, astringes the small and large intestines, stops urination and defecation, treats seminal emission, throat bi (impediment), cough, and glomus heat below the heart and flanks. One name is Muge.
