## 菖蒲

### Source Text

無毒。主治耳聾、癰瘡，溫腸胃，止小便利，四肢濕痹，不得屈伸，小兒溫瘧，身積不解，可作浴湯。久服聰耳明目，益心智，高志不老。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It mainly treats deafness and abscesses and sores, warms the intestines and stomach, stops excessive urination, and treats damp bi [濕痹] of the four limbs that cannot bend or extend, and warm malaria [溫瘧] in children with unresolved accumulation of the body; it can be made into a bath decoction. Taken over a long time, it sharpens the ears and brightens the eyes, boosts the mind and wisdom, and raises the will without aging.
