## 熊脂

### Source Text

生雍州，十一月取。《本經》原文：熊脂，味甘，微寒。主風痹不仁筋急，五臟腹中積聚，寒熱羸瘦，頭瘍白禿

### Literal Translation

It grows in Yongzhou; taken in the eleventh month. Original text of the Benjing: Xiongzhi (bear fat), sweet in flavor, slightly cold. It chiefly treats wind bi (impediment) with numbness and sinew contraction, accumulations and gatherings in the abdomen of the five viscera, cold-heat with emaciation, and head sores and white baldness.
