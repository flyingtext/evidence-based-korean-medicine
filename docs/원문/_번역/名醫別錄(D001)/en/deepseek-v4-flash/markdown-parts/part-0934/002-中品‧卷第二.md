## 心

### Source Text

主除熱下氣，長筋，強腰脊，壯健，強意利志，輕身不飢。脯，治寒熱痿痹。屎，名馬通，微溫。

### Literal Translation

It treats removing heat and sending qi downward (除熱下氣), growing the sinews (長筋), strengthening the lumbus and spine (強腰脊), making one robust and strong (壯健), strengthening the will and sharpening the resolve (強意利志), lightening the body, and not feeling hunger. The jerky (脯) treats chills and fever and wei and bi (痿痹). The dung is named Matong (馬通) and is slightly warm.
