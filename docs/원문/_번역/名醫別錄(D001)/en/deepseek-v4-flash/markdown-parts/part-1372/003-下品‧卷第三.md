## 羊桃

### Source Text

主　熱，身暴赤色，風水積聚，惡瘍，除小兒熱。一名鬼桃，一名羊腸。生川穀。

### Literal Translation

It governs  (yù) heat (熱), sudden redness of the body (身暴赤色), wind-water accumulations (風水積聚), malignant ulcers (惡瘍), and removes heat in children (除小兒熱). One name is 鬼桃 (guǐtáo), one name is 羊腸 (yángcháng). It grows in river valleys.
