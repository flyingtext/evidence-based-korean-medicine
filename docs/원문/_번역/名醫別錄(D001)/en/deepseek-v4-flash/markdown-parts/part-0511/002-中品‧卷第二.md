## 雌黃

### Source Text

味甘，大寒，有毒。蝕鼻中息肉，下部　瘡，身面白駁，散皮膚死肌，及恍惚邪氣殺蜂蛇毒。久服令人腦滿。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, greatly cold, poisonous. It erodes nasal polyps, lower-body ulcers, white patches on body and face, disperses dead sinew of the skin, and with trance-like (恍惚) pathogenic qi kills the poison of bees and snakes. Taken over a long time it causes the brain to fill.
