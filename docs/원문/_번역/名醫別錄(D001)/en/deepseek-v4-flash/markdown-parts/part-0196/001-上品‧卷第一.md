## 獨活

### Source Text

草得風不搖，無風自動。生雍州，或隴西南安。二月、八月采根，曝乾。

### Literal Translation

The herb does not sway in the wind, and moves by itself without wind. It grows in Yongzhou, or in Longxi and Nan'an. Gather the root in the second and eighth months and dry in the sun.
