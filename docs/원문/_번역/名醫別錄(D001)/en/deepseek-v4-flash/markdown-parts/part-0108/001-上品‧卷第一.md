## 天門冬

### Source Text

二月、三月、七月、八月采根，曝乾。（垣衣、地黃為之使，畏曾青。）

《本經》原文：天門冬，味苦，平。

### Literal Translation

Gather the root in the second, third, seventh and eighth months and dry in the sun. (Moss [垣衣] and Rehmannia [地黃] are its envoys; it fears layered azurite [曾青].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Asparagus [天門冬], flavor bitter, neutral.
