## 蔥實

### Source Text

《本經》原文：蔥實，味辛，溫。主明目，補中不足。其莖，可作湯，主傷寒寒熱，出汗，中風面目腫。

### Literal Translation

The original text of the Benjing: Congshi (蔥實), acrid in flavor, warm. It treats brightening the eyes and supplementing insufficiency in the center (補中不足). Its stem can be made into a decoction, and treats cold damage with chills and fever, sweating, and wind stroke with facial swelling (中風面目腫).
