## 地膚子

### Source Text

一名地麥。生荊州及田八月、十月采實，陰乾。又，地膚子，搗絞取汁，主赤白痢；洗目去熱暗，雀盲、澀痛。

### Literal Translation

One name is Dimai [地麥]. It grows in Jingzhou and in the fields; gather the fruits in the eighth and tenth months and dry in the shade. Also, pound and squeeze the juice of Kochia fruit [地膚子]; it mainly treats red and white dysentery; wash the eyes to remove heat and dimness, night blindness and astringent pain.
