## 牡桂

### Source Text

《本經》原文：牡桂，味辛，溫。主上氣咳逆結氣，喉痹吐吸，利關節，補中益氣。久服通神，輕身不老。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Mu Gui [牡桂], flavor pungent, warm. It mainly treats ascent of qi, cough and counterflow and bound qi, throat bi [喉痹] with spitting and inhaling, frees the joints, supplements the center and boosts qi. Taken over a long time, it penetrates the spirit, lightens the body and does not age.
