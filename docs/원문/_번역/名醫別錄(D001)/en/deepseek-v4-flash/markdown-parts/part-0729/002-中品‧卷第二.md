## 茅根

### Source Text

無毒。主下五淋，除客熱在腸胃，止渴，堅筋，婦人崩中。久服利人。

### Literal Translation

Not poisonous. It chiefly down-drives the five strangurias, dispels traveler-heat within the intestines and stomach, stops thirst, strengthens the sinews, and treats women's flooding (崩中). Taken over a long time it benefits persons.
