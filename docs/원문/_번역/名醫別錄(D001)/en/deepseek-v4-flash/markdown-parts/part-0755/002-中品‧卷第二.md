## 紫葳

### Source Text

一名陵苕，一名　華，一名陵時生西海及陽。《本經》原文：紫葳，味酸，微寒。主婦人產乳余疾，崩中，症瘕血閉，寒熱羸瘦，養胎。

### Literal Translation

One name is 陵苕, one name is 華, one name is 陵時. It grows at the West Sea and in Yang [zhou]. Original text of the Benjing: Campsis (紫葳), flavor sour, slightly cold. It chiefly treats leftover disease of women after childbirth and lactation, flooding, masses with blood stoppage, cold-heat and emaciation weakness, and nourishes the fetus.
