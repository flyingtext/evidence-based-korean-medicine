## 木虻

### Source Text

《本經》原文：木虻，味苦，平。主目赤痛，　傷淚出，瘀血血閉，寒熱酸　，無子。一名魂常。

Original text of the Benjing: Musha, flavor bitter, neutral. It governs red and painful eyes, injuries with tearing, stagnant blood and blood closure, chills and fever with soreness, and childlessness. One name is Hunchang.
