## 彼子

### Source Text

主腹中邪氣，去三蟲，蛇螫蠱毒，鬼注伏尸，生山谷。

### Literal Translation

It governs evil-qi in the abdomen (腹中邪氣), removes the three worms (去三蟲), snake-sting and gu-poison (蛇螫蠱毒), and ghost-attack and corpse-attack (鬼注伏尸). It grows in mountain valleys.
