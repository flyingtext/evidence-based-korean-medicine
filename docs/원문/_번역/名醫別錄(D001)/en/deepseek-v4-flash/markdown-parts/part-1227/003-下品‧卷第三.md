## 射干

### Source Text

微溫，有毒。主治老血在心肝脾間，咳唾，言語氣臭，散胸中氣。久服令人虛。

### Literal Translation

Slightly warm, toxic. It governs old blood between the heart, liver, and spleen (老血在心肝脾間), cough and spitting (咳唾), foul breath in speech (言語氣臭), and disperses the qi of the chest (散胸中氣). Taken for a long time it makes one deficient.
