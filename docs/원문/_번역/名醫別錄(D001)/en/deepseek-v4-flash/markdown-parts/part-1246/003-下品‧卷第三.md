## 桐葉

### Source Text

《本經》原文：桐葉，味苦，寒。主惡蝕瘡著陰。皮，主五痔，殺三蟲。

### Literal Translation

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 桐葉 (tóngyè), flavor bitter, cold. It governs evil erosive sores on the genitals (惡蝕瘡著陰). The bark governs the five hemorrhoids (五痔) and kills the three worms (殺三蟲).
