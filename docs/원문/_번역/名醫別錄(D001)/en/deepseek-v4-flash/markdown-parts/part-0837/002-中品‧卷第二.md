## 龍腦香及膏香

### Source Text

膏，主耳聾。又，龍腦治婦人難產，取龍腦研末少許，以新汲水調服，立瘥。

### Literal Translation

The ointment treats deafness. Also, longnao (龍腦) treats difficult labor in women; take a little of ground longnao and mix it with newly drawn well water and take it, and it is immediately cured.
