## 麻子

### Source Text

（畏牡蠣、白薇，惡茯苓。）

《本經》原文：麻　，味辛，平。主五勞七傷，利五臟，下血寒氣，多食，令人見鬼狂走。

### Literal Translation

(It fears Muli (oyster shell) and Baiwei (Cynanchum atratum), and is averse to Fuling (Poria).)

Original text of the Benjing: Ma (hemp), pungent in flavor, neutral. It chiefly treats the five labors and seven injuries, benefits the five viscera, sends blood and cold qi downward; eating much makes one see ghosts and run about madly.
