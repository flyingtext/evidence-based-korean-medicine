## 蠐螬

### Source Text

主惡血血瘀，御覽作血痹痹氣破折，血在脅下堅滿痛，月閉，目中淫膚

### Literal Translation

It treats malignant blood and blood stasis (惡血血瘀); the Yulan reads blood bi and bi qi (血痹痹氣), breaking and snapping (破折), blood at the flanks below with firmness, fullness, and pain (血在脅下堅滿痛), closure of the menses (月閉), and dim films within the eyes (目中淫膚).
