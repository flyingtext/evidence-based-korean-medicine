## 豚卵

### Source Text

齒，治小兒驚五月五日取。膏，主生發。肪膏，主煎諸膏藥，解斑蝥、芫青毒。

### Literal Translation

The teeth treat children's fright; take on the fifth day of the fifth month. The fat governs the growth of hair. The lard governs frying various ointments and medicines, and resolves the poison of Banmao and Yuanqing.
