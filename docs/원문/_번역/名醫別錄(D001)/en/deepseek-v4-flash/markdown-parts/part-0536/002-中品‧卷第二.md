## 陽起石

### Source Text

一生，一名羊起石，雲母根也。生齊山及　，或云山、陽起山，采無時。（桑螵蛸為之惡澤瀉、菌桂、雷丸、蛇蛻皮，畏菟絲。

### Literal Translation

One kind born [thus]; one name is 羊起石; it is the root of mica (雲母). It grows at Mount Qi and [elsewhere], or is said (to grow at) Mount Yun, Yangqi Mountain; gather without a fixed time. (Mantis ootheca 桑螵蛸 is averse to it; it is averse to Alisma 澤瀉, 菌桂, 雷丸, snake slough 蛇蛻皮, and is in dread of dodder 菟絲.

### Translator's Notes

- 陽起石 = actinolite; the phrase 一生 is obscure, possibly textual corruption.
