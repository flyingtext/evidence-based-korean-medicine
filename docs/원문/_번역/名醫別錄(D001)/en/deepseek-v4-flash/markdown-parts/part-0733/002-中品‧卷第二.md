## 百合

### Source Text

無毒。主除浮腫，臚脹，痞滿，寒熱，通身疼痛，及乳難喉痹腫，止涕淚。一名重一名重邁，一名摩羅，一名中逢花，一名強瞿。

### Literal Translation

Not poisonous. Its chief actions are to dispel edema, dropsy-distension (臚脹), glomus fullness (痞滿), cold-heat, pain throughout the whole body, and (treat) difficult lactation and throat impediment with swelling, and stop tears and nasal discharge. One name is 重 [瓣], one name 重邁, one name 摩羅, one name 中逢花, one name 強瞿.
