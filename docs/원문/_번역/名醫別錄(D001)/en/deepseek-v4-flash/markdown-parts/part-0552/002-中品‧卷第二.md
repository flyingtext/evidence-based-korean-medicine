## 鋼鐵

### Source Text

味甘，平，無毒。主治金創，煩滿熱中，胸膈氣塞，食不化。一名跳鐵。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, level-natured, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat metal wounds (金創), vexation and fullness with internal heat, obstruction and blockage of chest and diaphragm qi, and undigested food. One name is 跳鐵.
