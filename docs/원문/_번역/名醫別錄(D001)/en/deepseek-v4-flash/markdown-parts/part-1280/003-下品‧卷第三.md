味甘、酸，無毒。止膿血，諸　，惡瘡，熱瘡，消酒，除消渴，補絕傷，產後內塞。作金瘡膏。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet and sour, non-toxic. It stops pus and blood (膿血), the various  (lòu), malignant sores (惡瘡), heat sores (熱瘡), disperses wine (消酒), removes wasting-thirst (除消渴), mends severed injuries (補絕傷), and [treats] internal blockage after childbirth (產後內塞). It is made into a metal-wound ointment (金瘡膏).
