## 麻子

### Source Text

無毒。主治中風汗出，逐水，利小便，破積血，復血脈，乳婦產後余疾，長發，可為沐藥。久服神仙。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It chiefly treats wind stroke with sweating, expels water, promotes urination, breaks static blood, restores the blood vessels, treats residual illness of nursing women after childbirth, grows the hair, and may be used as a hair-washing medicine. Taken over a long time it becomes an immortal.
