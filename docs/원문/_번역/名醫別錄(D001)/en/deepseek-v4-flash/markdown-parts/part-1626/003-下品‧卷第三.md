## 蜚虻

### Source Text

五月腹有血者良。《本經》原文：蜚虻，味苦，微寒。主逐瘀血，破下血積，堅痞症瘕，寒熱，通利血脈及九竅。

### Literal Translation

In the fifth month, those with blood in the abdomen are good. Original text of the Benjing: Feisha, flavor bitter, slightly cold. It expels stagnant blood, breaks and discharges blood accumulations, hard bindings and abdominal masses, chills and fever, and unblocks the blood vessels and the nine orifices.
