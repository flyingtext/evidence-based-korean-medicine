## 青葙子

### Source Text

無毒。主治惡瘡、疥虱、痔蝕，下部　瘡。生平穀道傍。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It governs malignant sores, scabies and lice (惡瘡、疥虱), hemorrhoid erosion (痔蝕), and sores of the lower part (下部瘡). It grows beside the road in level valleys.
