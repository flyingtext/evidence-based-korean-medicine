寒，無毒。主除煩滿不樂，久服寒中。可作面脂，令悅澤。

Cold, non-toxic. It chiefly removes vexation and fullness with displeasure; taken over a long time it chills the center. It may be made into a face ointment to make one pleasing and lustrous.
