## 白蘚

### Source Text

味鹹，無毒。主治四肢不安，時行腹中大熱、飲水、欲走、大呼，小兒驚癇，婦人產余痛。生上穀及宛朐。

### Literal Translation

Flavor salty, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat restlessness of the four limbs, great heat within the abdomen at seasonal times, with desire to drink water, to run away, and to shout loudly, children's fright-epilepsy, and women's post-partum residual pain. It grows in Shanggu and Wanqu.
