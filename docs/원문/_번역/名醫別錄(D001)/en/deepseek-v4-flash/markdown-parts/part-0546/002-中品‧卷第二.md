## 長石

### Source Text

味苦，無毒。主治胃中結氣，止消渴，下氣，除脅肋肺間邪氣。一名土石，一名直石理如馬齒，方而潤澤玉色。

### Literal Translation

Flavor bitter, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat bound qi within the stomach, stop wasting-thirst, down-drive qi, and dispel pathogenic qi between the flanks, ribs, and lungs. One name is 土石, one name is 直石; its veining resembles horse teeth, square and moist, glossy like jade.
