## 鐵落

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。除胸膈中熱氣，食不下，止煩，去黑子。一名鐵液，可以染皂。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, not poisonous. It dispels heat qi within the chest and diaphragm, (treats) inability to eat, stops vexation, and removes black moles. One name is 鐵液; it can be used to dye black (皂).
