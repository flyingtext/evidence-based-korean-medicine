## 牡狗陰莖

### Source Text

心，治憂恚氣，除邪。腦，痹痛，療下部　瘡，鼻中息肉。齒，治癲癇，寒熱，卒風痱，伏日取之。

### Literal Translation

The heart treats qi of worry and anger (憂恚氣) and dispels evil. The brain (腦) [treats] bi-pain (痹痛), cures lower-body sores (下部瘡) and nasal polyps (鼻中息肉). The teeth treat mania-epilepsy (癲癇), chills and fever, and sudden wind-waste (卒風痱); they are taken on fu days.
