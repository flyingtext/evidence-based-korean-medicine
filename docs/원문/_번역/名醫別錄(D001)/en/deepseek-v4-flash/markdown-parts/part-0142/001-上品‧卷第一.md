## 菊花

### Source Text

生雍州田野。正月采根，三月采葉，五月采莖，九月采花，十一月采實，皆陰乾。（朮、枸杞根桑根白皮為之使。

### Literal Translation

It grows in the fields of Yongzhou. Gather the root in the first month, the leaves in the third month, the stems in the fifth month, the flowers in the ninth month, and the fruits in the eleventh month; all are dried in the shade. (Atractylodes [朮], wolfberry root [枸杞根] and mulberry root white bark [桑根白皮] are its envoys.
