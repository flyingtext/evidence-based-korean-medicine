## 蛇全

### Source Text

八月采，陰乾。《本經》原文：蛇全，味苦，微寒。主驚癇，寒熱邪氣，除熱，金創疽痔，鼠　惡瘡頭瘍。

### Literal Translation

Gathered in the eighth month, dried in the shade. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 蛇全 (shéquán), flavor bitter, slightly cold. It governs fright-epilepsy (驚癇), cold-heat and evil-qi (寒熱邪氣), removes heat (除熱), metal wounds, carbuncle and hemorrhoids (金創疽痔), rat [fistula] and malignant sores and head ulcers (鼠瘻惡瘡頭瘍).
