## 磁石

### Source Text

味鹹，無毒。主養腎臟，強骨氣，益精，除煩，通關節，消癰腫，鼠　，頸核，喉痛小兒驚癇，練水飲之。亦令人有子。

### Literal Translation

Flavor salty, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to nourish the kidney viscus, strengthen qi of the bones, boost essence, remove vexation, free the joints, dissipate abscesses and swellings, rat fistulas, swellings of the neck, sore throat, and children's fright-epilepsy (驚癇); taken dissolved in water (練水). It also enables one to bear children.

### Translator's Notes

- 練水飲之 interpreted as taking the magnetite by drinking a water infusion.
