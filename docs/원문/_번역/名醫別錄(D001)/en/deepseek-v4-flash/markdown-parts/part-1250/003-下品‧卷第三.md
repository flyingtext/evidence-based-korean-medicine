## 梓白皮

### Source Text

主熱，去三蟲。葉，搗敷豬瘡，飼豬肥大三倍。生山谷。

### Literal Translation

It governs heat and removes the three worms (去三蟲). The leaves, pounded and applied to pig sores, feed the pigs to make them fat threefold. It grows in mountain valleys.
