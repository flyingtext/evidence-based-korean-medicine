## 銅弩牙

### Source Text

主治婦人產難，血閉，月水不通，陰陽隔塞。

### Literal Translation

It governs difficult childbirth (產難) in women, blood-closure (血閉), failure of the menses to flow (月水不通), and blockage and separation of yin and yang (陰陽隔塞).
