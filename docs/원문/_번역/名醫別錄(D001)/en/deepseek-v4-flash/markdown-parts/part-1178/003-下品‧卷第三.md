## 鉤吻

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：鉤吻，味辛，溫。主金創乳　，中惡風，咳逆上氣，水腫，殺鬼注蠱毒。一名野葛。

### Literal Translation

)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 鉤吻 (gōuwěn), flavor acrid, warm. It governs metal wounds and breast [sores] (金創乳), attack by evil wind (中惡風), cough and retching with upward counterflow (咳逆上氣), edema (水腫), and kills ghost-attack and gu-poison (鬼注蠱毒). One name is 野葛 (yěgé).
