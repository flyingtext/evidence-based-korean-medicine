## 白並

### Source Text

味苦，無毒。主治肺咳上氣，行五臟，令百病不起。一名王蕭，一名箭悍。

### Literal Translation

Bitter in flavor, non-toxic. It treats lung cough with rebellious qi (肺咳上氣), circulates through the five viscera (五臟), and causes the hundred diseases not to arise. One name is Wangxiao (王蕭), one is Jianhan (箭悍).
