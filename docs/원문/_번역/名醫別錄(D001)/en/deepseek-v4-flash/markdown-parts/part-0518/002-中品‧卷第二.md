## 殷孽

### Source Text

生趙國，又梁山及南海，采無時。（惡朮、防己。）

《本經》原文：殷孽，味辛，溫。

### Literal Translation

It grows in the state of Zhao, also on Mount Liang and at Nanhai; gather without a fixed time. (Averse to atractylodes 朮 and sinomenium 防己.) Original text of the Benjing: Yin'nie (殷孽), flavor pungent, warm.
