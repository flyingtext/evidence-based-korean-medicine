## 松蘿

### Source Text

五月采，陰乾

《本經》原文：松蘿，味苦，平。主　怒邪氣，止虛汗，頭風，女子陰寒腫痛。一名女蘿。

### Literal Translation

Gather in the fifth month, dry in the shade. Original text of the Benjing: Usnea (松蘿), flavor bitter, level. It chiefly treats anger-pathogen qi (怒邪氣), stops vexation sweat, [treats] head wind, and women's cold swelling and pain within the genitals. One name is 女蘿.
