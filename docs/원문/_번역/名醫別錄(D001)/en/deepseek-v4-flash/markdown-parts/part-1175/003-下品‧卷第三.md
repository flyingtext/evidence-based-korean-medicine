## 鉤吻

### Source Text

有大毒。破症積，除腳膝痹痛，四肢拘攣，惡瘡疥蟲，殺鳥獸。折之青煙出者，名其熱一宿，不入湯。

### Literal Translation

Greatly toxic. It breaks masses and accumulations (症積), removes bi pain of the legs and knees (腳膝痹痛), contracture of the four limbs (四肢拘攣), malignant sores and scabies worms (惡瘡疥蟲), and kills birds and beasts. When broken, those from which blue smoke issues are named...; its heat [should be] one night; it must not enter a decoction.
