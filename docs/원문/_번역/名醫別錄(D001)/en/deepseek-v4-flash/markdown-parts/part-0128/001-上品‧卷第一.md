## 菖蒲

### Source Text

（秦皮、秦艽為之使，惡地膽、麻黃去節。）

《本經》原文：菖蒲，味辛，溫。主風寒濕痹，咳逆上氣，開心孔，補五臟，通九竅，明耳目，出聲音。

### Literal Translation

(Fraxinus bark [秦皮] and Gentiana macrophylla [秦艽] are its envoys; it is averse to Euphorbia [地膽] and Ephedra with the nodes removed [麻黃去節].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Acorus [菖蒲], flavor pungent, warm. It mainly treats wind-cold-damp bi [風寒濕痹], cough and counterflow ascent of qi, opens the heart orifice, supplements the five viscera, frees the nine orifices, brightens the ears and eyes, and brings forth the voice.
