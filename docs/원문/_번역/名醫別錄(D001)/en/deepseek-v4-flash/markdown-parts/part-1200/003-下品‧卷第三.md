## 蓄

### Source Text

五月采，陰乾。《本經》原文：　蓄，味辛，平。主浸淫疥瘙疽痔，殺三蟲。

### Literal Translation

Gathered in the fifth month, dried in the shade. The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 蓄 (biān) 蓄, flavor acrid, neutral. It governs spreading erosion, scabies, itching, carbuncle, and hemorrhoids (浸淫疥瘙疽痔), and kills the three worms (殺三蟲).
