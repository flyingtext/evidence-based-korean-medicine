## 生地黃

### Source Text

生咸陽黃土地者佳。二月、八月采根，陰乾。（得麥門冬、清酒良，惡貝母，畏蕪荑。

### Literal Translation

That which grows in the yellow earth of Xianyang is good. Gather the root in the second and eighth months and dry in the shade. (With Ophiopogon [麥門冬] and clear wine it is good; it is averse to Fritillaria [貝母] and fears Ulmus macrocarpa [蕪荑].
