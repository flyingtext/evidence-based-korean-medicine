## 濃朴

### Source Text

三月、九月、十月采皮，陰乾。（乾薑之使，惡澤瀉、寒水石、硝石。）

《本經》原文：濃朴，味苦，溫。

### Literal Translation

Gather the bark in the third, ninth, and tenth months, dry in the shade. (Its envoy is dried ginger 乾薑; averse to alisma 澤瀉, glauberite 寒水石, and saltpeter 硝石.) Original text of the Benjing: Magnolia bark (厚朴), flavor bitter, warm.
