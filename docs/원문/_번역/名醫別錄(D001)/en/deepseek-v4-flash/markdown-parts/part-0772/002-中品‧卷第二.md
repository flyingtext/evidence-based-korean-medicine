## 營實

### Source Text

微寒，無毒。久服輕身益氣。根，止泄利腹痛，五臟客熱，除邪逆氣，疽癩，諸惡瘡金瘡，傷撻，生肉復肌。

### Literal Translation

Slightly cold, not poisonous. Taken over a long time it lightens the body and boosts qi. The root stops diarrhea and abdominal pain, the traveler-heat of the five viscera, dispels pathogenic counter-flow qi, skin ulcers and leprosy, the various foul ulcers, metal wounds and blows, and generates flesh to restore the sinews.
