## 石長生

### Source Text

《本經》原文：石長生，味鹹，微寒。主寒熱惡瘡大熱，辟鬼氣不祥。一名丹草。

### Literal Translation

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 石長生 (shíchángshēng), flavor salty, slightly cold. It governs cold-heat, malignant sores, and great heat, and wards off ghost-qi and inauspiciousness (辟鬼氣不祥). One name is 丹草 (dāncǎo).
