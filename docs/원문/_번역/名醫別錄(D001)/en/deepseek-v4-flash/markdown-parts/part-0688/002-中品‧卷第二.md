## 萆

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：萆　，味苦，平。主腰背痛強，骨節風寒濕周痹，惡瘡不瘳，熱氣。生山谷。

### Literal Translation

) Original text of the Benjing: Dioscorea (萆), flavor bitter, level. It chiefly treats pain and stiffness of the lumbar back, wind-cold-damp generalized impediment of the bones and joints, foul wounds that do not heal, and heat qi. It grows in mountain valleys.
