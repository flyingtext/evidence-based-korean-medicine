## 防葵

### Source Text

《本經》原文：防葵，味辛，寒。主疝瘕腸泄，膀胱熱結，溺不下，咳逆溫瘧，癲癇驚邪狂走。久服堅骨髓

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Peucedanum [防葵], flavor pungent, cold. It mainly treats hernia and jia [疝瘕] with intestinal diarrhea, heat binding of the bladder, failure of urine to descend, cough and counterflow with warm malaria [溫瘧], and mania, epilepsy, fright, evil and running. Taken over a long time, it firms the bone marrow
