## 蓍實

### Source Text

《本經》原文：蓍實，味苦，平。主益氣，充肌膚，明目，聰慧先知。久服，不飢不老輕身

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Achillea fruit [蓍實], flavor bitter, neutral. It mainly boosts qi, fills the skin and flesh, brightens the eyes, and makes one intelligent and prescient. Taken over a long time, one does not hunger, does not age and lightens the body
