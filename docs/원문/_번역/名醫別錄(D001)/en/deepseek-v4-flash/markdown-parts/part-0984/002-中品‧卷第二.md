## 蚱蟬

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主治驚悸，婦人乳難，胞衣不出，又墮胎。五月采，蒸干之，勿令蠹。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, non-toxic. It treats fright-palpitation (驚悸), women's difficult lactation (乳難), the placenta not emerging (胞衣不出), and also aborts the fetus (墮胎). It is gathered in the fifth month, steamed and dried; do not let it be worm-eaten.
