## 龍齒

### Source Text

（龍骨，得人參、牛黃良，畏石膏;龍角，畏乾漆、蜀椒、理石。）

《本經》原文：龍骨，味甘，平。主心腹鬼注，精物老魅，咳逆，泄利膿血，女子漏下，症殺精物。

### Literal Translation

(Longgu (dragon bone), with Renshen (ginseng) and Niuhuang (bezoar) is good; it fears Shigao (gypsum). The dragon horn fears Qiqi (lacquer), Shujiao (Sichuan pepper), and Lishi.)

Original text of the Benjing: Longgu (dragon bone), sweet in flavor, neutral. It chiefly treats ghost injection (guizhu) of the heart and abdomen, essence things and old demons, cough with counterflow, diarrhea with pus and blood, women's leaking below, and zheng ... it kills essence things.
