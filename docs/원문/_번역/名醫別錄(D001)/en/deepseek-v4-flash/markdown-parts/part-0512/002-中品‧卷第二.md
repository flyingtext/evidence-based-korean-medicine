## 雌黃

### Source Text

生武都，與雄黃同山生。其陰山有金，金精熏則生雌黃，采無時

《本經》原文：雌黃，味辛，平。主惡瘡頭禿痂疥，殺毒蟲虱身癢邪氣諸毒。

### Literal Translation

It grows at Wudu, growing on the same mountain as realgar. On the shaded (yin) side of that mountain there is gold; the warm essence of the gold fumigates (the stone) and then realgar-orpiment (雌黃) is born; gather without a fixed time. Original text of the Benjing: Orpiment, flavor pungent, level. It chiefly treats foul ulcers, head baldness, scabs and itches, and kills poisonous worms, lice, body itch, and pathogenic [qi].

### Translator's Notes

- 雌黃 = orpiment. The last clause is cut off in the source.
