## 雄黃

### Source Text

得銅可作金。生武都、敦煌山之陽，采無時。《本經》原文：雄黃，味苦，平、寒。

### Literal Translation

When combined with copper it can be made into gold. It grows on the southern slopes of Mount Wu and Mount Dunhuang; gather without a fixed time. Original text of the Benjing (本經): Realgar, flavor bitter, level and cold.
