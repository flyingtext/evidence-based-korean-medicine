## 薏苡仁

### Source Text

無毒。主除筋骨邪氣不仁，利腸胃，消水腫，令人能食。一名屋　，一名起實，生真定。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It mainly removes evil qi and numbness of the sinews and bones, frees the intestines and stomach, disperses edema, and makes one able to eat. One name is Wujie [屋], one name is Qishi [起實]. It grows in Zhending.
