## 酸漿

### Source Text

《本經》原文：酸漿，味酸，平。主熱煩滿，定志，益氣，利水道，產難，吞其實立產，一名醋漿。生川澤

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Ground-cherry (酸漿), flavor sour, level. It chiefly treats heat with vexation and fullness, settles the intention, boosts qi, benefits the waterway, [and treats] difficult labor; swallowing its fruit brings about delivery immediately. One name is 醋漿. It grows in river marshes ...
