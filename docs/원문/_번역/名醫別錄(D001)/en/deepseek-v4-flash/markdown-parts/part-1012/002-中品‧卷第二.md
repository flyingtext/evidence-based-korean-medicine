## 蠡魚

### Source Text

又，蠡魚腸及肝，主久敗瘡中蟲。《本經》原文：蠡魚，味甘，寒。主濕痹，面目浮腫，下大水。

### Literal Translation

Also, the intestines and liver of the li fish (蠡魚) treat worms in long-persisting spoiled sores (久敗瘡中蟲). The original text of the Benjing: Li yu (蠡魚), sweet in flavor, cold in nature. It treats damp bi (濕痹), swelling of the face and eyes (面目浮腫), and sends down great water (下大水).
