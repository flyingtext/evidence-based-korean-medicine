## 葶藶

### Source Text

大寒，無毒。下膀胱水，腹留熱氣，皮間邪水上出，面目腫，身暴中風熱痱癢，利久服令人虛。一名丁歷，一名　蒿。

### Literal Translation

Greatly cold, non-toxic. It descends the water of the bladder, [removes] retained heat-qi in the abdomen, evil water of between the skin rising out, swelling of the face and eyes, sudden wind-heat of the body with prickly-heat itching (風熱痱癢), and benefits [the bowels]; taken for a long time it makes one deficient. One name is 丁歷 (dīnglì), one name is 蒿 (tíng) artemisia.
