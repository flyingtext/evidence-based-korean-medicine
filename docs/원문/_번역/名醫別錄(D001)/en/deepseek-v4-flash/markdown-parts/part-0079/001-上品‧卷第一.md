## 六芝

### Source Text

青芝生太山；赤芝　生霍山；黃芝　生嵩山；白芝　生華山黑芝　生恆山；紫芝高夏。六芝皆無毒。六月、八月采。

### Literal Translation

The blue lingzhi [青芝] grows on Mount Tai; the red lingzhi [赤芝] grows on Mount Huo; the yellow lingzhi [黃芝] grows on Mount Song; the white lingzhi [白芝] grows on Mount Hua; the black lingzhi [黑芝] grows on Mount Heng; the purple lingzhi [紫芝] grows in Gaoxia. All six lingzhi are non-toxic. Gather them in the sixth and eighth months.
