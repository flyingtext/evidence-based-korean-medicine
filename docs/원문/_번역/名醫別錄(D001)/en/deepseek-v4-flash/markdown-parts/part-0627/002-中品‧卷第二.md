## 貝母

### Source Text

味苦，微寒，無毒。主治腹中結實，心下滿，洗洗惡風寒，目眩、項直，咳嗽上氣，煩熱渴，出汗，安五臟，利骨髓。一名藥實，一名苦華，一名苦菜，一名商草，一名勒母一名𤙛。

### Literal Translation

Flavor bitter, slightly cold, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat firm accumulations within the abdomen, fullness below the heart, the dripping shivering of aversion to wind-cold, dizziness with rigid neck, cough with ascent of qi, vexation and heat with thirst, sweating, calming the five viscera, and benefiting the bone marrow. One name is 藥實, one name is 苦 [菜].
