## 粉錫

### Source Text

無毒。去鱉瘕，治惡瘡，墮胎，止小便利。《本經》原文：粉錫，味辛，寒。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It removes turtle masses (鱉瘕), treats malignant sores, causes abortion (墮胎), and stops excessive urination (小便利). The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 粉錫 (fěnxī), flavor acrid, cold.
