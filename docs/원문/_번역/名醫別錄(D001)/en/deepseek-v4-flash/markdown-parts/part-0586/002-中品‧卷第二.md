## 黃芩

### Source Text

大寒，無毒。主治痰熱，胃中熱，小腹絞痛，消穀，利小腸，女子血閉、淋露、下血小兒腹痛。一名空腸，一名內虛，一名黃文，一名經芩，一名妒婦。

### Literal Translation

Greatly cold, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat phlegm-heat, heat within the stomach, acute twisting pain of the lower abdomen, digesting grain, and benefiting the small intestine; women's blood stoppage (血閉), dripping discharge (淋露), down-flowing blood, and children's abdominal pain. One name is 空腸, one name is 內虛, one name is 黃文, one name is 經 [芩].
