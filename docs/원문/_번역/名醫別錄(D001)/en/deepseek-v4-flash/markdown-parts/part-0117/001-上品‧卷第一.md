## 葳蕤

### Source Text

無毒。主治心腹結氣，虛熱，濕毒，腰痛，莖中寒，及目痛　爛淚出。一名熒，一名節，一名玉竹，一名馬薰。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It mainly treats bound qi of the heart and abdomen, deficiency heat, damp poison, lower back pain, cold in the penis, and eye pain with erosion and tearing. One name is Ying [熒], one name is Jie [節], one name is Yuzhu [玉竹], one name is Ma Xun [馬薰].
