## 鐵精

### Source Text

微溫，主治驚悸，定心氣，小兒風癇，陰　，脫肛。《本經》原文：鐵精，平。主明目，化銅。

### Literal Translation

Slightly warm; chiefly treats fright-palpitations, settles heart qi, children's wind-epilepsy (風癇), the [swollen] genitals, and prolapse of the anus. Original text of the Benjing: Iron essence (鐵精), level. It chiefly brightens the eyes and transforms copper.
