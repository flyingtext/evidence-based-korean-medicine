## 茯神

### Source Text

二月、八月采，陰乾。（馬間為之使。得甘草、防風、芍藥、紫石英、麥門冬共治五臟。

Gather it in the second and eighth months and dry in the shade. (Ma Jian [馬間] is its envoy. With licorice [甘草], Saposhnikovia [防風], peony [芍藥], purple quartz [紫石英] and Ophiopogon [麥門冬] it jointly treats the five viscera.
