## 蜂子

### Source Text

）

《本經》原文：蜂子，味甘，平。主風頭，除蠱毒，補虛羸傷中。久服，令人光澤，好顏色

### Literal Translation

)

Original text of the Benjing: Fengzi (bee larvae), sweet in flavor, neutral. It chiefly treats wind of the head, removes gu (parasitic) poison, and supplements deficiency, emaciation, and injury of the center. Taken over a long time, it makes one lustrous and gives a good complexion.
