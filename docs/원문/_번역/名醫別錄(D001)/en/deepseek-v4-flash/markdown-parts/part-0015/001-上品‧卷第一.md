## 空青

### Source Text

主青盲耳聾，明目，利久竅，通血脈，養精神。久服輕身延年不老。能化銅鐵鉛錫作金。

### Literal Translation

It mainly treats green blindness and deafness, brightens the eyes, frees the nine orifices, penetrates the blood vessels, and nourishes essence and spirit. Taken over a long time, it lightens the body, prolongs life and does not age. It can transform copper, iron, lead and tin to make gold.
