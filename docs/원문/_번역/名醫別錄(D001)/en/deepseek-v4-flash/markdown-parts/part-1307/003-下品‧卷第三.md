## 雷丸

### Source Text

主殺三蟲，逐毒瓦斯胃中熱。利丈夫，不利女子。作摩膏，除小兒百病。

### Literal Translation

It governs killing the three worms (殺三蟲) and expelling toxic qi and heat in the stomach (毒瓦斯胃中熱). It benefits men and does not benefit women (利丈夫，不利女子). Made into a rubbing ointment (摩膏), it removes the hundred diseases of children (小兒百病).
