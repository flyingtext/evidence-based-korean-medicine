## 茜根

### Source Text

無毒。主止血內崩，下血，膀胱不足，　跌，盅毒。久服益精氣，輕身。

Non-toxic. It chiefly stops bleeding, internal collapse (neibeng), downward bleeding, insufficiency of the bladder, ... falls, and gu (parasitic) poison. Taken over a long time it boosts essence qi and lightens the body.
