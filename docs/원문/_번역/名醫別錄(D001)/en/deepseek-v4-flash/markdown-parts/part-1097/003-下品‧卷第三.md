## 方解石

### Source Text

味苦、辛，大寒，無毒。主治胸中留熱、結氣，黃膽，通血脈，去蠱毒。一名黃石方山，采無時。

### Literal Translation

Flavor bitter and acrid, greatly cold, non-toxic. It governs retained heat in the chest, bound qi (結氣), jaundice (黃疸), frees the blood vessels, and removes gu-poison (蠱毒). One name is 黃石方山 (huángshífāngshān); gathered at no fixed time.
