## 人參

### Source Text

如人形者有神。生上黨遼東。二月、四月、八月上旬采根，竹刀刮，曝乾，無令見風。

### Literal Translation

That which resembles the human form has spirit. It grows in Shangdang and Liaodong. Gather the root in the first ten days of the second, fourth and eighth months, scrape it with a bamboo knife, dry it in the sun, and do not let it see wind.
