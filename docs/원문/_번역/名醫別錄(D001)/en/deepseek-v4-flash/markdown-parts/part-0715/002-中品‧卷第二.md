## 杜若

### Source Text

一名杜蓮，一名白連，柴胡

《本經》原文：杜若味辛，微溫。主胸脅下逆氣，溫中風入腦戶，頭腫痛，多涕淚出。久服，益精明目輕身

### Literal Translation

One name is 杜蓮, one name is 白連, [and?] bupleurum 柴胡... Original text of the Benjing: Pollia (杜若), flavor pungent, slightly warm. It chiefly treats counter-flow qi below the chest and hypochondrium, warms the wind that enters the brain's door (腦戶), swelling pain of the head, and copious tears and nasal discharge. Taken over a long time it boosts essence, brightens the eyes, lightens the body
