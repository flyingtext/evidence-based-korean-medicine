## 澤瀉

### Source Text

味鹹，無毒。主補虛損、五勞，除五臟痞滿，起陰氣，止泄精、消渴、淋瀝，逐膀焦停水。扁鵲云：「多服病患眼。

### Literal Translation

Flavor salty, non-toxic. It mainly supplements deficiency and damage and the five kinds of exhaustion [五勞], removes fullness of the five viscera, raises yin qi, stops seminal emission, wasting-thirst [消渴] and dribbling strangury [淋瀝], and expels retained water of the bladder and triple burner. Bian Que said: "Taking too much harms the patient's eyes."
