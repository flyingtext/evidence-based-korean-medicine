## 代赭

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主帶下百病，產難，胞衣不出，墮胎，養血氣，除五臟血脈中熱，血痹瘀，大人小兒驚氣入腹，及陰痿不起。一名血師。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, non-toxic. It governs the hundred diseases of vaginal discharge (帶下), difficult childbirth (產難), failure of the afterbirth to come out (胞衣不出), abortion (墮胎), nourishing blood and qi, removing heat within the blood vessels of the five viscera, blood-bi (血痹) and stasis, fright-qi entering the abdomen in adults and children, and failure of erection in impotence (陰痿). One name is 血師 (xuèshī).
