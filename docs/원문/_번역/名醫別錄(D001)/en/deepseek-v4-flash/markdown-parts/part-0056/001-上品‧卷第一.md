## 赤石脂

### Source Text

味甘、酸、辛，大溫，無毒。主養心氣，明目，益精，治腹痛，泄　，下痢赤白，利，及癰疽瘡痔，女子崩中漏下，產難，胞衣不出。久服補髓，好顏色，益智，不飢，延年。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, sour and pungent, greatly warm, non-toxic. It mainly nourishes heart qi, brightens the eyes, boosts essence, treats abdominal pain, diarrhea, dysentery with red and white discharge, and [frees] the bowels, and treats abscesses, deep-rooted sores, sores and hemorrhoids, uterine bleeding [崩中] with leaking discharge in women, difficult labor, and failure of the placenta to come out. Taken over a long time, it supplements the marrow, improves the complexion, boosts wisdom, does not hunger, and prolongs life.
