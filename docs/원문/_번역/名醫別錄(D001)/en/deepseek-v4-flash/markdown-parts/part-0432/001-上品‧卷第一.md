## 卵中白皮

### Source Text

主治久咳結氣，得麻黃、紫菀和服之立已。生朝鮮。《本經》原文：丹雄雞，味甘，微溫。

### Literal Translation

It chiefly treats chronic cough with binding qi; taken together with Mahuang (ephedra) and Ziwan (Aster) it stops immediately. It grows in Chaoxian. Original text of the Benjing: Danxiongji (red male chicken), sweet in flavor, slightly warm.
