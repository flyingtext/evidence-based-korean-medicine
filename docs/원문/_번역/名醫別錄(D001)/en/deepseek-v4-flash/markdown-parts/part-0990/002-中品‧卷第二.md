## 桑螵蛸

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主治男子虛損，五臟氣微，夢寐失精，遺溺。久服益氣，養神。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, non-toxic. It treats men's vacuity-injury (虛損), the qi of the five viscera being faint (五臟氣微), loss of essence in dreams and sleep (夢寐失精), and enuresis (遺溺). Taken for a long time it boosts qi (益氣) and nourishes the spirit (養神).
