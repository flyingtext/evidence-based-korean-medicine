## 茅根

### Source Text

《本經》原文：茅根，味甘，寒。主勞傷虛羸，補中益氣，除瘀血血閉寒熱，利小便。其苗，主下水。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Cogongrass root (茅根), flavor sweet, cold. It chiefly treats taxation-damage with vacuity emaciation, supplements the center and boosts qi, dispels static blood and blood stoppage with cold-heat, and benefits urination. Its sprout chiefly down-drives water.
