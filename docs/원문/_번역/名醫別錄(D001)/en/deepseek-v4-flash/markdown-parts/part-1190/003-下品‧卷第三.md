## 牽牛子

### Source Text

味苦，寒，有毒。主下氣，治腳滿水腫，除風毒，利小便。

### Literal Translation

Flavor bitter, cold, toxic. It governs descending qi (下氣), treats swelling of the feet and edema (腳滿水腫), removes wind-toxin (風毒), and benefits urination (利小便).
