## 松蘿

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主治淡熱，溫瘧，可為吐湯，利水道。生熊耳山松樹上。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat heat-phlegm (淡熱) and warm-malaria; it may be made into an emetic decoction and benefits the waterway. It grows on pine trees at Xiong'er Mountain.
