## 花葉

### Source Text

五月采實，陰乾。《本經》原文：蠡實，味甘，平。主皮膚寒熱，胃中熱氣，風寒濕痹，堅筋骨，令人嗜食。

### Literal Translation

Gather the fruit in the fifth month, dry in the shade. Original text of the Benjing: Iris seed (蠡實), flavor sweet, level. It chiefly treats cold-heat of the skin, heat qi within the stomach, wind-cold-damp impediment, strengthens sinew and bone, and makes one fond of eating.
