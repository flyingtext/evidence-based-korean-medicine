## 石葦

### Source Text

生華陰，不聞水及人聲者，良。二月采葉，（杏仁為之使，得菖蒲良。）

《本經》原文：石葦，味苦，平。

### Literal Translation

It grows at Huayin; that which does not hear water or human voices is good. Gather the leaf in the second month. (Apricot kernel 杏仁 is its envoy; obtaining sweet flag 菖蒲 is good.) Original text of the Benjing: Pyrrosia (石葦), flavor bitter, level.
