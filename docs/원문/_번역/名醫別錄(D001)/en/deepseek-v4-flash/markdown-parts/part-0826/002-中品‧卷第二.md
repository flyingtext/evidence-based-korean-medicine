## 乾薑

### Source Text

去淡，下氣，止嘔吐，除風邪寒熱。久服小志少智，傷心

《本經》原文：乾薑，味辛，溫。主胸滿咳逆上氣，溫中，止血，出汗，逐風濕痹，腸下利。

### Literal Translation

It removes phlegm (淡), sends qi downward, stops vomiting, and dispels wind-evil chills and fever. Taken for a long time it diminishes will and intellect and is injurious to the heart.

The original text of the Benjing: Ganjing (乾薑), acrid in flavor, warm. It treats chest fullness, cough with rebellious qi (咳逆上氣), warms the center, stops bleeding, promotes sweating, expels wind-damp bi (風濕痹), and stops intestinal dysentery with discharge.
