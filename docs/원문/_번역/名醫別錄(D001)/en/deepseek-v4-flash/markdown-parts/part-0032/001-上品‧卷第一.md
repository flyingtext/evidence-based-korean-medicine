## 朴硝

### Source Text

味辛，大寒，無毒。主治胃中食飲熱結，破留血、閉絕，停痰痞滿，推陳致新。煉之如銀，能寒、能熱、能滑、能澀，能辛、能苦、能咸、能酸。

### Literal Translation

Flavor pungent, greatly cold, non-toxic. It mainly treats heat binding of food and drink in the stomach, breaks retained blood and closure and severance, and stops phlegm stagnation and fullness; it expels the old and brings forth the new. Refined, it is like silver; it can be cold, can be hot, can be slippery, can be astringent, can be pungent, can be bitter, can be salty, can be sour.
