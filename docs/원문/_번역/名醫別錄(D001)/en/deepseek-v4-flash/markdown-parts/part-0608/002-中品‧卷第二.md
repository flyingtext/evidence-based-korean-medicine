## 芎

### Source Text

（白芷為之使，惡黃連。）

《本經》原文：芎　，味辛，溫。主中風入腦頭痛，寒痹筋攣緩急，金創，婦人血閉無子。

### Literal Translation

(Angelica root 白芷 is its envoy; it is averse to coptis 黃連.) Original text of the Benjing: Chuanxiong (川芎), flavor pungent, warm. It chiefly treats wind-stroke entering the brain with headache, cold-impediment with taut-sinew spasm and relaxation, metal wounds, and women's blood stoppage with childlessness.
