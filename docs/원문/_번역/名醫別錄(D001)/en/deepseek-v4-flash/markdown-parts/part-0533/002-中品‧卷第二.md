## 石膏

### Source Text

生齊山及齊盧山、魯蒙山，采無時。（雞子為之使，惡莽草、毒公。）

《本經》原文：石膏，味辛，微寒。

### Literal Translation

It grows at Mount Qi and Mount Qilu and Lu Meng Mountain; gather without a fixed time. (Chicken egg 雞子 is its envoy; it is averse to 莽草 and 毒公.) Original text of the Benjing: Gypsum (石膏), flavor pungent, slightly cold.
