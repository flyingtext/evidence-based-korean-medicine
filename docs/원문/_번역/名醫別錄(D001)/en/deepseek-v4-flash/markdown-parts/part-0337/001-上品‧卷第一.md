## 辛夷

### Source Text

無毒。溫中，解肌，利九竅，通鼻塞，涕出，治面腫引齒痛，眩冒，身洋洋如在車船上者。生須發，去白蟲。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It warms the center, releases the flesh, frees the nine orifices, opens nasal congestion, stops nasal discharge, treats facial swelling that draws tooth pain, dizziness and vertigo, and a body that feels as if floating on a cart or boat. It grows the beard and hair and removes white worms.
