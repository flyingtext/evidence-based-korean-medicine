## 白兔藿

### Source Text

無毒。主治風疰，諸大毒不可入口者，皆消除之。又去血，可末著痛上，立消。

### Literal Translation

Not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat wind-infestation (風疰) and the various great poisons that cannot enter the mouth, all of which it eliminates. It also removes blood; it may be powdered and applied on the pain, which resolves immediately.
