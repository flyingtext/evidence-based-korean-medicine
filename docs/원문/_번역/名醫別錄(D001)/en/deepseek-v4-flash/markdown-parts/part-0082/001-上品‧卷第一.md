## 赤箭

### Source Text

主消癰腫，下肢滿疝，下血。生陳倉、雍州、及太山、少室。三月、四月、八月采根曝乾。

### Literal Translation

It mainly treats and disperses abscesses and swellings, sends down lower abdominal fullness and hernia [疝], and downflow of blood. It grows in Chencang, Yongzhou, and on Mount Tai and Shaoshi. Gather the root in the third, fourth and eighth months and dry it in the sun.
