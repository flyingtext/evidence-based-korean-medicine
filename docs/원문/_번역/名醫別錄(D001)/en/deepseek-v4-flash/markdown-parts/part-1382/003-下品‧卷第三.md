## 陸英

### Source Text

《本經》原文：陸英，味苦，寒。主骨間諸痹，四肢拘攣疼酸，膝寒痛，陰痿，短氣不足，腳腫。生川穀。

### Literal Translation

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 陸英 (lùyīng), flavor bitter, cold. It governs the various bi between the bones (骨間諸痹), contracture and soreness of the four limbs (四肢拘攣疼酸), cold pain of the knees (膝寒痛), impotence (陰痿), shortness of breath with insufficiency (短氣不足), and swelling of the feet (腳腫). It grows in river valleys.
