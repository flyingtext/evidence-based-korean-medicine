## 曾青

### Source Text

（畏菟絲子。）

《本經》原文：曾青，味酸，小寒。主目痛，止淚出，風痹，利關節，通九竅，破症堅積聚。

### Literal Translation

(It fears dodder seed [菟絲子].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Layered azurite [曾青], flavor sour, slightly cold. It mainly treats eye pain, stops tearing, wind bi [風痹], frees the joints, opens the nine orifices, and breaks zheng [症] hard accumulations and gatherings.
