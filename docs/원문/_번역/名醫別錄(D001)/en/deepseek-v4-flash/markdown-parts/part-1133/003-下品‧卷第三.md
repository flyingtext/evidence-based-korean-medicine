## 黑石華

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主陰痿，消渴，去熱，治月水不利。生弗其勞山陰石間，采無時。

Flavor sweet, non-toxic. It governs impotence (陰痿), wasting-thirst (消渴), removes heat, and treats irregular menstruation (月水不利). It grows among the stones on the shaded side of Fuqilao Mountain; gathered at no fixed time.
