## 荊葉

### Source Text

味苦，平，無毒。主久痢，霍亂、轉筋，血淋，下部瘡，濕　薄腳，主香港腳腫滿。其根，味甘、苦，平，無毒。

### Literal Translation

Bitter in flavor, neutral, non-toxic. It chiefly treats chronic dysentery, cholera with cramping of the sinews, blood strangury, sores of the lower part, damp ... thin feet, and chiefly treats beriberi (jiaoqi) with swelling and fullness. Its root is sweet and bitter in flavor, neutral, non-toxic.
