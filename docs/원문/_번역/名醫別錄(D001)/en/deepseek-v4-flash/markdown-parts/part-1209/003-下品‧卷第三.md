## 天雄

### Source Text

味甘，大溫，有大毒。主治頭面風去來疼痛，心腹結積關節重不能行步，除骨間痛，長陰氣，強志，令人武勇，力作不倦。又墮胎。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, greatly warm, greatly toxic. It governs wind of the head and face coming and going with pain (頭面風去來疼痛), bound accumulations of the heart and abdomen (心腹結積), heaviness of the joints such that one cannot walk (關節重不能行步), removes pain between the bones (骨間痛), lengthens the yin-qi (長陰氣), strengthens the will (強志), makes one brave and martial (令人武勇), and strong and untiring in labor (力作不倦). Also it causes abortion (墮胎).
