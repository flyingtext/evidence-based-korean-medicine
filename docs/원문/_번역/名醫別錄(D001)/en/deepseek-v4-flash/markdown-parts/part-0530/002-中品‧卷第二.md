## 凝水石

### Source Text

味甘，大寒，無毒。主除時氣熱盛，五臟伏熱，胃中熱，煩滿，止渴，水腫，少腹一名寒水石，一名凌水石，色如雲母，可折者良，鹽之精也。生常山山谷，又中水縣及（解巴豆毒，畏地榆。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, greatly cold, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to dispel the exuberance of seasonal (epidemic) heat, the latent heat (伏熱) of the five viscera, heat within the stomach, vexation and fullness, stop thirst, (treat) edema, and [pain in] the lower abdomen. One name is 寒水石 (cold-water stone), one name is 凌水石; colored like mica, that which can be split well is fine; salt [of it] ...
