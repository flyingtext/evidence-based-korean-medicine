## 黃芩

### Source Text

《本經》原文：黃芩，味苦，平。主諸熱黃膽，腸　泄利，逐水，下血閉，惡瘡疽蝕火瘍。一名腐腸。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Scutellaria (黃芩), flavor bitter, level. It chiefly treats various heat and jaundice, intestinal [qi] diarrhea, down-drives water, frees blood stoppage, and treats foul ulcers, ulcerations and fire-sores. One name is 腐腸.
