## 蜀羊泉

### Source Text

無毒。主治齲齒，女子陰中內傷，皮間實積。一名羊泉，一名羊飴。

### Literal Translation

Not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat dental caries (齲齒), women's internal injury of the genitals, and firm accumulation within the skin. One name is 羊泉, one name is 羊飴.

- 蜀羊泉 = Solanum septemlobum.
