## 石斛

### Source Text

一名禁生，一名杜蘭，一名石　。生六安水傍石上。七月、八月采莖，陰乾。

### Literal Translation

One name is Jinsheng [禁生], one name is Dulan [杜蘭], one name is Shi [石]. It grows on rocks beside the water of Liu'an. Gather the stem in the seventh and eighth months and dry in the shade.
