## 姑活

### Source Text

《本經》原文：姑活，味甘，溫。主大風邪氣，濕痹寒痛。久服輕身，益壽耐老。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Guhuo, sweet in flavor, warm. It chiefly treats great wind evil qi and damp bi (impediment) with cold pain. Taken over a long time it lightens the body, augments longevity, and endures aging.
