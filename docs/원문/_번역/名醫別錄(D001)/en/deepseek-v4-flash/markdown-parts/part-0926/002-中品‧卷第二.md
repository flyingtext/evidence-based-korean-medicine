## 牛角鰓

### Source Text

髓，補中，填骨髓。久服增年。膽，可丸藥。

### Literal Translation

The marrow (髓) supplements the center (補中) and fills the bone marrow (填骨髓). Taken for a long time it increases the years. The gall can be used to make pills (丸藥).
