## 路石

### Source Text

生石上，天雨獨干，日出獨濡，花黃，莖赤黑。三歲一實，實赤如麻子。五月、十月采莖葉陰乾。

### Literal Translation

It grows on stones. When the sky rains it alone is dry, when the sun comes out it alone is moist. The flower is yellow and the stem is red-black. Once every three years it bears fruit; the fruit is red and resembles hemp-seed (麻子). The stems and leaves are gathered in the fifth and tenth months and dried in the shade.
