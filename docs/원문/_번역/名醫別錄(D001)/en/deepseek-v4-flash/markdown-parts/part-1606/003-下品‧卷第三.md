## 天鼠屎

### Source Text

（惡白蘞，白薇。）

《本經》原文：天鼠屎，味辛，寒。主面癰腫，皮膚洗洗時痛，腹中血氣，破寒熱積聚，除驚悸。

### Literal Translation

(It is averse to Bailian and Baiwei.)

Original text of the Benjing: Tianshushi, flavor acrid, cold. It governs abscesses and swellings of the face, skin washing and washing with occasional pain, blood and qi in the abdomen, breaks accumulations of chills and fever, and removes fright and palpitations.
