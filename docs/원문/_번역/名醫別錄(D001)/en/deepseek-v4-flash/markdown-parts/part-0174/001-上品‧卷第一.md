## 牛膝

### Source Text

二月、八月、十月采根，陰乾。（惡螢火、龜甲、陸英，畏白前。）

《本經》原文：牛膝，味苦。

### Literal Translation

Gather the root in the second, eighth and tenth months and dry in the shade. (It is averse to firefly [螢火], turtle shell [龜甲] and Sambucus [陸英], and fears Cynanchum [白前].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Achyranthes [牛膝], flavor bitter.
