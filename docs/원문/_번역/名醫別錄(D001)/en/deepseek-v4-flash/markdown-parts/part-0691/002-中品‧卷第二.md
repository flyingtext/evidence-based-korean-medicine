## 石葦

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主止煩，下氣，通膀胱滿，補五勞，安五臟，去惡風，益精氣。一名石用之去黃毛，毛射人肺，令人咳，不可治。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to stop vexation, down-drive qi, free the fullness of the bladder, supplement the five taxations, calm the five viscera, dispel foul wind, and boost essence qi. One name is 石用之去黃毛... the yellow hairs, [if left], shoot into the lungs and cause coughing that cannot be treated.

### Translator's Notes

- 石用之去黃毛: Pyrrosia leaves are used after removing the yellow hairs.
