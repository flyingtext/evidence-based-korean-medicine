## 生大豆

### Source Text

主治胃中熱，去腫，除痹，消穀，止腹脹。生太山，九（惡五參、龍膽，得前胡、烏喙、杏仁、牡蠣良。）

《本經》原文：大豆黃卷，味甘，平。

### Literal Translation

It treats heat in the stomach (胃中熱), removing swelling (去腫), removing bi (除痹), digesting grain (消穀), and stopping abdominal distention (止腹脹). It grows at Mount Tai in the ninth [month] (它 is averse to wushen (五參) and longdan (龍膽); it is good with qianhu (前胡), wuhui (烏喙), xingren (杏仁), and muli (牡蠣).)

The original text of the Benjing: Dadou huangjuan (大豆黃卷), sweet in flavor, neutral.
