## 白頸蚯蚓

### Source Text

三月取，陰乾。又，蚯蚓，鹽沾為汁，治耳聾。《本經》原文：蚯蚓，味鹹，寒。

### Literal Translation

Gather in the third month and dry in the shade. Also, the earthworm, dipped in salt to make juice, treats deafness. Original text of the Benjing: Qiuyin, flavor salty, cold.
