## 羊肉

### Source Text

味甘，大熱，無毒。主緩中，字乳余疾，及頭腦大風汗出，虛勞寒冷，補中益氣，安止驚。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, greatly hot, non-toxic. It treats relaxing the center (緩中), residual disease from childbirth and nursing (字乳余疾), and great wind with sweating of the brain (頭腦大風汗出), vacuity-labor cold (虛勞寒冷); it supplements the center and boosts qi (補中益氣), and settles and stops fright (安止驚).
