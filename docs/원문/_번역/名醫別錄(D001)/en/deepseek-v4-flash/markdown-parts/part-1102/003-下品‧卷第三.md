## 代赭

### Source Text

生齊國，赤紅青色，如雞冠有澤，染爪不渝者良，采無時。（畏天雄。）

《本經》原文：代赭，味苦，寒。

### Literal Translation

It grows in the state of Qi, reddish-red and blue-green in color, like a cockscomb with luster; those that dye the nails without fading are good; gathered at no fixed time. (It fears 天雄 (tiānxióng).)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 代赭 (dàizhě), flavor bitter, cold.
