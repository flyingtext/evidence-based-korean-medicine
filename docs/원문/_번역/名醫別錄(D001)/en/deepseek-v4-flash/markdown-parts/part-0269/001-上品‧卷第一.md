## 茵陳蒿

### Source Text

微寒，無毒。主治通身發黃，小便不利，除頭熱，去伏瘕。久服面白悅，長年。

### Literal Translation

Slightly cold, non-toxic. It chiefly treats yellowing of the whole body, difficult urination, removes heat of the head, and dispels latent abdominal masses (fujia). Taken over a long time the face becomes white and pleasing, and years are prolonged.
