## 葵根

### Source Text

味甘，寒，無毒。主惡瘡，治淋，利小便，解蜀椒毒。葉，為百菜主，其心傷人。

### Literal Translation

Sweet in flavor, cold, non-toxic. It chiefly treats malignant sores, treats strangury, promotes urination, and resolves the poison of Shujiao (Sichuan pepper). The leaves are the chief of the hundred vegetables; their heart injures people.
