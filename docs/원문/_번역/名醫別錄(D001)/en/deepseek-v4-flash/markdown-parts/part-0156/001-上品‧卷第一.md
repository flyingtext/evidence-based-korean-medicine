## 石龍芮

### Source Text

一名石熊名彭根，一名天豆。生太山石邊。五月五日采子，二月、八月采皮，陰乾。

### Literal Translation

One name is Shixiong [石熊], one name is Penggen [彭根], one name is Tiandou [天豆]. It grows beside rocks on Mount Tai. Gather the seeds on the fifth day of the fifth month, and the bark in the second and eighth months, and dry in the shade.
