## 甘草

### Source Text

無毒。主溫中，下氣，煩滿，短氣，傷臟，咳嗽，止渴，通經脈，利血氣，解百藥毒為九土之精，安和七十二種石，一千二百種草。一名蜜甘，一名美草，一名蜜草，一名生河西積沙山及上郡。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. It mainly warms the center, sends qi downward, [treats] vexation and fullness, short breath, damage to the viscera, cough, stops thirst, frees the channels and vessels, frees blood qi, and resolves the poison of the hundred drugs; it is the essence of the nine soils, and harmonizes and settles the seventy-two kinds of stone and the one thousand two hundred kinds of herbs. One name is Migan [蜜甘], one name is Meicao [美草], one name is Micao [蜜草], one name is [it grows in] Hexi Jisha Mountain and Shangjun.
