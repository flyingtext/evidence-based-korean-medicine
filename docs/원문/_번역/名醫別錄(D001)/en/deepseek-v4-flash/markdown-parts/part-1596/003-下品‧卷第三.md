## 豚卵

### Source Text

心，主驚邪憂腎，冷利，理腎氣，通膀胱。膽，治傷寒熱渴。肚，補中益氣，止渴利。

### Literal Translation

The heart governs fright and evil, and worry of the kidney; cold and promoting, it regulates kidney qi and unblocks the bladder. The gallbladder treats cold damage with heat and thirst. The stomach supplements the center and boosts qi, and stops thirst and diarrhea.
