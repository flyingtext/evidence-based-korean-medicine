## 石

### Source Text

味甘，生溫、熟熱，有毒。主明目，下氣，除膈中熱，止消渴，益肝氣，破積聚、痼腹痛，去鼻中息肉。久服令人筋攣。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, raw it is warm, cooked it is hot, toxic. It governs brightening the eyes, descending qi (下氣), removing heat within the diaphragm, stopping wasting-thirst (消渴), benefiting the liver qi (肝氣), breaking accumulations and fixed abdominal pain, and removing nasal polyps. Taken for a long time it makes the sinews contract.
