## 雲母

### Source Text

（澤瀉為之使，畏鱔甲，反流水，惡徐長卿。）

《本經》原文：雲母，味甘，平。主身皮死肌，中風寒熱，如在車船上，除邪氣，安五臟，益子精，明目。

### Literal Translation

(Alisma [澤瀉] is its envoy; it fears soft-shelled turtle [鱔甲], is incompatible with flowing water, and is averse to Cynanchum paniculatum [徐長卿].)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Mica [雲母], flavor sweet, neutral. It mainly treats dead flesh of the body's skin, wind-stroke with cold and heat, as if riding in a cart or boat, removes evil qi, settles the five viscera, boosts the essence of the offspring, and brightens the eyes.
