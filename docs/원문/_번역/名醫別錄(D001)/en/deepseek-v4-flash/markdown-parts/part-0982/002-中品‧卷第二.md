## 樗雞

### Source Text

七月采，曝乾。《本經》原文：樗雞，味苦，平。主心腹邪氣，陰痿，益精強志，生子，好色，補中輕身。

### Literal Translation

It is gathered in the seventh month and sun-dried. The original text of the Benjing: Chuji (樗雞), bitter in flavor, neutral. It treats evil qi in the heart and abdomen (心腹邪氣), yin-wei (陰痿), benefiting essence and strengthening the will (益精強志), giving birth to children, liking beauty (好色), supplementing the center and lightening the body (補中輕身).
