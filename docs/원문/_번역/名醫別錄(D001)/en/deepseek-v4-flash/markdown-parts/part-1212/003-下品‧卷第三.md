## 烏頭

### Source Text

味甘，大熱，有毒。消胸上淡冷，食不下，心腹冷疾，臍間痛，肩胛痛不可俯仰，目痛不可力視。又墮胎。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, greatly hot, toxic. It disperses cold phlegm above the chest (胸上淡冷), inability to eat (食不下), cold disease of the heart and abdomen (心腹冷疾), pain between the navel (臍間痛), pain of the shoulder blades such that one cannot bend or look up (肩胛痛不可俯仰), and eye pain such that one cannot look hard (目痛不可力視). Also it causes abortion (墮胎).
