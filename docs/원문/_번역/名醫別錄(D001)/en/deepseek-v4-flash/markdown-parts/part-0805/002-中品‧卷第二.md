## 垣衣

### Source Text

一名昔邪，一名烏韭，一名垣嬴，一名天韭，一名鼠韭。生古垣牆陰或三月三日采，陰乾。又，垣衣，主暴風口噤，金瘡、酒漬服之效。

### Literal Translation

One name is Xixie (昔邪), one is Wujiu (烏韭), one is Yuanying (垣嬴), one is Tianjiu (天韭), one is Shujiu (鼠韭). It grows in the shade of old walls, or it is gathered on the third day of the third month and dried in the shade. Also, Yuanyi (垣衣) treats sudden wind stroke with lockjaw (口噤), and metal wounds; it is effective when soaked in wine and taken.
