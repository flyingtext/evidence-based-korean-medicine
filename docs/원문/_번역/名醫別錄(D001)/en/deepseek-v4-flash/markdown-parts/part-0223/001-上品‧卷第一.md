## 枸杞

### Source Text

根大寒，子微寒，無毒。主治風濕，下胸脅氣，客熱頭痛，補內傷，大勞、噓吸，堅骨，強陰，利大小腸。久服耐寒暑。

### Literal Translation

The root is greatly cold, the seeds slightly cold, non-toxic. It mainly treats wind-damp, sends down qi of the chest and flanks, guest heat [客熱] and headache, supplements internal damage, great exhaustion and sighing [噓吸], firms the bones, strengthens yin, and frees the large and small intestines. Taken over a long time, it endures cold and heat.
