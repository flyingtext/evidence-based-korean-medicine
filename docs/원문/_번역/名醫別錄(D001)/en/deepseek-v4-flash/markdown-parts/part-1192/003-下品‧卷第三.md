## 野狼毒

### Source Text

二月、八月采根，陰乾，陳而沉水者良。（豆為之使，惡麥句薑。）

《本經》原文：野狼毒，味辛，平。

### Literal Translation

Gathered as root in the second and eighth months, dried in the shade; those that are old and sink in water are good. (豆 (dòu) is its envoy; it fears 麥句薑 (màijùjiāng).)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 野狼毒 (lángdú), flavor acrid, neutral.
