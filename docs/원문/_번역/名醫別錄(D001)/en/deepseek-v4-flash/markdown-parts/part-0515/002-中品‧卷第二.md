## 石鐘乳

### Source Text

不練服之，令人淋。一名公乳，一名蘆石，一名夏石。生少室及太山，采（蛇床為之使，惡牡丹、玄石、牡蒙，畏紫石英、　草。

### Literal Translation

If taken without being refined, it causes strangury (淋). One name is 公乳, one name is 蘆石, one name is 夏石. It grows at Shaoshi and Mount Tai; gather (at the proper season). (Snake-bed [蛇床] is its envoy; it is averse to peony [牡丹], stone-actite [玄石], and 牡蒙, and is in dread of purple quartz [紫石英] and 草.)
