## 鯉魚膽

### Source Text

無毒。肉，味甘，治咳逆上氣，黃膽，止渴。生者，治水腫腳滿，下氣。

### Literal Translation

Non-toxic. The flesh (肉), sweet in flavor, treats cough with rebellious qi (咳逆上氣), jaundice (黃膽), and stops thirst. Used fresh, it treats edema with foot fullness (水腫腳滿) and sends qi downward (下氣).
