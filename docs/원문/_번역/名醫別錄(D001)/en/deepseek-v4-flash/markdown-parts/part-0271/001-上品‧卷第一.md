## 茵陳蒿

### Source Text

《本經》原文：茵陳，味苦，平。主風濕寒熱邪氣，熱結黃膽。久服，輕身益氣耐老。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Yinchen (Artemisia capillaris), bitter in flavor, neutral. It chiefly treats wind-damp-cold-heat evil qi and heat binding with jaundice. Taken over a long time, it lightens the body, boosts qi, and endures aging.
