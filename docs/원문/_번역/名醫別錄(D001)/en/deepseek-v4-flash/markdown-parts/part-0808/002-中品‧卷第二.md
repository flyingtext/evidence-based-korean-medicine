## 艾葉

### Source Text

又，艾，生寒熟熱。主下血，衄血、膿血痢，水煮及丸散任用。

### Literal Translation

Also, mugwort (艾) is cold when raw and hot when cooked. It treats bleeding from the lower body (下血), nose-bleeding (衄血), dysentery with pus and blood, and it may be used either boiled in water or made into pills and powders as convenient.
