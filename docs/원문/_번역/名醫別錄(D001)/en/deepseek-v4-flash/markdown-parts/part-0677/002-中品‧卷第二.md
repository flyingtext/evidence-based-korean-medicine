## 白棘

### Source Text

生雍州

《本經》原文：白棘，味辛，寒。主心腹痛，癰腫潰膿，止痛。一名棘針。

### Literal Translation

It grows in Yongzhou. Original text of the Benjing: White jujube thorn (白棘), flavor pungent, cold. It chiefly treats heart and abdominal pain, abscesses and swellings ulcerating with pus, and stops pain. One name is 棘針.
