## 胡麻

### Source Text

以作油，微寒。利大腸，胞衣不落。生者摩瘡腫，生禿髮。

### Literal Translation

Made into oil, it is slightly cold. It frees the large intestine and (expels) the placenta that does not come down. The raw one is rubbed on sores and swellings and grows hair on baldness.
