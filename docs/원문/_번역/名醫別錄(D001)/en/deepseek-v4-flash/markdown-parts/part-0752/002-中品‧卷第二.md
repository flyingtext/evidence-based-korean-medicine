## 衛矛

### Source Text

八月采，陰乾

《本經》原文：衛矛，味苦，寒。主女子崩中下血，腹滿汗出，除邪，殺鬼毒蠱注。一名鬼箭。

### Literal Translation

Gather in the eighth month, dry in the shade. Original text of the Benjing: Euonymus (衛矛), flavor bitter, cold. It chiefly treats women's flooding with vaginal bleeding, abdominal fullness with sweating, dispels evil, kills ghost-poison and gu-infestation (蠱注). One name is 鬼箭.
