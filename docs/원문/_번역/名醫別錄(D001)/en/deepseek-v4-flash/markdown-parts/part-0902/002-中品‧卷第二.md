## 白菀

### Source Text

一名織女菀，一名苑。生漢中川穀，或山陽。正月、二月采，陰乾。

### Literal Translation

One name is Zhinvwan (織女菀), one name is Yuan (苑). It grows in the streams and valleys of Hanzhong, or at Shanyang. It is gathered in the first and second months and dried in the shade.
