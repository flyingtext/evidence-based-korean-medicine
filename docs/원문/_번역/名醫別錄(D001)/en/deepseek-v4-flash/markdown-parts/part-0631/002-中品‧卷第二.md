## 栝蔞根

### Source Text

莖葉，治中熱傷暑。生洪山陰地，入土深者良，生鹵地者有毒。二月、八月采根，曝乾，三十日成。

### Literal Translation

The stem and leaf treat heat-heat damage by the center and heat-stroke (中熱傷暑). It grows on shaded ground at Mount Hong; those that enter deep into the earth are good; those born in saline ground are poisonous. Gather the root in the second and eighth months, expose to sun to dry, completing in thirty days.
