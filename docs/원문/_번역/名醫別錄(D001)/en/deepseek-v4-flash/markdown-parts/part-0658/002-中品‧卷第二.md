## 續斷

### Source Text

味辛，無毒。主治崩中漏血，金瘡血內漏，止痛，生肌肉，及　傷、惡血、腰痛，關節緩急。一名接骨，一名南草，一名槐。

Flavor pungent, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat flooding with dripping blood, metal wound with internal blood effusion, stop pain, generate flesh, and (treat) injuries, foul blood, lumbar pain, and taut-relaxation (緩急) of the joints. One name is 接骨, one name is 南草, one name is 槐.
