## 鹿茸

### Source Text

味酸，微溫，無毒。主治虛勞洒洒如瘧，羸瘦，四肢酸疼，腰脊痛，小便利，泄精，血，破留血在腹，散石淋癰腫，骨中熱疽，養骨，安胎下氣，殺鬼精物，不可近陰令痿。久服耐老。

### Literal Translation

Sour in flavor, slightly warm, non-toxic. It treats vacuity-labor with shivering [chills and fever] like malaria (虛勞洒洒如瘧), emaciation (羸瘦), sore aches of the four limbs (四肢酸疼), pain of the lumbus and spine (腰脊痛), profuse and free urination (小便利), seminal discharge (泄精), blood, breaking retained blood (留血) in the abdomen, dispersing stone strangury (石淋) and carbuncle-swelling (癰腫), heat-carbuncle within the bone (骨中熱疽), nourishing the bones, settling the fetus and sending qi downward (安胎下氣), and killing ghost-essence and spirits (殺鬼精物); one must not let it approach the genitals, causing impotence (令痿). Taken for a long time it endures aging (耐老).
