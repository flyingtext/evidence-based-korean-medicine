## 李核仁

### Source Text

味甘、苦，平，無毒。主治僵仆躋，瘀血，骨痛。根皮，大寒，主消渴，止心煩逆實，味苦，除痼熱，調中。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet and bitter, neutral, non-toxic. It governs falling and collapse, stagnant blood, and bone pain. The root bark, greatly cold, governs wasting-thirst, stops vexation and counterflow of the heart; the fruit, flavor bitter, removes stubborn heat and regulates the center.
