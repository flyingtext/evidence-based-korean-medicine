## 發

### Source Text

小寒，無毒。合雞子黃煎之，消為水，治小兒驚熱下痢。《本經》原文：發　，味苦，溫。

### Literal Translation

Slightly cold, non-toxic. Fried together with egg yolk, it dissolves into water and treats infantile fright-heat with dysentery. Original text of the Benjing: Fa (human hair), bitter in flavor, warm.
