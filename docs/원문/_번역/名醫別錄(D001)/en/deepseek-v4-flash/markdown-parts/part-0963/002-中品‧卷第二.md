## 雀卵

### Source Text

味酸，溫，無毒。主下氣，男子陰痿不起，強之令熱，多精有子。腦，治耳聾。

### Literal Translation

Sour in flavor, warm, non-toxic. It treats sending qi downward (下氣), men's yin-wei with the genitals not rising (陰痿不起), strengthening them and making them hot (強之令熱), and producing much essence and having children (多精有子). The brain treats deafness.
