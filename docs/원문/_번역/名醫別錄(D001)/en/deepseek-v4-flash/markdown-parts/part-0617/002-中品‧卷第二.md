## 葛根

### Source Text

無毒。主治傷寒中風頭痛，解肌發表出汗，開腠理，療金瘡，止痛，脅風痛。生根汁，大寒，治消渴，傷寒壯熱。

### Literal Translation

Not poisonous. Its chief actions are to treat cold-damage and wind-stroke headache, relax the surface, release the exterior and induce sweating, open the interstices, treat metal wounds, stop pain, and treat wind-pain of the hypochondrium. The juice of the raw root is greatly cold; it treats wasting-thirst and cold-damage with vigorous heat.
