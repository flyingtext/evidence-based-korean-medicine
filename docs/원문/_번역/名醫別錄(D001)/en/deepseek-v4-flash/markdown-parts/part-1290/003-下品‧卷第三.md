## 紫參

### Source Text

微寒，無毒。主治腸胃大熱，唾血，衄血，腸中聚血，癰腫諸瘡，止渴，益精。一戎，一名童腸，一名馬行。

### Literal Translation

Slightly cold, non-toxic. It governs great heat of the intestines and stomach (腸胃大熱), spitting blood (唾血), nosebleed (衄血), accumulated blood in the intestines (腸中聚血), carbuncle swelling and the various sores (癰腫諸瘡), stops thirst (止渴), and benefits essence (益精). One name is 戎 (róng), one name is 童腸 (tóngcháng), one name is 馬行 (mǎxíng).
