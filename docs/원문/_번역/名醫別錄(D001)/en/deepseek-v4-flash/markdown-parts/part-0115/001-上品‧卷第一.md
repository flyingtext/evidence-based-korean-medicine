## 術

### Source Text

二月、三月、八月、九月采根，曝乾。（防風、地榆為之使。）

《本經》原文：術，味苦，溫。

### Literal Translation

Gather the root in the second, third, eighth and ninth months and dry in the sun. (Saposhnikovia [防風] and Sanguisorba [地榆] are its envoys.)

Original text of the Shennong Bencao Jing [本經]: Atractylodes [術], flavor bitter, warm.
