## 髓

### Source Text

腎，平，主治腎氣。肉，溫補中，強五臟，益氣力。生者治口僻，割薄之。

### Literal Translation

The kidney, neutral, treats kidney qi (腎氣). The flesh, warm, supplements the center (補中), strengthens the five viscera (強五臟), and boosts qi and strength (益氣力). Used fresh it treats deviation of the mouth (口僻); cut into thin slices and apply it.
