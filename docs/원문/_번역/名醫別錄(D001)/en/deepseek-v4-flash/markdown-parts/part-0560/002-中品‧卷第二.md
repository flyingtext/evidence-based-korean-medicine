## 厲石華

### Source Text

味甘，無毒。主益氣，養神，止渴，除熱，強陰。生江南，如石華，采無時。

### Literal Translation

Flavor sweet, not poisonous. Its chief actions are to boost qi, nourish the spirit-shen, stop thirst, dispel heat, and strengthen yin. It grows south of the Yangtze, similar to 石華; gather without a fixed time.
