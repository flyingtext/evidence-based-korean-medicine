## 馬刀

### Source Text

《本經》原文：馬刀，味辛，微寒。主漏下赤白，寒熱，破石淋，殺禽獸，賊鼠。生池澤。

### Literal Translation

Original text of the Benjing: Madao, flavor acrid, slightly cold. It governs leaking of red and white, chills and fever, breaks stone strangury, and kills birds and beasts and thieving rats. It grows in ponds and marshes.
