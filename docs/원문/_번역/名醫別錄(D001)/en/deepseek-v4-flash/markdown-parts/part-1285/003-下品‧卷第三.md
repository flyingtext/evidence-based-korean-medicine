## 五茄

### Source Text

五月、七月采莖，十月采根，陰乾。（遠志為之使，畏蛇皮、玄參。）

《本經》原文：五茄皮，味辛，溫。

### Literal Translation

Gathered as stems in the fifth and seventh months, as root in the tenth month, dried in the shade. (遠志 (yuǎnzhì) is its envoy; it fears 蛇皮 (shépí) and 玄參 (xuánshēn).)

The original text of the 《Bencao Jing》: 五茄皮 (wǔjiāpí), flavor acrid, warm.
