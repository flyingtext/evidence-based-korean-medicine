## 三時伏氣外感篇

### Source Text

幼兒斷乳納食，值夏月脾胃主氣，易於肚膨泄瀉，足心熱，形體日瘦，或煩渴善食，漸成五疳積聚，當審體之強弱，病之新久，有餘者疏胃清熱，食入糞色白，或不化，健脾佐消導清熱；若溼熱內鬱，蟲積腹痛，導滯驅蟲，微下之；緩調用肥兒丸之屬。

### Literal Translation

When young children are weaned and begin to eat, in summer the spleen and stomach govern the qi, so abdominal distension and diarrhea easily occur, with heat in the soles, the body wasting day by day, or vexation, thirst, and good appetite, gradually becoming the five gan accumulations. One should examine the strength of the body and the newness or chronicity of the disease. For those with excess, soothe the stomach and clear heat; if food is eaten but the stool is white or undigested, strengthen the spleen with the aid of digestives and heat-clearing. If damp-heat stagnates within with worm accumulation and abdominal pain, guide the stagnation, expel worms, and purge slightly. For slow regulation, use Fei'er Wan and the like.
