## 三時伏氣外感篇

### Source Text

診之大法，先明體質強弱，肌色蒼嫩，更詢起居致病因由；初病體堅質實，前法可遵；久病氣餒神衰，雖有腹痛後重，亦宜詳審，不可概以攻積清奪施法。

### Literal Translation

The great method of diagnosis is first to clarify the strength or weakness of the constitution and the ashen or tender quality of the complexion, then to inquire into the daily life and the cause of the illness. If the disease is recent and the body is firm and the constitution substantial, one may follow the previous method. If the disease is chronic with weakened qi and failing spirit, even with abdominal pain and tenesmus one should examine carefully and must not apply the methods of attacking accumulations and clearing-seizing indiscriminately.

Dysentery with lockjaw that cannot take in water and grain all comes from heat rising and turbidity attacking. Once the hot qi opens, one can at once take food; the medicine should be given frequently for two or three days.
