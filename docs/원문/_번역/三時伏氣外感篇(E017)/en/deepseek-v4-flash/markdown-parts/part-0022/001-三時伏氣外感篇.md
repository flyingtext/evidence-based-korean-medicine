## 三時伏氣外感篇

### Source Text

夏季秋熱，小兒泄瀉，或初愈未愈，滿口皆生疳蝕，嘗有阻塞咽喉致危者，此皆在裏溼盛生熱，熱氣蒸灼，津液不生，溼熱偏傷氣分，治在上焦，或佐淡滲，世俗常刮西瓜翠衣治疳，取其輕揚滲利也。

### Literal Translation

In the heat of summer and autumn, children have diarrhea, and whether just recovered or not, the whole mouth develops gan erosion, sometimes blocking the throat and endangering life. This is all because dampness prevails within and generates heat; the hot qi steams and scorches so fluids are not produced, and damp-heat injures the qi aspect in a one-sided way. Treat the upper burner, or assist with bland percolation. The common custom is to scrape watermelon rind (xigua cuiyi) to treat gan, taking advantage of its light, floating, percolating, and draining nature.

## 三時伏氣外感篇

### Source Text

夏季溼熱鬱蒸，脾胃氣弱，水穀之氣不運，溼著內蘊為熱，漸至浮腫腹脹，小水不利，治之非法，水溼久漬，逆行犯肺，必生欬嗽喘促，甚則坐不得臥，俯不得仰，危期速矣。

### Literal Translation

In summer, damp-heat stagnates and steams, the qi of the spleen and stomach weakens, the qi of water and grain does not move, dampness adheres and accumulates within to become heat, gradually reaching edema and abdominal distension, with difficult urination. If treated contrary to the method, the water-dampness soaks for a long time and flows retrograde to invade the lung, inevitably producing cough and rapid breathing; in severe cases one cannot lie down when sitting, nor look up when bent forward, and the danger comes quickly.
