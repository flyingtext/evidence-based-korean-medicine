## 三時伏氣外感篇

### Source Text

大凡喘必生脹，脹必生喘，方書以先喘後脹治在肺，先脹後喘治在脾，亦定論也。

### Literal Translation

In general, wheezing must give rise to distension, and distension must give rise to wheezing. The formularies hold that when wheezing precedes distension one treats the lung, and when distension precedes wheezing one treats the spleen; this too is an established view.

## 三時伏氣外感篇

### Source Text

閱近代世俗論水溼喘脹之證，以《內經》開鬼門取汗為表治，分利小便潔淨府為裏治；《經》旨《病能篇》謂：諸溼腫滿，皆屬於脾。

### Literal Translation

Looking at how the common physicians of recent times discuss water-dampness wheezing-distension, they take the Neijing's "opening the ghost gate" to induce sweating as the exterior treatment, and dividing and draining the urine to "cleanse the mansion" as the interior treatment. The Classic's meaning, in the Bingneng pian, says, "All damp swellings and fullness belong to the spleen."
