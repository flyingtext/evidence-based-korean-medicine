## 三時伏氣外感篇

### Source Text

斯時若以肺藥少加一味清降，使藥力不致直趨腸中，而上痹可開，諸竅自爽，無如市醫僉云結胸，皆用連、蔞、柴、枳苦寒直降，致閉塞愈甚，告斃者多。

### Literal Translation

At this time, if one adds a single clearing-and-descending herb to the lung medicines so that the medicinal force does not rush straight into the intestines, the obstruction above opens and the orifices clear by themselves. But the marketplace physicians all call it chest binding and use the bitter-cold, directly descending Lian, Lou, Chai, and Zhi, so that the blockage becomes still more severe and many die.
