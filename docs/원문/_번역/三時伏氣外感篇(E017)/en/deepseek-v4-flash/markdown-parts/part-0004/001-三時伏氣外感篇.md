## 三時伏氣外感篇

### Source Text

蓋足經順傳，如太陽傳陽明，人皆知之；肺病失治，逆傳心包絡，人多不知者。

### Literal Translation

The foot channels transmit in order, as when the Greater Yang transmits to the Bright Yang, which everyone knows. But that lung disease, when treatment is missed, transmits retrograde into the pericardium is something most people do not know.
