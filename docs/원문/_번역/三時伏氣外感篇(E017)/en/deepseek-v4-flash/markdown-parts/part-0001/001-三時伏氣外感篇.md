## 三時伏氣外感篇

### Source Text

春溫一證，由冬令收藏未固，昔人以冬寒內伏，藏於少陰，入春發於少陽，以春木內應肝膽也。

### Literal Translation

The condition of spring warmth (chunwen) arises because the storing and closing of essence in winter was not secure. The ancients held that the winter cold pathogen lurks within, is stored in the Lesser Yin (shaoyin), and erupts in spring from the Lesser Yang (shaoyang), because the wood qi of spring corresponds internally to the liver and gallbladder.
