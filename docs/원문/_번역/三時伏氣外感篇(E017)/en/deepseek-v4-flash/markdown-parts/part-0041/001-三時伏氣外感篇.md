## 三時伏氣外感篇

### Source Text

秋燥一證，氣分先受，治肺為急，若延綿數十日之久，病必入血分，又非輕浮肺藥可治，須審體質證端，古謂治病當活潑潑地，如盤走珠耳。

### Literal Translation

In the condition of autumn dryness, the qi aspect is first affected, so treating the lung is urgent. If it drags on for several tens of days, the disease must enter the blood aspect, and it can no longer be treated with light, floating lung medicines. One must examine the constitution and the details of the pattern. The ancients said, "In treating disease one should be lively and fluid, like a pearl rolling on a plate."
