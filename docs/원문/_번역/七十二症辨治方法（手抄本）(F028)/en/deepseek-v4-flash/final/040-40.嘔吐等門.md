## 40.嘔吐等門

### Source Text

聲物俱出謂之嘔，有物無聲見吐醫；有聲無物名乾嘔，其聲輕小短而微。
噦脘有聲亦無物，聲音重大濁長依；翻胃食已即來吐，也有朝食暮吐之。
噎膈思食難進口，噁心欲吐不吐奇；吞酸咽下酸津液，吐酸湧出水稀稀。
欸逆俗呼為打呃，哇聲發作勢蹺蹊；噯氣即今之阿欠，稠痰濕熱胃中迷。
蹧襟不外痰因火，痛不痛兮饑不饑；大都脾胃肺肝患，額汗口沫險無疑。
兩便不閉無妨事，保中湯飲任馳驅；順氣和中加減用，切脈因時再制宜。

保中湯：治飲食不下吐瀉不止。
保中湯內藿香苓，黃連梔子半砂仁；茯苓陳皮炙甘草，龍膽加上效如神。
順氣湯中香附芩，陳皮甘草半砂仁；朮枳連梔神麯簇，煨薑一片保安寧。

### Literal Translation

Sound and material both issuing is called vomiting (ou); there is material without sound, see it, treat as vomiting (tu). Sound without material is named dry retching; its sound is light, small, short and faint.
Hiccup-reflux (yue) has sound likewise and no material; its sound is loud and heavy, turbid, long-held. Swallowing-reversal (fange) — after eating, it comes then vomits; also there is the morning-eaten evening-vomited one.
Diaphragm-obstruction (yege): desiring to eat, hard to admit into mouth. Nausea: desire to vomit and not vomit — strange. Swallowing-sour: swallowing, sour gastric fluids; vomiting-acid: surging out like thin water.
Hi-cough (kuonin/欸逆), commonly called hiccup; the wa-sound erupting, the state is devious. Eructation (aiqi) is now the yawn; thick phlegm, damp-heat confused within the stomach.
Tatters and hems are nothing beyond phlegm caused by fire; pain or no pain, hungry or not hungry. For the most part it is a spleen-stomach and lung-liver illness; forehead sweat and mouth foam portend danger without doubt.
Urine and stool both not blocked, no hindrance — Baozhong Tang Yin (Protect-the-Centre Beverage) let it gallop. Shunqi Hezhong (Smooth-Qi Harmonize-the-Centre) with addition and subtraction; feel the pulse, according to the season, then adapt the treatment.

Baozhong Tang (Protect-the-Centre Decoction): treats eating and drinking not going down, vomiting and diarrhea not stopping.
Within Baozhong Tang are Huoxiang (Pogostemon) and Fuling (Poria); Huanglian (Coptis), Zhizi (Gardenia) and Ban-Sha (Pinellia & Sharen). Fuling and Chenpi with prepared Gancao (Licorice); add Longdan (Gentian) and the effect is like a spirit.
In Shunqi Tang (Smooth-Qi Decoction) Xiangfu (Cyperus) and Qin (Scutellaria); Chenpi, Gancao (Licorice), Ban-Sha (Pinellia & Sharen). Zhu (Atractylodes), Zhi (Zhike/Bitter Orange), Lian-Zhi (Coptis & Gardenia) and Shenqu (Medicated Leaven) gathered; one piece of roasted ginger ensures peace and tranquility.
