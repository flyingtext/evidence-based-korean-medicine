## 27.濕門方法

### Source Text

問君何以知中濕，沾染中間不自識；飛專雨水居處卑，豈都生冷汗氣積。
令人下體肉皮瘡，遍身浮腫脾胃失；脈來三至半奇寒，羌活除濕湯當食。
羌活除濕也防風，蒼朮槁本妙無倫。升麻柴胡相鼓舞，周天風濕痛無蹤。

夫濕性下流，泥濘經絡，今以風藥治濕，譬之卑濕之地，得太陽即乾。得和風則潔。亦治之一奇。

### Literal Translation

I ask you, how do you come to know struck-by-dampness? In touching and infecting it one does not recognize it of oneself; flying, dwelling in rain water, residing in lowly places — how would they not engender cold sweat and qi accumulation.
It makes one's lower body's flesh and skin ulcerated, the whole body swollen, the spleen-stomach losing function; the pulse comes three and a half beats (slow), oddly cold — Qianghuo Chushi Tang (Notopterygium Remove-Dampness Decoction) should be taken as food.
Qianghuo Chushi also with Fangfeng (Ledebourielle); Cangzhu (Atractylodes) and Gaoben (Ligusticum) are excellent beyond compare. Shengma (Cimicifuga) and Chaihu (Bupleurum) rouse and dance together; the wind-damp pain of the whole circuit has no trace.

Now dampness naturally flows downward, muddying and clogging the channels and collaterals; here treating dampness with wind medicinals is like a low, damp place — getting the Sun it becomes dry; getting harmonious wind it becomes clean. This is also a marvel of treatment.

- 脈來三至半=middle/weak pulse.
