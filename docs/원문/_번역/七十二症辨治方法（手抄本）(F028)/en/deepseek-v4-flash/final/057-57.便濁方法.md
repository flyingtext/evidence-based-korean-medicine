## 57.便濁方法

### Source Text

小便流濁白和紅，白如米泔赤如膿；脾胃濕熱痰流滲，歸入膀胱氣化成。
譬之天地沖和候，凡水澄波徹底清；天氣炎蒸水混濁，濕熱道理了然明。
又有當泄不泄致，也有忘想沖閃因；更有清濁牽絲膩，萆薢分清飲可尋。
萆薢分清飲最靈，豬苓澤瀉赤茯苓，石蒲遠志車前子，麥門甘草地黃蒸。

### Literal Translation

Urine flowing turbid, both white and red: white like rice-wash (water of washing rice), red like pus. The spleen and stomach, damp-heat, phlegm flows and seeps; returning into the bladder, the qi transforms and forms it.
It is like the temperate, harmonious season of heaven and earth; all still water settles to the bottom, thoroughly clear. When the weather is hot and sweltering, the water becomes mixed and turbid; the principle of damp-heat is clear and luminous.
There is also the one caused by failing to drain when one should drain; there is also the cause of wandering thoughts and sudden flashing (imagination). Further there is the clear-turbid with clinging silken thread and greasy; Bixie Fenqing Yin (Dioscorea Separate-the-Clear Beverage) may be sought.
Bixie Fenqing Yin (Dioscorea Separate-the-Clear Beverage) is most efficacious; Zhuling, Zexie and Chifuling (red Poria). Shipu (Acorus), Yuanzhi (Polygala) and Cheqianzi (Plantago Seed); Maimen (Ophiopogon), Gancao (Licorice) and Dihuang (Rehmannia) steamed.

### Translator's Notes

- 便濁=turbid urine; 萆薢分清飲=Bixie Fenqing Yin.
- 米泔=water of washing rice.
