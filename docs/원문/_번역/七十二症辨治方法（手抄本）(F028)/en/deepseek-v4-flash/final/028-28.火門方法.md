## 28.火門方法

### Source Text

心為君火配五行，尊君離位水相生；輔弼龍火名為相，元氣之賊實可憎。
乘機煽禍易忘動，侮水煎熬腎命爭；實火則瀉導赤散，虛火宜補撤薪丸。
各經邪火認脈症，清湯涼飲減和增；更有陰虛火動症，地黃六味各精填。
導赤散內藥三般，生地木通一處欑；甘草尾稍相佐使，心驚實火刻時寬。
清涼湯用生地黃，梔子玄參翹麥門；甘草桔梗黃連入，一杯之水撲療源。

撤薪丸
黃連　黃柏　黃芩　梔子　木通　犀角　生地　甘草　人參　五味　麥冬　柴胡。

夫心與小腸相表裏，故心熱則小腸亦熱，而小便必赤是方也。生地可以涼心血。甘草稍可以瀉熱毒，佐以木通則直去小腸膀胱矣。曰導赤散是瀉丙丁火邪。從便而泄也。

### Literal Translation

The heart is the sovereign fire, matching the five agents; venerating the sovereign in the Li position (south), water is born with it. The auxiliary wing, dragon-fire, is named minister fire; the thief of the primordial qi, truly hateful.
Taking advantage of opportunity it fans calamity, easily forgets to be moved (impulsive); bullying water it torments and boils, contending with the mingmen. For sthenia (replete) fire one drains with Daochi San (Drain-the-Red Powder); for deficiency fire one should supplement with Chaihenwan (Pull-the-Firewood Pill).
For the fire-pathogen of the individual channels, recognize the pulse and signs; from the clear decoction and cooling beverage subtract and increase. There is moreover the condition of yin deficiency with fire stirring; with Dihuang Liuwei (Six-Flavor Rehmannia), each essence-qi is replenished.
Daochi San within, three kinds of medicinals: Shengdi (Rehmannia) and Mutong (Akebia) gathered in one spot. Gancao (Licorice) tail-tip serving as deputy envoy; the startled heart with replete fire relaxes in an instant.
Qingliang Tang (Clear-Cool Decoction) uses Shengdihuang (Rehmannia); Zhizi (Gardenia), Xuanshen (Scrophularia), Lianqiao and Maimen (Ophiopogon). Gancao (Licorice), Jiegeng (Platycodon), Huanglian (Coptis) entering; a cup of water to quell the source of the ailment.

Chaihenwan (Pull-the-Firewood Pill)
Huanglian (Coptis), Huangbai (Phellodendron), Huangqin (Scutellaria), Zhizi (Gardenia), Mutong (Akebia), Xijiao (Rhinoceros horn/water buffalo horn), Shengdi (Rehmannia), Gancao (Licorice), Renshen (Ginseng), Wuwei (Schisandra), Maidong (Ophiopogon), Chaihu (Bupleurum).

Now the heart and small intestine are mutually exterior-interior; therefore when the heart is hot the small intestine is also hot, and urination must be red — this formula is for it. Shengdi can cool the heart blood. Gancao tip can drain the heat toxin, and assisted by Mutong it goes straight to the small intestine and bladder. Named Daochi San (Drain-the-Red); it is draining the bing-ding fire pathogen, discharging it by the urination.
