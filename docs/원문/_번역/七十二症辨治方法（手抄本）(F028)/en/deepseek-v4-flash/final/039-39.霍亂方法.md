## 39.霍亂方法

### Source Text

霍亂之症有何因，內外傷感病根深；忽然或吐而不瀉，或吐不瀉要認真。
心腹先吐腹先瀉，心腹齊痛吐瀉頻；若兼轉筋入腹死，濕霍亂兮虎狼名。
更有絞腸乾霍亂，吐瀉不得可憂驚；命在須臾無所禱，鹽湯探吐多救人。
藿香正氣三春用，五積隆冬注意尋；不伏水土形相似，本地砂泥最有靈。

藿香正氣散方見風門。

五積陳皮甘草薑，芎蒼芷芍茯苓詳；半夏桔梗歸厚朴，麻黃枳殼桂心良。

### Literal Translation

What brings about the condition of sudden turmoil (huo luan)? Interior and exterior contraction, the root of illness is deep. Suddenly perhaps vomiting without diarrhea, or vomiting or diarrhea — one must be careful to recognize.
Heart-abdomen first (pain), then vomit, then diarrhea; heart and abdomen uniformly painful with frequent vomiting and diarrhea. If additionally sinews turn and enter the abdomen (cramp), death; wet sudden turmoil carries the name of tiger and wolf.
There is also the wringing-intestine dry sudden turmoil, neither vomiting nor diarrhea obtainable — to be alarmed and feared. Life hangs by a thread with nothing to pray; the salt-water probing-emesis rescues many men.
Huoxiang Zhengqi (Pogostemon Rectifying-Qi) is used in the third spring; Wuji (Five-Accumulation) in deep winter, note to be sought. Not acclimating to the local water and soil, its form is similar (to huo luan); the local sand-mud is most efficacious.

Huoxiang Zhengqi San formula is seen in the wind gate (chapter 25).

Wuji (Five-Accumulation) with Chenpi, Gancao (Licorice) and Jiang (shengjiang); Chuanxiong, Cangzhu, Zhi (Baizhi), Shao (Peony) and Fuling (Poria) in detail. Banxia (Pinellia), Jiegeng (Platycodon), Dang Gui and Houpu; Mahuang (Ephedra), Zhiqiao (Bitter Orange) and Guixin (Cinnamon Heart) good.
