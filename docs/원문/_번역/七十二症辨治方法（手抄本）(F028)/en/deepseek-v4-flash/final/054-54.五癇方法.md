## 54.五癇方法

### Source Text

癇症方書列五名，今呼諸癇作癢眩；未生母腹沾驚怪，已出娘胎痰氣侵。
忽然僵仆流涎沫，口眼歪斜肢屈伸；聲為畜叫因為號，須臾蘇醒不知情。
天若失職濁居上，木部將軍可肅清；清心溫膽湯無讓，治法難逃內外因。
清心溫膽二陳全，人參遠志芎麥門；石蒲香附白芍朮，黃連枳實竹茹全。

### Literal Translation

For the epilepsy (xian) condition the formularies list five names; now people call the various epilepsies dizzy-dancing. Before birth, in the mother's womb, it caught fright and marvel; after exiting the mother's womb, phlegm-qi invades.
Suddenly stiff and fallen, streaming drool and foam; mouth and eyes deviated, the limbs bent and stretched. The sound being like an animal call, thereby it takes its name; in a moment it revives, unaware of the circumstances.
If heaven loses its duty (proper up-down), the turbid occupies the upper; the wood-general (liver) may be cleared and purged. Qingxin Wendan Tang (Clear-the-Heart Warm-the-Gallbladder Decoction) yields to none; the method of treatment does not escape the interior and exterior causes.
Qingxin Wendan (Clear-the-Heart Warm-the-Gallbladder) with Erchen (Two-Matured) all complete; Renshen (Ginseng), Yuanzhi (Polygala), Chuanxiong and Maimen (Ophiopogon). Shipu (Acorus), Xiangfu (Cyperus), Baishao and Zhu (Atractylodes); Huanglian (Coptis), Zhishi (Immature Bitter Orange) and Zhuru (Bamboo Shavings) all complete.
