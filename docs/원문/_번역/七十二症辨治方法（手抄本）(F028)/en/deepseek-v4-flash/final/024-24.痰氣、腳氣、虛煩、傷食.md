## 24.痰氣、腳氣、虛煩、傷食

### Source Text

痰氣類寒汗惡風，憎寒吐熱氣衝攻；頭項身體不強痛，幾希界限此中分。
腳氣如何亦類寒，頭痛心熱大便難，腳膝屈弱難移動，風寒濕熱發其間。
虛煩暴燥熱為魔，汗下功夫豈敢勞；不惡寒兮頭不痛，傷寒真似判絲毫。
傷食似寒何以知，頭眩身熱惡寒時；胸滿腹痛兼嘔吐，莫作傷寒一樣醫。

### Literal Translation

Phlegm-qi (tangi) mimics cold, sweating, fear of wind; aversion to cold, spitting heat, and qi rushing in attack. The head, nape and body are not stiff and painful; a hair's breadth boundary is here to distinguish.
How does beriberi (jiaoqi) likewise mimic cold? Headache, heart heat, difficult stool; knee and foot weak and bent, hard to move — wind, cold, damp and heat erupt from within them.
Vexing emptiness (xufan) with sudden irritation, heat is its devil; with the work of sweating and purging, how dare one toil with it? Not fearing cold, the head not aching — from cold damage truly one may discern to the last hair's breadth.
How is it known that food stasis mimics cold? At the time of dizziness, body heat and fearing cold; chest fullness and abdominal pain together with vomiting — do not treat it like the same as cold damage.

- 痰氣=tangi (phlegm-qi); 腳氣=jiǎoqì (beriberi, lower-limb qi disorder); 虛煩=xūfán (empty vexation).
