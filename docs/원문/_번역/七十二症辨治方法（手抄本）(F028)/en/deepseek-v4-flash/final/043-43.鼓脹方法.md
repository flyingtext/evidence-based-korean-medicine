## 43.鼓脹方法

### Source Text

中滿脹滿即鼓脹，四肢不腫頭無恙；臍凸筋青小水無，男從下起女從上。
按之不窟為逆症，不比水腫多般樣；分消湯飲可回春。家傳正氣誰堪讓。

分消湯
分消湯那內茯苓，蒼朴白朮枳砂仁；陳皮木香附澤瀉，大腹皮兮可坦平。
此方氣急加沉香，腫脹加菜頭子。

家傳正氣湯
家傳正氣烏藥奇，藿香附朴枳陳皮；半夏蒼朮大腹殼，理脾消脹任驅馳。

### Literal Translation

Middle fullness and bloating-fullness are (also) called drum-distension (guzhang); the four limbs not swollen, the head with no complaint. Navel protruding, sinews (veins) green, small water (scant urine); in men it rises from below, in women from above.
Press it and it does not make a hollow (pit) — a reversed condition; not comparable to many forms of water-swelling. Fenxiao Tang Yin (Separate-and-Drain Beverage) can return the spring; the family-transmitted Zhengqi (Rectify-Qi), who would yield its place?

Fenxiao Tang (Separate-and-Drain Decoction)
Fenxiao Tang (Separate-and-Drain) inside has Fuling (Poria); Cangzhu (Atractylodes), Houpu, Baizhu and Zhi (Zhike) and Sharen. Chenpi, Muxiang (Aucklandia), Xiangfu (Cyperus) and Zexie (Alisma); with Dafupi (Areca Peel) it may even and level.
In this formula, if qi urgent add Chenxiang (Aquilaria); if swollen, add root of radish.

Jiachuan Zhenqi Tang (Family-Transmitted Rectify-Qi Decoction)
The family-transmitted zhenqi, Wuyao (Lindera) is marvelous; Huoxiang (Pogostemon), Xiangfu, Houpu, Zhi (Zhike) and Chenpi. Banxia (Pinellia), Cangzhu (Atractylodes), Dafupi (Areca Peel) and shell; regulating the spleen and resolving distension, letting it gallop freely.
