## 35.哮吼方法

### Source Text

哮吼原來肺竅痰，又緣外熱內包寒；咽喉日夜無停響，聲音古怪若弦彈。
心染浮滑湯丸愈，久治沉澁覓靈丹；治哮湯頭兼發散，不必多方孟浪談。
定哮湯用參阿膠，麻黃甘草桑白加；五味半夏罌粟殼，臨期應變始為佳。

### Literal Translation

As to roaring/panting-with-whistle (xiaohou), originally it is the phlegm of the lung orifice, and again because of external heat wrapping internal cold. The throat day and night has no ceasing sound; the voice and timbre are strange, like a plucked string.
The heart polluted with floating-smooth (pulse) is cured by decoction or pill; long treatment, deep-hesitant (pulse), seek the efficacious elixir. Treat the panting (xiao) with a decoction-head combining dispersing the exterior; do not need many methods, rashly spoken.
Dingxiao Tang (Settle-the-Roaring-Panting Decoction) uses Renshen (Ginseng) and Ejiao (Ass-Hide Gelatin); Mahuang (Ephedra), Gancao (Licorice) and Sangbai (Mulberry Root Bark) added.
