## 31.鬱門方法

### Source Text

伏脈五鬱已先知，木喜條達急吐之；火癝上炎須汗發，土惠停滯奪為奇。
金本清虛雍何塞，水性奔流折莫遲；血氣濕熱痰與食，又名六鬱亦須知。
升者不升降不降，當變不變失其時；諸鬱病根由此始，解鬱湯頭造化機。
解鬱香附蒼朮芎，陳皮神麯白茯從；貝母翹梔蘇梗枳，再勞國老在其中。

### Literal Translation

With the hidden (fu) pulse, the five depressions (wu yu) are already known first; wood likes free reaching-abundance, urgently emesis it. Fire burning upward must be sweated out; earth (spleen) delights in stagnation, seizing (purging) it is marvelous.
Metal of itself is clear and empty — what is it clogged? Water's nature rushes and flows — break (unblock) it, do not delay. Blood, qi, damp-heat, phlegm and food are also named the six depressions (liu yu), one must know.
The ascending does not ascend, the descending does not descend; when it should transform and does not transform, it loses its season. The root causes of all depressions begin from this; Jieyu Tang (Resolve-Depression Decoction) head is the mechanism of creation.
Jieyu (Resolve-depression) with Xiangfu (Cyperus), Cangzhu (Atractylodes) and Chuanxiong; Chenpi (Tangerine), Shenqu (Medicated Leaven), Baifu (Poria) following. Beimu (Fritillaria), Lianqiao (Forsythia), Zhizi (Gardenia), Sugeng (Perilla stem) and Zhi (Zhike/Bitter Orange); again the state elder (Guolao=licorice) labors within it.

### Translator's Notes

- 五鬱=the five depressions (wood fire earth metal water); 六鬱=six depressions.
- 解鬱湯=Jieyu Tang.
