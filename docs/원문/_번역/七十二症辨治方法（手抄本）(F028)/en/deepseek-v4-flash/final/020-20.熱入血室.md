## 20.熱入血室

### Source Text

婦人傷感中風寒，頭痛發熱也惡寒；月信適來沖此症，身涼腰脊痛難堪。
晝則精明夜譫語，似瘧非瘧不一觀；熱入血室為名號，莫把杭州作汴看。

### Literal Translation

Women, when injured by wind or struck by wind-cold, have headache and fever and also fear cold. If menstruation happens to arrive amid this condition, the body cools and the lumbar spine aches unbearably.
Clear and lucid by day, delirious by night; like malaria yet not malaria, not a single view. “Heat entering the blood chamber” (re ru xueshi) is its name; do not take Hangzhou for Bian (city) — mistake not one for the other.

### Translator's Notes

- 熱入血室=heat entering the blood chamber (uterus).
- 莫把杭州作汴看=do not mistake one place for another (don't misdiagnose).
