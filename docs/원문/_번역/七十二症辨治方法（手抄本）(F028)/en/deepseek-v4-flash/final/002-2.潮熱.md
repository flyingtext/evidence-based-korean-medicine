## 2.潮熱

### Source Text

潮熱有常如潮水，陽明荊棘使之然；潮於寅卯屬肝木，其餘仿此細思量。
消導疏通為治法，溫能除熱忌寒涼；又有勞傷潮熱症，不比傷寒一樣腔。

### Literal Translation

Tidal fever is regular like the tide; Yangming (Stomach/Large Intestine) disorder causes it. A tide at the double-hour Yin-Mao (3-7 a.m.) belongs to liver wood; the rest are analogous, ponder them carefully.
Dispersing and freeing is the method of treatment; warmth can remove heat, and cold-cooling is to be avoided. There is also the tidal fever of overexertion injury, which is not the same tune as cold damage.

### Translator's Notes

- 潮熱=tidal fever (regular periodic fever).
- 寅卯=Yin and Mao double-hours of the wood/liver period.
