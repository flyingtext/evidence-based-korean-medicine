## 25.風門方法

### Source Text

諸風肝膽木招來，六部浮弦疾病胎；風痱癔脾偏枯四，千枝萬藥此中栽。
血氣中風難舞蹈，半身不遂臥形骸；癱瘓瘈瘲搐搦作，遍身疼痛可哀哉。
經絡中風忘痛癢，多因麻木肉皮頑；風中府兮神恍惚，昏迷不醒倒塵埃。
至於中臟腸堪斷，痰迷心竅眼歪斜；牙關噤閉聲音杳，痰涎壅盛噴唾花。
假道伐虢先鋒將，烏藥順氣扣關和；藿香正氣無拘泥，續命湯成發表證。
萬一餘黨未心服，防風通信和增減；最忌頭搖併發直，汗雨如珠面若妝。
鼻舌喉拽鋸魚咽，水道不進命難存；中氣形狀象中風，脈狀身冷不相同。
且無涎沫何難認，誤作風醫命難殂；更有許多相類處，須作風吹列調醫。
中氣者口角無涎沫，中風者口角有涎沫；中氣脈沉緩，輕者用藿香正氣散。
中風脈浮洪，重者用順風勻氣散。

通關散
皂角一味，為末，吹少許入鼻內，有嚏可救，無嚏不治。蓋肺氣絕也。若口不開則筋先絕矣。亦不治。

烏藥順氣湯
烏藥順氣僵蠶薑，枳殼陳皮芎芷詳；甘草麻黃和桔梗，中風先服此為良。

夫偏身麻痹表氣不順也，故治以麻黃川芎。語言澀，裏氣不順也。故治以烏藥陳皮枳殼。口眼歪斜面部之氣不順也。故治以白芷僵蠶，喉中氣急甘草可緩。肺氣上逆桔梗可也。痰之為物，寒則結滯，熱則流行，佐以乾薑行其滯也。此治標之劑也。

藿香正氣散
藿香正氣紫蘇臣，大腹陳皮芷朮苓；桔梗半夏甘草厚，風寒暑濕各相應。

凡治風必先理氣，氣順則痰自消。故用藿香正氣則清陽上升濁陰下降。天翻地覆之分定，何邪作梗耶。

續瞑湯下參附子，防風已桂麻黃使；甘草杏仁芎芍芩，不復四生反起死。

古人以此方混治中風，未詳其症。予謂麻黃杏仁湯也。仲景以之治太陽之傷寒。桂枝芍藥桂枝湯也。仲景治之太陽之中風。如是言之則中風而有頭痛，身熱脊強者皆在所必用也。人參、甘草四君之二也。局方用之補氣，芍藥四物之二也。局方用以養血。如此言之。則中風而有氣虛血虛也。皆在所必用者。風淫木疾故佐以防風，濕淫腹疾故佐以防己。陰淫寒疾故佐以附子。陽淫熱疾故佐以黃芩。蓋疾不單來。襟襟操而至，故其用藥亦兼該也。

防風通聖歸荊荷，國老將軍翹石膏。桔梗梔芎芩芍朮，麻黃芷滑起沉屙。

夫防風麻黃解表藥也。風熱之在皮膚也，得之由汗而泄。荊艾清上藥也，風熱之在巔頂也，得之由鼻而泄也。大黃芷消通利藥也，風熱之在陽胃也。得之由後而泄也。風熱之在決瀆者得之由溺而泄。風淫手鬲肺膚受邪。滑石桔梗清肺腑也。而連翹黃芩又所以祛諸經之遊火。風之為患肝木主之。川芎、當歸和肝血也。而甘草白朮又所以和胃氣而健脾胃者也。

定風丹
秦艽　羌活　甘草　防風　川芎　白芷　當歸　白朮　白芍　獨活　白芍　獨活　熟地　黃芩　生地　石膏　細辛。

許學士云中風虛邪也。留而不去其病則實，故用驅風養血之劑，以治之。用秦艽為君者。以其主宰一身之風。石膏去胸中總司之火。羌活去太陽百節之風疼，防風為諸風為藥中之軍卒。三陽數變之風則有白芷風熱干乎氣，清以黃芩。風熱干乎血涼以生地獨活。留風濕在足少陽甘草緩風邪。上逆於肺。乃當歸、熟地、黃耆，所以養血於疏風之後。一以濟風藥之燥。一使手得血而能握，足得血而能步也。

### Literal Translation

The various winds, liver and gallbladder wood summon them; in the six positions a floating-wiry pulse bodes illness. Wind-withering, sluggish, melancholy-spleen and hemiplegia are the four; a thousand branches and ten-thousand medicaments are planted in this midst.
Blood and qi stroke makes it hard to dance and move; hemiplegia, lying prostrate in form and limbs. Paralysis and flaccid-spasm, twitching and seizures arise; pain of the whole body throughout — how lamentable.
Channel and collateral wind stroke forgets pain and itching, much due to numb wood, the flesh and skin stiff. Wind striking the bowels makes the spirit confused; coma, unawakened, toppling into the dust.
Arriving at striking the viscera, the bowel to be severed is grievous; phlegm confounding the heart orifice, eyes and mouth deviated. Jaw teeth tightly locked, the voice silent and gone; phlegm-sputum congested and full, spitting spittle-flower.
Borrowing a path through Guo to strike Yu, the vanguard general — Wuyao Shunqi (Lindera Smoothing Qi) opens the passes and harmonizes; Huoxiang Zhengqi (Pogostemon Rectifying Qi) without constraint and binding; Xuming Tang (Resuscitating the Life Decoction) accomplished, issues the exterior signs.
Should one in a myriad of residual followers not yet be convinced at heart, Fangfeng Tongxin (Ledebouriella) communicate and adjust by increase and decrease. Most feared are head shaking with hair standing erect; sweat-rain like pearls, the face as if made up.
Nose, tongue, throat like a saw dragged; fish in swallowing, the water-way refuses entry, the life is hard to preserve. Qi-stroke's form is like wind stroke; the pulse shape and body being cold are not the same.
Moreover, having no drool and foam, how would it be hard to recognize? Wrongly treating it as wind, the life is hard to cut short. There are yet many similar aspects; one must treat them as wind-blown categories and arrange the medicine.
The qi-struck one: at the corner of the mouth there is no drool or foam; the wind-struck one: at the corner of the mouth there is drool and foam. Qi-stroke pulse is deep and slow; the light use Huoxiang Zhengqi San (Pogostemon Rectifying Qi Powder).
Wind-stroke pulse is floating and surging; the severe use Shunfeng Yungi San (Smooth-the-Wind Even-Qi Powder).

Tongguan San (Open-the-Pass Powder)
Gansu (Zaojiao, Gleditsia) alone as one ingredient, grind to powder, blow a little into the nostrils; if there is a sneeze it may be rescued, if no sneeze it is not treated. For the lung qi has ceased. If the mouth does not open then the sinews have already ceased first. Also not treated.

Wuyao Shunqi Tang (Lindera Smoothing Qi Decoction)
Wuyao (Lindera) soothing qi, Jiangcan (Bombyx batryticatus) and ginger; Zhiqiao (Bitter Orange), Chenpi (Tangerine Peel), Xiong (Chuanxiong) and Zhi (Baizhi) in detail; Gancao (Licorice), Mahuang (Ephedra) and Jiegeng (Platycodon); in wind stroke take these first as good.

Now hemiplegic body with numbness — the superficial (exterior) qi is not smooth, therefore treat with Mahuang and Chuanxiong. Slurred speech — the interior qi is not smooth, therefore treat with Wuyao, Chenpi and Zhiqiao. Mouth and eye deviated, the facial qi is not smooth, therefore treat with Baizhi and Jiangcan; throat qi urgent, Gancao may ease it. Lung qi ascending adversely, Jiegeng may serve. Of the phlegm as a thing, in cold it congeals and stagnates, in heat it flows; assist with ganjiang (dried ginger) to move its stagnation. This is a formula for treating the branch (manifestation).

Huoxiang Zhengqi San (Pogostemon Rectifying Qi Powder)
Huoxiang (Pogostemon) rectifying qi, Zisu (Perilla) as minister, Dafupi (Areca Peel), Chenpi, Baizhi, Zhu (Atractylodes) and Fuling; Jiegeng (Platycodon), Banxia (Pinellia), Gancao (licorice) and Houpu; wind, cold, damp and summer-heat each respond accordingly.

In all treatment of wind one must first regulate qi; when qi is smooth the phlegm naturally resolves itself. Therefore using Huoxiang Zhengqi makes lucid yang ascend above and turbid yin descend below. When the dividing of heaven turned over and earth settled is fixed, what pathogen would make obstruction?

Xuming Tang (Resuscitating-Life Decoction): below it takes Renshen (Ginseng) and Fuzi; Fangfeng (Ledebourielle) and Gui (Cinnamon) with Mahuang as envoys. Gancao (Licorice), Xingren (Apricot Kernel), Chuanxiong, Shaoyao (Peony) and Huangqin (Scutellaria); in no way returning to the four-sheng, it reverses death and raises the dying.

The ancients use this formula to conflate the treatment of wind stroke, without detailing the condition. I say it is a Mahuang and Xingren decoction. Zhongjing uses it to treat cold damage of Sun (Taiyang). Guizhi and Shaoyao are the Guizhi Tang (Cinnamon Twig Decoction). Zhongjing uses it to treat wind stroke of Sun. Saying it so, then wind stroke with headache, body heat and stiff spine are all cases that must use it. Renshen and Gancao are two of the four gentleman (Sijunzi Tang). The Bureau (Jufang) formulas use them to supplement qi; Shaoyao is two of the four-substances (Siwu). The Bureau formulas use them to nourish blood. Saying it so, then wind stroke with qi deficiency and blood deficiency, all are cases that must use them. Wind excess being a wood disease, therefore assist with Fangfeng; damp excess being an abdominal disease, therefore assist with Fangji; yin excess being a cold disease, therefore assist with Fuzi; yang excess being a heat disease, therefore assist with Huangqin (Scutellaria). For diseases do not come singly, they arrive hem and haw entangled together, therefore the use of medicinals also covers them all.

Fangfeng Tongsheng (Ledebourielle Unblock the Sages) gathers Jing (Schizonepeta), Dang Gui and He (Mint); the state elder (Guolao=licorice) and General (Dahuang=rhubarb) with Qiao (Forsythia) and Shigao (gypsum). Jiegeng (Platycodon), Zhi (Gardenia), Chuanxiong, Qin (Scutellaria), Zhu (Atractylodes) and Shao (Peony); Mahuang, Bai Zhi and Huashi (talc) lift the deep chronic ailment from its bed.

Now Fangfeng and Mahuang are exterior-resolving medicinals. Wind-heat that is in the skin, received, is discharged by sweat. Jing and Ai are upper-clearing medicinals. Wind-heat that is at the crown of the head, received, is discharged by the nose. Dahuang, Bai (Zhizi) and Huashi are free-flowing and purging medicinals; wind-heat that is in the yang stomach, received, is discharged by the rear. Wind-heat that is in the watercourse (bladder) is discharged by urine. Wind excess striking the diaphragm, the lung skin receives the pathogen. Huashi and Jiegeng clear the lung bowels. And Lianqiao and Huangqin moreover are what quell the wandering fire of the various channels. Wind as a harm, liver wood governs it. Chuanxiong and Dang Gui harmonize the liver blood. And Gancao and Baizhu moreover are what harmonize the stomach qi and fortify the spleen and stomach.

Dingfeng Dan (Settle-the-Wind Pill)
Qinjiao (Gentiana macrophylla), Qianghuo (Notopterygium), Gancao (Licorice), Fangfeng (Ledebourielle), Chuanxiong, Bai Zhi, Dang Gui, Baizhu, Baishao, Duhuo (Angelica pubescens), Baishao, Duhuo, Shudihuang (prepared Rehmannia), Huangqin (Scutellaria), Shengdihuang (fresh Rehmannia), Shigao (gypsum), Xixin (Asarum).

Master Xu says wind stroke is a deficiency pathogen. If it lingers and does not leave, that disease then becomes replete; therefore use wind-expelling and blood-nourishing formulas to treat it. Using Qinjiao as sovereign, for it governs the wind of the whole body. Shigao removes the fire of the general supervisor within the chest. Qianghuo removes the wind-pain of the hundred joints of Sun (Taiyang). Fangfeng is for the various winds the soldier-general in the medicine. For wind of the three yang's many transformations there is Bai Zhi; wind-heat drying it to qi, clear with Huangqin. Wind-heat drying it to blood, cool with Shengdi and Duhuo. Retained wind-damp in the foot lesser yang, Gancao eases the wind-pathogen. Ascending adversely to the lung — then Dang Gui, Shudihuang, Huangqi (Astragalus), in order to nourish blood after dispelling wind. One, to relieve the dryness of the wind medicinals; the other, to make the hand getting blood able to grasp, and the foot getting blood able to walk.
