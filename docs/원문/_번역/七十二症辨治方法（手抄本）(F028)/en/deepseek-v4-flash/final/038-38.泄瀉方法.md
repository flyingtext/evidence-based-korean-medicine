## 38.泄瀉方法

### Source Text

泄瀉之名有許多，大都濕勝土多魔；闌門注下無清濁，風能濕勝脈微毛。
治法升提須利水，澀法急投無奈何；斷流湯飲先分理，治瀉丸丹亦極屙。
斷流湯內桂防風，二朮二苓澤瀉兮；厚朴陳皮甘草芍，中流砥柱建膚功。

夫濕多成泄以致陽氣下陷，此清濁倒置也。風能濕勝故用防風。燥能勝濕故用二朮。淡能利濕故用二苓。土病水乘故用白芍。其桂朴之流又所以溫經佐使也。

止瀉丸：
肉豆　粟殼　訶子　黃連　白附
食積瀉、腹痛甚而瀉，之後痛減，肺細是也。用香砂平胃散去枳殼加白朮、茯苓。

五苓散：
茯苓八分　白朮八分　豬苓八分　澤瀉八分　山藥八分　陳皮八分　蒼朮八分　炒砂仁八分　肉蔻去油八分　訶子去殼八分
此方治濕瀉水多。而腹不痛腹響雷鳴，脈細是也。

理中湯：治寒瀉症
人參一錢　乾薑一錢　甘草五分　藿香七分　良薑七分　生薑三片　白朮一錢　桂五分　陳皮七分　茯苓七分　烏梅一粒　杏二枚　燈心一節
寒極手足冷、脈沉細，加附子去良、薑、桂。腹痛加砂仁、厚朴、木香、去人參。嘔噦噁心加燈心、半夏去良、薑、桂。瀉不止加蒼朮、山藥。瀉多不止加豆蔻、訶子、附子，去良薑桂。虛汗加黃耆去藿香桂。飽悶加厚朴、砂仁，去良薑、人參、官桂。

### Literal Translation

The name "xie-xie" (diarrhea) has many; mostly dampness prevails over the earth, with many demons. At the lanyue (ileocecal) gate it discharges rapidly, no clear and turbid; wind can prevail over dampness and the pulse is faint and hair-fine.
The method of treatment: raising and lifting, must free water; the astringing method hastened to cast — no recourse. Duanliu Tang Yin (Cut-the-Flow Beverage) first separates and regulates; treating diarrhea with pills and elixirs also is the extreme of ailment (treatment).
Within Duanliu Tang (Cut-the-Flow Decoction) are Gui (Cinnamomum cassia) and Fangfeng (Ledebouriella); Erzhu (two Atractylodes) and Erling (two Poria) with Zexie (Alisma). Houpu, Chenpi, Gancao (Licorice) and Shao (Peony); a midstream pillar, it establishes the skin (superficial) merit.

Now dampness in excess forms diarrhea, causing yang qi to sink downward; this is clear and turbid placed upside down. Wind can prevail over dampness therefore use Fangfeng. Dryness can prevail over dampness therefore use the two-zhu (Cangzhu & Baizhu). Paleness can drain dampness therefore use the two-ling (Zhuling & Fuling). Earth ill, water overcomes it, therefore use Baishao. Its Gui and Houpu stream, moreover, are what warm the channels, acting as deputy envoys.

Zhixie Wan (Stop-Diarrhea Pill):
Roudou (Nutmeg), Suke (Poppy Capsule), Hezi (Chebula), Huanglian (Coptis), Baifu (Aconite/Aralia sp.?)
Food-stasis diarrhea, severe abdominal pain then diarrhea, after which pain diminishes — the lung being fine is it. Use Xiangsha Pingwei San (Aucklandia-Amomum Level-the-Stomach Powder) removing Zhiqiao, adding Baizhu and Fuling.

Wuling San (Five-Peels Powder):
Fuling eight fen, Baizhu eight fen, Zhuling eight fen, Zexie eight fen, Shanyao (Dioscorea) eight fen, Chenpi eight fen, Cangzhu eight fen, stir-fried Sharen eight fen, Roudou (Nutmeg, oil removed) eight fen, Hezi (Chebula, shell removed) eight fen.
This formula treats damp-diarrhea with much water, and the abdomen not painful but thundering like thunder, and the pulse fine — it is it.

Lizhong Tang (Regulate-the-Centre Decoction): treats cold-diarrhea symptoms.
Renshen one qian, Ganjiang one qian, Gancao five fen, Huoxiang seven fen, Liangjiang (Alpinia) seven fen, Shengjiang three slices, Baizhu one qian, Gui five fen, Chenpi seven fen, Fuling seven fen, Wumei one grain, Xing (Xingren/Apricot) two pieces, Dengxin one node.
Extreme cold, hands and feet cold, pulse deep and fine — add Fuzi, remove Liang-Jiang-Gui (Liangjiang, ganjiang, guiguan). Abdominal pain add Sharen, Houpu, Muxiang, remove Renshen. Vomiting-retching, nausea add Dengxin, Banxia, remove Liangjiao-ganjiang-guiguan. Diarrhea not stopping add Cangzhu, Shanyao. Diarrhea profuse and not stopping add Doukou, Hezi, Fuzi, remove Liangjiang-Guiguan. Deficient sweat add Huangqi, remove Huoxiang-Gui. Full and oppressed add Houpu, Sharen, remove Liangjiang, Renshen, Guan-Gui (official cinnamon).
