### Source Text

癲狂兩症有陰陽，癲屬重陰狂屬陽；怒氣衝冠脾火實，明目張膽亂胡言。
親疏不避赤身舞，狂症分明可主張；斷魂落魄失心志，癡呆自笑自悲傷。

### Literal Translation

Mania (dian) and frenzy (kuang), the two conditions, have yin and yang; dian (depressive mania) belongs to double yin, kuang (frenetic mania) belongs to yang. Anger rushing to the crown, spleen-fire replete; open-eyed and bold, speaking chaotic nonsense.
Not avoiding kinsfolk and strangers, naked body dancing — the frenzy condition may be made clear and asserted. Lost-soul and fallen-spirit, losing the heart's intent; foolish-stupid, laughing alone and grieving alone.
This is the dian condition, how could it be hard to recognize? Evil-specter mania, distinguish it — burn a stick of incense. Yuanzhi Huatan Tang (Polygala Transform-Phlegm Decoction) weigh and use; regulate nutrient and nourish the stomach, think carefully.
