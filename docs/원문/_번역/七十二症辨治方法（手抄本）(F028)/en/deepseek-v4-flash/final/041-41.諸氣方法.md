## 41.諸氣方法

### Source Text

經云夫氣體之充，與血周流配始終；內外感傷病疾作，公私抑鬱利名窮。
有餘是火燎原野，不及生寒方寸中；獨參自古為良法，不是虛寒莫浪從。

獨參湯
人參一兩

氣者萬物之所資始也，天非此氣不足以養萬物，人非此氣不足以生。故曰一息不運則機誠窮。一毫不續則宵懷判。是故病，以氣為首務也。人參味甘性溫，得天地中和之氣，以成形，故用以補中和之氣。使其一息尚存，則可以次第。而療諸疾矣。煩躁加童便者。虛而有火也。身寒加附子者，回其孤陽也。虛實之辨，不可不察。獨參但可以治虛耳。若實症危急當因時制宜也。

治氣虛用四君子湯
人參　白朮　茯苓　砂仁　陳皮　薑汁炒厚朴　當歸　甘草各等分　生薑一片　棗二枚
虛甚加黃耆。

### Literal Translation

The Classics say qi is the filling of the body, circulating with the blood, matched to the beginning and end. Interior and exterior contraction make the illness erupt; public and private depression and suppression, exhausted by profit and fame.
Excess (of qi) is fire burning the wild plain; insufficiency engenders cold within a square inch (the heart). Dushen (Alone-Ginseng) since antiquity has been the good method; if it is not deficient-cold, do not heedlessly follow it.

Dushen Tang (Alone-Ginseng Decoction)
Renshen (Ginseng) one liang

Qi is what all things take to begin. Heaven, without this qi, is not sufficient to nourish the ten-thousand things; man, without this qi, is not sufficient to be born. Therefore it is said: one breath not transported, then the mechanism becomes truly exhausted; one hair not continued, then evening and dawn part company. Therefore in illness, qi is the foremost concern. Renshen tastes sweet, nature warm, obtaining the central-and-harmonious qi of heaven and earth to take shape, therefore use it to supplement the central harmonious qi. Making its one breath still remain, then one can proceed by degrees and heal the various illnesses. In vexation and agitation add urine of boy: deficiency with fire. Body cold add Fuzi: to return its solitary yang. The distinction of deficiency and repletion one cannot but examine. Dushen can only treat deficiency. If it be a replete condition, critical and urgent, one should suit the season and adapt the treatment.

To treat qi deficiency use Sijunzi Tang (Four-Gentleman Decoction)
Renshen (Ginseng), Baizhu, Fuling, Sharen, Chenpi, ginger-juice-stir-fried Houpu, Dang Gui, Gancao (Licorice) each equal parts, Shengjiang one slice, Zao (Jujube) two pieces.
Deficiency severe add Huangqi.
