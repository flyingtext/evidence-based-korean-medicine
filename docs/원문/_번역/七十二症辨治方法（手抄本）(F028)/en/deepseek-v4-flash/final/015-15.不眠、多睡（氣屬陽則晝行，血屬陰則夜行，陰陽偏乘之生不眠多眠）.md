## 15.不眠、多睡（氣屬陽則晝行，血屬陰則夜行，陰陽偏乘之生不眠多眠）

### Source Text

衛氣晝行陽似寤，陽盛陰虛主不眠；衛氣夜行陰似寤，陰盛陽衰目恣瞑。
所云不眠夜不寐，所云多眠晝亦眠；傷寒得此為反候，棗仁生地把來煎。

### Literal Translation

The defensive (wei) qi travels the yang by day, like waking; yang excessive, yin deficient, it governs sleeplessness. The defensive qi travels the yin by night, like sleeping; yin excessive, yang failing, the eyes freely close in sleep.
What is called not-sleeping is not sleeping at night; what is called much-sleeping is also sleeping by day. When cold damage brings this it is a reversed sign; Zaozi (Suanzaoren, Sour Jujube Seed) with Shengdi (Rehmannia) bring and decoct them.

### Translator's Notes

- 衛氣=wei (defensive/guarding) qi.
- 棗仁=Suanzaoren (Ziziphi Spinosae Semen); 生地=Shengdihuang (fresh Rehmannia).
