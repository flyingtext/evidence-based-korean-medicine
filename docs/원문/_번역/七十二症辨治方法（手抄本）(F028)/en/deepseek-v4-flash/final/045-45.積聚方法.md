## 45.積聚方法

### Source Text

積內陰血主有形，五臟傳染不容情；心曰伏梁肝肥氣，脾云痞氣息蕡金。
腎乃奔豚各部踞，古來五積有聲名；聚為陽氣巳無形，六腑衍和觸景成。
雖然業積無根蒂，東臨腫痛又西臨；復有痞塊總名色，症瘕痃癖要分明。
症者微者不移動，腹中堅硬痛堪憐；瘕者假也能移動，忽聚忽散實難憑。
痃在劑隅和彈指，時常作痛吐難伸；癖居脅肋形髣髴，雖多荊棘暫時形。
總是陰陽不和致，痰飲食積死血真；七氣湯中加減用，潰堅飲子再搜尋。

七氣湯
七氣湯中用益智，三菱莪朮藿香寄；青陳皮桂桔梗加，香附甘草請先嘗。

潰堅湯
潰堅湯內枳砂仁，半夏陳皮厚朴尋；香附當歸木香朮，山楂用盡積皆平。
左脅有塊加川芎，右脅加青皮。肉食成塊加薑汁炒黃連。粉面食積加神麯。血塊加桃仁、紅花桂，去半夏山楂。痰塊加枳實瓜簍海石，去山楂。胞脹加蘿蔔子檳榔，去朮。壯健人加山楂。瘦人加人參少許。

### Literal Translation

Accumulation (ji) is internally yin-blood and governs the formed-body; the five viscera transmit contagiously, not permitting feeling. In the heart it is called Fuliang (tumbling piglet rising toward the heart, 伏梁); in the liver, Feiqi (肥氣, fat-qi); in the spleen it is called Piqi (痞氣, glomus-qi) with metal (lung) suffocating gold.
In the kidney it is Bentun (奔跑/奔豚, running piglet), each dwelling in its parts; since antiquity the five accumulations have a famed name. Mass (ju) is yang qi, being formless (巳/無形); the six bowels over-flowing-healing, touch the scene and thereby form.
Though accumulated, it has no root and stem; to the east it comes, swelling pain, and to the west again. There are further glomus-blocks, the general name hue; zheng, jia, xuan and pi (症瘕痃癖) one must distinguish clearly.
Zheng is tiny and does not move; in the abdomen hard and solid, pain pitiable. Jia is false (假), and can move; at one moment gathering, another dispersing, truly hard to rely upon.
Xuan is at the corner-side like the snap of a finger; frequently it produces pain, hard to stretch out when vomiting. Pi lodges at the flanks and ribs, its form vaguely; though with many thorns-in-twenty, it is a transient form.
Always it is that yin and yang are not harmonious giving rise to it; phlegm, food-stasis, congealed blood are truly its reality. Qipai Tang (Seven-Qi Decoction) with additions and subtractions use; Kuijian Yin (Erode-the-Solid Beverage) then search out again.

Qiqitang (Seven-Qi Decoction)
In the Seven-Qi Decoction use Yizhi (Alpinia oxyphylla); Sanling (Triangular rhizome), Ezhu (Zedoary) and Huoxiang (Pogostemon) lodged within. Qingpi and Chenpi with Gui (Cinnamon) and Jiegeng (Platycodon) added; Xiangfu (Cyperus) and Gancao (Licorice), please taste first.

Kuijian Tang (Erode-the-Solid Decoction)
Inside Kuijian Tang are Zhi (Zhike/Bitter Orange) and Sharen; Banxia (Pinellia), Chenpi and Houpu sought. Xiangfu (Cyperus), Dang Gui, Muxiang (Aucklandia) and Zhu (Atractylodes); when Shanzha (Hawthorn) is used up, all accumulations level flat.
Left flank has a mass, add Chuanxiong; right flank add Qingpi. Meat-food forming a mass, add ginger-juice-stir-fried Huanglian. Powdered-flour food-stasis add Shenqu (Medicated Leaven). Blood mass add Taoren (Peach Kernel), Honghua (Safflower) and Gui, remove Banxia and Shanzha. Phlegm mass add Zhishi (Immature Bitter Orange), Gualou (Trichosanthes) and Haishi, remove Shanzha. Cellular distension add Luobozhi (Radish Seed) and Binglang (Areca), remove Zhu (Baizhu). Stout and healthy persons add Shanzha. Thin persons add a little Renshen.
