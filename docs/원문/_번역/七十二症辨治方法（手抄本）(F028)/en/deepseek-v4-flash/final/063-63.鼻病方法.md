## 63.鼻病方法

### Source Text

鼻為肺竅要清虛，難受埃塵半點居；外感風邪流清涕，聲重氣息壅唏噓。
鼻洲各別流濁涕，膽經邪熱腦中驅；腦滿時常流漿水，酒色過度腦門虛。
控腦砂兮腦亦痛，有蟲食腦痛難除；鼻外酒齇濕熱致，鼻中瘜贅肺邪除。
譬之雨霽之由地，突生芝草理堪思；治法清金兼降火，通竅之湯所必需。
通竅湯頭辛夷君，川芎白芷葛防風；蒼朮細辛生甘草，升麻羌活杏麻黃。

### Literal Translation

The nose is the orifice of the lung; it must be clear and empty, hard to endure half a speck of dust or dirt lodging within. Exterior contraction of wind-pathogen, flows clear snivel; the voice heavy, the breath qi congested, sighing.
The nose-streams (nasal discharge), each differing, flows turbid snivel; the gallbladder channel's heat-pathogen is driven from within the brain. The brain full, frequently flowing rice-wash water; with wine and sex excess, the brain gate is deficient.
Kongnaosha (控制腦砂/empty-brain-sand) is moreover with the brain also pain; having worms eating the brain, pain hard to remove. Outside, the nose's wine-red nose (rhinophyma) is from damp-heat; in the nose, polyp excess, the lung-pathogen is to be removed.
It is like the rain-cleared arising from the earth; suddenly sprouting ganoderma and grass, the principle is worth pondering. The method of treatment: clear metal (lung) together with descend fire; the unblock-the-orifice decoction is what is necessary.
Tongqiao Tang (Unblock-the-Orifice Decoction) head with Xinyi (Magnolia Flower) as sovereign; Chuanxiong, Baizhi, Ge (Pueraria/Ge gen) and Fangfeng (Ledebouriella). Cangzhu, Xixin and Sheng-Gancao (raw Licorice); Shengma (Cimicifuga), Qianghuo, Xing (Xingren/Apricot) and Mahuang (Ephedra).
