## 64.口舌方法

### Source Text

脾應中央戊己土，口舌同居通臟腑；口乾脾熱肝膽酸，肺辣心鹹心熱苦。
舌吐不收為陽強，舌縮難言令子母；口舌糜爛忽生瘡，咽喉涎漫苦難當。
重舌木舌舌本強，加減先鋒桔梗湯；己上赴宴湯堪用，金針刺疼造乾坤。
赴宴湯中用三黃，百煎梔子天麥門；乾薑細辛硼砂末，一杯揮去息燎源。

### Literal Translation

The spleen corresponds to the center, wu-ji earth; the mouth and tongue dwell together, unblocking the viscera and bowels. Mouth dry is spleen-heat; liver and gallbladder (disorder) is sour; lung-pungent, heart-salty, heart-heat is bitter.
Tongue protruding, not taken back, is yang-hardening (yang qiang); tongue shrinking, hard to speak — it makes the (mother-child) life in peril. Mouth and tongue ulcerated, suddenly engender sores; the throat, drool-spreading, bitter is hard to bear.
Double-tongue and wood-tongue, the tongue root stiff; with additions and subtractions, the vanguard is Jiegeng Tang (Platycodon Decoction). The above-the-stroke the Fuyan Tang (Attend-the-Feast Decoction) may be used; with the golden needle, poke and treat, creating the cosmos.
Fuyan Tang (Attend-the-Feast Decoction) uses the three-yellow (Huanglian/Huangqin/Huangbai); Baijian (百煎/百部?) with Zhizi (Gardenia), Tian-Maimen (Tianmendong-Ophiopogon). Ganjiang (Dried Ginger), Xixin (Asarum) and Pengsha (Borax) powder; one cup flung away, it quells the burning source.
