## 47.補益方法

### Source Text

勞傷虛損補為君，萬法千方治不同；說破五行生克理，實虛血氣要知兮。
氣虛不外四君子，血虛四物所當宗；六味地黃和小火，天王補心各一通。

### Literal Translation

In fatigue-injury and consumptive loss, supplementation is made lord; the myriad methods and thousand formulas treat them not the same. Speak out plainly the principle of the five agents generating and overcoming; the replete and deficient of blood and qi, one must know.
Qi deficiency does not step outside Four-Gentleman (Sijunzi); blood deficiency, Four-Substances (Siwu) is what should be followed. Liuwei Dihuang (Six-Flavor Rehmannia) harmonizes small fire; Tianwang Buxin (Heavenly-King Nourish-the-Heart) each one unblocks.
