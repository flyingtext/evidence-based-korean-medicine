## 5.惡風、背惡風

### Source Text

傷風惡風寒惡寒，腠理不密致邪干；惡風悉屬陽和氣，不比陰陽有惡寒。
又有傷寒背惡風，背為陽會腹為陰；令背惡寒陽不足，羌活沖和所必宗。

### Literal Translation

Wind damage fears wind, cold damage fears cold; when the interstices (couli) are not dense the pathogen gains entry. Aversion to wind belongs wholly to yang and harmonious qi, not comparable to yin-yang giving aversion to cold.
There is also cold damage with aversion to wind of the back; the back is the meeting of yang, the abdomen is yin. Making the back fear cold, yang is insufficient; Qianghu Chonghe is the one that must be followed.

### Translator's Notes

- 腠理=couli (interstices between skin and muscles).
- 背為陽會=the back is the gathering of yang; 腹為陰=abdomen is yin.
