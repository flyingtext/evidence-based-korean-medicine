## 34.喘急方法

### Source Text

上氣喘急最難醫，呼吸參差勢可悲；肺實熱邪雍促甚，肺虛寒思怯之微。
手足溫兮生一線，四肢厥冷夢無疑；定喘湯頭斟酌用，云泥虎鼠正幾希。
定清湯苓蘇子瓜，陳皮白果款冬花；甘草麻黃桑白杏，沉香半夏古來誇。

### Literal Translation

Above-rising qi, panting and rapid (chuanji), is hardest to treat; breathing uneven and irregular, the state is pitiable. Lung replete with heat-pathogen, it swells and hastens; lung deficient with cold, it is timid in its faintness.
Hands and feet warm — therein a thread of life; the four limbs cold, in dreams no doubt. Dingchuan Tang (Settle-the-Panting Decoction) head, weigh and use; cloud, mud, tiger, rat — truly a hair's breadth apart (life and death).
Dingchuan Tang (Settle-the-Panting) with Fuling (Poria) and Suzhi (Perilla seed) and Gua (Gualou/Fruitful Trichosanthes); Chenpi (Tangerine), Baiguo (Ginkgo) and Kuandonghua (Tussilago). Gancao (Licorice), Mahuang (Ephedra), Sangbai (Mulberry Root Bark) and Xing (Xingren/Apricot Kernel); Chenxiang (Aquilaria) and Banxia (Pinellia), praised from antiquity.

### Translator's Notes

- 定喘湯=Dingchuan Tang (Settle-the-Panting Decoction).
- 云泥虎鼠=cloud-vs-mud, tiger-vs-mouse: extremes of auspicious/ominous fate.
