## 50.汗門方法

### Source Text

經云汗乃心之液，多因心腎兩不交；自汗如雨怕亡陽，陽虛陰盛發厥疾。
盜汗睡醒汗即止，陰虛陽腠發熱極；此皆陰陽所偏勝，更有三般不治詳。
汗膩如雨津液竭，汗凝珠豆血氣傷；汗出如流冰且淡，建中兩得最為良。

建中兩得湯
建中兩得白朮芍，黃耆甘草浮麥謁；麻黃根一把來煎，治汗即止真妙訣。

### Literal Translation

The Classics say sweat is the fluid of the heart; much is from the heart and kidney not communicating. Spontaneous sweating like rain, fearing yang-collapse; yang deficient, yin abundant, it erupts into reversal illness.
Thief-sweat (daohan, night sweat), after sleeping it ceases at waking; yin deficient, yang interstices, fever extremely. These all are yin-yang each being partial in victory; there are moreover three kinds not-to-be-treated, speak in detail.
Sweat greasy like rain, fluids exhausted; sweat congealing like pearls and beans, blood and qi damaged. Sweat issuing like flowing, icy and insipid — Jianzhong Liangde (Build-the-Centre Two-Attainments) is best.

Jianzhong Liangde Tang (Build-the-Centre Two-Attainments Decoction)
Jianzhong Liangde (Build-the-Centre Two-Attainments) with Baizhu and Shao (Peony); Huangqi (Astragalus), Gancao (Licorice) and Fumai (Wheat) assembled. Mahuanggeng (Ephedra Root) one handful to decoct; treating sweat, it stops — truly a wonderful secret.

- 自汗/盜汗=spontaneous vs night (thief) sweating.
- 建中兩得湯=Jianzhong Liangde Tang.
