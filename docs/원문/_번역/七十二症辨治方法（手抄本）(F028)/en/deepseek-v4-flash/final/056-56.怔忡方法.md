## 56.怔忡方法

### Source Text

怔忡驚悸與恐怖，病同名異脈皆虛；蓋人所主在乎心，心之所養在乎血。
心血一虧眾疾作，如魚無睡不相安；惕惕如人將捕己，安神補血總司之。
安神補血總治虛，四物湯加遠志滋；朱砂作君石蒲使，怔忡等症一齊司。

### Literal Translation

Palpitation-with-apprehension (zhengchong), fright-throbbing (jingji) and terror (kongbu): the illnesses are the same in name but different, the pulses all deficient. For what a man presides over lies in the heart; what the heart nourishes lies in the blood.
Once the heart blood is depleted, the many illnesses arise; like a fish without sleep, no peace. Apprehensive, as if a man were about to seize oneself; Anshan Buxue (Settle-Spirit Nourish-Blood) oversees them all.
Anshan Buxue (Settle-Spirit Nourish-Blood), governing all deficiency; Siwu Tang (Four-Substances Decoction) adding Yuanzhi (Polygala) to nourish. Zhusha (Cinnabar) made sovereign, Shipu (Acorus) as messenger; palpitation-with-apprehension and such — all governed at once.
