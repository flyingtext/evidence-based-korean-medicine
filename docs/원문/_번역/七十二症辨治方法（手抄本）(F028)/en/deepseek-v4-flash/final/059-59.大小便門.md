## 59.大小便門

### Source Text

小便因何致不通，心經邪熱客紛紛；金能生水上源主，遇克難為氣化君。
譬如文房貯水器，上竅通兮下不通；又有小便難與短，難是艱難短欠長。
更有轉胞窿閉症，公差朵婦往如常；治法升提休下滲，此外通關加減良。
大便何事不通行，肺部原來欠化生；以致中洲失傳送，糟粕食積火相爭。
可憐腎臟津液竭，陽明愈結廁無登；老人血少多秘結，潤腸湯飲減和增。
若有大小便都閉，陰陽兩結熱風生；倒換蜣螂通臟腑，無清水火下如崩。

### Literal Translation

Why does the urine come to be obstructed? The heart channel's heat-pathogen lodges in profusion. Metal (lung) can engender water; in the upper source it governs. When it meets with overcoming, it is hard for the qi to be the transforming master.
It is like a water-storing vessel of the study chamber; the upper orifice open, then the lower is blocked. There is also difficult urination and short urination; difficulty (nan) is difficulty, shortness (duan) is lacking in length.
There is again the turned-bladder (zhuanbao) and closed-block (longbi) conditions; the public errand (pregnancy) of the wedded woman goes as usual. The method of treatment: raise and lift, do not let seep down; beyond this Tongguan (Open-the-Pass) with additions and subtractions, good.
Why does the stool not pass? The lung department, from the origin, lacks transformation and generation; thereby the middle province loses its conveyance and delivery; the chaff (residue) and food-stasis with fire contend with each other.
Pitiable, the kidney's body fluids exhausted; the yangming increasingly bound (constipated), with no stool-mound to mount. The aged have little blood and much bound accumulation; Runchang Tang Yin (Moisten-the-Intestine Beverage), subtract and increase.
If both the large and small urination be closed, yin and yang both bound, heat-wind engenders; Daohuan Qilang (Reverse-the-Change Dung-Beetle) unblocks the viscera and bowels, no clear water and fire, down gushes like a landslide.

Dang Gui, Shengdi (Rehmannia), Shudi (prepared Rehmannia), Huoma (Huomaren/Hemp Seed), Tao (Taoren/Peach Kernel), Xing (Xingren/Apricot Kernel), Ling (Fuling/Poria), Ke (Zhike/Bitter Orange), Pu (Houpu), Huang (Dahuang/Rhubarb), Cao (Gancao/Licorice).

Runchang Wan treats difficult stool; with Xiao (Mirabilitum) and Huang (Dahuang) and raw honey, how cold its nature! Shengdi, Dang Gui, Binglang and Zhi (Zhike).
