## 48.虛勞方法

### Source Text

虛勞俗語即怯勞，多因酒色太煎熬；手足心熱痰作嗽，遺精汗雨咯血多。
渾身且發蒸蒸熱，真陰消爍落皮毛；勞瘵有蟲能變化，薰陶傳染襲沉屙。
名色難分五六七，細微軟弱脈難逃；治怯滋陰須降火，肉脫龍丹怎奈何。
滋陰降火柏知全，兩地川芎歸麥門；甘草陳皮白芍朮，一杯渾下息燎原。

### Literal Translation

Consumptive fatigue (xulao), commonly called timid-fatigue, much is from over-induction of wine and sex, too burnt. Palms and soles hot, phlegm making cough; spermatorrhea, sweat-rain and hemoptysis in plenty.
The whole body moreover issues steaming fever; the true yin is consumed and scorched, falling off the skin and hair. Consumptive transmission (laozhai) has worms able to transform; nourishing by fumigation and transmitting contagion, it rides the deep chronic ailment.
Of the name and colors, hard to distinguish the five, six or seven; fine, tiny, soft and weak, the pulse is hard to escape. Treating timidity, one must nourish yin and descend fire; when the flesh falls off, how does the dragon elixir help?
Ziyin Jianghuo (Nourish-Yin and Descend-Fire) with Bai (Huangbai/Phellodendron) and Zhi (Zhimu/Anemarrhena) all complete; two-dehuang/Rehmannia (Shengdi-Shudi) with Chuanxiong, Dang Gui and Maimen (Ophiopogon). Gancao (Licorice), Chenpi, Baishao and Zhu (Atractylodes); one cup poured down, it quells the burning plain.
