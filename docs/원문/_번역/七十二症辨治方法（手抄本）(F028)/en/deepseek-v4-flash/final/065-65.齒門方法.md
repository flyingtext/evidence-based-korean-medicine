## 65.齒門方法

### Source Text

齒乃腎表骨之餘，上下牙痛腎熱居；上片痛兮胃虛熱，下斷大腸風熱虛。
胃病好寒惡飲熱，大腸飲熱怕寒虞；開口呷風疼痛者，陽明風氣可驅除。
牙齒搖動不堅固，胃元虛弱有瘕疵；齒縫流濃並出血，齦宣骨露待何如。
骨槽風變成流注，陽明濕熱莫躊躇；清胃湯丸加減用，達尊散子定無拘。
清胃湯兮川黃連，當歸生地朴硝鹽；丹皮地骨須同用，升麻佐使柴前胡。

達尊散
石膏一兩　白芷五錢　細辛五錢　甘草一錢。

### Literal Translation

The teeth are the surplus of bone, the surface (table) of the kidney; upper and lower tooth pain dwell in kidney-heat. When the upper part aches, stomach-deficiency-heat; the lower segment, large-intestine wind-heat deficiency.
Stomach disease likes cold, dislikes drinking heat; the large intestine drinks heat and fears cold, in fear. If opening the mouth and drawing in wind and pain, the yangming wind-qi can be expelled and removed.
The teeth shaking and not firm and strong, the stomach-qi weak and deficient with flaw; the tooth interstices streaming pus and also bleeding, the gum decline with bone exposed — what awaits?
The bone-trough wind (alveolar abscess) turns into flowing-abscess; the yangming damp-heat, do not hesitate; Qingwei Tang Wan (Clear-the-Stomach Decoction-Pill) with additions and subtractions use; the Dazun Sanzi (Honor-the-Noble Powder) is certainly without constraint.
Qingwei Tang (Clear-the-Stomach Decoction) uses Sichuan Huanglian (Coptis); Dang Gui, Shengdi (Rehmannia), Puxiao (Mirabilitum) and salt. Danpi (Moutan Bark) and Digupi (Lycium Root Bark) must be used together; Shengma (Cimicifuga) as deputy-envoy, with Chaihu (Bupleurum) and Qianhu (Peucedanum).

Dazun San (Honor-the-Noble Powder)
Shigao (Gypsum) one liang, Bai Zhi five qian, Xixin (Asarum) five qian, Gancao (Licorice) one qian.
