## 51.眩暈方法

### Source Text

眩暈無故忽昏迷，耳聾目閉可驚疑；如立舟車欲倒仆，肝風上壅下虛時。
太過不及皆有此，病中產後各般醫；兼痰理調為良法，清暈湯子古今奇。

清暈湯
清暈湯活半南星，甘草茯苓芷細辛；芎黃芩陳防風朮，任教眩暈保安寧。

### Literal Translation

Dizziness (xuan) without cause suddenly comas; deaf-ear and closed-eye can alarm and doubt. Like standing on a boat or cart, about to topple and fall; liver wind surging above with depletion below at the time.
Excess and deficiency both have this; in illness and after childbirth, treat each accordingly. Combined for phlegm, regulate as the good method; Qingyun Tangzi (Clear-Dizziness Beverage) is from ancient to modern a marvel.

Qingyun Tang (Clear-Dizziness Decoction)
Qingyun Tang with Huo (Qianghuo/Notopterygium), Ban (Pinellia) and Nanxing (Arisaema); Gancao (Licorice), Fuling, Zhi (Baizhi) and Xixin (Asarum). Chuanxiong, Huangqin, Chenpi and Fangfeng and Zhu (Atractylodes); allowing even dizziness, it keeps peace and tranquility.
