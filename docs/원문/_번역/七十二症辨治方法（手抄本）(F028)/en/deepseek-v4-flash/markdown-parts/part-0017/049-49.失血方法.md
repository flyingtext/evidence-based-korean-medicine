## 49.失血方法

### Source Text

失血由來熱作殃，久新虛實要參詳；有升無降上部見，陽陷陰中大小腸。
陰虛火動來見血，治法補陰更抑陽；氣降血歸無所忘，犀角地黃湯不涼。
犀角地黃湯最靈，丹皮赤芍黃連芩；當歸酒製還宜用，何愁失血不歸經。

歸脾湯（加味：柴胡梔子）
人參　白朮　黃耆　茯苓　龍眼肉　杏仁各二錢　遠志　木香　茯神　當歸。

### Literal Translation

Loss of blood from the beginning is heat making the calamity; new and old, deficiency and repletion one must examine in detail. With ascending and no descending, it appears in the upper part; yang sinking into the yin, the large and small intestine.
Yin deficiency with fire stirring comes to see blood; the method of treatment: supplement yin and moreover repress yang. Qi descending and blood returning, nothing is forgotten; Xijiao Dihuang Tang (Rhinoceros-Rehmannia Decoction) is not cool.
Xijiao Dihuang Tang is most efficacious; Danpi (Moutan Bark), Chishao (Red Peony), Huanglian (Coptis) and Huangqin (Scutellaria). Dang Gui, wine-processed, moreover should be used; what worry that lost blood does not return to its channels.

Guipi Tang (Spleen-Restoring Decoction) (with additions: Chaihu, Zhizi)
Renshen (Ginseng), Baizhu, Huangqi (Astragalus), Fuling, Longyanrou (Longan Flesh), Xingren each two qian, Yuanzhi (Polygala), Muxiang (Aucklandia), Fushen (Poria with hostwood), Dang Gui.

### Translator's Notes

- 犀角地黃湯=Xijiao Dihuang Tang; 歸脾湯=Guipi Tang.
- 陽陷陰中=yang sinking into the yin (interior).
