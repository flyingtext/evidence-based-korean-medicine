## 14.譫語、擲聲

### Source Text

譫語即是胡亂語，擲聲擲沖不正聲；邪氣甚兮則譫語，精氣奔兮則擲聲。
輕則睡中呢喃句，重則醒後亦妖言；獨言向壁一二語，熱邪輕重已分明。

### Literal Translation

Delirious speech (zhanyu) is confused, chaotic speech; throwing of voice, thrown and rushing, is an improper sound. When the pathogen qi is strong then there is delirium; when the essence qi rushes then there is throwing of voice.
Mild, then muttering within sleep; severe, then even after waking there are eerie words. Speaking to oneself two or three words toward the wall — the lightness or severity of heat-pathogen is already distinguished.
