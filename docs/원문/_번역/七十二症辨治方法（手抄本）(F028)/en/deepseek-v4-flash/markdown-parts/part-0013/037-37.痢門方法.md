## 37.痢門方法

### Source Text

請問如何能成痢，多因濕熱氣血滯；臟腑積聚生冷多，脾胃不和飲食致。
勿兮赤白與雜形，俱作濕熱標本治；身涼脈細腹多痛，身熱脈大口噤忌。
裏急後重覓香連，木香導氣須當備；四時發作有來因，不獨秋間有如是。

加味香連丸
木香　黃連　厚朴　甘草　蒼朮。

木香導氣芍檳榔，硝黃厚朴茯苓存；黃連歸尾薑茶引，諸般痢症總相當。

### Literal Translation

I ask how a dysentery (li) is able to come to be: much is from damp-heat stagnating qi and blood; the viscera and bowels accumulating cold-raw things in plenty, the spleen and stomach not harmonious from eating and drinking.
Do not (heed) red or white or mixed forms; treat them all as damp-heat, treating branch and root. Body cool with fine pulse, the abdomen much pain; body hot with big pulse, mouth-lock (trismus) is taboo.
Tenesmus (inner urgency, later heaviness) seek Xianglian (Aucklandia-Coptis); Muxiang Daoqi (Aucklandia Leading-Qi) must be prepared. Erupting in the four seasons has its causes; it is not only in autumn that such is so.

Jiawei Xianglian Wan (Supplemented Aucklandia-Coptis Pill)
Muxiang (Aucklandia), Huanglian (Coptis), Houpu, Gancao (Licorice), Cangzhu (Atractylodes).

Muxiang Daoqi (Aucklandia Leading-Qi) with Shao (Baishao/Peony) and Binglang (Areca); Xiaohuang (Mirabilitum & Rhubarb), Houpu and Fuling (Poria) retained. Huanglian (Coptis), Guiwei (Dang Gui tail) with ginger-tea as guide; the various dysentery conditions all correspond.
