## 44.水腫方法

### Source Text

水腫之病出乎脾，世人不識亂猜疑；腎水脾土兩要固，脾土一虧水無圍。
氾濫並流四肢去，使人浮腫黃光輝；風腫氣腫並血腫，陰水陽水亦須知。
風腫走注皮麻木，氣腫隨氣稍長之；血腫之症如何識，皮間赤縷血痕見。
陽水自熱陰水冷，陰水無如胃苓奇；所慮路歧平且坦，或加氣喘命幾希。

木香流氣散
木香流氣青陳皮，香附麥門大腹皮；木瓜通桂參苓朮，半朴葛檳果藿沉。

實脾飲
實脾澤瀉豬茯苓，蒼朴白朮枳砂仁；木香香附陳皮等，大腹皮加最有靈。

八正散
八正散腫車前子，瞿麥扁蓄山梔子；滑石木通炙大黃，陽水身熱堪授與。

胃苓湯
胃苓湯即兩湯名，甘草陳皮朴朮平；加上白朮和肉桂，豬苓澤瀉豬茯苓。

### Literal Translation

The disease of water-swelling comes from the spleen; the world does not recognize it and guesses wildly. Kidney water and spleen earth both must be steadied; once the spleen earth is depleted, water has no enclosure.
Overflowing and spreading, it flows toward the four limbs, making a person swollen with a yellow sheen. Wind-swelling, qi-swelling, and blood-swelling; yin-water and yang-water also one must know.
Wind-swelling moves and shoots, the skin numb; qi-swelling follows the qi and increases a little with it. How does one recognize the condition of blood-swelling? In the skin, red filaments, traces of blood are seen.
Yang-water naturally hot, yin-water cold; for yin-water none is as marvelous as Weiling (Stomach & Poria). What is feared is that, the road's forks being level and even, or with the addition of qi-panting, the life hangs by a thread.

Muxiang Liuqi San (Aucklandia Flow-Qi Powder)
Muxiang Liuqi (Aucklandia Flow-Qi) with Qing (Qingpi) and Chenpi; Xiangfu (Cyperus), Maimen (Ophiopogon) and Dafupi (Areca Peel). Mugua (Chaenomeles), Mutong (Akebia), Gui (Cinnamon), Renshen (Ginseng), Fuling and Zhu (Atractylodes); Ban (Pinellia), Houpu, Ge (Kudzu/Pueraria), Bing (Binglang/Areca), Guo (Caoguo/Cardamom), Huo (Huoxiang/Pogostemon) and Chen (Chenxiang/Aquilaria).

Shi Pi Yin (Enliven-the-Spleen Beverage)
Shipí (Enliven-the-Spleen) with Zexie (Alisma), Zhuling and Fuling; Cangzhu (Atractylodes), Houpu, Baizhu and Zhi (Zhike) and Sharen. Muxiang (Aucklandia), Xiangfu (Cyperus) and Chenpi together; add Dafupi (Areca Peel), most efficacious.

Bazheng San (Eight-Corrections Powder)
Bazheng (Eight-Corrections) for swelling uses Cheqianzi (Plantago Seed); Qumai (Dianthus), Bianxu (Polygonum aviculare) and Shanzhizi (Gardenia). Huashi (Talc), Mutong (Akebia), prepared Dahuang (Rhubarb); yang-water with body heat may be given it.

Weiling Tang (Stomach-and-Five-Peels Decoction)
Weiling Tang is the name of two decoctions (Pingwei + Wuling); Gancao (Licorice), Chenpi, Houpu and Zhu (Atractylodes) leveling. Add on Baizhu together with Rougui (Cinnamomum cassia); Zhuling, Zexie and Zhu (Zhuling)-Fuling.
