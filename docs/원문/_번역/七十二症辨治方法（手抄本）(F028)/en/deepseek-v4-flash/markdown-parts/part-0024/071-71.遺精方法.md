## 71.遺精方法

### Source Text

五臟六腑皆有精，腎為都會配生成；聽命於心行伎倆，不及太過自虧盈。
青春強制於持節，如瓶充滿夢遺精；所求不遂夢中泄，此是神交最損人。
下亢虛敗精流滑，行存坐臥若壺傾；酒色過度精液竭，餘精遺漏藕絲形。
陰虛火動未交泄，清心補腎理堪憑；再兼脾胃分清濁，蓮子清心妙如神。
清心蓮子治遺精，人參遠志及茯神，兩地蓮須石棗肉，麥門酸棗炒其仁。

### Literal Translation

The five viscera and six bowels all have essence; the kidney is the grand meeting, matched with its generation. It takes orders from the heart and works its arts; in deficiency or excess it depletes and loses of itself.
A youth suppresses and constrains himself, holding to restraint; like a bottle filled full, in dreams he leaks spermatorrhea. What is sought not attained, in dreams it lets forth; this is spiritual intercourse, the most harmful to a man.
The lower source (kidney) depleted and defeated, the essence flows and slips; in walk, stay, sit and lie, like a pot poured out. Wine and sex excess, the essence and fluids dried; the leftover essence leaks in the form of tangled lotus-threads.
Yin deficient, fire stirring, letting forth before intercourse; clear the heart and supplement the kidney, the principle is worth relying on. Again combine the spleen and stomach to separate the clear and turbid; Lianzi Qingxin (Lotus-Seed Clear-the-Heart) is marvelous as a spirit.
Qingxin Lianzi (Clear-the-Heart Lotus-Seed) treats spermatorrhea; Renshen (Ginseng), Yuanzhi (Polygala) and Fushen (Poria-with-hostwood). Two-di (Shengdi-Shudi = raw and prepared Rehmannia), Lianxu (Lotus Stamen) and Shizaorou (Jujube flesh), Maimen (Ophiopogon) and stir-fried Suanzooren (Sour Jujube Seed).
