## 70.不寐方法

### Source Text

不寐根由有數端，痰涎流注膽經攢；空思妄想多如此，寡婦尼僧不一觀。
若遇老年難寤寐，既衰血氣滯迴圈；白頭四物四君子，高枕無憂治壯年。

### Literal Translation

The root and ground of sleeplessness (bumei) has several ends: phlegm-fluid flowing and settling, gathering in the gallbladder. Empty musings and wild fancies are mostly thus; widows and nuns, not a single view.
If one meets the aged, hard to sleep-wake (insomnia); already declining, blood and qi stagnate in their circulation. White-headed use Siwu (Four-Substances) and Sijunzi (Four-Gentleman); with high pillow, no worries, it treats those in their prime.

### Translator's Notes

- 白頭四物四君子=mixing Siwu Tang (Four Substances) with Sijunzi Tang (Four Gentlemen) for the aged.
- 寡婦尼僧=widows and nuns (those with suppressed/ungratified desire).
