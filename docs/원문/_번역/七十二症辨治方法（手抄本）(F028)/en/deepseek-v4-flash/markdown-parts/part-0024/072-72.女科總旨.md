## 72.女科總旨

### Source Text

男女生來造化同，五行一理陰陽分；男主補氣水為主，女人補血以為君。
性有胎前與產後，崩漏淫帶不同徒；大都女人多病疾，多由經水不調通。
先期而至榮虛熱，衍恨而行血冷云；脈息陰陽左右反，四物湯飲古來宗。

調經方法
陰血流行灌一身，上為乳汁下為經；通塞莫逃寒與熱，臨行尤怕怒生嗔。
久病傷胎皆阻上。感冒稽遲號觸經；腰腹疼痛肢體困，血越上竅錯行經。
一月兩度沖經致，三月一致曰居經；七月無經損是的，取經湯子急家尋。

崩漏胎門
取經湯子孩兒茶，乳香沒藥制斑蟊，大黃巴豆蔥連白，調經受孕妙無加。
經虛陽搏經曰崩，女人五十理無生；經斷數年忽又至，既衰反候不為亨。
壯年經水行不止，名之曰漏不為崩；好把芎歸湯料理，莫用兜留止塞行。
又有帶下赤白濁，虛實補瀉休穿鑿；十婦九帶尺中微，解帶湯丸所當學。

解帶湯
解帶四物蒼白朮，玄胡香附陳皮茯；甘草丹皮和貝母，地榆乾薑黃柏簇。

### Literal Translation

Men and women are born with the same work of creation; the five agents are one principle, and yin and yang are divided. In men, supplementing qi and water is primary; in women, nourishing blood is made the sovereign.
Nature has pre-pregnancy (before birth) and post-partum; flooding and spotting, leucorrhea and discharge go by different paths. For the most part women have many illnesses, most from the menses not being regulated and free.
Coming ahead of its period is flourishing (qi) with deficiency-heat; coming delayed, stagnated and cold in the blood, says the text. The pulse-beratings, yin and yang reversed between left and right; Siwu Tang Yin (Four-Substances Beverage) is what antiquity has followed.

Regulate-the-Menses Method
The yin blood flows and irrigates the whole body; above it becomes milk, below it becomes the menses. Through and blocked, it cannot escape cold and heat; at the approach (of the period) it is especially afraid of anger, producing ire.
Prolonged illness injuring the fetus all block above; catching cold, delayed, is named "struck-the-menses". Loin and abdomen ache, the limbs are confined; blood surging above the orifices, misrunning the menses.
Twice a month is "struck-the-menses" occasion; once in three months it is called "dwelling-menses" (jujing). Seven months without menses, injury is certainly; quickly seek the Qu-jing Tangzi (Take-the-Menses Beverage) at home.

Flooding-Spotting and Fetal Gate
Qujing Tangzi (Take-the-Menses Beverage) with Haijicha (Tea); Ruxiang (Frankincense), Moyao (Myrrh) and prepared Banmao (Mylabris/Cantharis blister-beetle). Dahuang (Rhubarb), Badou (Croton), Cong (Onion stalk) joined with white (allium root?) — regulating the menses and receiving pregnancy, wondrous without equal.
The menses (flooding) from yang-stroke, the Classics call flooding (beng); a woman at fifty has the principle of no longer bearing (bearing child). If the menses, cut off for several years, suddenly comes again, this is an already-declined reversed omen, not auspicious.
In the prime of life, the menses flowing without ceasing is named spotting (lou), not flooding (beng). Just take the Xiong-Gui Tang (Chuanxiong-Dang Gui Decoction) to manage it; do not use retention and astringing to stop and block its course.
There is further the girdle-discharge with red and white turbidity; the deficiency and repletion, supplement and drain — do not force it artificially. Ten women nine have girdle (belly-band) discharge, the foot (chi) position faint; the Jiedai Tang Wan (Resolve-the-Girdle-Discharge Decoction-Pill) is what one should learn.

Jiedai Tang (Resolve-the-Girdle-Discharge Decoction)
Jiedai (Resolve-the-Girdle-Discharge) with Siwu and Cangzhu-Baizhu; Yuanhu (Corydalis), Xiangfu (Cyperus), Chenpi and Fu (Fuling/Poria). Gancao (Licorice), Danpi (Moutan Bark) and Beimu (Fritillaria); Diyu (Burnet), Ganjiang and Huangbai gathered.
