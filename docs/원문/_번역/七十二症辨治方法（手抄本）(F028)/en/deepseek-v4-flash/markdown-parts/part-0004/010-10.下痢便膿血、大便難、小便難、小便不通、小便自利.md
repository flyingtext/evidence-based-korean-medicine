## 10.下痢便膿血、大便難、小便難、小便不通、小便自利

### Source Text

下痢陰陽要認真，水來乘土病根因；傷寒誤下多如此，自便自利一般情。
三陽下痢身渾熱，手足溫和屬太陰；少陰則體水如冰，若是心涼救厥陰。
傷寒又有便膿血，腸胃陰寒邪所客；清濁不分帶經濕，不比尋常之失血。
其餘大小便諸症，可就本門尋法則；傷寒傳變似風雷，不象雜症易通寒。

### Literal Translation

With dysenteric diarrhea one must be careful to distinguish yin and yang; water coming to overcome earth is the root cause of the illness. Cold damage and wrongful purgation are mostly like this; free defecation and spontaneous free urination are of the same kind.
Diarrhea of the three yang has the whole body feverish; hands and feet warm belongs to the Great Yin (Taiyin). The Lesser Yin (Shaoyin) makes the body like ice; if the heart is cool (cold) it may rescue the Terminal Yin (Jueyin).
Cold damage again has purulent bloody stools; the intestines and stomach, cold yin, is what the pathogen lodges in. Clear and turbid not separated, carrying channel dampness; not comparable to common loss of blood.
As to the rest, the disorders of the large and small urination, in this very gate one may seek the rules. Cold damage transmission and change is like wind and thunder, not resembling miscellaneous disorders that readily pass through cold.
