## 11.傷寒霍亂、嘔吐、乾嘔、噦晼、咳逆、喘急、短氣之逆

### Source Text

短氣即氣難布息，邪氣業集胸中窒；氣逆粗華氣上衝，天下之事未可必。
霍亂嘔吐並乾嘔，噦逆咳嗽及喘急，此章重出反三隅，活法治之無一失。

### Literal Translation

Shortness of breath is that qi finds it hard to spread and disperse; the pathogen qi already gathers and fills the chest, causing oppression. Qi going against is coarseness and its rising upward; in the affairs of the world nothing is certain.
Huo luan (sudden turmoil), vomiting and dry retching, hiccup and reversal, cough and rapid breathing — this chapter repeats them, drawing the three-cornered inference by analogy, with the living method treat them and not one is lost.

- 霍亂=huoluan (sudden turmoil, violent vomiting/diarrhea).
- 反三隅=deduce by analogy (from the Analects' 舉一反三).
