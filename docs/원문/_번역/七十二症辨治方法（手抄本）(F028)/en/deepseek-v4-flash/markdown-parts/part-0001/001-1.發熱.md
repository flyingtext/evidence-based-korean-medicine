## 1.發熱

### Source Text

翕翕發熱為表熱，風寒甫客皮膚烈；此為表熱裏無恙，無汗解發功方捷。
蒸蒸發熱為裏熱，陽邪下陷入陰轍；譬之蒸籠氣達外，有渴下之休跋涉。
又有表邪未罷症，欲傳入裏興妖孽；裏來作熱難容受，半表半裏發為熱。
治法不外和為貴，傷寒重大不論舌；無汗麻黃汗桂枝，太陽分野別須知。

### Literal Translation

Flushed-skin fever indicates exterior heat; wind-cold having just lodged in, the skin cracks and tears. This is exterior heat with the interior unharmed; when there is no sweat, inducing sweating and resolving brings quick results.
Steaming fever indicates interior heat; yang pathogen sinking downward falls into the yin track. It is like a steaming basket, its vapor reaching outward; if thirsty, purge it and rest from the labor.
There is also a condition of exterior pathogen not yet terminated, about to transmit into the interior and stir up mischief. The interior coming to produce heat is hard to bear; half exterior and half interior it gives rise to fever.
In treatment method nothing surpasses harmonization as the key; for grave cold damage one does not examine the tongue. Without sweat use Mahuang (Ephedra); with sweat use Guizhi (Ramulus Cinnamomi), at the Sun (Taiyang) division and its distinctions one must know.

### Translator's Notes

- 翕翕=flushed-skin, gentle fever; 蒸蒸=steaming (high sustained fever).
- 太陽=Sun/Taiyang channel (Bladder/small intestine system).
