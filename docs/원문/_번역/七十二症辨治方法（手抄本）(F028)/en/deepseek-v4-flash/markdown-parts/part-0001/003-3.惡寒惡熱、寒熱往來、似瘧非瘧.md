## 3.惡寒惡熱、寒熱往來、似瘧非瘧

### Source Text

寒熱如何似往來，邪氣分爭膽受災；惡寒以卜邪居表，惡熱先知邪內栽。
若是邪居半裏表，所以寒熱有往來；日夜發作無定數，傳過別經即不來。
又有似瘧非瘧症，有時作止亦安排；通用柴胡湯和解，誤投下吐亦衰哉。

### Literal Translation

How do chills and fever come and go alternately? The pathogenic qi divides and struggles, and the gallbladder suffers injury. With chills one infers that the pathogen dwells on the exterior; with fever one first knows the pathogen is planted within.
If the pathogen dwells at half interior and half exterior, therefore chills and fever come and go. Erupting day and night with no fixed count, once it passes to another channel it no longer comes.
There is also a condition like malaria yet not malaria, with occasional onset and cessation also arranged. Generally use Chaihu Tang (Bupleurum Decoction) to harmonize and resolve; wrongly purging with emesis or down-draining would be lamentable.
