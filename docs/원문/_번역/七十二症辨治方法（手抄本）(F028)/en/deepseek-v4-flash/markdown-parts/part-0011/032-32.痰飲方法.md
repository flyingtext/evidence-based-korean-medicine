## 32.痰飲方法

### Source Text

痰之為病不勝言，稠則為痰稀飲詳；理氣治標雖捷法，扶脾治本更為良。
諸症來歷如何認，各色臨流浮與沉；二陳見景須加減，滾痰飲用細思量。
二陳甘草與陳皮，半夏茯苓白去皮；痰嗽陣中為上將，添兵減灶有回春。

夫痰者，人心之賊也。生於脾胃，不能自動，隨氣稍長，今之治痰，必先理氣，此說固是正治標之論無加健去濕，治本之萬全也；是方半夏辛溫能燥濕，茯苓甘淡能滲濕，濕去痰無再生，陳皮溫能利氣，甘草辛平能益脾，二陳不惟治痰，能使大便潤小便長。

### Literal Translation

The illnesses made by phlegm cannot all be spoken; the thick is phlegm, the thin is rheum (yin) in detail. Regulating qi to treat the branch is a quick method, though; fortifying the spleen to treat the root is even better.
How does one recognize the coming of the all conditions? Each hue, at the edge of the stream, is floating or sinking. Erchen (Two-Matured) formulas, when signs appear, must be increased or decreased; Gunan Tan (Roll-the-Phlegm Beverage) used, think carefully.
Erchen (Two-Matured) with Gancao (Licorice) and Chenpi (Tangerine Peel); Banxia (Pinellia) and Fuling (Poria) with skin removed. In the phlegm-cough battle formation it is the supreme general; adding soldiers and reducing cook-fires, it brings back spring.

Now phlegm is the thief of the human heart. Born in the spleen and stomach, unable to move of itself, it follows qi and little by little grows. Now in treating phlegm, one must first regulate qi; this statement being surely the correct treatment-of-branch theory. Without strengthening and removing dampness it is not the complete treatment-of-root. In this formula Banxia, pungent and warm, can dry dampness; Fuling, sweet and pale, can percolate dampness; dampness removed, phlegm has no rebirth. Chenpi, warm, can free the qi; Gancao, pungent and level, can benefit the spleen. Erchen not only treats phlegm, it can make the stool moist, the urine long.
