## 42.痞滿方法

### Source Text

痞與否同不通泰，不是五積之痞塊；輸輪失常或誤下，治濕法同消痞訣。
實痞便閉虛痞利，內雖滿悶外無恙；木香化滯先嘗試，黃連消痞後加臨。
木香化滯當歸稍，柴枳陳皮大腹包；白蔻紅花薑半夏，中洲茅塞任君投。

黃連消痞丸
黃連土炒　黃芩七錢　枳實五錢　半夏　陳皮四錢　茯苓一兩　豬苓三錢　澤瀉　鬱金　乾薑。

養胃方法
香附　砂仁　木香　枳實　炒各七錢　白朮　茯苓　半夏薑汁炒　陳皮各一錢　白蔻　藿香　厚朴薑汁炒　炙草二錢　生薑二片　棗一枚
食後服。瘦人心下痞滿，加黃連去半夏，血虛加當歸、白芍，去半夏。痰膈加瓜簍仁、貝母、桔梗、竹瀝、薑汁少許、白朮、半夏。脾泄加炒蒼朮、白芍，去半夏。

### Literal Translation

Glomus-pi same as the character no (否) — not free and unimpeded; not the glomus-block (píkuài) of the five accumulations. The transport-wheel loses its constancy, or is wrongly purged (down): treating dampness in the same way, like the resolve-the-glomus secret.
Replete glomus: hard stool; deficient glomus: free stool. Inside, though full and oppressed, outside there is no harm. Muxiang Huazhi (Aucklandia Dissolve-Stagnation) first try it; Huanglian Xiaoji (Coptis Resolve-Glomus) then add on thereafter.
Muxiang Huazhi (Aucklandia Dissolve-Stagnation) with Dang Gui tip; Chai (Chaihu/Bupleurum), Zhi (Zhike/Bitter Orange), Chenpi and Dafupi (Areca Peel) wrapping. Baidoukou (White Nutmeg/Cardamom), Honghua (Safflower), Jiang (shengjiang) and Banxia (Pinellia); the thatch-choked middle province, let you cast it.

Huanglian Xiaoji Wan (Coptis Resolve-Glomus Pill)
Huanglian (Coptis, earth-stir-fried), Huangqin (Scutellaria) seven qian, Zhishi (Immature Bitter Orange) five qian, Banxia, Chenpi four qian, Fuling one liang, Zhuling three qian, Zexie (Alisma), Yujin (Curcuma), Ganjiang (Dried Ginger).

Nourish-the-Stomach Method
Xiangfu (Cyperus), Sharen, Muxiang (Aucklandia), Zhishi (Immature Bitter Orange), stir-fried each seven qian, Baizhu, Fuling, Banxia, ginger-juice-stir-fried, Chenpi each one qian, Baidoukou, Huoxiang (Pogostemon), Houpu, ginger-juice-stir-fried, prepared Gancao (Licorice) two qian, Shengjiang two slices, Zao (Jujube) one piece.
Take after meals. Thin persons with glomus-fullness below the heart, add Huanglian, remove Banxia. Blood deficiency add Dang Gui, Baishao, remove Banxia. Phlegm-hindered diaphragm add Gualou ren (Trichosanthes seed), Beimu (Fritillaria), Jiegeng (Platycodon), Zhuli (Bamboo juice), a little ginger juice, Baizhu, Banxia. Spleen-diarrhea add stir-fried Cangzhu, Baishao, remove Banxia.
