## 66.咽喉方法

### Source Text

咽以咽物通胃脘，喉以候氣通五臟；齊驅並峙肺門間，清濁悠兮熱與寒。
臟熱腫熱多痛癢，肺寒縮硬又多痰；若夫猝然覺腫痛，水漿難入語言難。
會厭兩旁都腫痛，俗名喉痹發雙哦；會厭一邊腫突出，此為喉閉發單哦。
此為相火腫送致，金針刺發起沉屙。

### Literal Translation

The pharynx swallows food and unblocks the stomach duct; the larynx awaits the qi and unblocks the five viscera. Side by side they stand between the lung gates; clear and turbid, prolonged, heat and cold.
Viscera-heat swelling-heat, much pain and itch; lung-cold shrinking-hard and much phlegm. If suddenly one perceives swelling and pain, water and gruel hard to enter, speech hard.
At both sides of the epiglottis all is swollen and painful — commonly named throat-block (houbi), issuing a double 橋 (shout); at one side of the epiglottis swollen and protruding — this is throat-closure (houbi), issuing a single 橋 (shout).
This is minister-fire engendering and sending it; with the golden needle, prick it and rouse the deep chronic ailment.
