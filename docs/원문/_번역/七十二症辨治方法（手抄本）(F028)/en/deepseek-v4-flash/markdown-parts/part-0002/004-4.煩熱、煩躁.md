## 4.煩熱、煩躁

### Source Text

感冒風寒或失表，變為煩熱心中擾；又有煩熱不同科，陽虛陰實所當曉。
陽實錯亂心煩熱，陰虛憤怒腎煩躁；煩則熱輕燥熱甚，煩躁先後分燥煩。

### Literal Translation

Catching wind-cold or failing to resolve the exterior, it turns into vexing fever disturbing the heart. There is also vexing fever of a different category — yang deficiency with yin excess, which one should know.
Yang excess causes chaotic vexatious heat of the heart; yin deficiency causes anger and vexed agitation of the kidney. Vexation (fan) is mild heat, agitation (zao) is severe heat; vexation and agitation, which comes first and which is dryness versus vexation, distinguish them.

### Translator's Notes

- 煩=fán (vexation, mild restlessness); 躁=zào (agitation, severe restlessness).
- The last couplet distinguishes 煩熱 (mild) from 燥熱 (severe).
