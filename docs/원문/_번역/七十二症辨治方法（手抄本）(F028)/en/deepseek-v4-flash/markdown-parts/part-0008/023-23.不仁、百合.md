## 23.不仁、百合

### Source Text

不仁有呼為屍厥，痛癢針灸渾不知；肢體皮肉何頑梗，經絡痼冷恐難醫。
百合傷寒為破症，百骸受病藥難聘；無復經絡節次傳，汗吐下法不足信。
似寒無寒不怕寒，似熱無熱不怕熱；微食不食臥不臥，陽春一曲和皆難。

### Literal Translation

Numbness-without-feeling (buren) is also called corpse-reversal (shijue); pain, itching, acupuncture and moxibustion it entirely does not know. The body's limbs, skin and flesh, how is it stiff and stolid? The channels and collaterals chronically cold, I fear difficult to heal.
Hegemony-lily cold damage (baihe) is a broken condition; the hundred bones affected by illness, medicine is hard to engage. No longer is there transmission of the channels in stepwise order; sweating, vomiting and purging methods are not to be relied upon.
Like cold yet no cold, not fearing cold; like heat yet no heat, not fearing heat. Slightly eating yet not eating, lying yet not lying — even a song of spring, all harmonious, is hard to achieve.

### Translator's Notes

- 不仁=bùrén (numbness/loss of sensation); 百合=百合 bǎihé (lily syndrome).
- 陽春一曲=one tune of warm spring (a beautiful/harmonious thing).
