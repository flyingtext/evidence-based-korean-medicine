## 22.拂鬱懊憹

### Source Text

拂鬱行止不如意，肢體積氣由火氣；懊憹喜怒不寬舒，散悶淘情求益智。

### Literal Translation

Depression and vexation (fuyu): movement and rest both not as desired; the limbs accumulating qi, arising from fire qi. Constraint and regret (aonao): joy and anger not eased; dissipate the gloom and soothe the heart, seek to augment wisdom.
