## 30.傷飲方法

### Source Text

濕熱有毒如酒無，注入脾胃疾常有；積痛昏迷嘔吐頻，飲食少進脾家咎。
治法汗之利小便，上下分清其濕妙；葛花解醒湯不移，眾人皆醉我獨醒。
葛花解醒參乾薑，茯苓澤瀉朮木香；青陳皮與砂神麯，豆蔻加來治酒傷。

夫葛花之寒，能解酒中之毒，茯苓澤瀉之淡，能利酒中之濕。砂仁豆蔻木香生皮之辛。能行酒食之滯。乾薑所以開胃止嘔，神麯所以消磨炙膩。而人參白朮之甘所以補益被傷之脾胃。

### Literal Translation

Damp-heat toxic like (the toxin of) wine and its like; injected into the spleen and stomach, illness frequently present. Accumulated pain, coma, vomiting at frequent intervals; eating and drinking in small amounts is the spleen family's blame.
The method of treatment: sweat it and free the urine, above and below divide and clarify its dampness — wonderful. Gehua Jiexing Tang (Kudzu-Flower Relieve-Inebriation Decoction) does not change; all people are drunk but I alone am sober.
Gehua Jiexing (Kudzu-Flower Relieving-inebriation) with Renshen (Ginseng) and Ganjiang (Dried Ginger); Fuling (Poria), Zexie (Alisma), Zhu (Atractylodes) and Muxiang (Aucklandia). Qing (Qingpi) and Chenpi with Sharen (Amomum) and Shenqu (Medicated Leaven); with Doukou (Nutmeg/Cardamom) add to treat wine-damage.

Now the cold of Gehua (Kudzu Flower) can resolve the toxin within wine; the paleness of Fuling and Zexie can drain the dampness within wine. The pungency of Sharen, Doukou, Muxiang and Shengpi moves the stagnation of wine and food. Ganjiang is that which opens the stomach and stops vomiting; Shenqu is that which digests and grinds down roasted and greasy things. And the sweetness of Renshen and Baizhu is that which supplements and benefits the wounded spleen and stomach.
