## 55.健忘方法

### Source Text

健忘轉盼即遺忘，盡力思來記不全；做事始終此失彼，言談首尾不知完。
心脾思慮勞過度，神不守舍治其源；心血不足痰遺竅，歸脾湯子可扶匡。
歸脾湯飲治健忘，參耆神朮歸麥門；遠志石蒲龍眼肉，有熱加些生地黃。

### Literal Translation

Forgetfulness (jianwang) — a turn of the eyes and it is already forgotten; with all one's strength of mind seeking, yet one cannot remember completely. In matters, from beginning to end, this is lost and that missed; in discourse, head and tail, one does not know to complete them.
The heart and spleen, from brooding care, labor to excess; the spirit not dwelling in its abode, treat its source. Heart blood not sufficient, phlegm leaves the orifice; Guipi Tangzi (Spleen-Restoring Beverage) may support and uphold.
Guipi Tang Yin (Spleen-Restoring Beverage) treats forgetfulness; Shen (Renshen/Ginseng), Qi (Huangqi/Astragalus), Shen (Fushen/Poria), Zhu (Baizhu) and Gui (Dang Gui) and Maimen (Ophiopogon). Yuanzhi (Polygala), Shipu (Acorus) and Longyanrou (Longan Flesh); if there be heat add some Shengdihuang (fresh Rehmannia).
