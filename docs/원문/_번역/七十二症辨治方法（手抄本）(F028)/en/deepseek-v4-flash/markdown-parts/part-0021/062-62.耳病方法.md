## 62.耳病方法

### Source Text

兩耳經云腎之竅，虛則鳴雷左右要；婦人多有左邊聾，以其忿怒動肝火。
男子右邊多重聽，斷之欲色動相火；若遇兩耳俱鳴聾，膏粱脾甘動胃火。
又有腫痛流膿水，此是腎家風濕熱；滋腎通耳湯嘗試，也有痰火虛血氣。
滋腎通氣四物君，黃柏知母妙無倫；蘆薈相須龍膽草，何愁兩耳有鳴聾。

### Literal Translation

The two ears, the Classics say, are the orifice of the kidney; in deficiency then they ring thunder, left and right, the important. Women have much deafness of the left side, for their anger and ire stir the liver fire.
Men, the right side much hearing-heavy (impaired), break it off — desire-color stirring the minister fire. If one meets both ears both ringing and deaf, rich-food and spleen-sweet (rich diet) stirs the stomach fire.
There is moreover swollen-pain, flowing pus-water; this is the kidney family's wind-damp-heat. Zishen Tong'er Tang (Nourish-the-Kidney Unblock-the-Ear Decoction) try it; there is also phlegm-fire, deficient blood-qi.
Zishen Tongqi (Nourish-the-Kidney Unblock-Qi) with Siwu (Four-Substances) as sovereign; Huangbai (Phellodendron) and Zhimu (Anemarrhena) are incomparably marvelous. Luhui (Aloe) combined necessarily with Longdancao (Gentian); what worry that the two ears have ringing and deafness?
