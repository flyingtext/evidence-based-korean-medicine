## 61.頭痛方法

### Source Text

元首諸陽之都會，輕清須伏本根源；小火煎熬能作痛，治明部位有春回。
心主腦門肝主骨，脾居腰角肺額前；腎位頂癲命門後，左邊屬血氣右邊。
血氣兩虛頭盡痛，風痰六氣或相間；更有隨乾真頭痛，四肢厥逆聽乎天。
補中益氣湯為主，臨期再把引經添；若有外邪須並去，溫涼寒熱任君沾。

補中益氣湯見上。

頭痛引經歌
太陽川芎陽明芷，少陽柴胡湯半裏；太陰蒼朮少陰辛，厥陰茱萸能直抵。

### Literal Translation

The head is the grand meeting of the various yang; light and clear, one must submit to the root-source. Small fire tormenting and scorching can make pain; treat the part distinctly, then there is the return of spring.
The heart governs the brain gate, the liver governs the bones; the spleen dwells at the loin-corner, the lung before the forehead; the kidney abides at the crown-sky, after the mingmen; the left side belongs to blood and qi, the right side.
Blood and qi both deficient, the whole head aches; wind-phlegm, the six vapors, or intermixed. There is moreover the "sui-gan" (marrow-consumed) true-headache; the four limbs cold and reversed, listen to heaven.
Buzhong Yiqi Tang (Supplement-the-Centre and Benefit-Qi Decoction) as the governing formula; the time come, then add the channel-guiding (yingjing) herbs. If there be external pathogen, one must remove it together; warm, cool, cold and hot, let you taste.

Buzhong Yiqi Tang seen above.

Channel-Guiding Headache Song
Sun (Taiyang) use Chuanxiong; Yangming use Zhi (Baizhi); Shaoyang use Chaihu Tang to half the interior; Taiyin use Cangzhu; Shaoyin use Xin (Xixin/Asarum); Jueyin use Zhuyu (Evodia/Wuzhuyu) able to reach straight.
