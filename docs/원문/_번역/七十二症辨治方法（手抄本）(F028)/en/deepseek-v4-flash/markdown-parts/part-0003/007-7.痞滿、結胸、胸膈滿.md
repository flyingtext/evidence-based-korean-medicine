## 7.痞滿、結胸、胸膈滿

### Source Text

無熱惡寒發於陰，反不招來痞滿形；按之不痛傷寒血，胸膈滿分降無升。
發熱惡寒發於陽，誤下開門損寇然；按之方痛結胸小，不按自痛大堪傷。
熱結胸兮煩且渴，寒結胸兮悶怕涼；水結胸中腫且汗，不輕誤下別相商。

### Literal Translation

Without fever but fearing cold, it issues from yin; instead it does not summon the formation of a glomus-pi (stuffing) fullness. Press it and it does not pain — cold damage of blood; chest and diaphragm fullness distinguish descending and no ascent.
Fever with aversion to cold issues from yang; wrongly purging opens the door and injures the thief. Press and then it pains: binding in the chest, small (結胸小); unoppressed it pains by itself: large, grievous to bear.
Heat binding in the chest is vexed and thirsty; cold binding in the chest is oppressed and fears coolness. Water binding in the chest is swollen and sweats; do not lightly and wrongly purge, consult otherwise apart.

### Translator's Notes

- 痞=fú-pi (glomus/stuffing); 結胸=jiéxiōng (chest binding).
- The verse plays on 結胸小/大 (small vs large chest binding).
- Unresolved: "結胸小" could be "結胸下", 結胸 by descent
