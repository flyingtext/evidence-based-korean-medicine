## 8、腹痛、腹滿、小腹滿

### Source Text

脾為中央戊己土，太陰疾病腹受苦；陽毒為邪手足溫，痛有停止言語紛。
滿無稍長如斯已，陰毒生端又不同；腹滿欲便無休息，滿兼積瘀可消分。

### Literal Translation

The spleen is the central center wu-ji earth; in diseases of the Great Yin (Taiyin) the abdomen bears suffering. Yang toxin invading as pathogen, the hands and feet are warm; pain has stoppages, and speech is profuse.
Fullness without the least growth — so it stops there; yin toxin giving rise is different again. Abdominal fullness desiring defecation without rest; fullness together with accumulated blood stasis can be dispersed and distinguished.
