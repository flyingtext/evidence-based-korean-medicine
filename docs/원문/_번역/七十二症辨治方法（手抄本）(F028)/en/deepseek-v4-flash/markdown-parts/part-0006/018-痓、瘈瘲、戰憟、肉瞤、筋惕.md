## 痓、瘈瘲、戰憟、肉瞤、筋惕

### Source Text

原夫痓症屬膀胱，頭項強直背反張；此是傷風感寒濕，宗筋牽引致拘攣。
目開無汗為剛痓，屬陰身熱葛根湯；目閉有汗為柔痓，屬陰肢冷桂枝湯。
瘈瘲之症熱生風，風邪主動起紛飛；瘈則縮急瘲伸緩，補血榮筋藥帶溫。
戰憟陰陽兩相鬥，乾坤搖動不聊生；邪氣交爭則為戰，憟是邪氣內爭沖。
肉瞤筋惕不常有，惕者動者瞤者跳；過亡津液血枯乾，筋肉失養滋為妙。

### Literal Translation

As to its origin, the spasm (zhi) condition belongs to the bladder; head, neck stiff and straight, the back arched backward. This is wind damage contracting damp-cold; the ancestral sinews pulling cause the contraction and cramping.
Eyes open and no sweat: hard spasm (gang zhi); belonging to yin, body hot — Gegen Tang (Kudzu Root Decoction). Eyes closed and sweating: soft spasm (rou zhi); belonging to yin, cold limbs — Guizhi Tang (Cinnamon Twig Decoction).
The condition of flaccid-spasm (chizhong) has heat engendering wind; wind pathogen being active gives rise to flying chaos. Chi (瘈) is to contract and tighten, zhong (瘲) to extend and loosen; supplement blood and nourish sinews, the medicine carries warmth.
Fighting and shivering (zhan-su), yin and yang fight each other; Heaven and Earth rock and sway without life. When the pathogen and proper qi contend, that is zhan (shivering in struggle); su (憟) is the pathogen qi fighting and rushing within.
Flesh twitching and sinew throbbing (rou shun jin ti) are not frequent occurrences; ti (惕) is moving, shun (瞤) is jumping. When fluids are passed and blood dries and withers, sinew and flesh lose nourishment; nourishing them is excellent.
