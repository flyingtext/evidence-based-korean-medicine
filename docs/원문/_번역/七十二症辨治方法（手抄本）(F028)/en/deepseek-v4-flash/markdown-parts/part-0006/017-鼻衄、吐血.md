## 鼻衄、吐血

### Source Text

鼻衄病根熱迫血，妄行於鼻體汗塞；經云奔血者無汗，治法奔汗即無血。
若是七孔俱流血，傷寒紅汗為準則；吐血熱盛不須疑，引血歸經真一得。

### Literal Translation

Nosebleed's root cause is heat pressing the blood; running wantonly in the nose, the body sweats blocked. The Classics say one who dashes out blood has no sweat; in the method of treatment, make sweat rush and there will be no bleeding.
If all seven orifices flow blood together, take the cold damage red-sweat as the standard. Hematemesis with intense heat, do not doubt; leading the blood back to its channels is truly one attainment.

### Translator's Notes

- 紅汗=red sweat (nose bleed as a way of resolving exterior heat).
- 引血歸經=sending the blood back to its channels.
