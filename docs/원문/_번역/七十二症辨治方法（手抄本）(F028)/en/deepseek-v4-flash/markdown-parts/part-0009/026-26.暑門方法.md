## 26.暑門方法

### Source Text

暑傷於氣脈應虛，傷暑傷寒脈各殊；外症與寒雖仿佛，身熱無痛不拘攣。
奔勞無烈無暍熱，避冷幽清中暑餘；人心包絡與胃應，邪從牙齒入心居。
煩喝飲水或吐瀉，香薷飲子可先驅；五苓散用兮邪正，六和湯去莫躊躇。

香薷飲
香薷飲子參陳皮，白芩厚朴朮黃耆；甘草木瓜白扁豆，清心驅暑此為奇。

五苓散
五苓湯內豬茯苓，白朮澤瀉桂心求；燮理陰陽清暑濕，精神恍惚定安然。

六合鷹爪藿香薷，砂參甘草朮苓居；扁豆木瓜杏厚朴，半夏驅暑任馳驅。

夫六和也六腑和者，脾胃六腑之總司。故凡六腑不和之症。先於脾胃而調之，此妙訣也。香能治胃竅，故用藿砂，辛能散脾胃氣，故用半杏。淡能利濕熱，故用茯苓。甘能調脾胃，故用扁朮。補脾胃弱則脾胃復而諸疾平。蓋脾一治，水精四布，五經並行，雖百骸九竅解太和也。況六腑乎。

### Literal Translation

Summer-heat injuring qi, the pulse should be weak; summer-heat injury and cold injury have different pulses. The external signs, though alike to cold, have body fever without pain and without contracture.
Running and laboring without blazing heat, no heatstroke; avoiding the cold in still seclusion, the remainder of summer-heat stroke. Man's pericardium and stomach correspond; the pathogen enters from the teeth into the heart's abode.
Vexed, drinking water or vomiting-diarrhea, Xiangru Yinzi (Elsholtzia Beverage) may be the van. Wuling San (Five-Peels Powder) using, distinguishes the pathogen from the proper; Liuhe Tang (Six-Harmony Decoction), go — do not hesitate.

Xiangru Yin (Elsholtzia Beverage)
Xiangru Yinzi (Elsholtzia) with Renshen (Ginseng) and Chenpi (Tangerine); Bai (Baizhi), Qin (Scutellaria), Houpu and Zhu (Atractylodes) and Huangqi (Astragalus). Gancao (Licorice), Mugua (Chaenomeles) and Baibiandou (White Hyacinth Bean); clear the heart and expel summer-heat, this is marvelous.

Wuling San (Five-Peels Powder)
Inside Wuling Tang (Five-Peels Decoction) are Zhuling (Polyporus) and Fuling (Poria); Baizhu (Atractylodes), Zexie (Alisma) and Guixin (Cinnamon Heart) sought. Regulating yin and yang, clearing summer-damp, spirit confused and dazed, it settles them at ease.

Liuhe (Six-Harmony) with Eagle-Claw (Yingzhao) Huoxiang (Pogostemon) and Xiangru (Elsholtzia); Shashen (Glehnia), Gancao (Licorice), Zhu (Atractylodes) and Fuling (Poria) abiding. Biandou (Hyacinth Bean), Mugua (Chaenomeles), Xingren (Apricot Kernel) and Houpu; Banxia (Pinellia) expelling summer-heat, letting it gallop freely.

Now liuhe (six-harmony) is the harmony of the six bowels; the spleen and stomach are the general superintendent of the six bowels. Therefore in all symptoms of disharmony of the six bowels, regulate first at the spleen and stomach; this is the wonderful secret. Fragrance can treat the stomach orifice, therefore use Huo (Pogostemon) and Sha (Sharen); pungency can disperse the spleen-stomach qi, therefore use Ban (Pinellia) and Xing (Apricot). Paleness can drain damp and heat, therefore use Fuling. Sweetness can harmonize the spleen and stomach, therefore use Bian (Hyacinth) and Zhu (Atractylodes). When deficient spleen-stomach is supplemented, the spleen-stomach is restored and all the diseases level flat. For once the spleen is treated, the water essential is distributed to the four directions and the five channels travel together; though the hundred-limbs and nine orifices all dissolve into great harmony. How much more the six bowels?
