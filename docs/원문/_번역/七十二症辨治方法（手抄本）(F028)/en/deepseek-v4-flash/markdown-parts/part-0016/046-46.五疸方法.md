## 46.五疸方法

### Source Text

疸症身黃不雅觀，黃汗黃疸許多般；酒穀女勞雖有五，利水和中止兩端。
多因湯飲小便閉，中焦溫熱吃緊關；茵陳湯可回天意，轉瞬紅顏奏喜歡。

茵陳湯
茵陳專治其五疸，黃芩連朮草龍膽；豬苓澤瀉梔青皮，驅逐濕熱真勇敢。

### Literal Translation

The jaundice condition, body yellow, is not pleasing to look at; yellow-sweat (huanghan) and jaundice (huangdan) and many others. Though of wine, grain, and women's-labor there be five, drain water and harmonize the middle are the two ends.
Much is from soups and drinks stopping the urination; the middle jiao damp-heat is the pressing pass. Yinchen Tang (Artemisia Capillaris Decoction) can return the mandate of heaven; in the turning of an eye the rosy cheek plays at joy.

Yinchen Tang (Artemisia Capillaris Decoction)
Yinchen (Artemisia capillaris) exclusively treats the five jaundices; Huangqin (Scutellaria), Lian (Huanglian/Coptis), Zhu (Atractylodes), Gancao (Licorice) and Longdan (Gentian). Zhuling, Zexie, Zhi (Zhizi/Gardenia) and Qingpi; expelling and driving the damp-heat, truly brave.
