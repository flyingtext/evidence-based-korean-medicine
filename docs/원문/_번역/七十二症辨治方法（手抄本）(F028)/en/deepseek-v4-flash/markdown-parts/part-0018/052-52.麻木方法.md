## 52.麻木方法

### Source Text

麻木原來有淺深，亦有手足及周身；麻是氣虛知痛癢，補中益氣可相親。
木乃血虛忘痛癢，二陳端的莫留停；多兼濕痰與死血，清風湯飲可搜尋。
清風飲朮金銀花，檳榔萆薢半木瓜，當歸芩芍乾薑、羌活，陳皮烏藥等堪誇。

### Literal Translation

Numbness (ma), from the origin, has shallow and deep; also there is of the hands and feet and the whole body. Ma (numbness with sensation) is qi deficiency, it knows pain and itching; Buzhong Yiqi (Supplement-the-Centre and Benefit-Qi) may be made close kin.
Mu (wood/numbness without sensation) is blood deficiency, forgetting pain and itching; Erchen (Two-Matured) truly should not linger and stop. Much of it combines damp-phlegm and dead blood; Qingfeng Tang Yin (Clear-the-Wind Beverage) may be searched out.
Qingfeng Yin (Clear-the-Wind Beverage) with Zhu (Baizhu/Atractylodes) and Jinyinhua (Honeysuckle); Binglang (Areca), Bixie, Ban (Banxia/Pinellia) and Mugua (Chaenomeles). Dang Gui, Qin (Huangqin/Scutellaria), Shao (Peony) and Ganjiang (Dried Ginger), Qianghuo (Notopterygium) and Chenpi and Wuyao (Lindera) — equally praiseworthy.

### Translator's Notes

- 麻=ma (tingling numbness, qi deficiency); 木=mu (dense numbness without sensation, blood deficiency).
- 清風湯=Qingfeng Tang.
