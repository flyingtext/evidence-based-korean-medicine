## 21.蛔蚘、狐惑

### Source Text

蛔蚘即入三屍蟲，腸胃虛寒求食因；甚至吃人之臟腑，四肢倦怠目常眩。
聞食即吐蛔蚘症，治法酸鹹藥性尋。狐惑猶豫所不決，刺入腸胃刺入心。

### Literal Translation

Roundworm (huihui) enters as the three-corpse worm; the intestines and stomach, cold and deficient, seek food as the cause. At the worst it eats a person's viscera and bowels; the four limbs weary and fatigued, the eyes frequently dizzy.
Hearing of food then vomiting is the roundworm condition; the method of treatment seeks the nature of sour and salty medicinals. Hesitating and uncertain — fox-glutton (huhuo); piercing the intestines and stomach, piercing the heart.

### Translator's Notes

- 三屍蟲=three-corpse worms.
- 狐惑=húhuò (fox-glutton / syphilitic-like genito-pharyngeal erosion) syndrome.
