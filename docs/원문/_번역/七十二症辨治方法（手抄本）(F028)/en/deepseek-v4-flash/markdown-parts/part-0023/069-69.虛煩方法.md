## 69.虛煩方法

### Source Text

虛煩俗名為暴燥，左寸虛數作心煩；左關虛數為膽冷，病後逢之血氣虧。
腎水枯竭亦煩躁，滋陰降火不須疑；心煩見景施良劑，膽冷無過溫膽湯。
溫膽湯兮治虛煩，二陳再把竹茹攢；人參遠志須加入，不比它經可結盔。

### Literal Translation

Vexing emptiness (xufan), commonly named sudden irritation (baozhao); the left cun position, deficient and rapid, makes vexation of the heart. The left guan position, deficient and rapid, is cold of the gallbladder; meeting it after illness, the blood and qi are depleted.
The kidney water dried and exhausted also gives vexation and agitation; nourish yin and descend fire — do not doubt. Heart-vexation, seeing the signs, administer the good formula; gallbladder-cold, nothing surpasses Wendan Tang (Warm-the-Gallbladder Decoction).
Wendan Tang (Warm-the-Gallbladder Decoction) treats vexing emptiness; Erchen (Two-Matured) then gathers Zhuru (Bamboo Shavings) into it. Renshen (Ginseng) and Yuanzhi (Polygala) must be added; do not be like other channels, where one may end or cap off.
