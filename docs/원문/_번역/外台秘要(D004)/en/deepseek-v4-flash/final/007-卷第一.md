## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

陰陽大論云：春氣溫和，夏氣暑熱，秋氣清涼，冬氣凜冽。此則四時正氣之序也。冬時嚴寒，萬類深藏君子周密，則不傷於寒，觸冒之者乃名傷寒耳，其傷於四時之氣，皆能為病。

### Literal Translation

The Great Discourse on Yin and Yang says: The spring qi is warm and mild, the summer qi is hot and sultry, the autumn qi is clear and cool, and the winter qi is biting and cold. This is the order of the upright qi of the four seasons. In winter the cold is severe, and the ten thousand things hide deeply; if the gentleman is careful and thorough, then he is not injured by cold. Only those who encounter and brave it are called cold damage (shanghan). Being injured by the qi of the four seasons can all cause disease.

## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

以至春變為溫病；至夏變為暑病，暑病者，熱極重於溫也。是以辛苦之人，春夏多溫熱病者，皆由冬時觸冒寒冷之所致，非時行之氣也。凡時行者，春時應暖而反大寒，夏時應熱而反大冷，秋時應涼而反大熱，冬時應寒而反大溫，此非其時而有其氣，是以一歲之中長幼之病多相似者，此則時行之氣也。

### Literal Translation

Thus reaching spring it changes into warm disease (wenbing); reaching summer it changes into summer disease (shubing). Summer disease is heat at its extreme, heavier than warm disease. Therefore, that hard-working people have many warm-heat diseases in spring and summer is all caused by encountering and braving cold in winter; it is not the qi of the seasonal epidemic (shixing). As for the seasonal epidemic, in spring it should be warm but instead is greatly cold, in summer it should be hot but instead is greatly cold, in autumn it should be cool but instead is greatly hot, and in winter it should be cold but instead is greatly warm. This is having the qi when it is not the season; therefore, in the course of a year, the diseases of the old and young are mostly similar; this is the qi of the seasonal epidemic.

五曰汗不出，嘔下血者九曰熱而痙者死。熱病痙者，腰反折螈　齒噤　也，熱病七八日，脈微小，病者便血，口中干汗而脈尚躁盛，此陰脈之極也，死其得汗而脈靜者生，熱病脈常盛躁而不得汗者，此陽脈之極也，死，脈盛躁得汗者生（甲乙太素同士弱氏曰灌發灌驟也漬也先不出汗，後則若灌溉之驟至而淋漓浸漬亡陽也）。

短劇論曰：古今相傳稱傷寒為難療之病，天行溫疫是毒病之氣，而論療者不別傷寒與天行溫疫為異氣耳，云傷寒是雅士之辭，云天行溫疫是田舍間號耳，不說病之異同也考之眾經，其千金論曰：人生天地之間，命有遭際，時有否泰吉凶悔吝苦樂安危喜怒愛憎存亡憂畏關心之慮，日有千條謀身之道，時生萬計，乃度一日，是故天無一歲不寒暑，人無一日不憂喜，故立極之德，而不能廢之，雖不能廢之，而能以道御之，其次有賢人善於攝生，能知樽節，與時推移，亦得保全，天地有斯瘴癘，還以天地所生之物以防備之。

### Literal Translation

Fifth: those whose sweat does not come out and who vomit and have blood in the stool (ninth:) those who are hot and have convulsions die. In heat disease with convulsions, the waist bends backward, the limbs twitch, and the teeth clench. In heat disease of seven or eight days, if the pulse is minute and small, the patient has blood in the stool, the mouth is dry, and after sweating the pulse is still agitated and surging, this is the extreme of the yin pulse, and one dies. Those who get sweat and whose pulse is calm live. In heat disease, if the pulse is constantly exuberant and agitated and one cannot get sweat, this is the extreme of the yang pulse, and one dies; if the pulse is exuberant and agitated and one gets sweat, one lives (same as Jiayi and Taisu. Shi Ruo says: 'Pouring eruption' means pouring is sudden and soaking; first the sweat does not come out, then it is like the sudden arrival of irrigation, dripping and soaking, and one loses yang).

The Duanju discourse says: Past and present have transmitted that cold damage is a disease hard to treat, and that epidemic warm pestilence is the qi of a poisonous disease. Yet those who discuss treatment do not distinguish cold damage and epidemic warm pestilence as different qi, saying that cold damage is the speech of refined scholars and that epidemic warm pestilence is only the name of the countryside, not speaking of the sameness and difference of the diseases. Examining the various classics, the Qianjin discourse says: People are born between Heaven and Earth; life has its encounters, and the times have obstruction and success, good and ill fortune, regret and vexation, suffering and joy, safety and danger, joy and anger, love and hate, survival and death, worry and fear. The cares that concern the heart number a thousand items daily, and the way of planning for the body gives rise to ten thousand plans at each time, and so one passes a day. Therefore Heaven has no year without cold and heat, and people have no day without worry and joy. Hence the virtue of establishing the utmost cannot abolish it; although one cannot abolish it, one can control it with the Way. Next, there are worthy people who are good at nourishing life, can know frugality, and move with the times, and also obtain preservation. Heaven and Earth have these miasmas and pestilences, and one returns to use the things that Heaven and Earth produce to guard against them.

## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

命曰知方，則病無所侵矣。

然即須救療，迄至於病愈湯食競進，折其毒熱，自然而瘥，必不可令病氣自在，恣意攻人，拱手待斃，斯為誤矣。今博采群經以為上下兩卷，廣設備擬好養生者可得詳焉。

### Literal Translation

Calling it knowing the formulas, then disease has nothing that invades. 

But one must immediately rescue and treat; until the disease heals, the decoctions and food compete to advance, and if one breaks its poisonous heat, it naturally recovers. One must not let the disease qi be at ease, recklessly attacking the person, folding the hands and waiting for death; this is a mistake. Now I broadly gather the various classics to make the upper and lower two volumes, broadly preparing and providing, so that those who love nourishing life can obtain it in detail.

## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

又夫傷寒病者，起自風寒，入於腠理，與精氣分爭營衛否隔，周行不通，病一日至二日氣在孔竅皮膚之部，填塞胸心，故頭痛胸中滿，當吐之則愈。五日以上氣沉結在臟，故腹脹身重，骨節煩疼，當下之則愈。

又夏亦不可雖云時覺視病已積日，其病源結成，非復發汗解肌所除，當診其脈，隨時情勢救解求免也，不可苟以次第為固，失其機要乃致禍矣，此傷寒次第病三日以內發汗者，謂當風解衣夜臥失覆，寒濕藏不消，轉動稍難，頭痛身溫，其脈實大者，便可吐下之，不可發汗也。

### Literal Translation

Moreover, the disease of cold damage arises from wind and cold, enters the interstices, and contends with the essence qi, so that the constructive and defensive are blocked and separated, and the circulation does not pass. From the first to the second day of the disease, the qi is in the region of the orifices and skin, filling the chest and heart, so there is headache and fullness in the chest; one should make one vomit, and it heals. Above five days, the qi sinks and binds in the viscera, so there is abdominal distension, heavy body, and vexing pain of the joints; one should purge it, and it heals.

Also, in summer one also cannot (do this). Although it is said that one sometimes feels and sees the disease already accumulated over days, its disease source has formed and is no longer what sweating and resolving the muscle can remove. One should diagnose the pulse and, following the situation of the time, rescue and resolve to seek escape; one must not carelessly take the order as fixed, for losing its crucial mechanism brings disaster. This order of cold damage: for those who sweat within three days of the disease, it is said that when one faces the wind and removes the clothes, lies at night and loses the covering, the cold dampness is stored and does not disperse, movement is somewhat difficult, and there is headache and warm body, and the pulse is excess and large, then one can vomit and purge, and cannot sweat.

## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

又凡人有少病，苦似不如平常，則須早道，若隱忍不療，冀𣈂自瘥，須臾之間，以成痼疾，小兒女子益以滋甚，若天行不和，當自戒勒，若有小不和，則須救療，尋其邪由，及在腠理，以時早療，鮮有不愈者，患人忍之數日，乃說邪氣入臟則難可制，此雖和緩，亦無能為也，癰疽疔腫尤為其急，此自養之至要也。

又凡作湯藥，不可避晨夜時日吉凶，覺病須臾，即宜便治，不等早晚，則易愈矣，服藥當如方法，若縱意違師，不須療之也。

又凡得時氣病，五六日而渴欲飲水，飲不能多不當與也。

### Literal Translation

Moreover, whenever a person has a slight illness and suffers as if not as usual, one should speak of it early. If one hides and endures without treating, hoping to heal by itself, in a moment it becomes a chronic ailment; in children and women it becomes even more severe. If the epidemic is not harmonious, one should admonish and restrain oneself; if there is a slight disharmony, one should rescue and treat, seeking its evil cause while it is still in the interstices, and treating early in time; there are few who do not recover. If the patient endures it for several days and then says it, the evil qi has entered the viscera and is then hard to control; even He and Huan could do nothing. Carbuncles, ulcers, furuncles, and swellings are especially urgent; this is the most essential of self-nourishment.

Moreover, in making decoctions and medicines, one cannot avoid the morning and night, the days and times, and their good and ill fortune. If one feels the disease, in a moment one should treat it conveniently; not waiting for early or late, then it is easy to heal. In taking medicine one should follow the method; if one indulges the will and disobeys the master, there is no need to treat it.

Moreover, whenever one gets an epidemic qi disease, and on the fifth or sixth day is thirsty and wants to drink water, if one cannot drink much, one should not give it.

## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

所以爾者腹中熱尚少，不能消之，便更與人作病矣，若至七八日大渴欲飲水者，猶當依證而與之，勿令極意也，能飲一斗者與五升，若飲而腹滿，小便澀，若喘若噦者，不可與之，飲而忽然汗出者已愈也，人得病能飲水者欲愈也（出第九卷中）。

經心錄論曰：傷寒病錯療禍及，如反覆手耳，故諺云，有病不治自得中醫者，論此疾也，其病有相類者，傷寒熱病，風溫濕病，陰毒陽毒，熱毒溫疫，天行節氣，死生不同，形候亦別，宜審詳也。（出第二卷中）

### Literal Translation

The reason is that the heat in the abdomen is still little and cannot digest it, and then it makes disease for the person again. If reaching the seventh or eighth day one is greatly thirsty and wants to drink water, one should still give it according to the signs, but not let one be extreme. For one who can drink a dou, give five sheng. If after drinking there is abdominal fullness, difficult urination, or if there is panting or retching, one cannot give it. If after drinking one suddenly sweats, it is already healed. A person who gets the disease and can drink water wants to heal (from the ninth volume).

The Jingxin Lu discourse says: In cold damage, mistreating brings disaster, like turning over the hand. Therefore the proverb says, if one has a disease and does not treat it, one obtains a middling physician by oneself; this discusses this disease. There are diseases that resemble each other: cold damage heat disease, wind-warmth damp disease, yin toxin and yang toxin, heat toxin and warm pestilence, and epidemic seasonal qi; life and death are not the same, and the form and signs also differ; one should examine carefully. (From the second volume.)
