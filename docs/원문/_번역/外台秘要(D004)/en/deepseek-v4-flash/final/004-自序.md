## 自序

### Source Text

蓋聞上古之世，方不如醫，中古之世，醫不如方甚矣。醫與方之並重也，世降而方愈凌雜，莫不各據一家言，彼此互相是非，間有二三驗方，亦惟是父師傳之子弟，絕不輕以示人。而其鐫行於世者，率皆依樣葫蘆，時或改頭換面以博名高則已矣。

### Literal Translation

I have heard that in the age of high antiquity, formulas were not equal to physicians; in the age of middle antiquity, physicians were not equal to formulas, greatly. How medicine and formulas are both valued! As the age declined, the formulas became ever more mixed and disorderly; each relied on the words of one school, mutually affirming and denying each other. Occasionally there were two or three proven formulas, but they were only transmitted by fathers and teachers to their sons and disciples, and never lightly shown to others. And those that were carved and circulated in the world were mostly copies of the same gourd, sometimes changing the head and face to win a high reputation, and that was all.

余獨取《外台秘要》付之剞劂者何？請得而備言之。蓋自神農氏深明藥性，著《本草經》三卷而未有方也，軒轅氏日與岐伯、雷公剖析病機，著《素問》、《靈樞經》各九卷而未有方也；商周之間如伊芳尹、如和、如緩、如跗，皆以醫名而未有方也；越人受長桑君之禁方，所傳於世者，《八十一難經》及治虢太子尸厥耳，而其為帶下醫、小兒醫、耳目痹醫，俱未悉其所以為方也。倉公受公乘陽慶之禁方，所可曉者莨菪子湯、苦參湯耳，其他火齊湯、下氣湯、陽劑剛石、陰劑柔石，亦未悉其所以為方也。

### Literal Translation

Why do I alone take the 《Waitai Miyao》 and commit it to carving? Let me obtain and speak of it fully. From the time Shennong deeply understood the nature of drugs and wrote the three volumes of the 《Bencao Jing》 (Classic of Materia Medica), there were no formulas; when Xuanyuan daily analyzed the mechanisms of disease with Qibo and Leigong and wrote the nine volumes each of the 《Suwen》 and the 《Lingshu Jing》, there were no formulas; in the Shang and Zhou periods, such as Yi Yin, He, Huan, and Fu, all were famous for medicine but had no formulas; the Yueren (Bian Que) received the forbidden formulas of Changsang Jun, and what was transmitted to the world was only the 《Eighty-one Difficult Classic》 and the treatment of the corpse-like reversal of the crown prince of Guo; and as for his being a physician of the belt-and-discharge, a physician of children, and a physician of ear, eye, and impediment, none knew how he made his formulas. Canggong received the forbidden formulas of Gongsun Yangqing; what could be understood were only the Langdangzi Tang (Henbane Seed Decoction) and the Kushen Tang (Sophora Decoction); the other Huoqi Tang (Fire-Order Decoction), Xiaqi Tang (Descending Qi Decoction), the yang formula of hard stone, and the yin formula of soft stone, none knew how he made his formulas.

若夫刳腸、湔胃無論，其方不傳，即令華元化方傳至今而亦難乎效其為方也。惟是仲景氏出有《卒病論》以治傷寒，著方一百一十三；有《金匱要略》以治雜病，著方一百一十二醫方，實開先焉，蓋鼻祖也。又得叔和王氏為之詮次，俾仲景之微旨益以彰明，叔和氏不更立方，即述仲景之方為方者也。

### Literal Translation

As for cutting open the intestines and washing the stomach, let us not speak of it; their formulas were not transmitted. Even if Hua Yuanhua's formulas had been transmitted to the present, it would still be hard to imitate his making of formulas. Only when Master Zhongjing appeared was there the 《Zubing Lun》 (Treatise on Sudden Disease) to treat cold damage, writing 113 formulas, and the 《Jingui Yaolue》 (Essential Prescriptions of the Golden Coffer) to treat miscellaneous diseases, writing 112 medical formulas; truly he opened the way first, being the ancestor. He also obtained Wang Shuhe to interpret and arrange them, making Zhongjing's subtle purport even more manifest. Shuhe did not create new formulas, but simply narrated Zhongjing's formulas to make formulas.

### Literal Translation

Huangfu Shi'an's 《Jiayi Jing》 (Systematic Classic of Acupuncture) especially valued needling and had no formulas; Chao Yuanfang's 《Bingyuan》 (Sources of Disease) had a source for every disease, and every source established a discourse, but had no formulas. Those who read them all regretted the lack of formulas. When it reached the Tang dynasty, there was the Perfected Sun (Sun Simiao), who first wrote thirty volumes of formulas and later added another thirty, cherishing his formulas and calling them the 《Qianjin》 (Thousand Golden Prescriptions); the medical formulas were comparatively clear and complete, being the great school.

### Literal Translation

If one truly devotes the mind in ordinary times, comprehends the intention of how the formulas were made, and weighs the formula for the symptoms at hand, then if it truly belongs to heat and should be cold, why not consult Hejian? If damp and should be dried, why not consult Dongyuan? If it can be sweated, can be vomited, and can be purged, why not consult Zihe? If yin and yang are disordered and the constructive and defensive are out of harmony, why not consult Danxi? Be broadly versed in the former formulas, do not cling to my own view, and aim at truly saving, not being ashamed before the former worthies' mind of saving the people and benefiting things; then taking the one book 《Waitai Miyao》 as the fish-trap and rabbit-snare of the physician's family, why is it not acceptable?
