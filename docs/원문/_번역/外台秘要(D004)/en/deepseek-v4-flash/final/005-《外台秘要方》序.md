## 《外台秘要方》序

### Source Text

<ins>唐銀青光祿大夫持節鄴郡諸軍事兼守刺史上柱國</ins>

<ins>清源縣開國伯王燾撰</ins>

昔者農皇之治天下也，嘗百藥，立九候，以正陰陽之變診，以救性命之昏扎，俾厥土宇用能康寧，廣矣哉。洎周之王，亦有塚卿，格於醫道，掌其政令，聚毒藥以供其事焉，歲終稽考而制其食，十全為上，失四下之。我國家率由茲典，動取厥中，置醫學，頒良方，亦所以極元氣之和也。

### Literal Translation

<ins>Written by Wang Tao, Silver Blue-Green Radiance Grand Master, Holder of the Staff, Commander of the Military Affairs of Ye Commandery, concurrently Acting Prefect, Pillar of the State, and Founding Earl of Qingyuan County</ins>

In ancient times, when the Farmer Emperor (Shennong) governed the world, he tasted the hundred drugs and established the nine indicators, to correct the diagnostic changes of yin and yang and to rescue the confusion of life and destiny, so that his territory could be peaceful and healthy; how vast! When it reached the kings of Zhou, there were also chief ministers who were versed in the way of medicine, managing its government orders, gathering poisonous drugs to supply its affairs, and at the end of the year examining and fixing their emoluments, with the tenfold complete as the highest and four losses as the lowest. Our state follows this canon, taking the middle in every action, establishing the medical school and promulgating good formulas, also to bring the harmony of the primordial qi to its utmost.

夫聖人之德，又何以加於此乎？故三代常道，百王不易，又所從來者遠矣。自雷、岐、倉、緩之作，彭、扁、華、張之起，迨茲厥後，仁賢間出，歲且數千，方逾萬卷，專車之不受，廣廈之不容，然而載祀綿遠，簡編虧替，所詳者雖廣，所略者或深，討簡則功倍力煩，取舍則論甘忌苦，永言筆削，未暇尸之。

余幼多疾病，長好醫術，遭逢有道，遂躡亨衢，七登南宮，兩拜東掖，便繁台閣二十余載，久知弘文館圖籍方書等，繇是睹奧升堂，皆探其秘要。

### Literal Translation

Now the virtue of the sages, what more can be added to this? Therefore the constant way of the Three Dynasties, the hundred kings did not change, and its origin is far. From the works of Lei, Qi, Cang, and Huan, and the rise of Peng, Bian, Hua, and Zhang, down to the present, benevolent worthies have appeared from time to time, with years numbering in the thousands and formulas exceeding ten thousand volumes, which a single cart could not carry and a great hall could not contain. Yet the years and sacrifices are long and distant, and the bamboo slips and writings have decayed and been replaced; what is detailed is broad, but what is omitted is sometimes deep. To search the slips doubles the merit and troubles the strength; in choosing and rejecting, one discusses the sweet and avoids the bitter. Long have I spoken of editing, but I have not had leisure to take it up.

I was often ill in childhood, and when I grew up I loved the art of medicine. Meeting with an enlightened age, I trod the broad road, ascending the Southern Palace seven times and bowing at the Eastern Side twice, serving busily in the high court for more than twenty years, and long knowing the books and records of the Hongwen Academy. From this I saw the profundity and ascended the hall, and explored all its arcane essentials.

以婚姻之故，貶守房陵，量移大寧郡，提攜江上，冒犯蒸暑，自南徂北，既僻且陋，染瘴嬰　，十有六七，死生契闊，不可問天，賴有經方僅得存者，神功妙用，固難稱述，遂發憤刊削，庶幾一隅。凡古方纂得五六十家，新撰者向數千百卷，皆研其總領，核其指歸，近代釋僧深、崔尚書、孫處士、張文仲、孟同州、許仁則、吳升等十數家，皆有編錄，並行於代，美則美矣，而未盡善。何者？各擅風流，遞相矛盾，或篇目重雜，或商較繁蕪。

### Literal Translation

Because of marriage, I was demoted to guard Fangling, and then transferred to Daning Commandery. Carrying my family along the river, braving the steaming heat, from south to north, both remote and crude, I was infected by miasma, and six or seven in ten died; life and death were separated, and I could not ask Heaven. Relying on the canonical formulas that alone survived, whose spirit-work and marvelous use are indeed hard to describe, I then roused myself to edit and abridge, hoping for a corner of it. In all, I compiled some fifty or sixty schools of ancient formulas, and the newly written ones approached several thousand hundred volumes; I studied their general principles and verified their aims. In recent times, the monk Sengshen, Minister Cui, Hermit Sun, Zhang Wenzhong, Meng of Tongzhou, Xu Renze, Wu Sheng, and more than ten other schools all had compilations, circulating together in the age; beautiful they were, but not yet fully good. Why? Each excelled in its own style, contradicting one another in turn, with some having overlapping and mixed chapter titles, and some having complicated and overgrown discussions.

今並味精英，鈐其要妙，俾夜作晝，經之營之，捐眾賢之砂礫，掇群才之翠羽，皆出入再三，伏念旬歲，上自炎昊，迄於聖唐，括囊遺闕，稽考隱秘，不愧盡心焉。

客有見余此方曰：嘻，博哉﹗學乃至於此邪？余答之曰：吾所好者壽也，豈進於學哉？至於遁天倍情，懸解先覺，吾常聞之矣。投藥治疾，庶幾有瘳乎？又謂余曰：稟生受形，咸有定分，藥石其如命何？吾甚非之，請論其目。

### Literal Translation

Now I savor the elite together, seal up their essential subtleties, making night into day, planning and managing, discarding the gravel of the many worthies and gathering the kingfisher feathers of the many talents, going in and out two or three times, and pondering for ten years, from Yan and Hao above down to the sagely Tang, gathering up the lost and omitted, examining the hidden and secret, not ashamed of exhausting the mind.

A guest, seeing my formulas, said: Hah, how broad! Has learning reached this? I answered him, saying: What I love is longevity; how would I advance in learning? As for fleeing Heaven and doubling the feelings, and the suspended release and the prior awakening, I have often heard of them. Throwing drugs to treat illness, will it perhaps be cured? He also said to me: In receiving life and taking form, all have fixed allotments; what can drugs and stones do against fate? I greatly consider this wrong; let me discuss its items.

### Source Text

若不能精究病源，深探方論，雖百醫守疾，眾藥聚門，適足多疑，而不能一愈之也。主上尊賢重道，養壽祈年，故張、王、李等數先生繼入，皆欽風請益，貴而遵之，故鴻寶金匱、青囊綠帙，往往而有，則知日月所照者遠，聖人所感者深，至於嗇神養和、休老補病者，可得聞見也。余敢采而錄之，則古所未有，今並繕緝，而能事畢矣。

### Literal Translation

If one cannot precisely study the sources of disease and deeply explore the discourses on formulas, then even if a hundred physicians guard the illness and many drugs gather at the gate, it only adds much doubt and cannot cure it even once. The sovereign honors the worthy and values the Way, nourishes longevity and prays for years, so several gentlemen such as Zhang, Wang, and Li successively entered, all admiring the style and requesting benefit, honoring and following it. Therefore the Hongbao (Great Treasure), the Jinkui (Golden Coffer), the Qingnang (Blue Bag), and the Lüzhi (Green Book) are often present; from this one knows that what the sun and moon illuminate is far, and what the sages move is deep. As for sparing the spirit and nourishing harmony, resting the old and supplementing the ill, one can hear and see them. I dare to gather and record them; then what was not present in antiquity, now I compile and arrange together, and the task is complete.

I have heard these words, but have not met such a person. I do not deceive the future; let me await the coming worthies.
