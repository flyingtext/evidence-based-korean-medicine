## 《外台秘要》序

### Source Text

天下事，久壞於庸人，而庸醫均之。所謂庸者，皆不學無術之人也。其遇事也，初不晰其受病之源，並不審其對治之方，而或以姑息養癰，或以鹵莽嘗試。

### Literal Translation

The affairs of the world have long been ruined by mediocre people, and mediocre physicians are the same. What is called mediocre are all people who do not learn and have no skill. When they encounter a matter, at first they do not clarify the source of the disease received, nor do they examine the method of treating it, but either they nourish the abscess with indulgence or they try it rashly and recklessly.

## 《外台秘要》序

### Source Text

究之鹵莽之為害，什居五；而姑息之為害，什居八。乃始藉手以貽後人，曰：吾姑徐徐無動為大耳，不知邪氣日深，元氣日削，而大命已隨之。所謂庸臣誤國與庸醫誤人，其情同，其罪均，而其原皆本於不學，使其學之而窮究古今之病源，細考古今之方略，其病之在標、在本也，其方之宜補、宜泄也，其病之或傳、或變也，其方之不執、不拘也，彼夫三年蓄艾，能治之於未病之先，與夫一匕回生，能治之於已病之後，皆是先聖先賢當日殫竭之心思，皆是醫國醫人當日素具之方略，懦者明理，奈何不亟亟講求，而以人命為戲耶﹗此余友敬通氏探析性命之微，雅抱之痛，而以好學不倦之旨，牖人以博施濟眾之方，先梓《外台秘要》，續補《聖濟總錄》，壽國壽民，具茲編矣，程子之功顧不偉歟。

### Literal Translation

In the end, the harm of recklessness is five in ten, and the harm of indulgence is eight in ten. Then they begin to use their hands to leave it to later people, saying: I will just slowly not move, and not make it great. They do not know that the evil qi deepens day by day, the primordial qi is shaved away day by day, and the great life has already followed it. What is called the mediocre minister ruining the state and the mediocre physician ruining people have the same circumstances and equal guilt, and their origin is all rooted in not learning. If they learned and exhaustively investigated the sources of disease of past and present, and carefully examined the formulas and strategies of past and present, whether the disease is in the branch or in the root, whether the formula should supplement or drain, whether the disease is transmitted or transformed, and whether the formula is not rigid and not constrained, then that one who stored mugwort for three years and could treat before the disease arose, and that one who with one spoon restored life and could treat after the disease had arisen, are all the minds that the former sages and worthies exhausted in their day, and all the formulas and strategies that the healers of the state and healers of the people had long possessed. The weak one understands principle; why not urgently study and discuss, and play with human life? This is why my friend Jingtong, exploring the subtlety of life and destiny, with the pain he cherished, and with the aim of loving learning without weariness, guides people with the method of broadly giving and saving the multitude. First he carved the 《Waitai Miyao》, and then supplemented the 《Shengji Zonglu》 (General Records of Sagely Benevolence). To give long life to the state and to the people is all contained in this work. Is Master Cheng's merit not great?
