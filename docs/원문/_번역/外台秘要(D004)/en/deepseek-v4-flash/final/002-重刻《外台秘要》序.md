## 重刻《外台秘要》序

### Source Text

嘗謂良醫與良相功齊，總之能為民生造命耳。予武夫也，不知相亦不知醫，惟是人之有疾，不啻已出，值此滄桑變故之際，民遭兵火，若患熱烈之症，予濫任撫綏，尤當視民如傷恨，不能人人而投以清涼之劑。自入新安以來，每問民間疾苦，見程生衍道者業儒而通醫，博施濟眾久矣，更構唐時王刺史《外台秘要》四十卷，詳訂而剞劂之。

### Literal Translation

I have often said that a good physician and a good minister have equal merit; in sum, they can create life for the people. I am a military man; I know neither ministers nor medicine. Only this: when people have illness, it is no less than if it came from myself. At this time of great upheaval and change, the people have suffered war and fire; if they suffer from hot and intense symptoms, I, having been rashly entrusted with pacification, should especially regard the people as if wounded, and regret that I cannot give everyone a cooling remedy. Since entering Xin'an, I have asked about the hardships of the people, and saw that Master Cheng Yandao, a Confucian by profession who is versed in medicine, has long been broadly giving and saving the multitude. He further obtained the forty volumes of the 《Waitai Miyao》 by Prefect Wang of the Tang dynasty, and carefully corrected and carved them.

## 重刻《外台秘要》序

### Source Text

蓋醫之有方，猶兵之有法也。說者曰：功在一隅者小，而功在四海者大。又曰：功在一時者小，而功在千古者大，是程生傳流方略意也。

### Literal Translation

For medicine having formulas is like the military having laws. Those who speak say: Merit in one corner is small, but merit in the four seas is great. They also say: Merit in one time is small, but merit in a thousand ages is great. This is Master Cheng's intention in transmitting and circulating the formulas and strategies.

## 重刻《外台秘要》序

### Source Text

若然，則程生志在要功矣，而非也。程生恐匹夫匹婦疾病而無告，又恐良醫良相，世代不全，有不能弭病於無病之先，尚可瘳病於有病之後，繼宋而鐫壽民，因以壽國，大有深心。文王畫卦使人知吉凶，孔子作《春秋》使人知君臣，即此意也。

### Literal Translation

If so, then Master Cheng's aim is to seek merit; but it is not so. Master Cheng fears that common men and women who are ill have no one to appeal to, and also fears that good physicians and good ministers are not complete in every generation; even if one cannot extinguish disease before it arises, one can still cure disease after it has arisen. Continuing the Song, he carved to give long life to the people, and thereby gave long life to the state; he has great deep intention. King Wen drew the hexagrams to make people know good and ill fortune, and Confucius wrote the 《Spring and Autumn Annals》 to make people know ruler and minister; this is the same intention.

## 重刻《外台秘要》序

### Source Text

程生一士子藉醫而行相道，然與予撫民一念默相吻合，又以醫而兼將矣，曷可與庸人道也。天下後世能按此而擴充之，又何病民之與有？

<ins>欽命提督徽寧池太等處地方總兵官都督同知榆林張天祿題</ins>

### Literal Translation

Master Cheng, a single scholar, uses medicine to practice the way of the minister; yet it secretly coincides with my single thought of pacifying the people, and he also combines the general with medicine. How can this be spoken of to common people? If the later generations of the world can follow this and expand it, then what harm to the people would there be?
