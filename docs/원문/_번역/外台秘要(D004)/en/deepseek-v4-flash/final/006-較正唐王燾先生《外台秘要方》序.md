## 較正唐王燾先生《外台秘要方》序

### Source Text

夫外台者，刺史之任也；秘要者，秘密、樞要之謂也。唐王燾台閣二十余年，久知弘文館，得古今方，上自神農，下及唐世，無不采摭，集成經方四十卷，皆諸方秘密樞要也。以出守於外，故號曰《外台秘要方》，凡一千一百單四門，以巢氏《病源》、諸家論辨各冠其首，一家之學，不為不詳。

### Literal Translation

Now, the outer terrace (Waitai) is the office of the prefect; the arcane essentials (Miyao) mean the secret and the pivotal. Wang Tao of the Tang served in the high court for more than twenty years, long knowing the Hongwen Academy, and obtained the formulas of past and present, from Shennong above down to the Tang age, gathering and collecting without exception, and assembled them into forty volumes of canonical formulas, all being the secret and pivotal of the various formulas. Because he went out to guard in the provinces, he named it the 《Waitai Miyao Fang》, with 1,104 sections in all, heading each with Chao's 《Bingyuan》 and the discourses of the various schools; the learning of one school is not without detail.

## 較正唐王燾先生《外台秘要方》序

### Source Text

王氏為儒者，醫道雖未及孫思邈，然而采取諸家之方頗得其要者，亦崔氏、孟詵之流也。且古之如張仲景、《集驗》、《短劇方》，最為名家，今多亡逸，方中，亦不能別白，王氏編次，各題名號，使後之學人，皆知所出，此其所長也。又謂針能殺生人，不能起死人，其法云亡且久，故取灸而不取針，亦醫家之蔽也。

### Literal Translation

Wang was a Confucian; although his way of medicine did not reach Sun Simiao, yet in taking the formulas of the various schools and obtaining their essentials, he is also of the class of Cui and Meng Shen. Moreover, the ancients such as Zhang Zhongjing, the 《Jiyan》 (Collected Proofs), and the 《Duanju Fang》 (Short and Complete Formulas) were the most famous schools, but many are now lost and scattered, and in the formulas one cannot distinguish them clearly. Wang arranged them in order, giving each a title and name, so that later learners all know their origin; this is his strength. He also said that needling can kill the living but cannot raise the dead, and that its method has long been lost, so he took moxibustion and did not take needling; this is also a defect of the physician's family.

## 較正唐王燾先生《外台秘要方》序

### Source Text

此方撰集之時，或得缺落之書，因其闕文、義理不完者多矣。又自唐歷五代，傳寫其本，訛舛尤甚，雖鴻都秘府，亦無善本。國家詔儒臣較正醫書，臣承命，以其書方證之重者，刪去以從其簡；經書之異者，注解以著其詳，魯魚豕亥，煥然明白。

### Literal Translation

When this work was compiled, he sometimes obtained incomplete books, and because of their missing passages, many have incomplete meaning and principle. Moreover, from the Tang through the Five Dynasties, the copies were transmitted and written, with errors especially severe; even the Hongdu secret repository had no good copy. The state ordered the Confucian ministers to correct the medical books. I, your servant, receiving the command, deleted the important of the book's formulas and evidence to follow its brevity, and annotated the differences of the canonical books to make manifest their detail, so that the errors of lu and yu, shi and hai were made clear and bright.

## 較正唐王燾先生《外台秘要方》序

### Source Text

臣謂三代而下，文物之盛者，必曰西漢，止以侍醫李柱國較方技，亦未嘗命儒臣也。臣雖濫吹儒學，但盡所聞見，以修正之，有所闕疑，以待來哲。總四十卷，並目錄一卷。

### Literal Translation

I, your servant, say that below the Three Dynasties, the one with flourishing culture and artifacts must be said to be the Western Han; it only used the imperial physician Li Zhuguo to collate the arts of formulas, and never ordered the Confucian ministers. Although I, your servant, blow in the Confucian learning without merit, I only exhaust what I have heard and seen to correct it, and where there is doubt I await the coming worthies. In all, forty volumes, together with one volume of table of contents.

### Source Text

恭惟主上盛德承統，深仁流化，頒此方論，惠及區宇，贊天地之生育，正萬物之性命，使歲無疵癘，人不夭橫，熙熙然歌樂於聖造者也。

### Literal Translation

I respectfully consider that the sovereign, with his great virtue, inherits the succession, and his deep benevolence flows in transformation, promulgating these discourses and formulas, bestowing grace on the whole realm, assisting the generation and nourishment of Heaven and Earth, and rectifying the life and destiny of the ten thousand things, so that the years have no calamity and pestilence, and people do not die prematurely; joyfully they sing and delight in the sagely creation.
