## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

今搜探仲景舊論，錄其證候診脈聲色對病真方有神驗者，擬防世急也。又土地高下，寒溫不同，物性剛柔餐居亦異，是故黃帝興─四方之問，岐伯舉四治之能，以訓後賢開其未悟，臨病之工，宜須兩審也。（短劇千金同）

又曰：夫表和裡病，（一作陽盛陰虛）下之而愈，汗之則死。

### Literal Translation

Now I search and explore Zhongjing's old discourses, recording those true formulas that match the disease with their signs, pulse diagnosis, voice, and complexion and have spirit-proof, to guard against the emergencies of the world. Moreover, the high and low of the land, the difference of cold and warmth, and the hardness and softness of things and the difference of food and dwelling all differ; therefore the Yellow Emperor raised the questions of the four directions, and Qibo set forth the abilities of the four treatments, to instruct later worthies and open their not-yet-awakened understanding. The artisan facing disease should indeed examine both. (Same as Duanju and Qianjin)

He also says: When the exterior is harmonious and there is disease in the interior (one version: yang exuberant, yin vacuous), purging it cures it, while sweating it kills.
