## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

（出第七卷中）

仲景傷寒論傷寒一二日，心中悸而煩，小建中湯主之方。

桂心（三兩）　甘草（炙二兩）　生薑（三兩）　大棗（十二枚擘）　膠飴（一升）　芍藥（六兩）

上六味切，以水七升，先煮五味，取三升，去滓納飴，更上火微煮，令消解，溫服一升，日三服，如嘔家不可服建中湯，以甜故也。忌海藻菘菜生蔥。

### Literal Translation

(From the seventh volume)

Zhongjing's Shanghan Lun: In cold damage of one or two days, if there is palpitation in the heart and vexation, the Xiao Jianzhong Tang (Minor Construct the Middle Decoction) governs it. Formula:

Guixin (cinnamon bark) (3 liang), Gancao (licorice, roasted, 2 liang), Shengjiang (fresh ginger, 3 liang), Dazao (jujube, 12 pieces, broken), Jiaoyi (malt sugar, 1 sheng), Shaoyao (peony, 6 liang).

The above six ingredients, cut. With 7 sheng of water, first boil the five ingredients, take 3 sheng, remove the dregs, add the malt sugar, and again put on the fire and boil slightly, letting it dissolve. Take 1 sheng warm, three times daily. If one is of the vomiting household, one cannot take the Jianzhong Tang, because of its sweetness. Avoid seaweed, cabbage, and raw scallion.
