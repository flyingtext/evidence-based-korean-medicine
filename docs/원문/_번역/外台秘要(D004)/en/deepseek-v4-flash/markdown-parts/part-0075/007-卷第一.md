## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

病源：傷寒八日，陽明病衰，身熱少愈，傷寒八日，病不解者，或是諸陰陽經絡重受於病，或因發汗吐下之後，毒瓦斯未盡，所以病證猶存也。

又傷寒九日，少陽病衰，耳聾微聞，傷寒九日以上病不除者，或初一經受病，即不能相傳，或已傳三陽訖，而不能傳於陰，致停滯累日，病證不罷者，或三陽三陰傳病已畢，又重感於寒，故日數多而病候改變。（出第七卷中）

仲景傷寒論：療傷寒八九日，風濕相搏，身體疼痛而煩，不能自轉側，不嘔不渴，下之脈浮虛而澀者，屬桂枝附子湯。

### Literal Translation

The Bingyuan: On the eighth day of cold damage, the yangming disease declines and the body heat heals somewhat. On the eighth day of cold damage, those whose disease does not resolve are either because the various yin and yang channels and collaterals receive the disease again, or because after sweating, vomiting, and purging, the poisonous qi is not exhausted, so the disease signs still remain.

Again, on the ninth day of cold damage, the shaoyang disease declines and the deafness is slightly heard. For those whose disease is not removed after nine days or more of cold damage, either the first channel received the disease and then could not transmit it, or it had already finished transmitting to the three yang and could not transmit to the yin, causing stagnation over many days and the disease signs not ceasing; or the three yang and three yin had already finished transmitting the disease and then were affected by cold again, so the number of days is many and the disease signs change. (From the seventh volume)

Zhongjing's Shanghan Lun: In cold damage of eight or nine days, wind and dampness contend with each other, the body is painful and vexed, one cannot turn over by oneself, and one does not vomit or thirst; if one purges, the pulse is floating, vacuous, and rough; it belongs to the Guizhi Fuzi Tang (Cinnamon Twig and Aconite Decoction).
