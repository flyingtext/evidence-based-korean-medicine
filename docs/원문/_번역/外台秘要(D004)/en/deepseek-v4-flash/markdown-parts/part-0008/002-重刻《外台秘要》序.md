## 重刻《外台秘要》序

### Source Text

蓋醫之有方，猶兵之有法也。說者曰：功在一隅者小，而功在四海者大。又曰：功在一時者小，而功在千古者大，是程生傳流方略意也。

### Literal Translation

For medicine having formulas is like the military having laws. Those who speak say: Merit in one corner is small, but merit in the four seas is great. They also say: Merit in one time is small, but merit in a thousand ages is great. This is Master Cheng's intention in transmitting and circulating the formulas and strategies.
