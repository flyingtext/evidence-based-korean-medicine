## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

忌羊肉餳兼主天行。（千金翼肘後同）

又傷寒十三日不解，胸脅滿而嘔，日晡所發潮熱，熱畢而微利，此本柴胡證，下之不得利，今反利者，知　以丸藥下之，此非其治也，潮熱者實也，先宜服小柴胡湯以解其外後以柴胡加芒硝湯主之方。

柴胡（二兩十六銖）　黃芩　人參　甘草（炙）　生薑（各一兩）　半夏（五枚）　大棗（四枚擘）　芒硝（二合）

上八味切，以水四升，煮七味，取二升去滓，下芒硝，更上火煎一二沸，分為兩服，未解更作，忌海藻菘菜羊肉餳等。

### Literal Translation

Avoid mutton and malt sugar; it also governs epidemic disease. (Same as Qianjin Yi and Zhouhou.)

Again, in cold damage of thirteen days that does not resolve, with fullness of the chest and flanks and vomiting, tidal fever breaking out at the time of the sun's decline, and after the heat ends slight benefit, this is originally a Chaihu sign; purging it, one did not obtain benefit, but now instead there is benefit, so one knows it was purged with a pill medicine; this is not its treatment. Tidal fever is excess; first one should take the Xiao Chaihu Tang to resolve the exterior, and afterward use the Chaihu Jia Mangxiao Tang (Bupleurum plus Glauber's Salt Decoction) to govern it. Formula:

Chaihu (bupleurum, 2 liang 16 zhu), Huangqin (scutellaria), Renshen (ginseng), Gancao (licorice, roasted), Shengjiang (fresh ginger, each 1 liang), Banxia (pinellia, 5 pieces), Dazao (jujube, 4 pieces, broken), Mangxiao (Glauber's salt, 2 ge).

The above eight ingredients, cut. With 4 sheng of water, boil the seven ingredients, take 2 sheng, remove the dregs, add the Mangxiao, and again put on the fire and decoct for one or two bubbling, divide into two doses. If not resolved, make it again. Avoid seaweed, cabbage, mutton, and malt sugar, etc.
