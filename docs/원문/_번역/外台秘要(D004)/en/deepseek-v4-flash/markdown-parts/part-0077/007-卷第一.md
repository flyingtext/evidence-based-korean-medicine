## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

附子白朮湯方白朮（四兩）　大棗（十二枚）　甘草（炙一兩）　生薑（二兩）　附子（三枚炮去皮四破）

上五味切，以水六升，煮取二升，去滓，溫分三服，初一服其人身如痹，半日許復服之都盡，其人如冒狀者勿怪，此以附子術並走皮中逐水氣未除，故使人如冒狀也，本云附子一枚，今加之二枚，名附子湯，忌蔥豬肉菘菜海藻桃李雀肉等。（千金翼同出第十一卷中，張仲景論法當加桂枝四兩此本一方二法以大便硬小便自利故去桂也以大便不硬小便不利當加桂附病源，傷寒十日，太陰病衰，腹減如故，則思飲食，十一日少陰病衰，渴止不滿，舌乾已而嚏，十二日，厥陰病衰，囊縱，少腹微下，大氣皆去，病日已矣。（出第七卷中素問太素甲乙並同）

仲景傷寒論：療吐下之後，不大便，五六日至十余日，日晡所發潮熱，不惡寒，獨語如見鬼狀，若劇者發則不識人，循衣摸床，惕而不安，微喘，但發熱譫（譫疾而寤寐自語也）

語者，屬大承氣湯方。

### Literal Translation

The Fuzi Baizhu Tang formula: Baizhu (atractylodes, 4 liang), Dazao (jujube, 12 pieces), Gancao (licorice, roasted, 1 liang), Shengjiang (fresh ginger, 2 liang), Fuzi (aconite, 3 pieces, roasted, skin removed, broken into four).

The above five ingredients, cut. With 6 sheng of water, boil and take 2 sheng, remove the dregs, and take warm in three divided doses. On the first dose the person's body is like an impediment; after about half a day, take it again until it is all gone. If the person is like one in a daze, do not wonder; this is because the Fuzi and Shu together go into the skin to expel the water qi, which has not yet been removed, so it makes the person like one in a daze. Originally it says one piece of Fuzi; now adding two pieces, it is named the Fuzi Tang. Avoid scallion, pork, cabbage, seaweed, peach, plum, and sparrow meat, etc. (Same as Qianjin Yi; from the eleventh volume. Zhang Zhongjing's discourse: by law one should add 4 liang of Guizhi; this is originally one formula with two methods, because the stool is hard and the urine is freely self-benefiting, so the Gui is removed. If the stool is not hard and the urine is not benefiting, one should add Gui and Fu. The Bingyuan: on the tenth day of cold damage, the taiyin disease declines, the abdomen decreases as before, and then one thinks of food and drink; on the eleventh day the shaoyin disease declines, the thirst stops and there is no fullness, the dry tongue has ceased and one sneezes; on the twelfth day the jueyin disease declines, the scrotum relaxes, the lower abdomen slightly descends, the great qi all departs, and the disease ends day by day. From the seventh volume; same as Suwen, Taisu, and Jiayi.)

Zhongjing's Shanghan Lun: After vomiting and purging, with no bowel movement from five or six days up to ten or more days, tidal fever breaking out at the time of the sun's decline (about 3-5 pm), no aversion to cold, and speaking alone as if seeing ghosts; if severe, when it breaks out one does not recognize people, feels along the clothes and touches the bed, is fearful and uneasy, slightly panting, and only has fever and delirium (delirium is speaking to oneself in the midst of sleeping and waking).
