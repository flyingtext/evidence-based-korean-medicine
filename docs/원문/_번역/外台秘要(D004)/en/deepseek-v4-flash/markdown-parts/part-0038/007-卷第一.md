## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

（仲景病源短劇千金同）

王叔和曰：傷寒之病，逐日淺深以施方治。今世人得傷寒，或始不早治，或治不對病，或日數久淹，困乃告醫，醫又不知次第而治之，則不中病。皆以臨時消息制方，無不效也。

### Literal Translation

(Same as Zhongjing, Bingyuan, Duanju, and Qianjin)

Wang Shuhe says: In the disease of cold damage, one applies the formula treatment according to the shallowness and depth of each day. People of today who get cold damage either do not treat it early at the start, or treat it without matching the disease, or let the days drag on long, and only when exhausted do they tell the physician. The physician, again not knowing the order, treats it, and then it does not hit the disease. If one always makes the formula by weighing the situation at the time, there is none that is not effective.
