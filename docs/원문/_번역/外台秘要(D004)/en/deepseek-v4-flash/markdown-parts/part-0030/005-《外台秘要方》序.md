## 《外台秘要方》序

### Source Text

其方凡四十卷，名曰《外台秘要方》，非敢傳之都邑，且欲施於後賢，如或詢謀，亦所不隱

<ins>是歲天寶十一載，歲在執徐，月之哉生明者也。</ins>

### Literal Translation

Its formulas are forty volumes in all, named the 《Waitai Miyao Fang》 (Arcane Essentials from the Imperial Library). I do not dare to transmit it to the capital cities, but wish to bestow it on later worthies; if they should consult and plan, I will not hide it.

<ins>This year is the eleventh year of Tianbao, the year being in Zhixu, on the day when the moon's light first appears.</ins>
