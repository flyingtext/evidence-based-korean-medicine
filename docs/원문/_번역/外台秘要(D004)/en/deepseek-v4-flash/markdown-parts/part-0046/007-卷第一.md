## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

三死一生，此輩皆多不愈。胃虛熱入爛胃也，其熱微者赤斑出，劇者黑斑出，赤斑出者五死一生，黑斑出者十死一生。但論人有強弱，病有難易，功效相倍耳。

### Literal Translation

Three die and one lives; this kind mostly does not recover. When the stomach is vacuous and heat enters and rots the stomach, those with mild heat have red spots appear, and those with severe heat have black spots appear. Those with red spots: five die and one lives; those with black spots: ten die and one lives. One only discusses the strength and weakness of the person and the difficulty and ease of the disease; the effect is doubled.
