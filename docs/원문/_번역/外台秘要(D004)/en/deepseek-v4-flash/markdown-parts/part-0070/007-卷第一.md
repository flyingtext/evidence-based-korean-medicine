## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

（千金翼同出第三卷中）

（張仲景傷寒論傷寒一二日內麻黃湯主之此云小建中湯非也此方但治心中悸而煩）

病源傷寒三日，少陽受病，少陽者，膽之經也，其脈循於脅上於頸耳，故得病三日，胸脅痛而耳聾也，三陽經絡始相傳病，未入於臟，故皆可汗而解。（出第七卷中）

仲景傷寒論，療太陽病三日發其汗，病不解，蒸蒸發熱者，屬調胃承氣湯方。

甘草（炙三兩）　芒硝（半升）　大黃（四兩）

上三味切，以水三升煮二物取一升去滓，納芒硝更煮微沸，溫溫頓服，以調胃承氣則愈。

### Literal Translation

(Same as Qianjin Yi; from the third volume)

(Zhang Zhongjing's Shanghan Lun: in cold damage of one or two days, the Mahuang Tang governs it; saying this is the Xiao Jianzhong Tang is wrong. This formula only treats palpitation in the heart and vexation.)

The Bingyuan: On the third day of cold damage, the shaoyang receives the disease. The shaoyang is the channel of the gallbladder; its vessel follows the flanks and ascends to the neck and ears, therefore on the third day of the disease there is pain of the chest and flanks and deafness. The three yang channels and collaterals begin to transmit the disease to each other and have not yet entered the viscera, therefore all can be sweated and resolved. (From the seventh volume)

Zhongjing's Shanghan Lun: For treating taiyang disease of three days, one sweats it, the disease does not resolve, and there is steaming fever; it belongs to the Tiaowei Chengqi Tang (Regulate the Stomach and Order the Qi Decoction). Formula:

Gancao (licorice, roasted, 3 liang), Mangxiao (Glauber's salt, half sheng), Dahuang (rhubarb, 4 liang).

The above three ingredients, cut. With 3 sheng of water, boil the two ingredients, take 1 sheng, remove the dregs, add the Mangxiao, and again boil until slightly bubbling. Take it warm in one draught; with the Tiaowei Chengqi it heals.
