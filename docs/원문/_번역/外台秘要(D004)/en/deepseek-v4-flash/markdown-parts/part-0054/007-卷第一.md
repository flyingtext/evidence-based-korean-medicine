## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

二曰泄而腹滿甚者死（甚一作黃）。三曰目不明，熱不已者死。四曰老人嬰兒熱病腹滿者死。

### Literal Translation

Second: those who have diarrhea and severe abdominal fullness die (severe, one version: yellow). Third: those whose eyes are not clear and whose heat does not stop die. Fourth: old people and infants with heat disease and abdominal fullness die.
