## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

若熱毒在胃外，未入於胃而先下之者，其熱乘虛便入胃，則爛胃也。然熱入胃病，要當須復下去之不得留於胃中也。胃若實熱，致此為病。

### Literal Translation

If the heat toxin is outside the stomach, and one purges it before it has entered the stomach, the heat, taking advantage of the vacuity, then enters the stomach and rots the stomach. However, in the disease of heat entering the stomach, one should indeed purge it again to remove it, and not let it remain in the stomach. If the stomach is truly hot, this causes the disease.
