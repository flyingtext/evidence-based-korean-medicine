## 重刻《外台秘要》序

### Source Text

若然，則程生志在要功矣，而非也。程生恐匹夫匹婦疾病而無告，又恐良醫良相，世代不全，有不能弭病於無病之先，尚可瘳病於有病之後，繼宋而鐫壽民，因以壽國，大有深心。文王畫卦使人知吉凶，孔子作《春秋》使人知君臣，即此意也。

### Literal Translation

If so, then Master Cheng's aim is to seek merit; but it is not so. Master Cheng fears that common men and women who are ill have no one to appeal to, and also fears that good physicians and good ministers are not complete in every generation; even if one cannot extinguish disease before it arises, one can still cure disease after it has arisen. Continuing the Song, he carved to give long life to the people, and thereby gave long life to the state; he has great deep intention. King Wen drew the hexagrams to make people know good and ill fortune, and Confucius wrote the 《Spring and Autumn Annals》 to make people know ruler and minister; this is the same intention.
