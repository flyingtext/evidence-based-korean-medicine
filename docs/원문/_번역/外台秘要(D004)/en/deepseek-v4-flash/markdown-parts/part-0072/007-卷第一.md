## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

（並出第七卷中）

仲景傷寒論：傷寒四五日，身熱惡風，頸項強，脅下滿，手足溫而渴者，小柴胡湯主之方。

柴胡（半斤）　栝蔞根（四兩）　桂心（三兩）　黃芩（三兩）　牡蠣（三兩）　甘草（炙二兩）　乾薑（三兩）

上七味切，以水一斗二升，煮取六升，去滓更煎取三升，溫服一升，日三服，初服微煩，溫覆汗出者便愈也。忌生蔥海藻菘菜。

### Literal Translation

(Both from the seventh volume)

Zhongjing's Shanghan Lun: In cold damage of four or five days, with body heat, aversion to wind, stiff neck and nape, fullness below the flanks, and warm hands and feet with thirst, the Xiao Chaihu Tang (Minor Bupleurum Decoction) governs it. Formula:

Chaihu (bupleurum, half jin), Gualougen (trichosanthes root, 4 liang), Guixin (cinnamon bark, 3 liang), Huangqin (scutellaria, 3 liang), Muli (oyster shell, 3 liang), Gancao (licorice, roasted, 2 liang), Ganjiang (dried ginger, 3 liang).

The above seven ingredients, cut. With 1 dou 2 sheng of water, boil and take 6 sheng, remove the dregs, and again decoct to take 3 sheng. Take 1 sheng warm, three times daily. On the first dose there is slight vexation; if one covers warmly and sweats, then it heals. Avoid raw scallion, seaweed, and cabbage.
