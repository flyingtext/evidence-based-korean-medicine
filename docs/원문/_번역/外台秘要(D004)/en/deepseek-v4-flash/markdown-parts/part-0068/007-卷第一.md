## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

岐伯曰：陽明者十二經脈之長也，其氣血盛，故不知人，三日其氣乃盡，故死。（出第九卷中甲乙太素同）

病源傷寒一日，太陽受病，太陽者，小腸之經也，為三陽之首，故先受病，其脈絡於腰脊，主於頭項，故得病一日而頭項背膊腰脊痛也。

又傷寒二日，陽明受病，陽明者，胃之經也，主於肌肉，其脈絡鼻入目，故得病二日，內熱鼻干不得眠也，諸陽在表表始受病在皮膚之間，故可摩膏火灸發汗而愈。

### Literal Translation

Qibo says: The yangming is the chief of the twelve channels and vessels; its qi and blood are exuberant, therefore one does not know people, and in three days its qi is then exhausted, therefore one dies. (From the ninth volume; same as Jiayi and Taisu)

The Bingyuan: On the first day of cold damage, the taiyang receives the disease. The taiyang is the channel of the small intestine, being the head of the three yang, therefore it first receives the disease; its vessel connects with the loins and spine and governs the head and nape, therefore on the first day of the disease there is pain of the head, nape, back, arms, loins, and spine.

Again, on the second day of cold damage, the yangming receives the disease. The yangming is the channel of the stomach, governing the flesh; its vessel connects with the nose and enters the eyes, therefore on the second day of the disease there is internal heat, dry nose, and inability to sleep. The various yang are on the exterior; the exterior first receives the disease and is within the skin, therefore one can rub with ointment and apply fire moxibustion to sweat, and it heals.
