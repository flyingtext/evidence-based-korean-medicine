## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

三陰三陽五臟六腑皆受病，營衛不行，五臟不通，則死矣。其不兩感於寒者，七日巨陽病衰，頭痛少愈。八日陽明病衰，身熱少愈。

### Literal Translation

When the three yin, three yang, five viscera, and six bowels have all received the disease, the constructive and defensive do not move, and the five viscera do not communicate, then one dies. For those who are not affected by cold twice, on the seventh day the great yang disease declines and the headache heals somewhat. On the eighth day the yangming disease declines and the body heat heals somewhat.
