## 重刻《外台秘要方》序

### Source Text

余因問程子：如欲知醫，何道而能精？曰：必好學。將何學？曰：先讀書。讀書可以醫乎？曰：讀書而不能醫者，有之矣，決未有不讀書而能醫者也。

### Literal Translation

I then asked Master Cheng: If one wishes to know medicine, by what way can one become proficient? He said: One must love learning. What shall one study? He said: First read books. Can reading books make one a physician? He said: There are those who read books and yet cannot be physicians, but there is certainly no one who can be a physician without reading books.
