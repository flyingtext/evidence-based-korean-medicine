## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

至再三發汗不解，當與湯，實者轉下之，其脈朝夕　者為實癖也，朝平夕　者非　也，轉下湯為可早與，但當少與，勿令下多耳，少與當數其間。

病有虛煩熱者與傷寒相似，然不惡寒，身不疼痛，故知非傷寒也。不可發汗，頭不痛，脈不緊數，故知非裡實也，不可下，如此內外皆不可攻，而師強攻之，必遂損竭多死矣。

### Literal Translation

If after sweating two or three times it does not resolve, one should give a decoction; for the excess, turn and purge. If the pulse is (rapid) morning and evening, it is an excess mass; if it is calm in the morning and (rapid) in the evening, it is not excess. The turning-and-purging decoction may be given early, but one should give only a little, and not let it purge too much; when giving a little, one should count the intervals.

When there is vacuous vexation and heat in the disease, it resembles cold damage, but it does not have aversion to cold and the body is not painful, so one knows it is not cold damage. One cannot sweat it; the head does not hurt and the pulse is not tight and rapid, so one knows it is not interior excess. One cannot purge it. If both interior and exterior cannot be attacked, yet the master forcefully attacks it, it will certainly end in depletion and exhaustion, and many die.
