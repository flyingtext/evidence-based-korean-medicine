## 《外台秘要方》序

### Source Text

夫喜怒不節，飢飽失常，嗜欲攻中，寒溫傷外，如此之患，豈由天乎？夫為人臣，為人子，自家刑國，由近兼遠，何談之容易哉？則聖人不合啟金滕，賢者曷為條玉版，斯言之玷，竊為吾子羞之。客曰：唯唯。嗚呼﹗齊梁之間，不明醫術者，不得為孝子，魯、閔之行，宜其用心。

### Literal Translation

Now, if joy and anger are not moderated, hunger and satiety lose their constancy, desires attack within, and cold and warmth injure without, how could such afflictions come from Heaven? Now, being a minister and being a son, one models the state on the family, and from the near includes the far; how easy is it to speak of this? Then the sage should not have opened the golden coffer, and why would the worthy have set out the jade tablets? The flaw of this speech, I privately feel ashamed for you. The guest said: Yes, yes. Alas! In the Qi and Liang periods, one who did not understand the art of medicine could not be a filial son; the conduct of Lu and Min should indeed use their minds.
