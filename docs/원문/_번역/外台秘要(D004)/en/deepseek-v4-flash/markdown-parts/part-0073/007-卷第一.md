## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

（范汪同出第三卷中，仲景傷寒論名柴胡薑桂也合用柴胡人參甘草黃芩半夏生薑大棗七味小柴胡湯是也玉函千金翼同）

病源：傷寒六日，厥陰受病，厥陰者肝之經也，其脈循陰器絡於肝，故得病六日煩滿而囊縮也，此則陰陽俱受病，毒瓦斯在胸，故可下而愈。

又傷寒七日，太陽病衰，頭痛少愈，傷寒七日病法當小愈，陰陽諸經傳盡故也，今七日以後，病反甚不除者，欲為再經病也，再經病者，是陰陽諸經絡重受病故也。（並出第七卷中）

仲景傷寒論：療傷寒不大便六七日，頭痛有熱，與承氣湯，其人小便反清者，知不在裡，仍在表也，當須發汗，若頭痛者必衄血，宜桂枝湯方。

### Literal Translation

(Same as Fan Wang; from the third volume. In Zhongjing's Shanghan Lun it is named Chaihu Jianggui; the Xiao Chaihu Tang combining the seven ingredients Chaihu, Renshen, Gancao, Huangqin, Banxia, Shengjiang, and Dazao is exactly it. Same as Yuhan and Qianjin Yi.)

The Bingyuan: On the sixth day of cold damage, the jueyin receives the disease. The jueyin is the channel of the liver; its vessel follows the genitals and connects with the liver, therefore on the sixth day of the disease there is vexation, fullness, and contraction of the scrotum. This is then yin and yang both receiving the disease, and the poisonous qi is in the chest, therefore one can purge and it heals.

Again, on the seventh day of cold damage, the taiyang disease declines and the headache heals somewhat. On the seventh day of cold damage, the disease by law should heal somewhat, because the various yin and yang channels have finished transmitting. Now, after the seventh day, those whose disease instead becomes severe and is not removed are about to become a re-channel disease. The re-channel disease is because the various yin and yang channels and collaterals receive the disease again. (Both from the seventh volume)
