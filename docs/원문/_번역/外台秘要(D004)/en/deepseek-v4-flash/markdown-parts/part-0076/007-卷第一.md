## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

若大便硬，小便自利者，附子白朮湯。

桂枝附子湯方桂心（四兩）　附子（三枚炮去皮）　生薑（三兩）　甘草（二兩炙）　大棗（十二枚擘）

上五味切，以水六升，煮取二升，去滓，溫分三服。忌生蔥豬肉海藻菘菜。

### Literal Translation

If the stool is hard and the urine is freely self-benefiting, it is the Fuzi Baizhu Tang (Aconite and Atractylodes Decoction).

The Guizhi Fuzi Tang formula: Guixin (cinnamon bark, 4 liang), Fuzi (aconite, 3 pieces, roasted, skin removed), Shengjiang (fresh ginger, 3 liang), Gancao (licorice, 2 liang, roasted), Dazao (jujube, 12 pieces, broken).

The above five ingredients, cut. With 6 sheng of water, boil and take 2 sheng, remove the dregs, and take warm in three divided doses. Avoid raw scallion, pork, seaweed, and cabbage.
