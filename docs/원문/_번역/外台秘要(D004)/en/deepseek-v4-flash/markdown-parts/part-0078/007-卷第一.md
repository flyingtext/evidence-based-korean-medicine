## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

大黃（去皮四兩）　陳枳實（炙五枚）　芒硝（三合）　濃朴（半斤）

上四味切，以水一斗，先煮二物，取五升，去滓，納大黃煮取二升去滓，納芒硝煮一二沸，分為兩服，初一服便得利者止，後服不必盡劑。（千金方並翼同出第五卷中）

又太陽病過經十余日，及二三下之後，四五日柴胡證仍在者，先與小柴胡湯，嘔不止，心下急，（一云嘔止小安）郁郁微煩者，為未解也，可與大柴胡湯下之即愈方。

柴胡（半斤）　黃芩　芍藥（各三兩）　半夏（半斤水洗）　大棗（十三枚擘）　生薑（五兩）　枳實（四枚上七味切，以水一斗二升煮至六升，去滓，更煎，取三升溫服一升，日三服，一方加大黃二兩，今不加大黃恐不名為大柴胡湯也。

### Literal Translation

Dahuang (rhubarb, skin removed, 4 liang), Chen Zhishi (old immature bitter orange, roasted, 5 pieces), Mangxiao (Glauber's salt, 3 ge), Houpo (magnolia bark, half jin).

The above four ingredients, cut. With 1 dou of water, first boil the two ingredients, take 5 sheng, remove the dregs, add the Dahuang, boil and take 2 sheng, remove the dregs, add the Mangxiao, boil for one or two bubbling, and divide into two doses. On the first dose, if one obtains a benefit, stop; the later dose need not finish the whole amount. (Same as the Qianjin Fang and Yi; from the fifth volume.)

Again, in taiyang disease passing the channel for more than ten days, and after two or three purgings, if on the fourth or fifth day the Chaihu signs are still present, first give the Xiao Chaihu Tang. If the vomiting does not stop, the region below the heart is urgent (one version: the vomiting stops and there is slight ease), and there is gloomy slight vexation, it is not yet resolved; one can give the Da Chaihu Tang (Major Bupleurum Decoction) to purge it, and it heals. Formula:

Chaihu (bupleurum, half jin), Huangqin (scutellaria), Shaoyao (peony, each 3 liang), Banxia (pinellia, half jin, washed in water), Dazao (jujube, 13 pieces, broken), Shengjiang (fresh ginger, 5 liang), Zhishi (immature bitter orange, 4 pieces. The above seven ingredients, cut. With 1 dou 2 sheng of water, boil until 6 sheng, remove the dregs, and again decoct, take 3 sheng, take 1 sheng warm, three times daily. One version adds 2 liang of Dahuang; if one does not add Dahuang now, I fear it cannot be named the Da Chaihu Tang.
