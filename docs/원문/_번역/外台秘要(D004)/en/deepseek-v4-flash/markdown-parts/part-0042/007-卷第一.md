## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

發表以桂枝；溫裡宜四逆。（四逆湯在第二卷傷寒不得眠部中。三○通按第二卷傷寒不得眠部並無四逆湯味者是也　三味方惟小便不利部內有四逆散加減法）

華佗曰：夫傷寒始得，一日在皮，當摩膏火灸即愈。

### Literal Translation

To release the exterior use Guizhi; to warm the interior use Sini (Four Counterflows). (The Sini Decoction is in the second volume, in the section on cold damage with inability to sleep. Note: the second volume's section on cold damage with inability to sleep has no Sini Decoction ingredients; the three-ingredient formula only has the Sini Powder with additions and subtractions in the section on difficult urination.)

Hua Tuo says: When cold damage is first contracted, on the first day it is in the skin; one should rub with ointment and apply fire moxibustion, and it heals.
