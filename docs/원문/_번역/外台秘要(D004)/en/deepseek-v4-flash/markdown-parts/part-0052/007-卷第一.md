## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

人性自有難使汗出者，非但病使其然，蒸之無不汗出也。（短劇千金同蒸法在此卷崔氏日數部巾阮河南法又有桃葉湯熏其法在第三卷天行部中文仲方支太素法是）

范汪論：「黃帝問於岐伯曰：『人傷於寒而得病，何以反更為熱。』岐伯曰：『極陰變陽，寒盛則生熱，熱盛則生寒。

### Literal Translation

There are people whose nature makes it hard to sweat; it is not only the disease that makes it so, but steaming them, there is none that does not sweat. (Same as Duanju and Qianjin. The steaming method is in the Cui family section on counting days in this volume, the Ruan Henan method; there is also the Taoye Decoction to fumigate, its method in the third volume, in the epidemic section, the Wenzhong formula, the Zhi Taisu method.)

Fan Wang's discourse: "The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, saying: 'When a person is injured by cold and gets the disease, why does it instead become heat?' Qibo said: 'Extreme yin changes into yang; when cold is exuberant, heat is born, and when heat is exuberant, cold is born.'"
