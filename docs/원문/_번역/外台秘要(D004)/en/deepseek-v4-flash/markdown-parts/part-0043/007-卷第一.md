## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

若不解者至二日在膚，可法鐵服解肌散發汗，汗出即愈。若不解者至三日在肌，復發汗則愈。若不解者，止。

### Literal Translation

If it does not resolve, reaching the second day it is in the dermis; one may take the Jieji Powder (Resolving the Muscle Powder) with the iron method to sweat, and once sweat comes out it heals. If it does not resolve, reaching the third day it is in the flesh; sweating again, it heals. If it does not resolve, stop.
