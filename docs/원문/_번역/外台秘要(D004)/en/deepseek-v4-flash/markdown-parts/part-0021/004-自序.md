## 自序

### Source Text

又謂：世代之推遷也，風氣之殊尚也，陳轍可以適時乎？余曰：不師其法，可也。且謂同病而異方也，同方而異治也，毫厘不幾千裡乎？余曰：三部微妙，五臟精華，察之在目，合色脈而後定方，求其曲當可也。總之，以方為方，方遂一成而不易；以矩為方，方乃萬變而不窮。

### Literal Translation

He also said: Generations push forward and shift, and customs differ in what they honor; can the old wheel ruts fit the times? I said: One need not take its method as teacher. He also said: The same disease has different formulas, and the same formula has different treatments; is not a hair's breadth nearly a thousand li? I said: The three parts are subtle, and the essence of the five viscera; the examination lies in the eyes, and only after combining the complexion and pulse does one fix the formula; one can seek its fitting exactly. In sum, if one makes formulas by formulas, the formulas are then fixed once and do not change; if one makes formulas by the measuring square, the formulas then change ten thousand times and are not exhausted.
