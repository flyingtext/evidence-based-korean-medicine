## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

九日少陽病衰，耳聾微聞。十日太陰病衰，腹減如故，則思飲食。十一日少陰病衰，渴止不滿，舌乾已而嚏。

### Literal Translation

On the ninth day the shaoyang disease declines and the deafness is slightly heard. On the tenth day the taiyin disease declines, the abdomen decreases as before, and then one thinks of food and drink. On the eleventh day the shaoyin disease declines, the thirst stops and there is no fullness, the dry tongue has ceased, and one sneezes.
