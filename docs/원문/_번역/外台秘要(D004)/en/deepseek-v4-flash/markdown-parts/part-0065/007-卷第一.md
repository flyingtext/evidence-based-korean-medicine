## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

十二日厥陰病衰，囊縱少腹微下，大氣皆去，病日已矣。

帝曰：治之奈何。岐伯曰：治之各通其臟。

### Literal Translation

On the twelfth day the jueyin disease declines, the scrotum relaxes and the lower abdomen slightly descends, the great qi all departs, and the disease ends day by day.

The Emperor says: How is it treated? Qibo says: In treating it, each one frees its own viscus.
