## 重刻《外台秘要》序

### Source Text

嘗謂良醫與良相功齊，總之能為民生造命耳。予武夫也，不知相亦不知醫，惟是人之有疾，不啻已出，值此滄桑變故之際，民遭兵火，若患熱烈之症，予濫任撫綏，尤當視民如傷恨，不能人人而投以清涼之劑。自入新安以來，每問民間疾苦，見程生衍道者業儒而通醫，博施濟眾久矣，更構唐時王刺史《外台秘要》四十卷，詳訂而剞劂之。

### Literal Translation

I have often said that a good physician and a good minister have equal merit; in sum, they can create life for the people. I am a military man; I know neither ministers nor medicine. Only this: when people have illness, it is no less than if it came from myself. At this time of great upheaval and change, the people have suffered war and fire; if they suffer from hot and intense symptoms, I, having been rashly entrusted with pacification, should especially regard the people as if wounded, and regret that I cannot give everyone a cooling remedy. Since entering Xin'an, I have asked about the hardships of the people, and saw that Master Cheng Yandao, a Confucian by profession who is versed in medicine, has long been broadly giving and saving the multitude. He further obtained the forty volumes of the 《Waitai Miyao》 by Prefect Wang of the Tang dynasty, and carefully corrected and carved them.
