## 重刻《外台秘要方》序

### Source Text

而今之學人，來輒問曰：欲就師，授十日，足乎？一若是其亟也，吾雖欲傳，其何從而傳？嗚呼﹗天下之學，大抵然耳，獨醫也乎？古之君子其於道也，其始也，急求人而已，及其求人不可得，則不得不有事於書，以冀其長存天地之間，其於書也，不必自著書，而章隱繼絕圖存古人之書。古人之書，其賴有心之後人以獲行世者，多矣。初不必後人之盡有心、盡好學而能讀也，而其書則不忍以不存，以為萬有一人焉，有心好學，而一能讀是書也，則是一書為遂不虛存也，更百世而當有人焉，出而視今日之敬通程子，亦猶程子之視唐宋王刺史、孫中丞兩先生也，則今日之及程子之門而欲以十日盡學其所學人，程子其亦可以謝之而無所負矣。

### Literal Translation

Yet the learners of today, when they come, immediately ask: If I go to a teacher and am taught for ten days, is that enough? If they are all this hasty, then even though I wish to transmit, from where can I transmit? Alas! The learning of the world is generally like this; is it only medicine? The gentleman of antiquity, in regard to the Way, at the beginning only urgently sought a person; and when he could not obtain a person to seek, then he had no choice but to take up the matter of books, hoping that they would long endure between Heaven and Earth. In regard to books, he did not necessarily need to write books himself, but to make manifest the hidden and continue the cut off, and to seek to preserve the books of the ancients. The books of the ancients, relying on later people of mind to obtain circulation in the world, are many. At first it is not necessary that all later people have mind and all love learning and be able to read; but the book cannot bear not to be preserved, thinking that if there is one person in ten thousand who has mind and loves learning and is able to read this book, then this one book is not preserved in vain. Even after a hundred generations there should be a person who comes forth and looks at today's Master Cheng Jingtong, just as Master Cheng looks at the two gentlemen, Prefect Wang and Censor-in-Chief Sun of the Tang and Song. Then to the person who comes to Master Cheng's gate today wishing to learn all he has learned in ten days, Master Cheng can also decline him without owing anything.
