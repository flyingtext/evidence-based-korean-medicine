## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

（仲景千金方同）

陳廩丘云：或問得病連服湯藥發汗，汗不出如之何？答曰：醫經云連發汗，汗不出者死吾思可蒸之，如蒸中風法，蒸濕之氣於外迎之，不得不汗出也。後以問張苗，苗云：曾有人作事疲極汗出臥單簟中冷得病，但苦寒蜷諸醫與丸散湯，四日之內，凡八發汗，汗不出，苗令燒地布桃葉蒸之，即得大汗，於被中就粉傳身極燥。乃𧺫便愈，後數以此發汗，汗皆出也。

### Literal Translation

(Same as Zhongjing and the Qianjin Fang)

Chen Linqiu says: Someone asks, if one gets the disease and takes decoctions continuously to sweat, and the sweat does not come out, what to do? I answer: The medical classic says that if one sweats continuously and the sweat does not come out, one dies. I think one can steam it, like the method of steaming wind stroke; meeting the steaming damp qi from outside, one cannot but sweat. Later I asked Zhang Miao, and Miao said: Once there was a man who, exhausted from work, sweated and lay in a single mat in the cold and got the disease, only suffering from cold and curling up. The various physicians gave pills, powders, and decoctions, and within four days, in all eight sweatings, the sweat did not come out. Miao had the ground burned and peach leaves spread, and steamed him, and he immediately got a great sweat; in the quilt he applied powder to the body and it became extremely dry. Then he rose and healed. Later he used this to sweat several times, and the sweat all came out.
