## 自序

### Source Text

題曰《秘要》，要也。自宋皇　詔諭刊布，後無復鋟梓以廣其傳，豈非沿習時尚而探源證本者之寡其儔哉﹗夫天下何事不宜師古，文則六經之外必追秦、漢，字則篆　而後必法鐘、王，至不然？昔祖訥云：辨釋經典，小有異同，不足以傷風教，湯藥小小不達，則後人此余亟欲以《外台秘要》公諸海內之深意也。向購寫本，訛缺頗多，因復殫力校　。

### Literal Translation

He titled it 《Miyao》 (Arcane Essentials), meaning the essential. Since the Song emperor's edict to publish it, no one has again carved it to broaden its transmission. Is this not because those who follow the current fashion and seek the source and verify the root are few among their kind! What affair in the world should not take the ancients as teacher? In writing, beyond the Six Classics one must pursue Qin and Han; in calligraphy, after the seal script one must model on Zhong and Wang. Is it not so to the utmost? Formerly Zu Ne said: In distinguishing and explaining the classics, small differences and similarities are not enough to harm the moral influence; but if the decoctions and medicines are slightly not understood, then later people... This is my deep intention in urgently wishing to make the 《Waitai Miyao》 public within the seas. Previously I purchased a manuscript copy, which had quite many errors and omissions, so I again exhausted my strength to collate it.
