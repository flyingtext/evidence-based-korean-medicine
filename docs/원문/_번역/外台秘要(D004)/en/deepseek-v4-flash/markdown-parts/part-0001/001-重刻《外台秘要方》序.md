## 重刻《外台秘要方》序

### Source Text

程子敬通，醇儒也，而旁通於醫。吾於醫固未之學，而一接程子指脈說病則目無全人，微言高論疊見層生，聞未聞，解未解。程子之於醫幾乎﹗道又見其醫人也，雖極賤貧，但一接手則必端問詳審，反復精思，未嘗有厭怠之色。

### Literal Translation

Master Cheng Jingtong is a pure Confucian scholar, and he is also versed in medicine. I have never studied medicine myself, but once I encountered Master Cheng taking the pulse and explaining illness, there was no whole person in my eyes; subtle words and lofty discourses appeared in layers, hearing what I had not heard and understanding what I had not understood. How nearly Master Cheng approaches medicine! I also saw him treating people: even for the extremely poor and lowly, once he took up a case he would always ask carefully and examine thoroughly, reflect and think precisely, and never show a look of weariness or sloth.
