## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

（出玉函經一方芒硝三合桑螵蛸五個大黃四分煮取一升半溫服五合微下愈本云柴胡湯再服以解其外取愈一方外加芒硝大黃桑螵蛸是也）

### Literal Translation

(From the Yuhan Jing. One version: 3 ge of Mangxiao, 5 Sangpiaoxiao (mantis egg cases), and 4 fen of Dahuang, boiled to take 1.5 sheng, taken warm 5 ge, slightly purging, heals. Originally it says to take the Chaihu Tang twice to resolve the exterior and obtain healing. One version adds Mangxiao, Dahuang, and Sangpiaoxiao on the outside; this is it.)
