## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

病者過日，不以時下之，熱不得泄，亦若得病無熱，但狂言煩躁不安，精采言語與人不相主當者，勿以火迫之，但以五苓散一方寸匕水和服之。（五苓散仲景云豬苓散是也，在第二卷傷寒中風部中，千金翼方五味者是也）當以新汲井水強飲一升許，若一升半可至二升益佳，令以指刺喉中吐之，病隨手愈，不即吐者，此病輩多不善，勿強與水，水停即結心下也。當更以余藥吐之，皆令相主當者不爾即危，若此病不急，以豬苓散吐解之者，其死殆速耳，亦可先吐去毒物及法針之尤佳。

### Literal Translation

If the patient passes the days and is not purged in time, the heat cannot be discharged. Also, if one gets the disease without heat, but only with raving, restlessness, and agitation, and the spirit and speech do not correspond with the person, do not press it with fire, but only take one square-inch spoon of Wuling Powder (Five-Ingredient Poria Powder) mixed with water. (The Wuling Powder is what Zhongjing calls the Zhuling Powder; it is the one of five ingredients in the second volume, in the section on cold damage with wind stroke, in the Qianjin Yifang.) One should make him drink about one sheng of freshly drawn well water; if one and a half sheng, up to two sheng, is even better. Have him stick a finger into the throat to vomit, and the disease heals with the hand. If he does not vomit at once, this kind of disease is mostly not good; do not force water on him, for if the water stops it will bind below the heart. One should make him vomit with other medicines, all making them correspond; otherwise it is dangerous. If this disease is not urgent, and one does not vomit and resolve it with the Zhuling Powder, his death is nearly swift. One may also first vomit out the poisonous matter and needle with the method; this is even better.
