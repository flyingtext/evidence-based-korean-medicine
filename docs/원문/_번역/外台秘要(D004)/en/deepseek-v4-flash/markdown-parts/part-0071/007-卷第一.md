## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

忌海藻菘菜，經心錄同。（出第十卷中，張仲景傷寒論三日亦可服麻黃湯此云調胃承氣湯非也此方但治三日發汗不解蒸蒸發熱者）

病源：傷寒四日，太陰受病，太陰者，脾之經也，為三陰之首，是知三日以前，陽受病訖，傳之於陰，而太陰受病焉，其脈絡於脾，主於喉嗌，故得病四日腹滿而嗌干，其病在胸膈也，故可吐而愈。

又傷寒五日，少陰受病，少陰者，腎之經也，其脈貫腎絡肺系於舌，故得病五日，口燥舌乾，渴而引飲也，其病在腹，故可下而愈矣。

### Literal Translation

Avoid seaweed and cabbage; same as the Jingxin Lu. (From the tenth volume. Zhang Zhongjing's Shanghan Lun: on the third day one may also take the Mahuang Tang; saying this is the Tiaowei Chengqi Tang is wrong. This formula only treats those who, after three days of sweating, do not resolve and have steaming fever.)

The Bingyuan: On the fourth day of cold damage, the taiyin receives the disease. The taiyin is the channel of the spleen, being the head of the three yin. From this one knows that before three days, the yang has finished receiving the disease and transmits it to the yin, and then the taiyin receives the disease. Its vessel connects with the spleen and governs the throat, therefore on the fourth day of the disease there is abdominal fullness and dry throat; the disease is in the chest and diaphragm, therefore one can vomit and it heals.

Again, on the fifth day of cold damage, the shaoyin receives the disease. The shaoyin is the channel of the kidney; its vessel pierces the kidney, connects with the lung system, and ties to the tongue, therefore on the fifth day of the disease there is dry mouth, dry tongue, thirst, and drawing in drink; the disease is in the abdomen, therefore one can purge and it heals.
