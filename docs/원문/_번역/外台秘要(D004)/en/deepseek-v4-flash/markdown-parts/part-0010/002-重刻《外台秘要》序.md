## 重刻《外台秘要》序

### Source Text

程生一士子藉醫而行相道，然與予撫民一念默相吻合，又以醫而兼將矣，曷可與庸人道也。天下後世能按此而擴充之，又何病民之與有？

<ins>欽命提督徽寧池太等處地方總兵官都督同知榆林張天祿題</ins>

### Literal Translation

Master Cheng, a single scholar, uses medicine to practice the way of the minister; yet it secretly coincides with my single thought of pacifying the people, and he also combines the general with medicine. How can this be spoken of to common people? If the later generations of the world can follow this and expand it, then what harm to the people would there be?

<ins>Inscribed by Zhang Tianlu of Yulin, by imperial command Commander-in-Chief of the troops of Huining, Chitai and other places, and Assistant Commander-in-Chief</ins>
