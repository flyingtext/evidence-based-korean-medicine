## 自序

### Source Text

遇有疑義，則旁引類證，錄於篇側，其無文可征者，不敢強釋，以俟解人。十載始竣厥工。客閱而謂余曰：奧義之難析也，圓機之莫辨也，淺識可以漫試乎？余曰：用其所信，闕也。

### Literal Translation

When I met doubtful meanings, I cited analogous evidence from the side and recorded it beside the chapter; for those that could not be verified by any text, I did not dare to force an explanation, but waited for a person who could interpret. After ten years I finally completed the work. A guest, reading it, said to me: The profound meaning is hard to analyze, and the perfect mechanism is hard to distinguish; can shallow knowledge try it recklessly? I said: Use what one trusts, and leave the gaps.
