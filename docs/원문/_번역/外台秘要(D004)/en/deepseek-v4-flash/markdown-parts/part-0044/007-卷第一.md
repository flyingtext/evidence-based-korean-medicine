## 諸論傷寒八家合一十六首

### Source Text

勿復發汗也至四日在胸，宜服藜蘆丸微吐則愈。若更困，藜蘆丸不能吐者服小豆瓜蒂散吐之則愈，視病尚未醒，醒者復一法針之。（藜蘆丸近用損人不錄之瓜蒂散在卷末雜療中范汪方二味者是也）五日在腹六日入胃，入胃則可下也。

### Literal Translation

Do not sweat again. Reaching the fourth day it is in the chest; one should take the Lilu Pill (Veratrum Pill) and slight vomiting cures it. If it is more severe, and the Lilu Pill cannot make one vomit, take the Xiaodou Guadi Powder (Small Bean and Melon Stalk Powder) to vomit, and it heals. See whether the disease has not yet awakened; if it awakens, needle it with another method. (The Lilu Pill, being recently used, harms people, so it is not recorded; the Guadi Powder is the one of two ingredients in Fan Wang's formula in the miscellaneous treatments at the end of the volume.) On the fifth day it is in the abdomen, and on the sixth day it enters the stomach; once it enters the stomach, one can purge it.
