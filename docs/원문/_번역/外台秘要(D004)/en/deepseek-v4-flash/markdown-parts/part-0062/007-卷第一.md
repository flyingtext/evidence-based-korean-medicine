## 論傷寒日數病源並方二十一首

### Source Text

四日太陰受之，太陰脈布胃中絡於隘，故腹滿而隘干。五日少陰受之，少陰脈貫腎絡肺系舌本，故口燥舌乾而渴。六日厥陰受之，厥陰脈循陰器而終於肝，故煩滿而囊縮。

### Literal Translation

On the fourth day the taiyin receives it; the taiyin vessel spreads in the stomach and connects with the throat, therefore there is abdominal fullness and dry throat. On the fifth day the shaoyin receives it; the shaoyin vessel pierces the kidney, connects with the lung system, and ties to the root of the tongue, therefore there is dry mouth, dry tongue, and thirst. On the sixth day the jueyin receives it; the jueyin vessel follows the genitals and finally connects with the liver, therefore there is vexation, fullness, and contraction of the scrotum.
