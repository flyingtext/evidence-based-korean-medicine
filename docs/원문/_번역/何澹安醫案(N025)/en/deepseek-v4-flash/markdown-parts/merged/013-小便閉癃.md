## 小便閉癃

### Source Text

外症已愈，小溲仍然不爽，脈象亦未柔軟，大補不宜（陰囊潰爛。）

命門氣虧，兼之中虛氣陷，清氣不升，小便溺塞，診得六脈沉弱，所以涼劑不宜多服。

### Literal Translation

The external condition has already healed, yet urination is still not free, and the pulse is also not yet soft — great supplementing is not appropriate (the scrotum is ulcerated and rotten).

Sheng Yangshen, Danpi, Bixie, Guiban, Gancao shao, Zhi Shouwu, Chiling, Miren, Lianxu.

The qi of the Mingmen (命門) is deficient, and in addition there is middle deficiency with sinking of qi, the clear qi not rising, urination blocked and obstructed; examining the six pulses they are deep and weak, so that cooling agents should not be taken in excess.

Xi Dangshen, Shengma, Tusizi, Bixie, Hupo xie, Zhi Yuzhu, Chiling, Gancao shao, Zexie.

Damp fire descends, with pain on urination, the six pulses wiry and tight. One must use a method of separating the clear and guiding downward, and then effect may be promised.

Jinwen (rhubarb, 錦紋), Lianxu, Bixie, Niuxi, Gancao shao, Chuanlian, Guixu, Zexie, Chiling, Dongguazi.

Frequent urination with pain, the left pulse fine and soft — this is deficiency of the Shaoyin (少陰) with the bladder harboring heat. Use a method of nourishing the kidney.

Shudi 4 qian, Shengma 4 fen, honey-fried, Chi Fuling 2 qian, Chuan Bai 1 qian, sheng Gancao 4 fen, Rougui 4 fen, Bixie 1.5 qian, Baizishuang 1.5 qian, Danpi 1.5 qian, Hupo xie 3 fen, ground and taken with water.
