### Translator's Notes

- 類中 (lei zhong) rendered as 'stroke-like' to preserve the classical sense of a condition resembling wind-stroke without equating it to a modern diagnosis.
- 佐金法 (zuo jin fa) rendered literally as 'assisting metal method'.
