## 淋濁

### Literal Translation

The middle qi is insufficient, with turbid discharge descending — treat by using Sijun (四君) to secure the essence.

Zhen Xidang, Fushen, Muli, Bei Wuwei, Zhi Cao, Huai Shanyao, Bixie, Niuxi, stir-fried Baishao.

Another pill formula: the previous formula with Yuzhu, Miren, and Xianglian rou added.

Prolonged turbid discharge with deficiency of the primordial, the six pulses without strength — strengthen the middle and raise the clear, and it will naturally be comfortable.

Zhi Yuzhu, Bixie, sheng Miren, Muli, Dongguazi, Chiling, Zexie, Sha Jili (Astragalus complanatus, 沙蒺藜), Shanyao, Gancao shao (licorice tip, 甘草梢).

The spleen and kidney are both deficient, with lumbar pain and turbid discharge; unavoidably one has used the mind too much, and the sovereign fire (君火) sinks; but this illness has long entangled the Mingmen (命門), the yang qi not restraining, so that the upper pulse is deep and weak and the stools are thin and loose. I propose a method of Buzhong Yiqi (補中益氣) combined with nourishing the kidney (previously took several liang of Dahuang (rhubarb) without effect).

Zhen Xidang, Shudi, Fushen, Rougui, Zhi Cao, Zhi Yuzhu, Shengma (Cimicifuga, 升麻), Zaoren, Bixie, Xianglian.

Pill formula: remove Bixie; add Wuwei, Jinyingzi, and Ejiao; pound into pills.

Mistakenly taking medicated wine, heat pours into the bladder, with turbid discharge in the urine, prolonged without recovery, vexation and thirst with wasting of the flesh. Examining the six pulses, they are not wiry and rapid. Here use the Buzhong Yiqi (補中益氣) method with additions and subtractions.

Xi Dangshen, Shudi, Maidong, Bixie, Shengma, Lugen, Zhi Yuzhu, Fushen, Wuwei, Muli, Xianglian.

Turbid discharge with lumbar pain, the governing vessel (督脈) injured — treat by securing the essence and supplementing qi.

Da Shudi, Jinyingzi, Chuanduan, Longgu (dragon bone, 龍骨), Jing Guishen, Qianshi (Euryale, 芡實), Gouji, Duzhong.

Prolonged turbid discharge without stopping, with pain and flaccidity of the kidney region — treat by separating the clear and warming and moistening.

Xi Dangshen, Chuan Bixie, Dan Congrong, Duzhong, sheng Guishen, Da Shudi, Chi Fuling, Sha Jili, Muli, Xianglian rou.

Blood in the urine together with turbid discharge, frequent urination with pain, the left hypochondrium not harmonious — I fear there may be accumulated blood; this formula is for temporary use.

Chuanlian, Bixie, Walengzi, Gancao, Guixu, Chiling, Yuanhusuo.

Turbid discharge lingering for a long time — damp fire descending. Treat by raising the clear and separating and draining.

Yuan Shengdi, Chuan Bixie, Zhi Shengma, Chiling, Lianxu, Danpi, Gancao shao, sheng Miren, Zexie.

### Translator's Notes

- 四君 (si jun) refers to the Sijunzi Tang (Four Gentlemen Decoction); kept as 'Sijun'.
- 補中益氣 (Buzhong Yiqi) is a formula name; kept as 'Buzhong Yiqi'.
