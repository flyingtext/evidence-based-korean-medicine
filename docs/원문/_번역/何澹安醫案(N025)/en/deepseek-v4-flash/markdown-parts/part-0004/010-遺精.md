### Literal Translation

The ministerial fire (相火) is vigorous and in addition damp-heat descends, so that there is erection of the yang and seminal emission, the six pulses wiry and slippery. Use a method of nourishing and clearing, assisted by bitter draining.

Fresh Shengdi, Lianxu, Chiling, Danpi, Miren, Chuan Bai, Guiban, Bixie, Zexie, Gancao.

The seminal gate is not secure, with pain and flaccidity of the lumbar spine, the six pulses fine and soft without strength. One should astringe the essence and warm and supplement.

Stir-fried Shudi, Huai Shanyao, Chuanduan, Bei Wuwei, Xianglian rou, Zhi Yuzhu, Jinyingzi (Rosa fruit, 金櫻子), Duzhong, Bai Fuling.

Pill formula: remove Jinying (Rosa fruit); add Dangshen, Gouji, Xianyu jiao (fish glue, 線魚膠), and Sharen (Amomum, 砂仁).

The qi of the Jueyin (厥陰) is depressed, with distension of the diaphragm and dim vision, the sovereign fire (君火) pressing internally, the yin essence leaking, the pulse not soft — one should use a method of joining the heart and kidney with bitter draining (and with excessive use of the mind and palpitations, treat it as liver fire; because of overuse of the mind, taking supplementing agents had no effect).

Chuan Huanglian, Hei Shanzhi, Zexie, Lianxu, Baizishuang, Fa Banxia, Chiling, Yujin, Juye.

Kan and Li (坎離) do not join, with fright and spontaneous sweating, and recently seminal emission; the essence, qi and spirit are all deficient, the pulse empty and without strength. One must use heavy doses and supplement frequently (the feet are flaccid and walking is difficult).

Zhi Qi 3 qian, Wuwei 4 fen, honey-fried, Fushen 2 qian, calcined Muli 5 qian, Chuan Bai 1 qian, Gonggan (dried longan, 貢干) 1 liang, Shudi 8 qian, Maidong 3 qian, Zaoren 3 qian, Huai Shanyao 3 qian, Xianglian 7 grains.

Loss of blood together with seminal emission, deficiency and damage of the liver and kidney, the sinews contracted so that sitting and lying are restless, the six pulses fine and soft without strength — this belongs to lower deficiency and blood not nourishing the liver. And with leakage and spontaneous sweating, this is a condition of great deficiency; one must use heavy doses to cultivate and supplement.

Zhi Qi 2 qian, Bei Wuweizi 4 fen, Fushen 2 qian, Jin Gouji 1.5 qian, Gan Qizi 2 qian, Shudi 5 qian, Maidong rou 2 qian, Zaoren 3 qian, calcined Longchi 2 qian, Chuan Yujin 1 qian.

The heart and kidney do not join, with much dreaming and seminal emission, and in ordinary times there is blood in the stool. One should regulate with Hei Guipi (黑歸脾).

Hemoptysis recurs, and recently there is seminal emission; fortunately the pulse is not rapid and there is no wheezing — one can see that the yin aspect is not greatly depleted; it is injury to the yin collaterals. First regulate, then supplement.

The yin aspect is insufficient, and in addition damp fire rushes upward, the crown of the head sinks, with seminal emission, pain and flaccidity of the governing vessel (督脈) — treat by clearing the upper and absorbing the lower.
