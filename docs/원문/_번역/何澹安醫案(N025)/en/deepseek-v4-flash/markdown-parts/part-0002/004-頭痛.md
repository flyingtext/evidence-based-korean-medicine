### Literal Translation

Head wind (頭風) for several years, arising from deficiency of the liver and kidney, and in addition the exterior yang is empty and open; with the slightest touch of external contraction, internal wind is stirred.

Zhi Mianqi (honey-fried Astragalus, 炙綿耆), Nüzhenzi, Guishen, Shijueming, Qingyan (salt, 青鹽) a little, Da Shudi, winter mulberry leaf, Fushen, Ganjü.

Qi deficiency and weak exterior, so that guest evil easily enters, stirring the liver wind, with headache and vomiting; treat by securing the exterior and nourishing the liver.

Zhi Mianqi, Bai Guishen, Baijili, Ganjü, Hei Shanzhi, Zhi Shouwu, Fa Banxia, Shijueming, Baishao, Sangye.

Distension of the diaphragm and headache — heat depression of the Shaoyang (少陽); guard against abdominal fullness.

Chaihu, Shijueming, Yujin, Xinhui pi, Niuxi, Hei Shanzhi, Gualoupi (Trichosanthes peel, 栝蔞皮), Baishao, Zexie, Foshou (Buddha's hand, 佛手).

Exterior deficiency with headache, and when severe there is abdominal diarrhea and vomiting. This is the liver wind insulting the earth (spleen); temporarily use the Wendan method (溫膽法).

Chuanlian, Fa Banxia, Fuling, Chiju, Hei Shanzhi, Jiao Zhu (stir-fried Atractylodes, 焦術), Shijueming, Guang Huo, Baishao.
