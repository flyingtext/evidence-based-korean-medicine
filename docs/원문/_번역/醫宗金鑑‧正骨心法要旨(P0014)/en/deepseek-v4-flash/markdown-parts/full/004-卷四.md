### Source Text

今之正骨科，即古跌打損傷之證也。專從血論，須先辨或有瘀血停積，或為亡血過多，然後施以內治之法，庶不有誤也。夫皮不破而內損者，多有瘀血；破肉傷䐃，每致亡血過多。

### Literal Translation

The present specialty of bone-setting (正骨科) is the condition of the ancient injuries from falls and blows (跌打損傷). It is treated entirely from the standpoint of blood; one must first distinguish whether there is stagnant blood (瘀血) accumulated, or whether there is excessive loss of blood (亡血), and only then apply the method of internal treatment, so that there will be no error. When the skin is not broken but there is internal injury, there is often stagnant blood; when the flesh is broken and the muscle (䐃) is injured, it frequently leads to excessive loss of blood.
