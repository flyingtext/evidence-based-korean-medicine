## 旋臺骨

### Source Text

### Literal Translation

The Xuantai bone (旋臺骨), also called the jade pillar bone (玉柱骨), is the three sections of the neck bone at the back of the head, also called the heavenly pillar bone (天柱骨). When this bone is injured, it is divided into four conditions: first, falling from a high place, causing the neck bone to insert into the cavity while the left and right still move, treat with the lifting-neck method (提項法); second, being struck, with the head low and unable to rise, treat with the straightening method (端法); third, falling injury, with the left and right tilted and askew, treat with the aligning method (整法); fourth, falling injury, with the face upturned and the head unable to hang down, or the sinews lengthened and the bone misaligned, or the sinews gathered, or the sinews stiff and the bone following the head low, treat with the four methods of pushing (推), straightening (端), rejoining (續), and aligning (整). In all treatment, when facing the case, ask whether the person was injured by falling from a cart or horse and spraining, or fell from a high place and broke, or was struck heavily and fell; then ask whether the person desires food and drink or does not desire food and drink, or whether the four limbs are uninjured and the spirit is not diminished, or the spirit is scanty, or can sit up and walk, or is drowsy and does not speak, or the pain does not stop, with stasis accumulating and congealing, hard swelling, and sinew distension; in all cases one should internally take Zheng Gu Zi Jin Dan (正骨紫金丹), externally apply Wan Ling Ointment (萬靈膏), and also wash with Hai Tong Pi Tang (海桐皮湯), and moxibustion-iron with Ding Tong San (定痛散).
