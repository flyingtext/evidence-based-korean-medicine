## 鎖子骨

### Source Text

鎖子骨，經名拄骨。橫臥於兩肩缺盆之外，其兩端外接肩解。擊打損傷，或騎馬乘車，因取物偏墜於地，斷傷此骨，用手法先按胸，再將肩端向內合之，揉摩斷骨令其復位，然後用帶掛臂於項，勿令搖動。

### Literal Translation

The clavicle (鎖子骨) is called the pillar bone (拄骨) in the classics. It lies horizontally outside the supraclavicular fossae (缺盆) of both shoulders, and its two ends connect externally to the shoulder joints (肩解). When this bone is broken by blows, or when one falls sideways to the ground while reaching for something from a horse or carriage, use manual technique: first press the chest, then bring the shoulder end inward to close it, knead and rub the broken bone to restore it to position, and then use a band to hang the arm from the neck, and do not let it move.
