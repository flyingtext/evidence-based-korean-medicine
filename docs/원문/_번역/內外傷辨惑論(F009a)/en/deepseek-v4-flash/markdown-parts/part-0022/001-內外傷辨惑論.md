## 論酒客病。

### Source Text

夫酒者，大熱有毒，氣味俱陽，乃無形之物也，若傷之，止當發散汗出則愈矣，此最妙法也，其次莫如利小便，二者乃上下分消其濕，何酒病之有？今之酒病者，往往服酒癥丸大熱之藥下之，又有用牽牛大黃下之者，是無形元氣受病，反下有形陰血，乖誤甚矣，酒性大熱，已傷元氣而復重瀉之，況亦損腎水真陰，及有形陰血俱為不足，如此則陰血愈虛，真水愈弱，陽毒之熱大旺，反增其陰火，是謂元氣消亡，七神無依，折人長命，不然則虛損之病成矣，金匱要略云：酒疸下之，久久為黑疸，慎不可犯此戒，不若令上下分消其濕，葛花解醒湯主之。

葛花解酲湯：

白豆蔻仁，縮砂仁，葛花（各五錢），乾生薑，神麯（炒黃），澤瀉，白朮（各二錢），橘皮（去白），豬苓（去皮），人參（去蘆），白茯苓（各一錢五分），木香（五分），蓮花青皮（去穰三分）。

右為極細末秤和勻，每服三錢匕，白湯調下，但得微汗，酒病去矣，此蓋不得已而用之，豈可恃賴，日日飲酒，此藥氣味辛辣，偶因酒病服之則不損元氣，何者敵酒病故也，若頻服之，損人天年。

除濕散：

治傷馬乳并牛羊酪水一切冷物。

神麯（炒黃一兩），茯苓（七錢），車前子（炒香），澤瀉（各五錢），半夏（洗湯），乾生薑（各三錢），甘草（炙），紅花（各二錢）。

右同為極細末，每服三錢匕，白湯調下，食前。

五苓散：

治傷寒溫熱病，表裏未解，頭痛發熱，口燥咽乾，煩渴飲水，或水入即吐，或小便不利，及汗出表解，煩渴不止者宜服之，又治霍亂吐利，燥渴引飲之證。

澤瀉（二兩五錢），豬苓，茯苓，白朮（各一兩五錢），桂（一兩）。

右為細末，每服二錢，熱湯調下，不計時候，服訖多飲熱湯，有汗出即愈。

又治瘀熱在裏，身熱黃疸，濃煎茵陳蒿湯調下，食前服之。

如疸發渴及中暑引飲，亦可用水調服之。

### Literal Translation

Now wine is greatly hot and poisonous; in qi and flavor both it is yang; it is a thing without form. If [one] is injured by it, [one] should only diffuse and issue, and when perspiration issues [the disease] is cured — this is the most excellent method. Next to it, nothing surpasses promoting urination. These two are the separate dispersal of its dampness above and below; what then is there of wine disease? The wine-disease [sufferers] of today often take the greatly hot medicament Jiu Zheng Wan to drain down, and some use qian niu [and] da huang to drain down — this is [injury by a thing] without form, the Original Qi receiving the disease, on the contrary draining down the formed yin blood; the error is very great. Wine's nature is greatly hot; it has already injured the Original Qi and one again heavily drains it; moreover, also damaging the true yin of the kidney water, and both becoming insufficient with the formed yin blood — being so, the yin blood becomes the more vacuous, the true water the more weakened, and the poison-heat of the yang toxin greatly flourishes, on the contrary augmenting its yin fire. This is called the Original Qi disappearing and perishing, with the seven spirits having nothing on which to rely, cutting short a person's long life. If not so, then the disease of vacuity depletion is completed. The Jinkui Yaolue states: "[In] wine jaundice, [if one] drains it, for long [it becomes] black jaundice." One must be careful not to violate this admonition. It is not as good as causing its dampness to be separately dispersed above and below; Ge Hua Jie Xing Tang governs. [Note: the title in the source reads 解醒/解酲 both meaning 'to sober up from drink.']
