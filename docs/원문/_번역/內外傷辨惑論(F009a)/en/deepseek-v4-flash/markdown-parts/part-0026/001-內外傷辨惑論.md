## 重明木鬱則達之之理。

### Source Text

或曰：食盛填塞於胸中，為之窒塞也，令吐以去其所傷之物，物去則安，胸中者，太陰肺之分野，木鬱者，遏於厥陰肝木於下，故以吐伸之，以舒暢陽和風木之氣也，此吐乃瀉出太陰之塞，何謂木鬱？請問其說，答曰：此大神靈之問，非演說大道，不能及於此天地之間，六合之丙，惟水與火耳，火者，陽也，升浮之象也，在天為體，在地為用，水者，陰土也，降沉之象也，在地為體，在天為殞殺收藏之用也，其氣上下交則以成八卦矣，以醫書言之，則是升浮降沉溫涼寒熱四時也，以應八卦，若天火在上，地水在下，則是天地不交，陰陽不相輔也，是萬物之道大易之理絕滅矣，故經言：獨陽不生，獨陰不長，天地陰陽何交會矣，故曰：陽本根於陰，陰本根於陽，若不明根源，是不明道，故六陽之氣生於地，則曰：陽本根於陰，以人身言之，是六腑之氣生發長散於胃土之中也，既陽氣鼓舞萬象有形質之物，於天為浮散者也，物極必反，陽極變陰，既六陽升浮之力在天，其力盡是陽道終矣，所以鼓舞六陰有形之陰水在天在外也，上六無位，必歸於下，此老陽變陰之象也，是五臟之源在於天者也，天者，人之肺以應之，故曰：陰本源於陽，水出高源者是也，人之五臟，其源在肺，肺者，背也，背在天也，故足太陽膀胱寒生長，其源在申，故陰寒自此而降，以成秋收氣，寒之漸也，降至於地下，以成冬藏，伏諸六陽，在九泉之下者也，故五臟之氣生於天，以人身是五臟之氣，收降藏沉之源出於肺氣之上，其流下行，既陰氣下行，沉墜萬化，有形質之物皆收藏於地，為降沉者也，物極必反，陰極變陽，既六陰降沉之力在地，其力既盡，是陰道終矣，是老陰變陽，乃初九無位，是一歲四時之氣終而復始，為上下者也，莫知其紀，如環無端，且太陰者，肺金收降之氣，當居下體，今反在於上，抑遏厥陰風木，反居於下，是不得上升也，故曰：木鬱，故令其吐出窒塞有形土化之物，使太陰秋肺收於下體，復其本以衰之，始上升手足厥陰之木，元氣以伸其舒暢上升之志得其所矣，又況金能克木，以吐伐之，則金衰矣，金者，其道當降，是塞因塞用，歸其本矣，居於上則遏其本，故以吐伸之，乃瀉金以助木也，偏考內經中所說，木鬱則達之之義，止是食傷太陰有形之物，窒塞於胸中，克制厥陰木氣，伏潛於下，不得舒伸於上，止此耳，別無異說，以六淫有餘，運氣中論之，仲景傷寒論云：懊憹煩躁不得眠，不經汗下，謂之實煩，瓜蒂散主之，曾經妄汗妄吐妄下，謂之虛煩者，梔子豉湯主之。

老夫欲令醫者治陰陽之證，補瀉不至錯誤，病家雖不知醫，明曉所得之病當補當瀉之法，將黃帝鍼經第一卷第五篇說，形氣有餘不足當補當瀉之理錄之於前，予自注者附之。

黃帝曰：形氣之逆順奈何？岐伯答曰：形氣不足，病氣有餘，是邪勝也，急當瀉之，形氣有餘，病氣不足，急當補之，形氣不足，病氣不足，此陰陽俱不足也，不可剌之，剌之重不足，重不足則陰陽俱竭，血氣皆盡，五臟空虛，筋骨髓枯，老者絕滅，壯者不復矣，形氣有餘，病氣有餘，此謂陰陽俱有餘也，急瀉其邪，調其虛實，故曰：有餘者瀉之，不足者補之，此之謂也，故曰：刺不知逆順，真邪相搏滿者，補之則陰陽四溢，腸胃充廓，肝肺內填，陰陽相錯，虛而瀉之則經脈空虛，血氣枯竭，腸胃㒤辟，皮膚薄著，毛勝天燋子之死期，故曰：用鍼之要，在於知調陰與陽，調陰與陽，精氣乃光，合形與氣，使神內藏，故曰：上工平氣，中工亂脈，下工絕氣危生，故曰：下工不可不慎也，必審五臟變化之病，五脈之應，經絡之實虛，皮膚之柔麤而後取之也。

聖人垂慈之心已詳矣，不合立言，老夫誠恐，市井莊農山野間人，不知文理，故以俚語開解之云，但病來潮作之時，病氣精神增添者，是為病氣有餘，乃邪氣勝也，急瀉之以寒涼酸苦之劑，若病來潮作之時，神氣困弱者，為病氣不足，乃真氣不足也，急補之以辛甘溫熱之劑，不問形氣有餘，并形氣不足，只取病氣有餘不足也，不足者補之，有餘者瀉之，假令病氣有餘者，當急瀉之以寒涼之劑，為邪氣勝也，病氣不足者，急當補之以辛甘溫熱之劑，此真氣不足也，夫形氣者，氣謂口鼻中氣息也，形謂皮膚筋骨血脈也，形勝者，為有餘，消瘦者，為不足，其氣者，審口鼻中氣，勞役如故，為氣有餘也，若喘息氣促氣短或不足以息者，為不足也，故曰：形氣也，乃人之身形中氣血也，當補當瀉，全不在於此，只在病勢潮作之時，病氣增加者，是邪氣勝也，急當瀉之，如潮作之時，精神困弱，語言無力及懶語者，是真氣不足也，急當補之，若病人形氣不足，病來潮作之時，病氣亦不足，此乃陰陽俱不足也，禁用鍼，宜補之以甘藥，不可以盡劑，不灸弗已，臍下一寸五分氣海穴是也，凡用藥，若不本四時，以順為逆，四時者，是春升夏浮秋降冬沉，乃天地之升浮化降沉（化者，脾土中造化也），是為四時之宜也，但宜補之以辛甘溫熱之劑及味之薄者，諸風藥是也，此助春夏之升浮者也，此便是瀉秋收冬藏之藥也，在人之身，乃肝心也，但言瀉之以酸苦寒涼之劑并淡味滲泄之藥，此助秋冬之降沉者也，在人之身，是肺腎也，用藥者宜用此法度，慎毋忽焉。

內外傷辨卷下終。

### Literal Translation

Someone asked: "Excess food filling and blocking the chest is called obstruction (窒塞). One has one vomit to expel the injured food, and when the food is gone one is at ease. The chest is the branch territory of the taiyin lung; wood depression (木鬱) is the wood of the jueyin liver being suppressed below. Therefore one uses vomiting to stretch it out, in order to bring ease and harmony to the yang-harmonious wind-wood qi. This vomiting is the draining out of the taiyin blockage. How is it called wood depression? I beg to ask the explanation." The answer said: "This is a great, spirit-numinous question. Unless one expounds the great Way, one cannot reach this. Within heaven and earth, within the six directions, there are only water and fire. Fire is yang; it is the sign of ascending and floating; in heaven it is the substance and on earth it is the function. Water is yin-earth; it is the sign of descending and sinking; on earth it is the substance, and in heaven it is the function of killing, gathering and storing. When its qi alternates above and below, then the eight trigrams are completed. Speaking in medical terms, this is the four seasons of ascending-floating-descending-sinking and warm-cool-cold-hot, corresponding to the eight trigrams. If heaven-fire is above and earth-water is below, then heaven and earth do not mix and yin and yang do not supplement each other; this is the utter extinction of the great Way of the ten thousand things and of the great change (易). Therefore the Canon says: 'lone yang does not engender, lone yin does not grow.' How then are heaven and earth, yin and yang brought to meet? Therefore it is said: 'yang is rooted in yin, and yin is rooted in yang.' If one does not understand the root, one does not understand the Way. Therefore the six yang qi is born in earth; it is said: 'yang is rooted in yin.' Speaking in terms of the human body, this is the six bowels' qi being born, proliferating, and spreading within stomach-earth. Since the yang qi stirs up the ten thousand tangible things, in heaven it is scattering and floating; things reach their extreme and then reverse, yang reaches its extreme and changes into yin. Once the six yang's ascending-floating force is in heaven and that force is exhausted, then the yang Way has come to an end; therefore the six yin's tangible yin-water is aroused and is in heaven, outside. The uppermost six has no position and must return below; this is the sign of old yang turning into yin. This is the five viscera's source lying in heaven. Heaven is responded to by the human lung. Therefore it is said: 'the source of yin-fluid is in yang; water issues from the high source.' The human five viscera have their source in the lung; the lung is the back; the back is in heaven. Therefore foot taiyang bladder cold engenders and grows, its source being in shen (申), so yin-cold descends from this to bring about the autumn harvesting qi; this is the gradual onset of cold. Descending to reach the earth, it brings about winter storing, hiding the various yang in the nine springs below. Therefore the five viscera's qi is born in heaven; in the human body the five viscera'qi, harvesting-descending-storing-sinking, has its source issuing from above the lung qi, and its flow descends. Once the yin qi descends and sinks, sinking down the ten thousand transformations, the tangible things are all stored in the earth, as what descends and sinks. Things reach their extreme and then reverse; yin reaches its extreme and turns into yang. Once the six yin's descending-sinking force is in the earth and that force is exhausted, then the yin Way has come to an end. This is old yin turning into yang, being the 'initial nine' with no position; it is the one year's four-season qi ending and again beginning, as the above and below. None knows its boundaries; like a ring it has no end. Moreover, the taiyin, the lung-metal harvesting-descending qi, should dwell in the lower body; now instead it is above and suppresses the jueyin wind-wood, which instead dwells below and cannot ascend. Therefore it is called wood depression. Therefore one makes him vomit out the obstructing tangible earth-transformed food, causing the taiyin autumn lung to receive [it] into the lower body, restoring its root in order to weaken it, and then causing the hand-foot jueyin wood to ascend, the original qi being stretched and its intent of ease and ascent being obtained. Moreover, metal can overcome wood, and vomiting attacks it, so metal is weakened. Metal, the Way of which is to descend, is 'fill the obstruction with obstruction' (塞因塞用), returning to its root. Dwelling above, it suppresses its root, so one stretches it with vomiting; this is draining metal to assist wood. Surveying what the Inner Canon says, the meaning of 'wood depression, then reach it' is only that food injures the taiyin substantial thing, obstructs and blocks the chest, overcomes and controls the jueyin wood qi, which lies latent below and cannot unfold above; nothing more, and there is no other explanation. Discussed among the six excesses as surplus in fortune-qi theory, Zhongjing's Shanghai lun (Treatise on Cold Damage) says: vexation and fidgeting, restlessness, inability to sleep, without passage through sweating and purging, is called substantial vexation; Gualu san (Melon-Stalk Powder 瓜蒂散) governs it. After wrongful sweating, wrongful vomiting, wrongful purging, it is called vacuity vexation; Zhizi chi tang (Gardenia and Fermented Soybean Decoction 梔子豉湯) governs it.

This old man wishes the physicians to treat the yin and yang patterns such that supplementation and draining do not err; and although the patient's family does not know medicine, so that they may clearly understand the method of when the obtained disease should be supplemented and when drained, I record before this what 'Huangdi's Zhenjing' Chapter 1, Section 5 says about the principle of when form-qi is surplus or insufficient and when to supplement and drain, and append my own commentary. Huangdi said: 'What about the adverse and favorable sequence of form-qi?' Qibo answered: 'When form-qi is insufficient and disease-qi is surplus, this is the pathogen prevailing; promptly drain it. When form-qi is surplus and disease-qi is insufficient, promptly supplement it. When form-qi is insufficient and disease-qi is insufficient, this is yin and yang both being insufficient; one may not needle it, for needling aggravates the insufficiency, and aggravating the insufficiency then drains yin and yang both, blood and qi exhaust together, the five viscera become empty, sinews, bones and marrow wither; the old perish and the strong do not recover. When form-qi is surplus and disease-qi is surplus, this is called yin and yang both surplus; promptly drain the pathogen and regulate its vacuity and repletion.' Therefore it is said: 'the surplus one is drained, the insufficient one is supplemented'—this is what is meant. Therefore it is said: 'If needling does not know the adverse and favorable, and with genuine and pathogen mutually clashing, the full one is supplemented, then yin and yang overflow on all four sides, the intestines and stomach fill and distend, the liver and lung fill within, yin and yang intercross; the vacuous one is drained, then the channels and vessels become empty and depleted, blood and qi wither and dry, the intestines and stomach collapse and widen, the skin becomes thin and adherent, and the downy hair and dry scorched appearance mark the death day of the son.' Therefore it is said: 'The essential of using the needle lies in knowing how to harmonize yin and yang; harmonizing yin and yang, then the essence-qi becomes luminous; joining form with qi, so that the spirit is stored within.' Therefore it is said: 'The superior workman evens qi; the middle workman confuses the vessels; the inferior workman cuts off qi and endangers life.' Therefore it is said: 'The inferior workman must beware; one must examine the diseases of the changes of the five viscera, the responses of the five vessels, the vacuity and repletion of the channels and collaterals, and the softness or roughness of the skin, and only then take [the treatment].'

The holy one's compassionate heart has already been detailed. As for what is not suitable for establishing in words, this old man truly fears that, among the market-town, farming-village and mountain-wilderness people, no one understands literary reasoning; therefore I open it out with rustic speech, saying: Only when the illness comes in its rising tide, with disease-qi and spirit increased, is this disease-qi surplus, being the pathogen prevailing; promptly drain it with cold-cool, sour-bitter formulae. If when the illness comes in its rising tide the spirit-qi is weak and exhausted, this is disease-qi insufficiency, being genuine-qi insufficiency; promptly supplement it with pungent-sweet, warm-hot formulae, not questioning whether form-qi is surplus or form-qi is insufficient, but only taking where disease-qi is surplus or insufficient. The insufficient is supplemented; the surplus is drained. Suppose disease-qi is surplus, one should promptly drain it with cold-cool formulae, because the pathogen prevails. If disease-qi is insufficient, one should promptly supplement it with pungent-sweet, warm-hot formulae, because this is genuine-qi insufficiency. As for form-qi, qi means the breath in the mouth and nose; form means the skin, sinews, bones, blood and vessels. The full form is the surplus; the emaciated is the insufficient. As for the qi, one examines the qi in mouth and nose; if in labor it is as before, that is qi surplus. If one has panting and urgent breathing, shortness, or breath insufficient to exhale, that is insufficiency. Therefore it is said: form-qi is the blood and qi of the human form. Whether to supplement or drain does not lie entirely in this; it lies only in the rising tide of the disease's force at the time: when disease-qi increases, that is pathogen prevailing, promptly drain it; when at the rising tide the spirit-qi is weak and exhausted, speech is feeble, and one is disinclined to speak, that is genuine-qi insufficiency, promptly supplement it.
