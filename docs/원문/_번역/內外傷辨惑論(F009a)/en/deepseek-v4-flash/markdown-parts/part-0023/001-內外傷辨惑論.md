## 臨病制方。

### Source Text

至真要論云：濕淫所勝，治以苦溫，佐以甘辛，以汗為故，而止以淡泄之，得其法者，分輕重而制方，金匱要略云：腰已上腫者，發汗乃愈，腰已下腫者，當利小便，由是大病差，後腰已下有水氣者，牡蠣澤瀉散主之，又云：治濕不利小便，非其治也，制五苓散以利之，孫真人療膚革腫，以五皮散，乃述類象形之故也，水熱穴論云：上為喘呼，下為腫滿，不得臥者，標本俱病，制神祕湯以去之，活人書云：均是水氣，乾嘔微利，發熱而咳為表有水，小青龍湯加芫花主之，身體涼，表證罷咳而脇下痛，為裏有水，十棗湯主之，亦是仲景方也，易張先生云：仲景藥為萬世法號群方之祖，治雜病若神，後之醫家宗內經法，學仲景心，可以為師矣。

### Literal Translation

The Zhi Yao Lun states: "When dampness excess conquers, treat with bitter and warm, with sweet and acrid as auxiliary; take sweating as the rationale, and stop with bland percolating-draining." He who attains its method weighs heavy and light and then establishes formulae. The Jinkui Yaolue states: "For swelling above the waist, induce sweating and [it] is cured; for swelling below the waist, one should promote urination." From this, [when] after great disease recovers and below the waist there is water qi, Muli Zexie San governs. And again it says: "In treating dampness, not promoting urination is not the treatment of it." [Thus one] establishes Wu Ling San to promote urine. Sun Zhenren, treating swelling of the skin and integument, used Wu Pi San — this is the rationale of 'describing kind [of] resembling form.' The Shui Re Xue Lun states: "Above, [there is] wheezing and panting; below, swelling and fullness; when [one] cannot lie down, both root and branch are diseased." [Thus one] establishes Shen Mi Tang to remove it. The Huo Ren Shu states: "Both being water qi — dry retching, mildly promoting [bowels], fever and cough — this is water in the exterior; Xiao Qing Long Tang with yuan hua governs. Body cold, the exterior pattern finished, cough and hypochondriac flank pain — this is water in the interior; Shi Zao Tang governs." This likewise is Zhongjing's formula. Master Zhang of Yishui says: "Zhongjing's medicines are the model and regulation of ten thousand generations, called the ancestor of the many formulae; in treating miscellaneous diseases they are like spirits. The physicians of later generations revere the method of the Suwen and study the mind of Zhongjing, and may be made teachers."
