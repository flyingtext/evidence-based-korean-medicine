## 辯勞役受病表虛不作表實治之。

### Source Text

或因勞役動作，腎間陰火沸騰，事閑之際，或於陰涼處解脫衣裳，更有新沐浴於背，陰處坐臥，其陰火下行，還歸腎間皮膚腠理，極虛無陽，但風來為寒涼所遏，表虛不任其風寒，自認外感風寒，求醫解表，以重絕元氣，取禍如反掌，苟幸而免者，致虛勞氣血皆弱，不能完復，且表虛之人為風寒所遏，亦是虛邪犯表，始病一二日之間，特與外中賊邪有餘之證頗相似處，故致疑惑，請醫者，只於氣少氣盛上辨之，其外傷賊邪，必語聲前輕後重，高厲而有力，若是勞役所傷，飲食不節，表虛不足之病，必短氣氣促，上氣高喘，懶語其聲，困弱而無力至易見也，若毫釐之誤，則千里之謬，已上者辯證，別有治法，用藥正論，故作此說，分解於後。

### Literal Translation

Or, from toil and movement, the yin fire between the kidneys bubbles and boils. In a moment of leisure, or by removing one's garments in a cool place, or after a fresh bath with the back exposed, sitting and lying in a yin place, its yin fire descends and returns to the kidney, into the skin and interstices, which are extremely vacuous and without yang. Only when wind comes is [the body] suppressed and obstructed by cold and coolness; the vacuous surface cannot withstand its wind and cold, and [the patient] takes himself to have caught external wind-cold and seeks a physician to resolve the exterior, thereby heavily severing the Original Qi and courting disaster like turning over the hand. If by luck one escapes [death], [the condition] turns into vacuity-toil with both the qi and blood weakened, unable to recover fully. Moreover, a person with a vacuous surface being suppressed and obstructed by wind-cold is likewise a vacuous pathogen invading the surface. In the first one or two days of the illness, [this] is especially similar in some respects to the pattern of externally contracted bandit pathogen that is in excess, and therefore gives rise to doubt. I ask physicians to differentiate only on [the criterion of] qi being scant as against qi being vigorous. As to externally contracted bandit pathogen, the voice must be light before and heavy after, raised, strong, and forceful. As to injury from toil, and immoderate food and drink, disease of vacuous surface and insufficiency — there must be shortness of breath, hurried [breath], uprushing qi and high-pitched wheezing, lazy speech with a voice [that is] trapped, weak and without force — most easy to see. If [there is] an error of a hair's breadth, then [there comes] error by a thousand li. Regarding the above, [after] differentiating the pattern, there is a separate method of treatment and correct treatises on using medicine; therefore I have composed this explanation, decomposing it below.
