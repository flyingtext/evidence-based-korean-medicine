## 辯外傷不惡食若勞役飲食失節寒溫不適此三者皆惡食。

### Source Text

仲景傷寒論云：中風能食，傷寒不能食，二者皆口中和而不惡食，若勞役所傷，及飲食失節，寒溫不適，三者俱惡食，口不知五味，亦不知五穀之味，只此一辯，足以分內外有餘不足二證也，傷寒證雖不能食而不惡食，口中和，知五味亦知穀味，蓋無內證，則心氣和，脾氣通，知五穀之味矣。

### Literal Translation

Zhongjing's Shanghan Lun states: "In wind stroke, [the patient] can eat; in cold damage, [the patient] cannot eat. In both, the mouth is harmonious within and [the patient] does not hate food." If there is injury from toil, and loss of regulation in food and drink, and discomfort in cold and warmth, in all three [the patient] hates food, the mouth does not know the five flavors, nor does it know the flavor of the five grains. This single differentiation suffices to divide the two patterns of internal[injury] excess-insufficiency and external[injury] excess-insufficiency. Although in the cold-damage pattern [the patient] cannot eat, he does not hate food; the mouth is harmonious [within], and he knows the five flavors and also knows the flavor of grain. Because without the internal pattern, the heart qi is harmonious and the spleen qi communicates, and [the patient] knows the flavors of the five grains.
