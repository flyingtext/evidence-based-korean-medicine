## 辯證與中熱頗相似。

### Source Text

復有一節，乘天氣大熱之時，在於路途中勞役得之，或在田野間勞形得之，更或有身體薄弱，食少勞役過甚，又有修善常齋之人，胃氣久虛，而因勞役得之者，皆與陽明中熱白虎湯證相似，必肌體捫摸之壯熱，必躁熱悶亂，大惡熱渴而飲水，以勞役過甚之故，亦身疼痛，始受病之時，特與中熱外得有餘之證相似，若誤與白虎湯，旬日必死，此證脾胃大虛元氣不足，口鼻中氣皆短促而上喘，至日轉以後，是陽明得時之際，病必少減，若是外中熱之病，必到日晡之際大作讝語，其熱增加，大渴飲水，煩悶不止，其勞役不足者，皆無此證，尤易為分解，若有難決疑似之證，必當待一二日，求醫治療，必不至錯誤矣。

內外傷辨卷上終。

### Literal Translation

There is again a [kind of] instance: while taking advantage of great heat of the weather, [a person] suffers it from toil on the road, or suffers it from taxing his form in the fields; or again one with a weak thin body who, eating little [and] toiling excessively; there are also those people of constant fasting who cultivate goodness, whose stomach qi has long been vacuous, and who suffer it from toil. All these are similar to the yangming heat-injury Bai Hu Tang pattern. There must be great heat when the body is felt; there must be restless heat, oppression and chaos; great aversion to heat, thirst, and drinking water. Because of the excessiveness of the toil, there is also pain of the body. At the time of first receiving the disease, [it] is especially similar to the external contraction of heat that is an excess pattern. If one wrongly administers Bai Hu Tang, death in ten days is certain. In this pattern the spleen and stomach are greatly vacuous and the Original Qi is insufficient; in both mouth and nose the qi is short and hurried and wheezes above. After the sun turns [midday], which is the time when the yangming gains its season, the disease must lessen somewhat. If it were an externally contracted heat disease, then at the time of the sun declining toward dusk [shen time] it must make great delerium, its heat increasing, with great thirst and drinking of water, vexation and oppression unceasing. Those with insufficiency from toil all do not have this pattern, and it is especially easy to decompose. If there is a pattern hard to settle and suspected [between the two], one must wait one or two days to seek the physician's treatment, and one will not make an error.
