## 辯頭痛。

### Source Text

內證頭痛，有時而作，有時而止，外證頭痛，常常有之，直須傳入裏實方罷，此又內外證之不同者也。

### Literal Translation

In the internal pattern, headache occurs at certain times and ceases at certain times. In the external pattern, headache is constant, and only after transmitting into the interior and becoming replete does it cease. This is another difference between the internal and external patterns.
