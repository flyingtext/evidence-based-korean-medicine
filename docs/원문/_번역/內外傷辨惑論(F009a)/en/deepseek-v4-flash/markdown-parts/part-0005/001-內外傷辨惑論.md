## 辯手心手背。

### Source Text

內傷及勞役，飲食不節，病手心熱手背不熱，外傷風寒則手背熱手心不熱，此辯至甚皎然。

### Literal Translation

In internal injury and in injury by toil [with] immoderate food and drink, one is sick with heat of the palm of the hand, and the back of the hand is not hot. In external contraction of wind-cold, the back of the hand is hot and the palm is not hot. This differentiation is supremely clear.
