## 內外傷辯序

### Source Text

僕幼自受難素於易水張元素先生，講誦既久，稍有所得，中年以來，更事頗多，諸所診治，坦然不惑，曾撰內外傷辯惑論一篇，以證世人用藥之誤，陵谷變遷，忽成老境，神志既惰，懶於語言，此論束之高閣十六年矣，崑崙范尊師曲相獎借，屢以活人，為言謂此書果行使天下之人，不致夭折，是亦仁人君子，濟人利物之事，就令著述不已，精力衰耗，書成而死，不愈於無益而生乎？予敬受其言，僅力疾就成之，雖未為完備，聊答尊師慈憫之志，師宋文正公之後也，丁未歲，重九日，東垣老人李杲明之題。

### Literal Translation

I, from youth, received the [teaching] of Nanjing and Suwen from Master Zhang Yuansu of the Yishui school. Having lectured and recited for a long time, I gradually gained some attainment. From middle age onward, having encountered many affairs and treated many cases in their diagnosis and treatment, I remained unconfused. I once wrote a discourse, "Differentiation of Internal and External Injury," to testify against the errors of the world in the use of medicine. The hills and valleys changed [in my life journey], and suddenly I entered old age; my spirit and mind having become sluggish and lazy in speech, this discourse has lain bound and put on the shelf for sixteen years. My honored master, Fan of Kunlun, generously praised and encouraged it, and repeatedly used [the words] "to save the living" — saying that if this book were truly put into circulation, so that the people of the world might not die untimely, this too is the business of a humane gentleman to rescue people and benefit things. Even if I exhaust myself in authorship and fritter away my vital strength, and die once the book is finished, would that not be better than living without benefit? I respectfully received his words and, mustering my strength despite illness, completed it. Although it is not yet complete and prepared, it briefly answers the compassionate will of my honored master. My master is a descendant of Grand Academician Song Wenzheng. On the Double Ninth Festival in the year of Ding-wei, inscribed by Dongyuan Laozi [the Old Man of the Eastern Plain], Li Gao, styled Mingzhi.
