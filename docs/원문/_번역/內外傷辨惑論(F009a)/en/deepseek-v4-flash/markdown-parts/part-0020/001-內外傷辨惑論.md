### Literal Translation

Regarding internal injury from food and drink in dispensing medicine — both the prescriber of the medicine and the receiver of the medicine regard it as a small and trivial minor matter; therefore what should be treated as important is made light. The harm and profit are by no means slight. [People] do not reflect at all that the stomach qi is the nutritive qi, the defensive qi, the grain qi, the clear qi, and it supplies the engendering qi of the shaoyang [that] arises. The prosperity and decline of a person's true qi all lie in food and drink entering the stomach. If the stomach is harmonious then the grain qi ascends. The grain qi is the ascending and soaring qi; it is another name for the first issuance of foot-shaoyang gallbladder and hand-shaoyang original qi, engendering and growing the ten thousand transformations. If harm to food and drink [has occurred] and the dispersing-guiding medicaments accurately match the thing that was injured, then the stomach qi becomes the more prosperous, and the essence of the five grains ascends on high — this is "the clear qi being Heaven." The jing qi and spirit qi are all strong and vigorous; the seven spirits guard and protect; the engendering qi does not fail; it increases and greatly flourishes; qi and blood circulate everywhere, then the hundred diseases cannot invade; even the great wind and virulent poisons cannot harm [the person]. Is not the use of this one medicament of the broadest profit? Master Zhang of Yishui once admonished that one must not use drastic and violent food-dispelling medicaments. When the food-dispelling medicament is swallowed, before the power of the pill has been extended and transformed, when the force of its covering/peel has just begun to break open, [people] then say [the patient] feels empty and quick [relief]. The injured thing has already departed; if one waits yet an hour or two such that the medicament's power is completely broken open and transformed, that drastic and violent medicament must have its nature [that harms]; after the disease has departed, how can the spleen and stomach not be damaged? Once the spleen and stomach are damaged, this means the true qi and Original Qi are destroyed, shortening a person's lifespan. At that time, [Master Zhang] put down one medicament: zhi shi one liang, stir-fried until yellow in bran as the measure of degree; bai zhu two liang; only these two flavors. Wrap[ped] and cooked rice with lotus leaf to make a pill. Using bai zhu's bitter, sweet, and warm [nature] — its sweetness and warmth supplement the Original Qi of the spleen and stomach, its bitterness eliminates the dampness-heat in the stomach, and benefits the blood about the lumbar-knee [region]; hence it first supplements the weakness of the spleen and stomach, exceeding by one-fold the attacking and transforming power of zhi shi. Zhi shi's flavor is bitter and cold; it discharges oppression and stuffiness below the heart and digests what was injured in the stomach. When this one medicament descends to the stomach, the injury cannot be removed at once; it must wait an hour or two, and then the food is digested. This is first supplementing that vacuity and only then transforming that which was injured, and so it is not drastic or violent. At that time, I did not yet awaken to the principle of using rice cooked and wrapped in lotus leaf to make the pill; only in old age, savoring it, did I attain [the understanding]. It may be called miraculous. That one thing the lotus leaf — its center is empty and vacuous, resembling the body of the zhen [thunder] trigram. Zhen is movement; when a person is moved by it, the foot-shaoyang jiayi-gallbladder is born. The jiayi gallbladder is wind; it is the root-stalk on which the ten thousand things are engendered and transformed. The Zuozhuan says: "Beginning at the commencement of the year, then the sequence is not disturbed." In a person's food and drink entering the stomach, the nutritive qi ascending is the qi of the shaoyang jiayi gallbladder. Its hand-shaoyang sanjiao channel is the person's Original Qi. The hand and foot channels are the same in method; this is exactly the engendering and issuing of shaoyang Original Qi. The stomach qi, grain qi, Original Qi, and jiayi gallbladder ascending-rising qi are one; although the names are many, they are only the stomach qi ascending. The body of the lotus leaf, being born below water and earth, and issuing forth amidst filth and impurity without being stained by that filth and impurity, stands erect and alone; its color is green, and its form is empty and clear, resembling wind-wood. When the food-dispelling medicament is moved by the transformation of this qi, how can the stomach qi not ascend? That [the master] used this one ingredient as a directing emissary may be called far-sighted, deep-thinking, and united with the Dao. Moreover, by using cooked rice bound with the medicament, coordinating with bai zhu [its force], nourishing the grain qi and supplementing, so that the stomach becomes strengthened and will not again suffer internal injury, its benefit is broad and great. If, in internal injury of the spleen and stomach, [the injury arises] from acrid-heat things, such as wine and meat, and [the patient] himself feels unwell and goes to a physician seeking medicine, this custom is taken as the norm. The physician likewise does not ask what was injured, but at once gives him Ji Xiang Wan [and] Ba Dou [croton] — great heat medicaments and the like — to drain. When the stool down, the thing goes away, leaving behind the heat-nature of the food [that was injured by]; the heat-nature of the medicament heavily wounds the Original Qi, and the seven spirits do not shine. The Classic states: "Heat wounds qi" — precisely this refers to. That person must become so without qi to move and thermally trapped, with the four limbs not raised; transmitting and changing into all manner of diseases, incalculable, thereby causing a person's true qi from this time to decline. If [the injury] is from raw-cold and hard things, the common physicians or else throw in the two great-cold medicaments da huang [and] qian niu, and the thing follows the medicament downward and what was injured is gone, leaving behind the cold-nature of the food and the cold-nature of the medicament, which heavily drains its yang. When yang departs, then the skin, sinews, bones, flesh, and blood vessels have nothing on which to lean, and [the condition] becomes a pattern of vacuity depletion. Speaking of this makes one's heart cold. As to acrid-hot, qi-thin medicaments — one must not recklessly take them without cause; this is not limited to qian niu only. The Zhi Yao Da Lun says: "The five flavors entering the stomach each first pursue that which it delights in attacking." "Attack" means restrain, overcomes, and drain. When the acrid flavor is swallowed down, it first attacks and drains the five qi of the lung. Qi is the true qi, the Original Qi. That qian niu's acrid-hot, fierce, and violent [nature] seizes away from a person all the more. In harm from food and drink, the intestines and stomach receive the pathogen; one should drain its intestines and stomach with the bitter flavor, and that is all. What offense have the lung and Original Qi committed? That qian niu must not be used — there are five [reasons]; this is one of them. Moreover, the stomach governs blood; [when] it is injured by things — things are things having form — these are all blood diseases; to drain qi in a blood disease — this is the second. And further, food and drink injure the middle burner; one should only restrain-transform and dispersing-guide the food, [rather than] heavily draining the already vacuous qi of the upper burner lung — this is the third. In food injury of the intestines and stomach, one should use 'stuffing for what is stuffed' and also 'cold for what is cold' — zhi shi [and] da huang, bitter-cold things, to discharge the formed is this; on the contrary to use acrid-hot qian niu to scatter and drain the true qi commits the great prohibition the fourth. [People] are unaware that in the first volume, first chapter of the Zhen Jing there is a saying: "With external guest pathogen — wind and cold injuring and entering the five viscera — if one mistakenly drains the stomach qi, [the patient] will die; erroneous supplementation also kills. As to its death: without qi to move, [the patient] therefore is still. If, with internal injury of the intestines and stomach, one instead drains the five viscera, [the patient] will certainly die; erroneous supplementation also kills. As to its death: the yin qi being in excess, [the patient] therefore is restless." Now, in internal injury of the intestines and stomach, this is called the disease of insufficiency of the six bowels; on the contrary to drain the vacuous, formless lung qi of the upper burner — the lung is one of the number of the five viscera — with qian niu and its kind, harming by morning and harming by evening, its Original Qi is consumed; this is secretly striking off a person's span in the dark, committing the great prohibition the fifth. Truly that which merits grief and lament. Therefore I have specially written this discourse together with its formulae, so that the four seas may hear and practice it, not dying untimely [due to] violent death. This is this old man's effort of mind. How can the stomach qi be un-nourished? To re-illuminate the principle of nourishing the stomach, the Classic states: "[With] peaceful grain [the person] flourishes; [with] absolute absence of grain [the person] perishes. When the water departs, the nutritive [qi] disperses; when the grain dissolves, the defensive [qi] perishes. When the nutritive disperses and the defensive perishes, the spirit has nothing on which to rely." Zhongjing states: "When water enters the channels, therein is the blood formed; when grain enters the stomach, the pulse-ways then move." Hence blood must not be un-nourished, and the stomach must not be unwarmed. With the blood warmed and the stomach harmonized, the Nutritive and Defensive [qi] will move, and one constantly possesses Heaven's allotted span. Grain is the great pivot of the body. Both the [Shang] shu and the Zhouli state: "Metal, wood, water, fire, and earth, and grain — and only [these], cultivated, serve to nurture the five viscera." Internal injury of food and drink is certainly not a trifling matter; if one recklessly takes food-medicaments and lightly throws away life and injures [one's own] fate, how can this be endured? The Huangdi Zhen Jing has a saying: "The stomach hates heat and delights in coolness; the large intestine hates cold-cool and delights in warmth. If the two are not harmonious, how can one regulate them?" Bo Qi [i.e., Qi Bo] said: "To regulate these, in both food and drink and clothing one also should make the cold and warmth comfortable: in cold, no bitter chill; in summer, no sweating. As to food and drink: in heat, no scorching; in cold, no biting chill. When the cold and warmth are both moderate, then the qi will be maintained, and then no deviant pathogen will be produced" (the detailed discussion appears below the entry of this classic). Now, there is bound to be a cause for use. How can one use medicine that is entirely cold and entirely hot to inflict harm, in accord with [this]? The Suwen states: "In internal injury, the qikou pulse is on the contrary larger than renying by one-fold, two-fold, or three-fold; distribute the channels and use medicine [accordingly]." And again: "If there is pulse in the upper parts and no pulse in the lower parts, and the person should vomit but does not vomit, [the person] dies." If [the patient] merely eats but does not ingest [digest], and is nauseated and wishes to vomit, regardless of one-fold or two-fold, one should not squarely give Gua Di San to vomit [him]; but only with the finger or with some thing explore and remove it. If the injured thing is not entirely removed, then again examine the pulse and ask what was injured, and use the food-medicament to remove it, so as to answer to "stuffing for what is stuffed" — which is also called "cold for what is cold." Discharging and descending answers to the function of taiyin; in the midst of it, further add engendering-issuing medicaments so that its Original Qi ascends. "Stuffing for what is stuffed," "by the crooked making it straight." What is "crooked"? — This is [that] the stomach qi is injured. What is "straight"? — This is [that] the stomach qi is raised and issued. Because one treats the internal injury of that food and drink so that the engendering qi is augmented and the stomach qi is fully restored, this is "by the crooked making it straight." If one follows the distribution by channels and uses medicine [by that], what is injured — cold, hot, warm, cool, raw, hard, soft — is not alike in injury, and it is hard to establish a fixed method. One only follows the difference in what was injured, establishing laws of treatment each [time], using addition and subtraction on the spot. In using medicine, one moreover should ask of the patient the original [constitution] — rise and fall of their inborn endowment — and whether what was injured was a cold thing or hot thing; [whether] it is [a case of] taking food because [one] delighted to eat? Then one must not take qi-breaking medicaments. If [one] ate because seized by hunger and exhaustion, then one should augment the stomach qi. Or [if one] was urged and exhorted by people to eat forcefully, one should deplete the blood and augment the qi. Examine its pulse and signs — in which viscus the injury lies — only then may one give the medicament matching the disease. How could one recklessly drain the Heaven-engendering true qi and lightly destroy the treasure of the body? And [for example] if one first ate a hot thing and was not injured, and next [ate] a cold thing, and because of the later eating even the former food was not digested and [so] injured [the stomach], one should inquire [about] the hot food and cold food — which was more, which less — and weigh it, then give the medicament; there is no case that will not be appropriate. For illustration, if one was injured by a hot thing two parts and a cold thing one part, then one should use cold medicament two parts and hot medicament one part, combined and given. Then the qi of the Nutritive and Defensive [qi] will certainly obtain free flow. And [there are] cases of first drinking wine and then being injured by cold-cold food, and of being injured by hot food, cold water, and ice; such being not alike, all one should verify the thing that was injured in its order/measure, approximately measure the parts of the cold-heat medicament [dosage], and give it matched to the pattern in each case; none fails to obtain efficacy. I myself, reflecting on the medicines and formulae set down, dare not yet take them to fully exhaust the principle of medicinal natures; I only use [them] to point out the confused and to resolve delusion.
