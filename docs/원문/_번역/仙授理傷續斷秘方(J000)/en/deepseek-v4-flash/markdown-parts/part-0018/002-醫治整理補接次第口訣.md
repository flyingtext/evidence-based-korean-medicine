## 四物湯

### Source Text

凡傷重腸內有瘀血者用此。

白芍藥　川當歸　熟地黃　川芎　上等分，每服三錢。水一盞半，煎至七分，空心熱服。一方只用當歸、大黃二味。

### Literal Translation

For all severe injuries with blood stasis in the intestines, use this.

Baishaoyao/white peony, Chuan Danggui/Angelica, Shudihuang/cooked rehmannia, Chuanxiong/Ligusticum. The above in equal parts. Each time take three qian. With one and a half cups of water, decoct until seven-tenths remains, and take hot on an empty stomach. One edition uses only the two ingredients Danggui and Dahuang.
