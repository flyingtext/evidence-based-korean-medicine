## 鱉甲散

### Source Text

治五癆七傷，四時傷寒，渾身增寒壯熱，骨節煩疼。嗽咳痰涎，酒色傷憊，四肢倦怠。及治山嵐瘴瘧，一切積氣。心腹膨脹，嘔吐泄瀉，應是風疾，並宜服之。

### Literal Translation

Treats the five consumptions and seven injuries, the four-season cold damage (shanghan), chills and high fever over the whole body, aching pain of the bones and joints. Coughing and spitting of phlegm and saliva, exhaustion from wine and sex, fatigue of the four limbs. And treats mountain-miasma malaria and all accumulated qi. Distension of the heart and abdomen, vomiting and diarrhea; whatever wind disorders there are, all are suitable to take.

Rougui/Cinnamon four liang, Chuanxiong/Ligusticum four liang, Baizhi/Angelica dahurica four liang, Qinjiao/Gentiana macrophylla four liang, Biejia/turtle shell four liang (vinegar-roasted three times until red in color), Ziwan/Aster four liang (cleanly washed, dried/baked), Mahuang/Ephedra four liang (do not remove the nodes), Qianghuo/Notopterygium four liang (one edition says Duhuo/Radix Angelicae pubescentis), Danggui/Angelica four liang (remove the tail), Ganjiang/dried ginger four liang, Jupi/tangerine peel four liang, Cangzhu/Atractylodes one jin (dried/baked), Tiantai Wuyao/Lindera seven liang, Zisu/Perilla four liang (not passed over fire), Jiegeng/Platycodon three jin and a half (dried/baked), Wuyao/Lindera seven liang, Chaihu/Bupleurum seven liang, Chuanwu/Aconiti radix half a piece (paozi/parched). The above are dried/baked and ground into a fine powder. Each time take two qian. With one cup of water, three slices of ginger, and one wumei (smoked plum), decoct together until seven-tenths remains, and take hot. For cold damage, add scallion white and decoct. For consumptive injury, add salt. Adjust with hot wine and take.
