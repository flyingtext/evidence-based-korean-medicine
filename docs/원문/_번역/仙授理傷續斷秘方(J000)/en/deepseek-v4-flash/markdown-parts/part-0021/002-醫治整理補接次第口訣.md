## 大成湯

### Source Text

（一名大承氣湯）

應傷損極重，大小便不通者，方服此。可加木通煎。如未通加朴硝，俟大小便通，方可服損藥。損藥不可用酒煎，愈不通矣。然亦須量人肥弱用，如孕婦小兒莫服。

大黃四兩　川芒硝　甘草　陳皮　紅花　當歸　蘇木　木通各二兩　枳殼四兩　厚朴（少許）　上件㕮咀，每服二錢。水一盞半，煎至一沸，去渣溫服，不拘時。此乃專治男子傷重瘀血不散，腹肚膨脹，大小便不通，上攻心腹，悶亂至死者。急將此藥通下瘀血後，方可服損藥。

### Literal Translation

(Also named Dachengqi Tang)

For injuries that are extremely severe, with both urination and defecation blocked, only then take this. Mutong/Akebia may be added and decocted. If still not open, add Puxiao/Mirabilite. Only after urination and defecation are open may the injury medicine be taken. The injury medicine must not be decocted in wine, for it will become even more blocked. However, one must also measure whether the person is fat or thin in using it. Pregnant women and children must not take it.

Dahuang/Rhubarb four liang, Chuan Mangxiao/Mirabilite, Gancao/Licorice, Chenpi/tangerine peel, Honghua/safflower, Danggui/Angelica, Sumu/sappan wood, Mutong/Akebia, each two liang, Zhiqiao/bitter orange four liang, Houpo/Magnolia bark (a little). The above items are roughly chopped (fuzu). Each time take two qian. With one and a half cups of water, decoct until it comes to one boil, remove the dregs, and take warm; not limited to a fixed time. This is specifically for treating men with severe injuries where blood stasis does not disperse, distension of the belly, blockage of urination and defecation, attacking upward into the heart and abdomen, and confusion and distress to the point of death. Urgently use this medicine to purge the blood stasis, and only then may the injury medicine be taken.
