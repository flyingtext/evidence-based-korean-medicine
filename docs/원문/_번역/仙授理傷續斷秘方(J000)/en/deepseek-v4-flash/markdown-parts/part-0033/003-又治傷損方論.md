## 洗藥

### Source Text

（後有仙正散方）

如傷重，先用洗，後卻用烏龍角貼。其洗藥同前方內。又參山泉方洗藥用。

木朔翟　石南葉　白芷　白楊皮　生蔥　何首烏　土當歸　荊芥　藁本　芍藥　上不拘多少，煎湯候溫，將洗損處令淨，用絹滲乾瘡口上為妙。

### Literal Translation

(After this there is the Xianzheng San formula)

If the injury is severe, first use the wash, then apply Wulong Jiao. The washing medicine is the same as in the previous formula. Also refer to the Shanshan Quanfang washing medicine for use.

Mushuozhe, Shinan ye/Photinia leaf, Baizhi/Angelica dahurica, Baiyangpi/white poplar bark, fresh scallion, Heshouwu/Polygonum multiflorum, native Danggui/Angelica, Jingjie/Schizonepeta, Gaoben/Ligusticum, Shaoyao/Peony. The above, without regard to amount, are decocted into a soup; when warm, wash the injured area until clean, and use silk to blot dry the wound opening; this is best.
