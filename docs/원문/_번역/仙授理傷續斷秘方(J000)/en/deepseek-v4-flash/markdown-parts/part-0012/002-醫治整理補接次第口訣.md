## 小黑丸

### Source Text

白蘞　白芨　南星　芍藥各十兩　當歸五兩　細辛三兩　赤小豆一升　百草霜六兩　右為末，醋糊為丸，如梧子大。每服三十丸，溫酒下。

### Literal Translation

Bailian/Ampelopsis, Baiji/Bletilla, Nanxing/Arisaema, Shaoyao/Peony, each ten liang, Danggui/Angelica five liang, Xixin/Asarum three liang, Chixiaodou/red adzuki bean one sheng, Baicaoshuang/soot from the bottom of a pan six liang. The above are ground into a powder, and vinegar paste is used to form pills the size of wutong seeds. Each time take thirty pills with warm wine.
