## 洗藥

### Source Text

治男子婦人骨斷，用此煎水洗後整骨了卻用烏龍角貼。

杜仲五兩　五加皮七兩　蔥根一把　上三味，水五升，煎至七分，去滓淋洗。每服二兩半重。傷破留口用藥摻。骨斷當夾縛，詳見前論。

### Literal Translation

Treats broken bones in men and women. Use this decocted in water to wash, then set the bone, and then use Wulong Jiao to apply.

Duzhong/Eucommia five liang, Wujiapi/Acanthopanax bark seven liang, Conggen/scallion root one handful. The above three ingredients, with five sheng of water, decoct until seven-tenths remains, remove the dregs, and pour over and wash. Each time use two and a half liang in weight. For a broken wound with an opening, use the medicine to sprinkle. For a broken bone, bind and splint; see the previous discussion for details.
