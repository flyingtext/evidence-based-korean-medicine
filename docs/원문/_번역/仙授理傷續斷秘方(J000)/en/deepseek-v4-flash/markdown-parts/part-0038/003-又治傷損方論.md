## 定痛丸

### Source Text

治腰痛不可忍。不問男子婦人室女老幼，並皆治之。

威靈仙半兩（去土）　金鈴子一兩（炒去核）　川烏一兩（炮）　八角茴香一兩　上為末，酒煮麵糊為丸。如梧子大，每服五十丸。鹽湯酒隨上下服之。

### Literal Translation

Treats low back pain that cannot be endured. Whether man, woman, young unmarried woman, old or young, all are treated.

Weilingxian/Clematis half a liang (remove the earth), Jinlingzi/Toosendan fruit one liang (stir-fried, remove the kernel), Chuanwu/Aconiti radix one liang (paozi/parched), Bajiaohuixiang/star anise one liang. The above are ground into a powder, and wine is used to cook a flour paste to form pills the size of wutong seeds. Each time take fifty pills, with salt water or wine, taken according to whether the disease is in the upper or lower body.
