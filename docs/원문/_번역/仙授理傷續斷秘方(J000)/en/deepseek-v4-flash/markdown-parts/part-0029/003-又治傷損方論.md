## 烏丸子

### Source Text

治打撲傷損，骨碎筋斷，瘀血不散，及一切風疾。筋痿力乏，左癱右瘓，手足緩弱，諸般風損。婦人血疾，產後敗血不散，灌入四肢，面目浮腫，並宜服之。惟孕婦勿服。

### Literal Translation

Treats injuries from blows and falls, broken bones and severed sinews, blood stasis not dispersing, and all wind disorders. Sinew weakness and loss of strength, left-sided paralysis and right-sided paralysis, weakness and slowness of the hands and feet, all kinds of wind injuries. Women's blood disorders, postpartum retained lochia not dispersing, pouring into the four limbs, swelling of the face and eyes; all are suitable to take. Only pregnant women must not take it.

Chixiaodou/red adzuki bean (stir-fried), Bailian/Ampelopsis, Chishaoyao/red peony, Heshouwu/Polygonum multiflorum (vinegar-boiled), Xixin/Asarum (remove the sprouts), Caowu/Aconiti kusnezoffii (vinegar-boiled seven times), Baiji/Bletilla (roasted in embers), Shangui/Cinnamon (remove the coarse bark), Nanxing/Arisaema (wrapped in dough and roasted in embers), Danggui/Angelica (soaked in wine one night), Chuanxiong/Ligusticum, Baicaoshuang/soot from the bottom of a pan, Gusuibu/Drynaria (remove the fuzz, stir-fry), Tiantai Wuyao/Lindera (boiled in black-bean wine, then dried/baked), the above each one liang. The above are ground into a fine powder, and the bean-boiling wine is used to cook a flour paste to form pills the size of wutong seeds. Each time take fifty pills, swallowed with roasted-scallion wine, or roasted-scallion tea as desired.
