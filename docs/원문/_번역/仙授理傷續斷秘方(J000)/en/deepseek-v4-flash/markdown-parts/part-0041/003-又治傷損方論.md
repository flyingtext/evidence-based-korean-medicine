## 糝瘡口方

### Source Text

但遇傷損，皮肉血出。或破腦傷風，血出不止，急用此藥擫之。

血竭二錢半（別研）　降真香四錢　燈心一把　龍骨（五花者）　二錢（別研）　雞一隻（連毛屎用醋煮後碎之，用黃泥封固以文武火煨乾後焙為末）　蘇木　（同降真香碾少許）　乳香五錢（同燈心研）　沒藥五錢（別研）　桔梗少許　紅花（要馬頭者）二錢（焙乾為末）　當歸三錢　上為細末，每用少許，乾擫瘡口上。如血流湧出不止，多擫之。候血藥將干，又用清油調塗於瘡上。（可制一料以備急用）

### Literal Translation

Whenever one meets an injury, with blood flowing from the broken skin and flesh. Or a broken head with wind injury, blood flowing out without stopping, urgently use this medicine to press onto it.

Xuejie/Dragon's blood two and a half qian (ground separately), Jiangzhenxiang/Dalbergia heartwood four qian, Dengxin/lampwick one handful, Longgu/dragon bone (the five-flowered kind) two qian (ground separately), one chicken (used whole with feathers and droppings, boiled in vinegar, then crushed, sealed with yellow mud, roasted dry over civil and military fire, then dried/baked and ground into a powder), Sumu/sappan wood (ground together with Jiangzhenxiang, a little), Ruxiang/Frankincense five qian (ground together with lampwick), Moyao/Myrrh five qian (ground separately), Jiegeng/Platycodon a little, Honghua/safflower (must be the horse-head kind) two qian (dried/baked and ground into a powder), Danggui/Angelica three qian. The above are ground into a fine powder. Each time use a little, pressed dry onto the wound opening. If blood gushes out without stopping, press on more. When the blood medicine is about to dry, then use clear oil to adjust and smear it on the wound. (One batch may be prepared for emergency use.)
