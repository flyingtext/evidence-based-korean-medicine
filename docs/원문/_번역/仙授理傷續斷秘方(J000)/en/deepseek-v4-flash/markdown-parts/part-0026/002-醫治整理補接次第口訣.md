## 至真散

### Source Text

（一名奪命散）

治打破傷損，破腦傷風頭疼，角弓反張。

天南星（炮）七次　防風（去蘆叉）　上等分為末，凡破傷風病，以藥敷貼瘡口，即以溫酒調一錢服之。如牙關緊急，以童便調二錢服。垂死心頭微溫，童便調二錢，並進三服。

### Literal Translation

(Also named Duoming San)

Treats injuries from beating and breaking, broken head with wind injury, headache, and opisthotonos (arching of the back like a bow).

Tiannanxing/Arisaema (paozi/parched) seven times, Fangfeng/Saposhnikovia (remove the root and branches). The above in equal parts, ground into a powder. For all tetanus (broken-wind) disease, apply the medicine to the wound opening, and also adjust one qian with warm wine and take it. If the jaw is tight and locked, adjust two qian with children's urine and take. If near death but the heart is still slightly warm, adjust two qian with children's urine and take, and administer three doses in succession.
