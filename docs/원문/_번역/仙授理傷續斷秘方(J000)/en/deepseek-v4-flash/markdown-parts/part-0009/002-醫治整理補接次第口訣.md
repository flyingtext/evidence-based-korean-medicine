## 乳香散

### Source Text

損傷撲跌治，皮肉破綻，筋骨寸斷，敗血壅滯，結腫爛壞，疼痛至甚。或勞役所損，背肩四肢疼痛。損後中風，手足痿痹，不能舉動。筋骨乖縱，攣縮不舒。大能續筋接骨，卓有奇驗。常服結血止疼生力。每服二錢，溫湯調下，不拘時候。

肉桂　乾薑各三兩　牛膝　羌活各四兩　白芷二兩　川芎　細辛　薑黃各四兩　骨碎補　當歸各六兩　芍藥　草烏　川烏各四兩　蒼朮六兩　桔梗十兩　赤小豆一升　乳香半斤　沒藥五兩　何首烏十四兩　木鱉（去殼麩炒）六兩　乳沒別研（一方去木鱉加海桐皮）　上焙碾為末，續入乳、沒末和湯。使調服如前。

### Literal Translation

Treats injuries from falls and blows, broken and split skin and flesh, sinews and bones broken into inch-long pieces, stagnant and congested blood, bound swelling and rotten flesh, pain to the extreme. Or injuries from toil and labor, pain of the back, shoulders, and four limbs. After injury, struck by wind, the hands and feet become atrophic and numb, unable to move. The sinews and bones become slack and disordered, contracted and not relaxed. It is greatly able to reconnect sinews and join bones, and has outstanding miraculous efficacy. Regular taking binds the blood, stops pain, and generates strength. Each time take two qian, adjusted with warm soup and taken; not limited to a fixed time.

Rougui/Cinnamon, Ganjiang/dried ginger, each three liang, Niuxi/Achyranthes, Qianghuo/Notopterygium, each four liang, Baizhi/Angelica dahurica two liang, Chuanxiong/Ligusticum, Xixin/Asarum, Jianghuang/turmeric, each four liang, Gusuibu/Drynaria, Danggui/Angelica, each six liang, Shaoyao/Peony, Caowu/Aconiti kusnezoffii, Chuanwu/Aconiti radix, each four liang, Cangzhu/Atractylodes six liang, Jiegeng/Platycodon ten liang, Chixiaodou/red adzuki bean one sheng, Ruxiang/Frankincense half a jin, Moyao/Myrrh five liang, Heshouwu/Polygonum multiflorum fourteen liang, Mubie/Semen momordicae (remove the shell, stir-fry with bran) six liang. Ruxiang and Moyao are ground separately (one edition removes Mubie and adds Haitongpi/Erythrina bark). The above are dried/baked and ground into a powder, then the Ruxiang and Moyao powders are added and mixed with the soup. Take adjusted as before.
