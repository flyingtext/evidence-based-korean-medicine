## 小承氣湯

### Source Text

大黃四兩　芒硝二錢（煎熱漸入）　枳實二兩　厚朴八兩　上治效同大成湯，此較力輕。不拘婦人女子小兒，皆可服之。

### Literal Translation

Dahuang/Rhubarb four liang, Mangxiao/Mirabilite two qian (added gradually while hot), Zhishi/immature bitter orange two liang, Houpo/Magnolia bark eight liang. The above treats the same as Dacheng Tang, but this one is lighter in strength. It is not restricted; women, girls, and children may all take it.
