## 麻丸子

### Source Text

治踒折傷損，皮破骨出，手足碎斷，肌肉壞爛，疼痛至甚，日夜叫呼，百治不止。手足久損，筋骨差爻，舉動不能。損後傷風濕，肢節攣縮，遂成偏廢，勞傷筋骨，肩背疼痛，四肢廢乏，動作無力。常服壯筋骨，活經絡，生氣血。及治婦人血氣。惟孕婦勿服。

### Literal Translation

Treats sprains and injuries from falls, broken skin with bone protruding, hands and feet broken into pieces, rotten flesh and sinews, pain to the extreme, crying out day and night, with a hundred treatments not stopping it. Long-standing injuries of the hands and feet, sinews and bones dislocated, unable to move. After injury, attacked by wind and dampness, the limbs contract and shrink, and thus it becomes a partial disability. Overstrain injures the sinews and bones, pain of the shoulders and back, fatigue and weakness of the four limbs, weakness in movement and action. Regular taking strengthens the sinews and bones, activates the channels and collaterals, and generates qi and blood. And it treats women's blood-qi disorders. Only pregnant women must not take it.

Chuan Danggui/Angelica (remove the sprouts, cleanly wash), Jiegeng/Platycodon (named bu luo bo), Niuxi/Achyranthes, each half a liang (not soaked in wine), Gusuibu/Drynaria two liang (remove the fuzz), Chuanwu/Aconiti radix (not exposed to fire, cut into slices and vinegar-boiled), Chuanxiong/Ligusticum one jin, Baicaoshuang/soot from the bottom of a pan one jin, Caowu/Aconiti kusnezoffii (soaked in alum-ash juice) one jin, Mubiezi/Semen momordicae (remove the oil and shell), Chishaoyao/red peony, each half a jin, Wudou/black bean one dou (soaked in wine, boiled, dried/baked), Jinmao Gouji/Cibotium (remove the tail). The above are ground into a powder, and wine is used to cook a flour paste to form pills the size of wutong seeds. Each time take fifty pills with warm wine. For women, take with mugwort-vinegar soup.
