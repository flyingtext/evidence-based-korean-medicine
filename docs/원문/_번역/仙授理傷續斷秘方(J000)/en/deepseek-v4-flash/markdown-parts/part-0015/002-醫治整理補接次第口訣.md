## 黑虎丹

### Source Text

治男子婦人手足麻痹。

川烏　木鱉各一斤　地龍十兩　黑豆半升　五靈脂二兩　松墨二兩（醋炒）　上四味為末，五靈脂醋研碎煮。麵糊為丸，龍眼大，每服一丸，或二丸。細嚼酒下，薄荷茶亦可，不拘時。

### Literal Translation

Treats numbness of the hands and feet in men and women.

Chuanwu/Aconiti radix, Mubie/Semen momordicae, each one jin, Dilong/earthworm ten liang, Heidou/black soybean half a sheng, Wulingzhi/Trogopterus dung two liang, Songmo/ink two liang (stir-fried with vinegar). Of the above, four ingredients are ground into a powder; the Wulingzhi is crushed and boiled in vinegar. Cook flour paste to form pills the size of longan fruit. Each time take one pill, or two pills. Chew finely and take with wine; mint tea is also acceptable; not limited to a fixed time.
