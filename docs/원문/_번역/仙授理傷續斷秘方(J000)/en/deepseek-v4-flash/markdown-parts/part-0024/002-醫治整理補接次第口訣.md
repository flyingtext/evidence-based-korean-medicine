## 接骨藥

### Source Text

下窟烏，一名鶚。用骨燒存性，用古銅錢一個，煅醋淬七次為末，等分，骨斷夾縛訖，用藥一錢，以酒調下，不可過多。病在下空心服，在上食後服。此方極驗。

常用整骨藥

用大草烏。刮去皮為細末，每服半錢，溫酒調下。如未覺，再添二分藥，酒下。

### Literal Translation

Xia ku wu, also named E (osprey). Use the bone burned to preserve its nature. Use one ancient copper coin, calcined and quenched in vinegar seven times, ground into a powder. In equal parts. After the broken bone is bound and splinted, take one qian of the medicine, adjusted with wine and taken; it must not be excessive. For disease in the lower body, take on an empty stomach; for disease in the upper body, take after meals. This formula is extremely effective.

Commonly used bone-setting medicine

Use large Caowu/Aconiti kusnezoffii. Scrape off the skin and grind into a fine powder. Each time take half a qian, adjusted with warm wine and taken. If not yet felt, add another two fen of the medicine, taken with wine.
