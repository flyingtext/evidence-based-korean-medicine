## 首烏丸

### Source Text

治風損寬筋。

何首烏十斤（黑豆半升同蒸熟）　牽牛子十兩（炒）　牛膝　薄荷各二十兩　川烏二兩　青木香五兩　皂角二斤（一斤燒存性，一斤蜜炙用）上為末，酒糊丸，如梧子大，每服三十丸，蔥湯或薄荷湯，不拘時下。

### Literal Translation

Treats wind-injury and relaxes the sinews.

Heshouwu/Polygonum multiflorum ten jin (steamed together with half a sheng of black soybean until cooked), Qianniuzi/Pharbitis seed ten liang (stir-fried), Niuxi/Achyranthes, Bohe/mint, each twenty liang, Chuanwu/Aconiti radix two liang, Qingmuxiang/Aristolochia root five liang, Zaojiao/Gleditsia two jin (one jin burned to preserve its nature, one jin honey-roasted for use). The above are ground into a powder, and wine paste is used to form pills the size of wutong seeds. Each time take thirty pills, with scallion soup or mint soup, not limited to a fixed time.
