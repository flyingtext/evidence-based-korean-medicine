## 七氣湯

### Source Text

治積年久損，入經絡，服藥無效。腰背拘急，咳嗽痰涎。風勞發動，日漸羸瘦，每到秋來損病復作，不問男子婦人，並皆治之。

### Literal Translation

Treats long-standing injuries of many years that have entered the channels and collaterals, where taking medicine has no effect. Tension and contraction of the lower back and spine, coughing with phlegm and saliva. Wind-consumption (lao) attacks, daily growing thin and emaciated, and every time autumn comes the injury disease recurs. Whether man or woman, all are treated.

Qingpi/green tangerine peel (remove the white, stir-fry), Chenpi/tangerine peel (remove the white), Sanleng/bur-reed (wrapped in wet paper and roasted in embers), Bei Geng/Platycodon (remove the root), Rougui/Cinnamon (remove the coarse bark), Huoxiang/Agastache (remove the branches), Yizhi/Alpinia (remove the shell, stir-fry), Xiangfuzi/Cyperus (stir-fried), Gancao/Licorice (honey-roasted), Banxia/Pinellia (soaked in hot water), Chishaoyao/red peony, Wuyao/Lindera, Duhuo/Radix Angelicae pubescentis, Qianghuo/Notopterygium (remove the root), Jiangzhenxiang/Dalbergia heartwood, each one liang. The above are roughly chopped (fuzu). Each time take five qian, with one large and a half cups of water, three slices of ginger, and one jujube, decoct until seven-tenths remains, remove the dregs, and take according to whether the disease is in the upper or lower body.
