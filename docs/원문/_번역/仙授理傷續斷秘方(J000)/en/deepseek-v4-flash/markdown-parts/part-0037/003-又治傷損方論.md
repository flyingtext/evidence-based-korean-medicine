## 七寶散

### Source Text

治冷水風腳濕氣下注，腳膝生瘡。左癱右瘓，筋脈拘急。腳下隱痛，不得伸屈，不能踏地，並皆治之。

晚蠶砂一升（炒）　蛇床子一升（炒）　肉桂二兩（去皮）　荊芥穗五兩　乾荷葉二兩　藁本五兩（去土）　川烏二兩（炮）　薏苡仁三兩　上件㕮咀，約二兩重。用水五升，加花椒、連須蔥，同煎至七分。去渣。於痛處熱斟淋冼。

### Literal Translation

Treats cold-water wind-foot dampness pouring downward, sores on the feet and knees. Left-sided paralysis and right-sided paralysis, tension and contraction of the sinews and vessels. Hidden pain under the feet, unable to extend or flex, unable to step on the ground; all are treated.

Wan Cansha/late silkworm droppings one sheng (stir-fried), Shechuangzi/Cnidium one sheng (stir-fried), Rougui/Cinnamon two liang (remove the skin), Jingjiesui/Schizonepeta spike five liang, Ganheye/dried lotus leaf two liang, Gaoben/Ligusticum five liang (remove the earth), Chuanwu/Aconiti radix two liang (paozi/parched), Yiyiren/Coix seed three liang. The above items are roughly chopped (fuzu), about two liang in weight. Use five sheng of water, add Huajiao/Zanthoxylum and scallion with roots, and decoct together until seven-tenths remains. Remove the dregs. At the painful area, pour the hot [decoction] over and wash.
