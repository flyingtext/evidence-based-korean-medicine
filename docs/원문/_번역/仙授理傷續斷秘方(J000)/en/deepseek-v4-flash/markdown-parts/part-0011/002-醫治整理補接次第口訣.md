## 小紅丸

### Source Text

烏頭一個　何首烏　蒼朮　蛇床子　五靈脂　牛膝　赤小豆　白膠香　當歸各一兩　乳香二錢　上為末，好酒煮糊為丸，如綠豆大。每服三十丸，溫酒送下。

### Literal Translation

Wutou/Aconiti radix one piece, Heshouwu/Polygonum multiflorum, Cangzhu/Atractylodes, Shechuangzi/Cnidium, Wulingzhi/Trogopterus dung, Niuxi/Achyranthes, Chixiaodou/red adzuki bean, Baijiaoxiang/white resin, Danggui/Angelica, each one liang, Ruxiang/Frankincense two qian. The above are ground into a powder, and good wine is used to cook a paste to form pills the size of green beans. Each time take thirty pills, sent down with warm wine.
