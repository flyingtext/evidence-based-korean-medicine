## 紅丸子

### Source Text

治打撲傷損，骨碎筋斷，疼痛痹冷，內外俱損。瘀血留滯，外腫內痛。肢節疼倦，應諸傷損。不問年月日久，並宜服之。常服補損，堅筋固骨，滋血生力，神效不可具述。孕婦勿服。

### Literal Translation

Treats injuries from blows and falls, broken bones and severed sinews, pain, numbness and cold, damage to both the interior and exterior. Retained blood stasis, external swelling and internal pain. Aching and fatigue of the limbs and joints; it responds to all injuries and damage, whether of recent or long standing, and is suitable to take. Regular taking repairs damage, strengthens the sinews and firms the bones, nourishes the blood and generates strength; its miraculous efficacy cannot be fully described. Pregnant women must not take it.

Niuxi/Achyranthes (soaked in wine one night), Chuanwu/Aconiti radix (paozi/parched), Nanxing/Arisaema (vinegar-boiled three times), Xixin/Asarum (remove the sprouts, cleanly wash), Heshouwu/Polygonum multiflorum (boiled in water until cooked), Jiegeng/Platycodon (remove the root), Shangui/Cinnamon (remove the coarse bark), Danggui/Angelica, Zirantong/Pyritum (calcined and quenched in vinegar seven times, ground separately), Bailian/Ampelopsis, Chishaoyao/red peony, Gusuibu/Drynaria (remove the fuzz), Moyao/Myrrh (ground separately), Qianghuo/Notopterygium (remove the root), Chixiaodou/red adzuki bean (not exposed to fire). Of the above, except the medicines ground separately, the rest are all beaten and mixed, stir-fried dry, and ground into a powder. Wine is used to cook a flour paste to form pills. Each time take fifty pills, taken according to whether the disease is in the upper or lower body.
