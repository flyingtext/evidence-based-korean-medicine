## 黃藥末

### Source Text

治跌撲傷損，皮肉破綻，筋肉寸斷，敗血壅滯，結癰爛壞，疼痛至甚。或勞役所損，肩背四肢疼痛，損後中風，手足痿痹，不能舉動，筋骨乖張，攣縮不伸。續筋接骨，卓有奇功。常服活血止腫生力。

### Literal Translation

Treats injuries from falls and blows, broken and split skin and flesh, sinews and flesh broken into inch-long pieces, stagnant and congested blood, bound carbuncles and rotten flesh, pain to the extreme. Or injuries from toil and labor, pain of the shoulders, back, and four limbs; after injury, struck by wind, the hands and feet become atrophic and numb, unable to move, the sinews and bones slack and disordered, contracted and not extended. It reconnects sinews and joins bones, and has outstanding miraculous merit. Regular taking activates the blood, stops swelling, and generates strength.

Chuanwu/Aconiti radix (paozi/parched), Caowu/Aconiti kusnezoffii (vinegar-boiled), Fengxiang/Liquidambar resin (ground separately), each three jin, Danggui/Angelica (remove the root head, soak in wine one night, dry in the shade), Chishaoyao/red peony half a liang, Chuan Duhuo/Radix Angelicae pubescentis (remove the root), Chuanxiong/Ligusticum (soaked in hot water seven times), Xixin/Asarum (remove the sprouts, cleanly wash), Xiang Baizhi/Angelica dahurica, Shangui/Cinnamon (remove the coarse bark), Baijiang/dried ginger (wrapped in dough and roasted in embers), Huangjiang/ginger (wrapped in wet paper and roasted in embers), Wujiapi/Acanthopanax bark (cleanly wash, remove the bone), Jiegeng/Platycodon (remove the root), Gusuibu/Drynaria (remove the fuzz, stir-fry), Cangzhu/Atractylodes (vinegar-boiled seven times), Heshouwu/Polygonum multiflorum (boiled seven times in black-bean wine), the above each two jin, Zhimu/Anemarrhena half a jin, Moyao/Myrrh half a jin, Niuxi/Achyranthes two jin (soaked in wine seven days, dried/baked). The above items are ground into a fine powder. Each time take two qian, adjusted with salt wine. For disease in the upper body, take after meals; for disease in the lower body, take on an empty stomach. For injury all over the body, take before sleep. Pregnant women must not take it.
