## 勻氣散

### Source Text

凡傷重先下此藥調氣，然後服損藥。

茴香　青皮　厚朴（制）　白芷　烏藥　杏仁（去皮尖）　各半兩　陳皮　麥蘖　前胡　桔梗　蒼朮　粉草各一兩　上為末，每服二錢。水一盞，薑、棗煎至八分，空心服。

### Literal Translation

For all severe injuries, first take this medicine to regulate the qi, then take the injury medicine.

Huixiang/Fennel, Qingpi/green tangerine peel, Houpo/Magnolia bark (processed), Baizhi/Angelica dahurica, Wuyao/Lindera, Xingren/apricot kernel (remove the skin and tip), each half a liang, Chenpi/tangerine peel, Maie/germinated barley, Qianhu/Peucedanum, Jiegeng/Platycodon, Cangzhu/Atractylodes, Fencao/licorice, each one liang. The above are ground into a powder. Each time take two qian. With one cup of water, decoct with ginger and jujube until eight-tenths remains, and take on an empty stomach.
