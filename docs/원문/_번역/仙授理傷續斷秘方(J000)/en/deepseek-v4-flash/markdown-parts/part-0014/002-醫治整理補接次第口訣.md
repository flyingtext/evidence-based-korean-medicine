## 驅風丸

### Source Text

（治效同黑丸子）

骨碎補五兩　川烏　川芎各一兩　草烏　川當歸　牛膝　木鱉各二兩　何首烏四兩　烏金四兩（即百草霜，一云京墨）上為末，醋糊丸，如梧子大。每服三十丸，空心鹽湯下，或荊芥茶湯食後下。

### Literal Translation

(Therapeutic effect the same as Hei Wanzi)

Gusuibu/Drynaria five liang, Chuanwu/Aconiti radix, Chuanxiong/Ligusticum, each one liang, Caowu/Aconiti kusnezoffii, Chuan Danggui/Angelica, Niuxi/Achyranthes, Mubie/Semen momordicae, each two liang, Heshouwu/Polygonum multiflorum four liang, Wujin four liang (that is, Baicaoshuang/soot from the bottom of a pan; one edition says Jingmo/ink stick). The above are ground into a powder, and vinegar paste is used to form pills the size of wutong seeds. Each time take thirty pills, on an empty stomach with salt water, or after meals with Jingjie/Schizonepeta tea soup.
