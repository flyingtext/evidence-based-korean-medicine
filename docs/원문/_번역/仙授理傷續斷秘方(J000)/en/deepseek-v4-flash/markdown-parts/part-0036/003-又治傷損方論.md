## 紫金散

整骨續筋生肌止痛，內傷肝肺，嘔血不止，或在心腹脹痛，四肢無力，左右半身風瘓，並宜服之。

### Literal Translation

Sets bones, reconnects sinews, generates flesh, and stops pain. For internal injury to the liver and lungs, vomiting of blood without stopping, or distending pain in the heart and abdomen, weakness of the four limbs, and wind-paralysis of the left or right half of the body; all are suitable to take.

Zijin tengpi/purple-gold rattan bark two liang, Jiangzhen/Dalbergia heartwood two liang, Xuduan/Dipsacus five liang (must be the fine ones), Gusuibu/Drynaria two liang, Wumingyi/Pyrolusite three liang (burned red and quenched in vinegar seven times), Hupo/amber two liang, Niuxi/Achyranthes three liang (remove the sprouts, soak in wine one night), Danggui/Angelica two liang (remove the tail), Taoren/peach kernel two liang (remove the skin), Puhuang/cattail pollen one liang, Dahuang/Rhubarb one liang (roasted in embers), Puxiao/Mirabilite half a liang (dissolved in hot water, filtered through flower-petal paper seven times). The above items are ground into a powder, adjusted with Sumu/sappan wood-decocted wine, and taken three times a day; it will take effect.
