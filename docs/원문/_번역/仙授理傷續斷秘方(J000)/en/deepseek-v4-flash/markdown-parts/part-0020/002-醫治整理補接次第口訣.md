## 五積散

### Source Text

治五癆七傷，凡被傷頭痛，傷風發寒，姜煎二錢，仍入蔥白，食後熱服。

蒼朮　桔梗各二十兩　枳殼　陳皮各六兩　芍藥　白芷　川芎　川歸　甘草　肉桂　茯苓　半夏（湯泡）各三兩　厚朴　乾薑各四兩　麻黃（去根節）六兩　上除枳殼、桂兩件外，余細銼，用慢火炒，令色變攤冷，入枳殼、桂令勻，每服三錢。水一盞，姜三片，煎至半盞熱服。

### Literal Translation

Treats the five consumptions and seven injuries. For all head injuries, wind injury with chills and fever, decoct two qian with ginger, and also add scallion white, and take hot after meals.

Cangzhu/Atractylodes, Jiegeng/Platycodon, each twenty liang, Zhiqiao/bitter orange, Chenpi/tangerine peel, each six liang, Shaoyao/Peony, Baizhi/Angelica dahurica, Chuanxiong/Ligusticum, Chuan Gui/Angelica, Gancao/Licorice, Rougui/Cinnamon, Fuling/Poria, Banxia/Pinellia (soaked in hot water), each three liang, Houpo/Magnolia bark, Ganjiang/dried ginger, each four liang, Mahuang/Ephedra (remove the root and nodes) six liang. Of the above, except the two items Zhiqiao and Gui (Rougui), the rest are finely chopped, stir-fried over slow fire until the color changes, spread out to cool, then the Zhiqiao and Gui are added and mixed evenly. Each time take three qian. With one cup of water and three slices of ginger, decoct until half a cup remains and take hot.
