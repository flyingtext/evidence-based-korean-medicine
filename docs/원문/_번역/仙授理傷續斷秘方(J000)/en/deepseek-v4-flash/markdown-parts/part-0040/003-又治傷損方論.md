## 仙正散

### Source Text

（洗藥）

治男子婦人骨斷，用此煎水洗後整骨。卻用烏龍角貼之。如破留口當夾縛。即依前方為之。

肉桂二錢（去皮）　當歸三錢（去尾）　胡索五錢　白芷五錢　蒼朮一兩　赤芍藥五錢　防風一兩　荊芥四兩　上㕮咀。每服五錢，水五升，乾荷葉兩皮，煎至七分，去滓。於損處斷處，及冷水風腳，筋脈拘急不得屈伸，行步艱苦，用此藥熱蒸，用被蓋覆，候溫淋洗。

### Literal Translation

(Washing medicine)

Treats broken bones in men and women. Use this decocted in water to wash, then set the bone. Then apply Wulong Jiao. If there is a break, leave an opening and bind and splint. Then follow the previous formula in doing it.

Rougui/Cinnamon two qian (remove the skin), Danggui/Angelica three qian (remove the tail), Husuo/Corydalis five qian, Baizhi/Angelica dahurica five qian, Cangzhu/Atractylodes one liang, Chishaoyao/red peony five qian, Fangfeng/Saposhnikovia one liang, Jingjie/Schizonepeta four liang. The above are roughly chopped (fuzu). Each time take five qian, with five sheng of water and two pieces of dried lotus leaf, decoct until seven-tenths remains, and remove the dregs. At the injured place and the broken place, and for cold-water wind-foot, tension of the sinews and vessels unable to flex or extend, and difficulty in walking, use this medicine to steam hot, cover with a quilt, and when warm, pour over and wash.
