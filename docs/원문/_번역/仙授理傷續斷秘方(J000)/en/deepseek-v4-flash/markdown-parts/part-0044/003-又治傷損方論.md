## 陰紅湯

### Source Text

專治婦人傷損，瘀血不散，腹肚膨脹，大小便不通，上攻心腹，悶亂至死者。急將此藥通下。卻依前，次第服藥。

鹿角膠　產婦油發各一錢（燒）　沒藥三錢　上用酒一大盞煎服。

### Literal Translation

Specifically treats women's injuries, blood stasis not dispersing, distension of the belly, blockage of urination and defecation, attacking upward into the heart and abdomen, confusion and distress to the point of death. Urgently use this medicine to purge it down. Then follow the previous [formulas] and take the medicines in sequence.

Lujiaojiao/deer-horn glue, Chanfu youfa/oiled hair of a woman in labor, each one qian (burned), Moyao/Myrrh three qian. The above are decocted in one large cup of wine and taken.
