## 又方

### Source Text

用乳香、沒藥各一兩，別研，次用血竭、自然銅、無名異、醋煮黃木鱉子各一兩，地龍二兩，併為末，蜜丸如龍眼大。嚼爛，熱酒嚥下。俟了，用生蔥嚼解。

### Literal Translation

Use Ruxiang/Frankincense and Moyao/Myrrh, each one liang, ground separately. Next use Xuejie/Dragon's blood, Zirantong/Pyritum, Wumingyi/Pyrolusite, and vinegar-boiled yellow Mubiezi/Semen momordicae, each one liang, and Dilong/earthworm two liang, all ground into a powder, and form honey pills the size of longan fruit. Chew thoroughly and swallow with hot wine. After it is done, chew fresh scallion to resolve it.
