## 大活血丹

### Source Text

治撲損傷折，骨碎筋傷，疼痛浮腫，腹有瘀血，灌注四肢，煩滿不安。癰疽發背，筋肉壞爛。諸般風疾，左癱右瘓，手足頑麻。婦人血風諸疾，產後敗血不行，流入四肢，頭面浮腫，血氣疼痛。渾身疼痹。經脈湛濁，風癆發動，百節痠疼，並宜服之。每服半丸，用無灰酒磨化，微煎三五沸溫服，不拘時，不限多少。此藥將紗葛袋收掛淨處，經久不壞，可備急用。孕婦莫服。損在上食後服，在下空心服，傷重不拘，余仿此。

天南星一斤（薑汁浸一宿，焙）　芍藥一斤（赤白皆可）　骨碎補一斤（焙，石上生者佳）　黑豆一斤（酒煮焙乾）　大慄間一斤（老者去皮，焙）　川烏一斤（炮）　自然銅半斤（火煅，酸醋淬存性，取半斤）　血竭六兩（別研）　細辛十兩（去苗葉，取十兩）　白芷一斤　木鱉半斤（去殼細切，麩炒，取半斤）　川牛膝一斤（去蘆，酒浸，焙，取一斤）　沒藥四兩（別研，如無，降真香為末代）　乳香半斤（別研，如無，以三倍楓香代之）　青桑炭十斤（青桑木取如臂大者，去皮葉，炭火煅，令赤煙起，用酸醋殺為炭）　上桑、慄、豆、補、星、藥六味為末，和余藥研為細末，用米醋煮糯糊拌入。臼搗千杵，方聚眾人急下手丸。下手稍緩則拆。陰乾半月，然後用火焙，或曬一日。大丸重六文，濕中丸重三文。濕干則以漆抹在手上。取兩三丸挪漆為衣。每服半丸。合此藥勿令四眼見之。更忌雞犬婦人，見之則折矣。

### Literal Translation

Treats injuries from falls and blows, broken bones and injured sinews, pain and swelling, blood stasis in the abdomen, pouring into the four limbs, fullness and restlessness. Carbuncles and deep abscesses on the back, rotten flesh and sinews. All kinds of wind disorders, left-sided paralysis and right-sided paralysis, numbness of the hands and feet. Women's various blood-wind disorders, postpartum retained lochia not moving, flowing into the four limbs, swelling of the head and face, pain of blood and qi. Pain and numbness of the whole body. Turbid and stagnant channels and vessels, wind-consumption (lao) attacks, aching pain of all the joints; all are suitable to take. Each time take half a pill, grind and dissolve it in ash-free wine, slightly decoct for three to five boils, and take warm. Not limited to a fixed time, and not limited in amount. This medicine should be kept in a gauze or kudzu-cloth bag hung in a clean place; it will not spoil for a long time and can be kept for emergencies. Pregnant women must not take it. For injuries in the upper body, take after meals; for injuries in the lower body, take on an empty stomach; for severe injuries, no restriction; the rest follow this.

Tiannanxing/Arisaema one jin (soaked in ginger juice one night, dried/baked), Shaoyao/Peony one jin (red or white both acceptable), Gusuibu/Drynaria one jin (dried/baked; that which grows on stone is best), Heidou/black soybean one jin (boiled in wine, dried/baked), Dali jian/chestnut one jin (old ones, remove the skin, dried/baked), Chuanwu/Aconiti radix one jin (paozi/parched), Zirantong/Pyritum half a jin (calcined over fire, quenched in sour vinegar to preserve its nature, take half a jin), Xuejie/Dragon's blood six liang (ground separately), Xixin/Asarum ten liang (remove the sprouts and leaves, take ten liang), Baizhi/Angelica dahurica one jin, Mubie/Semen momordicae half a jin (remove the shell, finely cut, stir-fried with bran, take half a jin), Chuan Niuxi/Achyranthes one jin (remove the root, soak in wine, dry/bake, take one jin), Moyao/Myrrh four liang (ground separately; if none, use Jiangzhenxiang/Dalbergia heartwood ground into powder as a substitute), Ruxiang/Frankincense half a jin (ground separately; if none, substitute with three times the amount of Fengxiang/Liquidambar resin), Qingsang tan/green mulberry charcoal ten jin (take green mulberry wood about the size of an arm, remove the bark and leaves, calcine over charcoal fire until red smoke rises, then use sour vinegar to quench it into charcoal). Of the above, the six ingredients sang (mulberry), li (chestnut), dou (bean), bu (Gusuibu), xing (Tiannanxing), yao (peony) are ground into powder, and mixed with the remaining medicines ground into a fine powder. Use rice vinegar to cook glutinous rice paste and mix it in. Pound in a mortar one thousand pestles, then gather many people and quickly form the pills by hand. If the hands are slightly slow, [the pills] will break apart. Dry in the shade for half a month, then dry over fire, or sun-dry for one day. Large pills weigh six wen; wet medium pills weigh three wen. When wet and dry, apply lacquer on the hands. Take two or three pills and roll them in lacquer as a coating. Each time take half a pill. When preparing this medicine, do not let four-eyed [i.e., pregnant] people see it. Also avoid chickens, dogs, and women; if they see it, [the medicine] will break apart.
