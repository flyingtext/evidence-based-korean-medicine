## 膠艾湯

### Source Text

專治婦人尋常經脈不通，宜先服此，後服鱉甲散。

乾地黃三錢　阿膠一錢　川芎　艾葉各一錢　上㕮咀。每服二錢，水一大盞，酒半盞，煎至八分，不拘時溫服。

### Literal Translation

Specifically treats women's ordinary blockage of the channels and vessels. One should first take this, then take Biejia San.

Gan Dihuang/dried rehmannia three qian, Ejiao/donkey-hide gelatin one qian, Chuanxiong/Ligusticum, Aiye/mugwort leaf, each one qian. The above are roughly chopped (fuzu). Each time take two qian, with one large cup of water and half a cup of wine, decoct until eight-tenths remains, and take warm without regard to time.
