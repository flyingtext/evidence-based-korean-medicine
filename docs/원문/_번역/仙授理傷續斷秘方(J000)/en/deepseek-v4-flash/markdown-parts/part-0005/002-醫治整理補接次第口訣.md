## 小紅丸

### Source Text

治踒折傷損，皮破骨出，手足碎斷，筋肉壞爛疼痛。甚至晝夜叫呼，百治不止。手足久損，筋骨差爻，舉動不得。損後傷風濕，支節攣縮，遂成偏廢。勞傷筋骨，肩背疼痛，四肢疲乏，動用無力，常服壯筋骨，治經絡，生氣血。每服三十丸，用生薑煎酒，或鹽湯吞下。不拘時候，孕婦莫服。

### Literal Translation

Treats sprains and injuries from falls, broken skin with bone protruding, hands and feet broken into pieces, rotten flesh and sinews with severe pain. Even to the point of crying out day and night, with a hundred treatments not stopping it. Long-standing injuries of the hands and feet, sinews and bones dislocated, unable to move. After injury, attacked by wind and dampness, the limbs contract and shrink, and thus it becomes a partial disability. Overstrain injures the sinews and bones, pain of the shoulders and back, fatigue of the four limbs, weakness in movement and use. Regular taking strengthens the sinews and bones, regulates the channels and collaterals, and generates qi and blood. Each time take thirty pills, swallowed with ginger-decocted wine, or salt water. Not limited to a fixed time. Pregnant women must not take it.

Gusuibu/Drynaria six liang (processed with ginger, dried/baked, take six liang), native Danggui/Angelica six liang (dried/baked and taken), Chuanwu/Aconiti radix six liang (roasted in embers), Baiyangpi/white poplar bark six liang (dried/baked), Rougui/Cinnamon four liang (not exposed to fire), Ezhu/Zedoary two liang (dried/baked), Dingxiang/Clove three liang, Ganjiang/dried ginger two liang (dried/baked), Chuanxiong/Ligusticum three liang, Xixin/Asarum four liang (dried/baked), Fuzi/Aconiti lateralis radix preparata three and a half liang (roasted in embers, remove the skin), Ruxiang/Frankincense three qian (ground separately, not dried/baked), Moyao/Myrrh three qian (ground separately), Shaoyao/Peony six liang (dried/baked). Of the above, the four ingredients bu (Gusuibu), yao (peony), gui (Danggui), yang (Baiyangpi) use those native to the locality; the remaining eight ingredients are ground into a fine powder, and the Ruxiang and Moyao are separately prepared. Mix with vinegar paste to form pills the size of green beans, coated with xinzhu (cinnabar). Each time take thirty pills with warm wine. For external application, use fresh ginger juice to decoct wine, or salt water, both acceptable; not limited to a fixed time.
