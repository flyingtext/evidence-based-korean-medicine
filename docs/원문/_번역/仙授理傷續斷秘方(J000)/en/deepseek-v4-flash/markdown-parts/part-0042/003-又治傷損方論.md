## 接骨散

### Source Text

治飛禽骨斷，從高墜下，驢馬跌折，筋斷骨碎，痛不可忍。此乃接骨續筋，止痛活血。

硼砂一錢半　水粉　當歸各一錢　上為末，每服二錢，煎蘇木湯服訖。時時但飲蘇木湯立效。

### Literal Translation

Treats broken bones of flying birds, falls from high places, fractures from donkeys and horses, severed sinews and broken bones, with pain that cannot be endured. This reconnects bones and sinews, stops pain, and activates the blood.

Pengsha/borax one and a half qian, Shuifen/water powder, Danggui/Angelica, each one qian. The above are ground into a powder. Each time take two qian, decoct Sumu/sappan wood soup and take it. From time to time, simply drink Sumu soup and it will take effect immediately.
