## 黑丸子

### Source Text

治打撲傷損，驢馬跌墜，骨斷筋碎，百節疼痛，瘀血不散，浮腫結毒，一切風疾，四肢疼痹，筋痿力乏，渾身倦怠，手足緩弱，行步不前，婦人諸般血風勞損，並宜服之。每服二十丸三十丸，用煨蔥酒或茶任下，孕婦莫服。

白蘞一斤（焙）　白芨四兩（焙）　南星六兩（焙）　芍藥十兩（焙）　土當歸四兩（焙）骨碎補八兩（焙）　川烏三兩（焙）　牛膝六兩（焙）　百草霜十兩　赤小豆一斤　上除星、芍、歸、補、膝、豆用土產者，草霜釜上取。同為末，醋糊為丸。如梧子大，每服三二十丸。

### Literal Translation

Treats injuries from blows and falls, falls from donkeys and horses, broken bones and crushed sinews, pain of all the joints, blood stasis not dispersing, swelling and accumulated toxins, all wind disorders, pain and numbness of the four limbs, sinew weakness and loss of strength, fatigue of the whole body, weakness and slowness of the hands and feet, inability to walk forward, and women's various blood-wind consumptive injuries; all are suitable to take. Each time take twenty to thirty pills, swallowed with roasted-scallion wine or tea as desired. Pregnant women must not take it.

Bailian/Ampelopsis one jin (dried/baked), Baiji/Bletilla four liang (dried/baked), Nanxing/Arisaema six liang (dried/baked), Shaoyao/Peony ten liang (dried/baked), native Danggui/Angelica four liang (dried/baked), Gusuibu/Drynaria eight liang (dried/baked), Chuanwu/Aconiti radix three liang (dried/baked), Niuxi/Achyranthes six liang (dried/baked), Baicaoshuang/soot from the bottom of a pan ten liang, Chixiaodou/red adzuki bean one jin. Of the above, except that xing (Nanxing), shao (Shaoyao), gui (Danggui), bu (Gusuibu), qi (Niuxi), dou (bean) use locally produced ones, and the Baicaoshuang is taken from the bottom of the pan. Grind all together into a powder, and form pills with vinegar paste, the size of wutong seeds. Each time take thirty to twenty pills.
