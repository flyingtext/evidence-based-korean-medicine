## 大紅丸

### Source Text

治撲損傷折，骨碎筋斷，疼痛痹冷，內外俱損，瘀血留滯，外腫內痛，肢節痛倦，應諸損痛，不問年深日近，並宜服之。常服補損，堅筋固骨，滋血生力，神驗不可具述。每服三十丸，溫酒醋湯任下，不拘時候。孕婦莫服。

赤蘞一斤（即何首烏，焙乾）　川烏一斤七兩（火煨坼）　天南星一斤（焙）　芍藥一斤（焙）　土當歸十兩（焙）　骨碎補一斤（薑製焙）　牛膝十兩（酒浸焙）　細辛八兩（去苗葉，焙）　赤小豆二升（焙）　自然銅四兩（煅存性）　青桑炭五斤（煅醋淬，欠此一味亦可，其上俱要制焙後方秤斤兩）　上蘞、星、芍藥、歸、補、膝、辛七味，並用當土者。同餘藥羅為末，醋煮麵糊為丸。如梧桐子大，朱為衣。每服三十丸，溫酒下，醋湯亦可。損在上食後服，在下空心服。傷重不拘時服。或與小紅丸互用亦可。

### Literal Translation

Treats injuries from falls and blows, broken bones and severed sinews, pain, numbness and cold, damage to both the interior and exterior, retained blood stasis, external swelling and internal pain, aching and fatigue of the limbs and joints. It responds to all injury pain, whether of recent or long standing, and is suitable to take. Regular taking repairs damage, strengthens the sinews and firms the bones, nourishes the blood and generates strength; its miraculous efficacy cannot be fully described. Each time take thirty pills, with warm wine or vinegar soup as desired; not limited to a fixed time. Pregnant women must not take it.

Chilian one jin (that is, Heshouwu/Polygonum multiflorum, dried/baked), Chuanwu/Aconiti radix one jin seven liang (roasted over fire until split), Tiannanxing/Arisaema one jin (dried/baked), Shaoyao/Peony one jin (dried/baked), native Danggui/Angelica ten liang (dried/baked), Gusuibu/Drynaria one jin (processed with ginger, dried/baked), Niuxi/Achyranthes ten liang (soaked in wine, dried/baked), Xixin/Asarum eight liang (remove sprouts and leaves, dried/baked), Chixiaodou/red adzuki bean two sheng (dried/baked), Zirantong/Pyritum four liang (calcined to preserve its nature), Qingsang tan/green mulberry charcoal five jin (calcined and quenched in vinegar; if this one ingredient is lacking it is also acceptable; all of the above must be processed and dried/baked before weighing out the jin and liang). Of the above, the seven ingredients lian (Chilian), xing (Tiannanxing), shaoyao (peony), gui (Danggui), bu (Gusuibu), qi (Niuxi), xin (Xixin) all use those native to the locality. Together with the remaining medicines, sift into a powder, and cook vinegar-boiled flour paste to form pills the size of wutong seeds, coated with cinnabar. Each time take thirty pills with warm wine; vinegar soup is also acceptable. For injuries in the upper body, take after meals; for injuries in the lower body, take on an empty stomach. For severe injuries, take without regard to time. It may also be used interchangeably with Xiaohong Wan.
