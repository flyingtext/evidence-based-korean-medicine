## 當歸散

### Source Text

治打撲傷損，皮肉破碎，筋骨寸斷，瘀壅滯結，腫不散。或作癰疽，疼痛至甚。因損後中風，手足痿痹，不能舉動。筋骨縫縱，攣縮不舒。及勞役所損，肩背四肢疼痛，並宜服之。此藥大能續筋接骨，剋日取效。

澤蘭十兩　川當歸十兩　芍藥　白芷　川芎　肉桂（去粗皮）各五兩　川續斷十兩　牛膝十兩　川烏　川椒（去目）各三兩　桔梗　甘草各四兩　白楊皮（不用亦可）　細辛五兩（以上俱要淨秤）　上為極細末，每服二錢。熱酒調下，不拘時候。

### Literal Translation

Treats injuries from blows and falls, broken skin and flesh, sinews and bones broken into inch-long pieces, blood stasis congested and bound, swelling not dispersing. Or forming carbuncles and deep abscesses, with pain to the extreme. Because after injury one is struck by wind, the hands and feet become atrophic and numb, unable to move. The sinews and bones become slack and loose, contracted and not relaxed. And injuries from toil and labor, pain of the shoulders, back, and four limbs; all are suitable to take. This medicine is greatly able to reconnect sinews and join bones, and takes effect within a fixed number of days.

Zelan/Lycopus ten liang, Chuan Danggui/Angelica ten liang, Shaoyao/Peony, Baizhi/Angelica dahurica, Chuanxiong/Ligusticum, Rougui/Cinnamon (remove the coarse bark), each five liang, Chuan Xuduan/Dipsacus ten liang, Niuxi/Achyranthes ten liang, Chuanwu/Aconiti radix, Chuanjiao/Zanthoxylum (remove the eyes) each three liang, Jiegeng/Platycodon, Gancao/Licorice each four liang, Baiyangpi/white poplar bark (may be omitted), Xixin/Asarum five liang (all of the above must be weighed clean). The above are ground into an extremely fine powder. Each time take two qian, adjusted with hot wine and taken; not limited to a fixed time.
