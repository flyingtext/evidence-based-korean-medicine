## 白藥末

### Source Text

治打撲傷損，皮肉破碎，筋骨寸斷，瘀血壅滯，結腫不散。或作癰疽，疼痛至甚。或因損後中風，手足痿痹，不能舉動，筋骨偏縱，攣縮不伸。及勞傷破損，肩背四肢疼痛。並宜服之。此藥大宜續筋接骨。刻日取效。婦人產後諸血疾，並皆治之。

### Literal Translation

Treats injuries from blows and falls, broken skin and flesh, sinews and bones broken into inch-long pieces, blood stasis congested, bound swelling not dispersing. Or forming carbuncles and deep abscesses, with pain to the extreme. Or because after injury one is struck by wind, the hands and feet become atrophic and numb, unable to move, the sinews and bones slack and stretched, contracted and not extended. And injuries from overstrain and breakage, pain of the shoulders, back, and four limbs. All are suitable to take. This medicine is greatly suited to reconnecting sinews and joining bones, and takes effect within a fixed number of days. Women's various postpartum blood disorders are all also treated by it.

Baiyangpi/white poplar bark twelve liang (soaked in rice water one night), Jiegeng/Platycodon ten liang (remove the sprouts), Chishaoyao/red peony nine liang (soaked in wine one night), Chuanxiong/Ligusticum half a jin (soaked in hot water seven times), Baizhi/Angelica dahurica ten liang, Shangui/Cinnamon half a jin (remove the coarse bark), Xixin/Asarum half a jin (remove the sprouts), Gancao/Licorice ten liang (honey-roasted), Huajiao/Zanthoxylum five liang (remove the seeds, closed-mouth ones), Chuanwu/Aconiti radix six liang (paozi/parched), Xuduan/Dipsacus six liang (soaked in rice water), Niuxi/Achyranthes six liang (remove the sprouts, soak in wine one night), Zelanye/Lycopus leaf nine liang (remove the forked branches), Danggui/Angelica six liang, Xiangfuzi/Cyperus six liang (stir-fried). The above are ground into a fine powder. Each time take two qian, adjusted with wine and taken; the method of taking is the same as before. Women's various blood-wind qi disorders are also all treated by it.
