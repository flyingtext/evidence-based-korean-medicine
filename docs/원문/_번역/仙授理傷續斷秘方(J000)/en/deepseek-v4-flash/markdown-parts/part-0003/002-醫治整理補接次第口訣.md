## 黑龍散

### Source Text

（貼用）

治跌撲傷損，筋骨碎斷，差爻出臼。先煎蔥湯或藥汁淋洗。拔伸整擦，令骨相續平正後，卻用生薑汁或生地黃汁，和水調稀。卻將熟帛或皮紙，量損處大小，薄攤於上貼之。次以木皮，約如指大片疏排令周匝，將小繩三度縛之要緊，三日一次。再如前淋洗，換藥，貼裹。不可去夾。須護毋令搖動。候骨生牢穩方去夾，則復如故。若被刀箭傷，蟲獸傷齧成瘡穰爛，肌肉不生，跌磕腫痛。並用薑汁，和水調貼。有破則留口，以風流散填塗。

穿山甲六兩（炒黃或燒存性）　丁香皮（六兩）　土當歸（二兩）　百草霜（散血入半兩）　枇杷葉根（去毛入半兩，雲山枇杷根）上焙碾為細末，薑汁水調，或研地黃汁調用。

### Literal Translation

(For external application)

Treats injuries from falls and blows, broken sinews and bones, and dislocations out of the joint. First decoct scallion soup or the medicinal juice and wash [the wound]. Pull out and straighten and set [the bone], so that the bones are made to continue and level. Then use ginger juice or fresh rehmannia juice, mixed with water, to adjust [the medicine] to a thin consistency. Then take cooked silk cloth or bark paper, measure the size of the injured area, spread [the medicine] thinly on it, and apply it on top. Next take tree bark, cut into pieces about the size of a finger, arrange them sparsely all around, and bind them tightly with small cord three times, once every three days. Again wash as before, change the medicine, and apply and wrap. Do not remove the splint. It must be protected and not allowed to move. Wait until the bone grows firm and stable, then remove the splint, and it will be restored as before. If wounded by knife or arrow, or bitten by insects or beasts forming a festering sore, with flesh not growing, or swelling and pain from a fall or knock, in all cases use ginger juice mixed with water to adjust and apply. If there is a break, leave an opening, and fill and apply Fengliu san (Wind-Dispelling Powder) into it.

Chuanshanjia/Pangolin scales six liang (stir-fried yellow, or burned to preserve its nature), Dingxiangpi/Cinnamon bark (six liang), native Danggui/Angelica (two liang), Baicaoshuang/soot from the bottom of a pan (for dispersing blood, add half a liang), Pipaye gen/loquat leaf root (remove the fuzz, add half a liang; said to be mountain loquat root). The above are dried (baked), ground into a fine powder, adjusted with ginger juice and water, or ground rehmannia juice may be used to adjust.
