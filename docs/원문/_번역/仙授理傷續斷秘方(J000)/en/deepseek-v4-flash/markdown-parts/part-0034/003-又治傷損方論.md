## 烏龍角

### Source Text

（貼藥）

治跌撲傷損，筋骨碎斷，差爻出臼。其用法詳見前方黑龍散同。先洗擦整理後調貼夾縛。亦可用此干糝瘡口。

白殭蠶六兩（去絲嘴，炒）　赤小豆六兩　川牛膝六兩（去蘆）　山桂（去皮）　桔梗　白芨　百草霜　山枇杷葉（生，銼，陰乾）各一斤　當歸尾　骨碎補（去毛，炒）　北細辛（去苗）各半斤　白芷　赤芍藥　南星（煨）　何首烏各十兩　白蘞十兩　知母　草烏各三兩（用薑汁煮）　上為細末，如藥潤，亦可焙乾碾之。每用以薑汁或冷水茶水調，攤紙上，於痛腫處貼之。三日一洗一換貼。骨碎須夾。

### Literal Translation

(Application medicine)

Treats injuries from falls and blows, broken sinews and bones, and dislocations out of the joint. Its method of use is detailed in the previous formula, the same as Heilong san. First wash, wipe, and straighten, then adjust and apply, bind and splint. It may also be used dry to sprinkle on the wound opening.

Baijiangcan/white silkworm six liang (remove the silk and mouth, stir-fry), Chixiaodou/red adzuki bean six liang, Chuan Niuxi/Achyranthes six liang (remove the root), Shangui/Cinnamon (remove the skin), Jiegeng/Platycodon, Baiji/Bletilla, Baicaoshuang/soot from the bottom of a pan, Shanpipaye/mountain loquat leaf (fresh, chopped, dried in the shade), each one jin, Dangguiwei/Angelica tail, Gusuibu/Drynaria (remove the fuzz, stir-fry), Bei Xixin/Asarum (remove the sprouts), each half a jin, Baizhi/Angelica dahurica, Chishaoyao/red peony, Nanxing/Arisaema (roasted in embers), Heshouwu/Polygonum multiflorum, each ten liang, Bailian/Ampelopsis ten liang, Zhimu/Anemarrhena, Caowu/Aconiti kusnezoffii, each three liang (boiled in ginger juice). The above are ground into a fine powder; if the medicine is moist, it may also be dried/baked and then ground. Each time for use, adjust with ginger juice or cold water or tea water, spread on paper, and apply to the painful and swollen area. Wash and change the application once every three days. A broken bone must be splinted.
