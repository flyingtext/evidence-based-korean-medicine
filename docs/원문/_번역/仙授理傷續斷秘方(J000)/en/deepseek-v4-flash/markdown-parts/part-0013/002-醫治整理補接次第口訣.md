## 搜風丸

### Source Text

治風損腰痛頭疼（治效與黑丸子同）

何首烏　南星　骨碎補　川烏各半斤　土牛膝　芍藥各五兩（一云二兩）　細辛三兩　當歸十兩　白蘚皮　上為末，醋糊為丸，如梧子大。每服三十丸，溫酒鹽湯不拘時吞下。

### Literal Translation

Treats wind-injury low back pain and headache (its therapeutic effect is the same as Hei Wanzi).

Heshouwu/Polygonum multiflorum, Nanxing/Arisaema, Gusuibu/Drynaria, Chuanwu/Aconiti radix, each half a jin, native Niuxi/Achyranthes, Shaoyao/Peony, each five liang (one edition says two liang), Xixin/Asarum three liang, Danggui/Angelica ten liang, Baixianpi/Dictamnus bark. The above are ground into a powder, and vinegar paste is used to form pills the size of wutong seeds. Each time take thirty pills, swallowed with warm wine or salt water, not limited to a fixed time.
