## 洗藥

### Source Text

凡傷重者，用此方煎湯洗之，然後敷藥。

生蔥（切斷，一本用生薑）　荊芥（銼）　土當歸　上三味煎湯，溫熱淋洗。

### Literal Translation

For all severe injuries, use this formula, decoct it into a soup and wash [the wound], then apply the medicine.

Fresh scallion (cut into sections; one edition uses fresh ginger), Jingjie/Schizonepeta (chopped), native Danggui/Angelica. Decoct these three ingredients into a soup, and when warm, pour it over and wash [the wound].
