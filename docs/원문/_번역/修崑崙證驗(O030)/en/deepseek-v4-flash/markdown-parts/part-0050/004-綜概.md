## 綜概

### Source Text

十、《莊子》有云：「道引之士，養形之人」，《留侯世家》：「道引不食谷」，注云：「服氣法」。此法不知世有傳書否。然細繹「道引」二字之義，道之使動，引之使行，似與揉法相近。

### Literal Translation

Ten: The 《Zhuangzi》 says: “The scholar of guiding and leading, the person who nourishes the form,” and the 《Biography of the Marquis of Liu》 says: “Guiding and leading, not eating grain,” with a note saying: “The method of taking qi.” I do not know whether this method has a transmitted book in the world. But carefully analyzing the meaning of the two words “guiding and leading” (道引), dao is to make it move, and yin is to make it go; it seems close to the rubbing method.
