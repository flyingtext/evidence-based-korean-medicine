## 綜概

### Source Text

不必亟亟亂揉也。今將揉夾車及顴，吊動行消之積列後：

（一）自腦後，由頂鬢至夾車下去。

（二）由夾車上出。

### Literal Translation

There is no need to rub hastily and in disorder. Now I will list the accumulations that are drawn and moved and dispersed by rubbing the jaw and cheekbones:

(1) From behind the head, through the top and temples, down to the jaw.

(2) Going out from above the jaw.
