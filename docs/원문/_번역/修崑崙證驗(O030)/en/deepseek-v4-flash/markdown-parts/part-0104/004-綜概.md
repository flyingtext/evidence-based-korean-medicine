## 綜概

### Source Text

再凡有揉者，從無腦暈頭痛之病，眾口同聲，此修崑崙不死之明證也。

三十八、予揉夾車起手，終於海底，所歷之境如此，若積有淺深，揉有先後，殊途同歸，原無一定板法也。年餘竭力，舊疴悉除，耳目重明，手足便利，陽萎復起，禿髮再生，實不自覺為七十也。

### Literal Translation

Moreover, among all who rubbed, there was never the disease of dizziness or headache; all mouths speak with one voice; this is the clear evidence that cultivating Kunlun prevents death.

Thirty-eight: I began by rubbing the jaw and ended at the perineum; the realms I passed through were like this. If the accumulation has shallow and deep, and the rubbing has before and after, the paths differ but the destination is the same; there is originally no fixed rigid method. After more than a year of effort, all the old ailments were removed, the ears and eyes became bright again, the hands and feet became convenient, impotence rose again, and hair grew back on the bald head; I truly did not feel that I was seventy.
