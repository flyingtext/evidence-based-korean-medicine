## 綜概

### Source Text

眥𡟬，按也，摩也。以兩手按目四眥，令眼神光明；按𡟬皺紋，可以沐浴老容。

古書：頂中旋毛中為百會穴，前寸半為前頂，前三寸為囟門。

### Literal Translation

The canthus (眥) is pressing and rubbing. Press the four canthi of the eyes with both hands, making the eye spirit bright; press and rub the wrinkles, and one can bathe the old countenance.

Ancient book: In the center of the whorl of hair on the top of the head is the Baihui point (百會穴); one and a half inches in front is the Qianding (前頂); three inches in front is the fontanel (囟門).
