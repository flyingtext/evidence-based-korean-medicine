## 綜概

### Source Text

大約皆六陰之積上至夾車，還而入喉，雖粗大亦寬綽，而行若六陽經，於下胲剝削細小，面上能容，方始上走。項後亦有粗大能上走者，須揉頭頂及角吊之。若脊骨四周之積，必由耳門後上，粗大亦可吊消，仍由頂額下夾車、大迎穴，由頸中人迎穴入腹，別無出路故也。

### Literal Translation

Approximately all are accumulations of the six yin that rise to the jaw, return, and enter the throat. Although thick and large, they are also roomy, and they travel like the six yang channels, being pared down and made small at the lower jaw, so that the face can contain them, and only then do they begin to go upward. Behind the neck there are also thick and large ones that can go upward; one must rub the crown and the corners to draw them. If it is the accumulation around the spine bone, it must rise behind the ear gate; even if thick and large, it can be drawn and dispersed, and still descends from the crown and forehead to the jaw and Daying point, and from the Renying point in the neck enters the abdomen, because there is no other outlet.
