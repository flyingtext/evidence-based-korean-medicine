## 綜概

### Source Text

人之腹中亦然，飲食不時，胃中蘊郁成熱，時則有吞酸之病，氣蒸而酸，久必作泡。泡輕而浮，先氣而行，周流四肢、百竅，氣可轉回，小泡淤停黏著，日積日多，成條成塊，凡有孔隙窒塞而百病生矣。吊之能行，揉之即散，所以為水泡之論也。

### Literal Translation

It is the same in the human abdomen: if one eats and drinks at irregular times, the stomach accumulates and becomes hot, and at times there is the disease of acid swallowing; the qi steams and turns sour, and over time it must produce bubbles. The bubbles are light and float, traveling ahead of the qi, circulating through the four limbs and the hundred orifices; the qi can turn back, but the small bubbles silt up, stop, and stick, accumulating day by day and becoming strips and lumps; wherever there are gaps and holes, they are blocked, and the hundred diseases arise. Drawing them, they can move; rubbing them, they disperse; this is the basis of the water blister theory.
