## 序

### Source Text

欲益反損，雖悔莫追，漫云三品已成，孰是十全為上，我乾一先生以《修崑崙證驗》欠示，惟藉「揉」之一法療病之百源，注蒙叟之養生，鄙韓康之賣藥。運其心中之矩，垂為肘後之方，證以終身，效昭昭而可案；易於反掌，功歷歷其堪稽，比摩頂放踵之為，可利天下；無濡首剝膚之害，足導後生。

道光丁未六月上浣錫山侯桐謹序

### Literal Translation

Wanting to benefit but instead harming, though one regrets it, it cannot be pursued. To speak carelessly that the three grades are already complete, who knows that the tenfold complete is best? My Master Qianyi, with the 《修崑崙證驗》 (Evidence of Cultivating Kunlun), shows that only by relying on the one method of “rubbing” (揉) can one treat the hundred sources of disease, annotating the elder's (Zhuangzi's) nourishing of life, and despising Han Kang's selling of medicine. He moves the compass in his heart, and hands it down as a formula kept at the elbow. He proves it with his whole life, and the effect is clear and can be examined; it is as easy as turning the palm, and the merit is evident and can be verified. Compared to the act of rubbing the head and wearing out the heels, it can benefit the world; without the harm of soaking the head and peeling the skin, it is sufficient to guide later generations.

Respectfully written as a preface by Hou Tong of Xishan, in the first ten days of the sixth month of the dingwei year of the Daoguang reign.
