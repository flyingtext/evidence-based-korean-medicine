## 跋

### Source Text

此惟精惟一，允執厥中，之所以大聖人也。方書云：誨言病、怕治病，即是病，即此意也。然則忠恕絜矩，自反多識，前言往行，以畜其德，人不同與。

### Literal Translation

This is why being only refined and only single, and truly holding the middle, is how one becomes a great sage. The formula books say: To be ashamed to speak of disease and afraid to treat disease is itself disease; this is the same meaning. Then loyalty, reciprocity, the measuring square, self-reflection, and much knowledge, and the former words and past deeds, to store up one's virtue, are these not the same for people?
