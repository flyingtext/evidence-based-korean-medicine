## 跋

### Source Text

予六十餘年之積，亦云多矣，竭數月之工已獲大效，年餘之後，一洗而淨，可不謂之神速乎？不知我獨如此，或人人皆可如此。茫茫天壤，前無古人，後無來者，孰從而就正之哉？於其完也，書此一冊，交子侄等，留以咨訪，亦有同予者否，乞其鑑定焉。或有同志自修，仿為以保其身，亦可匡予之不逮然。

### Literal Translation

My accumulation of more than sixty years can also be said to be much; exhausting several months of work, I already obtained great effect, and after more than a year, it was washed clean in one go. Can this not be called spirit-like and swift? I do not know whether I alone am like this, or whether everyone can be like this. In the vast heaven and earth, there is no one before and no one after; from whom can I seek correction? At its completion, I wrote this one book and gave it to my sons and nephews, leaving it for consultation; is there also one like me? I beg their appraisal. If there are those of like mind who cultivate themselves, imitating it to preserve their bodies, they can also correct my shortcomings.
