## 曬說

### Source Text

第不可遮蓋著衣，及致受熱也。月之未申，歲之伏也；時之未申，日之伏也。急病則隨日可曬，亦見奇效。

### Literal Translation

Only one must not cover or wear clothes, so as to receive heat. The wei and shen of the month are the fu of the year; the wei and shen of the hour are the fu of the day. For acute disease, one can sun according to the day, and also see marvelous effect.
