## 揉積論

### Source Text

從古良醫代不乏人，真可起死回生，實是出神入化，然垂名千古而心法無傳，徒留醫案方藥，後人則效無從。今茲之道，若大路然，人人能為，時時可行，惟患人之不求，不患法之不傳也。集說既成，弁以揉積論，願先覽焉。

### Literal Translation

From ancient times good physicians have not been lacking in each generation; truly they could raise the dead and restore life, truly entering the spirit and leaving the transformation. Yet they left their names for a thousand ages while their heart-methods were not transmitted, leaving only medical cases and formulas, so that later people had nothing to follow. The present Way is like a great road: everyone can do it, and at any time it can be practiced. One only worries that people do not seek it, not that the method is not transmitted. The collected sayings being complete, I place the Discourse on Rubbing Accumulation at the head, and wish that it be read first.
