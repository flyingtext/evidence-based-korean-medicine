## 綜概

### Source Text

淤積之處，其皮糟，揉破塗礬，結痂愈快。先以礬塗，可免擦損。或以大礬塊代揉，兼可以括磨筋骨上之積，事半而功倍矣。

### Literal Translation

At the places of silted accumulation, the skin is rough; if it is rubbed and broken, apply alum (礬), and the scab forms and heals faster. First apply alum, and one can avoid abrasion and injury. Or use a large lump of alum in place of rubbing, and one can also scrape and grind the accumulation on the sinews and bones, achieving twice the merit with half the effort.
