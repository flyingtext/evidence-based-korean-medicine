## 綜概

### Source Text

二十四、各處已淨，喉中忽作梗芥，方知貼項前又有二根向外之尖所刺，須於耳上揉吊乃消。行逕由額角上頂，下腦後，自夾車闊三寸余，二時方完。稍下寸余，接連又是二根，直出耳上，交頂而下，惟揉頭角吊之。

### Literal Translation

Twenty-four: The various places were already clear, when suddenly a choking cough arose in the throat; only then did I know that attached in front of the neck there were two more roots with tips pointing outward that were pricking, and I had to rub and draw above the ears to disperse them. The route went from the forehead corners up to the crown, down behind the head, from the jaw more than three inches wide, finishing in two hours. A little more than an inch lower, two more roots came in succession, going straight out above the ears, crossing the crown and descending; I only rubbed the head corners to draw them.
