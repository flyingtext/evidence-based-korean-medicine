## 綜概

### Source Text

三十二、經絡自手交肩至腦後，出耳上，下夾車至缺盆，三陽經左右六根，自缺盆回往頸夾已成十二根，共十八根，皆環貼頸項。一經淤積，經脈仍通，積則淤停，再積則多，再多則長，再長則彎，彎則垂為雙頭，多一根矣。一根變而為三，成為五十四根，頸項焉得不粗？翳膜取給於上，頭皮焉得不緊？頭上從此亦生淤積矣。

### Literal Translation

Thirty-two: The channels cross from the hands to the shoulders, reach behind the head, come out above the ears, descend the jaw to Quepen; the three yang channels, six roots on left and right, returning from Quepen to the neck and jaw, have already become twelve roots, eighteen in all, all encircling and attaching to the neck. Once silted and accumulated, the channels and vessels still flow, but the accumulation is silted and stops; accumulating again it becomes more, more again it becomes longer, longer again it becomes bent, and bent it hangs down as a double head, one root more. One root changes into three, becoming fifty-four roots; how could the neck not become thick? The nebula takes its supply from above; how could the scalp not become tight? From this the head also produces silted accumulation.
