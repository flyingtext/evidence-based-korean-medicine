## 曬說

### Source Text

因思氣血無不由頸上下，不論所以然，且揉頸項以圖目前，不知所謂經絡也。幸無甚病，而頸中間亦鬆軟，惟無法淨去耳。又每逢行走急促，胸膈作木石碰聲，左脅牽痛而喘，逢冬咳嗽吐痰、耳足凍瘃，腰腿間作痠痛，此皆積久蔓延而然；彼時實不知也。

### Literal Translation

I thought that qi and blood all pass up and down through the neck, and without discussing the reason, I rubbed the neck to seek immediate relief, not knowing what was called the channels. Fortunately there was no great disease, and the middle of the neck also became loose and soft, only there was no way to remove it completely. Also, whenever I walked hurriedly, the chest and diaphragm made a sound like wood and stone colliding, the left flank was pulled and painful and I panted; in winter I coughed and spat phlegm, my ears and feet had chilblains, and soreness and pain arose between the waist and legs; all of this was because the accumulation had long spread and extended, but at that time I truly did not know it.
