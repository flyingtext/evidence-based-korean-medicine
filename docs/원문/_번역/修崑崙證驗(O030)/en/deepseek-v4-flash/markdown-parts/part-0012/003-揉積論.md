## 揉積論

### Source Text

皮緊、面鼓、項粗。腮縮，耳反、唇掀、結喉、露齒，此形之不足於外者也。再當要害之地，手足則麻木、癱瘓；頸項則瘰癧、噎嗝；口舌則瘖啞、歪斜；耳目則聾瞶、糊塗，此急不待時者也。

### Literal Translation

The skin is tight, the face is swollen, the neck is thick. The cheeks shrink, the ears turn back, the lips are turned up, the Adam's apple protrudes, and the teeth are exposed; these are the forms that are deficient externally. When it reaches the vital places, the hands and feet become numb and paralyzed; the neck becomes scrofula and dysphagia; the mouth and tongue become mute and deviated; the ears and eyes become deaf and confused; these are urgent and cannot wait for time.
