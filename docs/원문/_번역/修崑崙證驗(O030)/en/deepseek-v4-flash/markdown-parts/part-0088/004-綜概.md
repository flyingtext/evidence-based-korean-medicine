## 綜概

### Source Text

又私意六陽六陰皆該入腦，陽在外，走前；陰在內，走後，交接而行，循環無首，隨在分名，無另起理。此皆臆說矣，俟後來者駁之。即以目論，書云眼為肝竅，五臟六腑之精氣上注於目而為之精，筋骨氣血之精與脈並而為之系，上屬於腦，後出於項中，此六經入腦之一證也，經絡歌上惟心肝二經系連目系而已。

### Literal Translation

And I privately think that the six yang and six yin should all enter the brain; yang is outside and goes in front, yin is inside and goes behind, meeting and traveling, circulating without a head, dividing names wherever they are, with no reason for a separate origin. These are all conjectures, to be refuted by later people. Taking the eye as an example, the books say the eye is the orifice of the liver, and the essence-qi of the five viscera and six bowels pours up into the eye and becomes its essence; the essence of sinew, bone, qi, and blood, together with the vessels, becomes its system, which belongs above to the brain and comes out behind in the neck; this is one evidence of the six channels entering the brain. In the channel song, only the heart and liver channels connect with the eye system.
