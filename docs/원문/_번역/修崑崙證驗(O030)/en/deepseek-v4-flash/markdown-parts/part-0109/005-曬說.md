## 曬說

### Source Text

自是每伏必曬，諸積病悉不為患，而潮濕拘牽則截然而止，不乞靈於草木者，幾二十年矣，今則無分冬夏，晴日必曬，間有微汗，無病故也。曬之功力，可云大矣，壯先天之元陽，滋後天之真陰，神光洞徹，表裡不遺，陰翳潛消，營衛無間，即使周身大積，能令伏不為患，非氣血充足能若是乎？當積伏也，血足以養之；及積出也，氣足以運之，去邪扶正，更云神乎？今見孫真人格言，悟而為之，若有鬼神通之者。遂將六十餘年之積期月盡消，內外諸病一掃而清，此正藉氣血之充足也，非數年曬功，能若斯之速乎？所謂自天祐之，吉無不利也。

### Literal Translation

From then on, every dog day I must sun myself, and all the accumulation diseases were no longer a harm, and the dampness and drawing and pulling stopped abruptly. I have not begged for numina from plants and trees for nearly twenty years; now, regardless of winter or summer, on clear days I must sun myself, and occasionally there is slight sweat, because there is no disease. The power and merit of sunning can be said to be great: it strengthens the primordial yang of the prenatal, nourishes the true yin of the postnatal, the spirit light penetrates and is clear, the exterior and interior are not left out, the yin nebula secretly disperses, and the constructive and defensive have no gap. Even if there is great accumulation throughout the body, it can make it lie hidden and not be a harm; if qi and blood were not sufficient, could it be like this? When the accumulation lies hidden, the blood is sufficient to nourish it; and when the accumulation comes out, the qi is sufficient to move it; removing the evil and supporting the upright, can one say it is even more spirit-like? Now I see the maxim of the Perfected Sun, and awakened, I practice it, as if ghosts and spirits penetrate it. So I dispersed the accumulation of more than sixty years completely within a month, and swept clear all the internal and external diseases; this is precisely relying on the sufficiency of qi and blood. Without several years of sunning merit, could it be this fast? This is what is called Heaven blessing it, auspicious and without disadvantage.
