## 綜概

### Source Text

夾車，耳門下之鉤骨也，此處一通，內外上下皆無滯塞矣。其積附於項骨之旁，停於肩背脅肋腰胯腿腳，外視微粗，不能望見也。於夾車直骨中揉之，自能吊動。

### Literal Translation

The jaw (夾車) is the hook bone below the ear gate (耳門). Once this place is opened, there is no obstruction inside or outside, above or below. Its accumulation attaches beside the neck bone, and stops in the shoulders, back, flanks, ribs, waist, hips, legs, and feet; seen externally it is slightly thick, and cannot be seen. Rubbing in the straight bone of the jaw, one can naturally draw and move it.
