## 綜概

### Source Text

因知人病咳嗽，是積尖刺礙肺管；久而吐血，是積大脹破肺管，故血色鮮紅。若積消長合，血止而愈，否則大吐矣。推而至於噎嗝，積成條塊擠住食嗓，陽分開，陰分閉，非積之使然乎？余病可以類推，然仍是陰陽不和之故。

### Literal Translation

From this I know that when a person has the disease of coughing, it is because the tip of the accumulation pricks and obstructs the lung tube; and when it lasts long and one spits blood, it is because the accumulation has grown large and burst the lung tube, so the blood is bright red. If the accumulation disperses and grows in balance, the blood stops and one heals; otherwise one vomits greatly. Extending to dysphagia (噎嗝), the accumulation forms strips and lumps that squeeze and block the food pipe; the yang aspect opens and the yin aspect closes; is this not caused by the accumulation? The rest of my diseases can be inferred by analogy, but still it is the reason of yin and yang not being harmonious.
