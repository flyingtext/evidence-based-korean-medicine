## 跋

### Source Text

譬此揉工，庶幾近似。

<ins>丙午六月立秋日</ins>

<ins>天休子跋之兼以自勖云爾</ins>

心之官則思，思則得之，不思則不得也。若是乎人同此心，心同此理，莫不欲得而竟有不思者，何也？非不欲思也，有所蔽而不能自主也。

### Literal Translation

Compared to this rubbing work, it is nearly similar.

<ins>On the day of the Beginning of Autumn in the sixth month of the bingwu year</ins>

<ins>Tian Xiuzi writes this colophon and also encourages himself</ins>

The office of the heart is to think; thinking, one obtains it; not thinking, one does not obtain it. In this way people share this heart, and the heart shares this principle; none do not want to obtain, yet there are those who do not think. Why? It is not that they do not want to think, but that there is something obscuring them and they cannot govern themselves.
