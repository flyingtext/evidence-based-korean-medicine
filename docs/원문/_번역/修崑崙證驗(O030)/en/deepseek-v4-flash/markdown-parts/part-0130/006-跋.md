## 跋

### Source Text

遂自易名曰天休子，並系以偈：雖休勿休，作德日休，其心休休，何天之休。

休致歸來，死前世事皆肯休矣，惟肯休而不容於肯休者，惟此身之生氣耳。存其心、養其性，修身以俟天休而已。

### Literal Translation

So I changed my name to Tian Xiuzi (Heaven's Resting Son), and appended a verse: Though resting, do not rest; making virtue, rest daily; the heart rests and rests; what is Heaven's rest?

Returning after retirement, all the affairs before death I am willing to rest; only what is willing to rest but cannot be allowed to rest is the vital qi of this body. Preserve the heart, nourish the nature, cultivate the self, and await Heaven's rest.
