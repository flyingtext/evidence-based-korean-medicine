## 綜概

### Source Text

如六陽上頭、夾車諸處宜揉，六陰過莖、海底諸處亦應揉也。思之思之，鬼神通之，信夫！

六、眉心、目眥，六陽經交接處；夾車、下胲，陽經上下出入之路；腎囊、陽莖，陰經上下會走之處；十手足指尖，陰陽交接處；手足腕背，經脈出入處，手足大指後腕骨之上廉曰關，陰陽交互處。數處待常揉之，可以流通氣血。

### Literal Translation

Places such as the six yang rising to the head and the jaw should be rubbed; places such as the six yin passing the penis and the perineum (海底) should also be rubbed. Think, think; the ghosts and spirits penetrate it; believe it!

Six: The space between the brows and the canthi are the places where the six yang channels meet; the jaw and lower jaw are the roads where the yang channels enter and exit above and below; the scrotum (腎囊) and penis (陽莖) are the places where the yin channels meet and travel above and below; the tips of the ten fingers and toes are the places where yin and yang meet; the backs of the wrists and ankles are the places where the channels and vessels enter and exit; the upper edge of the wrist bone behind the big toe of the hand and foot is called the pass (關), the place where yin and yang interact. These several places, if one regularly rubs them, can free the flow of qi and blood.
