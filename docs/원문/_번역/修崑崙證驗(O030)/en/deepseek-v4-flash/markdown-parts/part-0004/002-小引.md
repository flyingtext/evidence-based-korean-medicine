## 小引

### Source Text

《易》曰：懲忿窒欲，又曰：慎言語、節飲食。此修於未病前也。若病臨於身，只以醫藥為修矣，《靈樞》、《素問》，歷歷可考。

### Literal Translation

The 《Changes》 says: Repress anger and stop desire, and also says: Be cautious in speech and moderate in food and drink. This is cultivation before illness arises. If illness comes upon the body, then one only uses medicine as cultivation; the 《Lingshu》 and 《Suwen》 can be examined in detail.
