## 綜概

### Source Text

揉久手痠，則直伸兩臂用力摜兩手腕，痠軟可立止，此神仙自拳然之法。神仙拳：掐訣直臂，閉目唸咒，其手自摜，少時訣散而拳作矣，確有仙氣。予因手痠而截取之，先可流通氣血也。

### Literal Translation

If one rubs for a long time and the hand becomes sore, then stretch both arms straight and forcefully swing both wrists, and the soreness and weakness can be stopped at once; this is the method of the immortals' self-clenching fist. The immortal's fist: pinch the finger gesture, straighten the arm, close the eyes and recite the incantation, and the hand swings by itself; after a short while the gesture disperses and the fist is made; indeed there is immortal aura. Because my hand was sore, I took and used this, and first it can free the flow of qi and blood.
