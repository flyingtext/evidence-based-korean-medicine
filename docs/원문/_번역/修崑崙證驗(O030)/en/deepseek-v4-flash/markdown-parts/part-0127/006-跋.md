## 跋

### Source Text

其中四十餘年，齊、魯、吳、越、楚、粵、燕、豫，馳驅南北，足跡摩停；地方河工，官場皆歷；名韁利絆，辛苦備嘗。此中所恃以立骨者，和平簡易，肯吃虧而不佔便宜，常存知止有定之訓，從無爭競不肯之心，自幼本性直貫於今。己亥冬月，引見更蒙召見，以才具平庸休致，此天休於前也。

### Literal Translation

Among them, for more than forty years, in Qi, Lu, Wu, Yue, Chu, Yue, Yan, and Yu, I galloped north and south, my footprints never stopping; I experienced local river works and officialdom; bound by the reins of fame and the fetters of profit, I tasted all the hardships. What I relied on to stand firm in this was peace, harmony, simplicity, and ease, being willing to suffer loss and not seize advantage, always keeping the teaching of knowing when to stop and having stability, never having a mind of contention or unwillingness, and my nature from childhood has run straight through to the present. In the winter month of the jihai year, I was introduced and further received an audience, and because my talents were mediocre, I was retired; this was Heaven resting me in the front.
