## 綜概

### Source Text

《說文》：囟，頂門骨空，自囟之心如絲相貫不絕。

《六書精蘊》：元神何宅？心為之宅。元神何門？囟為之門。

### Literal Translation

The 《Shuowen》 (Explaining Simple and Compound Characters): The fontanel is the hollow of the top-gate bone; from the fontanel to the heart it is like a thread running through without interruption.

The 《Liushu Jingyun》 (Essence of the Six Scripts): What is the abode of the primordial spirit? The heart is its abode. What is the gate of the primordial spirit? The fontanel is its gate.
