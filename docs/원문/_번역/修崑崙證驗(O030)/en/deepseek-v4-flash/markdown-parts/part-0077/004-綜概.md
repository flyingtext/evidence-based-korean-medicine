## 綜概

### Source Text

隨出之積不復大塊長條，無從再為分別矣。推原其故，頭上清楚之後，如壇去蓋，以下之積皆須上消，走夾車下人迎穴。即如厥陰肝經起足大趾，自腿而上，繞陰器，抵小腹，入胃，屬肝，絡膽，上貫膈，其積尚不多，布於脅肋，尋喉嚨，脅肋多則積亦多矣，必由夾車上消清，方上頏顙進目系也。

### Literal Translation

The accumulation that came out was no longer great lumps and long strips, and there was nothing more to distinguish. Tracing back the reason, after the head was clear, it was like removing the lid of a jar; all the accumulation below had to disperse upward, going to the jaw and down to the Renying point. That is, the jueyin liver channel arises at the big toe, goes up from the leg, winds around the genitals, reaches the lower abdomen, enters the stomach, belongs to the liver, connects with the gallbladder, and runs up through the diaphragm. Its accumulation is not yet much, spread over the flanks and ribs, seeking the throat; if the flanks and ribs are many, the accumulation is also many, and it must disperse clear upward through the jaw before it can go up to the upper throat and enter the eye system.
