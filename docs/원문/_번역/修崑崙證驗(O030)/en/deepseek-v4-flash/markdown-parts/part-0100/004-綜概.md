## 綜概

### Source Text

然則欲治死病者，當舍藥物而求於揉曬矣。附此以勸戒者，吾將以為類者緣也。暴病不吃，覺餓即愈，新病揉之曬之即愈，舊病久揉久曬亦愈，如此而已。

### Literal Translation

Then those who want to treat the disease of death should abandon medicines and seek in rubbing and sunning. I attach this as admonition and warning, for I will take it as the affinity of those of the same kind. For a sudden illness, do not eat; when one feels hungry, it heals. For a new disease, rub it and sun it and it heals. For an old disease, rub it long and sun it long and it also heals. That is all.
