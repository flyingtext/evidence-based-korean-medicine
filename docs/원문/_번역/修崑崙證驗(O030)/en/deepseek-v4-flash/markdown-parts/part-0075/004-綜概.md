## 綜概

### Source Text

揉半日，左肩湧起一塊，斜貫右夾下，回頭由中而下，而腦後乃壅。乃揉耳門上，方始上額頂，反腦後，由夾車消。源源湧上，其大如杯，幸得暢行，方知是太陽經。

### Literal Translation

After rubbing for half a day, a lump surged up on the left shoulder, ran obliquely through below the right jaw, turned back and descended through the middle, and then the back of the head became congested. So I rubbed above the ear gate, and only then did it go up to the forehead and crown, turn back behind the head, and disperse through the jaw. It surged up continuously, as large as a cup; fortunately it flowed freely, and only then did I know it was the taiyang channel.
