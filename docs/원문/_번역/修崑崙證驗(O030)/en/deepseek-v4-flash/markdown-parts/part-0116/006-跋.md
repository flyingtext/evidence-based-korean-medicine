## 跋

### Source Text

天地之大德曰生，信有之矣，然必待人力以成也。如佃田然，除鋤草萊，五穀方茂，若聽其自然，則草滿而田荒矣，豈天不施地不生耶？由芸力之不至也。積之於人也，亦然。

### Literal Translation

The great virtue of Heaven and Earth is called life; truly it exists, but it must wait for human strength to complete it. It is like farming a field: if one removes and hoes the weeds, the five grains flourish; if one lets it be natural, then the weeds fill it and the field is wasted. Is it that Heaven does not bestow and Earth does not give birth? It is because the strength of weeding has not arrived. It is the same with accumulation in people.
