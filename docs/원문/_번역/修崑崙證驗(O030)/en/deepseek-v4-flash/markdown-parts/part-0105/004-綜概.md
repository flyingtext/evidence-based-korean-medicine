## 綜概

### Source Text

壯不如人，老將奚為？但願後來者趁壯年行之，當必精力倍增，早建事業，並試行有效，廣為傳習，同登彼岸，是則予之後望也夫！

### Literal Translation

In youth I was not equal to others; now old, what shall I do? I only hope that later people will practice it in their prime, and they will certainly have doubled energy and establish their careers early; and having tried it and found it effective, they will spread and transmit it widely, and together board the other shore; this is my later hope!
