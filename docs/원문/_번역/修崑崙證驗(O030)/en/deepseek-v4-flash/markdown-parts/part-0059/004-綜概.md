## 綜概

### Source Text

以次而下，退揉人中，不意鼻梁脫殼而下，隨即揉散。予右側臥鼻息不通十餘年矣，不知何故，今忽現出而消，想因諸積漸清，自不能容耳，從此可以右側臥矣。五官有病者，亦可類此求之。

### Literal Translation

Proceeding downward in order, I retreated and rubbed the philtrum (人中); unexpectedly the bridge of the nose shed its shell and came down, and I immediately rubbed it away. I had not been able to breathe through the nose while lying on my right side for more than ten years, and did not know why; now it suddenly appeared and dispersed. I think it was because the various accumulations gradually cleared and could no longer contain themselves; from this I can lie on my right side. Those with disease of the five sense organs can also seek it in this way.
