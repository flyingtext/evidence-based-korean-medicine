## 序

### Source Text

蓋自九鼎雲英與赤薤虹丹而並寶，八瓊絳雪偕玉津風實以稱奇。剎帝利之神丸可飽終日，阿伽陀之珍餌能救眾生，莫不精求赭鞭之原，竊善青囊之術。然而鵲醫罕遇，荀令肺腑能語，則色如土矣，兔藥雖成，但恃甲乙名經，則疾入肓矣。

### Literal Translation

From the beginning, the cloud essence of the Nine Tripods and the rainbow elixir of the red shallot were treasured together, and the eight jade crimson snow together with jade fluid and wind fruit were called extraordinary. The divine pill of the Kshatriya can satisfy one for a whole day, and the precious bait of Agada can save all living beings. None do not carefully seek the origin of the red whip, and secretly excel in the art of the blue bag. Yet the physician Bian Que is rarely encountered; if Xun's viscera could speak, then the complexion would be like earth. Even if the rabbit elixir is completed, if one relies only on the famous classics of the jia and yi, then the disease enters the diaphragm (肓).
