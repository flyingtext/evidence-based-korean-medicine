## 曬說

### Source Text

丁亥年回蘇省親，時年五十二歲，因指麻唇吊，頸項堅硬，筋多瘰癧，肩背有癬，腰作蟲行，雖飲食起居尚是照常，惟於陽事不健而已。親命就名醫診視，雲氣血兩虧，難期脫體，非重用附桂大補氣血不可，立方而散。予以向服熱藥牙必出血，置之。

### Literal Translation

In the dinghai year I returned to Suzhou to visit my parents, when I was fifty-two years old. Because my fingers were numb and my lips were drawn, my neck was stiff, my sinews had much scrofula, my shoulders and back had tinea, and my waist felt like worms crawling, although eating, drinking, and daily life were still as usual, only my virility was not strong. My parents ordered me to be examined by a famous physician, who said that qi and blood were both depleted, and it was hard to expect to escape the body; it was necessary to use aconite and cinnamon heavily to greatly supplement qi and blood. He set a formula and left. Because I had previously found that taking hot medicines always made my teeth bleed, I set it aside.
