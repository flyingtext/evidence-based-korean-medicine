## 綜概

### Source Text

得此揉法，非但可以自治已病，並可以治病之未生，豈非《素問》所云至人治未病不治已病之謂耶？語人曰：吾不能，是不為也，非不能也。安於吾身不能居仁由義，謂之自棄也。可惜乎？否乎？孟子曰：拱把之桐梓，人苟欲生之，皆知所以養之者，豈愛身不若桐梓哉？縱無竟日長工，亦隨在可偷半日閒也。

### Literal Translation

Having obtained this rubbing method, one can not only govern one's own existing disease, but also treat disease that has not yet arisen. Is this not what the 《Suwen》 means by saying that the perfected person treats disease before it arises and does not treat disease that has already arisen? If one says to people, “I cannot,” this is not doing it, not being unable. To be content that one's own body cannot dwell in benevolence and follow righteousness is called self-abandonment. Is it a pity? Or not? Mencius says: For the tung and catalpa trees of a hand-span, if people want them to live, they all know how to nourish them; how could one love one's body less than the tung and catalpa trees? Even if there is no all-day laborer, one can still steal half a day of leisure wherever one is.
