## 綜概

### Source Text

手足指退皮綻裂出紫血，擠盡即愈。大積將上，周身微寒，出頭即止，積塊雖大，喉自能容，梗芥作咳，不礙飲食，此六陰之積也。

八、頭皮寬厚者，壽象也；麵皮緊薄者，夭相也，有積無積之分耳。

### Literal Translation

When the skin of the fingers and toes peels and cracks and purple blood comes out, squeeze it all out and it heals. When a great accumulation is about to rise, the whole body is slightly cold; when the head comes out, it stops. Although the accumulation mass is large, the throat can naturally contain it; it causes a choking cough but does not obstruct eating and drinking; this is the accumulation of the six yin.

Eight: A scalp that is broad and thick is a sign of longevity; a face skin that is tight and thin is a sign of early death; it is only the difference between having and not having accumulation.
