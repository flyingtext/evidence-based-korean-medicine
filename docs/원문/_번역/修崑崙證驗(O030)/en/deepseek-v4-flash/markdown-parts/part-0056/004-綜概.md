## 綜概

### Source Text

入結喉分左右旋者，想是腔內腔外之分也。

十五、每於項旁揉時，摸得腹中及脅下之積直衝而上，走結喉內消。及摸得兩肋骨尖，長而忽軟，揉之脫殼，改為一條上消。

### Literal Translation

Those that enter the Adam's apple and rotate left and right are, I think, the distinction between inside and outside the cavity.

Fifteen: Whenever I rubbed at the side of the neck, I could feel the accumulation in the abdomen and below the flanks rushing straight up and going into the Adam's apple to disperse. And when I felt the tips of the two ribs, they were long and suddenly soft; rubbing them, they shed their shell and changed into one strip that dispersed upward.
