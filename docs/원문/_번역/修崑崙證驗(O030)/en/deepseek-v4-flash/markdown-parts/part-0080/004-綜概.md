## 綜概

### Source Text

五十七歲後，右耳輪邊常生小瘡，膿潰結痂，觸之痛甚，十餘年雖揉亦未已也。今左耳中又生小瘡，一連三次，出膿方愈，痛亦異前，而右耳輪之痛亦止，莫名其所以然。向意經絡，左、右之上，即右、左之下，或者是乎？不然何以兩耳同愈也？

### Literal Translation

After the age of fifty-seven, small sores often grew at the edge of the right ear rim, festering and scabbing, and touching them hurt greatly; for more than ten years, though I rubbed, it did not stop. Now a small sore has again grown in the left ear, three times in a row, and only when pus came out did it heal; the pain was also different from before, and the pain of the right ear rim also stopped. I cannot name the reason. I previously thought that in the channels, the upper of left and right is the lower of right and left; perhaps that is so? Otherwise why do both ears heal together?
