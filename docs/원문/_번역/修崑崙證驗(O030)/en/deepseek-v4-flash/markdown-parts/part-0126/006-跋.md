## 跋

### Source Text

「誰人肯向死前休」，帝君簽語也。幼年誦之，既訝設詞之異，又詫撒手之難，心嚮往之，或一見焉。於時雙親蔭芘，未涉世緣，轉瞬七十年矣，夢境迷離，如侍膝下，恍然在目前也。

### Literal Translation

“Who is willing to rest before death?” This is the oracle verse of the Emperor Lord. In my youth I recited it, both marveling at the strangeness of the wording and wondering at the difficulty of letting go, and my heart yearned toward it, hoping perhaps to see it once. At that time, sheltered by my two parents, I had not yet entered the world's entanglements; in the blink of an eye seventy years have passed, and the dream realm is blurred, as if serving at their knees, suddenly before my eyes.
