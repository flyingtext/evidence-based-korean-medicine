## 綜概

### Source Text

（八）揉顴及夾車下尖，不知長短一大根，碎去，此太陽經也。

（九）揉夾車吊動足少陽，約自銳眥由耳門至夾車，下大迎去。

（十）由耳門上，靠後吊動，由兩鬢太陽穴至眉心交下。

### Literal Translation

(8) Rub the cheekbone and the lower tip of the jaw; a great root of unknown length is broken and removed; this is the taiyang channel.

(9) Rub the jaw to draw and move the foot shaoyang; approximately from the outer canthus (銳眥) through the ear gate to the jaw, down to Daying.

(10) From above the ear gate, leaning back, draw and move, from the temples and taiyang points on both sides, crossing down to the space between the brows.
