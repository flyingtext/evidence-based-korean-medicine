## 綜概

### Source Text

茲則一言以蔽之曰，揉夾車，以清頭上六陽之積，下部六陰之積由足上，繼之曰揉海底，疏通腿胯，為善後之計，終仍歸總於夾車。易而且簡，易知易從者也。今雖身試獲效，實不料其至於如此之極耳。

### Literal Translation

Now I can sum it up in one word: rub the jaw to clear the accumulation of the six yang on the head; the accumulation of the six yin in the lower part rises from the feet; next, rub the perineum to free and open the legs and hips, as a plan for aftercare; and in the end it still returns to the jaw. It is easy and simple, easy to know and easy to follow. Although I have now tried it on my body and obtained effect, I truly did not expect it to reach such an extreme.
