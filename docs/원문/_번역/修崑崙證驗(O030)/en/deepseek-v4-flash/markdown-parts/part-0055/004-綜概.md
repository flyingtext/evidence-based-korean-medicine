## 綜概

### Source Text

直上右夾，下結喉中去，厚皮消去一半，隨去積亦多。

十四、新正八日未刻，覺周身微寒，意必項後有積來，專力揉腦後，一時許，項之左右並出二根，上夾車灣入結喉，二刻方完。完後忽從脊中湧出一大片，中硬邊軟，闊五六寸，右旋而入結喉，亦數刻方完，意者前二條是脊左右，後一片是脊中帶肋積而出也。

### Literal Translation

It went straight up to the right jaw and down into the Adam's apple, and the thick skin was reduced by half, and along with it much accumulation was removed.

Fourteen: On the eighth day of the new year, at the wei hour, I felt the whole body slightly cold, and thought that there must be accumulation coming at the back of the neck. I concentrated my strength on rubbing behind the head; after about an hour, two roots came out together on the left and right of the neck, went up to the jaw, curved into the Adam's apple, and finished in two ke (刻). After it finished, suddenly a great sheet surged out from the middle of the spine, hard in the middle and soft at the edges, five or six inches wide, rotating to the right and entering the Adam's apple, also finishing in several ke. I thought the first two strips were the left and right of the spine, and the later sheet was the accumulation of the ribs carried out from the middle of the spine.
