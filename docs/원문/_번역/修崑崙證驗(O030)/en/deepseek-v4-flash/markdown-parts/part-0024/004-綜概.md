## 綜概

### Source Text

《孫真人衛生歌》：汝欲不死修崑崙，雙手揩摩常在面。《庭經》語：崑崙，山名，西北至高之位，以喻首也。下句真人身體力行，得要訣而言也。

### Literal Translation

The 《Sun Zhenren Weisheng Ge》 (Song of Health by the Perfected Sun): If you wish not to die, cultivate Kunlun; with both hands rub and wipe constantly on the face. The words of the 《Ting Jing》: Kunlun is a mountain name, the highest position in the northwest, used as a metaphor for the head. The next line is the Perfected One practicing it in body and deed, having obtained the essential formula and speaking of it.
