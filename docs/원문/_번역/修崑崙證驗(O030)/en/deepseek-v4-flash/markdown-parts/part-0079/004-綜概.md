## 綜概

### Source Text

且陽明、太陽過耳門、出耳上，始積風鳴，重則聾矣。予三十一歲，左耳中生瘡，俗名耳底是也。痛悶不堪，極其至也，終夜掩耳繞走於庭，錚然一鳴，氣出而愈，此耳聾之始也。

### Literal Translation

And yangming and taiyang pass the ear gate and come out above the ear; at first wind ringing accumulates, and if severe, deafness. When I was thirty-one, a sore grew in my left ear, commonly called the ear bottom (耳底). The pain and oppression were unbearable, to the extreme; all night I covered my ear and walked around the courtyard, and with a clang it rang once, the qi came out and it healed; this was the beginning of my deafness.
