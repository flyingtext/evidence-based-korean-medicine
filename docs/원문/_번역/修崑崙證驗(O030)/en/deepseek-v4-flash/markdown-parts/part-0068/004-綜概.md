## 綜概

### Source Text

二十二、自始至今從無痛癢。一日正揉間，兩脅兩臂奇癢似癬，斷不敢搔，逐處分揉，三兩日，串至膻中、缺盆而愈，想是兩處之積上消矣。於是各處始有癢者，上下大小久暫不一，揉之即愈。

### Literal Translation

Twenty-two: From the beginning to now there has been no pain or itching. One day while rubbing, the two flanks and two arms itched strangely like tinea, and I absolutely did not dare scratch; I rubbed each place separately, and in two or three days it spread to Danzhong and Quepen and healed; I think the accumulation of the two places had dispersed upward. Then various places began to have itching, of varying up and down, large and small, long and short; rubbing it, it healed at once.
