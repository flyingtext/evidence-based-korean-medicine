## 小引

### Source Text

其揉若何？請假賢傳引《詩》之語為則，曰如切如磋，如琢如磨者，自修也。若是易知易能，人人得為，何待於言？正恐其道在邇，而求諸遠；事在易而求諸難也。故縷述經驗，信而有證，為修身者忠告而善道之。

### Literal Translation

What is this rubbing? Let me borrow the words that the worthy commentary quotes from the 《Poetry》 as the standard: like cutting and filing, like chiseling and grinding, this is self-cultivation. If it is this easy to know and easy to do, and everyone can do it, why wait for words? It is precisely because I fear that the Way is near, yet one seeks it in the far; the matter is easy, yet one seeks it in the difficult. Therefore I set forth my experience in detail, trustworthy and with evidence, as a loyal admonition and good guidance for those who cultivate the self.
