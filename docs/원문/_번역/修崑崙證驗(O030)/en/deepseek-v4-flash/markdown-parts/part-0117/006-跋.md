## 跋

### Source Text

臟腑筋皮秉受氣血，互相生養，有益無礙，各完本形。若至於積，如鐵生鏽，附骨肉臟腑以生，分氣血筋膜以成，初如絲粗，有長無消，及其至也，日引月長，歲粗時大，非但氣血皆為所奪，即部位亦為所侵，空竅塞滿，百病叢生，人反無以自完矣。養癰成患，誰任其咎？今得此揉法，積可立消，病永不生，真起死回生之術，宜其為養形之法矣。

### Literal Translation

The viscera, bowels, sinews, and skin receive qi and blood, mutually giving birth and nourishing, with benefit and no obstruction, each completing its own original form. But when it reaches accumulation, it is like iron producing rust, attaching to the bones, flesh, viscera, and bowels to grow, dividing qi, blood, sinews, and membranes to form. At first it is as thin as a thread, with growth and no dispersal; and when it reaches its extreme, it is drawn day by day and grows month by month, becoming thick with the years and large with the seasons. Not only is all the qi and blood seized by it, but even the positions are invaded by it, the empty orifices are filled, the hundred diseases arise in clusters, and the person instead has no way to complete himself. Nourishing an abscess into a calamity, who bears the blame? Now having obtained this rubbing method, the accumulation can be dispersed at once, and disease will never arise; truly it is the art of raising the dead and restoring life, and it is fitting that it be the method of nourishing the form.
