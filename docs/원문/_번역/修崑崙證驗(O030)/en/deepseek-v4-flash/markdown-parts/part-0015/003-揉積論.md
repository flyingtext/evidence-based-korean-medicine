## 揉積論

### Source Text

今之所謂積者，即如癭瘤使之反生於內，得不統謂氣血之積乎？獨可專仗藥力消之乎？總之，凡百病症，皆以氣血為主，通則無積，不通則積，新則積小，久則積大。不論大小內外病症，果能揉之，使經絡氣血通暢，則病無不愈者，不必先爭此揉積之名分今古也。再以淺近者申之，如頭痛揉提太陽及眉心，立見輕爽，喉痛重提項前，亦見效驗；小有腫痛瘡癤，揉之立時解散。

### Literal Translation

The so-called accumulation of today is just like making the goiter and tumor grow internally instead; can one not call it all an accumulation of qi and blood? Can one rely solely on the power of medicine to disperse it? In sum, all the hundred diseases take qi and blood as primary. If it flows, there is no accumulation; if it does not flow, there is accumulation. If new, the accumulation is small; if old, the accumulation is large. Regardless of large or small, internal or external disease, if one can truly rub it so that the channels and collaterals and qi and blood flow freely, then there is no disease that will not be cured. There is no need to first dispute the name and the ancient-modern distinction of this rubbing of accumulation. Let me further explain with something shallow and near: for headache, rub and lift the temple (太陽) and the space between the brows (眉心), and one immediately feels light and refreshed; for sore throat, lift the front of the neck heavily, and one also sees effect; for a small swelling, pain, sore, or boil, rubbing it disperses it at once.
