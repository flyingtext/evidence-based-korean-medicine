## 綜概

### Source Text

《黃庭經》：尺宅寸田可治生。尺宅，面也。寸田，兩眉間，為上丹田。

### Literal Translation

The 《Huangting Jing》 (Yellow Court Classic): The foot-sized dwelling and the inch-sized field can govern life. The foot-sized dwelling (尺宅) is the face. The inch-sized field (寸田) is between the two eyebrows, and is the upper elixir field (上丹田).
