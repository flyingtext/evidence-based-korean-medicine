## 綜概

### Source Text

其天民之先覺者乎？

一、予已試之矣，有奇效焉。面者統言之也，非靠顴與夾車，不能著力揉也。一身血脈條直，唯夾車十二經血脈上下匯走，屈曲交互，易致壅積。

### Literal Translation

Is he not the one who first awakened among the people of Heaven?

One: I have already tried it, and there is marvelous effect. The face is spoken of in general; without relying on the cheekbones and the jaw (夾車), one cannot exert force to rub. The blood vessels of the whole body run straight and orderly; only the blood vessels of the twelve channels at the jaw gather and flow up and down, bending and crossing, easily causing congestion and accumulation.
