## 綜概

### Source Text

遇與不遇，有緣無緣，盡吾之心焉而已；信與不信，有積無積，任人之意焉而已。

三十七、自揉至今，同人揉效者列後：手痠麻者、結喉露齒者、頸結瘰癧者、眼中生翳者、腦漏兼痔者、面生白瘤者、喉痹時發者、睡難翻身者、指不任用者、腿上生癬者、以上皆揉顴夾及海底而愈。至予之積久病多，揉愈者，散見於前矣。

### Literal Translation

Meeting and not meeting, having affinity and not having affinity, I only exhaust my mind; believing and not believing, having accumulation and not having accumulation, I leave it to people's will.

Thirty-seven: Since I began rubbing, I list below those who rubbed with me and had effect: those with sore and numb hands, those with protruding Adam's apple and exposed teeth, those with scrofula knotted in the neck, those with nebula growing in the eyes, those with nasal discharge combined with hemorrhoids, those with white tumors growing on the face, those with throat impediment that broke out from time to time, those who had difficulty turning over in sleep, those whose fingers could not be used, and those with tinea growing on the legs; all of these were cured by rubbing the cheekbones, jaw, and perineum. As for my own long-standing accumulation and many diseases that were cured by rubbing, they are scattered in the foregoing.
