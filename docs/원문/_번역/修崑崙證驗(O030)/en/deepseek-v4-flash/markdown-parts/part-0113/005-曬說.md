## 曬說

### Source Text

統而論之，增長人之精神氣血者，曬也。積雖並育而不害，感傷能散，積解未形，於以見生成之大。除刈積之根本枝蔓者，揉也。

### Literal Translation

Speaking in general, what increases a person's spirit, qi, and blood is sunning. Although the accumulation is nourished together and does not harm, injury and influence can disperse, and the accumulation resolves before it takes form; in this one sees the greatness of generation. What removes and cuts the root and branches of the accumulation is rubbing.
