## 跋

### Source Text

非蔽於物也，乃本身中自生之積耳。夫心之於五行，火也，請以火喻。人譬爐也，食譬炭也，心譬火也，思譬焰也、積譬灰也。

### Literal Translation

It is not being obscured by things, but it is the accumulation that arises by itself within one's own body. Now, the heart in the five phases is fire; let me use fire as an analogy. The person is like a furnace, food is like charcoal, the heart is like fire, thinking is like the flame, and accumulation is like the ashes.
