## 綜概

### Source Text

亦須見景生情，神領意會，不專心致知則不得也。

三、手三陰，自腹上頭走手指；手三陽，自手上頭走面；足三陽，自面下腹走足指，足三陰，自足入腹走頭，各至交宮。陰走膜內、頏顙、結喉、柔內、腋下、臂腕；陽走皮里、肩背、大椎、夾車、大迎、人迎、缺盆、膻中。

### Literal Translation

also requires seeing the scene and giving rise to feeling, grasping with the spirit and understanding with the mind; without concentrating the mind and extending knowledge, one cannot obtain it.

Three: The three hand yin channels go from the abdomen up to the head and to the fingers; the three hand yang channels go from the hands up to the head and to the face; the three foot yang channels go from the face down the abdomen to the toes; the three foot yin channels go from the feet into the abdomen and to the head, each reaching the crossing palace. Yin goes within the membranes, the upper throat (頏顙), the Adam's apple (結喉), the soft inner (柔內), the armpits, and the wrists; yang goes within the skin, the shoulders, back, Dazhui (大椎), jaw, Daying, Renying, Quepen (缺盆), and Danzhong (膻中).
