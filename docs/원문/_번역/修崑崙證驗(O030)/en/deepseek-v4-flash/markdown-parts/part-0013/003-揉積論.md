## 揉積論

### Source Text

倘不甚重，尚可苟延，逮至晚年，頭尖、項壅、背駝、肩聳、腿胯直強、手足痿痹，四肢塞滿，空隙毫無，生意隔絕，而人積偕亡矣。此無他法，惟揉以去之，倘得消多長少，或是一條生路也。或者曰：所言皆病之內症，古有醫案方藥，安見所謂積？安見揉有效？自古無此治病法也。

### Literal Translation

If it is not very severe, one can still drag out a living. But reaching old age, the head becomes pointed, the neck congested, the back hunched, the shoulders raised, the legs and hips stiff and straight, the hands and feet atrophied and paralyzed, the four limbs filled up, with no gap at all, and the vital energy is cut off, and the person and the accumulation perish together. There is no other method for this; only rubbing can remove it. If one can make it disperse more and grow less, perhaps that is a path of life. Someone says: What you speak of are all internal symptoms of disease; in ancient times there were medical cases and formulas. Where do you see this so-called accumulation? Where do you see that rubbing is effective? Since ancient times there has been no such method of treating disease.
