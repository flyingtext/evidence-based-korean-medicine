## 綜概

### Source Text

予自幼右肋下有積，橫梗心下，醫云伏梁，由來已久，揉後無之。又予左臂生癬，內如細粟，揉盡而愈，此水泡之證也。逢冬咳嗽吐痰、手足凍瘃，揉後均愈。

### Literal Translation

Since childhood I have had an accumulation below the right rib, running transversely across below the heart; the physician called it the hidden beam (伏梁), and it had been there for a long time, but after rubbing it was gone. Also, tinea grew on my left arm, with the inside like fine millet; I rubbed it all away and it healed; this is evidence of the water blister. When winter came, I coughed and spat phlegm, and my hands and feet had chilblains; after rubbing, they all healed.
