## 綜概

### Source Text

三十六、去冬晤孫真人修崑崙語，推廣為之，節節見效。正如山窮水盡忽而柳暗花明，境界不可思擬，轉瞬即難追想，隨時隨筆記之，直錄無文者也。意在善與人同，不恤言之詳盡，其中不無重複矛盾者。

### Literal Translation

Thirty-six: Last winter I met the words of the Perfected Sun on cultivating Kunlun, and extended and practiced them, seeing effect at every step. Just as when the mountains are exhausted and the waters are spent, suddenly the willows are dark and the flowers bright, the realm cannot be imagined, and in the blink of an eye it is hard to recall; I recorded it with the brush as it came, writing directly without ornament. The intention is to share the good with others, not sparing the detail of the words, and among them there is no lack of repetition and contradiction.
