## 綜概

### Source Text

陰陽氣血相生，何至不和？譬如夫婦本和，小人間之，必致仳離。小人者積也，可不亟亟去之乎？

三十四、凡人物秉受生氣者，皆不能無病。物不能自治，人則可以自治，天之生成於人獨厚矣。

### Literal Translation

Yin and yang and qi and blood give birth to each other; how could they become disharmonious? For example, a husband and wife are originally harmonious, but if a petty person comes between them, they must come to separation. The petty person is the accumulation; can one not urgently remove it?

Thirty-four: All people and things that receive the vital qi cannot be free of disease. Things cannot govern themselves, but people can govern themselves; Heaven's generation is uniquely generous to people.
