## 綜概

### Source Text

二十一、各處雖淨，然肩項兩旁、枕骨之下似尚有滯，於是專揉夾車，久之四面皆吊動矣。三陽經自手交肩，走耳後，出耳上，反夾車，下大迎，入人迎而至缺盆。少陽走枕骨，上出耳前；太陽出夾車中；陽明走夾車下角。

### Literal Translation

Twenty-one: Although the various places were clear, the two sides of the shoulders and neck, below the occipital bone, still seemed to have obstruction, so I concentrated on rubbing the jaw, and after a long time all four sides were drawn and moved. The three yang channels cross from the hands to the shoulders, go behind the ears, come out above the ears, turn back at the jaw, descend to Daying, enter Renying, and reach Quepen. Shaoyang goes to the occipital bone and comes out in front of the ear; taiyang comes out in the jaw; yangming goes to the lower corner of the jaw.
