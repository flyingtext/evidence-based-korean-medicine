## 綜概

### Source Text

意謂目生翳膜，是少陽經從目後包轉以致失明，若頻揉四眥及眉心、山根，經絡活動，其翳自退。目有病者，曷試之？曾遇幼瞽，兩目皆白翳矇住，叫其揉四眥，一日後改滿紅絲，淚出如泣，惜其不願而止。勢無中立，焉知不能退盡復明耶？新生翳揉即退，已屢試屢驗矣。

### Literal Translation

I think that when the eye grows a nebula (翳膜), it is the shaoyang channel wrapping around from behind the eye and causing blindness. If one frequently rubs the four canthi and the space between the brows and the root of the nose, the channels and collaterals become active, and the nebula naturally recedes. Those with disease of the eye, why not try it? I once met a young blind person, both eyes covered with white nebula; I told him to rub the four canthi, and after one day it changed to being full of red threads, with tears flowing as if weeping; unfortunately he was unwilling and stopped. The momentum has no middle ground; how do I know it could not recede completely and become bright again? A newly grown nebula recedes when rubbed; I have already tried it repeatedly and it has been effective every time.
