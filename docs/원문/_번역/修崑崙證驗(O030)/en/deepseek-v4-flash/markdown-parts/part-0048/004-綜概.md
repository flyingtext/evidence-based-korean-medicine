## 綜概

### Source Text

積形似蛇，大小長短不一，常時一塊一條，貼伏筋骨之間，必待原處吊動，勃起項間，直串兩夾，盤繞頸項，下由結喉內去，而貼伏者悉帶去矣，雖極粗大，喉間自然開讓。予項所出不下數十百條，久暫不一。甚有夜半睡夢肩夾塞滿，顙中作惡，驚醒坐起，揉消。

The accumulation has a shape like a snake, with size, length, and shortness not uniform; at ordinary times it is one lump and one strip, lying attached between the sinews and bones. It must wait for the original place to be drawn and moved, then it rises up in the neck, threads straight through both jaws, coils around the neck, and goes down through the inside of the Adam's apple, carrying away all that was lying attached. Although it is extremely thick and large, the throat naturally opens and yields. What came out of my neck was no less than several tens or hundreds of strips, of varying duration. There were even times in the middle of the night, in sleep and dreams, when the shoulders and jaws were filled up and evil arose in the forehead; I woke with a start, sat up, and rubbed it away.
