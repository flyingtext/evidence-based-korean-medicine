## 綜概

### Source Text

由夾車之上，下耳後出走大迎，入頸，人迎下消。行次按三陽經，魚貫不紊，實非人力能致。此積一去，則肓上膏下無滯，病自不生。

### Literal Translation

From above the jaw, it descends behind the ear and goes out to Daying (大迎), enters the neck, and disperses below Renying (人迎). When one proceeds and presses the three yang channels, they follow like a file of fish without disorder; this truly cannot be achieved by human strength. Once this accumulation is removed, then above the diaphragm and below the fat there is no obstruction, and disease will not arise by itself.
