## 綜概

### Source Text

兩鬢塞滿，揉山根、眉心。項後壅起，揉夾車反推。胸膈之積，自腦下反至大椎，亦於夾車反推。

### Literal Translation

If the two temples are filled up, rub the root of the nose (山根) and the space between the brows. If the back of the neck bulges, rub the jaw and push it back. The accumulation of the chest and diaphragm, from below the brain back to Dazhui, is also pushed back at the jaw.
