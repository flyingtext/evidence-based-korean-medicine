## 綜概

### Source Text

各經皆然，夾車之積所以不易淨也。要之總有盡日，惟問功夫之到不到耳。

二十八、耳中聽宮穴是小腸太陽本經，何以方書云耳聾耳鳴皆屬腎虛？緣少陽三焦自手交肩而上，直插耳根後，出耳上走夾車，所積皆蔽聽宮。

### Literal Translation

All the channels are like this; this is why the accumulation of the jaw is not easy to clear. In sum, there is always a day of completion; one only asks whether the effort has arrived or not.

Twenty-eight: The Tinggong point (聽宮穴) in the ear is the proper channel of the small intestine taiyang. Why do the formula books say that deafness and tinnitus all belong to kidney vacuity? Because the shaoyang triple burner, crossing from the hands to the shoulders and going up, thrusts straight behind the ear root, comes out above the ear and goes to the jaw, and what accumulates all obscures Tinggong.
