## 綜概

### Source Text

三十三、或有問積究竟何似者？予曰：考之方書，陰陽不和，臟腑虛弱，四氣七情失常，所以為積聚，久為癥瘕，中為痰飲，右為食積，左為死血。以予年來所覺，是皆另病，積則非是。何也？以其堅如石，流如沙，體其質似皆小水泡也。

### Literal Translation

Thirty-three: Someone asks what the accumulation is ultimately like. I say: Examining the formula books, yin and yang are not harmonious, the viscera and bowels are vacuous and weak, and the four qi and seven emotions are abnormal, so accumulation and gathering arise, becoming concretions and conglomerations over time, phlegm-rheum in the middle, food accumulation on the right, and dead blood on the left. From what I have felt in recent years, these are all other diseases; accumulation is not like this. Why? Because it is hard as stone and flows like sand, and examining its substance, it all seems to be small water blisters.
