## 綜概

### Source Text

譬之遊山獨逢佳境，喜而歸告，不自覺其拉雜矣。芒芒助長之心，只欲人知揉之可為，而亦為之，說僅大略，未能達意也。若果有為者，則造道自得，必另有一番境遇，如山陰道上應接不暇，光景亦推善同之意，向後來者告，有餘師矣，暇計予言之是非耶？非然者將謂山徑蹊間之路，必無為間茅塞之理，則予言更是有若無矣。

### Literal Translation

For example, when one travels in the mountains and alone meets a fine scene, one joyfully returns and tells of it, not aware of its disorder. My vast and hasty heart of helping growth only wants people to know that rubbing can be done, and to do it as well; the words are only a general outline and cannot convey the meaning. If there are truly those who do it, then they will attain the Way and obtain it themselves, and there must be another realm, like on the Shanyin road where one cannot respond to all; the scene also extends the meaning of sharing the good, telling it to later people, and there is a surplus of teachers. Would I have leisure to consider whether my words are right or wrong? Otherwise, one will say that the paths of the mountain trails and byways must have no reason to be blocked by grass; then my words are even more like having and not having.
