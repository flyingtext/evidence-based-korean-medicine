## 綜概

### Source Text

按摩諸書世固不乏，惜予少見。今揉而有效，私意即以名此可乎？倘世有古傳揉法予未及見，則不免河東白首之誚，否則即請以此冊為修身之嚆矢。

十一、一隅三反，此後原可無庸瑣言，但予積久而多，歷時許久方克盡淨，恐後有為者不知究竟，畏難中止，以致前功之或棄也，故備記巔未以勖之。

### Literal Translation

Books on massage are certainly not lacking in the world, but unfortunately I have seen few. Now rubbing is effective; may I privately take this to name it? If there is an ancient transmitted rubbing method in the world that I have not yet seen, then I cannot avoid the ridicule of the white-haired man of Hedong; otherwise, I ask that this book be taken as the first arrow of self-cultivation.

Eleven: From one corner one can infer three; after this there is originally no need for trivial words. But my accumulation was long-standing and abundant, and it took a long time before I could completely clear it. I feared that later practitioners, not knowing the outcome, would fear the difficulty and stop midway, causing the previous merit to be abandoned. Therefore I have recorded the beginning and end in full to encourage them.
