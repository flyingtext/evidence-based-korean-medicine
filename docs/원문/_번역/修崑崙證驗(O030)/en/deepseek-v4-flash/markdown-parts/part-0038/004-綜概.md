## 綜概

### Source Text

一概由足入腹，似不再上夾車也。

五、人身統此夾皮袋耳，中之硬者為骨，實處是血，虛處是氣，其餘筋肉皆應綿軟方是無病。一經淤積，骨則粗而大，肉則壅而滯，筋則拳而曲，皮則繃而緊，能無病乎？凡有滯積，無不宜揉，隨宜而施，何能執一？神而明之，存乎其人。

### Literal Translation

all enter the abdomen from the feet, and seem not to rise again to the jaw.

Five: The human body is entirely this skin bag. Within it, the hard parts are bone, the solid places are blood, the empty places are qi, and the rest, the sinews and flesh, should all be soft and yielding to be free of disease. Once silted and accumulated, the bone becomes thick and large, the flesh congested and stagnant, the sinews curled and bent, and the skin stretched and tight; can one be free of disease? Wherever there is stagnant accumulation, there is nothing that should not be rubbed; apply it according to what is appropriate, how can one hold to a single method? To be spirit-like and illuminate it lies with the person.
