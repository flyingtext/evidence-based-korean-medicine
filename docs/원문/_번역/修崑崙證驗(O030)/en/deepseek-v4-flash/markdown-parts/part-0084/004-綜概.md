## 綜概

### Source Text

方書無傳，創法新奇，人雖不信，我已奏功，神乎其神矣。

三十、手少陽由耳後二至目銳眥，足少陽起目銳眥，上頭角下耳後，支從耳後出耳前至目後。邇來揉動似乎少陽脈交山根而下，不然何以兩鬢塞滿，揉山根即消耶？然未見前言也。

### Literal Translation

It is not transmitted in the formula books, and the method is novel and strange; although people do not believe it, I have already achieved merit. How spirit-like, its spirit!

Thirty: The hand shaoyang goes from behind the ear to the outer canthus; the foot shaoyang arises at the outer canthus, goes up the head corner and down behind the ear, and a branch goes from behind the ear out in front of the ear to behind the eye. Lately, when I rub and move, it seems that the shaoyang vessel crosses the root of the nose and descends; otherwise why, when the two temples are filled up, does rubbing the root of the nose disperse it? But I have not seen this in the earlier words.
