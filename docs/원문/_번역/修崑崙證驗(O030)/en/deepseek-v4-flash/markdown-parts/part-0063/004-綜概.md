## 綜概

### Source Text

至腔內之積，一經吊動，則自頸環圓鋪至缺盆，如筒而上，揉撥之，則左旋於結喉內消，而大椎之筋亦無矣。

十九、二月朔夜，摸兩肩，其髀骨埋入積中，臂動不見骨之稜角起伏，其積一片，中高寸余，四垂而薄，長闊約尺余，意謂太陽經出肩繞髀，必其積也。揉本處不動，退揉大椎，為太陽交下缺盆處也，盡日之功，揉亦不動，惟見手少陽經由耳後走耳上及耳門後，亦有上者，皆有稜有脊，橫梗而行，匯至鬢額眉心交下，摸頸項間亦有跡矣。

### Literal Translation

As for the accumulation inside the cavity, once drawn and moved, it spreads in a circle from the neck to Quepen, rising like a tube; rubbing and pulling it, it rotates left and disperses inside the Adam's apple, and the sinew of Dazhui is also gone.

Nineteen: On the night of the first day of the second month, I felt the two shoulders, and the thigh bone was buried in the accumulation; moving the arm, one could not see the ridges and undulations of the bone. The accumulation was one sheet, raised more than an inch in the middle, hanging down thin on four sides, about a foot long and wide. I thought that the taiyang channel comes out of the shoulder and winds around the thigh, so it must be its accumulation. Rubbing the original place, it did not move; I retreated and rubbed Dazhui, the place where taiyang crosses and descends to Quepen. I spent the whole day's effort, but rubbing did not move it either; I only saw the hand shaoyang channel going from behind the ear to above the ear and behind the ear gate, and there were also those that went up, all with ridges and spines, traveling transversely, gathering at the temples, forehead, and the space between the brows and crossing down; feeling the neck, there were also traces.
