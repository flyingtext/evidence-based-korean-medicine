## 跋

### Source Text

數年以來，並筆墨事亦休，諉云懶於用心，其實是不能收放心，又安能用思心哉？向有自修之法，曰「曬」，今有自修之法，曰「揉」。皆前人成法，未有發明之言，不著證驗之實，故後人以老生常談置之，而反求醫於人、乞靈於草木，孰從而知為脫胎換骨、起死回生之神術乎？故不憚覙縷立說以表張之。問途已經，遵而道之，方信予言之不謬也，茲又為之現身說法焉。

### Literal Translation

For several years, even the affairs of brush and ink I have rested, excusing myself by saying I am lazy in using the mind; in truth, it is that I cannot gather back the released mind, and how could I use the thinking mind? Formerly there was a method of self-cultivation called “sunning” (曬); now there is a method of self-cultivation called “rubbing” (揉). Both are methods completed by former people, with no words of invention and no record of the reality of evidence, so later people set them aside as the common talk of old men, and instead seek medicine from people and beg numina from plants and trees. Who would know that they are the spirit arts of shedding the body and changing the bones, and raising the dead and restoring life? Therefore I do not fear to set forth the doctrine in detail to display and promote it. Having already asked the way, if one follows and practices it, only then will one believe that my words are not mistaken; now I also preach by example for it.
