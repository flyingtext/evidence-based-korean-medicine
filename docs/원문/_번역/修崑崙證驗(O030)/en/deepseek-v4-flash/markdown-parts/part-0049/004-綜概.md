## 綜概

### Source Text

以此告人，誰能信之，實是去積良法，何以諸書不載？或以去積太速、太盡有損於人乎？予現身試，有益無損也。惟方書治風痹用白花蛇，或取其形似乎？雖未明言，前人可謂智矣。然藥服於胃中，而欲令藥性速行於皮里膜內，消黏著不動之死積，想見其難，徒耗正氣而已。

### Literal Translation

If I tell this to people, who can believe it? It is truly a good method for removing accumulation; why is it not recorded in the various books? Is it perhaps because removing accumulation too quickly and too completely harms people? I have tried it on my body, and it has benefit and no harm. Only the formula books treat wind impediment with the white-flowered snake (白花蛇); perhaps they took its shape? Although not stated clearly, the ancients can be called wise. Yet the medicine is taken into the stomach, and one wants the medicinal nature to move quickly within the skin and membranes to disperse the dead accumulation that is stuck and unmoving; one can imagine the difficulty, and it only wastes the upright qi.
