## 綜概

### Source Text

囟，頂門也。子在母胎，諸竅尚閉，臍納氣，囟出氣。既生竅開，口鼻納氣，閭尾泄氣，囟乃漸合，陰陽升降之道也。

### Literal Translation

The fontanel (囟) is the top gate. When the child is in the mother's womb, the various orifices are still closed; the navel takes in qi, and the fontanel lets out qi. Once born, the orifices open, the mouth and nose take in qi, and the tail lets out qi, and the fontanel gradually closes; this is the way of the ascent and descent of yin and yang.
