## 綜概

### Source Text

上登五福之二，下免六極之三，於己取之而已。不得式者，以十五六成童比較為樣，如同診脈可以知積之有無，以人治人，復自己之本來面目，無病而止，非同索隱行怪之為也。

三十五、予嘗慨治生者之惑矣，既欲其病，又不準其病，是惑也。

### Literal Translation

Above, one ascends to two of the five blessings; below, one avoids three of the six extremes; one takes it from oneself. Those who have not obtained the model can compare with a fifteen or sixteen year old youth as the standard; just as taking the pulse can tell whether there is accumulation, one governs people with people, restores one's own original face, and stops at being free of disease; this is not the act of seeking the hidden and practicing the strange.

Thirty-five: I have often sighed at the delusion of those who govern life. They both want the disease and do not allow the disease; this is delusion.
