## 綜概

### Source Text

予自揉後，上下唇常合，鼻脊自高起，眼內眥向不容指，今已綽有餘地，是亦可為一證。

九、予之揉也，實無所師承，苦自病而試得者也。亦曾見有按摩諸書，名目紛多，無從記憶，且莫明所以然，繁而且難者也。

### Literal Translation

Since I rubbed, the upper and lower lips are always closed, the bridge of the nose naturally rises, and the inner canthus of the eye, which formerly could not admit a finger, now has ample room; this can also serve as one piece of evidence.

Nine: My rubbing truly has no teacher or lineage; it was obtained by suffering from my own illness and trying it. I have also seen various books on massage, with many and various names, none of which I could remember, and I could not understand the reason; they were numerous and difficult.
