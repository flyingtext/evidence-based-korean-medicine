## 綜概

### Source Text

惟初揉卻難見功，何也？各處經絡久已淤閉，即夾車骨亦淤積闊大，先已蓋滿，必得潛心耐性，逐漸推動，初覺氣滿充溢，各處似有開裂，豁然貫通，功到自悉自悟也。

二、若論平時，本應常揉百會、眉心、眼內外眥、顴夾以通氣血，免致生積。而頭皮緊急，頸項壅粗，肩背脅肋塞滿，非於顴夾著骨揉之不能吊動，且其消動自有先後。

### Literal Translation

Only at the beginning of rubbing is it hard to see merit. Why? The channels and collaterals in various places have long been silted and closed; even the jaw bone is silted and accumulated, broad and large, already covered and filled. One must quiet the mind and be patient, gradually pushing and moving. At first one feels qi full and overflowing, and the various places seem to crack open; then suddenly it penetrates. When the merit arrives, one naturally knows and naturally understands.

Two: As for ordinary times, one should regularly rub Baihui, the space between the brows, the inner and outer canthi of the eyes, and the cheekbones and jaw to free the qi and blood and avoid the arising of accumulation. But if the scalp is tight, the neck congested and thick, and the shoulders, back, flanks, and ribs filled up, then unless one rubs on the bone of the cheekbones and jaw, one cannot draw and move it; and moreover its dispersing and moving naturally has its own order.
