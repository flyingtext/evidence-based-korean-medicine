## 綜概

### Source Text

正揉間，覺其自項後左右同往上竄，如以帛蒙頂。此積所佔去之皮還歸原處，而頂中積仍復累然，揉兩夾車即消，頂上腦後俱清矣。

二十五、頭項俱清之後，仍揉頂心夾車間，有條塊隨即揉消。

### Literal Translation

While rubbing, I felt them rushing upward together from behind the neck on left and right, like covering the crown with silk. The skin that this accumulation had occupied returned to its original place, and the accumulation in the crown was still piled up again; rubbing the two jaws, it dispersed, and the crown and behind the head were both clear.

Twenty-five: After the head and neck were both clear, I still rubbed between the crown center and the jaw, and if there were strips and lumps, I rubbed them away at once.
