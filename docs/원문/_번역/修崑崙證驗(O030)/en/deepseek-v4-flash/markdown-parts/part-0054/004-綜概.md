## 綜概

### Source Text

十三、揉至歲除，腦後總未見消。因思六陽經皆會大椎，其積甚厚，或為其蓋住，或手腕不通。於是先揉手腕，次揉大椎，積厚頗費尋覓，及見骨後揉之良久，骨節忽然高起，湧而上行，如脫殼然。

### Literal Translation

Thirteen: I rubbed until the end of the year, but behind the head I still never saw it disperse. I thought that since the six yang channels all meet at Dazhui, its accumulation is very thick; perhaps it was covered by it, or the wrist was not open. So I first rubbed the wrist, then rubbed Dazhui. The accumulation was thick and took considerable searching; when I saw the bone, I rubbed it for a long time, and the bone joint suddenly rose up, surged and went upward, as if shedding its shell.
