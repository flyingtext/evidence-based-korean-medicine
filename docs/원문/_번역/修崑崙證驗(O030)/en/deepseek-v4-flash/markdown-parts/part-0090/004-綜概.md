## 綜概

### Source Text

且垂下雙頭至肩背則駝，至腰胯則痠痛，至腿腳則痿痹、臃腫、腳氣，上衝則頭痛，在面前則壓於膈上，五臟六腑能無病乎？即如六陽經自缺盆回頭，其積則垂至腿中，統於下胲、承漿穴，揉根以吊之，方始上消。自頭下者，則隨與足三陰上消。其積如石在土，日有增長，以積引積，久而自大，雖堅硬如磐石而轉折如流沙，此天留生路以與人也。

### Literal Translation

And the hanging double heads, reaching the shoulders and back, cause a hump; reaching the waist and hips, cause soreness and pain; reaching the legs and feet, cause atrophy, paralysis, swelling, and beriberi; rushing upward, they cause headache; being in front, they press on the diaphragm; can the five viscera and six bowels be free of disease? That is, as the six yang channels return from Quepen to the head, their accumulation hangs down into the legs, governed by the lower jaw and the Chengjiang point (承漿穴); rubbing the root to draw it, only then does it disperse upward. Those that descend from the head then disperse upward following the foot three yin. The accumulation is like a stone in the earth, growing day by day, drawing accumulation with accumulation, and over time it becomes large by itself; although hard as a boulder, it turns and folds like quicksand; this is Heaven leaving a path of life for people.
