## 跋

### Source Text

曰是道心也，為明而能思者言可。若積蔽者復人心而不能，奚暇論道心哉？向惜天分限者，無治心法，今始得法，去其積則心可復明，而無思不得矣。果能揉之，五行無滯，相養相生，百節疏通，萬竅玲瓏，天君泰然，四肢從令，雖愚必明，雖柔必強，所謂修身，在正其心。

### Literal Translation

I say this is the heart of the Way, and can be spoken to those who are bright and able to think. If one is obscured by accumulation, one cannot even restore the human heart; how could one have leisure to discuss the heart of the Way? I previously regretted that those limited by their heavenly endowment had no method of governing the heart; now for the first time I have obtained the method. Remove the accumulation and the heart can become bright again, and there is nothing that thinking cannot obtain. If one can truly rub, the five phases have no obstruction, mutually nourishing and mutually generating, the hundred joints are freed, the ten thousand orifices are exquisite, the heavenly ruler is calm, and the four limbs follow orders; though foolish, one must become bright, and though weak, one must become strong. What is called cultivating the self lies in rectifying the heart.
