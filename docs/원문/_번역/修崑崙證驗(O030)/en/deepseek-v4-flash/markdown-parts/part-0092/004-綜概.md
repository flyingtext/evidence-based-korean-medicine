## 綜概

### Source Text

水泡非病，由少至多實足以致病，如大河水漲，岸邊水沫聚成大塊，皆小水泡之所積也。人身何以有此？請譬之。即如家常餘留飯菜，一經受熱則酸而起泡，再遲則白沫上湧，皆蘊熱為之也。

### Literal Translation

A water blister is not a disease, but from few to many it is truly sufficient to cause disease. Just as when a great river rises, the foam at the bank gathers into great masses, all being accumulations of small water blisters. Why does the human body have this? Let me use an analogy. Just as leftover food and dishes at home, once heated, turn sour and produce bubbles, and if delayed further, white foam surges up; all of this is caused by accumulated heat.
