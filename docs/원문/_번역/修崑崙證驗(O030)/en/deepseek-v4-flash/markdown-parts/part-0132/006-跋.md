## 跋

### Source Text

向云懶於用心，非我之懶於用自己之心，實自己之心懶為我用也，是懶也。何以未揉前如彼，既揉後如此，期月間前後各異，或即前所云即是病之說耶？自揉至今，不知心已收得否，又不知肯為我用否，因作心說一首、自偈一首，心中不似竟無頭緒者，不知是否揉而收得，一而二、二而一者也。然耶，否耶？若果然耶？則懶竟是病矣。

### Literal Translation

Formerly I said I was lazy in using the mind; it is not that I am lazy in using my own mind, but that my own mind is lazy in being used by me; this is laziness. Why was it like that before rubbing and like this after rubbing, with the before and after differing within a month? Is it perhaps the earlier saying that it is itself disease? Since I began rubbing, I do not know whether the heart has been gathered back, nor whether it is willing to be used by me, so I composed one Discourse on the Heart and one self-verse; the heart does not seem to be entirely without a clue, but I do not know whether it was gathered by rubbing; it is one and two, two and one. Is it so, or not? If it is truly so, then laziness is after all a disease.
