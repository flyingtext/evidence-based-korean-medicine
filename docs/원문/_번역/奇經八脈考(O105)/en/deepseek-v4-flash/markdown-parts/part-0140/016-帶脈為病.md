## O105:p016:t0009

### Source Text

灸既著穴, 不得不去. 自是凡有病此者, 每為之按此穴, 莫不應手痠痛, 令歸灸之, 無有不愈. 其穴, 在兩脅季肋之下一寸八分, 若更灸百會穴尤佳.

### Literal Translation

Once the moxa hit the point, it could not but leave. From then on, whenever anyone had this disease, I would press this point for them, and it was always sore and painful to the touch; I had them go home and moxa it, and none were not cured. The point is one cun and eight fen below the floating rib of the two flanks; if one also moxaes the Baihui point it is even better.
