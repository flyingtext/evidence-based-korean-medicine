### Source Text

素問骨空論云:督脈生疾, 從少腹上衝心而痛, 不得前後, 為沖疝, 女子為不孕、u癃u痔、遺溺、u嗌u乾. 治在骨上, （謂腰橫骨上毛際中, 曲骨穴也）甚者在臍下營. （臍下一寸, 陰交穴也） 王啟玄曰:此乃任沖二脈之病, 不知何以屬之督脈.

### Literal Translation

The Plain Questions, Bone Hollows Discourse says: When the Governing Vessel (Dumai) gives rise to disease, [qi] rushes upward from the lower abdomen to the heart causing pain, and one cannot pass urine or stool; this is Chong hernia (chongshan). In women it is infertility, strangury (long), hemorrhoids, enuresis, and dry throat (yi). Treatment is on the bone above (this refers to the region of the pubic hair on the transverse bone of the waist, the Qugu point); in severe cases, at the ying below the navel (one cun below the navel, the Yinjiao point). Wang Qixuan said: These are diseases of the Ren and Chong vessels; I do not know why they are attributed to the Governing Vessel.
