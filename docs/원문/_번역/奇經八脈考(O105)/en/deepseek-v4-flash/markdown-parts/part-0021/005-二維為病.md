## O105:p005:t0007

### Source Text

至於陰維為病主心痛, 潔古獨以三陰溫里之藥治之, 則寒中二陰者宜矣, 而三陰熱厥作痛, 似未備矣. 蓋陰維之脈, 雖交三陰而行, 實與任脈同歸, 故心痛多屬少陰、厥陰、任脈之氣上衝而然. 暴痛無熱, 久痛無寒, 按之少止者為虛, 不可接近者為實.

### Literal Translation

As for the Yin Wei disease governing heart pain, [the fact that] Jiegu alone treats it with the warming-the-interior medicinals of the three yin is appropriate for cold striking the two yin, but [for] heat reversal causing pain in the three yin, [his treatment] seems not yet complete. This is because the Yin Wei vessel, although it travels intersecting through the three yin, in fact returns together with the Ren meridian, and therefore heart pain is mostly [due to] the qi of Shaoyin, Jueyin, and the Ren meridian rushing upward. Sudden pain without heat, prolonged pain without cold; pain that is slightly relieved by pressing is vacuity, while that which cannot be approached or pressed close is excess.
