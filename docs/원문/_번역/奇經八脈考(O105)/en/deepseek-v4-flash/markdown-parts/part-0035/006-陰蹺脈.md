## O105:p006:t0003

### Source Text

採之惟在陰蹺為先, 此脈才動, 諸脈皆通. 次督、任、沖三脈, 總為經脈造化之源. 而陰蹺一脈, 散在丹經, 其名頗多:曰天根、曰死戶、曰覆命關、曰酆都鬼戶、曰死生根, 有神主之, 名曰桃康, 上通泥丸, 下透湧泉.

### Literal Translation

In gathering [the one-qi], one takes the Yin Qiao first of all; as soon as this vessel moves, the various vessels are all opened. Next [come] the Du, Ren, and Chong three vessels, which together are the source of the creating-and-transforming [power] of the meridians. As for this single Yin Qiao vessel, it is scattered throughout the inner-alchemy classics (dan jing) with quite many names: [it is] called Tiangen (root of heaven), Sihu (dead door), Fuming Guan (gate of restoring life), Fengdu ghuihu (ghost door of Fengdu), Sisheng gen (root of death-and-life); there is a spirit that governs it, named Taokang, which above penetrates Niwan (the crown elixir field) and below passes through Yongquan (KI1).
