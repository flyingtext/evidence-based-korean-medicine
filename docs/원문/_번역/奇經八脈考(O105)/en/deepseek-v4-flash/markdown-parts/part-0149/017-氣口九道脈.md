## O105:p017:t0006

### Source Text

中部如內者, 足太陰脾也, 動苦腹滿, 胃中痛, 上管有寒, 食不下, 腰上狀如居水中. 沉澀, 為身重, 足脛寒痛, 煩滿不能臥, 時咳唾有血, 泄利食不化. 後部如內者, 足少陰腎也.

### Literal Translation

When the middle region is as if internal, it is the foot Greater Yin Spleen; when it moves it causes abdominal fullness, pain within the stomach, cold in the upper tube (stomach), inability to eat, and the lower back as if dwelling in water. Deep and rough is heaviness of the body, cold and painful foot and shin, vexation and fullness with inability to lie down, occasional cough with spitting of blood, and diarrhea with undigested food. When the rear region is as if internal, it is the foot Lesser Yin Kidney.
