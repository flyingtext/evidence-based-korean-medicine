## O105:p005:t0014

### Source Text

其脈如貫珠者, 男子兩脅下實, 腰中痛;女子陰中痛, 如有瘡狀. 素問腰痛論曰:陽維之脈, 令人腰痛, 痛上u怫u然腫. 刺陽維之脈與太陽合腨間, 去地一尺.

### Literal Translation

When its pulse is like strung pearls, in a man the two flanks below the ribs are full and there is pain in the lumbar region; in a woman there is pain in the genital, as though there were a sore. The Lumbago Theory of the Su wen says: The Yang Wei vessel makes a person suffer lumbar pain, with swelling rising up on the pain. Puncture the Yang Wei vessel where it joins Taiyang between the two legs (chuijian), one chi from the ground.
