## O105:p010:t0019

### Source Text

（甘草瀉心湯） 李瀕湖曰:此乃臍之左右上下, 有氣築築然牢而痛, 正沖、任、足少陰、太陰四經病也. 成無己註文, 以為左肝右肺, 上心下脾, 蓋未審四臟乃兼邪耳. 岐伯曰:海有東西南北, 人亦有四海以應之.

### Literal Translation

(Gancao Xiexin Tang.) Li Binhu said: This is when, on the left, right, above, and below the navel, there is qi that is firm and painful like a rampart; it is precisely the disease of the four channels of the Chong, Ren, Foot-Shaoyin, and Foot-Taiyin. Cheng Wuji's commentary considered it as left liver, right lung, above heart, below spleen, but he had not examined that the four viscera also carry pathogenic factors together. Qibo said: The sea has east, west, south, and north, and a person also has four seas to respond to it.
