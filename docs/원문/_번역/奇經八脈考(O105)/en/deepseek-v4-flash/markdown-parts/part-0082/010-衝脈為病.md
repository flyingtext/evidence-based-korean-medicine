### Source Text

不已刺氣街, 按之立已. 李東垣曰:秋冬之月, 胃脈四道為衝脈所逆, 脅下少陽脈二道而反上行, 名曰厥逆. 其證:氣上衝, 咽不得息而喘息有音, 不得臥.

### Literal Translation

If it does not stop, needle Qijie (ST30); pressing it, it stops immediately. Li Dongyuan said: In the autumn and winter months, the four pathways of the stomach vessel are counterflowed by the Chong vessel, and the two pathways of the Shaoyang vessel below the ribs instead run upward; this is called jue ni (reversal and counterflow). Its signs: qi rushes upward, the throat cannot breathe, and there is sound in breathing, and one cannot lie down.
