## O105:p005:t0010

### Source Text

（在耳前起骨上廉, 開口有空. 乃手足少陽、陽明之會） 又曰:寸口脈, 從少陽斜至厥陰, 是陰維脈也. 動苦u癲u癎僵仆羊鳴, 又苦僵仆失音, 肌肉痹癢, 應時自發汗出, 惡風身u洗洗u然也. 取陽白、金門、（見前）僕參.

### Literal Translation

(On the upper border of the bone that rises in front of the ear, with a hollow when the mouth is opened; it is the confluence of hand-foot Shaoyang and Yangming.) He also said: When the pulse at the cum-kou runs obliquely from Shaoyang to Jueyin, this is the Yin Wei vessel. In motion it causes suffering from mania-epilepsy (dian-xian), falling stiffly with bleating like a goat; and again suffering from falling stiffly with loss of voice, numbness and itching of the muscles, and spontaneously sweating at the [regular] time, aversion to wind, and a shivering (xixi) appearance of the body. Take Yangbai, Jinmen (see before), and Pucan (BL61).
