## O105:p005:t0017

### Source Text

王啟玄曰:肉裡之脈, 少陽所生, 陽維脈氣所發, 絕骨之後, 陽維所過分肉穴也. 在足外踝直上絕骨之端, 如後二分筋肉分間, 刺可五分. 飛陽之脈, 令人腰痛, 痛拂拂然, 甚則悲以恐.

### Literal Translation

Wang Qixuan said: The vessel of the flesh-interior (rouli zhi mai) is that born of Shaoyang and is [that by which] the vessel-qi of the Yang Wei is issued; behind the juegu (severed bone) is the acupoint of the fleshy division through which the Yang Wei passes, which is directly above the outer ankle at the end of the juegu bone, in the division between sinew and flesh about two fen behind — one may puncture five fen. The Feiyang vessel makes a person suffer lumbar pain, the pain waving (fufu), and in severe cases [he is] grieved and fearful.
