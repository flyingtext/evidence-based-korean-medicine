## O105:p008:t0010

### Source Text

內筋, 即陰蹺之郄, 交信穴也. 素問繆刺論曰:邪客於足陽蹺之脈, 令人目痛, 從內眥始. 刺外踝之下半寸所各二痏, （即申脈也）左刺右, 右刺左, 如人行十里頃而已.

### Literal Translation

The inner sinew is the xi (cleft) of the Yin Qiao, the Jiaoxin point. The Miaoci Lun (On Needling the Collaterals) of the Su wen says: When a pathogenic [influence] lodges in the vessel of the Foot Yang Qiao, it makes a person suffer eye pain, beginning from the inner canthus. Puncture at the place half a cun below the outer ankle with two points each (this is Shenmai), needling the left for the right and the right for the left, and [the disease] is settled after [a time] roughly like a person walking about ten li.
