## O105:p009:t0004

### Source Text

天宦不脫於血, 而任沖不盛, 宗筋不強, 有氣無血, 唇口不榮, 故須亦不生. 素問水熱穴論曰:三陰之所交, 結於腳也. 踝上各一行者, 此腎脈之下行也.

### Literal Translation

In the natural eunuch, blood is not lost, but the Ren and Chong are not abundant and the ancestral sinew is not strong; there is qi but no blood, and the lips and mouth are not nourished, therefore the beard also does not grow. The Suwen Shuire Xue Lun (Discussion of Water-Heat Acupoints) says: The place where the three yin meet is bound at the foot. The one line above each ankle is the downward course of the kidney vessel.
