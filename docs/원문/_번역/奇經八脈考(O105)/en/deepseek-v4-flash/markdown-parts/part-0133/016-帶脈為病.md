## O105:p016:t0002

### Source Text

張潔古曰:帶脈之病, 太陰主之, 宜灸章門二穴, 三壯. 素問曰:邪客於太陰之絡, 令人腰痛引小腹控䏚, 不可以養息. （䏚謂季脅下之空軟處） 張仲景曰:大病瘥後, 腰以下有水氣, 牡蠣澤瀉散主之.

### Literal Translation

Zhang Jiegu said: For disease of the Belt Vessel, the Greater Yin governs it; one should moxa the two Zhangmen points, three zhuang. The Plain Questions says: When pathogenic qi lodges in the collateral of the Greater Yin, it causes lower back pain that draws the lower abdomen and pulls at the flank (miao), so that one cannot rest and breathe (miao refers to the empty soft region below the floating rib). Zhang Zhongjing said: After a serious illness has recovered, if there is water-qi below the lower back, the Muli Zexie Powder governs it.
