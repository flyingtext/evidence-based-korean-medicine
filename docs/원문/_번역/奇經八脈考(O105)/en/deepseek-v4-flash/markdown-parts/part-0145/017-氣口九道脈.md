## O105:p017:t0002

### Source Text

各出診法, 乃岐伯秘授黃帝之訣也, 扁鵲推之, 獨取寸口以決死生. 蓋氣口為百脈流注朝會之始故也. 三部雖傳, 而九道淪隱, 故奇經之脈, 世無人知.

### Literal Translation

Each gives forth its examination method; these are the secret formulas that Qibo secretly transmitted to the Yellow Emperor. Bian Que extended them, taking only the cunkou to determine life and death. This is because the qi opening is the beginning where the hundred vessels flow and converge. Although the three regions have been transmitted, the nine paths have sunk into obscurity, and so the vessels of the extraordinary channels are unknown to the world.
