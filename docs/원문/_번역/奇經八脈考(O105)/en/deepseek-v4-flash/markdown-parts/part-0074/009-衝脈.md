## O105:p009:t0007

### Source Text

足少陰腎脈與衝脈合而盛大, 故曰太衝. 兩脈相合為表裡也. 衝脈在脾之下, 故曰其衝在下, 名曰太陰.

### Literal Translation

The Foot-Shaoyin kidney vessel and the Chong vessel combine and become great and abundant, therefore it is called Taichong. The two vessels combine with each other as exterior and interior. The Chong vessel is below the spleen, therefore it is said that its Chong being below is named Taiyin.
