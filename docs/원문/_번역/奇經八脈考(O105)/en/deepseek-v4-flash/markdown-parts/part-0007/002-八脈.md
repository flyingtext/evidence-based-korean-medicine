## O105:p002:t0003

### Source Text

督主身後之陽, 任、沖主身前之陰, 以南北言也. 帶脈橫束諸脈, 以六合言也. 是故醫而知乎八脈, 則十二經、十五絡之大旨得矣.

### Literal Translation

The Du governs the yang at the back of the body, while the Ren and the Chong govern the yin at the front of the body — spoken of in terms of north and south. The Dai vessel binds the various vessels transversely — spoken of in terms of the six directions (liuhe). For this reason, if a physician knows the eight vessels, then the great purport of the twelve primary channels and the fifteen connecting vessels is attained.
