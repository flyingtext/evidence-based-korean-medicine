### Literal Translation

(Foot-Yangming is two cun from the midline of the abdomen, Foot-Shaoyin is five fen from the midline of the abdomen, and the Chong vessel runs between the two channels. Henggu is in the pubic bone above the genitals, shaped like a reclining crescent moon, one and a half cun from the midline of the abdomen.) It runs beside the navel, five fen on each side, ascending through Dahe (KI12), (one cun above Henggu, one and a half cun from the midline of the abdomen) Qixue (KI13), (that is, the Baomen, also called Zihu; one cun above Dahe, one and a half cun from the midline of the abdomen; the meeting of Foot-Shaoyin and the Chong vessel) Siman (KI14), (one cun above Qixue) Zhongzhu (KI15), (one cun above Siman) Huangshu (KI16), (one cun above Zhongzhu) Shangqu (KI17), (two cun above Huangshu) Shiguan (KI18), (one cun above Shangqu) Yindu (KI19), (one cun above Shiguan) Tonggu (KI20), (one cun above Yindu) Youmen (KI21), (one cun above Tonggu, flanking both sides of Juque (CV14), each five fen, in the hollow) until it reaches the chest and disperses, twenty-four points in all. The Lingshu Jing says: The Chong and Ren both arise within the uterus, ascend along the back internally, and are the sea of the channels and collaterals. Their superficial and external [branch] ascends along the right side of the abdomen, meets at the throat, and branches to connect the lips and mouth.
