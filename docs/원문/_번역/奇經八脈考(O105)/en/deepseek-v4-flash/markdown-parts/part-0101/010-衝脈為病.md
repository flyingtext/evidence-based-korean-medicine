## O105:p010:t0021

### Source Text

血海有餘, 則常想其身大, 怫然不知其所病;血海不足, 亦常想其身小, 狹然不知其所病. 水穀之海有餘, 則腹滿, 水穀之海不足, 則飢不受食. 髓海有餘, 則輕勁多力, 自過其度;髓海不足, 則腦轉耳鳴, 脛酸眩冒, 目無所見, 懈怠安臥.

### Literal Translation

When the sea of blood is in excess, one constantly thinks one's body is large, and is gloomy without knowing one's disease; when the sea of blood is insufficient, one also constantly thinks one's body is small, and is narrow without knowing one's disease. When the sea of water and grain is in excess, there is abdominal fullness; when the sea of water and grain is insufficient, one is hungry but does not receive food. When the sea of marrow is in excess, one is light, vigorous, and strong, surpassing one's own measure; when the sea of marrow is insufficient, the brain turns, the ears ring, the shins are sore, there is dizziness and dim vision, the eyes see nothing, and one is languid and fond of lying down.
