## O105:p015:t0002

### Source Text

靈樞經曰:足少陰之正, 至膕中, 別走太陽而合, 上至腎, 當十四椎, 出屬帶脈. 楊氏曰:帶脈總束諸脈, 使不妄行, 如人束帶而前垂, 故名. 婦人惡露, 隨帶脈而下, 故謂之帶下.

### Literal Translation

The Spiritual Pivot Classic says: The branch of the foot Lesser Yin (Kidney) reaches the popliteal fossa, then branches to the Greater Yang and joins it, ascends to the kidney, at the fourteenth vertebra emerges and belongs to the Belt Vessel. Master Yang said: The Belt Vessel binds and restrains all the vessels, keeping them from running recklessly, just as a person's belt hangs down in front; hence its name. In women, lochia (elou) descends along the Belt Vessel, and so it is called vaginal discharge (daixia).
