動苦少腹痛上搶心, 有u瘕u疝遺u溺, u脅支滿煩, 女子絕孕. 又曰:尺寸俱牢, 直上直下, 此乃衝脈, 胸中有寒疝也. 張仲景曰:傷寒動氣在右不可發汗, 汗之則衄而渴, 心苦煩, 飲水即吐;（先以五苓散, 次以竹葉湯）不可下, 下之則津液內竭, 頭眩咽燥, 鼻乾心悸.

### Literal Translation

When it moves, one suffers lower abdominal pain rushing up to the heart, with u瘕u (abdominal masses), hernia, and enuresis, fullness and vexation of the flanks, and in women, loss of the ability to conceive. It also says: When cun and chi are both firm, going straight up and straight down, this is the Chong vessel, with cold hernia in the chest. Zhang Zhongjing said: In cold damage, when the moving qi is on the right, one must not cause sweating; if one sweats it, there will be nosebleed and thirst, vexation of the heart, and vomiting immediately upon drinking water; (first use Wuling San, then Zhuye Tang) one must not purge; if one purges it, the fluids will be exhausted internally, with dizziness, dry throat, dry nose, and palpitations.
