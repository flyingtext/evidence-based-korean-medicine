## O105:p005:t0015

### Source Text

王啟玄曰:陽維起於陽, 則太陽之所生, 並行而上至腨, 下復與太陽合而上也. 去地一尺, 乃承山穴也. 在銳腨之下, 分肉間陷中, 可刺七分.

### Literal Translation

Wang Qixuan said: The Yang Wei arises in the yang, then it is that born of Taiyang, running in parallel and ascending to the calf, then below joining once more with Taiyang and ascending. One chi from the ground is the Chengshan point (BL57). It is below the sharp calf, in the depression between the fleshy divisions; one may puncture seven fen.
