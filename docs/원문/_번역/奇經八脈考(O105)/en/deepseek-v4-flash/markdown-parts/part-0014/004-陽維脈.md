## O105:p004:t0004

### Source Text

（在瞳人直上, 入髮際五分陷中）下額與手足少陽、陽明, 五脈會於陽白. （眉上一寸, 直瞳人相對）循頭, 入耳, 上至本神而止. （本神直耳上入髮際中）凡三十二穴.

### Literal Translation

(Directly above the pupil, entering the hairline five fen, in the depression.) Descending on the forehead, it joins hand-foot Shaoyang and Yangming, five vessels, at Yangbai (GB14). (One cun above the eyebrow, directly facing the pupil.) Following the head it enters the ear, and ascending reaches Benshen (GB13) and stops. (Benshen is directly above the ear in the hairline.) In all there are thirty-two points.
