### Source Text

則寒熱之在表而兼太陽證者, 有汗當用桂枝、無汗當用麻黃;寒熱之在半表半裡而兼少陽證者, 當用小柴胡加減治之. 若夫營衛惵卑, 而病寒熱者, 黃耆建中及八物湯之類主之. 潔古獨以桂枝一證屬之陽維, 似未u擴u充.

### Literal Translation

Then, for the chills and fever that are in the exterior and also have the Taiyang signs, if there is sweating one should use Guizhi, and if there is no sweating one should use Mahuang (Ephedra); for the chills and fever that are half-interior half-exterior and also have the Shaoyang signs, one should treat it with Xiaochaihu Tang (Minor Bupleurum Decoction) with additions and subtractions. As for that wherein ying and wei are debilitated and [the person] has chills and fever, Huangqi Jianzhong (Tang) (Astragalus Center-Fortifying Decoction) and Babu Tang (Eight-Ingredient Decoction) and the like govern it. That Jiegu alone ascribes the single Guizhi pattern to the Yang Wei seems not yet fully extended/developed.
