## O105:p010:t0008

### Source Text

素問u痿u論曰:治痿獨取陽明者何也?曰:陽明者, 五臟六腑之海也, 主潤宗筋, 宗筋主束骨而利機關. 衝脈者, 經脈之海, 主滲灌溪谷, 與陽明合於宗筋, 會於氣街, 而陽明為之長, 皆屬於帶脈, 而絡於督脈. 故陽明虛則宗筋縱、帶脈不引, 故足痿不用.

### Literal Translation

The Suwen u痿u Lun (Discussion of Atrophy) says: Why does the treatment of atrophy take only the Yangming? It says: The Yangming is the sea of the five viscera and six bowels; it governs moistening the ancestral sinew, and the ancestral sinew governs binding the bones and benefiting the joints. The Chong vessel is the sea of the channels and vessels; it governs seeping and irrigating the valleys [between muscles], and together with the Yangming meets at the ancestral sinew and converges at Qijie, and the Yangming is its chief; they all belong to the Dai vessel and connect to the Du vessel. Therefore when the Yangming is deficient, the ancestral sinew relaxes and the Dai vessel does not draw, and therefore the feet atrophy and are not used.
