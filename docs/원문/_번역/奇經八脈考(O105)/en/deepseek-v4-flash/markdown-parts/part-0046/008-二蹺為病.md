## O105:p008:t0002

### Source Text

又曰:寸口脈前部左右彈者, 陽蹺也. 動苦腰背痛, 叉為癲u癇u僵仆羊鳴, 惡風偏枯、𤸷痹、身體強. 又曰:微u澀u為風癇, 並取陽蹺, 在外踝上三寸, 直絕骨是穴.

### Literal Translation

He also said: When the pulse at the front part of the cum-kou [position] rebounds to the left and right, it is the Yang Qiao. In motion it causes suffering from lumbar and back pain, and in addition mania-epilepsy with falling stiffly and bleating like a goat, aversion to wind, hemiplegia (pianku), a numbness-bi (𤸷痺), and a stiff body. He also said: Slight choppiness (se) is wind-epilepsy; also take the Yang Qiao, [which is] three cun above the outer ankle, straight at the juegu [bone] — that is the point. (That is the Fuyang point.)
