陰蹺為病, 陰急則陰厥脛直, 五絡不通, 表和里病;陽蹺為病, 陽急則狂走目不u昧, u表病里和. 陰病則熱, 可灸照海、陽陵泉, （在膝下一寸䯒外廉陷中, 足少陽之合也, 筋病治此）陽病則寒, 可針風池、風府. （風府在項後入髮際一寸, 大筋內宛宛中, 督脈, 太陽、陽維之會也）又曰:在陽表者當汗之, 在陰里者當下之.

### Literal Translation

In the disease of the Yin Qiao, when the yin is tense, then there is yin reversal with straightened shanks and the five luo blocked, the exterior harmonious and the interior diseased; in the disease of the Yang Qiao, when the yang is tense, then one runs wildly with eyes that do not close, the exterior diseased and the interior harmonious. In yin disease there is heat, and one may moxa Zhaohai and Yanglingquan (GB34) (one cun below the knee in the depression at the outer border of the shank bone, which is the he-(sea) point of Foot Shaoyang; sinew diseases are treated here); in yang disease there is cold, and one may needle Fengchi and Fengfu (DU16) (Fengfu is one cun [in] entering the hairline at the back of the neck, in the hollow in the middle within the great sinew; it is the confluence of the Du, Taiyang, and Yang Wei). He also said: Those on the exterior at the yang[side] should be sweated out, and those on the interior at the yin[side] should be purged.
