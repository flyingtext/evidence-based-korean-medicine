## O105:p006:t0002

### Source Text

張紫陽八脈經云:八脈者:衝脈在風府穴下, 督脈在臍後, 任脈在臍前, 帶脈在腰, 陰蹺脈在尾閭前陰囊下, 陽蹺脈在尾閭後二節, 陰維脈在頂前一寸三分, 陽維脈在頂後一寸三分. 凡人有此八脈, 俱屬陰神, 閉而不開, 惟神仙以陽氣衝開, 故能得道. 八脈者, 先天大道之根, 一氣之祖.

### Literal Translation

The Ba Mai Jing (Eight Vessels Classic) of Zhang Ziyang says: The eight vessels are: the Chong meridian below the Fengfu point, the Du meridian behind the navel, the Ren meridian in front of the navel, the Dai meridian at the waist, the Yin Qiao meridian in front of the coccyx (weilü) below the scrotum, the Yang Qiao meridian two segments behind the coccyx, the Yin Wei meridian one cun and three fen in front of the crown, and the Yang Wei meridian one cun and three fen behind the crown. Every person has these eight vessels; they all belong to the yin spirit and are sealed and not opened, and only the immortals burst them open with yang-qi, and so can attain the Dao. The eight vessels are the root of the great way of former heaven (xiantian) and the ancestor of the one qi.
