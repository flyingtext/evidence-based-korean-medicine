## O105:p010:t0004

### Source Text

又曰:凡逆氣上衝, 或兼裡急, 或作躁熱, 皆衝脈逆也. 若內傷病此, 宜補中益氣湯加炒蘖、炒連、知母, 以泄衝脈. 凡腎火旺, 及任、督、沖三脈盛者, 則宜用酒炒黃蘖、知母, 亦不可久服, 恐妨胃也.

### Literal Translation

He also said: Whenever counterflow qi rushes upward, whether accompanied by internal urgency or producing restless heat, it is all counterflow of the Chong vessel. If internal injury produces this, one should use Buzhong Yiqi Tang (Supplement the Center and Boost Qi Decoction) with the addition of stir-fried Huangbai, stir-fried Huanglian, and Zhimu, to drain the Chong vessel. Whenever kidney fire is exuberant, and the Ren, Du, and Chong three vessels are abundant, one should use wine-fried Huangbai and Zhimu; but they also must not be taken for long, for fear of harming the stomach.
