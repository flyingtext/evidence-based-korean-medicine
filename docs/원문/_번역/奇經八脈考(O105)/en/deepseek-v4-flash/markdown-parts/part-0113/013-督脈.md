## O105:p013:t0004

### Source Text

凡三十一穴. 督脈別絡, 自長強走任脈者, 由少腹直上, 貫臍中央, 上貫心, 入喉, 上頤, 環唇, 上系兩目之下中央, 會太陽於目內眥睛明穴. （見陰蹺下）上額, 與足厥陰同會於巔.

### Literal Translation

Thirty-one points in all. The separate collateral of the Du vessel, going from Changqiang to the Ren vessel, ascends straight from the lower abdomen, pierces the center of the navel, ascends piercing the heart, enters the throat, ascends the chin, encircles the lip, ascends to connect the center below both eyes, and meets Taiyang at the Jingming point (BL1) at the inner canthus. (See under Yin Qiao.) It ascends the forehead and meets Foot-Jueyin together at the crown.
