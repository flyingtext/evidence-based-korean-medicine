## O105:p009:t0006

### Source Text

素問陰陽離合論曰:聖人南面而立, 前曰廣明, 後曰太衝. 太衝之地, 名曰少陰, 其衝在下, 名曰太陰. 啟玄曰:心臟在南, 故前曰廣明, 衝脈在北, 故後曰太衝.

### Literal Translation

The Suwen Yinyang Lili Lun (Discussion of the Separation and Union of Yin and Yang) says: The sage stands facing south; the front is called Guangming, and the back is called Taichong. The place of Taichong is named Shaoyin; its Chong being below is named Taiyin. Qixuan said: The heart viscus is in the south, therefore the front is called Guangming; the Chong vessel is in the north, therefore the back is called Taichong.
