Next, Siwu [decoction] with honghua, adjusted with linglijia (pangolin scales), taoren (peach kernel), and gui (cinnamon), with boy's urine, decocted in wine and taken. Last, Siwu [decoction] with honghua, adjusted with Yilao Moyao Powder. Zhang Zihe said: The twelve channels and the seven extraordinary vessels all flow up and down in a circuit; only the Belt Vessel arises at the side of the lower abdomen, below the floating rib, encircles the body once, binds the lower back and passes through, like the form of a belt.
