### Source Text

白淫者, 白物淫衍, 如精之狀, 男子因溲而下, 女子綿綿而下也, 皆從濕熱治之, 與治痢同法. 赤白痢乃邪熱傳於大腸, 赤白帶乃邪熱傳於小腸, 後世皆以赤為熱、白為寒, 流誤千載, 是醫誤之矣. 又曰:資生經載一婦人患赤白帶下, 有人為灸氣海未效, 次日為灸帶脈穴, 有鬼附耳云, 昨日灸亦好, 只灸我不著, 今灸著我, 我去矣, 可為酒食祭我.

### Literal Translation

White emission is white matter that spreads and overflows, like the form of semen; in men it descends with urination, in women it descends continuously. All are treated as damp-heat, by the same method as treating dysentery. Red and white dysentery is pathogenic heat transmitted to the large intestine; red and white vaginal discharge is pathogenic heat transmitted to the small intestine. Later generations all take red as heat and white as cold, an error that has flowed for a thousand years; this is a mistake of physicians. He also said: The Zisheng Classic records a woman suffering red and white vaginal discharge; someone moxaed Qihai for her without effect. The next day he moxaed the Daimai point, and a ghost attached to her ear said: Yesterday's moxa was also good, only it did not hit me; now you have hit me, I am leaving. You may prepare wine and food to sacrifice to me.
