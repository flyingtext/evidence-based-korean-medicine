## O105:p012:t0001

### Source Text

素問曰:任脈為病, 男子內結七疝, 女子帶下瘕聚. 又曰:女子二七而天癸至, 任脈通, 太衝脈盛, 月事以時下, 七七任脈虛, 太衝脈衰, 天癸竭, 地道不通, 故形壞而無子. 又曰:上氣有音者, 治其缺盆中.

### Literal Translation

The Suwen says: When the Ren vessel is diseased, in men it binds internally as the seven hernias, and in women as vaginal discharge and abdominal masses. It also says: In women, at two times seven [years] the heavenly gui arrives, the Ren vessel is open, and the Taichong vessel is abundant, and the menses come down at the proper time; at seven times seven [years] the Ren vessel is deficient, the Taichong vessel declines, the heavenly gui is exhausted, and the earthly pathway is not open, therefore the body is damaged and there is no child. It also says: When the upper qi has sound, treat its supraclavicular fossa center.
