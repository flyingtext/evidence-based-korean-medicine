## O105:p010:t0012

### Source Text

李瀕湖曰:濕熱成痿, 乃不足中有餘也. 宜滲泄之藥. 若精血枯涸成痿, 乃不足中之不足也, 全要峻補之藥.

### Literal Translation

Li Binhu said: Damp-heat producing atrophy is excess within deficiency. One should use medicines that seep and drain. If withered and dried essence and blood produce atrophy, it is deficiency within deficiency, and one entirely needs medicines that strongly supplement.
