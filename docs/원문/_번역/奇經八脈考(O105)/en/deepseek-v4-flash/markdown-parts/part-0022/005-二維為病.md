## O105:p005:t0008

### Source Text

凡寒痛, 兼少陰及任脈者, 四逆湯;兼厥陰者, 當歸四逆湯;兼太陰者, 理中湯主之. 凡熱痛, 兼少陰及任脈者, 金鈴散、延胡索散;兼厥陰者, 失笑散. 兼太陰者, 承氣湯主之.

### Literal Translation

For all cold pain, when it also involves Shaoyin and the Ren meridian, Sini Tang governs it; when it also involves Jueyin, Danggui Sini Tang; when it also involves Taiyin, Lizhong Tang. For all heat pain, when it also involves Shaoyin and the Ren meridian, Jinling San (Melia-toosendan Powder) and Yanhusuo San (Corydalis Powder); when it also involves Jueyin, Shixiao San (Laughing-Dispersion Powder). When it also involves Taiyin, Chengqi Tang (Qi-Carrying Decoction) governs it.
