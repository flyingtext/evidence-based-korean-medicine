## O105:p005:t0009

### Source Text

若營血內傷, 兼夫任、沖、手厥陰者, 則宜四物湯、養營湯、妙香散之類. 因病藥之, 如此則陰陽虛實, 庶乎其不u瘥u矣. 王叔和脈經曰:寸口脈, 從少陰斜至太陽, 是陽維脈也, 動苦肌肉痹癢, 皮膚痛, 下部不仁, 汗出而寒;又苦顛u仆u羊鳴, 手足相引, 甚者失音不能言, 宜取客主人.

### Literal Translation

If the ying-blood is internally injured and it also involves the Ren, Chong, and Hand Jueyin, then one should use Si Wu Tang (Four-Substance Decoction), Yangying Tang (Nourishing-the-Ying Decoction), Miaoxiang San (Wonderful-Fragrance Powder) and the like. Treating the disease with these medicinals accordingly — in this way, with regard to the emptiness and substance of yin and yang, one will almost not err. The Maijing of Wang Shuhe says: When the pulse at the cum-kou runs obliquely from Shaoyin to Taiyang, this is the Yang Wei vessel; in motion it causes suffering from numbness and itching of the muscles, pain of the skin, numbness of the lower parts, sweating out and [aversion to] cold; and again suffering from falling down with bleating like a goat (yang-ming), hands and feet drawn together, and in severe cases loss of voice so one cannot speak — one should take Kezhuren (Kezhuren, i.e. Shangguan, GB3).
