## O105:p017:t0008

### Source Text

中部中央直中者, 手厥陰心主也, 動苦心痛, 面赤多喜怒, 食苦咽. 微浮苦悲傷恍惚, 澀為心下寒, 沉為恐怖, 如人將捕之狀, 時寒熱, 有血氣. 後部中央直者, 手太陰肺、手陽明大腸也, 動苦咳, 逆氣不得息, 浮為風, 沉為熱, 緊為胸中積熱, 澀為時咳血.

### Literal Translation

When the middle region is straight in the center, it is the hand Absolute Yin Heart Governor; when it moves it causes heart pain, red face with much anger and joy, and bitter taste with difficulty swallowing. Slightly floating causes bitter grief and trance; rough is cold below the heart; deep is fear and terror, like the appearance of one about to be seized, with occasional chills and fever, and blood and qi. When the rear region is straight in the center, it is the hand Greater Yin Lung and hand Yang Ming Large Intestine; when it moves it causes cough, rebellious qi with inability to breathe; floating is wind, deep is heat, tight is accumulated heat in the chest, rough is occasional coughing of blood.
