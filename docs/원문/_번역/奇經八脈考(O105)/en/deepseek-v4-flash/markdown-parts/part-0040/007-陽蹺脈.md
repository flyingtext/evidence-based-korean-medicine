## O105:p007:t0002

### Source Text

（在外踝上三寸, 足太陽之穴也）直上循股外廉, 循脅後髀. 上會手太陽、陽維於臑腧. （在肩後大骨下胛上廉陷中）上行肩髆外廉, 會手陽明於巨骨, （在肩尖端上行兩叉骨罅間陷中）會手陽明少陽於肩髃.

### Literal Translation

(Three cun above the outer ankle is a point of Foot Taiyang.) Ascending straight it follows the outer border of the thigh, [then] follows behind the flank to the hip. Above, it joins Hand Taiyang and the Yang Wei at Naoshu (SI10) (in the depression on the upper border of the scapula below the large bone behind the shoulder). Ascending along the outer border of the shoulder-blade, it joins Hand Yangming at Jugu (LI16) (ascending at the shoulder tip within the depression in the crevice between the two forked bones), and joins Hand Yangming and Shaoyang at Jianyu (LI15).
