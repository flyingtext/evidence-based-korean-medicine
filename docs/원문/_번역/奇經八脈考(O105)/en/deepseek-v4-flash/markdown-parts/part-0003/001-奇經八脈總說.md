## O105:p001:t0003

### Source Text

奇經凡八脈, 不拘制於十二正經, 無表裡配合, 故謂之奇. 蓋正經猶夫溝渠, 奇經猶夫湖澤, 正經之脈隆盛, 則溢於奇經. 故秦越人比之:天雨降下, 溝渠溢滿, 霶霈妄行, 流於湖澤, 此發靈、素未發之秘旨也.

### Literal Translation

The extraordinary meridians number eight vessels in all: they are not constrained or controlled by the twelve regular channels and have no interior-exterior pairing, and so they are called extraordinary. This is because the regular channels are like ditches and canals, whereas the extraordinary meridians are like lakes and marshes; when the qi of the regular channels is vigorous and full, it then overflows into the extraordinary meridians. Therefore Qin Yueren compared it: when heavenly rain descends, the ditches and canals overflow and fill, pouring out in a driving torrent, flowing into the lakes and marshes — this brings out the secret purport that the Ling and Su [jing] had not yet revealed.
