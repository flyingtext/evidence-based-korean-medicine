### Source Text

奇經八脈者:陰維也、陽維也、陰u蹺u也、陽蹺也、沖也、任也、督也、帶也. 陽維起於諸陽之會, 由外u踝u而上行於衛分;陰維起於諸陰之交, 由內踝而上行於營分, 所以為一身之綱維也. 陽蹺起於u跟u中, 循外踝上行於身之左右;陰蹺起於跟中, 循內踝上行於身之左右, 所以使機關之蹺捷也.

### Literal Translation

The eight extraordinary vessels are: the Yin Wei, the Yang Wei, the Yin Qiao, the Yang Qiao, the Chong, the Ren, the Du, and the Dai. The Yang Wei arises at the confluence of all the yang [meridians], and from the outer ankle ascends traveling in the wei-[qi] division; the Yin Wei arises at the intersection of all the yin [meridians], and from the inner ankle ascends traveling in the ying-[qi] division, and so it is the general cord and outline of the whole body. The Yang Qiao arises in the middle of the heel (gen), follows the outer ankle, and ascends to the left and right of the body; the Yin Qiao arises in the middle of the heel, follows the inner ankle, and ascends to the left and right of the body, and so it makes the joints nimble and quick.
