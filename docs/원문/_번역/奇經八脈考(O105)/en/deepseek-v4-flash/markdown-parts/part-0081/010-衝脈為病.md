## O105:p010:t0001

### Source Text

越人難經曰:衝脈為病, 逆氣而裡急. 靈樞經曰:氣逆上, 刺膺中陷下者, 與下胸動脈. 腹痛, 刺臍左右動脈, 按之立已.

### Literal Translation

Yue Ren's Nanjing (Classic of Difficulties) says: When the Chong vessel is diseased, there is counterflow qi and internal urgency. The Lingshu Jing says: When qi counterflows upward, needle the hollow below the chest center and the artery of the lower chest. For abdominal pain, needle the arteries on the left and right of the navel; pressing them, it stops immediately.
