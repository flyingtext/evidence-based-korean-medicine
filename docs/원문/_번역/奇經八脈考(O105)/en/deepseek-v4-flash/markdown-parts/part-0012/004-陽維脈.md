## O105:p004:t0002

### Source Text

（在章門下八寸, 監骨上陷中）循脅肋, 斜上肘上, 會手陽明、手足太陽於臂臑. （在肘上七寸, 兩筋u罅u陷中, 肩髃下一寸）過肩前, 與手少陽會幹臑會、天髎. （臑會在肩前廉, 去肩端三寸宛宛中. 天髎在缺盆中, 上u毖u骨際, 陷中央）卻會手足少陽、足陽明於肩井.

### Literal Translation

(Eight cun below Zhangmen (LR13), in the depression above the jian gu bone.) Following the flank and ribs it ascends obliquely above the elbow, joining Hand Yangming and hand-foot Taiyang at Binao (LI14). (Seven cun above the elbow, in the crevice-depression between the two sinews, one cun below Jianyu (LI15).) Passing in front of the shoulder, it joins Hand Shaoyang at Naohui (TE13) and Tianliao (TE15). (Naohui is in front of the shoulder, in the middle hollow three cun from the shoulder tip. Tianliao is in the middle hollow on the superior border of the bone at the top of the supraclavicular fossa.) Then it joins hand-foot Shaoyang and Foot Yangming at Jianjing (GB21).
