而沖、任二脈, 循腹脅, 夾臍旁, 傳流於氣衝, 屬於帶脈, 絡於督脈, 沖、任、督三脈, 同起而異行, 一源而三岐, 皆絡帶脈. 因諸經上下往來, 遺熱於帶脈之間, 客熱鬱抑, 白物滿溢, 隨u溲u而下, 綿綿不絕, 是為白帶. 內經云:思想無窮, 所願不得, 意淫於外, 入房太甚, 發為筋痿, 及為白淫.

### Literal Translation

And the Chong and Ren vessels follow the abdomen and flanks, flanking the side of the navel, flowing to Qichong, belonging to the Belt Vessel, and connecting to the Governing Vessel. The Chong, Ren, and Governing vessels arise together but travel differently; one source with three branches, all connecting to the Belt Vessel. Because the various channels come and go above and below, they leave residual heat among the Belt Vessel; guest heat becomes depressed and suppressed, and white matter overflows, descending with urination (sou), continuous and unending; this is white vaginal discharge. The Inner Classic says: When thoughts are boundless and one's desires are not obtained, and the mind is given over to external things, and intercourse is too excessive, it develops into sinew flaccidity (jinwei) and also into white emission (baiyin).
