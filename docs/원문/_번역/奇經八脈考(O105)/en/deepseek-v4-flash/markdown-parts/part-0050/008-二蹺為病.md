### Source Text

又曰:癲癇晝發灸陽蹺, 夜發灸陰蹺. 素問腰痛論曰:腰痛不可舉者, 申脈、僕參舉之. （太陽之穴, 陽蹺之本也）又曰:會陰之脈, 令人腰痛, 痛上u漯漯u然汗出, 汗乾令人慾飲, 飲已欲走, 刺直陽之脈上三痏.

### Literal Translation

He also said: For mania-epilepsy that occurs by day, moxa the Yang Qiao; for [that which occurs] by night, moxa the Yin Qiao. The Lumbago Theory of the Su wen says: For lumbar pain where one cannot raise [the body], Shenmai and Pucan raise it. (The points of Taiyang, the root of the Yang Qiao.) It also says: The vessel of Huiyin makes a person suffer lumbar pain, with dripping sweat welling up on the pain; when the sweat dries it makes one want to drink, and after drinking one wants to run about. Puncture the vessel of the straight-yang (Zhiyang) above with three points.
