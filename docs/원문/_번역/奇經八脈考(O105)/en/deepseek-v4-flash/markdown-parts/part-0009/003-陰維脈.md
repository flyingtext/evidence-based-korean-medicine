## O105:p003:t0001

### Source Text

陰維起於諸陰之交, 其脈發於足少陰築賓穴, 為陰維之郄, 在內踝上五寸腨肉分中. 上循股內廉, 上行入小腹, 會足太陰、厥陰、少陰、陽明於腑舍. （在腹結下三寸, 去腹中行四寸半）上會足太陰於大橫、腹哀.

### Literal Translation

The Yin Wei arises at the intersection of all the yin [meridians]; its vessel issues at the Zhubin point (KI9) of Foot Shaoyin, which is the xi (cleft) point of the Yin Wei, situated in the fleshy division of the calf, five cun above the inner ankle. Ascending following the inner border of the thigh, it runs up into the lower abdomen, where it joins Foot Taiyin, Jueyin, Shaoyin, and Yangming at Fushe. (Three cun below Fujie (SP14), four and a half cun from the abdominal midline.) Above, it joins Foot Taiyin at Daheng (SP15) and Fu'ai (SP16).
