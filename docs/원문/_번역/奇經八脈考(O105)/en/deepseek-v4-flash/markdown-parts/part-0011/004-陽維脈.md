## O105:p004:t0001

### Source Text

陽維起於諸陽之會, 其脈發於足太陽金門穴, 在足外踝下一寸五分. 上外踝七寸會足少陽於陽交, 為陽維之郄. （在外踝上七寸, 斜屬二陽之間）循膝外廉, 上u髀厭, u抵少腹側, 會足少陽於居髎.

### Literal Translation

The Yang Wei arises at the confluence of all the yang [meridians]; its vessel issues at the Jinmen point (BL63) of Foot Taiyang, one cun and five fen below the outer ankle. Ascending seven cun above the outer ankle, it joins Foot Shaoyang at Yangjiao (GB35), which is the xi (cleft) point of the Yang Wei. (Seven cun above the outer ankle, obliquely attached between the two yang [meridians].) Following the outer border of the knee it ascends past the hip joint (bi-yan), reaches the side of the lower abdomen, and joins Foot Shaoyang at Juliao (GB29).
