## O105:p007:t0004

### Source Text

（見陰蹺下）從睛明上行入髮際, 下耳後, 入風池而終. （風池在耳後, 夾玉枕骨下發際陷中）凡二十二穴. 難經曰:蹺脈從足至目, 長七尺五寸, 合一丈五尺.

### Literal Translation

(See under the Yin Qiao below.) From Jingming it ascends entering the hairline, descends behind the ear, and, entering Fengchi, ends there. (Fengchi is behind the ear, in the depression in the hairline flanking the Yuzhen bone below.) In all there are twenty-two points. The Nanjing says: The Qiao vessel from the foot to the eye is seven chi five cun long; joined [with its mate] it makes one zhang five chi.
