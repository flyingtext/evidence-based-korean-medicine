## O105:p009:t0011

### Source Text

三焦者, 從頭至心、心至臍、臍至足, 為上中下三焦, 其實真元一氣也. 故曰有臟無腑. 脈訣云:三焦無狀空有名, 寄在胸中膈相應.

### Literal Translation

The Sanjiao extends from the head to the heart, from the heart to the navel, and from the navel to the foot, constituting the upper, middle, and lower Sanjiao; in reality it is the single primordial qi. Therefore it is said to have a viscus but no bowel. The Maijue (Pulse Song) says: The Sanjiao has no form and only an empty name, lodged in the chest corresponding to the diaphragm.
