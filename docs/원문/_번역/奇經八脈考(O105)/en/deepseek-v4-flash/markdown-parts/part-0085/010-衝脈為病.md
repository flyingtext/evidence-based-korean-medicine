### Source Text

或腹中刺痛, 或裡急, 宜多用甘草, 或虛坐而大便不得者, 皆屬血虛, 血虛則裡急, 宜用當歸. 逆氣裡急, 膈咽不通, 大便不行者, 宜升陽瀉熱湯主之. （方見蘭室秘臟）麻木, 厥氣上衝, 逆氣上行, 妄聞妄見者, 宜神功丸主之.

### Literal Translation

Or for stabbing pain in the abdomen, or internal urgency, one should use much Gancao (Licorice); or when one strains to sit and cannot pass stool, it all belongs to blood deficiency; when blood is deficient there is internal urgency, and one should use Danggui (Angelica). For counterflow qi and internal urgency, with the diaphragm and throat not passing and the stool not moving, one should use Shengyang Xiehuo Tang (Raise Yang and Drain Fire Decoction) as the main treatment. (The formula is seen in Lanshi Micang.) For numbness, reversal qi rushing upward, counterflow qi ascending, and hearing and seeing things that are not there, one should use Shengong Wan (Spirit Achievement Pill) as the main treatment.
