## O105:p012:t0002

### Source Text

（謂天突穴也, 陰維、任脈之會, 刺一寸, 灸三壯） 脈經曰:寸口脈來緊細實, 長至關者, 任脈也. 動苦少腹繞臍, 下引橫骨、陰中切痛, 取關元治之. 又曰:橫寸口邊, 脈u丸丸u者, 任脈也.

### Literal Translation

(This refers to the Tiantu point, the meeting of Yinwei and the Ren vessel; needle one cun, moxa three zhuang.) The Maijing (Pulse Classic) says: When the cunkou pulse comes tight, fine, and solid, long reaching to the guan, it is the Ren vessel. When it moves, one suffers lower abdominal pain encircling the navel, drawing down to the pubic bone, with cutting pain in the genitals; take Guanyuan (CV4) to treat it. It also says: When, at the side of the cun opening, the pulse is u丸丸u (round and rolling), it is the Ren vessel.
