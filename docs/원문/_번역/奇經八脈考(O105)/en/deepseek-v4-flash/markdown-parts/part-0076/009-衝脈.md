### Source Text

其上者出於u頏uu顙, u滲諸陽, 灌諸精. 其下者注於少陰之大絡, 起於腎下, 出於氣街, 循陰股內廉, 斜入膕中, 伏行骭骨內廉, 並少陰之經, 下入內踝之後, 入足下;其別者並於少陰, 滲三陰, 斜入踝, 伏行出屬u跗, u屬下, 循跗上, 入大指之間, 滲諸絡而溫足脛肌肉. 故其脈常動, 別絡結則跗上不動, 不動則厥, 厥則寒矣.

Its upper [branch] emerges at the u頏uu顙u (nasopharynx), seeps into all the yang, and irrigates all the essence. Its lower [branch] pours into the great collateral of Shaoyin, arises below the kidney, emerges at Qijie, runs along the inner border of the yin thigh, obliquely enters the popliteal fossa, runs hidden along the inner border of the shin bone, and together with the Shaoyin channel descends behind the inner ankle into the sole of the foot; its branch runs together with Shaoyin, seeps into the three yin, obliquely enters the ankle, runs hidden and emerges at the u跗u (instep) attachment, below the attachment, along the instep, enters between the great toes, seeps into all the collaterals and warms the muscles of the foot and leg. Therefore its vessel is constantly moving; when the branch collateral binds, the instep does not move, and when it does not move there is reversal, and when there is reversal it becomes cold.
