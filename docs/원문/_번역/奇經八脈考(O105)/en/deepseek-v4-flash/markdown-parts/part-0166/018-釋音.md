## O105:p018:t0011

### Source Text

髎　音寥, 骨空處也. 窌　與髎同, 說文音疱, 窖也. 癲　音顛, 僕病也.

### Literal Translation

髎 (liao): read liao, the hollow places of the bones. 窌 (jiao): the same as 髎; the Shuowen reads it pao, a cellar. 癲 (dian): read dian, the disease of falling (mania).
