## O105:p008:t0011

### Source Text

靈樞經曰　uuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuu目中赤痛, 從內眥始, 取之陰蹺. （交信穴也） 又曰:風u痙u反折, 先取足太陽及膕中及血絡出血, 若中有寒邪, 取陰蹺及三毛上及血絡出血. 李瀕湖曰:足太陽, 京骨穴也.

### Literal Translation

The Lingshu jing says　uuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuu, 'when there is redness and pain in the middle of the eye beginning from the inner canthus, take the Yin Qiao.' (That is the Jiaoxin point.) It also says: With wind-cramp (fengjing) and the body folding backward, first take Foot Taiyang and the popliteal fossa (weizhong) and the blood-luo to let blood; if there is a cold pathogenic [influence] within, take the Yin Qiao and the three hairs above and the blood-luo to let blood. Li Binhu said: Foot Taiyang is the Jinggu point.
