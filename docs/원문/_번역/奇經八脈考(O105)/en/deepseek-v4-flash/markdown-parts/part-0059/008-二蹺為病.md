## O105:p008:t0015

### Source Text

陽氣盛則瞋目, 陰氣盛則u瞑u目, 熱厥取足太陽、少陽. 甲乙經曰:人病目閉不得視者, 衛氣留於陰, 不得行於陽, 留於陰則陰氣盛, 陰氣盛則陰蹺滿, 不得入於陽則陽氣虛, 故目閉也. 病目不得瞑者, 衛氣不得入於陰, 常留於陽, 留於陽則陽氣滿, 陽氣滿則陽蹺盛, 不得入於陰則陰氣虛, 故目不瞑也.

### Literal Translation

When yang-qi is exuberant, [the eyes] open wide (chen mu); when yin-qi is exuberant, [the eyes] close (ming mu); in heat reversal take Foot Taiyang and Shaoyang. The Jia Yi jing says: When a person is ill with the eyes closed and unable to see, the wei-qi remains in the yin and cannot move into the yang; remaining in the yin then the yin-qi is exuberant, with the yin-qi exuberant the Yin Qiao is full; being unable to enter the yang then the yang-qi is deficient, and therefore the eyes close. When a person is ill with the eyes unable to close, the wei-qi cannot enter the yin and constantly remains in the yang; remaining in the yang then the yang-qi is full, with the yang-qi full the Yang Qiao is exuberant; being unable to enter the yin then the yin-qi is deficient, and therefore the eyes do not close.
