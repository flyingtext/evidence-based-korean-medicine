## O105:p005:t0002

### Source Text

又曰:陽維為病苦寒熱, 陰維為病苦心痛. （溶溶, 緩慢貌） 張潔古曰:衛為陽, 主表, 陽維受邪為病在表, 故苦寒熱;營為陰, 主裡, 陰維受邪為病在裡, 故苦心痛. 陰陽相維, 則營衛和諧矣;營衛不諧, 則悵然失志, 不能自收持矣.

### Literal Translation

He also said: The disease of the Yang Wei is suffering from chills and fever, and the disease of the Yin Wei is suffering from heart pain. (Rongrong means the appearance of slowness.) Zhang Jiegu said: wei is yang and governs the exterior; when the Yang Wei receives pathogenic [influence], the disease is in the exterior, and therefore there is suffering from chills and fever. ying is yin and governs the interior; when the Yin Wei receives pathogenic [influence], the disease is in the interior, and therefore there is suffering from heart pain. When yin and yang bind each other, then ying and wei are harmonious; but when ying and wei are not harmonious, then [the person] is disconsolate and loses his resolve, unable to gather and control himself.
