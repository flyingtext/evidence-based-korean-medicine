## O105:p008:t0007

### Source Text

在蹺上郄下五寸橫居, 視其盛者, 出血. 王啟玄雲:足太陽之脈, 循腰下會於後陰, 故曰會陰. 直陽之脈, 挾脊下行, 貫u臀u至膕, 循腨, 過外踝之後, 條直而行者, 故曰直陽之脈也.

### Literal Translation

[It is] three cun, lying transversely above the Qiao and below the xi (cleft); look at where it is full and let blood. Wang Qixuan said: The vessel of Foot Taiyang follows the lumbar region, descends, and meets at the posterior yin, and so it is called Huiyin. The straight-yang vessel, flanking the spine and descending, pierces the buttocks to reach the popliteal fossa, follows the calf, crosses behind the outer ankle, and runs straight — and so it is called the straight-yang vessel.
