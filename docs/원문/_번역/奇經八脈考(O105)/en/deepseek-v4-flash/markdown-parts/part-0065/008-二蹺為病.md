## O105:p008:t0021

### Source Text

巢元方云:脾病困倦而嗜臥, 膽病多煩而不眠. 王叔和脈經云:水流夜疾有聲者, 土休故也, 人亦應之. 人夜臥, 則脾不動搖, 脈為之數疾也.

### Literal Translation

Chao Yuanfang said: Spleen disease causes fatigue and drowsiness with excessive sleeping, and gallbladder disease causes much vexation and sleeplessness. Wang Shuhe's Maijing (Pulse Classic) says: When water flows rapidly at night with sound, it is because the earth rests; humans also respond to this. When a person lies down at night, the spleen does not move or stir, and the pulse becomes rapid because of it.
