## O105:p010:t0007

### Source Text

程篁墩曰:太平侯病膻中痛, 喘嘔吞酸, 臍上一點氣, 上至咽喉如冰, 每子後申時輒發, 醫以為大寒, 不效. 祝橘泉曰:此得之大醉及厚味過多, 子後申時相火自下騰上, 故作痛也. 以二陳加芩、連、梔子、蒼朮, 數飲而愈.

### Literal Translation

Cheng Huangdun said: The Marquis of Taiping suffered pain in the danzhong (chest center), with panting, vomiting, and acid swallowing; a point of qi above the navel rose to the throat like ice, and it always broke out after zi hour and at shen hour. The physicians considered it great cold, but it was ineffective. Zhu Juchuan said: This was contracted from great drunkenness and an excess of rich food; after zi hour and at shen hour the ministerial fire rises from below, therefore it produces pain. Using Erchen [Tang] (Two Aged [Decoction]) with the addition of Huangqin (Scutellaria), Huanglian (Coptis), Zhizi (Gardenia), and Cangzhu (Atractylodes), after several drinks it was cured.
