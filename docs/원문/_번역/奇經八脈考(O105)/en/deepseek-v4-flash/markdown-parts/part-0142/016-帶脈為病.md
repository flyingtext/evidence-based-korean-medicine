### Source Text

以帶脈為病得名, 亦以病形而名, 白者屬氣, 赤者屬血, 多因醉飽房勞, 服食燥熱所致. 亦有濕痰流注下焦者, 腎肝陰淫濕勝者;或驚恐而木乘土位, 濁液下流;或思慕無窮, 發為筋痿, 所謂二陽之病發心脾也;或余經濕熱, 屈滯於少腹之下, 或下元虛冷, 子宮濕淫. 治之之法, 或下或吐, 或發中兼補, 補中兼利, 燥中兼升發, 潤中兼溫養, 或溫樸, 或收澀, 諸例不同, 亦病機之活法也.

### Literal Translation

It is named from the Belt Vessel being diseased, and also named from the form of the disease; white belongs to qi, red belongs to blood. It is mostly caused by drunkenness, overeating, and sexual overexertion, and by taking dry and hot foods. There are also cases of damp phlegm flowing and lodging in the lower burner, and of kidney and liver yin with damp excess; or from fright and fear causing wood to encroach on the earth position, with turbid fluid flowing downward; or from boundless longing, developing into sinew flaccidity, what is called disease of the two yangs arising in the heart and spleen; or from damp-heat of the remaining channels, bent and stagnating below the lower abdomen; or from vacuity and cold of the lower origin, with damp excess of the uterus. The methods of treating it are either to purge downward or to vomit, or to supplement within the midst of dispersing, to promote urination within the midst of supplementing, to raise and disperse within the midst of drying, to warm and nourish within the midst of moistening, or to warm and strengthen, or to astringe; the various examples differ, and this is also the living method of disease mechanism.
