## O105:p010:t0013

### Source Text

靈樞經曰:胸氣有街、腹氣有街、頭氣有街、脛氣有街. 故氣在頭者止之於腦;氣在胸者止之膺與背腧, 氣在腹者止之背腧與衝脈於臍之左右之動脈;氣在脛者. 止之於氣街與承山踝上以下.

### Literal Translation

The Lingshu Jing says: The chest qi has a street, the abdominal qi has a street, the head qi has a street, and the shin qi has a street. Therefore qi in the head stops at the brain; qi in the chest stops at the chest and the back shu points; qi in the abdomen stops at the back shu points and at the arteries of the Chong vessel on the left and right of the navel; qi in the shin stops at Qijie and at Chengshan (BL57) and below the ankle.
