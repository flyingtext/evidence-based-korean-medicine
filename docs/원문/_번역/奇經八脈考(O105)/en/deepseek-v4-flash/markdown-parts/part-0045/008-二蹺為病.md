## O105:p008:t0001

### Source Text

秦越人難經曰:陰絡者, 陰蹺之絡;陰絡者, 陽蹺之絡. 陰蹺為病, 陽緩而陰急;陽蹺為病, 陰緩而陽急. 王叔和脈經曰:陰蹺脈急, 當從內踝以上急, 外踝以上緩;陽蹺脈急, 當從外踝以上急, 內踝以上緩.

### Literal Translation

The Nanjing of Qin Yueren says: The yin-luo is the luo of the Yin Qiao, and the yang-luo is the luo of the Yang Qiao. In the disease of the Yin Qiao, the yang is relaxed and the yin is tense; in the disease of the Yang Qiao, the yin is relaxed and the yang is tense. The Maijing of Wang Shuhe says: When the Yin Qiao vessel is tense, the tension should be from above the inner ankle, with [the region] above the outer ankle relaxed; when the Yang Qiao vessel is tense, the tension should be from above the outer ankle, with [the region] above the inner ankle relaxed.
