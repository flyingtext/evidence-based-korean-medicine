## O105:p005:t0016

### Source Text

肉裡之脈, 令人腰痛, 不可以咳. 咳則筋縮急. 刺肉裡之脈為二痏, 在太陽之外、少陽絕骨之後.

### Literal Translation

The vessel of the flesh-interior (rouli zhi mai) makes a person suffer lumbar pain such that one cannot cough. When one coughs, the sinews contract and become tense. To treat it, puncture the vessel of the flesh-interior with two points (er wei), to the outside of Taiyang and behind the juegu (severed bone) of Shaoyang.
