## O105:p010:t0011

### Source Text

經曰:下氣不足, 則痿厥心悗. 宜以清燥去濕熱之藥, 或生脈散合四苓散. 加酒洗黃蘖、知母, 以泄其濕熱.

### Literal Translation

The Classic says: When the lower qi is insufficient, there is atrophy, reversal, and vexation of the heart. One should use medicines that clear dryness and drain damp-heat, or Shengmai San (Generate the Pulse Powder) combined with Siling San (Four Poria Powder), with the addition of wine-washed Huangbai (Phellodendron) and Zhimu (Anemarrhena), to drain its damp-heat.
