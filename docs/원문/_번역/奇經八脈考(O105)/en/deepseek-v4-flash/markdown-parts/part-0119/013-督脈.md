## O105:p013:t0010

### Source Text

黃庭經言:皆在心內運天經, 晝夜存之自長生. 天經乃吾身之黃道, 呼吸往來於此也. 鹿運尾閭, 能通督脈;龜納鼻息, 能通任脈, 故二物皆長壽.

### Literal Translation

The Huangting Jing says: All operate the heavenly channel within the heart, and preserving it day and night one naturally lives long. The heavenly channel is the yellow path of one's own body, where the breath comes and goes. The deer moves the weilii (tailbone) and can open the Du vessel; the tortoise draws in nasal breath and can open the Ren vessel, therefore both creatures are long-lived.
