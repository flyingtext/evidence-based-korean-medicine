## O105:p014:t0002

### Source Text

李瀕湖曰:督脈雖行於背, 而別絡自長強走任脈者, 則由少腹直上貫臍, 中貫心, 入喉, 上頤, 環唇, 而入於目之內眥. 故顯此諸證, 啟玄蓋未深考爾. 素問曰:督脈實則脊強反折, 虛則頭重高搖之, 挾骨之有過者, 取之所別也.

### Literal Translation

Li Binhu said: Although the Governing Vessel travels along the back, its branch collateral, going from Changqiang to the Ren vessel, runs straight up from the lower abdomen through the navel, passes through the heart in the middle, enters the throat, ascends to the chin, encircles the lips, and enters the inner canthus of the eye. Therefore these various signs appear; Qixuan had evidently not examined this deeply. The Plain Questions says: When the Governing Vessel is in excess, the spine becomes stiff and arches backward; when it is deficient, the head becomes heavy and sways high; for those with affliction along the bone, take [treatment] from its branch collateral.
