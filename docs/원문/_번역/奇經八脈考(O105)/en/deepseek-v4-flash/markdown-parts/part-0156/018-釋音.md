### Source Text

蹺　腳、卻、喬、蹺四音, 舉足高也. 又蹺捷也. 跗　音膚, 足背也.

### Literal Translation

蹺 (qiao): has the four readings jiao, que, qiao, and qiao; it means raising the foot high. Also, 蹺 means nimble and swift. 跗 (fu): read fu, the dorsum of the foot.
