## O105:p005:t0005

### Source Text

李瀕湖曰:陽維之脈, 與手足三陽相維, 而足太陽, 少陽, 則始終相聯附者. 寒熱之證, 惟二經有之, 故陽維為病亦苦寒熱. 蓋衛氣晝行於陽, 夜行於陰, 陰虛則內熱, 陽虛則外寒, 邪氣在經, 內與陰爭而惡寒, 外與陽爭而發熱.

### Literal Translation

Li Binhu said: The Yang Wei vessel binds with the hand-foot three yang, and among these Foot Taiyang and Foot Shaoyang are those to which it is adhered and joined from beginning to end. The pattern of chills and fever belongs only to these two meridians, and therefore the disease of the Yang Wei likewise suffers from chills and fever. This is because the wei-qi by day travels in the yang and by night travels in the yin; when yin is deficient there is internal heat, and when yang is deficient there is external cold. When pathogenic qi is in the meridians, internally it contends with yin and gives aversion to cold, and externally it contends with yang and gives fever.
