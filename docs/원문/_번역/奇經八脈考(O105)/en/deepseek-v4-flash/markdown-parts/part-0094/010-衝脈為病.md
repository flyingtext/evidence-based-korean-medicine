## O105:p010:t0014

### Source Text

取此者, 用毫針, 先按在上, 久應手乃刺而與之. 所治者, 頭痛眩僕, 腹痛中滿暴脹, 及有新積作痛. 素問舉痛論曰:寒氣客於衝脈, 衝脈起於關元, 隨腹直上.

### Literal Translation

In taking these, use the filiform needle; first press above, and only after it responds to the hand for a long time, then needle and give [the treatment]. What it treats: headache, dizziness, and falling, abdominal pain, fullness, sudden distension, and pain from newly accumulated [masses]. The Suwen Jutong Lun (Discussion of Pain) says: When cold qi lodges in the Chong vessel, the Chong vessel arises at Guanyuan (CV4) and ascends straight along the abdomen.
