## O105:p005:t0018

### Source Text

啟玄曰:此陰維之脈也, 去內踝上五寸腨分中, 並少陰經而上也, 刺飛陽之脈, 在內踝上一寸, 少陰之前, 與陰維之會, 築賓穴也. 甲乙經云, 太陽之絡, 別走少陰者, 名曰飛陽.

### Literal Translation

Qixuan said: This is the Yin Wei vessel; [it runs] five cun above the inner ankle in the division of the calf, ascending in parallel with the Shaoyin meridian. To puncture the Feiyang vessel [one needles] one cun above the inner ankle, in front of Shaoyin, at its confluence with the Yin Wei — this is the Zhubin point. The Jia Yi jing says: The collateral (luo) of Taiyang that separately runs to Shaoyin is named Feiyang.
