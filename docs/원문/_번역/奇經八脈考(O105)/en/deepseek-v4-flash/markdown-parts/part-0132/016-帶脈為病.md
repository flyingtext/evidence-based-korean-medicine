## O105:p016:t0001

### Source Text

秦越人曰:帶之為病, 腹滿, 腰溶溶如坐水中. （溶溶緩慢貌） 明堂曰:帶脈二穴, 主腰腹縱溶溶如囊水之狀. 婦人少腹痛, 裡急後重, 瘛瘲月事不調, 赤白帶下, 可針六分, 灸七壯.

### Literal Translation

Qin Yueren said: When the Belt Vessel is diseased, the abdomen is full, and the lower back is loose and relaxed as if sitting in water (rongrong means a slow, relaxed appearance). The Mingtang says: The two Belt Vessel points govern a lower back and abdomen that are loose and relaxed like a bag of water. In women, lower abdominal pain, internal urgency and tenesmus, convulsions, irregular menstruation, and red and white vaginal discharge; one may needle six fen and moxa seven zhuang.
