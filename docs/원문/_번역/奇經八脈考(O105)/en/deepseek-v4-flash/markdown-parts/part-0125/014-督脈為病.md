## O105:p014:t0003

### Source Text

秦越人難經曰:督脈為病, 脊強而厥. 王海臟曰:此病宜用羌活、獨活、防風、荊芥、細辛、藁本、黃連、大黃、附子、烏頭、蒼耳之類. 張仲景金匱云:脊強者, 五痓之總名.

### Literal Translation

Qin Yueren's Classic of Difficulties says: When the Governing Vessel is diseased, the spine is stiff and there is reversal (jue). Wang Haizang said: For this disease one should use qianghuo (Notopterygium), duhuo (Angelica pubescens), fangfeng (Saposhnikovia), jingjie (Schizonepeta), xixin (Asarum), gaoben (Ligusticum), huanglian (Coptis), dahuang (Rhubarb), fuzi (Aconite), wutou (Aconite root), canger (Xanthium) and the like. Zhang Zhongjing's Essentials of the Golden Cabinet says: Stiffness of the spine is the general name for the five types of convulsion (chi).
