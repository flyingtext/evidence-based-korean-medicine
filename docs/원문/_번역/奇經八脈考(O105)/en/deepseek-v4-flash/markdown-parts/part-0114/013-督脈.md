## O105:p013:t0005

### Source Text

入絡於腦, 又別自腦下項, 循肩胛, 與手足太陽、少陽會於大杼第一椎下兩旁, 去脊中一寸五分陷中, 內挾脊抵腰中, 入循u膂u絡腎. 難經曰:督脈、任脈四尺五寸, 合共九尺. 靈樞經曰:頸中央之脈, 督脈也, 名曰風府.

### Literal Translation

It enters to connect the brain, and again separately descends from the brain along the neck, runs along the scapula, and meets the Hand and Foot Taiyang and Shaoyang at Dazhu (BL11), below the first vertebra on both sides, one and a half cun from the midline of the spine in the hollow; internally it flanks the spine and reaches the waist, enters and runs along the u膂u (back muscles) to connect the kidney. The Nanjing says: The Du and Ren vessels are four chi five cun each, together nine chi in all. The Lingshu Jing says: The vessel in the center of the neck is the Du vessel, named Fengfu (GV16).
