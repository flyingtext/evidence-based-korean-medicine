## O105:p009:t0005

### Source Text

名曰太衝. 王啟玄曰:腎脈與衝脈並下行循足, 合而盛大, 故曰太衝. 一云衝脈起於氣衝, 沖直而通, 故謂之沖.

### Literal Translation

It is named Taichong. Wang Qixuan said: The kidney vessel and the Chong vessel run downward together along the foot, combine and become great and abundant, therefore it is called Taichong. One text says: The Chong vessel arises at Qichong, and since it goes straight and passes through, it is called Chong.
