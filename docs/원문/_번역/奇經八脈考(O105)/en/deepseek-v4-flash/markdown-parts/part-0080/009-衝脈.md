## O105:p009:t0013

### Source Text

腎間動氣者, 真元一氣, 分為三路, 人之生命也, 十二經之根本也. 李瀕湖曰:三焦即命門之用. 與沖、任、督相通者, 故附著於此.

### Literal Translation

The moving qi between the kidneys is the single primordial qi, divided into three paths; it is the life of a person and the root of the twelve channels. Li Binhu said: The Sanjiao is the function of Mingmen. Because it communicates with the Chong, Ren, and Du, it is appended here.
