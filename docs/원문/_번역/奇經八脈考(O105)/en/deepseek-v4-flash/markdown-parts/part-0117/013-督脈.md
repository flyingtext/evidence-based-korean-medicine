## O105:p013:t0008

### Source Text

故魏伯陽參同契云:上閉則稱有, 下閉則稱無, 無者以奉上, 上有神德居, 此兩孔穴法, 金氣亦相須. 崔希範天元入藥鏡云:上鵲橋, 下鵲橋, 天應星, 地應潮;歸根竅, 覆命關, 貫尾閭, 通泥丸. 大道三章直指云:修丹之士, 身中一竅, 名曰玄牝.

### Literal Translation

Therefore Wei Boyang's Cantong Qi says: When the upper is closed it is called having; when the lower is closed it is called not having; the not-having is used to serve the upper, and above dwells spiritual virtue; in the method of these two orifice points, the metal qi also mutually requires [them]. Cui Xifan's Tianyuan Ruyao Jing says: The upper magpie bridge, the lower magpie bridge; heaven responds with stars, earth responds with tides; return to the root orifice, cover the life gate, pierce the weilii (tailbone), and open the niwan (brain). The Dadao Sanzhang Zhizhi says: In the practitioner of elixir cultivation, there is one orifice in the body called the xuanpin (mysterious female).
