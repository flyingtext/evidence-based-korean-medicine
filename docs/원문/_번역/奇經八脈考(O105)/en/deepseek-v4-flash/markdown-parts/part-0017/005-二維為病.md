## O105:p005:t0003

### Source Text

何以知之?仲景云:病常自汗, 是衛氣不與營氣和也, 宜桂枝湯和之. 又云:服桂枝反煩不解, 先刺風池、風府, 卻與桂枝湯. 此二穴, 乃陽維之會也, 謂桂枝後, 尚自汗發熱惡寒, 其脈寸浮尺弱而反煩, 為病在陽維, 故先針此二穴.

### Literal Translation

How is this known? Zhongjing said: When a disease constantly has spontaneous sweating, it is because the wei-qi is not harmonizing with the ying-qi, and it is appropriate to harmonize it with Guizhi Tang (Cinnamon Twig Decoction). He also said: If after taking Guizhi there is instead vexation and [the disease] is not resolved, first puncture Fengchi and Fengfu, then [again] give Guizhi Tang. These two points are the confluence of the Yang Wei; this means that after [taking] Guizhi, if there is still spontaneous sweating, fever, and aversion to cold, and the pulse is floating at the cum [position] and weak at the chi [position] but is instead vexed, [this shows] the disease is in the Yang Wei, and so one first needles these two points.
