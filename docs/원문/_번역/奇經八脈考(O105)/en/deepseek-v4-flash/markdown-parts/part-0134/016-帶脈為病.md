### Source Text

若不已, 灸章門穴. 王叔和曰:帶脈為病, 左右繞臍, 腰脊痛, 沖陰股也. 王海臟曰:小兒㿗疝, 可灸章門三壯而愈, 以其與帶脈行於厥陰之分, 而太陰主之.

### Literal Translation

If it does not stop, moxa the Zhangmen point. Wang Shuhe said: When the Belt Vessel is diseased, [pain] encircles the navel on left and right, with pain of the lower back and spine, rushing to the yin and thigh. Wang Haizang said: For infantile hernia (tui), one may moxa Zhangmen three zhuang and it will be cured, because it travels with the Belt Vessel in the region of the Absolute Yin, and the Greater Yin governs it.
