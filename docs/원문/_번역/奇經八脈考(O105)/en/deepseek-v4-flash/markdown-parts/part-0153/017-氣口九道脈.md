## O105:p017:t0010

### Source Text

三部俱牢, 直上直下者, 衝脈也, 苦胸中有寒疝. 脈經曰:脈來中央堅實, 徑至關者, 衝脈也, 動苦少腹痛, 上搶心, 有瘕疝遺溺, 女子絕孕. 前部左右彈者, 陽蹺也, 動苦腰背痛, 癲癇僵仆羊鳴, 偏枯、𤸷痹, 身體強.

### Literal Translation

When all three regions are firm (lao) and run straight up and down, it is the Chong vessel; it causes cold hernia in the chest. The Pulse Classic says: When the pulse comes with the center solid and firm, reaching straight to the guan, it is the Chong vessel; when it moves it causes lower abdominal pain rushing upward to the heart, with abdominal masses, hernia, and enuresis, and in women loss of pregnancy. When the front region bounces left and right, it is the Yang Heel (qiao) vessel; when it moves it causes lower back and spine pain, mania-epilepsy with falling and sheep-like bleating, hemiplegia, bi with numbness, and stiffness of the body.
