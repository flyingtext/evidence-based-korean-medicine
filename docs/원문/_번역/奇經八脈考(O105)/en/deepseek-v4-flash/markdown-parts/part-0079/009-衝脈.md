## O105:p009:t0012

### Source Text

一云:其腑在氣街中. 上焦在胃上口, 治在膻中;中焦在胃管, 治在臍旁;下焦在臍下膀胱上口, 治在臍. 經曰:原氣者, 三焦之別使也.

### Literal Translation

One text says: Its bowel is in the Qijie. The upper jiao is at the upper opening of the stomach, governed at Danzhong (CV17); the middle jiao is at the stomach duct, governed beside the navel; the lower jiao is below the navel at the upper opening of the bladder, governed at the navel. The Classic says: The primordial qi is the separate envoy of the Sanjiao.
