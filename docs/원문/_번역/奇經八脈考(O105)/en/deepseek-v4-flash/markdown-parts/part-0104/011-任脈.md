## O105:p011:t0003

### Source Text

（唇下陷中）環唇上, 至下齗交, 復出分行, 循面, 系兩目下之中央, 至承泣而終. （目下七分, 直瞳子陷中, 二穴）凡二十七穴. 難經、甲乙經, 並無循面以下之說.

### Literal Translation

(In the hollow below the lip.) It encircles the lip, ascends to the lower gum junction, then again emerges and divides, runs along the face, connects to the center below both eyes, and ends at Chengqi (ST1). (Seven fen below the eye, directly below the pupil in the hollow, two points.) Twenty-seven points in all. The Nanjing and the Jiayi Jing both do not have the statement of running along the face and below.
