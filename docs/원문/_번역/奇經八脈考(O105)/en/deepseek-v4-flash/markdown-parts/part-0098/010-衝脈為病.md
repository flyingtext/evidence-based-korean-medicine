### Source Text

（竹葉湯）動氣在左不可發汗, 汗之則頭眩汗不止, 筋u惕u肉瞤, 此為難治;（或先用防風白朮牡蠣湯, 次用小建中湯）不可下, 下之則腹裡拘急不止, 動氣反劇, 身雖有熱反欲拳. （先服甘草乾薑湯, 次服小建中湯）動氣在上不可發汗, 汗之則氣上衝, 正在心端, （李根湯）不可下, 下之則掌握熱煩, 身熱汗泄, 欲水自灌. （竹葉湯）動氣在下不可發汗, 汗之則無汗, 心中大煩, 骨節疼、頭痛目運, 惡寒吐谷;（先服大陳皮湯, 次服小建中湯）不可下, 下之則腹滿, 卒起頭眩, 食則下清穀, 心下痞堅.

### Literal Translation

(Zhuye Tang.) When the moving qi is on the left, one must not cause sweating; if one sweats it, there will be dizziness, sweating that does not stop, and twitching of the sinews and quivering of the flesh; this is difficult to treat; (or first use Fangfeng Baizhu Muli Tang, then Xiao Jianzhong Tang) one must not purge; if one purges it, the abdomen will be cramped and not stop, the moving qi will instead become severe, and although the body has heat, one instead wants to curl up. (First take Gancao Ganjiang Tang, then take Xiao Jianzhong Tang.) When the moving qi is above, one must not cause sweating; if one sweats it, qi will rush upward, right at the heart end, (Ligen Tang) one must not purge; if one purges it, there will be heat and vexation in the palms, body heat, sweating, and a desire to pour water over oneself. (Zhuye Tang.) When the moving qi is below, one must not cause sweating; if one sweats it, there will be no sweat, great vexation of the heart, pain in the bones and joints, headache, dizziness of the eyes, aversion to cold, and vomiting of food; (first take Da Chenpi Tang, then take Xiao Jianzhong Tang) one must not purge; if one purges it, there will be abdominal fullness, sudden dizziness upon rising, passing clear grain in the stool when eating, and a hard mass below the heart.
