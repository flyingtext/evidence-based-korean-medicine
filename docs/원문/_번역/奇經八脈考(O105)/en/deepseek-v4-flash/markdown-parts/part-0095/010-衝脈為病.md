## O105:p010:t0015

### Source Text

寒氣客則脈不通, 脈不通則氣因之, 故喘動應手. 王叔和脈經曰:兩手脈浮之俱有陽, 沈之俱有陰, 陰陽皆盛, 此沖、督之脈也. 沖、督之脈, 為十二經之道路也.

### Literal Translation

When cold qi lodges, the vessel does not pass; when the vessel does not pass, qi follows it, therefore there is panting and movement responding to the hand. Wang Shuhe's Maijing (Pulse Classic) says: When both hands' pulses, floated, both have yang, and sunk, both have yin, and yin and yang are both abundant, this is the pulse of the Chong and Du vessels. The Chong and Du vessels are the pathways of the twelve channels.
