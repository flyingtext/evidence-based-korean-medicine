## O105:p017:t0011

### Source Text

中部左右彈者, 帶脈也, 動苦少腹痛引命門, 女子月事不來, 絕繼復下, 令人無子, 男子少腹拘急, 或失精也. 後部左右彈者, 陰蹺也, 動苦癲癇寒熱, 皮膚強痹, 少腹痛, 裡急, 腰胯相連痛, 男子陰疝, 女子漏下不止. 從少陰斜至太陽者, 陽維也, 動苦顛仆羊鳴, 手足相引, 甚者失音不能言, 肌肉痹癢.

### Literal Translation

When the middle region bounces left and right, it is the Belt Vessel; when it moves it causes lower abdominal pain drawing to the life gate, in women absence of menstruation, and after the line of succession is cut off it descends again, making one childless; in men, cramping of the lower abdomen, or seminal loss. When the rear region bounces left and right, it is the Yin Heel vessel; when it moves it causes mania-epilepsy with chills and fever, bi of the skin with stiffness, lower abdominal pain, internal urgency, pain connecting the lower back and hip, in men yin hernia, in women incessant leaking. When [the pulse] goes obliquely from the Lesser Yin to the Greater Yang, it is the Yang Linking (wei) vessel; when it moves it causes falling with sheep-like bleating, hands and feet drawing each other, and in severe cases loss of voice with inability to speak, and bi with itching of the flesh.
