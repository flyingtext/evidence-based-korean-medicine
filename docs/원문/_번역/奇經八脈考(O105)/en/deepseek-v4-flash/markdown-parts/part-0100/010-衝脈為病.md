## O105:p010:t0020

### Source Text

胃者水穀之海, 其輸上在氣街, 下至三里;衝脈為十二經之海, 其輸上在於大杼, 下出於巨虛之上下廉;膻中者為氣之海, 其輸上在於柱骨之上下, 前在人迎. 腦為髓之海, 其輸上在於蓋, 下在風府. 氣海有餘, 氣滿胸中悗息面赤;氣海不足, 則氣少不足以言.

### Literal Translation

The stomach is the sea of water and grain; its shu points are above at Qijie and below reaching to Zusanli (ST36); the Chong vessel is the sea of the twelve channels; its shu points are above at Dazhu (BL11) and below emerging at the upper and lower borders of Juxu (ST37/ST39); the danzhong is the sea of qi; its shu points are above at the upper and lower [regions] of the pillar bone, and in front at Renying (ST9). The brain is the sea of marrow; its shu points are above at the crown and below at Fengfu (GV16). When the sea of qi is in excess, qi fills the chest with vexation and sighing, and the face is red; when the sea of qi is insufficient, qi is scanty and insufficient for speech.
