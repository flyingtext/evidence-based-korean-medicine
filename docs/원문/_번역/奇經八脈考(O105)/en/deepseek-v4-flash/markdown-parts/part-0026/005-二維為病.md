## O105:p005:t0012

### Source Text

邪在陰維、陰蹺, 則發癲邪;在陽維、陽蹺, 則發癎. 癎動而屬陽, 陽脈主之. 癲靜而屬陰, 陰脈主之.

### Literal Translation

When pathogenic [qi] is in the Yin Wei and Yin Qiao, it then produces the mania (dian) evil; when it is in the Yang Wei and Yang Qiao, it produces epilepsy (xian). Epilepsy moves and belongs to yang, so the yang vessels govern it. Mania is still and belongs to yin, so the yin vessels govern it.
