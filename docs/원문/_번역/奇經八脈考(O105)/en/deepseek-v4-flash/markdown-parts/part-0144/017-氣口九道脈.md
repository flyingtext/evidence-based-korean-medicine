## O105:p017:t0001

### Source Text

手檢圖曰:肺為五臟華蓋:上以應天, 解理萬物, 主行精氣;法五行, 應四時, 知五味;氣口之中, 陰陽交會, 中有五部;前後左右, 各有所主, 上下中央, 分為九道. 診之則知病邪所在也. 李瀕湖曰:氣口一脈, 分為九道, 總統十二經並奇經八脈.

### Literal Translation

The Hand Examination Chart says: The lung is the canopy of the five viscera: above it responds to heaven, it regulates and orders the myriad things, and governs the movement of essence-qi; it models the five phases, responds to the four seasons, and knows the five flavors; within the qi opening (qikou), yin and yang meet and join, and within it there are five regions; front and back, left and right, each has what it governs, and above, below, and center are divided into nine paths. Examining it, one knows where the pathogenic disease is. Li Binhu said: The single vessel of the qi opening is divided into nine paths, governing in general the twelve channels and the eight extraordinary vessels.
