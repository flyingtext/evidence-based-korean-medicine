## O105:p001:t0001

### Source Text

凡人一身有經脈、絡脈, 直行曰經, 旁支曰絡. 經凡十二:手之三陰、三陽, 足之三陰、三陽是也. 絡凡十五:乃十二經各有一別絡, 而脾又有一大絡, 並任、督二絡為十五也.

### Literal Translation

Everyone's body has the primary channels (jingmai) and connecting vessels (luomai): that which runs straight is called the primary channel, and that which branches off sideways is called the connecting vessel. The primary channels number twelve in all: these are the three yin and three yang of the hand and the three yin and three yang of the foot. The connecting vessels number fifteen in all: for each of the twelve primary channels there is one bie-luo (divergent connecting vessel), and in addition the Spleen has one great luo, and together with the Ren and Du two luo they make fifteen.
