## O105:p016:t0010

### Source Text

內經云:上有病下取之, 下有病, 上取之. 又曰:上者下之, 下者上之, 是矣. 劉宗厚曰:帶下多本於陰虛陽竭, 營氣不升, 經脈凝澀, 衛氣下陷, 精氣積滯於下焦奇經之分, 蘊釀而成.

### Literal Translation

The Inner Classic says: When there is disease above, take [treatment] from below; when there is disease below, take [treatment] from above. It also says: For that above, treat below; for that below, treat above; this is so. Liu Zonghou said: Vaginal discharge is mostly rooted in yin vacuity and yang exhaustion, with the nutritive qi not ascending, the channels and vessels congealed and rough, the defensive qi sinking, and essence-qi accumulating and stagnating in the region of the extraordinary vessels of the lower burner, brewing and forming it.
