## O105:p005:t0001

### Source Text

越人曰:陽維、陰維者, 維絡於身, 溢蓄不能環流, 灌溉諸經者也. 故陽維起於諸陽之會, 陰維起於諸陰之交. 陽維維於陽, 陰維維於陰, 陰陽不能自相維, 則悵然失志, 溶溶不能自收持.

### Literal Translation

Yueren said: The Yang Wei and the Yin Wei are those that connect and bind the body, gathering [qi] with overflow and storing it up, yet unable to circulate it in a ring and irrigate the various vessels. Therefore the Yang Wei arises at the confluence of all the yang, and the Yin Wei arises at the intersection of all the yin. The Yang Wei binds the yang, and the Yin Wei binds the yin; when yin and yang are unable to bind each other, then [the person] is disconsolate and loses his resolve, drifting (rongrong) and unable to gather and control himself.
