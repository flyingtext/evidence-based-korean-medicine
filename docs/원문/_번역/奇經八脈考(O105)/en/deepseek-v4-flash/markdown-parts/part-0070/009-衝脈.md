## O105:p009:t0003

### Source Text

血氣盛則充膚熱肉, 血獨盛則淡滲皮膚, 生毫毛. 婦人有餘於氣, 不足於血, 月下數脫血, 任沖並傷, 脈不榮其口唇, 故髭鬚不生. 宦者去其宗筋, 傷其衝任, 血瀉不復, 皮膚內結, 唇口不榮, 故須亦不生.

### Literal Translation

When blood and qi are abundant, it fills the skin and warms the flesh; when blood alone is abundant, it seeps and moistens the skin and produces fine body hair. In women, qi is in excess and blood is insufficient; each month blood is repeatedly lost, and the Ren and Chong are both injured, so the vessel does not nourish the lips and mouth, and therefore beard and moustache do not grow. In eunuchs, the ancestral sinew is removed and the Chong and Ren are injured; the blood is drained and does not recover, the skin and flesh bind internally, and the lips and mouth are not nourished, therefore the beard also does not grow.
