## O105:p008:t0004

### Source Text

張潔古曰:蹺者, 捷疾也. 二脈起於足, 使人蹺捷也. 陽蹺在肌肉之上, 陽脈所行, 通貫六腑, 主持諸表, 故名為陽蹺之絡;陽蹺在肌肉之下, 陰脈所行, 通貫五臟, 主持諸里, 故名為陰蹺之絡.

### Literal Translation

Zhang Jiegu said: Qiao means nimble and quick. The two vessels rise from the foot and make a person nimble and quick. The Yang Qiao is above the muscles, [where] the yang vessels travel, penetrating through the six fu and taking charge of all the exterior, and so it is named the luo of the Yang Qiao; the Yin Qiao is below the muscles, [where] the yin vessels travel, penetrating through the five zang and taking charge of all the interior, and so it is named the luo of the Yin Qiao.
