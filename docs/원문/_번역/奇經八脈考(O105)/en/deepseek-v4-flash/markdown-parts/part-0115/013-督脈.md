## O105:p013:t0006

### Source Text

張潔古曰:督者, 都也, 為陽脈之都綱. 任者, 妊也, 為陰脈之妊養. 王海臟曰:陰蹺、陽蹺同起跟中, 乃氣並而相連, 任脈、督脈同起中極之下, 乃水溝而相接.

### Literal Translation

Zhang Jiegu said: Du means to be the capital; it is the capital and guide of the yang vessels. Ren means to conceive; it is the nourishment of the yin vessels. Wang Haizang said: Yin Qiao and Yang Qiao both arise in the heel, being qi that combines and connects; the Ren and Du vessels both arise below Zhongji, being the water groove that joins them.
