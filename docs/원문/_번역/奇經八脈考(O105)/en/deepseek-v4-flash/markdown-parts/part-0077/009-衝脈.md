## O105:p009:t0010

### Source Text

王海臟曰:手少陽三焦相火為一腑, 右腎命門為相火, 心包主亦名相火, 其脈同診. 腎為生氣之門, 出而治臍下, 分三歧, 上衝夾臍過天樞, 上至u膻u中兩乳間, 元氣所繫焉. 又足太陽之別, 並足太陽正路入絡膀胱, 約下焉.

### Literal Translation

Wang Haizang said: The Hand-Shaoyang Sanjiao ministerial fire is one bowel; the right kidney Mingmen is ministerial fire; the Pericardium is also called ministerial fire, and their pulses are examined together. The kidney is the gate of engendering qi; it emerges and governs below the navel, dividing into three branches, ascending and rushing, flanking the navel, passing through Tianshu (ST25), ascending to the u膻u (dan) center between the two breasts, where the primordial qi is attached. Also, the branch of Foot-Taiyang runs together with the proper path of Foot-Taiyang, entering to connect the bladder, and binds below.
