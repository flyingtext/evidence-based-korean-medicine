## O105:p014:t0005

### Source Text

王叔和脈經曰:尺寸俱浮, 直上直下, 此為督脈. 腰背強痛, 不得俯仰, 大人癲病, 小兒風癇. 又曰:脈來中央浮直, 上下動者, 督脈也.

### Literal Translation

Wang Shuhe's Pulse Classic says: When both the cun and chi are floating, running straight up and down, this is the Governing Vessel. There is stiffness and pain of the lower back and spine, inability to bend and stretch; in adults, mania (dian); in children, wind-epilepsy (fengxian). He also said: When the pulse comes with the center floating and straight, moving up and down, it is the Governing Vessel.
