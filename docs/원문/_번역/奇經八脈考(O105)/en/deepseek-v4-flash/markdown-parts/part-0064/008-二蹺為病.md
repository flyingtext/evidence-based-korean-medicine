## O105:p008:t0020

### Source Text

老人氣血衰、氣道澀, 衛氣內伐, 故晝不精而夜不瞑. 又云:多臥者, 腸胃大而皮膚澀, 分肉不解, 衛氣行遲故也. 張子和云:思氣所至為不眠、為嗜臥.

### Literal Translation

In the aged, qi and blood decline, the qi pathways are rough, and the defensive qi attacks internally, therefore they are not alert by day and do not sleep at night. It also says: Those who sleep excessively have large intestines and stomach and rough skin, and the intermuscular spaces are not separated, because the defensive qi moves slowly. Zhang Zihe said: When the thought-qi reaches [a state], it produces sleeplessness and excessive sleeping.
