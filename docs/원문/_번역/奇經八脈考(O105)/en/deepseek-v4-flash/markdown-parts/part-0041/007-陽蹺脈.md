### Source Text

（在髆骨頭, 肩端上, 兩骨罅陷宛宛中. 舉臂取之有空）上人迎夾口吻, 會手足陽明、任脈於地倉. （夾口吻旁四分, 外如近下有微脈動處）同足陽明上而行巨窌, （夾鼻孔旁八分, 直瞳子, 平水溝）復會任脈於承泣. （在目下七分, 直瞳子陷中）至目內眥, 與手足太陽、足陽明、陰蹺, 五脈會於睛明穴.

### Literal Translation

(At the head of the shoulder-blade bone on the shoulder rim, in the hollow of the depression between the two bones; raise the arm to find it, and there is a gap.) Ascending at Renying it flanks the corner of the mouth, joining hand-foot Yangming and the Ren meridian at Dicang (ST4) (flanking the corner of the mouth four fen, toward the outside and slightly below, where there is a place of a faintly moving vessel). Together with Foot Yangming it ascends running at Juliao (ST3) (flanking the nostril eight fen, directly below the pupil, level with Shuigou (DU26)), then once more joins the Ren meridian at Chengqi (ST1) (seven fen below the eye, directly below the pupil, in the depression). Reaching the inner canthus of the eye, it joins the five vessels — hand-foot Taiyang, Foot Yangming, and the Yin Qiao — at the Jingming point.
