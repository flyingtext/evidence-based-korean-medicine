（大橫在腹哀下三寸五分. 腹哀在日月下一寸五分. 並去腹中行四寸半）循脅肋會足厥陰於期門. （直乳下一寸半）上胸膈挾咽, 與任脈會於天突、廉泉, 上至頂前而終. （天突在結喉下四寸半宛宛中. 廉泉在結喉上二寸中央是穴）凡一十四穴.

### Literal Translation

(Daheng is three cun and five fen below Fu'ai. Fu'ai is one cun and five fen below Riyue (GB24). Both are four and a half cun from the abdominal midline.) Following the flank and the ribs, it joins Foot Jueyin at Qimen (LR14). (Directly one cun and a half below the nipple.) Above, it runs through the chest and diaphragm, flanking the throat, and joins the Ren meridian at Tiantu (CV22) and Lianquan (CV23), ascending to the front of the crown and ending there. (Tiantu is in the middle hollow four cun and a half below the Adam's apple (laryngeal prominence). Lianquan is the point in the center two cun above the Adam's apple.) In all there are fourteen points.
