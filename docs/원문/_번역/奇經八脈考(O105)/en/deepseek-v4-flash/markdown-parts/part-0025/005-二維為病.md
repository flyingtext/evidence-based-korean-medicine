## O105:p005:t0011

### Source Text

（見陽蹺） 瀕湖曰:王叔和以癲癎屬陰維陽維, 靈樞經以癲癎屬陰蹺陽蹺, 二說義異旨同. 蓋陽維由外踝而上, 循陽分而至肩肘, 歷耳額而終行於衛分諸陽之會;陰維由內踝而上, 循陰分而上脅至咽, 行於營分諸陰之交. 陽蹺起於跟中, 循外踝上行於股外, 至脅肋肩髆, 行於一身之左右, 而終於目內眥;陰蹺起於跟中, 循內踝上行於股內、陰器, 行於一身之左右, 至咽喉, 會任脈, 而終於目內眥.

### Literal Translation

[Pucan] (see under the Yang Qiao.) Binhu said: Wang Shuhe attributes mania-epilepsy to the Yin Wei and Yang Wei, while the Lingshu jing attributes mania-epilepsy to the Yin Qiao and Yang Qiao; the two explanations differ in wording but agree in purport. This is because the Yang Wei, rising from the outer ankle and following the yang division up to the shoulder and elbow, passing through the ear and forehead, finally travels among the confluence of the various yang of the wei-[qi] division; while the Yin Wei, rising from the inner ankle and following the yin division up over the flank to the throat, travels among the intersection of the various yin of the ying-[qi] division. The Yang Qiao rises in the middle of the heel, follows the outer ankle, and ascends along the outside of the thigh to the flanks, ribs, and shoulder-blade, traveling over the left and right of the whole body and finally ending at the inner canthus of the eye; the Yin Qiao rises in the middle of the heel, follows the inner ankle, and ascends through the inner thigh and the genitals, traveling over the left and right of the whole body, reaching the throat, joining the Ren meridian and finally ending at the inner canthus of the eye.
