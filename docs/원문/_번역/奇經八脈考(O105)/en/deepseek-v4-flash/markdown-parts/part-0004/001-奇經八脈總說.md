## O105:p001:t0004

### Source Text

八脈散在群書者, 略而不悉. 醫不知此, 罔探病機;仙不知此, 難安爐鼎. 時珍不敏, 參考諸說, 萃集於左, 以備學仙、醫者, 筌蹄之用云.

### Literal Translation

The eight vessels, scattered throughout the various books, are treated briefly and in no wise exhaustively. If a physician does not know these, he cannot probe into the mechanism of disease; if a Daoist [who cultivates immortality] does not know these, he cannot secure his furnace and tripod. Shizhen, being not clever, has consulted the various explanations and gathered them together below, to serve as the trap and snare (quanti) for those who study immortality and medicine [alike].
