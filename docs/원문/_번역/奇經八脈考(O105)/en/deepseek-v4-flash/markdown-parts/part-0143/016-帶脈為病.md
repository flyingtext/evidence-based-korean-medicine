## O105:p016:t0012

### Source Text

巢元方病源曰:腎著病, 腰痛冷如冰, 身重腰如帶五千錢, 不渴, 小便利, 因勞汗出, 衣里冷濕而得, 久則變為水也. 千金用腎著湯, 三因用滲濕湯, 東垣用獨活湯主之.

### Literal Translation

Chao Yuanfang's Treatise on the Origins of Disease says: In kidney-attachment disease (shenzhuo), the lower back is cold as ice, the body is heavy and the lower back is like wearing five thousand coins, there is no thirst, urination is free; it is obtained from sweating during exertion, with the inside of the clothes cold and damp, and if it lasts long it changes into water. The Qianjin uses the Shenzhuo Decoction, the Sanyin uses the Shenshi Decoction, and Dongyuan uses the Duhuo Decoction to govern it.
