## O105:p007:t0006

### Source Text

如環之無端, 莫知其紀, 終而復始. 其流溢之氣, 內溉臟腑, 外濡腠理.

### Literal Translation

Like a ring without beginning or end, no one knows its limits; it ends and then begins again. Its flowing and overflowing qi internally irrigates the zang and fu, and externally moistens the couli (interstices).
