## O105:p007:t0005

### Source Text

甲乙經曰:蹺脈有陰陽, 何者當其數?曰:男子數其陽, 女子數其陰, 當數者為經, 不當數者為絡. 氣之在身也, 如水之流, 如日月之行不休. 故陰脈營其臟, 而陽脈營其腑.

### Literal Translation

The Jia Yi jing says: The Qiao vessel has both yin and yang; which one is taken as its number? It says: In a man one takes its number by the yang, and in a woman one takes its number by the yin; that which takes the number is the primary channel (jing), and that which does not take the number is the connecting vessel (luo). The qi in the body is like the flow of water and the movement of the sun and moon without rest. Therefore the yin vessels nourish the zang-organs and the yang vessels nourish the fu-organs.
