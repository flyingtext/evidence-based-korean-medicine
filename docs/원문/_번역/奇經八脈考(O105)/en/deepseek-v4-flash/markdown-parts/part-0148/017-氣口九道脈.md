## O105:p017:t0005

### Source Text

浮為氣, 澀為風, 急為轉筋、為勞. 前部如內者, 足厥陰肝也, 動苦少腹痛引腰, 大便不利, 男子莖中痛, 小便難, 疝氣兩丸上入. 女子月水不利, 陰中寒, 子戶閉, 少腹急.

### Literal Translation

Floating is qi, rough is wind, rapid (ji) is cramping of the sinews and taxation. When the front region is as if internal, it is the foot Absolute Yin Liver; when it moves it causes lower abdominal pain drawing to the lower back, unfavorable defecation, in men pain within the penis, difficult urination, and hernia with the two testicles ascending into [the abdomen]. In women, unfavorable menstruation, cold within the genitals, closure of the uterus, and urgency of the lower abdomen.
