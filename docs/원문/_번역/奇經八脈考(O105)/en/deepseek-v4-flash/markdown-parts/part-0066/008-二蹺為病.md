## O105:p008:t0022

### Source Text

一云:脾之候在u瞼, u瞼動則知脾能消化也. 脾病則瞼澀嗜臥矣. 數說皆論目閉目不瞑, 雖不言及二蹺, 蓋亦不離乎陰陽營衛虛實之理.

### Literal Translation

One text says: The spleen's sign is in the u瞼u (eyelid); when the eyelid moves, one knows the spleen can digest. When the spleen is diseased, the eyelid is rough and one is drowsy and sleeps excessively. These several statements all discuss the opening and closing of the eyes and sleeplessness; although they do not mention the two qiao vessels, they still do not depart from the principle of the deficiency and excess of yin-yang and nutritive-defensive [qi].
