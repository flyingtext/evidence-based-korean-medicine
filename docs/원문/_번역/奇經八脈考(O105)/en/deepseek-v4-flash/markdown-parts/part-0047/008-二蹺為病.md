### Source Text

（附陽穴也） 又曰:寸口脈後部左右彈者, 陰蹺也. 動苦癲癇、寒熱, 皮膚淫痹, 又為少腹痛, 裡急, 腰及u髖u窌下相連, 陰中痛, 男子陰u疝, u女子漏下不止. （髖, 髀骨也. 窌, 腰下穴也） 又曰:癲癇瘛瘲, 不知所苦, 兩蹺之下, 男陽女陰.

### Literal Translation

He also said: When the pulse at the rear part of the cum-kou [position] rebounds to the left and right, it is the Yin Qiao. In motion it causes suffering from mania-epilepsy, chills and fever, and a damp-numbness (yin bi) of the skin; and in addition it causes pain in the lower abdomen, inner urgency, the lumbar region and below the hip-socket (kuanliao) connected continuously, and pain in the genitals — in a man inguinal swelling (yin-shan), and in a woman continuous uterine bleeding (lou xia) without stopping. (Kuan is the thigh bone. Liao is the point below the lumbar region.) He also said: In mania-epilepsy with cramping (chizong), not knowing the place of suffering; below the two Qiao[s], [take] the yang in a man and the yin in a woman.
