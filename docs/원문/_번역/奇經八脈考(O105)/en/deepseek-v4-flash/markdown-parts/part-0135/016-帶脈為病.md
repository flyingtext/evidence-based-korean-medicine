## O105:p016:t0004

### Source Text

又曰:女子經病血崩, 久而成枯者, 宜澀之益之. 血閉久而成竭者, 宜益之破之. 破血有三治, 始則四物入紅花, 調黃耆、肉桂.

### Literal Translation

He also said: In women, when menstrual disease and metrorrhagia last long and become withered, one should astringe and supplement it. When blood blockage lasts long and becomes exhausted, one should supplement and break it. Breaking blood has three treatments: first, Siwu [decoction] with honghua (safflower), adjusted with huangqi (Astragalus) and rougui (cinnamon).
