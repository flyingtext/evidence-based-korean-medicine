## O105:p008:t0017

### Source Text

衛氣者, 出其u悍u氣之慓疾, 而先於四末分肉皮膚之間, 而不休焉. 晝日行於陽, 夜行於陰, 常從足少陰分間, 行於五臟六腑. 今厥氣客於五臟六腑, 則衛氣獨衛其外, 行於陽不得入於陰, 行於陽則陽氣盛, 陽氣盛則陽蹺陷, 不得入於陰則陰氣虛, 故目不瞑也.

### Literal Translation

As for the wei-qi, it issues out the keen and swift nature of its hasty (han) qi, and first [runs] among the four extremities and the divisions of flesh and skin, without rest: by day it travels in the yang and by night it travels in the yin, constantly from the division of Foot Shaoyin, traveling through the five zang and six fu. Now, when rebellious qi (jueqi) lodges in the five zang and six fu, then the wei-qi alone guards its exterior, traveling in the yang yet unable to enter the yin; traveling in the yang then the yang-qi is exuberant, with the yang-qi exuberant the Yang Qiao sinks (xian); and being unable to enter the yin then the yin-qi is deficient, and therefore the eyes do not close.
