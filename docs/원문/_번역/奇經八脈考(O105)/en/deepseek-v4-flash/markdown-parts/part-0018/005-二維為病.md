## O105:p005:t0004

### Source Text

仲景又云:臟無他病時, 發熱自汗出而不愈, 此衛氣不和也, 桂枝湯主之. 又曰:陰維為病苦心痛, 治在三陰之交. 太陰證則理中湯, 少陰證則四逆湯, 厥陰證則當歸四逆湯、吳茱萸湯主之.

### Literal Translation

Zhongjing also said: When there is no other disease in the zang-organs, but fever with spontaneous sweating arises and is not cured, this is disharmony of the wei-qi, and Guizhi Tang governs it. He also said: The disease of the Yin Wei is suffering from heart pain, and treatment lies at the intersection of the three yin. For the Taiyin pattern use Lizhong Tang (Center-Regulating Decoction), for the Shaoyin pattern use Sini Tang (Frigid Extremities Decoction), and for the Jueyin pattern use Danggui Sini Tang (Angelica Frigid Extremities Decoction) and Wuzhuyu Tang (Evodia Decoction).
