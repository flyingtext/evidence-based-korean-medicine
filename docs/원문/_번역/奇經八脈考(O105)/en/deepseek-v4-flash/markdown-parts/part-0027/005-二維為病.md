## O105:p005:t0013

### Source Text

大抵二疾當取之四脈之穴, 分其陰陽而已. 王叔和曰:診得陽維脈浮者, 暫起目眩, 陽盛實者, 苦肩息, u洒洒u如寒. 診得陰維脈沉大而實者, 苦胸中痛, 脅下支滿, 心痛.

### Literal Translation

In general, for these two diseases one should take the points of the four vessels, only differentiating their yin and yang. Wang Shuhe said: When one diagnoses the Yang Wei vessel as floating, [the patient] suddenly rises with dizziness; when the yang is exuberant and excess, [he] suffers from shoulder breathing and a chill-like shivering (sasa). When one diagnoses the Yin Wei vessel as deep, large, and excess, [he] suffers from pain in the chest, fullness in the ribs below the flank, and heart pain.
