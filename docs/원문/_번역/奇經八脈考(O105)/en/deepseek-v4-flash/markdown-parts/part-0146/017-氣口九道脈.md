### Source Text

今撰為圖, 並附其說於後, 以泄千古之秘臟云. u診右手內外反此 u岐伯曰:前部如外者, 足太陽膀胱也, 動苦目眩, 頭、項、腰、背強痛, 男子陰下濕癢, 女子少腹痛引命門, 陰中痛, 子臟閉, 月水不利. 浮為風, 澀為寒, 滑為勞熱, 緊為宿食.

### Literal Translation

Now I have composed a chart, and appended its explanation after it, to reveal the secret treasury of a thousand ages. [When] examining the right hand, inside and outside are reversed from this. Qibo said: When the front region is as if external, it is the foot Greater Yang Bladder; when it moves it causes dizziness, and stiffness and pain of the head, neck, lower back, and spine; in men, damp and itchy genitals below; in women, lower abdominal pain drawing to the life gate (mingmen), pain within the genitals, closure of the uterus, and unfavorable menstruation. Floating is wind, rough (se) is cold, slippery (hua) is taxation-heat, tight (jin) is retained food.
