## O105:p001:t0002

### Source Text

（難經作陰絡, 陽絡）共二十七氣, 相隨上下, 如泉之流, 如日月之行, 不得休息. 故陰脈營於五臟, 陽脈營於六腑, 陰陽相貫, 如環無端, 莫知其紀, 終而夏始. 其流溢之氣, 入於奇經, 轉相灌u溉, u內溫臟腑, 外濡腠理.

### Literal Translation

(In the Nanjing they are written as yin-luo and yang-luo.) Altogether twenty-seven qi, following one another up and down, like the flow of a spring, like the movement of the sun and moon, without rest. Therefore the yin vessels nourish the five zang-organs, and the yang vessels nourish the six fu-organs; yin and yang connect with each other in succession, like a ring without beginning or end, no one knows [their] limits, ending and then beginning anew [like summer]. Its flowing and overflowing qi enters the extraordinary meridians, being mutually irrigated and circulated around; internally it warms the zang and fu, and externally it moistens the couli (interstices).
