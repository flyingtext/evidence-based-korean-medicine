## O105:p013:t0009

### Source Text

正在乾之下、坤之上、震之西、兌之東、坎離交媾之地, 在人身天地之正中, 八脈、九竅、十二經、十五絡聯輳, 虛間一穴, 空懸黍珠, 醫書謂之任、督二脈. 此元氣之所由生, 真息之所由起, 修丹之士, 不明此竅, 則真息不生, 神化無基也. 俞琰注參同契云:人身血氣, 往來循環, 晝夜不停, 醫書有任、督二脈, 人能通此二脈, 則百脈皆通.

### Literal Translation

It is precisely below qian, above kun, west of zhen, east of dui, the place of intercourse of kan and li, at the exact center of the heaven and earth of the human body, where the eight vessels, nine orifices, twelve channels, and fifteen collaterals converge; in the empty space there is one orifice, hanging empty like a millet pearl, which the medical books call the Ren and Du two vessels. This is where the primordial qi is engendered and where the true breath arises; if the practitioner of elixir cultivation does not understand this orifice, then the true breath is not engendered and spiritual transformation has no foundation. Yu Yan's commentary on the Cantong Qi says: The blood and qi of the human body circulate back and forth, day and night without stopping; the medical books have the Ren and Du two vessels, and if a person can open these two vessels, then all the hundred vessels are open.
