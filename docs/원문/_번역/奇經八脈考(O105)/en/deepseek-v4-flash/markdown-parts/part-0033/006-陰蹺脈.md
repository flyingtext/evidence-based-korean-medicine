## O105:p006:t0001

### Source Text

陰蹺者, 足少陰之別脈, 其脈起於跟中, 足少陰然谷穴之後, （然谷在內踝前下一寸陷中）同足少陰循內踝下照海穴, （在內踝下五分）上內踝之上二寸, 以交信為郄. （交信在內踝骨上, 少陰前太陰後筋骨間）直上循陰股入陰, 上循胸裡入缺盆, 上出人迎之前, 至咽嚨, 交貫衝脈, 入頄內廉, 上行屬目內眥, 與手足太陽、足陽明、陽蹺, 五脈, 會於睛明而上行. （睛明在目內眥外一分宛宛中）凡八穴.

### Literal Translation

The Yin Qiao is the bie (divergent) vessel of Foot Shaoyin; its vessel arises in the middle of the heel, at [the place] after the Rangu point (KI2) of Foot Shaoyin (Rangu is in the depression one cun below and in front of the inner ankle); together with Foot Shaoyin it follows down the inner ankle to the Zhaohai point (KI6) (five fen below the inner ankle); ascending two cun above the inner ankle, it takes Jiaoxin as its xi (cleft) point (Jiaoxin is on the bone above the inner ankle, between Shaoyin in front and Taiyin behind, among the sinew and bone). Ascending straight it follows the inner thigh and enters the genitals, ascends following the interior of the chest into the supraclavicular fossa, ascends out in front of Renying (ST9), reaches the throat, intersecting and penetrating the Chong meridian, enters the inner border of the cheek (qiu), runs upward and belongs to (connects to) the inner canthus of the eye, gathering with the five vessels — hand-foot Taiyang, Foot Yangming, and the Yang Qiao — at Jingming (BL1), and ascends. (Jingming is in the hollow one fen outside the inner canthus of the eye.) In all there are eight points.
