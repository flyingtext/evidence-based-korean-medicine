## O105:p006:t0004

### Source Text

倘能知此, 使真氣聚散, 皆從此關竅, 則天門常開, 地戶永閉, 尻脈周流於一身, 貫通上下, 和氣自然上朝, 陽長陰消, 水中火發, 雪裡花開. 所謂天根月窟閒來往, 三十六宮都是春. 得之者, 身體輕健, 容衰返壯, 昏昏默默, 如醉如癡, 此其驗也.

### Literal Translation

If one can know this, [so that] the gathering and dispersing of the true qi all [proceed] through this gate and aperture, then the Tianmen (heaven gate) will constantly be open, and the Dihu (earth door) forever closed; the coccyx-vessel (kao mai) will circulate throughout the whole body, penetrating through above and below, and the harmonious qi will naturally ascend to the court; yang grows and yin wanes, fire bursts forth in water, and flowers bloom in snow. This is what is called 'the heaven-root and moon-cavern come and go at leisure, and in the thirty-six palaces it is all spring.' He who attains it is light of body and nimble, his failing looks returning to vigor, in a dazed and heedless state, as if drunk, as if foolish — this is its verification.
