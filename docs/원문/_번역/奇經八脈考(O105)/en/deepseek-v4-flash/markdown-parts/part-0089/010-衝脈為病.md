## O105:p010:t0009

### Source Text

治之當各補其營而通其腧, 調其虛實, 和其逆順, 筋、脈、骨、肉各以其時受月, 則病已. （謂肝甲乙、心丙丁、脾戊己、主氣法時月也） 李東垣曰:暑月病甚, 則傳腎肝為痿厥. 痿, 乃四肢痿軟.

### Literal Translation

In treating it, one should supplement its nutritive [qi] and open its shu points, regulate its deficiency and excess, harmonize its counterflow and smooth [flow], and the sinews, vessels, bones, and flesh each receive the month according to their time, then the disease is cured. (This refers to the liver [governing] jia-yi, the heart [governing] bing-ding, the spleen [governing] wu-ji, the qi method following the time and month.) Li Dongyuan said: When the disease is severe in the summer month, it transmits to the kidney and liver as atrophy and reversal. Atrophy is the four limbs becoming weak and soft.
