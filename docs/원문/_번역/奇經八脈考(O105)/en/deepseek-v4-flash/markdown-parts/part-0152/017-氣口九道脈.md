## O105:p017:t0009

### Source Text

前部橫於寸口丸丸者, 任脈也, 動苦少腹痛, 逆氣搶心, 胸拘急不得俯仰. 脈經云:寸口脈緊細實長, 下至關者, 任脈也, 動苦少腹繞臍痛, 男子七疝, 女子瘕聚. 三部俱浮直上直下者, 督脈也, 動苦腰脊強痛, 不得俯仰, 大人癲, 小兒癇.

### Literal Translation

When the front region lies across the cunkou and is round and round (wanwan), it is the Ren vessel; when it moves it causes lower abdominal pain, rebellious qi rushing to the heart, and cramping of the chest with inability to bend and stretch. The Pulse Classic says: When the cunkou pulse is tight, fine, solid, and long, reaching down to the guan, it is the Ren vessel; when it moves it causes lower abdominal pain encircling the navel, in men the seven hernias, in women abdominal masses (jiaju). When all three regions are floating and run straight up and down, it is the Governing Vessel; when it moves it causes stiffness and pain of the lower back and spine, inability to bend and stretch, in adults mania, in children epilepsy.
