## O105:p008:t0008

### Source Text

蹺, 為陽蹺所生, 申脈穴也. 蹺上郄下, 乃承筋穴也, 即腨中央如外陷者中也. 太陽脈氣所發, 禁針刺, 但視其兩腨中央有血絡盛滿者, 乃刺之出血.

### Literal Translation

Qiao is that from which the Yang Qiao is generated; it is the Shenmai point. 'Above the Qiao and below the xi' is the Chengjin point (BL56), that is, the center of the calf in the place that is hollowed on the outside. It is where the vessel-qi of Taiyang issues; needling is forbidden [there], and one only looks [for] where, in the center of the two calves, there is a blood-luo full and exuberant and then punctures it to let blood.
