## O105:p010:t0003

### Source Text

宜調中益氣湯加吳茱萸五分, 隨氣多少用之. （脾胃論）夏月有此, 乃大熱之證, 用黃連、黃柏、知母各等分, 酒洗炒為末, 白湯和丸, 每服一二百丸, 空心白湯下, 即以美膳壓之, 不令停留胃中, 直至下元, 以瀉衝脈之邪也. 蓋此病隨四時寒熱溫涼治之.

### Literal Translation

One should use Tiaozhong Yiqi Tang (Regulate the Center and Boost Qi Decoction) with the addition of five fen of Wu Zhuyu (Evodia); use it according to the amount of qi. (Piwet Lun, Discussion of the Spleen and Stomach.) When this occurs in summer, it is a sign of great heat; use Huanglian (Coptis), Huangbai (Phellodendron), and Zhimu (Anemarrhena) in equal parts, washed in wine and stir-fried, ground into powder, mixed with plain water into pills; take one to two hundred pills each time on an empty stomach with plain water, then press them down with fine food, not allowing them to remain in the stomach, until they reach the lower origin, in order to drain the pathogenic factor of the Chong vessel. For this disease, treat according to the cold, heat, warmth, and coolness of the four seasons.
