## O105:p008:t0016

### Source Text

靈樞曰:五穀入於胃也, 其糟粕、津液、宗氣為三隧. 故宗氣積於胸中, 出於喉嚨, 以貫心肺而行呼吸焉. 營氣者, u泌u其津液, 注之於脈, 化而為血, 以榮四末, 內注五臟六腑, 以應刻數焉.

### Literal Translation

The Lingshu says: When the five grains enter the stomach, their dregs, fluids (jinye), and ancestral-qi (zongqi) become the three passageways (sansui). Therefore the ancestral-qi accumulates in the chest, issues from the throat, in order to penetrate through the heart and lungs and carry out respiration. The ying-qi seeps out its fluids, pours them into the vessels, transforms them into blood, in order to nourish the four extremities, and internally pours into the five zang and six fu, in order to correspond to the counting of the [water-]clock markings.
