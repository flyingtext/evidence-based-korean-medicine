## 秘寶圍藥方

### Source Text

<ins>秘寶圍藥方</ins>

治一切腫毒。

陳墨碾碎（二錢）　川大黃（二兩）　藤黃（六錢）　黃柏（五錢）　冰片　麝香（各五分）　雄豬膽（五個）　陳醋（生薑自然汁各一小杯）

上將藥共為末，和豬膽、陳醋、薑汁搗勻，作錠子，晒乾。每用陳醋磨塗患處，散腫拔毒生肌神效。

### Literal Translation

Treats all swelling-masses and toxics (zhong-du).

Aged ink, crushed 2 qian. Sichuan rhubarb 2 liang. Gamboge (teng-huang) 6 qian. Phellodendron 5 qian. Borneol and musk (each 5 fen). Male-pig gall 5 pieces. Aged vinegar (and a small cup each of fresh-ginger juice).

Grind the above drugs together into powder; mix with the pig gall, aged vinegar, and ginger juice, pound evenly, form into lozenges (ding-zi), and sun-dry. Each time grind with aged vinegar and smear on the affected area. It disperses swelling, draws out toxin, and generates flesh; the effect is miraculous.

## 太乙五行膏

### Source Text

<ins>太乙五行膏</ins>

統治一切無名腫毒。

牛蹄甲　馬蹄甲　驢蹄甲　豬蹄甲　羊蹄甲（各五兩）　連翹　三棱　莪朮　黑丑　白丑　木香　胡連沙參　地骨皮　元參　柴胡（各一錢五分）　白芥子　天花粉（各一錢）　山查　麥芽　神麯（各六分）

上先將五蹄甲入麻油二斤四兩熬枯，去渣，再入連翹等十六味，熬焦，濾清。俟油熬至滴水成珠為度，再入陶丹一斤二兩，水飛收膏，攤貼患處。散腫拔毒生肌，神效。惟眉心耳後忌貼。

### Literal Translation

Treats all nameless swellings and toxins of every kind.

Hoof-shells of ox, horse, donkey, pig, and sheep (each 5 liang). Forsythia, trigonella (san-leng), zedoary, black morning-glory seed, white morning-glory seed, costus root, picrorhiza, glehnia root, lycium root bark, scrophularia, and bupleurum (each 1 qian 5 fen). White mustard seed and trichosanthes powder (each 1 qian). Hawthorn fruit, barley sprout, and medicated leaven (each 6 fen).

The five hoof-shells are first placed into 2 jin 4 liang of sesame oil and decocted dry; the dregs are discarded; then the sixteen drugs beginning with forsythia are added, decocted charred, strained clear. Wait until the oil decocts to the point that a dropping drop forms a pearl; then add 1 jin 2 liang of potter's minium (tao-dan), levigate with water to collect the paste, and spread it on the affected area. It disperses swelling, draws out toxin, and generates flesh; the effect is miraculous. Only avoid sticking it on the center of the brow and behind the ears.

## 紫靈丹

### Source Text

<ins>紫靈丹</ins>

專治瘡癤腫毒。

冰片　麝香　乳香（去油）　沒藥（去油，各四錢八分）　血竭（一兩二錢）　朱砂（一錢）　前胡元參（各一錢二分）　母丁香（八分）　班毛（一兩六錢，淨，去頭，足，翅，用糯米炒）

上共為細末，收固。每用少許，放膏上貼患處。

### Literal Translation

Specially treats sores, boils, swellings, and toxics.

Borneol, musk, frankincense (oil removed), and myrrh (oil removed) (each 4 qian 8 fen). Dragon's blood 1 liang 2 qian. Cinnabar 1 qian. Peucedanum and scrophularia (each 1 qian 2 fen). Mother-clove 8 fen. Mylabris beetle (ban-mao) 1 liang 6 qian (purified, head, legs, and wings removed, stir-fried with glutinous rice).

Grind the above together into a fine powder and store it sealed. Each time use a little, place it on a poultice, and stick it on the affected area.
