## 口疳散

### Source Text

<ins>口疳散</ins>

薄荷末（三錢）　兒茶（一錢五分）　黃柏（一分）　珍珠　生甘草（各五分）　冰片（三分）　龍骨醋煅（二分）　白芷（二分五厘，腫痛加倍）

上共為極細末。遇口疳吹之，神效。初起熱甚，倍薄荷。久病多加珍、珠兒茶。

龍骨即長肉痘疹後去黃柏、龍骨加牛黃。

疳重加滴乳香、朱砂各少許。

### Literal Translation

Peppermint powder 3 qian; catechu (er-cha) 1 qian 5 fen; phellodendron 1 fen; pearl and raw licorice (each 5 fen); borneol 3 fen; dragon bone (calcined in vinegar) 2 fen; angelica dahurica (bai-zhi) 2 fen 5 li (double it if there is swelling and pain).

Grind the above together into a very fine powder. When mouth-canker (kou-gan) is met, blow it on; the effect is miraculous. If at the onset the heat is intense, double the peppermint. In a long illness add more pearl and catechu.

Dragon bone is what grows flesh. After pox-and-eruption, remove the phellodendron and dragon bone and add bezoar.

If the canker is severe, add a little drop-liquid frankincense and cinnabar each.

## 青蓮散

### Source Text

<ins>青蓮散</ins>

專治一切喉風生蛾等症。

山豆根　兒茶　胡連（各一錢）　川黃連（三分）　冰片（一分）　青魚膽（二錢）

上共乳極細，收固，聽用。吹之立愈。

### Literal Translation

Specially treats all throat-wind (hou-feng) and pharyngeal abscess (sheng-e) type disorders.

Tonsil-root (shan-dou-gen), catechu, and picrorhiza (hu-lian) (each 1 qian); Sichuan golden thread (chuan-huang-lian) 3 fen; borneol 1 fen; green-fish gall 2 qian.

Grind the above together very fine, store sealed, and set aside for use. Blow it on and it cures at once.

## 臌症第一方

### Source Text

<ins>臌症第一方</ins>

當歸　白朮　白芍（各二兩）　茯苓　檳榔　常山（酒浸，焙透）　草果（各一兩）　枳殼　厚朴青皮（無鹽醋，拌炒）　陳皮（各一兩五錢）

上共為細末，加細針砂粉四兩，和勻。每日服兩次，用三分，以紅棗七枚，已嚼服藥，及愈後禁鹽百日，永戒食牛肉。如犯背平、腳底平、臍凸，乃不治之症，不必服。

### Literal Translation

Angelica (dang-gui), atractylodes (bai-zhu), and white peony (bai-shao) (each 2 liang). Poria, betel-nut, dichroa (chang-shan, soaked in wine and baked through), and tsaoko cardamon (cao-guo) (each 1 liang). Aurantium (zhi-ke), magnolia bark (hou-po), green citrus peel (qing-pi, stir-fried with saltless vinegar), and aged tangerine peel (chen-pi) (each 1 liang 5 qian).

The above are ground together into a fine powder; add 4 liang of fine needle-iron-sand powder and mix evenly. Take twice daily, a dose of 3 fen with seven red dates chewed before taking the medicine. After recovery avoid salt for a hundred days and permanently abstain from beef. If one comes to have a flat back, flat soles, and a protruding navel, that is an untreatable syndrome, so there is no need to take it.
