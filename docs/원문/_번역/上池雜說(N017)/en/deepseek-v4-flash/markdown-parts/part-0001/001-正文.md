## 太極光

### Source Text

<ins>太極光</ins>

統治男婦大小百病惡症，瘡疽腫毒，筋骨疼痛，左癱右瘓諸症。

孔雀尾（四錢，用甘草水洗，撮土搓之。復用水洗淨，晒乾，為末。純用是尾，端圓處更勝）

乳香　沒藥（各去油，淨）　蜈蚣　全蠍　磁石火煅麝香（各二錢）　螻蛄（晒乾）　雄黃（醋浸透，換白蘿菔汁，煮用）　朱砂（各三錢）　水銀（五錢）　牙硝（一兩二錢五分）　硫黃（二兩五錢）

上共為一處，碾成細末，文火，用磁碗一只，將藥末每錢許，匙挑入碗內，以竹刀炒，如米粒大小不等，勿令焦枯。收入磁瓶封固聽用。每症各取藥置患處，以火焠著灸之。灸時要避風。如遍身風氣痛，則置藥於各處骨節間，遍灸之。重症灸後須避風七日，神效。

### Literal Translation

Cures all the many illnesses and severe syndromes of men, women, young and old: sores, ulcers, swelling-masses, sinew-and-bone pain, and hemiplegia on the left and right.

Peacock tail (4 qian): wash with licorice water, rub with earth, wash clean again with water, dry in the sun, and reduce to powder. Use the tail alone; the round tip is even better.

Frankincense and myrrh (each: remove the oil, purify). Centipede, scorpion, magnetite (calcined in fire), and musk (each 2 qian). Mole cricket (sun-dried), realgar (soaked through in vinegar, then boiled in turn in radish juice for use), and cinnabar (each 3 qian). Quicksilver 5 qian. Nitre (ya-xiao) 1 liang 2 qian 5 fen. Sulfur 2 liang 5 qian.

The above are all gathered in one place, ground into fine powder; over a gentle fire, use a porcelain bowl, spoon about one qian of the powder into the bowl, stir it with a bamboo knife, so it forms grains of varying rice-grain sizes, without letting it burn dry. Put it in a porcelain jar, seal it, and set it aside for use. For each syndrome take the medicine and place it on the affected area, set flame to it, and moxa it. When moxaing, avoid drafts. If there is wind-qi pain all over the body, place the medicine among the various bones and joints and moxa everywhere. In severe cases, after moxaing, one must avoid drafts for seven days; the effect is miraculous.

## 紺雪丹

### Source Text

<ins>紺雪丹</ins>

專治一切目疾，並去翳膜，如神。

六月雪根燒灰存性　冰片量加。

上不拘多少，共乳極細，收用。加熊膽少許更神。

### Literal Translation

Specially treats all eye disorders and removes nebula and films, as if by spirit.

Serissa root: burn to ash and preserve its nature. Borneol: add the right amount (as needed).

Regardless of amount, grind the above together very fine, store, and use. Adding a little bear gall makes it even more miraculous.

## 去老膜翳障神方

### Source Text

<ins>去老膜翳障神方</ins>

珍珠（豆腐煮，研）　荸薺粉（各四分）　熊膽（箸皮上焙乾）　陀僧　朱砂（各水飛）　蕤仁（去油，各三分）硇砂　白丁香（水飛，各二分）

上為細末如面，磁瓶收固，用金銀角簪點患處。

### Literal Translation

Pearl (boiled in bean-curd and ground), water-chestnut powder (each 4 fen). Bear gall (parched dry on chopstick-skin), litharge (tuo-seng), and cinnabar (each: levigate in water) and zhiren seed (oil removed) (each 3 fen). Sal ammoniac (nao-sha) and white sparrow droppings (levigated in water) (each 2 fen).

Grind the above into a powder as fine as flour, store it sealed in a porcelain jar, and apply it to the affected area with a gold-or-silver horn hairpin.
