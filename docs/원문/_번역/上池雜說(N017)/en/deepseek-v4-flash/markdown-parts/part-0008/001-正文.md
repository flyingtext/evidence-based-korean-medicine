## 治小兒臍風撮口方

### Source Text

<ins>治小兒臍風撮口方</ins>

巴豆（一，粒去殼，研爛）　明雄黃（一錢，乳細）

上二味，和勻。每用三五粒，新汲井水調下。覺胸腹有響聲，大便下，痰即愈。此症最危，百無一治，亦少妙劑。此方神效，勿略之。

### Literal Translation

Croton (ba-dou): one seed, husk removed, ground to a pulp. Bright realgar: 1 qian, ground fine.

The above two ingredients are mixed and blended even. Each time use three to five of them, mixed with freshly drawn well water and washed down. When a sound is felt in the chest and belly and the stool passes, the phlegm is then cured. This syndrome is most dangerous, one in a hundred not cured, and there are also few wondrous prescriptions. This formula has a divine effect; do not take it lightly.

## 治痘毒神方

### Source Text

<ins>治痘毒神方</ins>

用白瑞香花葉，不拘多少，入冰糖少許，同搗極爛敷之。初起即消，已成即潰，已潰收功，他方醫愈後或年余數月復發者，無不神效。

### Literal Translation

Use the leaves of the white daphne flower (bai-rui-xiang-hua), whatever amount, add a little rock sugar, and pound them together extremely fine, then apply. If it has just arisen it disperses at once; if it has already formed it ruptures; if it is already ruptured it completes the success. Even among those who, cured by other formulas, later relapse after more than a year or after a few months, there is none without a divine effect.

## 震升丸

### Source Text

<ins>震升丸</ins>

專治痔瘡並腸風下血。

荷葉不拘多少，燒灰存性。

上一味用，生鱔魚血合搗為丸，如桐子大。每早空心白湯下三四錢。

### Literal Translation

Specially treats hemorrhoids and intestinal-wind bleeding from the anus (chang-feng xia-xue).

Lotus leaf, whatever amount, burned to ash preserving its nature.

Using this single ingredient, mix it with the blood of a raw eel, pound it into pills as large as paulownia seeds. Each morning take 3 to 4 qian on an empty stomach, washed down with plain boiled water.
