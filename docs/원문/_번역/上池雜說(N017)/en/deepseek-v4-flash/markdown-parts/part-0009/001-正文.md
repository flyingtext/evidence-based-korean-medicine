## 治噤曰痢方

### Source Text

<ins>治噤曰痢方</ins>

用燕窠泥不拘多少，研末，同鴨蛋青調勻，入麝香三分，小兒二分，敷臍上，泥干。又以蛋清潤之，一二日即愈。

### Literal Translation

Use swallow-nest mud in whatever amount; grind it into powder, mix it evenly together with duck-egg white, put in 3 fen of musk (children 2 fen), and apply it on the navel. When the mud dries, moisten it again with egg white. In one or two days it cures.

## 黃芽丸

### Source Text

<ins>黃芽丸</ins>

治胃強脾弱，能食不能消者，並脾泄等症。

制穀芽（四兩，半生半炒　制法：用糯穀三四升，韭葉搗汁，浸數日，候穀出芽，取起篩盛微，日晒略干，即以韭菜汁洒之，以芽帶綠色為度，晒乾聽用。）　人參（一兩，如不用，以黨參，黃耆代之）　芡實（二兩，炒）　蓮子肉（四兩，去心，取肉連皮，入豬肚內煮透，去肚，晒乾）

上共為細末，用荷葉一張，煮汁，和山藥末打糊為丸，如綠豆大。每服二錢，米飲下，日三次。

### Literal Translation

Treats those with a strong stomach but weak spleen, able to eat but unable to digest, as well as spleen-diarrhea and similar conditions.

Prepared grain-sprout (zhi-gu-ya) 4 liang (half raw and half stir-fried. Method: soak three or four dou of glutinous rice in the juice of pounded leek leaves for several days; wait for the grain to sprout; take it up, heap it in a sieve, sun-dry it slightly, then immediately sprinkle it with leek juice, making it just carry a green tint; sun-dry and set aside for use). Ginseng 1 liang (if not used, substitute codonopsis and astragalus). Euryale seed (qian-shi) 2 liang, stir-fried. Lotus seed flesh (lian-zi-rou) 4 liang (heart removed, the flesh taken with its skin, placed inside a pig's stomach, boiled through, the stomach removed, and sun-dried).

Grind the above together into a fine powder; boil one lotus leaf into a juice, mix it with dioscorea powder, and paste it into pills the size of mung beans. Take 2 qian each time, washed down with rice-water, three times daily.

## 治脫疽方

### Source Text

<ins>治脫疽方</ins>

此症發於足指，漸上至膝，色黑痛不可忍，逐節脫落而斃，至惡之症也。亦有發於手指者，同治。

用土蜂房一個，研細醋調搽，應手而愈，真仙方也。

### Literal Translation

This disorder arises at the toes, gradually ascends to the knee, is black in color with unbearable pain, and falls off joint by joint until death; it is the most evil of syndromes. There are also cases arising at the fingers, which are treated the same.

Use one wild-bee nest (tu-feng-fang), grind it fine, mix it with vinegar, and rub it on. It heals the moment one applies the hand; truly a transcendent formula.
