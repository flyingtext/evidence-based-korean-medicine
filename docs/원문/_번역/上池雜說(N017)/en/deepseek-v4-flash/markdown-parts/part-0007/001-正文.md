## 荔奴散

### Source Text

<ins>荔奴散</ins>

專治一切金瘡跌磕。

龍眼核不拘多少，燒灰存性。

上為細末，收貯。敷傷處立愈。

### Literal Translation

Specially treats all metal (blade) wounds and falls-and-bumps.

Longan seeds, whatever amount, burned to ash and preserving their nature.

Reduce the above to a fine powder and store it. Applied to the wound, it heals at once.

## 解鉛粉毒

### Source Text

<ins>解鉛粉毒</ins>

綠豆粉（一兩）　牙硝（二錢）　檳榔末（五分）

上共為細末，每用清米湯調如稀糊，一匙一匙漸次進服，不可太急，候吐止。腹內不痛，大便瀉盡，方可飲清米湯，然後吃粥調理。

### Literal Translation

Mung-bean powder 1 liang. Nitre (ya-xiao) 2 qian. Areca-seed powder 5 fen.

Grind the above together into a fine powder; each time mix with clear rice-water into a thin gruel and take it spoonful by spoonful, gradually, not too hastily. Wait until the vomiting stops. Only when the belly no longer aches and the stool has been entirely purged may one drink clear rice-water; then eat congee to recuperate.

## 蛇咬神方

### Source Text

<ins>蛇咬神方</ins>

朱砂　麝香（各二分）　雄黃（三分）　牙硝（五分）　殼珠（即假珠火炙，一分）　豬牙皂角（瓦炙，五個）　萆麻子（十粒，去殼，炙）

上為細末，磁瓶收固。用時將傷處銀針挑破，點藥少許，並點眼角片時，流去毒水，立效。

### Literal Translation

Cinnabar and musk (each 2 fen). Realgar 3 fen. Nitre (ya-xiao) 5 fen. Shell-pearl (i.e., imitation pearl, roasted in fire) 1 fen. Pig's-tooth locust-pod (zhu-ya-zao-jiao, roasted on tile) 5 pieces. Castor seed 10 pieces (skins removed, roasted).

Reduce the above to a fine powder and store it sealed in a porcelain jar. When using, prick open the wound with a silver needle, apply a little of the medicine, and also apply a little to the corner of the eye; after a while the venomous water flows away, and the effect is immediate.
