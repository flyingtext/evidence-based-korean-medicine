## 膿疥瘡煎方

### Source Text

<ins>膿疥瘡煎方</ins>

川芎　大腹皮　丹皮　生首烏　牛蒡子　當歸　紅花　赤芍　金銀花　生甘草（各一錢）

上加燈心二十根，水煎服五六劑後，再搽沒藥、花椒末、大風子肉、白芷、硫黃、檳榔等分，為細末，雄豬油同搗極爛搽之。

### Literal Translation

Ligusticum, areca peel, moutan bark, raw fleeceflower-root (he-shou-wu), burdock seed, angelica, safflower, red peony, honeysuckle, and raw licorice (each 1 qian).

Add 20 strands of rush pith (deng-xin) to the above, decoct in water, and take. After five or six doses, again rub on a powder of myrrh, ground peppercorn, hydnocarpus flesh, angelica dahurica, sulfur, and areca-seed in equal parts, pounded extremely fine together with male-pig fat.

## 透骨湯

### Source Text

<ins>透骨湯</ins>

專治跌打損傷，滿身青紫，危重者皆效。

五加皮　自然銅　青皮　紫荊皮　杜仲　紅花　川山甲　白蒺藜　歸尾　乳香　沒藥（以上各一錢）

活土鱉（三個，搗碎，沖）

上用水煎服，外加透骨草。更神傷骨者，加尋骨風。心慌者，加朱砂。輕者一劑至重者二劑，無不愈。

### Literal Translation

Specially treats injuries from falls and blows, a body covered in blue-and-purple bruises, and is effective even for the gravely endangered.

Acanthopanax root bark, native copper (zi-ran-tong), green citrus peel, redbud bark (zi-jing-pi), eucommia, safflower, pangolin scales, puncture-vine, angelica tail (gui-wei), frankincense, and myrrh (the above each 1 qian).

Live ground beetle (tu-bie) 3 pieces (crushed and infused).

Decoct the above in water and take; additionally add penetrating-bone herb (tou-gu-cao). For the even more wondrous case of injured bone, add xun-gu-feng (Aristolochia). For the anxious-hearted, add cinnabar. The mild need one dose and the severe two doses; there is none that does not heal.

## 接骨紫金丹

### Source Text

<ins>接骨紫金丹</ins>

土鱉（酒炙，去足，淨）　乳香　沒藥　歸尾（酒炒）　自然銅（醋煅，七次各三錢）　血竭　大黃（酒炒）

骨碎補（去毛，打碎，酒浸，晒乾）　硼砂（各一錢）

上共為細末，磁瓶收貯。跌打損傷，瘀血攻心，好酒下一分八厘。破傷吐血不止者，用當歸、桃仁、紅花各五分，煎酒下。

### Literal Translation

Ground beetle (roasted in wine, feet removed, purified), frankincense, myrrh, angelica tail (stir-fried in wine), and native copper (calcined in vinegar seven times) (each 3 qian). Dragon's blood and rhubarb (stir-fried in wine).

Drynaria (bone-broken-ash, gu-sui-bu: hairs removed, broken up, soaked in wine, and sun-dried) and borax (each 1 qian).

Grind the above together into a fine powder and store in a porcelain jar. For falls-and-blow injuries where stagnant blood attacks the heart, take 1 fen 8 li washed down with good wine. For those with open wounds whose blood-spitting will not stop, use 5 fen each of dang-gui, peach kernel, and safflower, decocted in wine and washed down.
