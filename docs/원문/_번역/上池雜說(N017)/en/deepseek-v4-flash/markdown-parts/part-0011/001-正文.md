## 離骨取牙方

### Source Text

<ins>離骨取牙方</ins>

烏梅十七個，童便浸，春夏秋兩日，冬三日，甘草水漂過。

上二味，等分，陰陽瓦焙乾，研細。每末一錢，加黃牙丹五分，拌勻。但看骨槽風有骨橫在牙床者，用糯米漿少許，拌藥成條，塞在骨縫兩邊，塞滿。如無縫，即散放牙縫內。一日兩次，上藥二十日，其骨自動，再著眼明手輕之人，鉗去之。切不可將藥誤黏好齒，致傷好牙。此方甚神而奇。

### Literal Translation

Seventeen dark plums (wu-mei), soak them in a boy's urine: in spring, summer, and autumn for two days, in winter for three; then rinse them through licorice water.

The above two (i.e., the prepared plums and licorice) in equal parts are baked dry on a yin-yang tile and ground fine. To each 1 qian of powder add 5 fen of Yellow-tooth Elixir (huang-ya-dan) and blend evenly. When one sees bone-socket wind (gu-cao-feng) with a bone lying across the tooth-bed, use a little glutinous-rice paste to mix the medicine into a strip, and plug it full into both sides of the bone-seam. If there is no seam, just scatter it within the tooth-gap. Twice a day, after applying the medicine for twenty days, the bone moves of itself; then have a bright-eyed, light-handed person pull it out with forceps. Absolutely do not let the medicine mistakenly stick to good teeth and injure the good teeth. This formula is extremely miraculous and extraordinary.

## 黃牙丹

### Source Text

<ins>黃牙丹</ins>

此丹去污生新，治疳要藥。

汞（一兩）　藤黃（五分）　牙硝　明礬（各一兩五錢）　蛇含石（八分）

上共研勻，結胎，武火升煉三炷香，取藥。每一兩加冰片四分，收貯聽用。

### Literal Translation

This elixir removes filth and generates the new; it is the essential medicine for treating gan (canker/childhood malnutrition).

Quicksilver 1 liang. Gamboge (teng-huang) 5 fen. Nitre and alum (each 1 liang 5 qian). Serpentine stone (she-han-shi) 8 fen.

Grind the above together and blend evenly; form it into a lozenge-base, then with fierce fire sublime-and-refine it for the burning of three incense-sticks, and take out the medicine. To each 1 liang add 4 fen of borneol, store it, and set it aside for use.
