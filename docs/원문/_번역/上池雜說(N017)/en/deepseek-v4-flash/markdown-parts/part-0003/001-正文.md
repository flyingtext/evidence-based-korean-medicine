## 金彈丸

### Source Text

<ins>金彈丸</ins>

專治小兒急驚結胸等症，奇效。

牛黃　珍珠（各四分）　琥珀　川鬱金　半夏　射干　礞石（火硝煅，各二錢）　朱砂（水飛）　明雄黃（各一錢）　陳膽星　川貝母　天竺黃　巴豆（去殼，淨，各四錢）　甘草　生薑（各三錢）　冰片麝香（各一分）

上共為細末，煉白蜜為丸，每粒重三分，金箔為衣，或熔蠟為丸，護之更妙。

### Literal Translation

Specially treats children's acute fright (ji-jing), chest-binding (jie-xiong), and similar syndromes, with wondrous effect.

Bezoar and pearl (each 4 fen). Amber, Sichuan turmeric (chuan-yu-jin), pinellia, blackberry lily root (she-gan), and lazurite (meng-shi, calcined in saltpetre) (each 2 qian). Cinnabar (levigated in water) and bright realgar (each 1 qian). Aged bile-arisaema (chen-dan-xing), Sichuan fritillaria bulb (chuan-bei-mu), bamboo-sap (tian-zhu-huang), and croton (ba-dou, husks removed and purified) (each 4 qian). Licorice and fresh ginger (each 3 qian). Borneol and musk (each 1 fen).

The above are ground together into a fine powder; boil down white honey into pills, each pill weighing 3 fen, coated with gold leaf, or melt wax into pills to protect them, which is even better.

## 疝氣方

### Source Text

<ins>疝氣方</ins>

新鮮大小薊根，不拘多少。

上一味搗爛，酒煎服立效。

### Literal Translation

Fresh large thistle (da-ji) and small thistle (xiao-ji) roots, in whatever amount.

Pound this single ingredient to a mash, decoct it in wine, and take it; the effect is immediate.

## 一切腫毒初起煎方

### Source Text

<ins>一切腫毒初起煎方</ins>

金銀花　紫花地丁（各一兩）　用井水（二碗，河水二碗，煎成二碗，去渣，入後藥，加當歸一錢）　白芷陳皮（各二錢）　甘草（八分，用前藥水煎成一碗加水酒一碗入後藥加）　乳香　沒藥　土貝母（各二錢）　穿山甲（三片）

共煎成一碗，去渣、服，神驗。乳毒加蒲公英（一兩）

### Literal Translation

Honeysuckle flower and Viola (zi-hua-di-ding, each 1 liang). Decoct with two bowls of well water and two bowls of river water into two bowls, discard the dregs, then into the subsequent drugs add 1 qian of dang-gui. Angelica dahurica (bai-zhi) and aged tangerine peel (each 2 qian). Licorice 8 fen (decoct the earlier drugs with water into one bowl, add one bowl of water-wine, and add it into the subsequent drugs). Frankincense, myrrh, and Zhejiang fritillaria (tu-bei-mu, each 2 qian). Pangolin scales 3 pieces.

Decoct all together into one bowl, discard the dregs, and take; it is divinely proven. For breast-toxin (ru-du) add 1 liang of dandelion.
