## 湯火傷

### Source Text

<ins>湯火傷</ins>

用生薑不拘多少，搗爛撲之。又方，冬月收壞橘不拘多少，貯磁瓶中封，置靜處日，久自化為清水。用時取水塗患處，神效。

### Literal Translation

Use fresh ginger in whatever amount, pound it to a mash, and pat it on. Another formula: in the winter month collect spoiled tangerines (huai-ju) in whatever amount, store them in a porcelain jar and seal it, and place it in a quiet place; after a long time they transform of themselves into clear water. When used, take that water and smear it on the affected area; the effect is miraculous.

## 瘤⿸疒贅驗方

### Source Text

<ins>瘤⿸疒贅驗方</ins>

川貝母不拘多少，嚼，時時敷之，不過月余，自潰而愈。

頭上打破，如雞子大，傷痕奇效方。

白麻石內有石筋、石線，取出研細末，敷滿，布包扎好，三日長肉生肌，屢驗。

### Literal Translation

Chew Sichuan fritillaria bulb in whatever amount and apply it from time to time; within a little over a month it ruptures by itself and heals.

A miraculously effective formula for a wound where the head has been broken open, large as a hen's egg.

Inside white ma-stone (bai-ma-shi) there are stone-veins and stone-threads. Take them out, grind them into a fine powder, apply it so it fills the wound, and wrap it well with cloth. In three days it grows flesh and generates muscle; it has been repeatedly proven.
