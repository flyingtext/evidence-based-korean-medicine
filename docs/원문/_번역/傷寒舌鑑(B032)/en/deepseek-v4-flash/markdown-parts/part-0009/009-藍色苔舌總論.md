## 藍色苔舌總論

### Source Text

藍色苔者。乃肝木之色發見於外也。傷寒病久。已經汗下。胃氣已傷。致心火無氣。胃土無依。肺無所生。木無所畏。故乘膈上而見純藍色。是金木相併。火土氣絕之候。是以必死。如微藍、或稍見藍紋。猶可用溫胃健脾。調肝益肺藥治之。如純藍色者。是肝木獨盛無畏。雖無他證。必死。

舌見純藍色。中土陽氣衰微。百不一生之候。切勿用藥。

舌見藍紋。乃胃土氣衰。木氣相乘之候。小柴胡去黃芩、加炮薑。若因寒物結滯。急宜附子理中、大建中湯。

### Literal Translation

A blue coating is the color of the liver wood appearing externally. In cold damage, when the illness is long, and sweating and purging have already been done, and the stomach qi is already injured, causing the heart fire to have no qi, the stomach earth to have nothing to rely on, the lung to have nothing to generate, and the wood to have nothing to fear, therefore it rides above the diaphragm and shows a pure blue color. It is the sign of metal and wood combining, and the qi of fire and earth being exhausted. Therefore death is certain. If it is slightly blue, or slightly blue striations are seen, one may still use medicines that warm the stomach and fortify the spleen, and regulate the liver and boost the lung. If it is a pure blue color, it is the liver wood alone flourishing without fear. Although there are no other symptoms, death is certain.

*微藍舌\pt108a113.bmp*

When the tongue shows a pure blue color, it is the sign of the yang qi of the middle earth being faint and failing, a sign where not one in a hundred lives. One must not use medicine.

*藍紋舌\pt108a114.bmp*

When blue striations are seen on the tongue, it is the sign of the qi of the stomach earth failing and the wood qi riding on it. [Use] Xiao Chaihu (Minor Bupleurum) with Huangqin (Scutellaria) removed and roasted ginger added. If it is caused by cold things binding and stagnating, urgently use Fuzi Lizhong (Aconite Regulate the Middle) and Da Jianzhong tang (Major Construct the Middle Decoction).
