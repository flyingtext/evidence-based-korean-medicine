## 黃苔舌總論

### Source Text

黃苔者。裡證也。傷寒初病無此舌。傳至少陽經。亦無此舌。直至陽明府實。胃中火盛。火乘土位。故有此苔。當分輕重瀉之。初則微黃。次則深黃有滑。甚則干黃焦黃也。其證有大熱、大渴、便秘、譫語、痞結、自利。或因失汗發黃。或蓄血如狂。皆濕熱太盛。小便不利所致。若目白如金。身黃如橘。宜茵陳蒿湯，五苓散。梔子柏皮湯等。如蓄血在上焦。犀角地黃湯。中焦、桃仁承氣湯。下焦，代抵當湯。凡血證見血則愈。切不可與冷水。飲之必死。大抵舌黃證雖重。若脈長者。中土有氣也。下之則安。如脈弦下利、舌苔黃中有黑色者。皆危證也。

舌見干黃。裡熱已極。急下勿緩。下後脈靜身涼者生。反大熱而喘脈躁者死。

*黃苔中黑通尖舌\pt108a35.bmp*

*黃尖舌\pt108a37.bmp*

*黃苔灰根舌\pt108a38.bmp*

*黃尖紅根舌\pt108a39.bmp*

*黃大脹滿舌\pt108a42.bmp*

舌黃而脹大者。乃陽明胃經濕熱也。證必身黃、便秘、煩躁。茵陳蒿湯。如大便自利而發黃者。五苓散加茵陳、梔子、黃連等治之。

*黃尖白根舌\pt108a43.bmp*

舌根白尖黃。其色倒見。必是少陽經傳陽明府病。若陽明證多者。大柴胡湯。少陽證多者。小柴胡湯。如譫語煩躁者。調胃承氣湯。

*黃根白尖舌\pt108a44.bmp*

舌尖白根黃。乃表邪少而裡邪多也。天水散、涼膈散合用。如陽明無汗、小便不利、心中懊憹者。必發黃。

茵陳蒿湯。

*黃根灰尖舌\pt108a45.bmp*

舌乃火位。今見根黃尖灰。是土來侮火也。不吐不利、心煩而渴者。乃胃中有鬱熱也。調胃承氣加黃連。

*黃根白尖短縮舌\pt108a46.bmp*

舌見根黃尖白而短硬。不燥不滑。但不能伸出。證多譫妄煩亂。此痰挾宿食占據中宮也。大承氣加薑、半主之。

### Literal Translation

A yellow coating is an interior pattern. In the initial disease of cold damage there is no such tongue. When it transmits to the shaoyang channel, there is also no such tongue. Only when it reaches the repletion of the yangming fu, and the fire in the stomach is exuberant, and fire rides the position of earth, is there therefore this coating. One should purge it according to the light and heavy [degree]. At first it is slightly yellow, next it is deep yellow with slipperiness, and in the extreme it is dry yellow and scorched yellow. Its symptoms include great heat, great thirst, constipation, delirious speech, glomus and oppression, and spontaneous diarrhea. Or because of loss of sweating there is yellowing, or there is blood accumulation with mania. These are all caused by damp-heat being too exuberant and urination being inhibited. If the whites of the eyes are like gold and the body is yellow like an orange, it is appropriate to use Yinchenhao tang (Oriental Wormwood Decoction), Wuling san (Five-Ingredient Powder with Poria), Zhizi Baipi tang (Gardenia and Phellodendron Decoction), and the like. If the blood accumulation is in the upper burner, [use] Xijiao Dihuang tang (Rhinoceros Horn and Rehmannia Decoction). In the middle burner, [use] Taoren Chengqi tang (Peach Kernel Order the Qi Decoction). In the lower burner, [use] Dai Didang tang (Substitute the Resist Decoction). In all blood patterns, when blood is seen, [the patient] is cured. One must absolutely not give cold water; if drunk, death is certain. In general, although a yellow-tongue pattern is severe, if the pulse is long, the qi of the middle earth is present. Purging it will bring ease. If the pulse is wiry with diarrhea, and the tongue coating is yellow with black in it, these are all dangerous patterns.

*黃苔黑斑舌\pt108a34.bmp*

If the pulse is rough, with delirious speech, picking at the clothes and groping in the air, and the body is yellow with black spots, these are all not treated. If thin black stool is passed, there is death.

*黃苔中黑通尖舌\pt108a35.bmp*

In those with severe aversion to cold, death is also certain. If there is no aversion to cold, and the mouth is dry and the throat parched with diarrhea of foul water, one may use Tiaowei Chengqi tang (Regulate-Stomach, Order-Qi Decoction) to purge it. When the tongue is yellow, dry, and rough with separated petals, it is the evil heat entering the stomach, and the toxin binding already deep. *黃尖舌\pt108a37.bmp*

When the coating of the tip of the tongue is yellow, the heat evil has first transmitted to the stomach fu. One should use Tiaowei Chengqi tang (Regulate-Stomach, Order-Qi Decoction). If the pulse is floating with aversion to cold, the exterior pattern is not exhausted; use Da Chaihu (Major Bupleurum) to resolve both.

If there is no vexation-agitation or staring straight ahead, and the pulse is sunken and forceful, treat it with Da Chaihu (Major Bupleurum) with additions and subtractions.

When the root is red and the tip is yellow, it is damp-heat riding the position of fire. In the initial disease of warm heat, there is often this tongue. Use Liangge (Cool the Diaphragm) and Jiedu (Detoxification) and such medicines, treating according to the circumstances.

When the coating of the tongue is extremely old yellow with black thorns in it, it is all caused by loss of sweating. The evil toxin has already sunk deep within. Urgently use Tiaowei Chengqi (Regulate-Stomach, Order-Qi) to purge it. One or two in ten may be preserved.

Use Tianshui san (Celestial Water Powder) and Liangge san (Cool the Diaphragm Powder) together. If yangming has no sweating, inhibited urination, and vexation and agitation in the heart, there must be yellowing. [Use] Yinchenhao tang (Oriental Wormwood Decoction).

*黃根灰尖舌\pt108a45.bmp*

The tongue is the position of fire. Now seeing a yellow root and gray tip is earth coming to insult fire. If there is no vomiting or diarrhea, with vexation in the heart and thirst, it is depressed heat in the stomach. [Use] Tiaowei Chengqi (Regulate-Stomach, Order-Qi) with Huanglian (Coptis) added.
