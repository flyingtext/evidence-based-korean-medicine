## 黴醬色苔舌總論

### Source Text

黴醬色苔者。乃夾食傷寒。一二日間即有此舌。為寒傷太陰。食停胃腑之證。輕者苔色亦薄。雖腹痛。不下利。桂枝湯加橘、半，枳、朴。痛甚加大黃。冷食不消加乾薑、厚朴。其苔色厚而腹痛甚不止者。必危。舌見醬色。乃黃兼黑色。為土邪傳水。證必唇口乾燥大渴。雖用下奪。鮮有得愈者。

*純黴醬色舌\pt108a110.bmp*

舌見黴色。乃飲食填塞於胃。復為寒邪鬱遏。內熱不得外泄。濕氣熏蒸。罨而變此色也。其脈多沉緊。其人必煩躁腹痛。五七日下之不通者。必死。太陰少陰氣絕也。

*中黴浮厚舌\pt108a111.bmp*

傷寒不戒葷膩。致苔如醬餅浮於舌中。乃食滯中宮之象。如脈有胃氣。不結代。嘴不尖。齒不燥。不下利者。可用枳實理中湯、加薑汁炒川連。若舌苔揩去復長仍前者。必難救也。

*黴色中黃苔舌\pt108a112.bmp*

舌黴色中有黃苔。乃濕熱之物鬱滯中宮也。二陳加枳實、黃連。若苔干黃。更加酒大黃下之。

### Literal Translation

A moldy-sauce-colored coating is cold damage with food mixed [in]. Within one or two days there is already this tongue. It is a pattern of cold injuring the greater yin and food stopping in the stomach fu. In light cases the coating color is also thin. Although there is abdominal pain, there is no diarrhea. Use Guizhi tang (Cinnamon Twig Decoction) with tangerine peel, pinellia, unripe bitter orange, and magnolia bark added. If the pain is severe, add Dahuang (rhubarb). If cold food is not digested, add dried ginger and magnolia bark. If the coating color is thick and the abdominal pain is severe and does not stop, there must be danger. When the tongue shows a sauce color, it is yellow combined with black, the earth evil transmitting to water. The symptoms must be dry lips and mouth with great thirst. Even if one uses purging and seizing, few obtain recovery.

*純黴醬色舌\pt108a110.bmp*

When the tongue shows a moldy color, it is food and drink filling and stopping in the stomach, again depressed and blocked by the cold evil, so that the internal heat cannot be discharged outward, and the damp qi steams and fumigates, being covered and transforming into this color. Its pulse is mostly sunken and tight. The person must have vexation-agitation and abdominal pain. If at five or seven days purging does not pass, death is certain. The qi of the greater yin and lesser yin is exhausted.

*中黴浮厚舌\pt108a111.bmp*

In cold damage, if one does not abstain from meat and greasy [food], so that the coating floats in the middle of the tongue like a sauce cake, it is the image of food stagnation in the middle palace. If the pulse has stomach qi, is not knotted or intermittent, the mouth is not pointed, the teeth are not dry, and there is no diarrhea, one may use Zhishi Lizhong tang (Unripe Bitter Orange Regulate the Middle Decoction) with ginger-juice-fried Chuanlian (Sichuan coptis) added. If the tongue coating, when wiped away, grows back the same as before, it must be difficult to save.

*黴色中黃苔舌\pt108a112.bmp*

When the tongue is moldy-colored with a yellow coating in the middle, it is damp-heat things depressed and stagnant in the middle palace. [Use] Erchen (Two Aged [Ingredients]) with Zhishi (unripe bitter orange) and Huanglian (Coptis) added. If the coating is dry yellow, further add wine-prepared Dahuang (rhubarb) to purge it.
