## 白苔舌總論

### Source Text

傷寒邪在皮毛。初則舌有白沫。次則白涎白滑。再次白屑白疱。有舌中、舌尖、舌根之不同。是寒邪入經之微甚也。

舌乃心之苗。心屬南方火。當赤色。今反見白色者。是火不能制金也。初則寒鬱皮膚。毛竅不得疏通。熱氣不得外泄。

故惡寒發熱。在太陽經。則頭痛、身熱，項背強、腰脊疼、等症。傳至陽明經。則有白屑滿舌。雖症有煩躁。如脈浮緊者。猶當汗之。在少陽經者。則白苔白滑。用小柴胡湯和之。胃虛者。理中湯溫之。如白色少變黃者。大柴胡。大小承氣、分輕重下之。白舌亦有死症。不可忽視也。

*微白滑苔舌\pt108a1.bmp*

寒邪初入太陽。頭疼、身熱、惡寒、舌色微白有津。香蘇散、羌活湯之類發散之。

*薄白滑苔舌\pt108a2.bmp*

此太陽裡證舌也。二三日未曾汗。故邪入丹田漸深。急宜汗之。或太陽與少陽合病。有此舌者。柴胡桂枝湯主之。

*厚白滑苔舌\pt108a3.bmp*

病三四日。其邪只在太陽。故苔純白而厚。卻不干燥。其證頭疼發熱。脈浮而緊。解表自愈。

*干厚白苔舌\pt108a4.bmp*

病四五日。未經發汗。邪熱漸深。少有微渴。過飲生冷。停積胸中。營熱胃冷。故令發熱煩躁。四肢逆冷。

而苔白干厚。滿口白屑。宜四逆散加乾薑。

*白苔黃心舌\pt108a5.bmp*

此太陽經初傳陽明府病舌也。若微黃而潤。宜再汗。待苔燥裡證具。則下之。若煩躁嘔吐。大柴胡湯加減。

亦有下淡黃水沫。無稀糞者。大承氣湯下之。

*白苔黃邊舌\pt108a6.bmp*

舌中見白苔。外有微黃者。必作泄。宜用解毒湯。惡寒者五苓散。

*干白苔黑心舌\pt108a7.bmp*

此陽明府兼太陽舌，其苔邊白中心干黑者。因汗不徹。傳至陽明所致。必微汗出、不惡寒、脈沉者。可下之。如二三日未曾汗。有此舌必死。

*白滑苔尖灰刺舌\pt108a8.bmp*

此陽明府兼少陽舌也。三四日自利脈長者生。弦數者死。如有宿食。用大承氣下之。十可全五。

*白苔滿黑刺干舌\pt108a9.bmp*

白苔中生滿干黑芒刺。乃少陽之裡證也。其證不惡寒反惡熱者。大柴胡加芒硝急下之。然亦危證也。

*白滑苔黑心舌\pt108a10.bmp*

白苔中黑。為表那入裡之候。大熱譫語。承氣等下之。倘食復而發熱、或利不止者。難治。

*半邊白滑舌\pt108a11.bmp*

白苔見於一邊。無論左右。皆屬半表半裡。宜小柴胡湯。左加葛根。右加茯苓。有咳嗽引脅下痛、而見此舌苔者。小青龍湯。夏月多汗自利。人參白虎湯。

*臟結白滑舌\pt108a12.bmp*

或左或右。半邊白苔。半邊或黑或老黃者。寒邪結在臟也。黃連湯加附子。結在咽者。不能語言。宜生脈散合四逆湯。可救十中一二。

*白苔黑斑舌\pt108a13.bmp*

白苔中有黑小斑點亂生者。乃水來剋火。如無惡候。以涼膈散承氣湯下之。十中可救一二。

*白苔燥裂舌\pt108a14.bmp*

傷寒胸中有寒。丹田有熱。所以舌上白苔。因過汗傷營。舌上無津。所以燥裂。內無實熱。故不黃黑。宜小柴胡加芒硝微利之。

*白苔黑根舌\pt108a15.bmp*

舌苔白而根黑；火被水剋之象。雖下亦難見功也。

*白尖黃根舌\pt108a16.bmp*

邪雖入裡。而尖白未黃。不可用承氣。宜大柴胡湯加減。下後無他證。安臥神清。可生。倘再有變證。多凶。

*白苔雙黃舌\pt108a17.bmp*

此陽明裡證舌也。黃乃土之色。因邪熱上攻。致令舌有雙黃。如脈長惡熱。轉矢氣煩躁者。大柴胡調胃承氣下之。

*白苔雙黑舌\pt108a18.bmp*

白苔中見黑色兩條。乃太陽少陽之邪入於胃。因土氣衰絕。故手足厥冷。胸中結痛也。理中湯瀉心湯選用。

如邪結在舌根。咽嗌而不能言者。死證也。

*白苔雙灰色舌\pt108a19.bmp*

此夾冷食舌也。七八日後見此舌而有津者。可治。理中四逆選用。無津者不治。如干厚見裡證。則下之。

得湯次日灰色去者安。

*白尖中紅黑根舌\pt108a20.bmp*

舌尖白而根灰黑。少陽邪熱傳腑。熱極而傷冷飲也。如水停津液固結而渴者。五苓散。自汗而渴者。白虎湯。

下利而渴者。解毒湯。如黑根多、白尖少、中不甚紅者。難治。

*白苔尖紅舌\pt108a21.bmp*

滿舌白滑而尖卻鮮紅者。乃熱邪內盛。而復感客寒入少陽經也。小柴胡湯加減。

*白苔中紅舌\pt108a22.bmp*

此太陽初傳經之舌也。無汗者發汗。有汗者解肌。亦有少陽經者。小柴胡湯加減。

*白苔變黃舌\pt108a23.bmp*

少陽證罷。初見陽明裡證。故苔變黃色。兼矢氣者。大柴胡湯下之。

*白尖紅根舌\pt108a24.bmp*

舌尖苔白。邪在半表半裡也。其證寒熱、耳聾、口苦、脅痛、脈弦。小柴胡湯和解之。

*白苔尖灰根黃舌\pt108a25.bmp*

此太陽濕熱並於陽明也。如根黃色潤、目黃小便黃者。茵陳蒿湯加減。

*白苔尖根俱黑舌\pt108a26.bmp*

舌根尖俱黑而中白。乃金水太過。火土氣絕於內。雖無凶證。亦必死也。

*熟白舌\pt108a27.bmp*

白苔老極。如煮熟相似者。心氣絕而肺色乘於上也，始因食瓜果冰水等物。陽氣不得發越所致。為必死候。

用枳實、理中。間有生者。

*淡白透明舌\pt108a28.bmp*

年老胃弱。雖有風寒。不能變熱。或多服湯藥。傷其胃氣。所以淡白透明。似苔非苔也。宜補中益氣加減治之。

*白苔如積粉舌\pt108a29.bmp*

此舌乃瘟疫初犯募原也。達原飲。見三陽表證。隨經加柴胡、葛根、羌活。見裡證。加大黃。

### Literal Translation

In cold damage, when the evil is in the skin and hair, at first the tongue has white froth, next white saliva that is white and slippery, and again white flakes and white blisters. There are differences of the middle of the tongue, the tip of the tongue, and the root of the tongue. These are the slight and severe [degrees] of the cold evil entering the channels.

The tongue is the sprout of the heart. The heart belongs to the fire of the south. It should be red in color. That it now instead shows a white color is because fire is unable to control metal. At first the cold is depressed in the skin, the pores cannot be opened and freed, and the heat qi cannot be discharged outward.

Therefore there is aversion to cold and fever. In the taiyang channel, there are headache, body heat, stiffness of the nape and back, and pain of the lumbar spine, and such symptoms. When it transmits to the yangming channel, then there are white flakes filling the tongue. Although the symptoms include vexation and agitation, if the pulse is floating and tight, one should still sweat it. In the taiyang... in the shaoyang channel, then there is a white coating that is white and slippery; use Xiao Chaihu tang (Minor Bupleurum Decoction) to harmonize it. For those with vacuous stomach, use Lizhong tang (Regulate the Middle Decoction) to warm it. If the white color slightly changes to yellow, use Da Chaihu (Major Bupleurum) and the Da and Xiao Chengqi [decoctions], purging according to the light and heavy [degree]. A white tongue also has fatal patterns; it must not be regarded lightly.

*微白滑苔舌\pt108a1.bmp*

When the cold evil first enters taiyang, with headache, body heat, aversion to cold, and a tongue color slightly white with moisture, use Xiangsu san (Cyperus and Perilla Powder), Qianghuo tang (Notopterygium Decoction), and the like to disperse and effuse.

*薄白滑苔舌\pt108a2.bmp*

This is the tongue of the interior pattern of taiyang. For two or three days there has been no sweating, and so the evil enters the elixir field and gradually deepens. It should be sweated urgently. Or if there is a combined disease of taiyang and shaoyang, and there is this tongue, use Chaihu Guizhi tang (Bupleurum and Cinnamon Twig Decoction) to govern it.

*厚白滑苔舌\pt108a3.bmp*

When the illness is three or four days, its evil is only in taiyang, and so the coating is purely white and thick, yet it is not dry. Its symptoms are headache and fever, and the pulse is floating and tight. Resolving the exterior will heal it by itself.

*干厚白苔舌\pt108a4.bmp*

When the illness is four or five days and has not yet been sweated, the evil heat gradually deepens, and there is a slight thirst. Drinking too much raw and cold [drink] causes it to accumulate and stop in the chest. The nutritive [qi] is hot and the stomach is cold, and so it causes fever and vexation-agitation, and the four limbs are reversed and cold.

And the coating is white, dry, and thick, with white flakes filling the mouth. It is appropriate to use Sini san (Frigid Extremities Powder) with dried ginger added.

*白苔黃心舌\pt108a5.bmp*

This is the tongue of the disease of the yangming fu when the taiyang channel first transmits to it. If it is slightly yellow and moist, one should sweat again. When the coating is dry and the interior pattern is complete, then purge it. If there is vexation-agitation and vomiting, use Da Chaihu tang (Major Bupleurum Decoction) with additions and subtractions.

There are also those who pass down light yellow water and froth, without thin stool. Use Da Chengqi tang (Major Order the Qi Decoction) to purge it.

*白苔黃邊舌\pt108a6.bmp*

When white coating is seen in the middle of the tongue, and there is slight yellow on the outside, there will certainly be diarrhea. It is appropriate to use Jiedu tang (Detoxification Decoction). For those with aversion to cold, use Wuling san (Five-Ingredient Powder with Poria).

*干白苔黑心舌\pt108a7.bmp*

This is a tongue of the yangming fu combined with taiyang. When the coating is white at the edges and dry and black in the center, it is caused by sweating that was not thorough, transmitting to yangming. If there is slight sweating, no aversion to cold, and a sunken pulse, one may purge it. If for two or three days there has been no sweating, and there is this tongue, death is certain.

*白滑苔尖灰刺舌\pt108a8.bmp*

This is a tongue of the yangming fu combined with shaoyang. If after three or four days there is spontaneous diarrhea and a long pulse, there is life. If the pulse is wiry and rapid, there is death. If there is retained food, use Da Chengqi (Major Order the Qi Decoction) to purge it; ten [cases] may be saved in five.

*白苔滿黑刺干舌\pt108a9.bmp*

When dry black awn-thorns grow all over within a white coating, this is the interior pattern of shaoyang. If its symptoms are no aversion to cold but instead aversion to heat, use Da Chaihu (Major Bupleurum) with mirabilite added to purge it urgently. Yet it is also a dangerous pattern.

*白滑苔黑心舌\pt108a10.bmp*

When there is black in the middle of a white coating, it is the sign of the exterior evil entering the interior. With great heat and delirious speech, use Chengqi and the like to purge it. If there is fever from relapse of eating, or diarrhea that does not stop, it is difficult to treat.

*半邊白滑舌\pt108a11.bmp*

When a white coating appears on one side, whether left or right, it all belongs to half exterior and half interior. It is appropriate to use Xiao Chaihu tang (Minor Bupleurum Decoction). If on the left, add Pueraria root; if on the right, add Poria. If there is cough drawing pain below the ribs, and this tongue coating is seen, [use] Xiao Qinglong tang (Minor Green-Blue Dragon Decoction). In summer months, with much sweating and spontaneous diarrhea, [use] Renshen Baihu tang (Ginseng and White Tiger Decoction).

*臟結白滑舌\pt108a12.bmp*

When on either the left or the right, half of the coating is white, and the other half is black or old yellow, the cold evil is bound in the viscera. Use Huanglian tang (Coptis Decoction) with aconite added. If it is bound in the throat, [the patient] cannot speak. It is appropriate to use Shengmai san (Generate the Pulse Powder) combined with Sini tang (Frigid Extremities Decoction). One or two in ten may be saved.

*白苔黑斑舌\pt108a13.bmp*

When small black spots grow in disorder within a white coating, it is water coming to overcome fire. If there are no ominous signs, purge it with Liangge san (Cool the Diaphragm Powder) and Chengqi tang (Order the Qi Decoction). One or two in ten may be saved.

*白苔燥裂舌\pt108a14.bmp*

In cold damage, when there is cold in the chest and heat in the elixir field, therefore the tongue has a white coating. Because excessive sweating injures the nutritive [qi], the tongue has no moisture, and so it is dry and fissured. There is no true heat in the interior, and so it is not yellow or black. It is appropriate to use Xiao Chaihu (Minor Bupleurum) with mirabilite added to mildly purge it.

*白苔黑根舌\pt108a15.bmp*

When the tongue coating is white and the root is black, it is the image of fire being overcome by water. Even if purged, it is difficult to see effect.

*白尖黃根舌\pt108a16.bmp*

Although the evil has entered the interior, if the tip is white and not yet yellow, one must not use Chengqi. It is appropriate to use Da Chaihu tang (Major Bupleurum Decoction) with additions and subtractions. If after purging there are no other symptoms, and [the patient] sleeps peacefully with a clear spirit, there can be life. If there are further transformed patterns, it is mostly ominous.

*白苔雙黃舌\pt108a17.bmp*

This is the tongue of the interior pattern of yangming. Yellow is the color of earth. Because the evil heat attacks upward, it causes the tongue to have double yellow. If the pulse is long with aversion to heat, and there is passing of gas and vexation-agitation, use Da Chaihu and Tiaowei Chengqi (Regulate-Stomach, Order-Qi) to purge it.

*白苔雙黑舌\pt108a18.bmp*

When two black strips are seen within a white coating, it is the evil of taiyang and shaoyang entering the stomach. Because the qi of earth is exhausted, therefore the hands and feet are reversed and cold, and there is binding pain in the chest. Use Lizhong tang (Regulate the Middle Decoction) or Xiexin tang (Drain the Heart Decoction), selecting [one].

If the evil is bound at the root of the tongue, and the throat is blocked so that [the patient] cannot speak, it is a fatal pattern.

*白苔雙灰色舌\pt108a19.bmp*

This is the tongue of cold food mixed [in]. If after seven or eight days this tongue is seen and has moisture, it can be treated. Use Lizhong (Regulate the Middle) or Sini (Frigid Extremities), selecting [one]. If there is no moisture, it is not treated. If it is dry and thick with an interior pattern, then purge it.

If after taking the decoction, on the next day the gray color departs, [the patient] is at ease.

*白尖中紅黑根舌\pt108a20.bmp*

When the tip of the tongue is white and the root is gray-black, the evil heat of shaoyang transmits to the fu. The heat is extreme and injures cold drink. If water stops and the fluids are fixed and bound, causing thirst, [use] Wuling san (Five-Ingredient Powder with Poria). If there is spontaneous sweating and thirst, [use] Baihu tang (White Tiger Decoction).

If there is diarrhea and thirst, [use] Jiedu tang (Detoxification Decoction). If the black root is much, the white tip is little, and the middle is not very red, it is difficult to treat.

*白苔尖紅舌\pt108a21.bmp*

When the whole tongue is white and slippery but the tip is fresh red, it is the evil heat flourishing internally, and moreover contracting guest cold entering the shaoyang channel. Use Xiao Chaihu tang (Minor Bupleurum Decoction) with additions and subtractions.

*白苔中紅舌\pt108a22.bmp*

This is the tongue of the taiyang channel when it first transmits. If there is no sweating, effuse sweat. If there is sweating, resolve the flesh. There are also those of the shaoyang channel; use Xiao Chaihu tang (Minor Bupleurum Decoction) with additions and subtractions.

*白苔變黃舌\pt108a23.bmp*

When the shaoyang pattern has ended and the interior pattern of yangming first appears, therefore the coating changes to a yellow color. If there is also passing of gas, use Da Chaihu tang (Major Bupleurum Decoction) to purge it.

*白尖紅根舌\pt108a24.bmp*

When the coating of the tip of the tongue is white, the evil is in half exterior and half interior. Its symptoms are cold and heat, deafness, bitter mouth, rib pain, and a wiry pulse. Use Xiao Chaihu tang (Minor Bupleurum Decoction) to harmonize and resolve it.

*白苔尖灰根黃舌\pt108a25.bmp*

This is the damp-heat of taiyang combining with yangming. If the root is yellow and moist, and the eyes are yellow and the urine is yellow, use Yinchenhao tang (Oriental Wormwood Decoction) with additions and subtractions.

*白苔尖根俱黑舌\pt108a26.bmp*

When both the root and the tip of the tongue are black and the middle is white, it is metal and water being excessive, and the qi of fire and earth being exhausted within. Although there are no ominous signs, death is also certain.

*熟白舌\pt108a27.bmp*

When the white coating is extremely old, resembling something cooked, it is the qi of the heart being exhausted and the color of the lung riding on top. It is initially caused by eating melons, fruits, ice water, and such things, so that the yang qi cannot be effused and released. It is a sign of certain death.

Use Zhishi (unripe bitter orange) and Lizhong (Regulate the Middle). Occasionally there are those who live.

*淡白透明舌\pt108a28.bmp*

In the aged, when the stomach is weak, although there is wind-cold, it cannot transform into heat. Or if one has taken much decoction medicine, injuring the stomach qi, therefore [the tongue] is pale white and transparent, like a coating yet not a coating. It is appropriate to treat it with Buzhong Yiqi (Supplement the Middle and Boost Qi) with additions and subtractions.

*白苔如積粉舌\pt108a29.bmp*

This tongue is when pestilence first attacks the membrane source. [Use] Dayuan yin (Reach the Membrane Source Drink). If the exterior patterns of the three yang are seen, add Chaihu (Bupleurum), Gegen (Pueraria), and Qianghuo (Notopterygium) according to the channel. If an interior pattern is seen, add Dahuang (rhubarb).
