## 灰色舌總論

### Source Text

灰色舌、有陰陽之異。若直中陰經。則即時舌便灰黑而無積苔。若熱傳三陰。必四五日表證罷而苔變灰色也。有在根在尖在中者。有渾舌俱灰黑者。大抵傳經熱證。則有灰黑干苔。皆當攻下泄熱。若直中三陰之灰黑無苔者。即當溫經散寒。又有蓄血證。其人如狂。或瞑目譫語。亦有不狂不語。不知人事。而面黑舌灰者。當分輕重以攻其血。切勿誤與冷水。引領敗血入心而致不救也。

*純灰舌\pt108a61.bmp*

舌灰色無苔者。直中三陰而夾冷食也。脈必沉細而遲。不渴不煩者。

附子理中四逆湯救之。次日。舌變灰中有微黃色者生。如漸漸灰縮干黑者死。

*灰中舌\pt108a62.bmp*

灰色現於中央。而消渴、氣上衝心、飢不欲食、食即吐蛔者。此熱傳厥陰之候。烏梅丸主之。

*灰黑苔干紋裂舌\pt108a63.bmp*

土邪勝水。而舌見灰黑紋裂。涼膈調胃皆可下之。十中可救二三。下後。渴不止熱不退者。不治。

*灰根黃尖中赤舌\pt108a64.bmp*

舌根灰色而中紅尖黃。乃腸胃燥熱之證。若大渴譫語。五六日不大便。轉矢氣者。下之。如溫病熱病。惡寒脈浮者。涼膈、雙解選用。

*灰色重暈舌\pt108a65.bmp*

此瘟病熱毒。傳遍三陰也。熱毒傳內一次。舌即灰暈一層。毒盛故有重暈。最危之證。急宜涼膈、雙解解毒。承氣下之。一暈尚輕。二暈為重。三暈必死。亦有橫紋二三層者。與此重暈不殊。

*灰黑干刺舌\pt108a66.bmp*

灰黑舌中又有干刺。而見咽乾、口燥、喘滿。乃邪熱結於少陰。當下之。然必待其轉矢氣者。方可下。若下之早。令人小便難。

*灰黑尖舌\pt108a67.bmp*

已經汗解而見舌尖灰黑。有宿食未消。或又傷飲食。邪熱復盛之故。調胃承氣湯下之。

*灰黑尖干刺舌\pt108a68.bmp*

舌尖灰黑有刺而干。是得病後猶加飲食之故。雖證見耳聾、脅痛、發熱、口苦。不得用小柴胡。必大柴胡或調胃承氣加消導藥。方可取效。

*灰中墨滑舌\pt108a69.bmp*

淡淡灰色中間。有滑苔四五點如墨汁。此熱邪傳裡。而中有宿食未化也。大柴胡湯。

*灰黑多黃根少舌\pt108a70.bmp*

舌灰色而根黃。乃熱傳厥陰。而胃中復有停滯也。傷寒六七日不利。便發熱而利、汗出不止者死。正氣脫也。

*邊灰中紫舌\pt108a71.bmp*

舌邊灰黑而中淡紫。時時自齧舌尖為爽。乃少陰厥氣逆上。非藥可治。

### Literal Translation

A gray tongue has the difference of yin and yang. If it directly strikes the yin channels, then at once the tongue becomes gray-black without accumulated coating. If heat transmits to the three yin, then only after four or five days, when the exterior pattern has ended, does the coating change to gray. There are those at the root, at the tip, and in the middle, and those where the whole tongue is gray-black. In general, in the heat patterns of channel transmission, there is a gray-black dry coating, and one should all attack and purge to drain heat. If in direct striking of the three yin there is gray-black without coating, then one should warm the channels and dissipate cold. There are also blood accumulation patterns, where the person is like a maniac, or closes the eyes and speaks deliriously. There are also those who are neither manic nor speaking, and do not know people or affairs, with a black face and gray tongue. One should attack their blood according to the light and heavy [degree]. One must not mistakenly give cold water, leading the defeated blood into the heart and causing [the patient] to be beyond rescue.

*純灰舌\pt108a61.bmp*

When the tongue is gray without coating, it is direct striking of the three yin with cold food mixed [in]. The pulse must be sunken, fine, and slow, without thirst and without vexation. Use Fuzi Lizhong (Aconite Regulate the Middle) and Sini tang (Frigid Extremities Decoction) to rescue it. On the next day, if the tongue changes to gray with a slight yellow color in it, there is life. If it gradually becomes gray, contracted, dry, and black, there is death.

*灰中舌\pt108a62.bmp*

When gray appears in the center, with wasting thirst, qi rushing up to the heart, hunger without desire to eat, and vomiting of roundworms immediately after eating, it is the sign of heat transmitting to the jueyin. Wumei wan (Mume Pill) governs it.

*灰黑苔干紋裂舌\pt108a63.bmp*

When the earth evil overcomes water, and the tongue shows gray-black with dry striations and fissures, both Liangge (Cool the Diaphragm) and Tiaowei (Regulate-Stomach) may purge it. Two or three in ten may be saved. If after purging the thirst does not stop and the heat does not recede, it is not treated.

*灰根黃尖中赤舌\pt108a64.bmp*

When the root of the tongue is gray and the middle is red and the tip is yellow, it is a pattern of dryness-heat of the intestines and stomach. If there is great thirst and delirious speech, and no stool for five or six days, with passing of gas, purge it. If in warm disease and heat disease, with aversion to cold and a floating pulse, select from Liangge (Cool the Diaphragm) and Shuangjie (Double Resolve).

*灰色重暈舌\pt108a65.bmp*

This is the heat toxin of warm disease transmitting throughout the three yin. Each time the heat toxin transmits once within, the tongue gains one layer of gray halo. Because the toxin is exuberant, there are therefore repeated halos. It is the most dangerous pattern. Urgently use Liangge (Cool the Diaphragm), Shuangjie (Double Resolve), and Jiedu (Detoxification), and Chengqi (Order the Qi) to purge it. One halo is still light, two halos are severe, and three halos mean certain death. There are also those with two or three layers of horizontal striations; these are not different from the repeated halos.

*灰黑干刺舌\pt108a66.bmp*

When within the gray-black tongue there are again dry thorns, and there is dry throat, dry mouth, and panting and fullness, it is the evil heat binding in the lesser yin. One should purge it. Yet one must wait for the passing of gas before one may purge. If one purges too early, it causes difficulty in urination.

*灰黑尖舌\pt108a67.bmp*

When, after already sweating and resolving, a gray-black tip of the tongue is seen, there is retained food not yet digested, or [the patient] has again injured [the stomach] with food and drink, and the evil heat again flourishes. Use Tiaowei Chengqi tang (Regulate-Stomach, Order-Qi Decoction) to purge it.

*灰黑尖干刺舌\pt108a68.bmp*

When the tip of the tongue is gray-black with thorns and dry, it is because after contracting the illness [the patient] still added food and drink. Although the symptoms show deafness, rib pain, fever, and bitter mouth, one must not use Xiao Chaihu (Minor Bupleurum). One must use Da Chaihu (Major Bupleurum) or Tiaowei Chengqi (Regulate-Stomach, Order-Qi) with digestion-guiding medicines added before effect can be obtained.

*灰中墨滑舌\pt108a69.bmp*

When within a faint gray there are four or five spots of slippery coating like ink juice, it is the heat evil transmitting to the interior, with retained food in the middle not yet transformed. [Use] Da Chaihu tang (Major Bupleurum Decoction).

*灰黑多黃根少舌\pt108a70.bmp*

When the tongue is gray with a yellow root, it is heat transmitting to the jueyin, with stagnation again stopping in the stomach. In cold damage at six or seven days without diarrhea, if there is then fever with diarrhea and sweating that does not stop, there is death. The upright qi is shedding.

*邊灰中紫舌\pt108a71.bmp*

When the edges of the tongue are gray-black and the middle is faint purple, and [the patient] frequently bites the tip of the tongue himself for relief, it is the jueyin qi rebelling upward. It is not treatable by medicine.
