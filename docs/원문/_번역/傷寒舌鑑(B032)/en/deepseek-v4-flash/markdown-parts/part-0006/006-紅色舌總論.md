## 紅色舌總論

### Source Text

夫紅舌者。伏熱內蓄於心胃。自裡而達於表也。仲景云。冬傷於寒。至春變為溫病。至夏變為熱病。故舌紅而赤。又有瘟疫疫癘。一方之內。老幼之病皆同者。舌亦正赤而加積苔也。若更多食。則助熱內蒸。故舌紅面赤。甚者面目俱赤而舌瘡也。然病有輕重。舌有微甚。且見於舌之根尖中下左右。瘡蝕脹爛。癟細長短。種種異形。皆瘟毒火熱蘊化之所為也。其所治亦不同。當解者內解其毒。當砭者砭去其血。若論湯液。無過大小承氣、黃連解毒、三黃石膏等。此類而推可也。

*純紅舌\pt108a72.bmp*

舌見純紅色。乃瘟疫之邪熱初蓄於內也。宜敗毒散加減。或升麻葛根湯等治之。

*紅中淡黑舌\pt108a73.bmp*

舌紅中見淡黑色而有滑者。乃太陽瘟疫也。如惡寒。有表證。雙解散合解毒湯微微汗之。汗罷急下。如結胸煩躁直視者。不治。

*紅中焦黑舌\pt108a74.bmp*

舌見紅色。中有黑形如小舌。乃瘟毒內結於胃。火極反兼水化也。宜涼膈散。若黑而干硬。以指甲刮之有聲者。急用調胃承氣湯下之。

*紅中黑斑舌\pt108a75.bmp*

見小黑斑星於紅舌上者。乃瘟熱乘虛入於陽明。胃熱則發斑也。或身上亦兼有紅赤斑者。宜黑參升麻湯、化斑湯等治之。

*紅內黑尖舌\pt108a76.bmp*

舌本紅而尖黑者。足少陰瘟熱乘於手少陰也。竹葉石膏湯。

*紅色人字紋裂舌\pt108a77.bmp*

舌紅甚而又有紋裂者。陽明熱毒熏蒸膈上。故現人字紋也。宜服涼膈散。如渴甚轉矢氣者。大承氣下之。

*紅斷紋裂舌\pt108a78.bmp*

相火來乘君位。致令舌紅燥而紋裂作痛。宜黃連解毒湯加麥門冬寒潤之。

*紅內紅星舌\pt108a79.bmp*

舌見淡紅色。又有大紅星點如瘡瘰者。濕熱傷於脾土。罨而欲發黃之候。宜茵陳蒿湯、五苓散選用。

*深紅蟲碎舌\pt108a80.bmp*

舌紅更有紅點。坑爛如蟲蝕之狀。乃水火不能既濟。熱毒熾盛也。不拘日數。宜小承氣湯下之。不退、再以大承氣下之。

*紅色紫瘡舌\pt108a81.bmp*

瘟疫多有此舌。其證不惡寒。便作渴煩躁。或咳痰者。宜解毒湯加黑參、薄荷。並益元散治之。尺脈無者必死。戰栗者亦死。

*紅中微黃根舌\pt108a82.bmp*

熱入陽明胃腑。故舌根微黃。若頭汗、身涼、小便難者。茵陳蒿湯加梔子、香豉。

*紅中微黃滑舌\pt108a83.bmp*

病五七日。舌中有黃苔。是陽明證。如脈沉實譫語。雖苔滑。宜大柴胡湯。若干燥者。此內邪熱盛。急用大承氣下之。

*紅長脹出口外舌\pt108a84.bmp*

舌長大脹出口外。是熱毒乘心。內服瀉心湯。外砭去惡血。再用片腦、人中黃摻舌上。即愈。

*紅餂舌\pt108a85.bmp*

舌頻出口為弄舌。餂至鼻尖上下或口角左右者。此為惡候。可用解毒湯加生地黃。效則生。不效則死。

*紅痿舌\pt108a86.bmp*

舌痿軟而不能動者。乃是心臟受傷。當參脈證施治。然亦十難救一也。

*紅硬舌\pt108a87.bmp*

舌根強硬失音。或邪結咽嗌以致不語者。死證也。如脈有神而外證輕者。可用清心降火去風痰藥。多有得生者。

*紅尖出血舌\pt108a88.bmp*

舌上出血如濺者。乃心臟邪熱壅盛所致。宜犀角地黃湯加大黃、黃連輩治之。

*紅中雙灰干舌\pt108a89.bmp*

瘟熱病而舌見兩路灰色。是病後復傷飲食所致。令人身熱譫語。循衣撮空。如脈滑者。一下便安。如脈澀下出黑糞者死。

*紅尖白根舌\pt108a90.bmp*

紅尖是本色。白苔為表邪。如惡寒、身熱、頭痛。宜汗之。不惡寒、身熱、煩渴者。此太陽裡證也。五苓散兩解之。

*紅戰舌\pt108a91.bmp*

舌戰者。顫掉不安。蠕蠕瞤動也。此證因汗多亡陽。或漏風所致。十全大補、大建中湯選用。

*紅細枯長舌\pt108a92.bmp*

舌色干紅而長細者。乃少陰之氣絕於內。而不上通於舌也。縱無他證。脈再衰絕。朝夕恐難保矣。

*紅短白疱舌\pt108a93.bmp*

口瘡舌短有疱。聲啞、咽乾、煩躁者。乃瘟疫強汗。或傷寒未汗而變此證。宜黃連犀角湯、三黃石膏湯選用。

*邊紅通尖黑干舌\pt108a94.bmp*

瘟病不知調治。或不禁飲食。或不服湯藥。而致舌心干黑。急下一二次。少解再下。以平為期。

*紅尖紫刺舌\pt108a95.bmp*

汗後食復而見紅尖紫刺。證甚危急。枳實梔子豉湯加大黃下之。仍刮去芒刺。不復生則安。再生則危。

*紅尖黑根舌\pt108a96.bmp*

瘟疫二三日。舌根灰黑。急用涼膈、雙解微下之。至四五日後。火極似水。漸變深黑。下無濟矣。若邪結於咽。目瞑脈絕油汗者。一二日內死。

*紅嫩無津舌\pt108a97.bmp*

汗下太過。津液耗竭。而舌色鮮紅柔嫩如新生。望之似潤。而實燥涸者。生脈散合人參三白湯治之。然多不應也。

### Literal Translation

As for the red tongue, it is hidden heat accumulating within the heart and stomach, reaching from the interior to the exterior. Zhongjing says: injured by cold in winter, transforming into warm disease by spring, and transforming into heat disease by summer. Therefore the tongue is red and crimson. There are also pestilence and epidemic diseases, where within one region the illnesses of old and young are all the same; the tongue is also properly red with accumulated coating. If one eats more, then it assists the heat to steam within, and so the tongue is red and the face is red. In the extreme, the face and eyes are both red with tongue sores. Yet the illness has light and severe [degrees], and the tongue has slight and extreme [degrees]. Moreover, [the changes] appear at the root, tip, middle, lower, left, and right of the tongue. Sores, erosion, swelling, and rottenness, emaciation, thinness, length, and shortness, all manner of different forms, are all the doings of the fire-heat of pestilence toxin accumulating and transforming. Its treatment is also not the same. When it should be resolved, resolve its toxin within; when it should be bled, bleed away its blood with a stone needle. If one discusses decoctions and liquids, none surpass the Da and Xiao Chengqi (Major and Minor Order the Qi), Huanglian Jiedu (Coptis Detoxification), Sanhuang Shigao (Three-Yellow and Gypsum), and the like. One may infer from this category.

*純紅舌\pt108a72.bmp*

When the tongue shows a pure red color, it is the evil heat of pestilence first accumulating within. It is appropriate to use Baidu san (Defeat Toxin Powder) with additions and subtractions, or Shengma Gegen tang (Cimicifuga and Pueraria Decoction) and the like to treat it.

*紅中淡黑舌\pt108a73.bmp*

When the tongue is red with a faint black color and slipperiness in the middle, it is the pestilence of taiyang. If there is aversion to cold, there is an exterior pattern; use Shuangjie san (Double Resolve Powder) combined with Jiedu tang (Detoxification Decoction) to sweat it slightly. When the sweating is done, purge urgently. If there is chest binding with vexation-agitation and staring straight ahead, it is not treated.

*紅中焦黑舌\pt108a74.bmp*

When the tongue shows a red color with a black form in the middle like a small tongue, it is the pestilence toxin binding within the stomach, fire in its extreme instead combining with the transformation of water. It is appropriate to use Liangge san (Cool the Diaphragm Powder). If it is black, dry, and hard, and scraping it with a fingernail makes a sound, urgently use Tiaowei Chengqi tang (Regulate-Stomach, Order-Qi Decoction) to purge it.

*紅中黑斑舌\pt108a75.bmp*

When small black spots like stars are seen on a red tongue, it is the warm heat, taking advantage of vacuity, entering yangming. When the stomach is hot, there is maculation. If the body also has red spots, it is appropriate to use Heishen Shengma tang (Scrophularia and Cimicifuga Decoction), Huaban tang (Transform Maculation Decoction), and the like to treat it.

*紅內黑尖舌\pt108a76.bmp*

When the body of the tongue is red and the tip is black, it is the warm heat of the foot lesser yin riding the hand lesser yin. [Use] Zhuye Shigao tang (Bamboo Leaf and Gypsum Decoction).

*紅色人字紋裂舌\pt108a77.bmp*

When the tongue is very red and moreover has striations and fissures, the heat toxin of yangming steams and fumigates above the diaphragm, and so the figure-eight striations appear. It is appropriate to take Liangge san (Cool the Diaphragm Powder). If there is extreme thirst with passing of gas, use Da Chengqi (Major Order the Qi Decoction) to purge it.

*紅斷紋裂舌\pt108a78.bmp*

When the minister fire comes to ride the sovereign position, it causes the tongue to be red and dry with striations and fissures causing pain. It is appropriate to use Huanglian Jiedu tang (Coptis Detoxification Decoction) with Maidong (Ophiopogon) added to cold-moisten it.

*紅內紅星舌\pt108a79.bmp*

When the tongue shows a faint red color with large red star points like sores and scrofula, it is damp-heat injuring the spleen earth, the sign of being covered and about to effuse yellowing. It is appropriate to select from Yinchenhao tang (Oriental Wormwood Decoction) and Wuling san (Five-Ingredient Powder with Poria).

*深紅蟲碎舌\pt108a80.bmp*

When the tongue is red with red points, pitted and rotten like the appearance of being eaten by insects, it is water and fire being unable to interact, and the heat toxin being blazing and exuberant. Regardless of the number of days, it is appropriate to use Xiao Chengqi tang (Minor Order the Qi Decoction) to purge it. If it does not recede, again use Da Chengqi (Major Order the Qi Decoction) to purge it.

*紅色紫瘡舌\pt108a81.bmp*

Pestilence often has this tongue. Its symptoms are no aversion to cold, then thirst, vexation, and agitation, or coughing phlegm. It is appropriate to use Jiedu tang (Detoxification Decoction) with Heishen (Scrophularia) and Bohe (Mint) added, and also Yiyuan san (Benefit the Origin Powder) to treat it. If the chi pulse is absent, death is certain. If there is shivering, death is also certain.

*紅中微黃根舌\pt108a82.bmp*

When heat enters the yangming stomach fu, therefore the root of the tongue is slightly yellow. If there is head sweating, a cool body, and difficult urination, [use] Yinchenhao tang (Oriental Wormwood Decoction) with Zhizi (Gardenia) and Xiangchi (fermented soybean) added.

*紅中微黃滑舌\pt108a83.bmp*

When the illness is five or seven days, and there is a yellow coating in the middle of the tongue, it is a yangming pattern. If the pulse is sunken and replete with delirious speech, although the coating is slippery, it is appropriate to use Da Chaihu tang (Major Bupleurum Decoction). If it is dry, this is the interior evil heat being exuberant; urgently use Da Chengqi (Major Order the Qi Decoction) to purge it.

*紅長脹出口外舌\pt108a84.bmp*

When the tongue is long, large, and distended, protruding out of the mouth, it is the heat toxin riding the heart. Internally take Xiexin tang (Drain the Heart Decoction); externally bleed away the evil blood with a stone needle. Then apply Piannao (borneol) and Renzhonghuang (urine sediment) on the tongue, and it will be cured.

*紅餂舌\pt108a85.bmp*

When the tongue frequently protrudes from the mouth, it is called playing with the tongue. When it licks up and down to the tip of the nose or to the left and right of the corners of the mouth, this is an ominous sign. One may use Jiedu tang (Detoxification Decoction) with Shengdihuang (raw Rehmannia) added. If effective, there is life; if not effective, there is death.

*紅痿舌\pt108a86.bmp*

When the tongue is flaccid and soft and unable to move, it is the heart viscus being injured. One should treat according to the pulse and symptoms. Yet it is also difficult to save one in ten.

*紅硬舌\pt108a87.bmp*

When the root of the tongue is hard and stiff with loss of voice, or the evil is bound in the throat so that [the patient] cannot speak, it is a fatal pattern. If the pulse has spirit and the exterior symptoms are light, one may use medicines that clear the heart, descend fire, and dispel wind-phlegm. Many obtain life.

*紅尖出血舌\pt108a88.bmp*

When blood issues from the tongue like splashing, it is caused by the evil heat of the heart viscus being congested and exuberant. It is appropriate to use Xijiao Dihuang tang (Rhinoceros Horn and Rehmannia Decoction) with Dahuang (rhubarb), Huanglian (Coptis), and the like added to treat it.

*紅中雙灰干舌\pt108a89.bmp*

In warm heat disease, when the tongue shows two strips of gray, it is caused by re-injuring [the stomach] with food and drink after the illness. It causes the person to have body heat and delirious speech, picking at the clothes and grasping at emptiness. If the pulse is slippery, one purge and it will be at ease. If the pulse is rough and black stool is passed, there is death.

*紅尖白根舌\pt108a90.bmp*

A red tip is the original color; a white coating is the exterior evil. If there is aversion to cold, body heat, and headache, it is appropriate to sweat it. If there is no aversion to cold, with body heat and vexation-thirst, this is the interior pattern of taiyang. Use Wuling san (Five-Ingredient Powder with Poria) to resolve both.

*紅戰舌\pt108a91.bmp*

A trembling tongue is one that shakes and is uneasy, wriggling and quivering. This pattern is caused by much sweating losing the yang, or by leaking wind. Select from Shiquan Dabu (All-Inclusive Great Tonification) and Da Jianzhong tang (Major Construct the Middle Decoction).

*紅細枯長舌\pt108a92.bmp*

When the tongue color is dry red and long and thin, it is the qi of the lesser yin being exhausted within, and not reaching up to communicate with the tongue. Even if there are no other symptoms, if the pulse is again failing and exhausted, morning and evening [life] is hard to preserve.

*紅短白疱舌\pt108a93.bmp*

When there are mouth sores, a short tongue with blisters, hoarse voice, dry throat, and vexation-agitation, it is pestilence being forcibly sweated, or cold damage not yet sweated and transforming into this pattern. It is appropriate to select from Huanglian Xijiao tang (Coptis and Rhinoceros Horn Decoction) and Sanhuang Shigao tang (Three-Yellow and Gypsum Decoction).

*邊紅通尖黑干舌\pt108a94.bmp*

In warm disease, if one does not know how to regulate and treat, or does not abstain from food and drink, or does not take decoction medicine, and so the center of the tongue becomes dry and black, urgently purge once or twice. When it is slightly resolved, purge again, taking the leveling as the period.

*紅尖紫刺舌\pt108a95.bmp*

When after sweating, from relapse of eating, a red tip with purple thorns is seen, the pattern is very urgent and dangerous. Use Zhizi Shizhi tang (Gardenia and Fermented Soybean Decoction) with Dahuang (rhubarb) added to purge it. Still scrape away the awn-thorns. If they do not grow again, there is ease; if they grow again, there is danger.

*紅尖黑根舌\pt108a96.bmp*

In pestilence at two or three days, when the root of the tongue is gray-black, urgently use Liangge (Cool the Diaphragm) and Shuangjie (Double Resolve) to purge slightly. When it reaches four or five days, fire in its extreme resembles water, gradually transforming into deep black, and purging is of no help. If the evil is bound in the throat, with closed eyes, a severed pulse, and oily sweat, death occurs within one or two days.

*紅嫩無津舌\pt108a97.bmp*

When sweating and purging are excessive, and the fluids are exhausted, and the tongue color is fresh red, soft and tender like newly born, looking as if moist but actually dry and exhausted, treat it with Shengmai san (Generate the Pulse Powder) combined with Renshen Sanbai tang (Ginseng Three-White Decoction). Yet it mostly does not respond.
