## 黑苔舌總論

### Source Text

傷寒五七日。舌見黑苔。最為危候。表證皆無此舌。如兩感一二日間見之。必死。若白苔上漸漸中心黑者。是傷寒邪熱傳裡之候。紅舌上漸漸黑者。乃瘟疫傳變。壞證將至也。蓋舌色本赤。今見黑者。乃水來剋火。水極似火。火過炭黑之理。然有純黑、有黑暈、有刺、有隔瓣、有瓣底紅、瓣底黑者。大抵尖黑猶輕。根黑最重。

如全黑者。總使神丹。亦難救療也。

遍舌黑苔。是火極似水。臟氣已絕。脈必代結。一二日中必死。切勿用藥。

*黑苔瓣底紅舌\pt108a48.bmp*

黃苔久而變黑。實熱亢極之候。又未經服藥。肆意飲食。而見脈伏、目閉、口開、獨語、譫妄。醫遇此證。必掘開舌苔。視瓣底紅者。可用大承氣下之。

*黑苔瓣底黑舌\pt108a49.bmp*

凡見瓣底黑者。不可用藥。雖無惡候。脈亦暴絕。必死不治。

*黑爛自齧舌\pt108a52.bmp*

*中黑邊白滑苔舌\pt108a53.bmp*

*紅邊中黑滑舌\pt108a54.bmp*

舌黑有津。證見譫語者。必表證時不曾服藥。不戒飲食。冷物結滯於胃也。虛人黃龍湯。或枳實理中加大黃。壯實者用備急丸熱下之。夏月中暍。多有此舌。以人參白虎湯主之。

*通尖黑干邊白舌\pt108a55.bmp*

*中燥舌\pt108a57.bmp*

*黑干短舌\pt108a60.bmp*

### Literal Translation

In cold damage, at five or seven days, when a black coating is seen on the tongue, it is the most dangerous sign. In all exterior patterns there is no such tongue. If it is seen within one or two days in double contraction, death is certain. If on a white coating the center gradually becomes black, it is the sign of the evil heat of cold damage transmitting to the interior. If on a red tongue it gradually becomes black, it is the transformation of pestilence, and a damaged pattern is about to arrive. For the tongue color is originally red; that black is now seen is the principle of water coming to overcome fire, water in its extreme resembling fire, and fire passing into charcoal black. Yet there are pure black, black halos, thorns, separated petals, petals with red base, and petals with black base. In general, a black tip is still light, and a black root is the most severe.

If it is entirely black, even if one uses a divine elixir, it is also difficult to rescue and treat.

*純黑舌\pt108a47.bmp*

When the whole tongue has a black coating, it is fire in its extreme resembling water, and the qi of the viscera is already exhausted. The pulse must be intermittent and knotted. Within one or two days death is certain. One must not use medicine.

*黑苔瓣底紅舌\pt108a48.bmp*

When a yellow coating has lasted long and turned black, it is the sign of true heat being at its extreme. Moreover, without having taken medicine, [the patient] has eaten and drunk wantonly, and shows a hidden pulse, closed eyes, open mouth, soliloquy, and delirious mania. When a physician meets this pattern, he must dig open the tongue coating and look. If the base of the petals is red, one may use Da Chengqi (Major Order the Qi Decoction) to purge it.

*黑苔瓣底黑舌\pt108a49.bmp*

In all cases where the base of the petals is seen to be black, one must not use medicine. Although there are no ominous signs, the pulse also suddenly ceases. Death is certain and it is not treated.

*滿黑刺底紅舌\pt108a50.bmp*

When the whole tongue has a black coating, dry and growing large thorns, and rubbing it touches the hand and makes a sound, and digging open the base of the thorns reveals a red color, the heart-spirit is still present. Although the fire is excessive, purging it can bring life. There are those who are fat and exuberant with much damp-heat, who catch a cold and have fever, with glomus, distention, oppression, and chaos. As soon as this tongue is seen, urgently use Da Xianxiong wan (Major Chest Binding Pill) to attack and purge. Afterwards give Xiao Xianxiong tang (Minor Chest Binding Decoction) to regulate.

*刺底黑舌\pt108a51.bmp*

"Black base of the thorns" means that when the awn-thorns are scraped away, the color of the flesh beneath is all black. In all cases where this tongue is seen, one need not distinguish which channel or which pulse. Although there are no ominous signs, death is certain and it is not treated.

*黑爛自齧舌\pt108a52.bmp*

When the tongue is black and rotten and [the patient] frequently wants to bite it, it must rot to the root and then death. Although there are no ominous signs or strange pulses, one must not use medicine.

*中黑邊白滑苔舌\pt108a53.bmp*

When the tongue shows a black middle and white slippery edges, both the exterior and interior are vacuous and cold. The pulse must be faint and weak, and the symptoms must be fear of cold. Use Fuzi Lizhong tang (Aconite Regulate the Middle Decoction) to warm it. If in summer months, from eating too much raw and cold [food], this tongue is seen, then it is appropriate to select from Dashun (Great Smoothing) and Lengxiang (Cold Aromatic).

*紅邊中黑滑舌\pt108a54.bmp*

When the tongue is black with moisture, and the symptoms show delirious speech, it must be that during the exterior pattern stage no medicine was taken, and diet was not abstained, and cold things became bound and stagnant in the stomach. For vacuous people, [use] Huanglong tang (Yellow Dragon Decoction), or Zhishi Lizhong (Unripe Bitter Orange Regulate the Middle) with Dahuang (rhubarb) added. For the robust and replete, use Beiji wan (Emergency Pill) to purge with heat. In summer months, in sunstroke, there is often this tongue; govern it with Renshen Baihu tang (Ginseng and White Tiger Decoction).

*通尖黑干邊白舌\pt108a55.bmp*

In double contraction, within one or two days, if a thick coating that is black in the middle and white at the edges is seen, even if one uses Da Qianghuo tang (Major Notopterygium Decoction), one fears it will be of no help.

*黑邊暈內微紅舌\pt108a56.bmp*

When the edges of the tongue are surrounded by black, with a slightly red halo within, it is the sign of the evil heat entering the pericardium. Therefore there is this color. It is appropriate to use Liangge (Cool the Diaphragm) combined with Da Chengqi (Major Order the Qi Decoction) to purge it.

*中燥舌\pt108a57.bmp*

When the center of the tongue coating is black, thick, and dry, it is the sign of exuberant heat and withered fluids. Urgently use Shengmai san (Generate the Pulse Powder) combined with Huanglian Jiedu tang (Coptis Detoxification Decoction) to resolve it.

*中黑無苔干燥舌\pt108a58.bmp*

When the tongue is black without coating and dry, the fluids are injured and vacuous fire is in charge. Urgently use Shengmai san (Generate the Pulse Powder) combined with Fuzi Lizhong tang (Aconite Regulate the Middle Decoction) to govern it.

*黑中無苔枯瘦舌\pt108a59.bmp*

In cold damage at eight or nine days, from excessive sweating, the fluids are withered and the blood is dry. The tongue has no coating and is black and thin. The stool has not moved for five or six days, the abdomen is not hard and distended, the spirit is clouded and [the patient] cannot sleep, or sometimes mutters and sighs. [Use] Zhigancao tang (Honey-Fried Licorice Decoction).

*黑干短舌\pt108a60.bmp*

When the tongue reaches dry black and short, the heat of the jueyin extreme is already deep, or it is caused by food filling the middle epigastrium and distention. Urgently use a large dose of Da Chengqi (Major Order the Qi Decoction) to purge it. One or two in ten may be saved. After taking it, if the stool is yellow and the heat recedes, there is life. If the stool is black and the heat does not stop, there is death.
