## 妊娠傷寒舌總論

### Source Text

妊娠傷寒。邪入經絡。輕則母病。重則子傷。枝傷果必墜。理所必然。故凡治此。當先固其胎氣。胎安則子母俱安。

面以候母。舌以候子。色澤則安。色敗則斃。面赤舌青者。子死母活。面舌俱青沫出者。母子俱死。亦有面舌俱白。母子皆死者。蓋謂色不澤也。

*孕婦傷寒白苔舌\pt108a115.bmp*

孕婦初傷於寒。而見面赤舌上白滑。即當微汗以解其表。如面舌俱白。因發熱多飲冷水。陽極變陰所致。當用溫中之藥。若見厥冷煩躁。誤與涼劑。則厥逆吐利而死。

*孕婦傷寒黃苔舌\pt108a116.bmp*

妊娠面赤舌黃。五六日裡證見。當微利之。庶免熱邪傷胎之患。若面舌俱黃。此失於發汗。濕熱入裡所致。

當用清熱利水藥。

*孕婦傷寒灰黑舌\pt108a117.bmp*

妊娠面舌俱黑。水火相刑。不必問其月數。子母俱死。面赤舌微黑者。還當保胎。如見灰黑。乃邪入子宮。其胎必不能固。若面赤者。根本未傷。當急下以救其母。

*孕婦傷寒純赤舌\pt108a118.bmp*

妊娠傷寒溫熱。而見面舌俱赤。宜隨證汗下。子母無虞。傷寒面色皎白。而舌赤者。母氣素虛。當用薑、桂等藥。

桂不墜胎。龐安常所言也。若面黑舌赤。亦非吉兆。若在臨月。則子得生而母當殞。

*孕婦傷寒紫青舌\pt108a119.bmp*

妊娠傷寒而見面赤舌紫。乃酒毒內傳所致。如淡紫戴青。為陰證夾食。即用枳實理中四逆輩。恐難為力也。

若面赤舌青。母雖無妨。子殞腹內。急宜平胃散加芒硝下之。

*孕婦傷寒卷短舌\pt108a120.bmp*

妊娠面黑而舌乾卷短。或黃黑刺裂。乃裡證至急，不下則熱邪傷胎。下之危在頃刻。如無直視循衣撮空等證。十中可救一二。

### Literal Translation

In cold damage during pregnancy, when the evil enters the channels and network vessels, in light cases the mother is ill, and in severe cases the child is injured. When the branch is injured, the fruit must fall. It is a natural principle. Therefore in all treatment of this, one should first secure the fetal qi. When the fetus is at ease, then both mother and child are at ease.

The face is used to examine the mother, and the tongue is used to examine the child. If the color is lustrous, there is ease; if the color is defeated, there is death. If the face is red and the tongue is blue, the child is dead and the mother lives. If both the face and the tongue are blue with froth issuing, both mother and child die. There are also those where both the face and the tongue are white, and both mother and child die. This is because the color is not lustrous.

*孕婦傷寒白苔舌\pt108a115.bmp*

When a pregnant woman is first injured by cold, and a red face with a white slippery tongue is seen, one should immediately sweat slightly to resolve her exterior. If both the face and the tongue are white, it is caused by fever with much drinking of cold water, the extreme yang transforming into yin. One should use medicines that warm the middle. If reversed cold and vexation-agitation are seen, and one mistakenly gives cold formulas, then there is reversal, vomiting, and diarrhea, and death.

*孕婦傷寒黃苔舌\pt108a116.bmp*

In pregnancy, when the face is red and the tongue is yellow, and at five or six days the interior pattern appears, one should mildly purge it, so as to avoid the harm of the evil heat injuring the fetus. If both the face and the tongue are yellow, this is caused by failure to effuse sweat, the damp-heat entering the interior. One should use medicines that clear heat and promote urination.

*孕婦傷寒灰黑舌\pt108a117.bmp*

In pregnancy, when both the face and the tongue are black, water and fire mutually punish. One need not ask the number of months; both mother and child die. If the face is red and the tongue is slightly black, one should still protect the fetus. If gray-black is seen, it is the evil entering the uterus, and the fetus must not be able to be secured. If the face is red, the root has not been injured; one should urgently purge to rescue the mother.

*孕婦傷寒純赤舌\pt108a118.bmp*

In cold damage and warm heat during pregnancy, when both the face and the tongue are red, one should sweat or purge according to the symptoms, and mother and child are without harm. In cold damage, if the face color is bright white and the tongue is red, the mother's qi is originally vacuous. One should use ginger, cinnamon, and such medicines. Cinnamon does not abort the fetus; this is what Pang Anchang said. If the face is black and the tongue is red, it is also not an auspicious sign. If it is near the month [of delivery], then the child obtains life and the mother should perish.

*孕婦傷寒紫青舌\pt108a119.bmp*

In cold damage during pregnancy, when a red face with a purple tongue is seen, it is caused by the wine toxin transmitting within. If it is faint purple with a tinge of green, it is a yin pattern with food mixed [in]. If one immediately uses Zhishi Lizhong (Unripe Bitter Orange Regulate the Middle) and Sini (Frigid Extremities) and the like, one fears it will be difficult to exert force. If the face is red and the tongue is blue, although the mother is without harm, the child perishes within the abdomen. Urgently use Pingwei san (Level the Stomach Powder) with mirabilite added to purge it.

*孕婦傷寒卷短舌\pt108a120.bmp*

In pregnancy, when the face is black and the tongue is dry, curled, and short, or yellow-black with thorns and fissures, it is the interior pattern being most urgent. If not purged, the evil heat injures the fetus. If purged, the danger is in a moment. If there are no symptoms of staring straight ahead, picking at the clothes, or grasping at emptiness, one or two in ten may be saved.
