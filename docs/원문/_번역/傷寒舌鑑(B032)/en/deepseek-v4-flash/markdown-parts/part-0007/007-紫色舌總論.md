## 紫色舌總論

### Source Text

紫舌苔者。酒後傷寒也。或大醉露臥當風。或已病而仍飲酒。或感冒不服藥。而用蔥薑熱酒發汗。汗雖出而酒熱留於心胞。沖行經絡。故舌見紫色。而又有微白苔也。苔結舌之根尖。長短厚薄。涎滑干焦。種種不同。

當參其源而治之。

*純紫舌\pt108a98.bmp*

傷寒以蔥酒發汗。酒毒入心。或酒後傷寒。皆有此舌。宜升麻葛根湯加石膏、滑石。若心煩懊憹不安。梔子豉湯。不然。必發斑也。

*紫中紅斑舌\pt108a99.bmp*

舌渾紫而又滿舌紅斑。或渾身更有赤斑者。宜化斑湯、解毒湯。俱加葛根、黃連、青黛。有下證者。涼膈散。

*紫上白滑舌\pt108a100.bmp*

舌紫而中見白苔者。酒後感寒。或誤飲冷酒所致。亦令人頭痛、惡寒、身熱。隨證解表可也。

*淡紫青筋舌\pt108a101.bmp*

舌淡紫帶青而潤。中絆青黑筋者。乃直中陰經。必身涼、四肢厥冷。脈沉面黑。四逆、理中等治之。

*紫上赤腫干焦舌\pt108a102.bmp*

舌邊紫而中心赤腫。足陽明受邪。或已下。便食酒肉。邪熱復聚所致。若赤腫津潤。大柴胡微利之。若煩躁厥逆脈伏。先用枳實理中。次用小承氣。

*紫上黃苔干燥舌\pt108a103.bmp*

嗜酒之人傷於寒。至四五日。舌紫。上積干黃苔者。急用大承氣下之。如表證未盡。用大柴胡湯。

*紫短舌\pt108a104.bmp*

舌紫短而團圞者。食滯中宮而熱。傳厥陰也。急用大承氣湯下之。下後熱退脈靜舌柔和者生。否則死。

*紫上黃苔濕潤舌\pt108a105.bmp*

舌淡青紫而中有黃濕苔。此食傷太陰也。脈必沉細。心下臍旁按之硬痛或矢氣者。小承氣加生附子。或黃龍湯主之。

*紫尖蓓蕾舌\pt108a106.bmp*

感寒之後。不戒酒食。而見咳嗽生痰。煩躁不寧。舌色淡紫。尖生蓓蕾。乃酒濕傷肺。味厚傷胃所致也。

宜小柴胡湯加減治之。

*熟紫老干舌\pt108a107.bmp*

舌全紫如煮熟者。乃熱邪傳入厥陰。至篤之兆。當歸四逆湯。

*淡紫帶青舌\pt108a108.bmp*

舌色青紫無苔。且滑潤瘦小。為直中腎肝陰證。吳茱萸湯、四逆湯急溫之。

*淡紫灰心舌\pt108a109.bmp*

舌淡紫而中心帶灰。或青黑。不燥不濕者。為邪傷血分。雖有下證。只宜犀角地黃湯加酒大黃微利之。

### Literal Translation

A purple tongue coating is cold damage after drinking wine. Or [it is from] being greatly drunk and sleeping exposed in the wind, or already being ill and still drinking wine, or catching a cold and not taking medicine but using scallion, ginger, and hot wine to effuse sweat. Although the sweat comes out, the heat of the wine remains in the pericardium, rushing and traveling through the channels and network vessels. Therefore the tongue shows a purple color, and moreover has a slight white coating. The coating binds the root and tip of the tongue, with long and short, thick and thin, slippery and dry, and scorched, in all manner of differences.

One should consult its origin and treat it.

*純紫舌\pt108a98.bmp*

In cold damage, when scallion and wine are used to effuse sweat, and the wine toxin enters the heart, or cold damage after drinking wine, both have this tongue. It is appropriate to use Shengma Gegen tang (Cimicifuga and Pueraria Decoction) with Shigao (gypsum) and Huashi (talcum) added. If there is vexation and agitation in the heart, uneasy, [use] Zhizi Chi tang (Gardenia and Fermented Soybean Decoction). Otherwise, there must be maculation.

*紫中紅斑舌\pt108a99.bmp*

When the tongue is entirely purple and moreover has red spots all over the tongue, or the whole body also has red spots, it is appropriate to use Huaban tang (Transform Maculation Decoction) and Jiedu tang (Detoxification Decoction), both with Gegen (Pueraria), Huanglian (Coptis), and Qingdai (indigo) added. If there is a purging pattern, [use] Liangge san (Cool the Diaphragm Powder).

*紫上白滑舌\pt108a100.bmp*

When the tongue is purple with a white coating seen in the middle, it is caused by catching cold after drinking wine, or mistakenly drinking cold wine. It also causes headache, aversion to cold, and body heat. One may resolve the exterior according to the symptoms.

*淡紫青筋舌\pt108a101.bmp*

When the tongue is faint purple with a tinge of green and moist, with blue-black sinews bound in the middle, it is direct striking of the yin channels. The body must be cool, the four limbs reversed and cold, and the pulse sunken with a black face. Use Sini (Frigid Extremities), Lizhong (Regulate the Middle), and the like to treat it.

*紫上赤腫干焦舌\pt108a102.bmp*

When the edges of the tongue are purple and the center is red and swollen, the foot yangming has received the evil. Or after already purging, [the patient] then eats wine and meat, and the evil heat again gathers. If the red swelling has moisture, use Da Chaihu (Major Bupleurum) to mildly purge it. If there is vexation-agitation, reversal, and a hidden pulse, first use Zhishi Lizhong (Unripe Bitter Orange Regulate the Middle), next use Xiao Chengqi (Minor Order the Qi).

*紫上黃苔干燥舌\pt108a103.bmp*

When a wine-loving person is injured by cold, and at four or five days the tongue is purple with a dry yellow coating accumulated on it, urgently use Da Chengqi (Major Order the Qi Decoction) to purge it. If the exterior pattern is not exhausted, use Da Chaihu tang (Major Bupleurum Decoction).

*紫短舌\pt108a104.bmp*

When the tongue is purple, short, and round, it is food stagnation in the middle palace with heat, transmitting to the jueyin. Urgently use Da Chengqi tang (Major Order the Qi Decoction) to purge it. If after purging the heat recedes, the pulse is still, and the tongue is soft and harmonious, there is life. Otherwise, death.

*紫上黃苔濕潤舌\pt108a105.bmp*

When the tongue is faint blue-purple with a yellow moist coating in the middle, this is food injuring the greater yin. The pulse must be sunken and fine. If pressing below the heart and beside the navel is hard and painful, or there is passing of gas, use Xiao Chengqi (Minor Order the Qi) with raw aconite added, or Huanglong tang (Yellow Dragon Decoction) to govern it.

*紫尖蓓蕾舌\pt108a106.bmp*

After catching cold, if one does not abstain from wine and food, and sees cough producing phlegm, vexation-agitation and restlessness, a faint purple tongue color, and buds growing at the tip, it is the wine dampness injuring the lung and the rich flavor injuring the stomach. It is appropriate to treat it with Xiao Chaihu tang (Minor Bupleurum Decoction) with additions and subtractions.

*熟紫老干舌\pt108a107.bmp*

When the whole tongue is purple like something cooked, it is the heat evil transmitting into the jueyin, the sign of reaching the critical point. [Use] Danggui Sini tang (Angelica Frigid Extremities Decoction).

*淡紫帶青舌\pt108a108.bmp*

When the tongue color is blue-purple without coating, and moreover slippery, moist, thin, and small, it is the direct striking of the kidney and liver yin patterns. Use Wuzhuyu tang (Evodia Decoction) and Sini tang (Frigid Extremities Decoction) to warm it urgently.

*淡紫灰心舌\pt108a109.bmp*

When the tongue is faint purple with a gray center, or blue-black, neither dry nor moist, it is the evil injuring the blood aspect. Although there is a purging pattern, one should only use Xijiao Dihuang tang (Rhinoceros Horn and Rehmannia Decoction) with wine-prepared Dahuang (rhubarb) added to mildly purge it.
